(U) Polish authorities successfully thwarted a covert Russian assassination plot targeting an American citizen of Ukrainian origin in Warsaw. Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk highlighted the operation as an unprecedented event—marking the first known instance where Russian services attempted to target a U.S. citizen within the territory of another NATO member state.
### **Key Details of the Plot**

- **The Target:** The intended victim was described as a U.S. citizen of Ukrainian descent who was viewed as "uncomfortable" or a threat by Vladimir Putin's regime. Further personal details were withheld for security reasons.
- **The Arrest:** The suspect, who was allegedly recruited by Russian secret services to carry out the execution, was apprehended by Polish law enforcement. A Warsaw court placed the suspect in custody for three months.
- **Intelligence Cooperation:** The successful disruption of the plot was the result of close coordination and intelligence-sharing between Polish authorities and U.S. security services.
### **Broader Context**

(U) Western intelligence and security officials have repeatedly warned that Russia has escalated a broader campaign of targeted killings, sabotage, and hybrid warfare across Europe since the 2022 invasion of Ukraine. However, this specific plot represents a major escalation due to its direct targeting of an American citizen on NATO soil.
