# The plot to stop the AfD Germany’s firewall is crumbling in Saxony-Anhalt

[Source](https://freerepublic.com/focus/f-chat/4394321/posts)

(U) MAGDEBURG, Germany — This opinion analysis argued that Germany's established parties are preparing extraordinary measures to prevent the far-right Alternative for Germany (AfD) from governing in Saxony-Anhalt, where the party was polling near 43 percent ahead of the September 6 state election. The author, Thomas Fazi, described reported contingency planning that included excluding the AfD from interior-minister conferences, withholding roughly 3 billion euros in fiscal equalization funds and invoking federal coercion under Article 37 of the Basic Law. The piece is a partisan commentary and its characterizations are the author's own.
