# Iran and US hit tankers in biggest wave of attacks on shipping since war began

[Source](https://uk.news.yahoo.com/iran-attacks-us-jordan-ships-040647971.html)

(U) DUBAI, United Arab Emirates — Iran attacked at least 10 ships near the Strait of Hormuz after US forces sank five Iranian oil tankers, in the largest wave of strikes on shipping since the six-month war began. One seafarer was killed and another reported missing, and Brent crude breached $100 a barrel. Iran also fired at a US base in Jordan.
