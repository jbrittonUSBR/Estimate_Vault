# TV6 Investigates: ‘You can never be too careful,’ UP water systems combat cyberattacks

[Source](https://www.uppermichiganssource.com/2026/09/30/tv6-investigates-you-can-never-be-too-careful-up-water-systems-combat-cyberattacks/)

(U) MARQUETTE, Mich. — TV6 investigated how Upper Peninsula water utilities are guarding against cyberattacks after more than 30 Minnesota water systems were hit over two days in July and nine Michigan communities were targeted in August, following an April federal advisory from the EPA, FBI, CISA and NSA. City of Marquette Public Works Superintendent Scott Cambensy said a disruption that released too much of a chemical could contaminate the water supply, and Escanaba Water and Wastewater Superintendent Jeff Lampi said his SCADA systems are self-contained with no internet connection, with outside access handled by hard cable and VPN. The EPA said unidentified threat actors had exploited internet-exposed programmable logic controllers at more than 100 drinking water and wastewater systems across 12 states, while Houghton Public Works Director Ryan Avendt cited redundant alarms and real-time security monitoring.
