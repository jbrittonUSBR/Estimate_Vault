# Yemen’s Houthis seize strategic Red Sea city of Mocha

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/10/yemens-houthis-seize-strategic-red-sea-city-of-mocha)

(U) MOCHA, Yemen — Yemen's Houthis seized the Red Sea city of Mocha on September 10, 2026, expanding their control over the Bab al-Mandeb Strait as internationally recognised government forces withdrew, sources told Al Jazeera and Reuters. Yemeni officials, including Presidential Leadership Council head Rashad al-Alimi and Foreign Minister Afrah al-Zouba, called for international intervention, while the UN Security Council held an emergency session at which Special Envoy Hans Grundberg warned Yemen faced its gravest risk of full-scale war since the 2022 truce. Houthi spokesman Mohammed Abdul Salam described the offensive as a "national operation" asserting Yemeni sovereignty and said Red Sea navigation remained safe, though the group maintained its ban on Saudi ships.
