# Global experts warn of evolving chemical security threats

[Source](https://www.interpol.int/News-and-Events/News/2026/Global-experts-warn-of-evolving-chemical-security-threats)

(U) PANAMA CITY, Panama — INTERPOL reported that 370 participants from 110 member countries met in Panama City from September 21 to 24, 2026, for the sixth plenary of the Global Congress on Chemical Security and Emerging Threats, warning that criminal and terrorist actors are exploiting emerging technologies, global supply chains and trafficking routes to obtain and divert chemicals of concern. INTERPOL Executive Director Stephen Fry said non-state actors are "operating with increasing complexity, scale, and speed" and called for borderless intelligence-sharing, robust legal frameworks and a refusal to work in silos. The plenary closed with five priorities, including stronger regional networks, timely information-sharing on emerging threats, monitoring of dual-use technologies and expansion of the network, which has grown to more than 2,000 members in 130 countries since 2018; the congress is implemented with Global Affairs Canada, the U.S. Defense Threat Reduction Agency and the UK Ministry of Defence.
