# What is Jordan’s Al-Azraq base and why is Iran targeting it?

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/9/what-is-jordans-al-azraq-base-and-why-is-iran-targeting-it)

(U) AMMAN, Jordan — Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps launched ballistic missiles at Jordan's Muwaffaq Salti Air Base, known as Al-Azraq, which hosts US forces and F-35, F-16 and F-22 aircraft. Jordan's Armed Forces said 18 of 20 missiles were intercepted and two fell in open areas, with no casualties reported. The base sits roughly 100 kilometers east of Amman and is a key hub for US air operations in the region.
