# 20 Years Since the Lancaster County Amish Schoolhouse Shooting

[Source](https://www.abc27.com/local-news/20-years-since-lancaster-county-amish-schoolhouse-shooting/)

(U) LANCASTER, Pa. — ABC27 marked the 20th anniversary of the 2006 shooting at the West Nickel Mines Amish schoolhouse in Lancaster County, Pennsylvania, in which a gunman killed five girls and wounded five others before taking his own life. The commemorative reporting revisits the attack and its aftermath, including the Amish community's widely noted acts of forgiveness toward the shooter's family, and reflects on its lasting impact on the community and on school-safety practices. The item is a retrospective rather than a report of a new incident.
