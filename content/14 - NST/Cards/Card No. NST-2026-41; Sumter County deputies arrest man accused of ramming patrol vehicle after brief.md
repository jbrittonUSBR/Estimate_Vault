# Sumter County deputies arrest man accused of ramming patrol vehicle after brief manhunt

[Source](https://www.fox35orlando.com/news/sumter-county-deputies-arrest-man-accused-ramming-patrol-vehicle-after-brief-manhunt)

(U) WILDWOOD, Fla. — The Sumter County Sheriff's Office arrested 34-year-old John Riehm at the Wildwood RV Village, about a mile from where he allegedly struck a deputy and a patrol vehicle with his own vehicle during a traffic stop on September 30, 2026 and then fled. Deputies said Riehm resisted arrest while they attempted to take him into custody on active warrants; detectives tracked him to the RV park overnight and took him into custody without further incident. The sheriff's office credited its detectives and said the arrest "brought this incident to a safe conclusion," FOX 35 reported.
