# Suspect arrested in alleged mass shooting threat at Texas State Capitol, ABC News sources say

[Source](https://abc13.com/post/planned-attack-texas-state-capitol-austin-thwarted-dps-fbi-authorities-say/19893302/)

(U) AUSTIN, Texas — Texas Department of Public Safety SWAT officers arrested Benny Caldera, 40, at his home in Converse, Texas, early on September 30, 2026, after receiving credible information that he was planning a mass shooting at the Texas State Capitol, ABC News reported, citing sources. Caldera was booked into the Bexar County Jail and charged with felony terroristic threat against a public servant, with the alleged attack reportedly set for October 1. State Senate Secretary Patsy Spaw told senators the threat was not cause for concern but that an enhanced DPS presence would continue at the Capitol, and an official briefed on the investigation said detectives were seeking search warrants to determine whether there were co-conspirators.
