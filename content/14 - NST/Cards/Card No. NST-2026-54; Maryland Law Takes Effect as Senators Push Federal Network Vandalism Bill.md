# Maryland Law Takes Effect as Senators Push Federal Network Vandalism Bill

[Source](https://broadbandbreakfast.com/maryland-law-takes-effect-as-senators-push-federal-network-vandalism-bill/)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — A new Maryland law aimed at making stolen telecommunications equipment harder to sell took effect as two U.S. senators introduced bipartisan legislation to strengthen federal penalties for vandalizing broadband networks. The law follows recurring copper theft in Cecil County, where Verizon crews told WMAR-TV that thieves had been cutting telecommunications cables multiple times a week, disrupting phone and internet service.
