# KPD cites man for damaging chuckwalla statue, makes arrest after unrelated police chase

[Source](https://www.kdminer.com/news/kpd-cites-man-for-damaging-chuckwalla-statue-makes-arrest-after-unrelated-police-chase/article_1d15e392-a12b-4a9b-a76f-5d39f3459ab7.html)

(U) KINGMAN, Ariz. — The Kingman Police Department cited Mike Graham, 56, for failing to report that he damaged a decorative 1,800-pound concrete chuckwalla sculpture near the railroad tracks in downtown Kingman, which was struck by a 2007 Chevrolet Silverado pickup on September 11, 2026. Deputy Police Chief Joel Freed described the case as "reptile road rage," saying witnesses told investigators Graham hated the lizard; Graham said he did not intend to run over it, held no grudge and would pay for the damage. Two days after his interview at the police department, Graham allegedly used the same truck to lead officers on pursuits and deliberately rammed a KPD patrol vehicle, and he was charged with two counts of aggravated assault, unlawful flight, reckless driving, criminal damage and resisting arrest.
