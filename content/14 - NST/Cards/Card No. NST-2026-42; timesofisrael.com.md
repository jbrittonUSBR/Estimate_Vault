# Israel's US Envoy Says Son Still on Life Support After Car-Ramming Attack; Netanyahu Visited

[Source](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/israels-us-envoy-says-son-still-on-life-support-after-car-ramming-attack-netanyahu-visited-today/)

(U) JERUSALEM — Israel's ambassador to the United States said his son remained on life support after being injured in a car-ramming attack, according to a Times of Israel liveblog entry. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu visited the envoy's family, the report said. The entry provides a continuation of coverage of the vehicle-ramming incident involving the ambassador's son, which the family described as a terror attack, with the victim still in critical condition.
