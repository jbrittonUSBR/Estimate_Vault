# Houthis seize key Yemeni Red Sea port, sources say, threatening global energy flows

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/defence/houthis-seize-key-yemeni-red-sea-port-sources-say-threatening-global-energy-flows/articleshow/134012590.cms)

(U) CAIRO — Iran-aligned Houthi militants seized control of Yemen's port city of Mocha on September 10, 2026, posing a further threat to global energy supplies, four Yemeni government military sources said, in a Reuters report. The sources said the Houthis had gained further leverage over the Bab el-Mandeb Strait and were launching attacks on the strategic Hanish islands, as government forces relocated south toward Dhubab. The advances came hours after President Donald Trump said he expected the war with Iran to end after the November midterm elections, and Brent crude prices hovered above $100 a barrel amid disrupted supply routes.
