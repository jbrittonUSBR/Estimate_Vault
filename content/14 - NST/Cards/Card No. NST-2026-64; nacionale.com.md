# Dutch Spy Agency Warns Modern Vehicle Technology Could Be Used for Espionage by Hostile States

[Source](https://www.nacionale.com/en/articles/dutch-agency-warns-vehicle-technology-espionage-hostile-states)

(U) THE HAGUE — The Dutch General Intelligence and Security Service (AIVD) issued an explainer, "The Modern Car as a Potential Spy: Digital Threats in Smart Vehicles," warning that connected-vehicle features — microphones, cameras, GPS, infotainment systems, telematics modules, and EV charging stations — could be exploited by hostile states for espionage. The AIVD stated it is "technically possible" to remotely activate microphones and cameras in some vehicles to eavesdrop on conversations and record audio in and around the vehicle. The advisory is aimed primarily at officials in business and government who handle sensitive information, recommending they avoid confidential conversations in or around vehicles, refrain from using vehicles with external cameras for travel to sensitive locations, avoid automatic phone connections in favor of USB data blockers, and limit sensitive addresses in built-in navigation. The warning coincided with rising concern over Chinese vehicle imports — Chinese brands accounted for roughly 15% of new UK car registrations in the first half of 2026 — and the UK Ministry of Defence said it was working to mitigate potential national security threats, including banning vehicles with Chinese parts at sensitive military locations.
