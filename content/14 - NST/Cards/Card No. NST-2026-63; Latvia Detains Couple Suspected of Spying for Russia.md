# Latvia Detains Couple Suspected of Spying for Russia

[Source](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/85922)

(U) Riga, Latvia — Latvia's State Security Service (VDD) said two Latvian citizens were detained on suspicion of collecting and passing information to Russian Military Intelligence (GRU), including details on the country's defence systems, NATO allies' activities and forms of support for Ukraine. The couple was detained on June 28 in cooperation with the Defense Intelligence and Security Service (MIDD), and criminal proceedings were opened on suspicion of transferring non-disclosable material to a foreign intelligence service. One suspect allegedly photographed the Ādaži Military Base, home to NATO's multinational brigade in Latvia, using a small private aircraft on behalf of the GRU; searches of three locations in Riga and Ādaži municipality seized electronic devices, documents and notes.
