# War, surveillance changing how Christians defend religious freedom, advocates say

[Source](https://www.christianpost.com/news/war-surveillance-pose-new-challenges-to-religious-freedom.html)

(U) DALLAS, United States — Religious freedom advocates told a gathering at Dallas Baptist University that armed conflict and state surveillance are undermining the methods Christians use to defend persecuted believers. Wissam al-Saliby of 21Wilberforce said 65 armed conflicts are active worldwide and that war weakens civil society, prompts restrictions on religion and expression, and disrupts international advocacy. Bob Fu of ChinaAid said China is exporting surveillance technology and monitoring people beyond its borders, while other panelists urged churches to work across denominational lines.
