# Criminal recruiters want people on your payroll

[Source](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/02/intel-471-insider-threat-recruitment-report/)

(U) ZAGREB, Croatia — Cybercriminals sought employees at specific organizations and offered payments to brokers and referrers to find suitable personnel, according to Intel 471's "Insiders for Hire" report. Recruitment accounted for 45 of the 85 records analyzed, with transportation the most frequently referenced target industry at 19 leads, followed by technology at 17 and telecommunications at 15. FedEx and UPS were each mentioned in nine of the 85 leads, the highest count for any organization.
