(U) A helicopter crash in central Kenya's Samburu County resulted in multiple fatalities after going down near Mount Ololokwe.
### **Key Details**

- **The Incident:** The crash involving a Eurocopter EC130 B4 helicopter occurred at approximately 09:13 local time.
- **Flight Details:** The aircraft was operating on a scenic charter flight carrying tourists, traveling from the Loisaba wildlife conservancy toward the Ewaso Nyiro river area.
- **Response & Investigation:** Due to the rugged and remote nature of the terrain around Mount Ololokwe, initial access to the crash site was heavily restricted, with responders utilizing drones to evaluate the scene. The Kenya Civil Aviation Authority (KCAA), the transport ministry, and the Air Accident Investigation Department initiated a formal inquiry into the cause of the disaster.
