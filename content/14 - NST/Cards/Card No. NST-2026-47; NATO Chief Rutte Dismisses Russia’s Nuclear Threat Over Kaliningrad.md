# NATO Chief Rutte Dismisses Russia’s Nuclear Threat Over Kaliningrad

[Source](https://united24media.com/world/i-dont-take-this-seriously-nato-chief-rutte-dismisses-russias-nuclear-threat-over-kaliningrad-23014)

(U) BRUSSELS, Belgium — NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte said on September 30, 2026 that he does not take seriously Russia's threat to use nuclear weapons if NATO members attempt to isolate Kaliningrad, telling journalists at the Euronews Defence & Space Summit 2026 that "Putin knows that he can never win from NATO." Rutte declined to disclose details of an unofficial letter reportedly sent by Moscow containing the threats, saying he was not authorized to make its contents public, but said NATO had responded directly and that the alliance is defensive. He urged Moscow to end its war in Ukraine and its nuclear rhetoric, and said Russian hybrid activity was intended to divide NATO and divert attention from Ukraine; Reuters reported that Russia privately warned NATO it could resort to nuclear weapons over Kaliningrad.
