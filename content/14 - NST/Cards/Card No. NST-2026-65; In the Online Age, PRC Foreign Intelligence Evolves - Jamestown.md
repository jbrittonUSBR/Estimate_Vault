# In the Online Age, PRC Foreign Intelligence Evolves

[Source](https://jamestown.org/in-the-online-age-prc-foreign-intelligence-evolves/)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — Chinese intelligence services are increasingly shifting from human intelligence collection to digital operations launched from within China, using online recruitment, commercial consulting arrangements and third-country intermediaries, according to a Jamestown Foundation China Brief analysis. The report said the Ministry of State Security uses LinkedIn en masse for headhunter-style approaches and that Beijing likely adopted the model after the 2018 arrest of MSS officer Xu Yanjun. Traditional face-to-face tradecraft remains important, with officers integrating in-person contact inside China to manage overseas contacts.
