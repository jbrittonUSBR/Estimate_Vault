(U) The Swedish Security Service (**Säpo**) announced that it successfully uncovered and disrupted a covert intelligence operation directed by Russia's Foreign Intelligence Service (**SVR**). The operation was designed to gather internal information to influence Swedish decision-making and undermine confidence in Sweden, the European Union (EU), and NATO.
### **Key Details of the Operation**

- **The Method:** Russian intelligence operatives used covert methods to recruit and utilize an agent embedded within a foreign diplomatic mission stationed in Sweden.
- **The Objectives:** The network sought to extract detailed insights into Sweden’s decision-making processes. Moscow intended to weaponize this information to fuel influence campaigns around polarizing or contentious issues, ultimately aiming to discredit Sweden and its Western alliances.
- **Säpo’s Intervention:** Säpo tracked the operation closely over an extended period, documenting meetings and assignments between the foreign agent and Russian handlers. Swedish authorities implemented measures to neutralize the threat and prevent further information leaks.
### **Aftermath**

Because the individuals involved operated under diplomatic cover, they could not be prosecuted criminally. However, Säpo confirmed that the targeted agent and the Russian intelligence officers involved have since left Sweden.
