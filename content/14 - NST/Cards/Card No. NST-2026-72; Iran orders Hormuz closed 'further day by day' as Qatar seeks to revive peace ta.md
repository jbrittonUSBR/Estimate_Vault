# Iran orders Hormuz closed 'further day by day' as Qatar seeks to revive peace talks

[Source](https://www.euronews.com/2026/11/24/iran-orders-hormuz-closed-further-day-by-day-as-qatar-seeks-to-revive-peace-talks)

(U) TEHRAN, IRAN — Iran's army commander-in-chief ordered the navy on November 23 to close the Strait of Hormuz "further day by day" until the United States met Tehran's seven conditions, warning that any new U.S. military action would draw "more severe, more crushing" blows without geographical limits, according to Euronews. Major General Amir Hatami said Iran would exercise stronger control over the strait, while Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf threatened military ways to break what he called the U.S. blockade. The Joint Maritime Information Centre said it recorded 114 passages through the strait with U.S. assistance between November 19 and 21 and could not confirm reports of tankers hitting mines; Qatari Prime Minister Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani met Secretary of State Marco Rubio and Vice President JD Vance in Washington to push for renewed talks.
