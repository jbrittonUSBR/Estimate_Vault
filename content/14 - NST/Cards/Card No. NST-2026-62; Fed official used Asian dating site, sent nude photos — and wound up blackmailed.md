# Fed official used Asian dating site, sent nude photos — and wound up blackmailed by alleged Chinese spy

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/09/30/business/fed-official-used-asian-dating-site-sent-nude-photos-and-wound-up-blackmailed-by-alleged-chinese-spy/)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — Former senior Federal Reserve official John Harold Rogers was arrested in January 2025 after prosecutors said he shared sensitive Fed information with a Chinese intelligence operative he knew as Hummin Lee. Rogers, who met his second wife on AsianDating.com, was later blackmailed over nude photos he sent from a Fed-issued phone; he was acquitted of conspiring to commit economic espionage but convicted of making false statements and sentenced to 38 months in prison. His lawyers denied the allegations and said he had not deliberately lied.
