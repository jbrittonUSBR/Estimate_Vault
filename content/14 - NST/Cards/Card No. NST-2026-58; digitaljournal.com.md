# Insider Threats Evolve as AI Exposes Hidden Pathways Across Corporate Systems

[Source](https://www.digitaljournal.com/article/insider-threats-evolve-as-ai-exposes-hidden-pathways-across-corporate-systems/)

(U) TORONTO — Digital Journal reports that insider threats are evolving as artificial intelligence tools expose hidden pathways and relationships across corporate systems, enabling malicious or negligent insiders to identify sensitive data and access routes more efficiently. The article describes how AI can accelerate reconnaissance and data exfiltration across interconnected enterprise systems, complicating traditional insider-threat detection and highlighting the need for updated governance and monitoring controls.
