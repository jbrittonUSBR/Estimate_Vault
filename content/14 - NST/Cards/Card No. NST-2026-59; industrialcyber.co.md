# Google GTIG finds AI accelerating vulnerability discovery across enterprise and critical infrastructure attack surfaces

[Source](https://industrialcyber.co/critical-infrastructure/google-gtig-finds-ai-accelerating-vulnerability-discovery-across-enterprise-and-critical-infrastructure-attack-surfaces/)

(U) MOUNTAIN VIEW, United States — Google Threat Intelligence Group reported that vulnerability disclosures doubled from 5,045 in January 2026 to 10,740 in August, while the average number of vulnerabilities exploited each month rose from 10.5 in 2025 to 18 in the first eight months of 2026. The group attributed the growth mainly to rapid weaponization of previously disclosed high-risk vulnerabilities rather than a major increase in zero-day exploitation. It also found that 50 percent of AI-discovered vulnerabilities resulted in remote code execution, compared with 26 percent across the broader CVE ecosystem.
