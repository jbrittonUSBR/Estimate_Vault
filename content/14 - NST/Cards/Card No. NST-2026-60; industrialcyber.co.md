# Alliance for Critical Infrastructure expands membership across US sectors to address evolving cyber, physical threats

[Source](https://industrialcyber.co/critical-infrastructure/alliance-for-critical-infrastructure-expands-membership-across-us-sectors-to-address-evolving-cyber-physical-threats/)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — The Alliance for Critical Infrastructure expanded its membership to nearly 50 companies across six U.S. critical infrastructure sectors, including communications, energy, financial services, information technology, transportation, and water systems. The alliance said it would focus on physical and cyber resilience, shared vulnerabilities, cross-sector dependencies, supply-chain security, and risks from large language models. JPMorganChase Chairman and CEO Jamie Dimon serves as chairman of the board, and the group said the expansion builds on more than a decade of private-sector national security work.
