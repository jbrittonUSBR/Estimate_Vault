# Suspected extortion arson shuts Negev sewage plant, sending raw waste toward Beersheba

[Source](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/hjrcw11aqgg)

(U) BEERSHEBA, Israel — Suspected extortion arson disabled the electrical room of the Neve Midbar water corporation's wastewater treatment plant, sending about 30,000 cubic meters of raw sewage per day through the Hebron Stream toward Beersheba, the company and Israel's Environmental Protection Ministry said. A fire investigator determined the blaze was most likely deliberate after finding diesel fuel and tires in the electrical room, and the corporation linked the attack to a year of protection-racket incidents and its refusal to pay extortion demands. The ministry estimated repairs could take several weeks and advised downstream communities including Al-Kasom, Omer, Bnei Shimon, Beersheba and Eshkol to warn residents of foul odors, while Beersheba Mayor Ruvik Danilovich accused the state of lawlessness and inaction against criminal gangs.
