# Arson Investigation Underway After Fire Destroys New Benjamin Harrison Statue in Indianapolis

[Source](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/an-arson-investigation-is-underway-after-a-suspected-sensless-act-of-vandalism-destroys-a-newly-installed-benjamin-harrison-statue-180989605/)

(U) INDIANAPOLIS — Police are conducting an arson investigation after a fire engulfed a life-size 3D-printed statue of President Benjamin Harrison at the Benjamin Harrison Presidential Site in Indianapolis. The blaze was reported around 1:30 a.m. on September 27 along the site's Neighborway pedestrian path; Indianapolis Fire Department crews responded within about 15 minutes but could not save the 5-foot-6 white polymer statue, which had been installed on September 16 during Constitution Week as part of the nation's 250th anniversary. The statue was a total loss — only its armature remains — and Indianapolis Metropolitan Police Department arson investigators are examining whether it was deliberately set, with site officials noting the fire-resistant material gave "every indication" of arson. Valued at roughly $8,500 and requiring more than 1,200 hours of student and faculty work at Indiana University Indianapolis's Herron School of Art + Design, the piece was the first installation in a new public-art initiative. The presidential site has launched a crowdfunding campaign to replace it.
