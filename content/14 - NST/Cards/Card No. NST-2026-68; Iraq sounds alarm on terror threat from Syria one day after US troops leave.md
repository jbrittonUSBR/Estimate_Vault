# Iraq sounds alarm on terror threat from Syria one day after US troops leave

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/iraq-sounds-alarm-terror-threat-syria-day-after-us-troops-leave)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — Iraqi Foreign Minister Fuad Hussein said Baghdad was preparing for the possibility that Islamic State fighters could cross the border from Syria, one day after the U.S. military formally ended its counter-ISIS mission in Iraq. Hussein told reporters that Iraq was watching ISIS activity in Syria and that Iraqi forces could handle the group without the large U.S.-led coalition presence. He also confirmed that a recent attack on Saudi Arabia originated from Iraqi territory near the Iranian border, prompting an investigation and consultations with Riyadh and Tehran.
