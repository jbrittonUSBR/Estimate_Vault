# Why universities are becoming a new frontline in China espionage concerns

[Source](https://decode39.com/16815/why-universities-are-becoming-a-new-frontline-in-china-espionage-concerns/)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — Britain's MI5 issued an "Espionage Alert" warning that more than 100 UK-linked academics contributed to projects funded by China's Ministry of State Security through the China General Technology Research Institute. According to an analysis by cybersecurity expert Antonio Teti cited by Decode39, the scheme did not necessarily require clandestine recruitment or classified documents, because strategic value can arise from ordinary collaborations, funding, joint publications and transfers of expertise. MI5 urged British universities to review cooperation involving CGTRI and trace the ultimate source of funding behind research partnerships with Chinese institutions.
