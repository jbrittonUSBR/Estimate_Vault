# San Francisco residents concerned after apartment call boxes vandalized

[Source](https://www.nbcbayarea.com/news/local/sf-residents-apartment-call-boxes-vandalized/4151932/)

(U) SAN FRANCISCO, United States — Residents of apartment buildings on Scott Street in the Marina District reported that call boxes were vandalized with water in what appeared to be an attempt to force entry and steal packages. A tenant said his property owner warned tenants Monday that the building's call box was out of service, and a security company president said his firm repairs dozens of water-damaged call boxes in the city each month. San Francisco police said they were not seeing a citywide uptick in such incidents and had made no arrests in the most recent case.
