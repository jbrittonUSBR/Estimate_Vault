(U) The Iranian military announced a formal bounty program offering financial rewards to citizens for killing or capturing American service members. The announcement underscores escalating tensions following direct military conflicts earlier in the year and the subsequent collapse of peace negotiations.
### **Key Details of the Bounty Program**

- **The Reward Structure:** Iran's army commander-in-chief, Amir Hatami, stated that anyone who kills or captures an "invading American military personnel" and hands them over will receive a reward equivalent to **$30,000** (5 billion tomans).
- **Double Incentive for Women:** The reward is **doubled to $60,000** if the action is carried out by an Iranian woman.
- **Origin of the Plan:** Hatami claimed the initiative was drafted in response to a "large number of requests" from the public looking to participate in financial support against U.S. forces.
### **Broader Context**

- **Lack of Ground Presence:** There has been no reported deployment of U.S. ground forces inside Iran during the ongoing Middle East conflict, save for a specialized rescue mission earlier in the year for a downed American pilot. U.S. casualties during the conflict have occurred outside Iran, such as in Jordan and Iraq.
- **Regional Posture:** Alongside the bounty announcement, Iranian leadership reiterated demands for the U.S. to completely withdraw from the region, asserting that American forces no longer have permission to operate freely in the Persian Gulf, the Gulf of Oman, or the Strait of Hormuz.
