# Two Swedes Detained in Finland on Suspicion of Espionage

[Source](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/two-swedes-detained-in-finland-on-suspicion-of-espionage/)

(U) HELSINKI, Finland — Finland's Central Criminal Police (KRP) is investigating two Swedish citizens, aged 37 and 40, on suspicion of espionage that may have occurred between August 30 and September 12, 2026. The Helsinki District Court remanded both men in custody on September 16 after prosecutors and the KRP requested detention under the Finnish legal threshold of "there is reason to suspect," and authorities have not disclosed where the suspected activity took place. The men share a surname and live at the same address in Härnösand, Sweden, according to the Finnish newspaper Ilta-Sanomat, and lead investigator Jussi Luoto said the inquiry was in its early stages.
