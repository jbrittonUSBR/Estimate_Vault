# Putin’s Kaliningrad threat is a bluff — and a nuclear trap

[Source](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/op-eds/4750398/putin-kaliningrad-nuclear-threat-nato/)

(U) WASHINGTON — In an October 2, 2026 op-ed for the Washington Examiner, geopolitical analyst Eric Wargotz argued that Russia's written warning to NATO that any blockade of Kaliningrad could trigger a nuclear response is a bluff but also a trap, because the real danger is miscalculation rather than a deliberate strike. Wargotz wrote that Kaliningrad, wedged between Poland and Lithuania and home to the Baltic Fleet and nuclear-capable Iskander missiles, would be a first target in any NATO-Russia conflict, and that President Vladimir Putin relies on nuclear saber-rattling because his conventional forces cannot fight a unified Western alliance. He cited Western intelligence assessments that Russia's strategic forces had not altered their readiness posture and urged NATO to continue operations such as Baltic Sentry while maintaining military-to-military communication; the Examiner noted the views were his own.
