(U//FOUO) Source posted two images with the caption, "Another university in Tehran got bombed by the Epstein (DECEASED) gang" (Collector Comment: Source's subsequent post identifies the site as Sharif University of Technology in Tehran, Iran). One image showed handwritten notes and a textbook among rubble; the other depicted a partially collapsed building with individuals and construction machinery. The post received 1.66k views, and 129 emoji reactions.

(U//FOUO) Five hours later, Source posted an image and text featuring a map of the Persian Gulf Region. The map displayed circles radiating from Iran and markers for U.S. university branches. The post stated, "In the Epstein gang's war on Iran... three big Iranian universities got airstruck: Iran University of Science and Technology (Mar 27), Shahid Beheshti University (Apr 3), and Sharif University of Technology (Apr 6)." The post noted, "Meanwhile, over the past 20 years, the U.S. has set up a huge network of its own universities right next to Iran".

(U//FOUO) The post asked, "Which U.S. universities are fair game for missile strikes?" and identified 14 educational institutions in the Persian Gulf Region, which included eight U.S. university branches and one Kuwaiti university partnered with a U.S.-based university. The post received 1.62k views and 83 emoji reactions.
