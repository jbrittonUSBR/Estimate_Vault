# FBI Arrests Hanford Nuclear Site Employee Accused of Helping Houthi Terrorists

[Source](https://www.tri-cityherald.com/news/local/crime/article317459693.html)

(U) RICHLAND, Wash. — The Seattle FBI office searched a home in Richland, Washington, on Thursday and arrested Ashton Hamed Ellaboudy, 51, an employee of the Hanford nuclear site, on allegations of helping Houthi terrorists. The arrest of a worker at the Department of Energy's Hanford Site — a former plutonium-production complex undergoing one of the nation's largest environmental cleanup efforts — underscores insider-threat concerns at sensitive nuclear facilities. The case was reported by the Tri-City Herald as a federal investigation involving the FBI's Seattle field office.
