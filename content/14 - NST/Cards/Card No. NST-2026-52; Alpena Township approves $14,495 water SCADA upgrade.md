# Alpena Township approves $14,495 water SCADA upgrade

[Source](https://www.thealpenanews.com/news/local-news/2026/10/alpena-township-approves-14495-water-scada-upgrade/)

(U) ALPENA, United States — The Alpena Township Board of Trustees voted unanimously to approve a $14,495 upgrade of cellular equipment on its water-system control network, one month after tabling the quote. The work, by UIS SCADA Inc. of Dexter, Michigan, covers 15 cellular modems with firewall functions and IP whitelisting for programmable logic controllers at wells and lift stations. Township Clerk Michele Palevich said the FBI contacted the township after learning the quote had been delayed, and the township is also applying for a State and Local Cybersecurity Grant Program review.
