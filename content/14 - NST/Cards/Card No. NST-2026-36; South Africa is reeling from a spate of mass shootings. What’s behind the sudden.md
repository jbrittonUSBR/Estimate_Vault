# South Africa is reeling from a spate of mass shootings. What’s behind the sudden spike?

[Source](https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/02/africa/south-africa-mass-shootings-rise-intl)

(U) DURBAN, South Africa — Five mass shootings in less than a week left at least 46 people dead, CNN reported on October 2, 2026, prompting national soul-searching over a spike in gun crime that threatens to undo recent progress in reducing one of the world's highest murder rates. The killings included an attack on a house party in KwaMakhutha township south of Durban, and experts cited by CNN blamed organized crime and the easy availability of illegal firearms. Julian Rademeyer, an adviser to the Global Initiative Against Transnational Organized Crime, told the outlet that "South Africa is awash with guns."
