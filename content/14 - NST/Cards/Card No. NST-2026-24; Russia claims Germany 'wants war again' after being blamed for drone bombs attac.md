# Russia claims Germany 'wants war again' after being blamed for drone bombs attack on airport

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16109455/Russia-claims-Germany-wants-war-drone-attack-airport.html)

(U) MOSCOW, Russia — Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov said Germany 'wants war again' after Berlin blamed Moscow for an explosive-laden drone found at Leipzig airport on August 4, accusing Chancellor Friedrich Merz of 'declaring war.' The exchange followed a reported attack with homemade explosive rockets on Germany's Jänschwalde power plant and the announcement of a 72-hour ceasefire, with US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner in Kyiv.
