# Experts Warn Of Emerging Security Threats In West Africa

[Source](https://nannews.ng/experts-warn-of-emerging-security-threats-in-west-africa/)

(U) ABUJA, Nigeria — Experts at a pre-West African Peace and Security Dialogue strategy meeting warned that West Africa's security landscape is changing rapidly and requires stronger regional cooperation. Prof. Charles Ukeje of Obafemi Awolowo University called for a more flexible, locally driven security architecture, while Building Blocks for Peace Foundation Executive Director Rafiu Lawal identified disinformation, information manipulation, drug trafficking and illicit economies as emerging threats. ECOWAS and Institute for Peace and Conflict Resolution officials also called for moving from dialogue to concrete action.
