# There have been more than 340 mass shootings so far in 2026

[Source](https://www.kcci.com/article/us-mass-shootings-map-2026/73874634)

(U) Des Moines, USA — A KCCI data analysis of Gun Violence Archive data found that as of Sept. 24, 2026 there had been 343 mass shootings across the United States in which four or more victims were injured or killed, excluding shooters, up from 319 at the same point in 2025. More than 320 people were killed and more than 1,440 injured in the shootings, which spanned 40 states and Washington, D.C., with Texas recording the most at 37. The report said 2026 was on track to be the sixth worst year for mass shootings since 2014, and noted both of the year's deadliest incidents were domestic violence cases.
