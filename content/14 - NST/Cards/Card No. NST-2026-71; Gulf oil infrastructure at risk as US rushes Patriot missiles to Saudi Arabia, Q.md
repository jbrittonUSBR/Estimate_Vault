# Gulf oil infrastructure at risk as US rushes Patriot missiles to Saudi Arabia, Qatar amid Iran tensions

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/defence/international/gulf-oil-infrastructure-at-risk-as-us-rushes-patriot-missiles-to-saudi-arabia-qatar-amid-iran-tensions/articleshow/134630692.cms)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — The U.S. military deployed two additional Patriot missile batteries to Saudi Arabia and Qatar to strengthen protection around key oil and gas facilities amid tensions with Iran, according to Axios, citing two U.S. officials and one regional source. One battery was sent to Saudi Arabia to protect a key oil facility and another to Qatar to protect a natural gas facility, after Saudi and Qatari leaders raised concerns about Iranian retaliation. The deployments came as the Pentagon expanded its regional presence with additional warships, aircraft and troops, and as Patriot interceptor inventories reportedly fell below 1,000.
