# AI Is Making US Mass Shootings Worse, Experts Warn

[Source](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/nation/2026/09/29/ai-us-mass-shootings-making-worse/92009451007/)

(U) WASHINGTON — A USA TODAY analysis reports that artificial intelligence is exacerbating the threat of mass shootings in the United States, citing experts who describe how AI tools can assist would-be attackers in planning, researching targets, and evading detection. The reporting examines the intersection of AI misuse and targeted violence, including how generative tools may lower barriers to planning attacks and complicate law enforcement prevention efforts, and discusses emerging approaches for detecting AI-enabled plotting.
