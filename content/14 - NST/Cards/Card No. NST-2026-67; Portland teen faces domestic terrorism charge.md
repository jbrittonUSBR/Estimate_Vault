# Portland teen faces domestic terrorism charge

[Source](https://www.kptv.com/2026/10/02/portland-teen-faces-domestic-terrorism-charge/)

(U) PORTLAND, United States — A 15-year-old Benson Polytechnic High School student appeared in court facing a first-degree domestic terrorism charge stemming from a Sept. 8 chemical release in the school cafeteria. The Multnomah County District Attorney's Office later announced 12 counts of attempted murder, and prosecutors said at least seven students and staff needed medical care after hydrogen sulfide and hydrogen cyanide were mixed. A judge ordered the teen released to his father to return to a locked treatment program, and a rehearing was scheduled for Oct. 6.
