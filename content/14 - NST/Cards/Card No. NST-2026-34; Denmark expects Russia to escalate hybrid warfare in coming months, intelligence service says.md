# Denmark expects Russia to escalate hybrid warfare in coming months, intelligence service says

[Source](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/other/denmark-expects-russia-to-escalate-hybrid-warfare-in-coming-months-intelligence-service-says/ar-AA2cUTeY?ocid=entnewsntp&pc=U531&cvid=6ab666a623fb40c4b09dae1639f0eac7&cvpid=893c42fb098541d4aa2b8f89d99d4cbc&ei=14)

(U) COPENHAGEN — Denmark’s Defence Intelligence Service assessed in a threat report published September 24 that Russia will intensify its hybrid war against the West and NATO with more frequent attacks “with greater consequences for the countries targeted than in the past,” including destructive cyberattacks crippling societal functions and sabotage with a high risk of casualties, Reuters reported. The service also cited “a low but growing risk” that Russia would launch a limited military attack on one or more NATO countries bordering Russia, such as isolated long-range strikes on Ukraine-supporting infrastructure, false-flag operations using Ukrainian-made drones, or a small troop incursion, intelligence chief Thomas Ahrenkiel said. The Russian embassy in Denmark rejected the assessment as lacking credible evidence, and the Danish Resilience Agency separately raised its cyber threat level; the article is a Reuters report republished by MSN.
