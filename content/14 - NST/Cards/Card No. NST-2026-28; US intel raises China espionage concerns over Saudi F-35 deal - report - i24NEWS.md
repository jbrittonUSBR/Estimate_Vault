# US intel raises China espionage concerns over Saudi F-35 deal - report

[Source](https://www.i24news.tv/en/news/international/americas/artc-us-intel-raises-china-espionage-concerns-over-saudi-f-35-deal-report)

(U) WASHINGTON — U.S. intelligence officials warned that a proposed sale of F-35 fighter jets to Saudi Arabia, valued at roughly $24 billion, could expose sensitive technology to China through espionage or Riyadh's growing military and telecommunications ties with Beijing, according to a New York Times report cited by i24NEWS on September 16, 2026. Analysts raised the possibility that China could acquire F-35 technology through spying inside Saudi Arabia or through its security relationships, and Representative Raja Krishnamoorthi cited the intelligence-community warnings in commenting on the transfer.
