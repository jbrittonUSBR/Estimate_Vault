# AI Agents still retain Access to Business Data and Act on Users Behalf

[Source](https://www.cybersecurity-insiders.com/ai-agents-still-retain-access-to-business-data-and-act-on-users-behalf/)

(U) Unspecified location — Cybersecurity Insiders reported on Delinea's 2026 Identity Security Report: The AI Enforcement Gap, which found that 99.7 percent of IT and security leaders said their organizations had a formal policy governing what data AI agents may access and how those agents are isolated from sensitive information. The study found a gap between those policies and the technical controls actually enforced, meaning AI agents may retain access to company networks, applications and sensitive data after that access is no longer needed for their assigned tasks. The report said organizations need clearly defined permissions, continuous monitoring and least-privilege restrictions for agent identities, and the outlet framed the findings as a shift from creating AI governance policies to actively enforcing them.
