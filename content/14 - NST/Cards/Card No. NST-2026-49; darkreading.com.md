# Warlock Ransomware Gang Expands Attacks on Spanish and Portuguese Targets

[Source](https://www.darkreading.com/cyberattacks-data-breaches/warlock-ransomware-spanish-portuguese)

(U) NEW YORK — Dark Reading reports that the Warlock ransomware operation has expanded its targeting to Spanish- and Portuguese-speaking organizations, conducting attacks and data-theft extortion against victims in Spain and Portugal. The reporting describes the group's tactics, techniques, and procedures and its extortion model, which includes data breaches and ransomware deployment. The expansion adds to the growing threat of ransomware activity against European organizations.
