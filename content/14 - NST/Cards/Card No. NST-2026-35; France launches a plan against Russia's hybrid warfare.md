# France launches a plan against Russia's “hybrid warfare”

[Source](https://www.mundoamerica.com/news/2026/09/25/6ab62e2ffdddff51748b45b9.html)

(U) PARIS — French Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez convened all prefects and announced preparation of a plan to protect the most sensitive industrial and technological defense sites and vital facilities, describing the threat as “real and well-documented, a hybrid warfare led by Russia,” Mundo América reported. Nuñez cited the foiled drone attack at Leipzig/Halle Airport in Germany as the most recent example and said energy, transport and telecommunications operators had reinforced patrols and were in contact with intelligence services; he said France had not yet been overflown by Russian drones but attributed cyberattacks such as a Christmas postal disruption and data breaches to Moscow. The reporting links the move to an August intelligence report warning that Russia had accelerated plans to attack European countries by 2027, and notes President Emmanuel Macron’s push to raise the issue with European leaders; the characterizations are those of French officials and the newspaper.
