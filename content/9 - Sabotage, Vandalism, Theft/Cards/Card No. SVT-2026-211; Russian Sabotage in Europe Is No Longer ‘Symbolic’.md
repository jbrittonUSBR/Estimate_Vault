# Russian Sabotage in Europe Is No Longer ‘Symbolic’

[Source](http://foreignpolicy.com/2026/10/09/russia-sabotage-europe-attacks-hybrid-warfare-estonia/)

(U) TALLINN, Estonia — In an October 9, 2026 Foreign Policy opinion analysis by Sam Skove, Estonia’s counterintelligence chief assessed that Russian sabotage in Europe had shifted from targeting symbolic objects to more disruptive strikes on defense-related and critical targets, including an August attack on Estonian robotics firm Milrem. According to the analysis, Moscow was increasingly using explosives, criminal intermediaries, and unwitting recruits, raising the risk of a mass-casualty event even as Estonia saw no sign of reduced support for Ukraine.
