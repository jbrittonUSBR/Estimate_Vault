# Nord Stream Sabotage Suspect Faces German War Crime Charge

[Source](https://gcaptain.com/nord-stream-sabotage-suspect-faces-german-war-crime-charge/)

(U) Hamburg, Germany — A trial opened against a 50-year-old Ukrainian former army officer accused of helping organize the 2022 Nord Stream pipeline explosions. Prosecutors alleged he acted with authorization from Ukrainian authorities and charged him with a war crime; he had been arrested in Italy and extradited to Germany, while the allegations remained contested.
