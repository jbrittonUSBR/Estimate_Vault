# Hackers heat up refrigerators at hundreds of supermarkets: "Unprecedented"

[Source](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/cyberattack-supermarkets-refrigerators-france-fires-food-losses/)

(U) Paris, France — On October 12, 2026, hackers reportedly tampered with refrigeration systems at hundreds of French supermarkets, raising temperatures in cold storage units and creating risks of food spoilage and potential fire hazards. French officials described the cyberattack as unprecedented, prompting investigations into the perpetrators and vulnerabilities in commercial refrigeration systems.
