# Subsurface check dams to revive drying Ballari aquifers

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/hubballi/subsurface-check-dams-to-revive-drying-ballari-aquifers/articleshow/134523768.cms)

(U) BALLARI — The Ballari district administration in Karnataka, India, planned to build 15 subsurface check dams at a cost of 40–50 crore rupees to revive drying aquifers, The Times of India reported. The first structure was to be built on the Vedavathi River in the Hagari region, with each dam taking about a month and the full program expected to take roughly five months. Zilla panchayat chief executive officer Mohammed Harris Sumeer said the dams would recharge groundwater and support irrigation.
