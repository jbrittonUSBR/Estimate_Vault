# Explosives found meters from the drone that crashed near a Bundeswehr air base—investigators say it matches the GRU's Leipzig attack devices

[Source](https://euromaidanpress.com/2026/09/23/explosives-found-meters-from-the-drone-that-crashed-near-a-bundeswehr-air-base-investigators-say-it-matches-the-grus-leipzig-attack-devices/)

(U) WUNSTORF, Germany — Investigators found what they believe to be explosives meters from a drone that crashed near the Bundeswehr's Wunstorf air base in Lower Saxony, and said the device resembled those used in the GRU-attributed attack at Leipzig airport, Süddeutsche Zeitung, NDR and WDR reported. Germany's federal prosecutor and the Federal Criminal Police Office took over the case; a farmer found the drone about a kilometer from the base on September 14. Wunstorf houses the Bundeswehr's entire A400M transport fleet, and investigators believe the drone may have been near the airfield for up to two years.
