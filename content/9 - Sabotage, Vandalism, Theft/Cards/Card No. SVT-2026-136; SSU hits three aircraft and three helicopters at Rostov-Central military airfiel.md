# SSU hits three aircraft and three helicopters at Rostov-Central military airfield, strikes Yaroslavl Oil Refinery, in joint operation with Defence Forces

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/ukraine/2026/09/ukraine-260917-ukraine-ssu01.htm)

(U) KYIV — Ukraine's Security Service (SSU) struck the Rostov-Central military airfield on the night of September 17, hitting three aircraft and three helicopters and causing fire and secondary detonations. The attack was reported by Ukrainian sources via aggregators like GlobalSecurity.org. The same night, Ukrainian strikes also targeted a refinery in Yaroslavl, as part of ongoing deep strikes against Russian military and energy infrastructure.
