# Police expand operation targeting Melbourne arsons and extortion

[Source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-06/vic-police-expand-arson-extortion-taskforce-phoenix/107353740)

(U) Melbourne, Victoria, Australia — ABC News reported that Victoria Police would expand Operation Phoenix, which targets arsons, extortion, drive-by shootings, and vehicle thefts linked to organized crime. Police said the operation had resulted in 236 arrests since July and cited concern that younger people were being directed to commit crimes; attribution to a particular criminal network remained an investigative assessment.
