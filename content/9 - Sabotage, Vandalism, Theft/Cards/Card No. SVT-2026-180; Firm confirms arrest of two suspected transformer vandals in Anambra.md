# Firm confirms arrest of two suspected transformer vandals in Anambra

[Source](https://punchng.com/firm-confirms-arrest-of-two-suspected-transformer-vandals-in-anambra/)

(U) NIMO, Anambra State — FirstPower Electricity Distribution Company Limited confirmed the arrest of two suspects, Nonso Okolodile and Emeka Onyeugwo, by the Nimo Vigilante Group for allegedly vandalizing a transformer at the Urunkpuke Distribution Substation; tools and vandalized items were recovered, and the suspects were transferred to the police for investigation.
