# Former Ukrainian officer goes on trial

[Source](https://abcnews.com/Business/wireStory/former-ukrainian-officer-trial-sabotage-nord-stream-pipelines-137247790)

(U) HAMBURG, GERMANY — The Associated Press reported that the trial of former Ukrainian army officer Serhii Kuznietsov opened in Hamburg, Germany, over the 2022 Nord Stream pipeline explosions. Prosecutors accused him of organizing and carrying out the underwater bombings, while his lawyers sought dismissal of the charges on the trial’s first day. The case concerned damage to civilian gas infrastructure and was expected to continue into the following year.
