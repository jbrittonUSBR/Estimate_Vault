# Saudi Arabia accuses Houthis of attacking Mecca, warns holy city is a ‘red line’

[Source](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/middle-east/saudi-arabia-mecca-houthis-drone-iran-war-trump-oil-gas-prices-rcna598075)

(U) MECCA, Saudi Arabia — Saudi military spokesman Maj. Gen. Turki al-Malki said the kingdom's air defense forces intercepted a drone south of Mecca shortly before 7 p.m. local time on September 15, 2026, and warned that the security of the holy city and its pilgrims was a "red line." The Houthis denied targeting Mecca, saying their operations had struck Saudi oil facilities and military bases "far removed" from the site and accusing Riyadh of spreading "fabrications and lies." The Saudi claim drew condemnation from across the Middle East, including Pakistani Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, while the United States raised its travel advisory for the kingdom to Level 3 and Brent crude traded at around $108 a barrel. NBC News reported that the United Nations said at least 125,000 people had been displaced in Yemen since the start of September.
