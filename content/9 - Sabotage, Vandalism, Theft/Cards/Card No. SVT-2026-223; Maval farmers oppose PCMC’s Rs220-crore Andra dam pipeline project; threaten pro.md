# Maval farmers oppose PCMC’s Rs220-crore Andra dam pipeline project; threaten protest march

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/pune/maval-farmers-oppose-pcmcs-rs220-crore-andra-dam-pipeline-project-threaten-protest-march/articleshow/134925981.cms)

(U) MAVAL, INDIA — The Times of India reported that farmers from at least 14 villages in Maharashtra’s Maval region opposed Pimpri Chinchwad Municipal Corporation’s proposed ₹220-crore pipeline to draw water from Andra dam. Farmers said the project could reduce water available for agriculture and warned that they would not allow work to proceed until their concerns were addressed. They threatened a protest march against the project.
