# Russian strikes on Ukraine cause power outages in five regions

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/25/russian-strikes-on-ukraine-cause-power-outages-in-five-regions)

(U) KYIV, UKRAINE — Russian attacks across Ukraine killed at least five people, wounded dozens and caused power outages in Dnipropetrovsk, Donetsk, Zaporizhzhia, Odesa and Kharkiv regions, according to Al Jazeera citing Ukrainian local authorities and Reuters. Deputy Energy Minister Artem Nekrasov said the Russian-occupied Zaporizhzhia Nuclear Power Plant lost external power after shelling and switched to diesel generators for the 17th time in 2026, and the Energy Ministry said repeat attacks and air alerts were delaying restoration. Separate strikes killed two people in Odesa, one in Chernihiv, one in Kramatorsk and one in Kharkiv, while a drone attack on Kyiv injured two and a Ukrainian drone attack in Russia's Samara region killed one and caused power cuts.
