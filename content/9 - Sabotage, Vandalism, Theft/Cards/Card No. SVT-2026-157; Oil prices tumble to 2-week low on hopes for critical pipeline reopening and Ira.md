# Oil prices tumble to 2-week low on hopes for critical pipeline reopening and Iran diplomacy

[Source](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/business-news/oil-prices-fall-saudi-pipeline-iran-hormuz-trump-talks-rcna599166)

(U) NEW YORK — Oil prices swung sharply on Sept. 22 as markets weighed President Donald Trump's U.N. General Assembly remarks against signs that Saudi Arabia's east-west pipeline could restart sooner than expected and reports of progress in U.S.-Iran talks. Brent briefly slipped below $98 a barrel before Trump's speech, then fell about 1% after he announced a "very good" three-hour meeting with Iranian representatives. NBC News said it could not confirm reports that Iran offered to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, and Iran's semi-official Fars news agency denied them.
