# Putin hybrid war fears renewed as Germany is hit by ANOTHER power grid sabotage a day after saying Russia was behind airport drone bomb attempt

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16098647/Putin-hybrid-war-fears-Germany-hit-power-grid.html)

(U) BERLIN, Germany — Germany suffered a second suspected sabotage attack on its electricity grid on September 2, 2026, the same day Berlin formally accused Russia of an attempted drone bomb attack at Leipzig/Halle Airport, according to the Daily Mail. The attacks targeted an Amprion substation near Bergheim in western Germany and the Jänschwalde power plant in the eastern state of Brandenburg, where authorities said homemade explosive rockets were used. Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt cited what he described as a "well-established pattern of Russian hybrid operations." The report drew a link between the sabotage incidents and renewed concerns over Russian hybrid warfare against Germany.
