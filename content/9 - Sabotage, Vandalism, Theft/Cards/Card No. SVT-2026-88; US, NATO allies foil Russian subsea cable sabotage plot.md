# US, NATO allies foil Russian subsea cable sabotage plot

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/09/10/world-news/us-nato-allies-foil-russian-subsea-cable-sabotage-plot/)

(U) SVALBARD, Norway — NATO allies caught Russian submarines training to deploy a secret weapon designed to disable critical undersea cables in Arctic waters near the Svalbard archipelago, Reuters reported, citing two Western officials. A joint operation involving Britain, Norway and the United States tracked and confronted the vessels, operated by Moscow's GUGI undersea warfare directorate, preventing them from completing the exercise. Norway's Defense Minister Tore Sandvik said the operation sent "a clear message to Russia that they cannot operate covertly," while Russia did not damage any cables and its Defense Ministry did not respond to a request for comment.
