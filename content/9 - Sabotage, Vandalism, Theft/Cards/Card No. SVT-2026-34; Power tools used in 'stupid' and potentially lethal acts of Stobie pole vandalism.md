(U) Vandals in Adelaide’s north have cut down four Stobie poles using power tools such as angle grinders, exposing high-voltage electrical lines and putting the public at serious risk of electrocution near popular walking and mountain bike tracks. In separate incidents in May and early June 2026, one pole on Harris Road in Concordia had its transformer stripped and brought down, while three poles in Cobbler Creek Recreation Park were toppled, with live wires left dangerously accessible. SA Power Networks described the acts as “shocking, stupid and brazen,” noting that even proximity to the exposed infrastructure could cause fatal electrocution without direct contact, and appealed for public tips via CrimeStoppers, suspecting a single individual or small group due to the close succession of the attacks. Although some copper was taken from one transformer, the poles themselves yielded little, and authorities stressed there was no clear motive beyond possible theft or vandalism. Quick reporting by residents allowed crews to make repairs promptly, but officials warned that such dangerous acts could easily have resulted in serious injury or death, particularly to young people using the recreation areas.
