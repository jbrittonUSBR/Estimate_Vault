# Germany Power Grid Sabotage: Launch Devices Found Near Weisweiler Plant

[Source](https://cedarnews.net/newstasks/970710/germany-power-grid-sabotage-launch-devices-found/)

(U) WEISWEILER, Germany — German police discovered multiple suspected launch devices positioned near a high-voltage power line close to the Weisweiler power plant in western Germany in September 2026, as authorities investigated a spate of suspected sabotage attacks on the electricity grid. The devices were designed to fire conductive material onto overhead lines to trigger short circuits and cut power from coal-fired plants. The discovery came amid a manhunt for a suspect who sent letters claiming responsibility for attacks on substations in three German states; the same individual was subsequently detained near Weisweiler while carrying explosives. Authorities reported finding improvised explosive devices at several sites and described the case as likely linked to "climate extremism."
