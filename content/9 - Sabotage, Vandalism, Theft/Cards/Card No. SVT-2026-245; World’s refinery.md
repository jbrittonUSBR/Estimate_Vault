# World’s refinery: India emerges as a swing supplier of refined fuels

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/toi-blogs/from-toi-print/worlds-refinery/articleshow/135079903.cms)

(U) October 21, 2026 — In an editorial, The Times of India argued that damage to refineries in the Middle East and Russia had tightened global supplies of refined fuels, even as crude flows remained comparatively steady. The editorial said India was positioned to supply the market because of its expanding refining capacity, ability to process varied crude grades, and proximity to West Asia. It cited reported Gulf capacity losses and India’s July exports to support its view of India as a “swing supplier.”
