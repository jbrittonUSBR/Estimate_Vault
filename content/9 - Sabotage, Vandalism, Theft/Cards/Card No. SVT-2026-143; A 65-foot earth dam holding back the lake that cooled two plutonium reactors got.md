# Sinkhole at Savannah River Site dam prompts DOE to siphon 16 billion gallons, raising mercury concerns

[Source](https://www.autonocion.com/us/plutonium-lake-dam-sinkhole-alligators/#google_vignette)

(U) South Carolina, United States — AutoNotion reported that a sinkhole in a 65-foot earth dam at the Savannah River Site prompted the Department of Energy to siphon off roughly 16 billion gallons of water to protect a downstream town, with stirred-up sediment leaving alligators in the lake with mercury levels about four times the WHO food limit.
