# Saudi Arabia intercepts drone, missile as Houthis report attack on Sanaa

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/22/houthis-report-attack-on-sanaa-as-saudi-arabia-intercepts-drone)

(U) Sanaa, Yemen — Yemen News Agency quoted a health ministry spokesman as saying Saudi aircraft struck a fuel station operated by the state-run oil company on 60th Street, wounding six employees and causing material damage; the Saudi-led coalition did not immediately comment on that claim. Coalition spokesman Turki al-Maliki said separately that air defenses destroyed a Houthi drone heading toward Riyadh, after reporting an intercepted ballistic missile aimed at the western Red Sea region, which contains the Yanbu oil terminal and Jazan refinery. Al Jazeera reported that, according to Saudi authorities, Houthi attacks had killed 26 people in the kingdom since September, including 23 in strikes on airports.
