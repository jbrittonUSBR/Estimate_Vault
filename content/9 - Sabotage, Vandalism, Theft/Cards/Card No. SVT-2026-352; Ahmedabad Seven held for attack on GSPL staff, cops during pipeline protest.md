# Ahmedabad: Seven held for attack on GSPL staff, cops during pipeline protest

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/ahmedabad/ahmedabad-seven-held-for-attack-on-gspl-staff-cops-during-pipeline-protest/articleshow/136081934.cms)

(U) Ahmedabad, India — Police said seven people were arrested after villagers protesting gas pipeline work in Sargwala village on 21 November allegedly attacked Gujarat State Petronet Ltd employees with spades, shovels and stones and smashed a company vehicle's windows. Officers said the crowd also pelted police with stones when they intervened, injuring some, and that Koth police registered a case including provisions for attempt to murder, unlawful assembly, rioting and assault on public servants. Police said they were seeking remand of the seven and identifying others allegedly involved.
