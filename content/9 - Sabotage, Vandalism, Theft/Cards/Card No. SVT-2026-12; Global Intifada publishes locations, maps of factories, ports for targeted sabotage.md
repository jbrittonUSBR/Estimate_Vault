(U) A new supply-chain mapping project and campaign is seeking to disrupt and target factories, ports, and vessels ostensibly connected to the State of Israel’s military infrastructure, with the eventual aim of disarming the [IDF](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/defense-news/article-897815). The campaign promotes not only picketing but also sabotage. The Global Intifada’s “Genocide Supply Chain” map supposedly provides the locations and details of sites and routes for arms equipment to Israel, showing a target bank of ports and routes taken by ships carrying the cargo. “They’ve all been mapped – the factories, the ports, the movements. So you can find your way in,” Global Intifada said in a Tuesday Instagram post. “This chain is global. So must be our resistance.” Profiles detail what each site supposedly produces and how it is connected to [Israel](https://www.jpost.com/tags/israel), with a confidence score for the reliability of information. One UK-based F-16 drogue parachute manufacturer was listed at a 95% degree of confidence for a connection to Israel because, supposedly, it was a recipient of the product alongside Greece and Turkey.
