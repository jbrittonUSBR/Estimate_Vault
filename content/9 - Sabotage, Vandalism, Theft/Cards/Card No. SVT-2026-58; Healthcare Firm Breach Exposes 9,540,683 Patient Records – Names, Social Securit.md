# Healthcare Firm Breach Exposes 9,540,683 Patient Records – Names, Social Security Numbers, Medical Data and More at Risk

[Source](https://dailyhodl.com/2026/09/03/healthcare-firm-breach-exposes-9500000-patient-records-names-social-security-numbers-medical-data-and-more-at-risk/)

(U) UNITED STATES — A data breach at healthcare firm Aesto Health exposed the records of 9,540,683 individuals, according to a filing reported on September 3, 2026. The breach, which occurred during an intrusion into the company's Amazon Web Services (AWS) environment between December 2 and December 18, 2025, affected people across 29 organizations, with names, Social Security numbers, and medical data among the information at risk. Aesto Health discovered the incident on December 18, 2025, and confirmed it on May 26, 2026, after which it began notifying affected individuals. The company said it had found no evidence that the data had been misused and was offering two years of credit monitoring through Experian; no threat actor had publicly claimed responsibility.
