# Pipeline bursts leave 3 Canacona areas without water

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/goa/pipeline-bursts-leave-3-canacona-areas-without-water/articleshow/134901599.cms)

(U) Canacona, India — On October 12, 2026, a water pipeline burst left three areas of Canacona, Goa, without water supply. Local authorities were working to repair the damaged pipeline and restore service, with no immediate indication of sabotage reported at the time.
