(U) Data released by the German Federal Ministry of the Interior revealed that the Federal Public Prosecutor’s Office has launched nearly 60 formal investigations into foreign intelligence activity, sabotage, and related offenses over a five-year span.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Scale of Investigations:** Between July 2021 and July 2026, German authorities opened **58 distinct investigations** related to espionage, sabotage, or preparations for sabotage (excluding covert cases still under wraps).
- **Enforcement Outcomes:** These multi-year counterintelligence efforts resulted in **30 arrests**, **19 indictments**, and **13 convictions**.
- **Foreign State Ties:** Out of the total caseload, **53 cases** have verified links to foreign state actors, though the government opted not to publicly disclose the specific countries involved.
- **Classified Information Boundaries:** The German government declined to answer several parliamentary questions regarding the operations—even within classified settings—citing the risk that foreign intelligence services could use the details to reverse-engineer Germany's counterintelligence tactics and capabilities.
