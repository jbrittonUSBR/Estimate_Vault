# ‘Felt by everyone’: Russia’s undersea war games expose nightmare scenario for global internet

[Source](https://www.wnd.com/2026/09/felt-everyone-russias-undersea-war-games-expose-nightmare/)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — A Daily Caller report republished by WND stated that Russian submarines allegedly rehearsed deploying technology designed to disable critical undersea communications cables in the Arctic during spring 2026. The exercises were said to have raised questions about how much of modern life depends on the subsea cable network. The account did not describe any confirmed cable damage and was attributed to reporting on Russian naval activity.
