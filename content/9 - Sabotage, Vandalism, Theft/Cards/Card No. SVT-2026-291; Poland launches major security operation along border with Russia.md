# Poland launches major security operation along border with Russia

[Source](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/poland-russia-border-operation-kaliningrad-sabotage-provocation-nato/)

(U) Kaliningrad border, Poland — CBS News reported that Poland deployed police, counterterrorism units, and military special forces in a security operation after intelligence warnings of possible sabotage or provocation near the Russian exclave. Prime Minister Donald Tusk said suspicious movements were observed and the response may have prevented an incident, while authorities had not released evidence of a specific plot.
