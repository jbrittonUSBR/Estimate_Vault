# Thomson Reuters hit by cyberattack that saw court documents across 11 states accessed by a hacker

[Source](https://www.techradar.com/pro/security/thomson-reuters-hit-by-cyberattack-that-saw-court-documents-across-11-states-accessed-by-a-hacker)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — Thomson Reuters disclosed that a hacker accessed court documents and personal information through a breach of its C-Track court case-management system, according to a report published September 3, 2026. The intrusion was detected on June 30, 2026, after having begun in March 2026, and affected court records in 11 U.S. states — Alabama, Pennsylvania, Kentucky, Montana, Nevada, North Dakota, South Carolina, Tennessee, Ohio, New Hampshire, and Wyoming — as well as the U.S. Virgin Islands and the Canadian province of Ontario. The company reported no operational disruption to court systems and said there was no confirmed identity theft stemming from the incident. No threat actor had publicly claimed responsibility for the breach.
