# Oil: Brent Below $104, Saudi Arabia Moves to Gradually Restore Critical East-West Pipeline

[Source](https://en.protothema.gr/2026/09/17/oil-brent-below-104-saudi-arabia-moves-to-gradually-restore-critical-east-west-pipeline/)

(U) ATHENS — Brent crude fell 1.75% below $104 for a second session after reports that about half of East-West pipeline capacity would resume in days and full capacity in about six weeks. The pipeline carries roughly 18 million barrels per day through the Strait of Hormuz region, making it critical to global supply, according to U.S. Energy Secretary Chris Wright. U.S. crude stocks fell by 640,000 barrels to 423.4 million as markets reacted to the potential restoration of flows.
