(U) A woman from Accomac, Virginia, has pleaded guilty to theft of government property for her role in a scheme that stole more than 60,000 pounds of copper wire and materials from Naval Station Norfolk, causing over $100,000 in damage to the Navy and generating more than $147,000 in illicit cash proceeds. Tyra Giddens admitted to aiding and abetting her husband, Almount Gunter—a Department of Defense civilian contractor—who was arrested in September 2023 while leaving the base with stolen high-voltage cable in a rented truck; Giddens had rented 21 trucks over nine months, maintained multiple recycling accounts, and continued selling the stolen copper even after his arrest. The couple’s operation, which ran from May to October 2023, involved scrapping valuable materials from the naval station’s supply yard for profit, with Giddens turning a blind eye to the illegal source while enjoying the financial benefits. She faces up to 10 years in prison but, under a plea agreement, prosecutors and defense jointly recommend no active jail time, provided she pays full restitution; sentencing is set for September 25 in Norfolk federal court, while her husband’s sentencing is scheduled for July.
