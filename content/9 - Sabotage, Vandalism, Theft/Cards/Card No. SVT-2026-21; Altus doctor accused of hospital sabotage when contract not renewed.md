(U) An Altus doctor, Dr. Rebecca A. Sager, 48, has been charged with felony sabotage and unlawfully concealing hazardous waste after allegedly sabotaging Jackson County Memorial Hospital following the non-renewal of her contract. According to the report, the charges were filed after she learned her contract would not be renewed, and she made her initial court appearance in Jackson County District Court on June 4, 2026. Each count carries a potential penalty of up to 10 years in prison. The article provides limited details on the specific actions alleged in the sabotage.
