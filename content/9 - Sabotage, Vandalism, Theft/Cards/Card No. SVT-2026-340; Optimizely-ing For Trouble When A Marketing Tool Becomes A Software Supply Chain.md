# Optimizely-ing For Trouble: When A Marketing Tool Becomes A Software Supply Chain Attack

[Source](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/optimizely-ing-for-trouble-when-a-marketing-tool-becomes-a-software-supply-chain-attack/)

(U) United States — In this Forrester analyst blog, the authors described how attackers exploited a signup-flow flaw in Optimizely Web Experimentation on November 16 to impersonate administrators and modify customer Project JavaScript without stealing passwords or compromising an Optimizely administrator account. The altered scripts were served through existing integrations and targeted payment-card data and cryptocurrency transactions; Optimizely also reported an attempt to deliver a second-stage exploit against iOS and iPadOS devices. The analysts urged security, risk, marketing and engineering leaders to treat marketing technology as part of the software supply chain, noting that content security policies and hosted payment iframes had limited some execution paths.
