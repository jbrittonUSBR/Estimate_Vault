# UK man charged with assisting Russian military spies in sabotage plot

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/9/uk-man-charged-with-assisting-russian-intelligence-connected-group)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — Joshua Cammidge, a 31-year-old British national, was charged on September 9 with assisting a foreign intelligence service, after allegedly receiving instructions from a suspected representative of the GRU Volunteer Corps, a group controlled by Russia's military intelligence agency, with the intent to commit an act of sabotage. According to the Crown Prosecution Service and London Counter Terrorism Command, Cammidge was arrested on Friday in Swindon, southwest England, and detained under the United Kingdom's National Security Act; he was due to appear at Westminster Magistrates Court on Thursday. The UK designated the GRU Volunteer Corps a national security threat in July for foreign intelligence collection and hostile covert operations on behalf of Russian military spies, with convictions for supporting the group carrying up to 14 years in prison.
