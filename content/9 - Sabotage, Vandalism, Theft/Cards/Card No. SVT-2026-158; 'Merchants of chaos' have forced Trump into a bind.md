# 'Merchants of chaos' have forced Trump into a bind

[Source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-22/yemen-houthis-leave-trump-in-a-bind/107177808)

(U) CANBERRA — In an analysis piece for ABC News, Lucia Stein wrote that the Houthis' September capture of Mokha and Perim Island gave the Iran-aligned group near-complete control of Yemen's Red Sea coastline and left President Donald Trump caught between Saudi requests for military help and his reluctance to intervene. Stein reported that Trump came close to ordering strikes on the Houthis over the weekend before calling them off, and that the Houthis had attacked Saudi oil facilities and the kingdom's east-west pipeline. The article is analysis and includes attributed expert commentary.
