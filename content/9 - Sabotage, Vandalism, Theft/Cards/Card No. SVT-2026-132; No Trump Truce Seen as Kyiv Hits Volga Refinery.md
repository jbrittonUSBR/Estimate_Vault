# No Trump Truce Seen as Kyiv Hits Volga Refinery

[Source](https://www.rigzone.com/news/wire/no_trump_truce_seen_as_kyiv_hits_volga_refinery-16-sep-2026-184625-article/)

(U) KYIV, Ukraine — Ukraine's military said it struck the Syzran refinery in Russia's Volga region overnight into September 15, 2026, along with a drone production facility in Taganrog, a drone preparation and launch site in the Oryol region and targets in the Black Sea. Ukraine's General Staff said a fire broke out at the refinery and that a primary crude processing unit and the tank farm were hit, while NASA satellite data showed a fresh heat anomaly at the site. The strikes underlined that Moscow and Kyiv had not signed up to the energy truce claimed by U.S. President Donald Trump, though both sides welcomed the idea; Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov called a halt to strikes on energy infrastructure a "good idea" without committing to it. The Bloomberg News report, carried by Rigzone, said Ukrainian attacks had cut Russia's oil-refining volumes to the lowest level in more than two decades.
