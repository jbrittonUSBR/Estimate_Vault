# Gosikhurd Dam Fills To 100%, But Totladoh Storage Falls To 45%

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/nagpur/gosikhurd-dam-fills-to-100-but-totladoh-storage-falls-to-45/articleshow/134950240.cms)

(U) MAHARASHTRA, INDIA — The Times of India reported that Gosikhurd dam had reached full storage capacity while the Totladoh reservoir stood at 45%. The article contrasted water levels at the two reservoirs and discussed their implications for local water management and supply. It described reported storage figures, not a sabotage or security incident.
