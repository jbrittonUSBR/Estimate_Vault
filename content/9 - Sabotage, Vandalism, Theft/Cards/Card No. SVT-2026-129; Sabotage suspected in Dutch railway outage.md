# Sabotage suspected in Dutch railway outage

[Source](https://www.wsbradio.com/news/business/suspected-sabotage/YDHFSYLSGI5ORKJJPMNOABJ7B4/)

(U) THE HAGUE, Netherlands — A major outage struck parts of the Dutch railway system on September 15, 2026, and ProRail, the company that maintains the national rail infrastructure, said it was treating the cause as suspected sabotage. ProRail spokesperson Joke van der Cruysen told The Associated Press that material had been found deliberately placed on the tracks at 30 locations, that some sites had more than one disruption, and that workers wore gloves so police could check for fingerprints. Police launched an investigation but gave no immediate comment on suspects or motives, and no claim of responsibility was reported. The stoppages affected thousands of passengers, mainly in the central and eastern Netherlands, on the day the Dutch government presented its annual budget to parliament.
