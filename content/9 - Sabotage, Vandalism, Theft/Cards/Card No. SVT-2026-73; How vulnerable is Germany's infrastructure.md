# How vulnerable is Germany's infrastructure?

(U) BERLIN, Germany — Germany's critical infrastructure — including energy, transport, water, and communications — remains highly vulnerable to sabotage, with authorities warning of increased Russian hybrid activity. Experts cite underfunded security, vast geographic exposure, and limited rapid-response capabilities. Recent incidents have prompted calls for greater investment in protection and improved coordination between federal and state authorities.
