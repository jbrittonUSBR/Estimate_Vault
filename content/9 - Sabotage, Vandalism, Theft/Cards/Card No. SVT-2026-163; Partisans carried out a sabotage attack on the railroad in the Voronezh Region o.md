# Partisans carried out a sabotage attack on the railroad in the Voronezh Region of the Russian Federation

[Source](https://ua.news/en/war-vs-rf/partizani-vlashtuvali-diversiiu-na-zaliznitsi-u-voronezkii-oblasti-rf)

(U) VORONEZH, Russia — The Ukrainian partisan movement ATESH claimed its agents disabled four railway relay cabinets near Voronezh, Novovoronezh, Lisok and Ostrogozhsk in Russia's Voronezh Oblast, disrupting automation on a line it said Russian forces use to move military cargo toward Lyman. ATESH said the damage delayed shipments of ammunition and engineering equipment for several Russian regiments and forced Moscow to deploy repair crews and additional guards. UA.NEWS noted there was no independent confirmation in open sources of the extent of the damage or its effect on military transport; the claims come from the group.
