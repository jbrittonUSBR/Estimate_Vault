# Bayelsa monarch warns youths against pipeline vandalism

[Source](https://punchng.com/bayelsa-monarch-warns-youths-against-pipeline-vandalism/)

(U) Yenagoa, Nigeria — On October 12, 2026, a Bayelsa monarch warned local youths against pipeline vandalism, citing the environmental, economic, and security consequences of sabotage in the Niger Delta. The appeal came amid ongoing concerns over crude oil theft and damage to energy infrastructure in the region.
