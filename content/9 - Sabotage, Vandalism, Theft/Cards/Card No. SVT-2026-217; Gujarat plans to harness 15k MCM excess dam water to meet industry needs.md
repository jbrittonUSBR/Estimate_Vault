# Gujarat plans to harness 15k MCM excess dam water to meet industry needs

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/ahmedabad/gujarat-plans-to-harness-15k-mcm-excess-dam-water-to-meet-industry-needs/articleshow/134901504.cms)

(U) Gandhinagar, India — On October 12, 2026, the Gujarat government announced plans to harness 15,000 million cubic meters of excess water from dams to meet growing industrial demand. The initiative aimed to optimize existing water infrastructure to support industrial needs without relying solely on new sources.
