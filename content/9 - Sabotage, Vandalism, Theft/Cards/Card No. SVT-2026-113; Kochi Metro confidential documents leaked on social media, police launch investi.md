# Kochi Metro confidential documents leaked on social media, police launch investigation

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/india/kochi-metro-confidential-documents-leaked-on-social-media-police-launch-investigation/articleshow/134236971.cms)

(U) KOCHI, India — Kochi Metro Police registered a case and opened an investigation into the alleged theft and circulation of confidential Kochi Metro Rail Ltd (KMRL) documents on social media, officials said on September 14, 2026. The FIR, filed on September 12 following a complaint from KMRL, alleged that unidentified individuals gained unauthorised access to computer systems at the metro's corporate office at Kaloor JLN Metro Station. Leaked documents said to include employee and financial details were circulated through a WhatsApp group, and investigators suspected insider involvement while seeking cyber police assistance to trace those responsible.
