# Poland says fire at Starlink station is sabotage as Denmark warns of rising Russian threat

[Source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmp30r1klk37o)

(U) WOLA KROBOWSKA, Poland — Polish Deputy Prime Minister Krzysztof Gawkowski said a fire at a state-owned Starlink satellite station in Masovian Voivodeship was deliberate sabotage intended to disrupt internet access for institutions including the Ukrainian army, adding that the investigation was ongoing and that indications pointed to a Russian hybrid-attack doctrine. Denmark's intelligence service separately assessed that Russia would escalate hybrid warfare in coming months while judging a limited military attack on NATO states a low but growing risk. The Kremlin did not comment.
