(U) Bojan Pancevski’s book *The Nord Stream Conspiracy* details how a small team of Ukrainian operatives, including a former Soviet-era intelligence officer and a female diver, carried out the September 2022 sabotage of the Nord Stream gas pipelines in the Baltic Sea, destroying three of the four lines in an operation codenamed “Operation Diameter.” Posing initially as a porn film crew before switching to leisure divers, the team planted explosives at a depth of 80 meters off the Danish island of Bornholm, an act that released 350,000 tons of methane and eliminated Russia’s ability to use cheap gas as leverage over Germany following its invasion of Ukraine. The $20 billion pipelines, long controversial in Europe, were targeted by what the book describes as a tiny Ukrainian “deep state” group operating with the approval of then-top general Valerii Zaluzhnyi, though President Zelensky has denied direct involvement. German investigators later arrested the skipper of the yacht used in the operation in Italy and issued a warrant for another suspect in Poland, but political reluctance in Berlin—amid strong support for Ukraine—has limited further pursuit, with some officials viewing the saboteurs more as heroes than criminals. The book, based on interviews with the conspirators, portrays the audacious mission as a successful Ukrainian intelligence operation that caught the world by surprise, having initially been attributed to Russia or Western actors rather than Kyiv.
