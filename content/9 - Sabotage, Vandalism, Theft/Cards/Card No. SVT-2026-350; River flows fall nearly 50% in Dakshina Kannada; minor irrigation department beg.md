# River flows fall nearly 50% in Dakshina Kannada; minor irrigation department begins early water conservation at vented dams after lower rainfall

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/mangaluru/river-flows-fall-nearly-50-in-dakshina-kannada-minor-irrigation-department-begins-early-water-conservation-at-vented-dams-after-lower-rainfall/articleshow/136080073.cms)

(U) Mangaluru, India — Assistant executive engineer Tajuddin of the minor irrigation department said flows in Dakshina Kannada's rivers were about half their level of a year earlier after reduced rainfall, prompting crews to close dam vents sooner than in the previous year. He said closure work would finish by the end of November in most areas and continue until 15 December in parts of Belthangady, Sullia and Kadaba, using wooden block, fibre-reinforced plastic and vertical lift gates. Tajuddin said damage to department structures was negligible because flooding was less intense, while another official said gates were being installed at Harekala, Jakribettu and Biliyur dams.
