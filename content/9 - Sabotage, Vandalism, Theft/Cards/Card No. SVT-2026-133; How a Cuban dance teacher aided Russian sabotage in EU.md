# How a Cuban dance teacher aided Russian sabotage in EU

[Source](https://www.dw.com/en/how-a-cuban-dance-teacher-aided-russian-sabotage-in-eu/video-79265288)

(U) BERLIN, Germany — An investigation by Deutsche Welle and three other European broadcasters traced a series of arson plots and reconnaissance operations in Poland, Czechia, Romania and Lithuania to recruits approached online and linked to a dance studio in Russia. The report said Latin American nationals were lured with promises of money and directed by handlers remotely, and that a Russia-born man living in Russia was accused of coordinating multiple operations. Court documents and interviews indicated that several European countries had seen Latin Americans implicated in Russian-backed sabotage. Deutsche Welle said the text accompanying the video report, first published on September 15, 2026, was updated after the U.S. Justice Department charged Oemis Romagoza Durruthy with participating in a conspiracy to finance terrorism.
