# Russia's secret plan to supercharge kamikaze drone production

[Source](https://www.businessinsider.com/russia-kamikaze-drone-factory-expansion-plans-2026-9#article)

(U) WASHINGTON — Russia plans to expand Rosatom's Alabuga-Volokno carbon fiber plant in Tatarstan to add 500 tons of military-grade aerospace carbon fiber annually by 2029, enough for an estimated 28,000 additional attack drones a year, according to internal company documents obtained by POLITICO and reviewed by Business Insider. The expansion would raise output of Geran-type drones by roughly 78% over Ukraine's estimate of more than 36,000 produced annually. U.S. and Ukrainian officials said they had reviewed the documents and deemed them authentic; Rosatom declined to comment.
