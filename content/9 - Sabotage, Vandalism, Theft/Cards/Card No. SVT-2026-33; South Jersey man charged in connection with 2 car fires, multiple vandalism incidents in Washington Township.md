(U) A 19-year-old Turnersville man, Aiden Logrande, has been charged with two counts of arson and multiple counts of criminal mischief after allegedly setting two vehicles on fire and committing acts of vandalism in Washington Township, New Jersey, on Saturday night. According to police, surveillance footage from one victim’s home captured Logrande smashing the rear window of a parked car on Tara Drive, pouring a flammable liquid inside, and igniting it; while officers responded, a second vehicle fire was reported nearby near Harrogate Court and Tenby Drive. Police located Logrande, who matched the suspect’s description, and recovered a hatchet believed to have been used in the crimes, along with additional charges tied to recent vandalism in the Washington Place and Bunker Hill condominium developments. He faces additional counts including possession of a weapon for an unlawful purpose, and authorities are asking residents with relevant surveillance or home security footage to contact Detective Joseph Russo as the investigation continues.
