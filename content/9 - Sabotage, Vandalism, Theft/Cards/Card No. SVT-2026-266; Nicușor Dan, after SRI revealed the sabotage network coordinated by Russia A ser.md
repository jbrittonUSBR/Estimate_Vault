# Nicușor Dan, after SRI revealed the sabotage network coordinated by Russia: A serious violation of the country’s sovereignty and security | President’s message

[Source](https://www.mediafax.ro/english/nicusor-dan-after-sri-revealed-the-sabotage-network-coordinated-by-russia-a-serious-violation-of-the-countrys-sovereignty-and-security-prime-ministers-message-23832467)

(U) Romania — President Nicușor Dan condemned alleged Russian involvement in sabotage after Romania’s intelligence service said it had uncovered a network linked to a fire at the Carfil industrial platform, Mediafax reported. Romanian authorities said two Belarusian citizens and one Ukrainian citizen were involved; these were official allegations, not independently established findings.
