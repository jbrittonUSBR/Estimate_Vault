# Parasitizing trust: How legitimate infrastructure becomes a delivery pipeline for attackers

[Source](https://blog.talosintelligence.com/parasitizing-trust-how-legitimate-infrastructure-becomes-a-delivery-pipeline-for-attackers/)

(U) October 21, 2026 — Cisco Talos reported observing abuse of more than 25 legitimate platforms over the previous year, describing attackers’ use of trusted notification and mail systems to deliver attacker-controlled content. Its research detailed order-notification campaigns involving Staples and Dell, registration-form reflection, and delivery-status notifications that carried phishing lures. Talos emphasized that sender authentication does not validate message content and recommended validating user-controlled fields and inspecting rendered messages, URLs, attachments, and nested email content.
