# Nord Stream pipeline sabotage may not have been a crime, say accused’s lawyers

[Source](https://www.irishtimes.com/world/europe/2026/10/14/nord-stream-pipeline-sabotage-may-not-have-been-a-crime-say-accuseds-lawyers/)

(U) HAMBURG, GERMANY — The Irish Times reported that lawyers for a suspect accused in the Nord Stream sabotage case argued that the pipeline destruction might not constitute a crime under the circumstances they described. The defense maintained that the act should be considered in the context of Ukraine’s war with Russia and international law. These were arguments advanced by the accused’s lawyers, not a court ruling on criminality or responsibility.
