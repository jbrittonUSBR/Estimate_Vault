# France: Attack ruled out in train derailment

[Source](https://www.dw.com/en/france-attack-ruled-out-in-train-derailment/a-79241365)

(U) ROUEN, France — The public prosecutor of Rouen said there was "no indication of a malicious act carried out with the deliberate intention of derailing the train, and certainly not of an attack," after a train derailed between Rouen and Caen in Normandy, DW reported. At least 44 people were injured when the train, carrying around 180 passengers, went off the rails near the municipalities of Cleon and Tourville-la-Riviere on the evening of Friday, September 11, 2026, with one young woman initially in critical condition. The prosecutor's remarks came after some politicians had suggested the derailment might have resulted from sabotage or other criminal activity, and police reports noted a piece of rail found on the track.
