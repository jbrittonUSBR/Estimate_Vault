# Nord Stream: Germany puts suspected Ukrainian pipeline saboteur on trial

[Source](https://www.dw.com/en/nord-stream-pipeline-sabotage-suspect-on-trial-germany-hamburg/a-79647617)

(U) Hamburg, Germany — On October 14, 2026, a German court began the trial of a Ukrainian national accused of involvement in the September 2022 explosions that damaged three of the four Nord Stream 1 and 2 gas pipelines in the Baltic Sea. The suspect is charged with offenses including attacking civilian energy infrastructure, disruption of public services, causing an explosion, and destruction of structures. The case has drawn political scrutiny in Germany over potential Ukrainian involvement amid continued support for Kyiv.
