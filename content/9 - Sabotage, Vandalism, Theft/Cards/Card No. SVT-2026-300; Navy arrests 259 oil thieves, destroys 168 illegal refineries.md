# Navy arrests 259 oil thieves, destroys 168 illegal refineries

[Source](https://punchng.com/navy-arrests-259-oil-thieves-destroys-168-illegal-refineries/)

(U) Nigeria — The Punch reported that the Nigerian Navy’s annual scorecard recorded 259 arrests, 168 illegal refining sites deactivated, 363 pits destroyed, and more than 7.48 million litres of stolen crude and illegally refined products recovered from November 2025 to November 2026. The Navy also reported four vessels impounded and nine convictions for crude-oil theft or pipeline vandalism; the figures were attributed to the service.
