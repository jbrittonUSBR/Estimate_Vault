# Five mild-intensity earthquakes recorded around Koyna dam in Satara district

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/kolhapur/five-mild-intensity-earthquakes-recorded-around-koyna-dam-in-satara-district/articleshow/134418332.cms)

(U) Satara, India — The National Center for Seismology recorded five mild-intensity earthquakes around the Koyna dam in Maharashtra's Satara district on September 22, 2026. The tremors, ranging from magnitude 2.5 to 3.4, occurred within a few hours of each other, though no damage or casualties were reported. The Koyna region is known for reservoir-induced seismicity due to the dam.
