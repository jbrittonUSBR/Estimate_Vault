(U) President Trump is facing mounting pressure to provide evidence for his claims that the recently renovated Lincoln Memorial reflecting pool was deliberately sabotaged with a large “350-foot gash,” after the $14.7 million project—completed in early June 2026 with a dark blue lining for America’s 250th anniversary celebrations—quickly developed algae blooms, peeling coating, and reports of dead birds. Trump posted on Truth Social accusing “vandals” of cutting and pulling apart the surface, claimed six arrests had been made, and vowed harsh punishment, while the Interior Department cited increased vandalism by “leftist activists” and deployed extra security including fencing and National Guard patrols. However, no concrete evidence of intentional damage has been released, and reports indicate the issues stem from maintenance and environmental factors, such as the dark lining promoting algae growth and hydrogen peroxide treatments potentially affecting the coating. Critics, including Senator Richard Blumenthal, have highlighted the no-bid contract awarded to a company with ties to Trump’s properties, calling the project a case of corruption and incompetence, while environmental groups are demanding investigations into the bird deaths. The pool is scheduled to be drained for repairs under warranty, as the administration struggles to substantiate claims of sabotage amid questions over transparency and project management.
