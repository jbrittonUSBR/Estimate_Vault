# Russian Strikes on Ukraine Border Checkpoints Disrupt Rerouted Arms Supply Lines

[Source](https://www.naturalnews.com/2026-09-12-russian-strikes-ukraine-border-checkpoints-disrupt-arms-supply.html)

(U) KRAKOW, Poland — Russian drone strikes targeted the Starokozachye border crossing between Ukraine and Moldova, an operation that analysts said would impede the flow of military supplies and commercial exports, Natural News reported, citing Sputnik International and other sources. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky confirmed two people were killed in the overnight attack on the crossing, located about 500 kilometers south of Kyiv, and that the facility was closed. The report, which carried a pro-Russian analytical framing sourced to Sputnik, said the strikes followed a recent Russian attack on the Orlovka-Isaccea crossing with Romania as Moscow sought to cut overland routes carrying Western aid.
