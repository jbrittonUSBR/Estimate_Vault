# Pentagon Treats Military Base Freezer Malfunctions as Suspected Cyberattack

[Source](https://www.naturalnews.com/2026-11-03-pentagon-military-base-freezer-malfunctions-suspected-cyberattack.html)

(U) United States — Natural News summarized Reuters reporting that the Pentagon was investigating freezer failures at at least six military commissaries as suspected cyberattacks after food spoiled. Officials had not publicly confirmed hacking, determined whether the incidents were linked, or identified a cause or perpetrator; the investigation was ongoing.
