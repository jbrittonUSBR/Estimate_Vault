(U) Cook County authorities recovered two stolen trailers worth more than $1.3 million in cargo at a truck yard in Elk Grove Township near Chicago, one containing approximately $300,000 in copper wire stolen from Pine Hill, Alabama, and the other holding about $1 million in data center equipment stolen from Jacksonville, Florida. The trailers, both delivered by the same individual, were located after investigators received a tip about the first on June 18 and the truck yard owner reported the second, which had been dropped off the previous week; the copper-wire trailer was also found with stolen Indiana license plates. The Cook County Sheriff’s Police Organized Retail Crime Unit led the recovery, though no arrests have been announced and the investigation into the thefts and the driver remains ongoing. Authorities have not released details on how the trailers were stolen, the cargo owners, intended destinations, or whether the incidents are directly connected beyond sharing the same delivery location and person.
