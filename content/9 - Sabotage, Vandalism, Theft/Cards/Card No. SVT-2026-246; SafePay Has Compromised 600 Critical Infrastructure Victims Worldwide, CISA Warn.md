# SafePay Has Compromised 600 Critical Infrastructure Victims Worldwide, CISA Warns

[Source](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/safepay-compromised-critical/)

(U) October 21, 2026 — Infosecurity Magazine reported that a joint advisory from U.S., German, and U.K. agencies said the SafePay ransomware group had affected at least 600 critical-infrastructure organizations worldwide since emerging in 2024. The advisory described reported initial-access methods, lateral movement, data theft, encryption, and double-extortion tactics, and recommended controls including multifactor authentication, patching, network segmentation, and tested offline backups. The victim count and threat details were attributed to the government advisory, not independently verified by the article.
