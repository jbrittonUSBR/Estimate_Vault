# Russia's hybrid attacks on Germany: When is it 'war'?

[Source](https://www.dw.com/en/russias-hybrid-attacks-on-germany-when-is-it-war/a-78784262)

(U) Berlin, Germany — In an analysis published September 2, 2026, DW reported on Germany's response to what it described as a pattern of Russian hybrid attacks, in the wake of Berlin's accusation that Moscow was behind an attempted explosive drone attack at Leipzig/Halle Airport on August 4. Germany's interior minister, Alexander Dobrindt, attributed the incident to Russia, and the government responded by closing the Russian House in Berlin and the Russian Consulate in Bonn, tightening entry rules, and taking action against Russia's shadow fleet. Critics, including the Greens' Sara Nanni and the CDU's Roderich Kiesewetter, characterized the response as too timid. Russia denied involvement, and President Vladimir Putin accused Germany of fabricating evidence; the piece is an analytical opinion article rather than a straight news report.
