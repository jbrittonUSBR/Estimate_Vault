(U) Sarasota County officials are warning residents about ongoing thefts of underground copper wire from roadway lighting systems, which have left multiple streets dark and created safety hazards for drivers, pedestrians, and cyclists. In recent incidents, thieves stole approximately 9,000 feet of wire from 17th Street, costing about $45,500 to repair, and around 7,000 feet from Bahia Vista Street, with repairs totaling about $9,000. The county is responding by replacing copper wiring with non-copper alternatives and coordinating with law enforcement to address the thefts, which are believed to be driven by the value of the metal. Residents are encouraged to report any suspicious activity near electrical infrastructure—such as unmarked vehicles at night—to 911 or to the non-emergency line at 941-316-1201 if damage is found. No suspects have been identified or arrested in connection with the recent thefts, and officials are emphasizing public vigilance to help prevent further incidents and protect community safety.
