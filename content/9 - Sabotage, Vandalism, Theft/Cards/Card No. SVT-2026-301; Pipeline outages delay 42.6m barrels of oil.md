# Pipeline outages delay 42.6m barrels of oil

[Source](https://punchng.com/pipeline-outages-delay-42-6m-barrels-of-oil/)

(U) Nigeria — The Punch reported that pipeline failures, terminal disruptions, equipment breakdowns, and maintenance delayed an estimated 42.55 million barrels of crude and condensate production in 2025, according to the upstream regulator. The report valued the deferred output at about $2.97 billion but stressed that this was an indicative gross value, not confirmed lost revenue, and that some production could be recovered later.
