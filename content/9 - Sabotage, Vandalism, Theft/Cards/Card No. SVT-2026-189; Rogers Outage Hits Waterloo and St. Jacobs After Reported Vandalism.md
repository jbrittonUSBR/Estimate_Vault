# Rogers Outage Hits Waterloo and St. Jacobs After Reported Vandalism

[Source](https://www.iphoneincanada.ca/2026/09/28/rogers-outage-hits-waterloo-and-st-jacobs-after-reported-vandalism/)

(U) WATERLOO, Ontario, Canada — On September 28, 2026, iPhone in Canada reported that a Rogers fiber line was cut just before midnight between Friday, September 25 and Saturday, September 26, 2026, causing an outage that affected television, internet, and home phone service in Waterloo and St. Jacobs. According to the report, service was restored by mid-afternoon on Saturday following reported vandalism to the fiber line.
