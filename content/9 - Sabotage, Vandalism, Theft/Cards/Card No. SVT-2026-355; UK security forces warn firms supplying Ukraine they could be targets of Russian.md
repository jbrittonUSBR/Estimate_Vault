# UK security forces warn firms supplying Ukraine they could be targets of Russian sabotage following spate of hacks across Europe

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16317783/british-security-warning-business-supplying-ukraine-sabotage.html)

(U) LONDON — MI5 and the Ministry of Defence warned British firms supplying Ukraine that they could be targets of Russian sabotage and should "act now," according to the Daily Mail, which framed the alert as following a spate of hacks across Europe. The joint notice urged companies to reduce opportunities for hostile actors to identify their organisations and personnel as targets, conduct hostile reconnaissance and attack people, sites or operations. The warning said Russia had stepped up sabotage attempts against European defence-related facilities.
