# Romania says Russian-linked sabotage plot foiled

[Source](https://united24media.com/world/russian-sabotage-plot-targeting-nato-sites-fails-after-romania-tracks-suspect-for-months-22353)

(U) BUCHAREST, Romania — Romania's intelligence service reported on September 8, 2026 that it had prevented a sabotage operation coordinated by Russia involving a Russian citizen who photographed military sites used by NATO allies and a Ukrainian Antonov cargo aircraft on Romanian territory. According to the Romanian Intelligence Service (SRI), the man had been under surveillance since February 2026 as part of an investigation into attempted acts of diversion, and an intermediary instructed him to film strategic military targets. The SRI said the tactics matched those of sabotage networks coordinated by Russian entities across European states and that the investigation indicated Russia continued to recruit people from vulnerable categories for such activities.
