(U) The University of Illinois Police Department (UIPD) released a statement following an investigation into criminal damage directed at the university's automated license plate reader (Flock) technology.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Incidents:** UIPD officers investigated multiple acts of criminal damage targeting state-supported property that occurred on **July 31 and August 10**.
- **Suspect Identification:** As a result of the ongoing investigation, detectives successfully identified a suspect in connection with the vandalism: **Mitchell Bryant**.
- **Targeting Surveillance Tech:** The investigation focused on intentional damage done to the campus's public safety infrastructure, specifically its automated camera and license plate reader networks.
