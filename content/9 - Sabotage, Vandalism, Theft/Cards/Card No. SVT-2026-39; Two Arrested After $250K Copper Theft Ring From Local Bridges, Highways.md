(U) A 10-month multi-agency investigation led by the Pensacola Police Department and Florida Highway Patrol has resulted in the arrests of two individuals tied to a copper theft ring that stole approximately $250,000 worth of wiring—including an estimated 100,000 feet—from light poles on bridges and interstate highways in the area. David Nielsen, 37, and Rae Ann Horne, 31, were taken into custody after authorities tracked their operation, which involved stripping copper from structures like the Barrancas bridge over Bayou Chico, leaving roads dark for months and posing safety risks to motorists. Nielsen, who owned the boat used in the thefts, was arrested after jumping into the water during the raid and faces first-degree felony charges for theft of copper from utility poles and criminal mischief, while Horne was charged with dealing in stolen property for allegedly selling the copper at local scrap yards. The investigation, supported by the Escambia County Sheriff’s Office, Florida Department of Law Enforcement, and Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission, remains active with additional arrests expected, highlighting the significant damage caused by organized copper theft to critical public infrastructure.
