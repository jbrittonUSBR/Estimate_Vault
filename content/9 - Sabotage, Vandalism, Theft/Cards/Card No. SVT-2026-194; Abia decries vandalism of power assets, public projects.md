# Abia decries vandalism of power assets, public projects

[Source](https://punchng.com/abia-decries-vandalism-of-power-assets-public-projects/)

(U) UMUAHIA, Nigeria — Abia State Commissioner for Information Prince Okey Kanu said vandalism of power infrastructure and public projects was disrupting electricity supply and driving up replacement costs, at a press briefing reported September 29, 2026. Kanu urged town union presidents-general, traditional rulers and security agencies to help protect public assets, arguing that community ownership was essential to deterring attacks. Officials also provided updates on drainage and flood-control work and on a state water scheme. The briefing was a government statement and its figures were not independently verified.
