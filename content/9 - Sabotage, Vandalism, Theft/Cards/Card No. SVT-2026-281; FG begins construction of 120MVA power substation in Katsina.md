# FG begins construction of 120MVA power substation in Katsina

[Source](https://punchng.com/fg-begins-construction-of-120mva-power-substation-in-katsina/)

(U) Mashi, Katsina, Nigeria — The Punch reported that the Transmission Company of Nigeria began building a 132/33 kV, 120 MVA substation, with completion scheduled for March 2027. The power minister urged residents to protect the site from vandalism.
