# Inside the old gas pipeline where Ukraine is fighting an underground war

[Source](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c32l507ev4v4o)

(U) Kharkiv region, Ukraine — Ukrainian National Guard troops said they spent weeks moving through a decommissioned gas pipeline to emerge behind Russian positions near Kupiansk, according to BBC reporting summarized by Slashdot. Newser reported that Ukrainian officials claimed the operation disrupted Russian positions, while CBS said it could not independently verify Ukraine’s account; Russian officials acknowledged a Ukrainian raid.
