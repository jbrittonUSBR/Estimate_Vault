# French blood banks hit by cyberattack days after European freezer hacks

[Source](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/cyberattack-france-blood-bank/)

(U) France — CBS News, citing the Associated Press, reported that a cyberattack altered temperature and defrost settings at three French Blood Establishment storage sites. Monitoring systems alerted staff, who moved or secured plasma products before damage occurred; the blood service said no products were lost and operations continued, while investigators had not identified a perpetrator.
