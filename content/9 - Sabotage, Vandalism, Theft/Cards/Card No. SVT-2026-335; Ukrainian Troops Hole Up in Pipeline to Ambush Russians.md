[Source](https://www.newser.com/story/400190/ukrainian-troops-hole-up-in-pipeline-to-ambush-russians.html)

(U) Near Kupiansk, Kharkiv region, Ukraine — Ukrainian National Guard members said they moved for miles through a decommissioned Soviet-era gas pipeline and emerged behind Russian positions, Newser reported. The unit described weeks underground and close combat; Newser noted that CBS could not independently verify the operational claims, although Russian officials acknowledged a Ukrainian raid.
