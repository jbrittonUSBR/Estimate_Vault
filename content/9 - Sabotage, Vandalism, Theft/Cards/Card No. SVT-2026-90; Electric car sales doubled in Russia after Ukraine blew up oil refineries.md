# Electric car sales doubled in Russia after Ukraine blew up oil refineries

[Source](http://electrek.co/2026/09/10/electric-car-sales-doubled-in-russia-after-ukraine-blew-up-oil-refineries/)

(U) MOSCOW, Russia — Electric vehicle sales in Russia more than doubled during the summer as motorists faced fuel shortages attributed to Ukrainian strikes on oil refineries, Electrek reported, citing Reuters. From June to August 2026, 26,543 battery-electric and plug-in hybrid vehicles were sold in Russia, up 118% from 12,187 a year earlier, as the country's refining capacity reportedly fell to 70% of domestic usage and a gasoline export ban was imposed. The article is a commentary piece that characterises the development approvingly and notes that combined BEV and PHEV market share remained around 6%.
