# Mexican authorities uncover illicit crypto mining operation linked to cartels

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/mexico-cartel-crypto-mining-bust-puebla/)

(U) PUEBLA, Mexico — Mexican authorities raided a clandestine cryptocurrency mining facility hidden in the Sierra Norte mountains of Puebla between September 6 and 8, 2026, seizing roughly 300 GPUs along with electrical transformers and satellite antennas, Crypto Briefing reported. The site allegedly siphoned power from federal infrastructure near the Nuevo Necaxa hydroelectric dam, a practice locally known as "huachicoleo." Officials said the raid was Puebla's fourth bust of an illegal mining operation since early 2025 and that investigators were examining possible links to organized crime and money laundering.
