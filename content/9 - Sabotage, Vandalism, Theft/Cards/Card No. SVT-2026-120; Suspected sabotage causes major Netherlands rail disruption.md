# Suspected sabotage causes major Netherlands rail disruption

[Source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c8ly49w9g1edo)

(U) AMSTERDAM — Pipes and cables were found at more than 35 incidents on the Dutch rail network, including in the centre and north of the country and around Amsterdam, the BBC reported. The objects caused track circuits to register phantom trains, forcing signal failures and cancellations. Prime Minister Rob Jetten called the disruption "disruptive and life-threatening," and a train struck a piece of metal near Steenwijk on Monday. The prosecutor's office and the AIVD domestic intelligence service were involved in the investigation, and the report noted that hay bales had been burned amid protests by the Farmers Defense Force, although no link had been established.
