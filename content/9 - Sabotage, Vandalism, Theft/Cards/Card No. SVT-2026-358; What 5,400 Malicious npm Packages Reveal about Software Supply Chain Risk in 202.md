# What 5,400 Malicious npm Packages Reveal about Software Supply Chain Risk in 2026

[Source](https://www.databricks.com/blog/what-5400-malicious-npm-packages-reveal-about-software-supply-chain-risk-2026)

(U) SAN FRANCISCO — Databricks Threat Research reported on November 23 that it had analyzed about 5,400 confirmed malicious npm packages published between December 2025 and October 2026, the first year after npm revoked long-lived authentication tokens. The report found that attacker-created packages made up 89% of the dataset but only 0.2% of recorded downloads, while compromised legitimate packages accounted for 7% of packages but more than 99% of downloads. Databricks said the concentration of downloads in hijacked legitimate packages showed that supply-chain risk was driven less by suspicious-looking packages than by compromised trusted maintainers.
