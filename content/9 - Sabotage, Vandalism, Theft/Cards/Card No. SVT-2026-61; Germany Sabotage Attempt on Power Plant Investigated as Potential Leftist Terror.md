# Germany: Sabotage Attempt on Power Plant Investigated as Potential Leftist Terrorism

[Source](https://www.breitbart.com/europe/2026/09/02/germany-sabotage-attempt-on-power-plant-investigated-as-potential-leftist-terrorism/)

(U) BERLIN, Germany — Two incidents on September 1–2, 2026, at German power infrastructure are being investigated as possible sabotage, with authorities examining both foreign involvement and left-wing extremism, according to reporting by Breitbart News, a partisan outlet that attributed the account in part to the Associated Press. Explosive devices were discovered at a substation near the Jänschwalde coal plant in the eastern state of Brandenburg, while a short circuit occurred at a substation in Bergheim near Cologne in the west. The incidents took a combined 4,200 megawatts of capacity offline, including RWE lignite-fired units. The report noted the incidents followed Germany's accusation that Russia was responsible for an August 4 drone incident at Leipzig/Halle Airport.
