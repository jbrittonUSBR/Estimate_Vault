# Chinese research vessels loiter near Pacific undersea cables more than any other nation, report finds

[Source](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/chinese-research-vessels-loiter-near-pacific-undersea-cables-more-than-any-other-nation-report-finds-civilian-fleet-using-lawnmower-patterns-may-be-mapping-seafloor-for-future-sabotage)

(U) Pacific Ocean — Tom’s Hardware reported that a study found Chinese research vessels spent more time near Pacific undersea cables than vessels from other countries. The article said some ship movements followed survey-like patterns and raised the possibility of seabed mapping; the suggestion of future sabotage was an inference, not evidence that cables had been damaged or an attack planned.
