# Finland runs Europe's biggest civil defense exercise since Cold War...

[Source](https://apnews.com/article/finland-russia-civil-defense-cyberattack-sabotage-3ea6f75d826669c6be138a0496934b5b)

(U) KUOPIO, Finland — On September 2, 2026, Finland held what the Associated Press described as Europe's largest civil defense exercise since the Cold War, with more than 2,000 volunteers taking part in the "Shelter 2026" drill in the eastern city of Kuopio. The exercise centered on a civilian shelter hewn into solid rock and designed to withstand nuclear, biological, and other attacks. Finland maintains shelter space for approximately 4.8 million people, about 87 percent of its 5.5 million population. The drill reflected Finland's heightened vigilance toward neighboring Russia, with which it shares a long border and a recent history of tensions following its NATO accession.
