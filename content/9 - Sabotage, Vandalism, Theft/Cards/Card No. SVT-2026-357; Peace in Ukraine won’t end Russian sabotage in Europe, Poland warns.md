# Peace in Ukraine won’t end Russian sabotage in Europe, Poland warns

[Source](https://www.politico.eu/article/ukraine-peace-wont-end-russian-sabotage-in-europe-poland-warns/)

(U) WARSAW, POLAND — Tomasz Siemoniak, Poland's minister coordinating intelligence services, told Politico on November 23 that Russian covert operations in Europe had intensified and would likely continue even if a peace deal ended the war in Ukraine. He said operatives had moved beyond warehouse and shopping-centre fires to target railways, communications equipment and defence facilities, in some cases to disrupt supplies to Kyiv, and said the campaign aimed to weaken European support for Ukraine and NATO. Siemoniak said Moscow sought to link peace to a "new security architecture" in Europe and wanted NATO to roll back its forces to 1997 levels; Russia had not responded to requests for comment and had previously denied responsibility for attacks attributed to it.
