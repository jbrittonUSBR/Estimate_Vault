# Russia’s grey-zone attacks on Europe are growing more brazen

[Source](https://www.economist.com/europe/2026/09/21/russias-grey-zone-attacks-on-europe-are-growing-more-brazen)

(U) LONDON — The Economist reported that Russia's campaign of sabotage and hybrid attacks against Europe had escalated sharply, citing President Emmanuel Macron's Sept. 18 warning to French party leaders that "no one is safe" and Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk's intelligence warning that Moscow could hit NATO states with drones or missiles and call them accidents. The magazine said the operations aim to coerce Europe into halting support for Ukraine, raise the cost of that support and make NATO look powerless, and often use local proxies or criminal gangs for deniability. The article is analysis and cites unnamed analysts and security officials.
