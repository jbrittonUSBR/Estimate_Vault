# Russian Spending on Refinery Subsidies Hits Highest since 2022

[Source](https://www.rigzone.com/news/wire/russian_spending_on_refinery_subsidies_hits_highest_since_2022-06-nov-2026-185024-article/)

(U) Moscow, Russia — Bloomberg, republished by Rigzone, reported that Russian fuel subsidies to oil producers reached 415 billion rubles in October, the highest monthly amount since April 2022. The payments support domestic fuel supply and were rising as refineries repaired damage from Ukrainian drone strikes; the article said October subsidies equaled about half of net budget oil revenue.
