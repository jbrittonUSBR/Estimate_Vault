# Cuban dance teacher ordered EU sabotage attacks from Russia

[Source](https://www.dw.com/en/cuban-dance-teacher-ordered-eu-sabotage-attacks-from-russia/a-79225946)

(U) BERLIN, Germany — An investigation by DW and three other European broadcasters found that a Cuban man who teaches Latin dance in northern Russia coordinated arson attacks in at least four EU countries in 2024 on behalf of Russian intelligence services. Operating under the aliases "Adrian" and "Dios," he posted job advertisements on Telegram promising $1,500 plus a bonus for each completed operation to Spanish-speaking recruits, including former soldiers, for monitoring, intelligence, logistics and tactical work. Reporters reconstructed the network from court documents, official statements, social media posts and leaked data, and found that not all of those recruited completed their assignments.
