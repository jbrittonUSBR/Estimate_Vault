# Britain Tells Households to Stockpile Three Days of Supplies Over Cyber Attack Fears as Australia Watches

[Source](https://www.ibtimes.com.au/australia-emergency-preparedness-britain-ready-campaign-1879180)

(U) Sydney, Australia — In an IBTimes analysis, the UK Cabinet Office's three-year Be Ready campaign, launched 22 October for England and Wales, urged households to keep water, non-perishable food, cash and documents on hand against severe weather and cyber disruption. Worldpanel by Numerator reported that in the ten days after launch, sales of bottled water rose 67%, tinned and jarred food 37% and hand sanitiser 83%; Defence Secretary Wes Streeting defended the campaign after an accusation of scaremongering. The analysis noted that Australian guidance varies by state, with New South Wales recommending a 14-day supply, and cited the Australian Signals Directorate's report of more than 1,100 cyber incidents in 2023-24, over 11% involving critical infrastructure.
