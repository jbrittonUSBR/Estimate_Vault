# Angry Dutch farmers blamed for railway sabotage: What happened?

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/16/angry-farmers-blamed-for-dutch-railways-sabotage-what-happened)

(U) AMSTERDAM — On September 15, saboteurs placed pipes and cables on Dutch railway tracks at more than 20 locations, causing at least 35 signal failures and major disruptions. ProRail called the act sabotage with no injuries reported. While the Farmers Defence Force denied responsibility and condemned the actions, it did not rule out that individual farmers were involved, coming amid ongoing protests around Prinsjesdag.
