# Saudi Arabia Plans Massive Expansion of Oil Pipeline to Bypass Hormuz

[Source](https://oilprice.com/Latest-Energy-News/World-News/Saudi-Arabia-Plans-Massive-Expansion-of-Oil-Pipeline-to-Bypass-Hormuz.html)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — On October 13, 2026, Saudi Arabia announced plans to massively expand its East-West oil pipeline system to increase capacity for bypassing the Strait of Hormuz. The move reflected ongoing concerns over regional security risks to maritime chokepoints and aimed to bolster the Kingdom's export flexibility in the event of disruption.
