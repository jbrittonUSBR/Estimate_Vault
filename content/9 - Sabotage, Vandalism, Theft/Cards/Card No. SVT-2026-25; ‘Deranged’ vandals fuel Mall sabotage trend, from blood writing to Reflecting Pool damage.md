(U) A surge of vandalism targeting the National Mall, including extensive damage to the newly renovated Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool and blood-written messages on the Washington Monument, has prompted federal officials to accelerate security measures and investigate what they describe as politically motivated "sabotage" by "deranged" activists. The Reflecting Pool, which reopened after a $14.7 million restoration ordered by President Trump featuring a dark blue lining for America’s 250th anniversary, suffered over 300 feet of razor-blade-style slashes to its liner, algae blooms, and peeling coating shortly after refilling, with Trump publicly claiming deliberate cuts and sharing before-and-after images on Truth Social while vowing harsh punishment for vandals. Authorities reported five arrests and multiple citations related to the pool damage, including former Olympic canoeist David Hearn, along with 14 police reports, and linked other incidents—such as "8647" etched into the grass and blood writing on the Monument—to anti-Trump protests, with fencing and extra patrols deployed early to prevent copycats. Interior Department officials noted a significant increase in such vandalism this summer, while critics and environmental groups raised separate concerns about project management and bird deaths, as the administration frames the attacks as part of a broader trend of leftist sabotage against national landmarks.
