# Sabotage suspected as parts of Dutch rail network come to a standstill

[Source](https://www.politico.eu/article/sabotage-suspected-as-parts-of-dutch-rail-network-come-to-a-standstill/)

(U) AMSTERDAM — Dutch rail authorities found pipes attached to tracks and other unauthorized materials at more than 20 locations, causing signal failures and halting trains across the country, Politico reported. The national police force took the lead in the investigation, and Secretary of State Annet Bertram called the situation "particularly worrying." Dutch media reporting suggested that activist farmers might be responsible. The Farmers Defense Force denied involvement but said it could not rule out that individual farmers had acted.
