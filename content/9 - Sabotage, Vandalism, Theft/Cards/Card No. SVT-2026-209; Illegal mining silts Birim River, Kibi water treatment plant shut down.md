# Illegal mining silts Birim River, Kibi water treatment plant shut down

[Source](https://thechronicle.com.gh/illegal-mining-silts-birim-river-kibi-water-treatment-plant-shut-down/)

(U) KIBI, Ghana — Illegal mining activities heavily silted the Birim River, forcing the shutdown of the Kibi water treatment plant and leaving residents without reliable access to treated water. Authorities raised concerns about the long-term environmental and public health impacts of unregulated galamsey operations.
