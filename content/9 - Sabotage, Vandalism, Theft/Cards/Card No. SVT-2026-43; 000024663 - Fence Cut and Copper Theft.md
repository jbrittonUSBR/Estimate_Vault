(TLP: AMBER+STRICT) An E-ISAC member reported that on February 17, 2026, an employee arriving at a rural storage yard discovered a cut in the chain‑link fence. A 500‑lb spool of 2/0–7W bare copper wire located just inside the breach was missing. Drag and roll marks in the gravel indicated the spool was moved through the fence cut. Due to the infrequency of staff going to this location, the exact time of the incident cannot be determined. A police report was filed, and law enforcement will review nearby traffic cameras. This site has no security cameras. Incident Type: Theft; Copper, Incident State/Province: Washington, NERC Region: WECC, Asset Affected: Laydown Yard; Fence/Gate Damage Only, Geographical Location: Rural, Result in customer outages?: No.
