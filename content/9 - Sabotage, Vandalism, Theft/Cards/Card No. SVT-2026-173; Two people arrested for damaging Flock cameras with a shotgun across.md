# Two people arrested for damaging Flock cameras with a shotgun across Kentucky

[Source](https://www.kentucky.com/news/local/crime/article317352448.html)

(U) LAWRENCEBURG, Ky. — Two suspects were arrested after allegedly shooting and destroying Flock cameras with a shotgun across Anderson, Franklin and Woodford counties, the Lawrenceburg Police Department said. Four Lawrenceburg cameras had been destroyed or removed, and a witness reported seeing a woman shoot a camera along Kentucky Route 33 before the truck was stopped in Lawrenceburg. Jim Thacker, 44, and Jovanna Grobe, 18, face criminal mischief and related charges; police found two shotguns and ammunition in the vehicle.
