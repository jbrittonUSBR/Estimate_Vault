# Swedish man detained over Kiev sabotage plot – FSB

[Source](https://rt-english.com/russia/647243-fsb-swedish-mercenary-plot/)

(U) MOSCOW, Russia — Russia's Federal Security Service announced on October 15, 2026, that a Swedish national had been detained on suspicion of entering the country to carry out sabotage and terrorist attacks against military facilities, RT reported. The man was detained in Sebezh District, Pskov Region, after illegally crossing the border from a neighboring country, and allegedly intended to use a declared desire to join the Russian armed forces as cover. In FSB-released footage, he identified himself as Freddy Mikael Stalkrantz Lindholm, 35, from Stockholm, and said he had signed a contract with Ukraine's Aidar unit and undergone FPV drone training. The FSB said he served between February and June 2026 as a drone squad commander in the 24th Separate Assault Battalion, also known as Aidar, which Russia has designated a terrorist organization. Investigators opened a criminal case for illegal border crossing and were considering additional charges related to terrorist activity and mercenary service. Sweden's Foreign Ministry said it was aware of the reports but was not currently involved; Ukraine did not immediately comment. (Note: RT is Russian state media; the account relies on FSB-released material and remains unverified.)
