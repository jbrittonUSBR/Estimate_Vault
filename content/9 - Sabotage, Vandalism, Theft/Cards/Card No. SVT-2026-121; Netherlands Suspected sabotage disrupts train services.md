# Netherlands: Suspected sabotage disrupts train services

[Source](https://www.dw.com/en/netherlands-suspected-sabotage-disrupts-train-services/a-79269600)

(U) AMSTERDAM — Pipes were found attached to railway lines, a ProRail spokesperson told the Dutch newspaper de Volkskrant, as signal failures disrupted train services across the central Netherlands, DW reported. The problems originated at 13 locations, according to the report. One train collided with a pipe, causing no injuries. The report said it was unclear whether the timing of the disruption, during Prinsjesdag, was deliberate.
