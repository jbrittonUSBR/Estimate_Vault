# Berlin Is Shedding Light on the Gray Zone

[Source](http://foreignpolicy.com/2026/09/08/germany-russia-drones-gray-zone-hybrid-warfare-putin/)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — In a September 8, 2026 opinion column for Foreign Policy, columnist Elisabeth Braw argued that Germany's public attribution of the Leipzig/Halle airport drone incident to Russia was a "brave decision" and that targeted countries should be ready to publicly name state sponsors of gray-zone attacks. Braw recounted that on September 1, Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt and Foreign Minister Johann Wadephul announced the intelligence linking the attempted drone attack to Russia and disclosed the closure of the Russian consulate-general in Bonn and termination of the Russian House lease in Berlin. She noted that Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov responded by alleging the accusation marked the beginning of "a real war," and argued that such menacing rhetoric should not deter governments from transparently informing the public about hybrid attacks.
