# Russia imports record fuel from India as Ukraine strikes hit refineries

[Source](https://www.business-standard.com/world-news/russia-imports-record-fuel-from-india-as-ukraine-strikes-hit-refineries-126091300111_1.html)

(U) NEW DELHI, India — Russia imported a record 172,000 tonnes of oil products in August 2026, including gasoline refined from Russian crude at India's Vadinar refinery, as Ukrainian drone strikes disrupted Moscow's domestic refining capacity, Business Standard reported, citing analysis by the Centre for Research on Energy and Clean Air (CREA). India supplied 70 percent of Russia's oil-product imports for the month, including 120,000 tonnes of gasoline worth 78 million euros, with the volume more than seven times the previous monthly record. CREA said Russia was effectively paying a refinery it partly owns—EU-sanctioned Nayara Energy, in which Rosneft holds a 49.13 percent stake—to process its crude and ship the fuel back.
