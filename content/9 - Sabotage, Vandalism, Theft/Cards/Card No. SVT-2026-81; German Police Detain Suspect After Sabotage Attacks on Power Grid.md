# German Police Detain Suspect After Sabotage Attacks on Power Grid

[Source](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/09/09/884441.htm)

(U) BERLIN, Germany — German police detained a 48-year-old man, identified under privacy rules as Daniel V., near Weisweiler, about 50 km west of Cologne, on suspicion of carrying out a series of sabotage attacks on the national electricity grid, police and prosecutors announced on September 8, 2026. The suspect was carrying explosive devices when detained, and officers found a nearby tent containing further charges that bomb disposal experts were examining. Investigators were probing a September 1 attack near the Jaenschwalde coal-fired power plant in Brandenburg, where improvised explosive devices were found at a substation, with similar incidents reported in North Rhine-Westphalia and Saxony. The case followed letters claiming responsibility that cited opposition to fossil fuels as the motive, prompting Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt to describe it as likely a case of "climate extremism."
