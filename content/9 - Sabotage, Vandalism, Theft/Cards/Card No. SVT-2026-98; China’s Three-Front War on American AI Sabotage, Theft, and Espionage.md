# China’s Three-Front War on American AI: Sabotage, Theft, and Espionage

[Source](https://legalinsurrection.com/2026/09/chinas-three-front-war-on-american-ai-sabotage-theft-and-espionage/)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — In a commentary for Legal Insurrection, author Leslie Eastman argued that China is waging a "three-front war" on American artificial intelligence through sabotage, theft and espionage. Citing a Reuters report, the piece said U.S. law enforcement and intelligence officials accused six Chinese companies, including DeepSeek, Moonshot AI and Alibaba, of copying U.S. AI intellectual property at "industrial scale" via distillation techniques "likely with Chinese government awareness." It also cited a Google Threat Intelligence Group finding that Chinese intelligence-linked hackers were increasingly targeting American AI research and using AI agents to automate intrusions, and referenced an OpenAI report that it disrupted a China-linked influence operation posing as grassroots opposition to U.S. data centers. The item reflects the author's opinion and an outlet with an established conservative editorial stance.
