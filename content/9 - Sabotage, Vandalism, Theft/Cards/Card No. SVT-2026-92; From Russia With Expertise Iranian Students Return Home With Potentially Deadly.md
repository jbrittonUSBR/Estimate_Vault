# From Russia With Expertise: Iranian Students Return Home With Potentially Deadly Knowledge Of Drones, AI, And More

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/iran/2026/09/iran-260910-rferl01.htm)

(U) PRAGUE, Czech Republic — An investigation by Radio Free Europe/Radio Liberty's Russian investigative unit, Systema, found that Russian universities and research institutes have trained thousands of Iranian specialists in defense-relevant fields, with nearly 10,000 Iranians studying at Russian universities by 2025, a 42 percent increase over two years. Published Russian-Iranian research collaboration spans GPS jamming and spoofing, artificial intelligence for drones, missile propulsion, and uranium enrichment, including work tied to the Moscow Aviation Institute and Bauman Moscow State Technical University. The report cites individuals such as Ali Mohammad Amouei, who studied gas turbine engines at the Moscow Aviation Institute before rising through Iran's defense-linked aviation industry, and Mohammad Hossein Khanmiri, who developed isotope-ratio measurement methods in St. Petersburg later applied at Iran's main uranium deposit.
