# French Train Derails, Injuring Almost 50 — Authorities Suspect Sabotage

[Source](https://freerepublic.com/focus/f-news/4395321/posts)

(U) CLEON, France — A regional train carrying 186 passengers derailed in northwestern France on the evening of Friday, September 11, 2026, injuring 44 people including a young woman critically, after a metal object was found on the track, Free Republic reported, attributing the account to a Red State article by Bob Hoge. A spokeswoman for French national police communications service SICOP told the Associated Press that the piece of rail found on the track was "probably" linked to the derailment, while Rouen prosecutor Sebastien Gallois said the reasons for its presence were unknown and would have to be determined by the investigation. The aggregation noted that officials suspected it was not an accident but that authorities had not yet concluded the incident was deliberate.
