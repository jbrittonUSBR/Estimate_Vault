# Saboteurs suspected in train derailment that injured 44 and left passengers in 'state of shock', French police say

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16126081/Saboteurs-train-derailment-French-police-say.html)

(U) CLEON, France — French police said they were treating sabotage as the main line of inquiry into a train derailment that injured 44 people, including one in critical condition, after discovering a rail fragment weighing between 250 and 300 kilograms on the track, the Daily Mail reported. The train, carrying 186 passengers, derailed near a Renault factory in the town of Cleon in Normandy after hitting the "massive section of rail," with one carriage overturning completely. Rouen prosecutor Sebastien Gallois said the reasons for the fragment's presence were unknown and would have to be determined by the investigation, while Normandy region president Herve Morin called the likely scenario a criminal act.
