# Texas Oil Regulator Hails 'Oil Theft Bust'

[Source](https://www.rigzone.com/news/texas_oil_regulator_hails_oil_theft_bust-03-sep-2026-184533-article/)

(U) AUSTIN, United States — On September 3, 2026, Texas Railroad Commission Commissioner Wayne Christian publicly hailed an "oil theft bust" resulting from a multistate investigation into the theft of petroleum products in the Permian Basin. The operation, dubbed "Operation Black Market Basin," resulted in six arrests and the seizure of more than $12 million in petroleum products, vehicles, cash, and other assets. In a mid-August seizure tied to the case, authorities recovered roughly 2,600 barrels of petroleum products valued at more than $220,000, some 60 vehicles worth more than $2.8 million, $786,215 in cash, luxury watches and jewelry, and eight bank accounts holding more than $8 million. Commissioner Christian, whose agency regulates the state's oil and gas industry, cited the case as a demonstration of coordinated enforcement against organized petroleum theft.
