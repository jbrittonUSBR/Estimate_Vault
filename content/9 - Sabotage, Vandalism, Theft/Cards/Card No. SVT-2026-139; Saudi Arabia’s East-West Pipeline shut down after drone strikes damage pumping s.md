# Saudi Arabia’s East-West Pipeline shut down after drone strikes damage pumping stations

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/saudi-east-west-pipeline-drone-attack/)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — Drone strikes launched from Iraq’s Maysan province damaged two pumping stations along Saudi Arabia’s East-West Pipeline, igniting fires and forcing Saudi authorities to shut down the Petroline as a precautionary measure.
