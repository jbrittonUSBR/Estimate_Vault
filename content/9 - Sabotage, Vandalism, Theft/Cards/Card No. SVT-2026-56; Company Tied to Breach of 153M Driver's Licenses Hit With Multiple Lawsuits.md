# Company Tied to Breach of 153M Driver's Licenses Hit With Multiple Lawsuits

[Source](https://uk.pcmag.com/security/167067/company-tied-to-breach-of-153m-drivers-licenses-hit-with-multiple-lawsuits)

(U) NEW ORLEANS — Four class-action lawsuits were filed against New Orleans-based identity-verification company IDScan.net in the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Louisiana after cybersecurity journalist Brian Krebs reported that the firm appeared to be the source of a dark-web site called Nexus selling access to more than 153 million driver's licenses from the United States and Canada. The plaintiffs, from California, Florida, Georgia, and Louisiana, alleged violations of Federal Trade Commission guidelines governing the safeguarding of personal data. PCMag reported that the FBI was "looking into the incident." The lawsuits followed the June 2026 disclosure of the Nexus marketplace.
