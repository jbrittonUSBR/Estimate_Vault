# Foreign Ministry summons Russian chargé d'affaires over sabotage attack targeting Milrem Robotics

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/russia/2026/09/russia-260929-estonia-mfa01.htm)

(U) TALLINN, Estonia — On September 29, 2026, Estonia's Foreign Ministry summoned the Russian chargé d'affaires over a sabotage attack targeting Milrem Robotics. According to Globalsecurity.org reporting, Estonia's Internal Security Service attributed the arson attack to Russia, suspects were in custody, and the incident came as Estonia noted roughly 200 civilians had been killed and more than 1,230 injured in Ukraine during September.
