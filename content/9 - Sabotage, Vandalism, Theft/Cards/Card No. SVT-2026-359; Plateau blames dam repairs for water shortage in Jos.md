# Plateau blames dam repairs for water shortage in Jos

[Source](https://punchng.com/plateau-blames-dam-repairs-for-water-shortage-in-jos/)

(U) JOS, NIGERIA — The Plateau State government said on November 23 that rehabilitation work at the Yakubu Gowon and Liberty dams and their treatment plants was responsible for water shortages in Jos and Bukuru, according to Punch. Jos Water Services Corporation general manager Apollos Samchi said production lines had been shut down for contractors, working under a N13.5 billion contract with China Geo-Engineering Corporation on an 18-month timeline that began in February. He said some pipe bursts stemmed from vandalism or pipes more than 50 years old, and the corporation had provided three tankers and five water kiosks while planning nearly 400 km of new pipe laying.
