(U) PENSACOLA, Fla. ([WKRG](https://www.wkrg.com/northwest-florida/escambia-county/pensacola-copper-theft-arrests/)) — Pensacola Police Department officers have arrested two people after a 10-month-long copper theft investigation. According to a PPD news release, 37-year-old David Nielsen and 31-year-old Rae Ann Horne were arrested after police and the Florida Highway Patrol investigated reported copper theft. The investigation began in September 2025. Officials said that during that time period, copper was being stripped from bridges and light poles, with a total of 100,000 feet taken — that’s $250,000 worth of copper. “Motorists who frequently travel the Barrancas bridge over Bayou Chico at night may have noticed the lights not functioning for months, and the theft of the wiring from the bridge is the reason the lights were out,” the PPD release said. Officials said that while they were executing the two arrest warrants on Neilsen’s boat, Neilsen jumped into Bayou Chico and was arrested by Florida Fish and Wildlife alongside PPD’s SWAT Unit. Nielsen is charged with theft of copper from utility poles, a first-degree felony, as well as felony criminal mischief. Horne was charged with dealing in stolen property after it was revealed that she had allegedly taken the copper to scrap yards for sale.
