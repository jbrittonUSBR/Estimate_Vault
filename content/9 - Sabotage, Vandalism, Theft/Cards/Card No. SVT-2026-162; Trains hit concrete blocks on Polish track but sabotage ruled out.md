# Trains hit concrete blocks on Polish track but sabotage ruled out

[Source](https://tvpworld.com/95539863/poland-probes-concrete-blocks-placed-on-railway-tracks)

(U) JEDLANKA, Poland — Two freight trains travelling in opposite directions struck concrete blocks placed on the tracks in Jedlanka, in eastern Poland's Lublin region, on the evening of Sept. 22, damaging the locomotives' snowploughs, but neither train derailed and no one was injured, officials said. Rail traffic in the area was suspended for several hours. Prime Minister Donald Tusk said preliminary findings were "not particularly alarming," and Lublin prosecutors, who opened an investigation into causing a threat of a railway disaster, said no explosives were found and the incident was most likely a "hooligan act."
