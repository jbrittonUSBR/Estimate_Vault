# Russia's shadow war is putting Europe on edge as Germany confronts a growing threat

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/world/russias-shadow-war-putting-europe-edge-germany-confronts-growing-threat)

(U) Berlin, Germany — Germany pushed European partners to create a rapid-response task force in the Baltic Sea region to counter what Berlin described as an escalating Russian hybrid-warfare campaign, including an attempted explosives-laden drone attack at Leipzig/Halle Airport on August 4. German Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt said the initiative, proposed after an August 28 meeting in Flensburg, would let Baltic Sea countries rapidly share information on drones, sabotage, cyberattacks and attacks on critical infrastructure. Berlin formally blamed Russia for the Leipzig incident and responded by closing Russia's consulate general in Bonn and tightening entry controls, while Russian officials denied responsibility. German police separately investigated a cluster of suspected sabotage incidents targeting electricity infrastructure.
