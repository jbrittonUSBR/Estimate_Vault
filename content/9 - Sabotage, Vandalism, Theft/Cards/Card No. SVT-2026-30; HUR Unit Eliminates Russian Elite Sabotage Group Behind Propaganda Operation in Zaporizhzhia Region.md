(U) Ukraine’s Defense Intelligence (HUR) “Artan” special forces unit eliminated a Russian elite sabotage and reconnaissance group from the Airborne Forces near Stepnohirsk in the Zaporizhzhia region in late June 2026, foiling an operation intended to create a propaganda victory by staging the capture of the settlement. The Russians, deployed at the end of May, infiltrated under cover of night, rain, and electronic warfare measures but were ambushed after Ukrainian forces offered them the chance to surrender; when they opened fire instead, the HUR unit cleared the area without suffering any losses and seized Russian propaganda flags as trophies. Unit commander Viktor Torkotiuk stated that the enemy attempted classic special forces tactics but was neutralized by proactive intelligence and a calculated response, proving the so-called “elite” unprepared against systematic Ukrainian defense. The successful operation is part of broader Ukrainian territorial advances in the region during May 2026, where Kyiv’s forces reportedly recaptured significantly more ground than Russia gained.
