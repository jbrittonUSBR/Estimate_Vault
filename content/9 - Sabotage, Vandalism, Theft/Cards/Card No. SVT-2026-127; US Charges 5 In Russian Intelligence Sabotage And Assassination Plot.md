# US Charges 5 In Russian Intelligence Sabotage And Assassination Plot

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/intell/library/news/2026/intell-260916-rferl01.htm)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — U.S. authorities unsealed a federal indictment on September 15, 2026, charging five people over an alleged Russian intelligence campaign of surveillance and assassination plots targeting dissidents in the United States and Europe. Among those charged were Kirill Khrameyev, identified as a colonel in the Federal Security Service, or FSB, and his father Yury, a retired FSB officer, along with two Cuban men, Oemis Durruthy and Yaidel Suarez, and a Venezuelan man, Angel Castro. The indictment alleged that Castro, then living in Brooklyn, was paid to surveil a prominent Russian dissident in Washington and was later offered $40,000 to "eliminate" or "disappear" the person, and that an American man living in Lithuania was offered $25,000 to kill a target in Vilnius. The group was also accused of a plot to set fire to buses in the Czech Republic, which the Czech prime minister said was "very likely" organized by Russian authorities.
