# Iraqi authorities seize 15 drone launch platforms after strikes on Saudi pipeline

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/iraq-seizes-drone-platforms-saudi-pipeline/)

(U) BAGHDAD, Iraq — Iraqi authorities seized 15 drone launch platforms and dismissed a provincial military commander after drones launched from Iraq's Maysan province struck Saudi Arabia's East-West oil pipeline in early September 2026, Crypto Briefing reported. The government also closed three border crossings with Iran and launched an investigation, with Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi stating Iraq would not permit its territory to be used as a staging ground for attacks on neighboring states. Saudi Arabia temporarily shut down the pipeline, which handles roughly 4-5 percent of global oil supply, and held off immediate military retaliation pending Baghdad's investigation.
