# Drone attacks shut key Saudi oil pipeline (SATELLITE PHOTOS)

[Source](https://www.rt.com/news/645534-saudi-oil-pipeline-drones/)

(U) RIYADH, Saudi Arabia — Saudi Arabia shut down its strategic East-West oil pipeline after drone attacks damaged pumping facilities, the kingdom's Energy Ministry said on September 11, 2026. The pipeline was hit at several locations in the Riyadh and Medina regions the previous day, causing some injuries and prompting authorities to halt operations as a precautionary measure. Two US officials cited by CNN said preliminary analysis indicated at least two pumping stations were struck, with satellite imagery showing extensive fire damage. The 1,200-km pipeline carries up to 7 million barrels per day from the Eastern Province to the Red Sea port of Yanbu, allowing exports to bypass the Strait of Hormuz. Riyadh said the drones came from Iraqi territory; Baghdad said it traced them to Maysan province and opened an inquiry. RT is a Russian state outlet.
