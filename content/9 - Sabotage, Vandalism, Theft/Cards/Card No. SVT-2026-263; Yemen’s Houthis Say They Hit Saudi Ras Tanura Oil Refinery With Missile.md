# Yemen’s Houthis Say They Hit Saudi Ras Tanura Oil Refinery With Missile

[Source](https://gcaptain.com/yemens-houthis-say-they-hit-saudi-ras-tanura-oil-refinery-with-missile/)

(U) Ras Tanura, Saudi Arabia — Yemen’s Houthis said they had hit the Ras Tanura oil refinery with a ballistic missile and caused a fire, Reuters reported. Saudi authorities had not immediately confirmed the strike; Reuters also cited a separate report that Saudi Arabia had contained a fire near the terminal.
