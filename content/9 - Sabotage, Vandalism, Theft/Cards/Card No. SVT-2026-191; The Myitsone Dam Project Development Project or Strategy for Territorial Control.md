# The Myitsone Dam Project: Development Project or Strategy for Territorial Control?

[Source](https://kachinnews.com/2026/09/30/the-myitsone-dam-project-development-project-or-strategy-for-territorial-control/)

(U) MYITKYINA, Myanmar — In a commentary published September 30, 2026, researcher Ma Dee argued that the proposed restart of the Myitsone Dam on the Irrawaddy River is less an energy project than a strategy for territorial control by Myanmar's military government against the Kachin Independence Organization and its armed wing. The project, begun in 2006 by China Power Investment and suspended in 2011, would displace about 12,000 people from roughly 65 villages and 2,600 households, according to the article. It noted that a Kachin State government committee has held consultations since early 2026. The piece is an opinion analysis and its claims could not be independently verified.
