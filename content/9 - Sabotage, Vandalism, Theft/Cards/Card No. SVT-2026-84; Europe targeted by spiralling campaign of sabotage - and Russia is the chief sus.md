# Europe targeted by spiralling campaign of sabotage - and Russia is the chief suspect

[Source](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c5y4g6meekro?xtor=AL-72-%5Bpartner%5D-%5Byahoo.north.america%5D-%5Bheadline%5D-%5Bnews%5D-%5Bbizdev%5D-%5Bisapi%5D)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — In a September 4, 2026 analysis, the BBC reported that Russia appeared to have escalated a sabotage campaign across Europe over August and early September 2026, with military and defence-sector sites the prime targets. Germany's interior minister declared Russia responsible for explosives-laden drones found at Leipzig airport on August 4, and August brought a series of suspicious fires at defence facilities from Italy and Bulgaria to Estonia, Poland and Slovakia, several of which governments blamed on Russia or were investigating for outside interference. Germany quoted a federal police report listing more than 165 suspected sabotage cases nationwide that year, and analysts cited by the BBC described the campaign as "coercive signalling" and probing of NATO vulnerabilities, often carried out by recruited proxies. Moscow denied the accusations, calling them "absurd."
