# Why Are Water Treatment Plants Still Connected to the Internet?

[Source](https://gizmodo.com/why-are-water-treatment-plants-still-connected-to-the-internet-2000807726)

(U) OAKLAND, Calif. — As reported by Gizmodo (analysis/opinion), water treatment plants remain connected to the internet largely because programmable logic controllers allow remote monitoring and control, creating cybersecurity vulnerabilities that state-aligned actors have exploited in recent attacks; experts warn that utilities must prepare manual fallback procedures and assume breaches will occur, though the operational necessity of remote access continues to delay full disconnection.
