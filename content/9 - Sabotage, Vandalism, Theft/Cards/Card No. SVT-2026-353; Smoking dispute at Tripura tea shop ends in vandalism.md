# Smoking dispute at Tripura tea shop ends in vandalism

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/guwahati/smoking-dispute-at-tripura-tea-shop-ends-in-vandalism/articleshow/136081723.cms)

(U) Khowai, India — A tea-shop owner told police that BJP Khowai district president Samir Das and other party figures kept smoking despite her no-smoking rule, and that furniture was damaged after she confronted them. CCTV footage circulated locally purportedly showed Das, his driver and another man damaging items in the shop, and the owner alleged that a BJP candidate later tried to remove the camera; BJP state president Abhishek Debroy said the party would investigate.
