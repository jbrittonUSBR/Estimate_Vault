# Farmers blow a fuse as thieves target transformers to steal copper and aluminium windings

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/hyderabad/farmers-blow-a-fuse-as-thieves-target-transformers-to-steal-copper-and-aluminium-windings/articleshow/136061526.cms)

(U) Telangana, India — The Times of India reported that thieves broke into at least 51 agricultural electricity transformers across Bhadradri Kothagudem district over the previous year, stripping copper and aluminium windings worth about Rs 5,000 in scrap while replacements cost farmers between Rs 50,000 and Rs 2.5 lakh. Farmers in Julurupadu mandal and other areas said the resulting power cuts disrupted irrigation pumping, with isolated, unattended fields giving thieves time to dismantle equipment. Electricity department superintending engineer Mahender said officials fitted protective bolts and alerted farmers, and that replacement transformers were installed promptly after police cases were registered.
