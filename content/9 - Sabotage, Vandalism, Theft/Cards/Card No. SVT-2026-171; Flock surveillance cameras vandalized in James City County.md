# Flock surveillance cameras vandalized in James City County

[Source](https://www.wavy.com/news/local-news/james-city-county/flock-surveillance-cameras-vandalized-in-james-city-county/)

(U) JAMES CITY COUNTY, Va. — Seven Flock automated license plate reader cameras were vandalized and destroyed in James City County, with spray paint used to cover the camera lenses, according to police. The James City County Police Department circulated images of the damage and said the affected units could no longer capture footage. Police estimated replacement at about $1,000 per camera and noted that damaging the cameras is a Class 6 felony.
