# Zelenskyy says Ukraine got no early warning from partners as Russia hits the power grid again

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/world-news/zelenskyy-says-ukraine-got-no-early-warning-from-partners-as-russia-hits-the-power-grid-again/articleshow/135055865.cms)

(U) October 19, 2026 — Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Russia's overnight missile and drone barrage damaged more than 10 energy facilities and left over half a million people without electricity, heating, and water. He said Ukraine received no early warning from partner countries, while US Secretary of State Marco Rubio said he had no information about the attack and that Washington had not stopped sharing intelligence. Russia's Defense Ministry said its strikes targeted military-related facilities.
