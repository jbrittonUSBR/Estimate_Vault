# Assad-era Syrian reactor was configured for nuclear weapons, IAEA chief says

[Source](https://abcnews.com/Technology/wireStory/assad-era-syrian-reactor-configured-nuclear-weapons-iaea-136255438)

(U) Vienna, Austria — IAEA Director General Rafael Grossi told reporters that the Assad-era government in Syria was constructing a nuclear reactor whose configuration and technical features were "very efficient in the production of fissile material that could be directly used for the fabrication of nuclear weapons." The assessment followed an August inspector visit to the Deir el-Zour site, where the agency confirmed cooling infrastructure consistent with a reactor, and to a previously undeclared site where it verified around 73 tonnes of natural uranium metal. Grossi said the material was now under IAEA safeguards, with inspectors having installed cameras at the location.
