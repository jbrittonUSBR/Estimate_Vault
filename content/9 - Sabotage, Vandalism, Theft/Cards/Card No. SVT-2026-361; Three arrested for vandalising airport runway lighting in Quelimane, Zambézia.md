# Three arrested for vandalising airport runway lighting in Quelimane, Zambézia

[Source](https://news.mmo.co.mz/three-arrested-for-vandalising-airport-runway-lighting-in-quelimane-zambezia-2026-11/)

(U) QUELIMANE, MOZAMBIQUE — Mozambican police arrested three people on suspicion of theft and vandalism of runway lighting equipment at Quelimane Airport, according to MMO News citing Notícias. Provincial police spokesperson Belarmina Henriques said one suspect was caught disassembling illuminated signalling lights, and that it was the third such vandalism this year, with losses estimated at about 1.6 million meticais. Airport director Maurício Alfredo warned that removing the devices jeopardized night landing operations, while two alleged accomplices denied involvement.
