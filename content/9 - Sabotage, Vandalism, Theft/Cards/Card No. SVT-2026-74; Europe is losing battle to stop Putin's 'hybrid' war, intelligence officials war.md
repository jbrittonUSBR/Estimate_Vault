# Europe is losing battle to stop Putin's 'hybrid' war, intelligence officials warn as Germany opens sabotage probe after another substation fire

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16111147/Europe-losing-battle-stop-Putins-hybrid-war-intelligence-officials-warn-Germany-opens-sabotage-probe-substation-fire.html)

(U) Berlin, Germany — Intelligence officials warned that Europe was losing the battle to stop Vladimir Putin's "hybrid" war as Germany opened a sabotage investigation following a fire at an electricity substation in Berlin's Moabit area. Defence officials quoted by the Financial Times said the continent must develop new methods to counter the Kremlin's campaign of sub-Article 5 provocations, with one source saying enough deterrence existed to prevent conventional and nuclear escalation but that the approach to hybrid threats needed rethinking. The incident came as police hunted a 48-year-old suspect accused of a string of substation attacks across Germany aimed at cutting supplies from coal-fired power plants, and after Berlin formally blamed Russia for an explosives-laden drone found at Leipzig/Halle Airport on August 4.
