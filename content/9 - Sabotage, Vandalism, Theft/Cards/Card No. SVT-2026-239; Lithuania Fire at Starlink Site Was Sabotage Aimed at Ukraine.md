# Lithuanian officials said fire at Starlink site was sabotage aimed at Ukraine

[Source](https://www.newser.com/story/398412/lithuania-fire-at-starlink-site-was-sabotage-aimed-at-ukraine.html)

(U) October 19, 2026 — Lithuanian officials said a fire at a data-center site hosting Starlink equipment was a deliberate attack intended to disrupt satellite connectivity used by Ukraine. Newser, an aggregator, reported that Prime Minister Mindaugas Sinkevicius said attackers used flammable liquids and Molotov cocktails; the attribution of motive was the officials' assessment, not an independently established finding.
