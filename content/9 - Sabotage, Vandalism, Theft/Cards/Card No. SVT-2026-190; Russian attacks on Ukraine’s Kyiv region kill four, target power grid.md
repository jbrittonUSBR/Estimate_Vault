# Russian attacks on Ukraine’s Kyiv region kill four, target power grid

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/30/russian-attacks-on-ukraines-kyiv-region-kill-four-target-power-grid)

(U) KYIV — An overnight barrage of Russian missiles and drones killed at least four people, including a child, in the Kyiv region and struck energy infrastructure, Al Jazeera reported September 30, 2026. Three of the deaths occurred in Kyiv city, and utility DTEK restored power to about 8,000 households after the strikes. Damage was also reported in the Zhytomyr, Mykolaiv, Kirovohrad and Rivne regions. Russia's defense ministry said it targeted energy facilities linked to Ukraine's military, a communications site in Kyiv and the port of Izmail.
