# Army foils pipeline vandalism attempt, arrests 18-Year-old suspect in Benue

[Source](https://punchng.com/army-foils-pipeline-vandalism-attempt-arrests-18-year-old-suspect-in-benue/)

(U) Benue State, Nigeria — The Punch reported that the Nigerian Army said it thwarted an attempted pipeline vandalism and arrested an 18-year-old suspect. The incident and arrest were described in the military’s account; the report did not establish the suspect’s guilt or confirm damage to the pipeline.
