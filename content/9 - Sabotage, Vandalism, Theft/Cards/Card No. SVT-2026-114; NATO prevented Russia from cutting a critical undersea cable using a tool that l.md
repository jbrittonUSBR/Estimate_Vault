# NATO prevented Russia from cutting a critical undersea cable using a tool that leaves no 'traceable fingerprints' on fibre-optic wires — 1,400km long lines are part of NASA's Near Space Network

[Source](https://www.techradar.com/pro/nato-prevented-russia-from-cutting-a-critical-undersea-cable-using-a-tool-that-leaves-no-traceable-fingerprints-on-fibre-optic-wires-1-400km-long-lines-are-part-of-nasas-near-space-network)

(U) SVALBARD, Norway — NATO allies disrupted a Russian naval exercise near Svalbard after detecting preparations involving a secret device intended for damaging subsea communications infrastructure, TechRadar reported. British, Norwegian, and American forces confronted vessels linked to Russia's deep-sea research directorate, known as GUGI, during exercises conducted in Arctic waters in spring 2026. No cable was damaged, but officials said the equipment could sever fibre-optic lines while removing evidence that would identify its use; two roughly 1,400-kilometre cables connect Svalbard with mainland Norway and support the SvalSat ground station, which serves NASA's Near Space Network.
