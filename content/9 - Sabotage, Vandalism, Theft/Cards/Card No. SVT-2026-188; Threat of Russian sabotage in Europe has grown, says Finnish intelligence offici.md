# Threat of Russian sabotage in Europe has grown, says Finnish intelligence official

[Source](https://yle.fi/a/74-20248687)

(U) Helsinki, Finland — Teemu Liikkanen, head of counterintelligence at the Finnish Security and Intelligence Service (Supo), said on Yle's breakfast show that the threat of Russian sabotage in Europe had increased over the summer and was real in Finland too, though he said there was no indication of immediate danger. He identified Finland's defence industry and activities supporting Ukraine as the targets most vulnerable to Russian sabotage. Retired colonel Jyrki Isokangas said Russia was seeking to drive a wedge between Western countries and undermine support for Ukraine, and President Alexander Stubb urged Finns to prepare mentally for possible acts of sabotage.
