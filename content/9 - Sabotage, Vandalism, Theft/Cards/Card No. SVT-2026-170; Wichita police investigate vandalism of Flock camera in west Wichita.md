# Wichita police investigate vandalism of Flock camera in west Wichita

[Source](https://www.kake.com/home/wichita-police-investigate-vandalism-of-flock-camera-in-west-wichita/article_150ff8c9-f9bb-4ce7-8074-242794bb60c8.html)

(U) WICHITA, Kan. — A Flock license plate reader near Central and Zoo Boulevard in west Wichita was cut down over the weekend, leaving only the base, and police opened a vandalism investigation with no arrests reported. A message associated with the "De-Flock Sedgwick County" movement was left near the site, and police said it was not the first Flock camera vandalized in the city. Department spokesperson Andrew Ford warned that offenders could face misdemeanor or felony charges, while opponents including Mock the Flock cited privacy concerns.
