# British man, 31, is accused of preparing to sabotage UK drone factory on behalf of Russia

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16120539/British-man-31-remanded-custody-charged-preparing-sabotage-UK-drone-factory-behalf-Russian-military-intelligence.html)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — A British man accused of preparing to sabotage a UK drone factory on behalf of Russian military intelligence was remanded in custody, the Daily Mail reported. Prosecutors told Westminster Magistrates' Court that Joshua Cammidge, 31, offered to set fire to drone factories in Swindon to show his "allegiance" to Russia and researched four drone manufacturing sites and a WiFi jamming device, allegedly receiving instructions from an individual linked to the GRU Volunteer Corps. He was charged under the National Security Act with assisting a foreign intelligence service and intending to prepare an act of sabotage, did not enter a plea, and his case was sent to the Old Bailey for a hearing on September 25. The charges were described in court as "strenuously denied."
