# ‘No imminent threat’: Rubio hits back at Iran dirty bomb report, brands intelligence sources ‘liars’

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/middle-east/no-imminent-threat-rubio-hits-back-at-iran-dirty-bomb-report-brands-intelligence-sources-liars/articleshow/135947349.cms)

(U) Manila, Philippines — US Secretary of State Marco Rubio told reporters that no attack on Washington was imminent and described the intelligence sources behind a CNN report as liars and, in some cases, criminals; CNN had cited seven US officials saying Iranian planning for possible attacks involving a dirty bomb or chemical weapons had been detected but was not imminent. White House spokesperson Lauren Bis said no credible threats to the homeland existed at that time. Iranian Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi rejected the allegations as pro-Israel propaganda, and parliament speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf called them false flags and war mongering.
