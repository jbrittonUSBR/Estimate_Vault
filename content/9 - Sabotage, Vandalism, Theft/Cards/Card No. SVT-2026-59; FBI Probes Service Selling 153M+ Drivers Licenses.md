# FBI Probes Service Selling 153M+ Drivers Licenses

[Source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/forums/t/818388/fbi-probes-service-selling-153m-drivers-licenses/)

(U) NEW ORLEANS, United States — In early September 2026, the FBI's New Orleans field office opened an inquiry into a dark-web service reportedly selling scanned images of more than 153 million U.S. and Canadian driver's licenses, according to a report by security journalist Brian Krebs. The service, identified as "Nexus," was also said to offer data from more than 10 million identity cards, 3 million travel documents, and 579,000 medical cards. Investigators traced the data to what appeared to be a Louisiana-based identity-verification vendor, suggesting a single large-scale source rather than multiple breaches. The findings were originally published by KrebsOnSecurity and later reposted to the BleepingComputer forum.
