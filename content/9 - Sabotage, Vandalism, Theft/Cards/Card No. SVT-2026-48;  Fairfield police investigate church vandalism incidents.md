(U) Local law enforcement agencies in Fairfield and neighboring Bridgeport launched an active investigation into a troubling series of disturbances and vandalism targeting local Catholic churches, which included damaged property and a decapitated religious statue.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Incidents:** Authorities responded to reports of vandalism and suspicious disturbances across local houses of worship, notably including **St. Anthony’s Church** in Fairfield, where a religious statue was found heavily damaged and headless.
- **Cross-Jurisdictional Scope:** Because multiple incidents occurred across municipal lines, police departments in Fairfield and Bridgeport coordinated their efforts to determine if the same individual or group was responsible for the coordinated spree.
- **Community and Church Response:** Church leaders and local parishioners expressed shock and dismay over the desecration of sacred spaces, while security measures and vigilance were heightened across regional parishes.
- **Ongoing Investigation:** Police stepped up patrols around local religious facilities, reviewing surveillance footage and appealing to the public for tips to help identify and apprehend the suspect or suspects involved.
