(U) An armed gang overpowered security guards at the Heidelberg Reservoir in South Africa on June 28 and stole electrical power cables essential for operating the water pumps, leaving thousands of residents across multiple suburbs without water. The theft disrupted supply to areas including Overkruin, Berg en Dal, Bergsig, the Heidelberg Mall precinct, Jordaan Park, and parts of Heidelberg Town, prompting the Lesedi Local Municipality to deploy technical teams to assess the damage and replace the stolen cables. Police are investigating the robbery, and authorities have appealed to the public for any information that could assist in the case while urging residents to use water sparingly during the outage. The incident highlights the growing problem of infrastructure-related crime targeting critical services in the region, with no arrests reported and the full restoration timeline still unknown.
