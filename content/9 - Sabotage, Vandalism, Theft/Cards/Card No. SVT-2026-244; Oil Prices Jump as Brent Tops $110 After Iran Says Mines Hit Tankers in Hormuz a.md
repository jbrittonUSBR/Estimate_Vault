# Oil Prices Jump as Brent Tops $110 After Iran Says Mines Hit Tankers in Hormuz and Houthis Strike Saudi Sites

[Source](https://www.ibtimes.com.au/oil-prices-rise-tanker-attacks-hormuz-supply-fears-1877115)

(U) October 21, 2026 — IBTimes Australia reported that Brent crude rose about 1.5% to $110.38 a barrel amid regional shipping concerns and declining U.S. inventories. The outlet attributed reports that Iranian Revolutionary Guard forces said mines had stopped three tankers in the Strait of Hormuz, but said U.S. Central Command rejected the account and called the claims false. The report also said the Houthis claimed attacks on Saudi energy sites; those claims and their effects were not independently established in the article.
