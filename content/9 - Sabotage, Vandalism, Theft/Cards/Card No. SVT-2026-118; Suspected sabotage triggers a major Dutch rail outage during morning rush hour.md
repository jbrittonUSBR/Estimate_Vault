# Suspected sabotage triggers a major Dutch rail outage during morning rush hour

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/09/15/world-news/suspected-sabotage-triggers-a-major-dutch-rail-outage-during-the-morning-rush-hour/)

(U) AMSTERDAM — A suspected act of sabotage disrupted rail traffic across the Netherlands on Tuesday morning, with the infrastructure manager ProRail reporting problems at about 24 locations, the Associated Press reported. ProRail said material had been deliberately placed on the tracks, and services were halted or delayed around Zwolle, Deventer and Amersfoort during the morning rush hour. Police opened an investigation, and no group had claimed responsibility. The disruption coincided with Prinsjesdag, the annual presentation of the government's budget.
