# ESB flags risk of ‘state actor’ attacks on assets following incidents in Europe

[Source](https://www.irishtimes.com/business/2026/09/12/esb-flags-risk-of-state-actor-attacks-on-assets-following-incidents-in-europe/)

(U) DUBLIN, Ireland — Irish state-owned electricity company ESB warned its debt investors of the risk of physical attacks on its assets and on third-party critical infrastructure, following a spate of incidents in Europe linked to Russian sabotage, The Irish Times reported. In a bond prospectus dated August 28, the company cited risks posed by "state actors" amid heightened geopolitical tensions, noting that similar assets in other European markets had been targeted over the past two years. The disclosure echoed warnings from other European power groups such as Vattenfall, Orsted and Fortum, and came as the conflict monitor ACLED recorded at least 150 incidents of sabotage, cyberattacks or drone strikes in European countries since Russia's 2022 invasion of Ukraine.
