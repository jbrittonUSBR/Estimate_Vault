# Suspect in Imperial Beach anti-Semitic vandalism incidents arrested

[Source](https://www.10news.com/news/south-bay/imperial-beach-couple-speaks-out-after-anti-semitic-vandalism-targets-their-home)

(U) IMPERIAL BEACH, Calif. — San Diego County sheriff's officials arrested 18-year-old Kristoffer Von Hassel in connection with a series of antisemitic vandalism incidents in Imperial Beach, saying surveillance video helped identify him. Von Hassel was accused of drawing hate symbols and graffiti at homes on Elm Avenue and Seacoast Drive on September 13 and of further vandalism at the Elm Avenue home on September 19; deputies said he was found carrying a device capable of interacting with wireless signals used by garage doors and gates. He was arrested on suspicion of vandalism and property damage motivated by civil rights violations as the investigation continued.
