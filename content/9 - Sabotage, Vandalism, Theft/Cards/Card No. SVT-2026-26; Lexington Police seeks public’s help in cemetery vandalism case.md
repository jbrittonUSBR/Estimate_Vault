(U) Lexington Police are asking for the public’s help in identifying those responsible for vandalizing Lexington Cemetery overnight between June 9 and 10, 2026, when unknown individuals damaged 17 resting places, causing an estimated $34,000 in harm. The department has not identified any suspects or made arrests and is urging anyone with information to contact investigators directly at (859) 258-3600 or submit anonymous tips through Bluegrass Crime Stoppers at (859) 253-2020, their website, or the P3 Tips app. The incident involved the destruction of gravesites in the historic cemetery, though police have not released details on the specific nature of the damage or any potential motives. Authorities are continuing their investigation and hope community assistance will lead to arrests in the case.
