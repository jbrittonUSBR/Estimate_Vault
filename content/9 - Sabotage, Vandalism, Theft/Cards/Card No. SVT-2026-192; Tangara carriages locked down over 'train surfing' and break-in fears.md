# Tangara carriages locked down over 'train surfing' and break-in fears

[Source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-30/nsw-tangara-trains-vandalism-security-measures/107207828)

(U) SYDNEY — Rail authorities in New South Wales locked the rear carriages of all Tangara trains and switched off their lights for the school holidays in response to train surfing and break-ins, ABC News reported September 30, 2026. The measures followed incidents in which people rode on the outside of moving trains, known as buffer riding, and breached crew cabins. A $447 million refurbishment announced in June 2024 has so far fitted only one of 55 Tangara trains with CCTV, and slanted nose cones reduced incidents on the T4 line to three between July and September from 127 in the previous 12 months. Transport Minister John Graham defended the response while shadow minister Natalie Ward criticized the pace of the rollout.
