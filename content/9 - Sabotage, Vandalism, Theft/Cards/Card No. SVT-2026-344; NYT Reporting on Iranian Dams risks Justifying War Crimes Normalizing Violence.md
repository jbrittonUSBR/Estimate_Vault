# NYT Reporting on Iranian Dams risks Justifying War Crimes: Normalizing Violence

[Source](https://www.juancole.com/2026/11/reporting-justifying-normalizing.html)

(U) College Park, Maryland — In this Informed Comment opinion piece, University of Maryland scholar Fatemeh Keshavarz criticized a November 16 New York Times article that speculated Iranian dams may have been built to conceal underground missile sites. She argued that the Times relied on unverifiable expert guesses, omitted Iranian responses and revived 'axis of evil' rhetoric without considering why Iran built underground facilities; the newspaper itself said a definitive explanation remained elusive. Keshavarz warned that treating the dams as legitimate targets could normalize environmental destruction, citing a US strike in Hormozgan province's Tarom protected area that killed a wildlife ranger's family.
