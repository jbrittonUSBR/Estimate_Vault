# Iran threatens undersea cables in Strait of Hormuz, calls them ‘legitimate new targets’

[Source](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/iran-threatens-to-attack-undersea-cables-in-the-strait-of-hormuz-calls-them-legitimate-new-targets-in-future-conflict-irgc-spokesman-warns-that-a-future-third-phase-in-war-with-us-could-lead-them-to-sever-global-data-links)

(U) October 21, 2026 — Tom’s Hardware reported that Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps spokesman Hossein Mohebbi described undersea fiber-optic cables in the Strait of Hormuz as “legitimate new targets” in a future conflict. The outlet said IRGC political deputy Yadollah Javani suggested Iran could attack the cables in response to strikes on Iranian infrastructure. These were Iranian officials’ threats; the report did not describe a cable attack or disruption.
