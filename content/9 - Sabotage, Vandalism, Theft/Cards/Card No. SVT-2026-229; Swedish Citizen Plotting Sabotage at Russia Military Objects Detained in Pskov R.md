# Swedish Citizen Plotting Sabotage at Russian Military Sites Detained in Pskov Region, FSB Says

[Source](https://sputnikglobe.com/20261015/swedish-citizen-plotting-sabotage-at-russia-military-objects-detained-in-pskov-region---fsb-1124883920.html)

(U) PSKOV REGION, RUSSIA — Sputnik reported that Russia’s Federal Security Service (FSB) had detained a Swedish citizen in the Pskov region on suspicion of plotting sabotage against Russian military facilities. The article relayed the FSB’s allegation that the individual had acted on instructions from Ukrainian intelligence and described the investigation as presented by Russian authorities. Those claims came from a Russian state-media outlet citing the FSB and were not independently verified in the report.
