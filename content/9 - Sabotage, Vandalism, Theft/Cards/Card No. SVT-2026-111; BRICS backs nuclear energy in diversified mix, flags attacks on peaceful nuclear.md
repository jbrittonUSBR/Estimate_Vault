# BRICS backs nuclear energy in diversified mix, flags attacks on peaceful nuclear facilities

[Source](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/brics-backs-nuclear-energy-in-diversified-mix-flags-attacks-on-peaceful-nuclear-facilities-14028618.html)

(U) NEW DELHI, India — The New Delhi Declaration adopted at the 18th BRICS Summit recognized nuclear energy among the sources and technologies contributing to energy security within a balanced and diversified mix, Moneycontrol reported. In its section on international peace and security, the grouping expressed "serious concern" over deliberate attacks on civilian infrastructure and peaceful nuclear facilities under International Atomic Energy Agency safeguards, saying such attacks violate international law and relevant IAEA resolutions. BRICS also called for greater cooperation on smart grids, energy storage and low-emission hydrogen, and backed digital technologies to strengthen the resilience of energy systems.
