# Railway blast near Pakistan border raises sabotage fears; no explosive remnants found

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/jaipur/railway-blast-near-pakistan-border-raises-sabotage-fears-no-explosive-remnants-found/articleshow/135545019.cms)

(U) Ramsinghpur, Rajasthan, India — The Times of India reported that an explosion on a railway track shattered two sleepers and damaged rails about 75 minutes after a passenger train passed. Authorities suspended rail movement and cordoned off the site; a search found no identifiable explosive-device remnants, and forensic testing was pending. Police said sabotage had not been ruled out, but investigators had not established the cause or any link to a separate October drone interception.
