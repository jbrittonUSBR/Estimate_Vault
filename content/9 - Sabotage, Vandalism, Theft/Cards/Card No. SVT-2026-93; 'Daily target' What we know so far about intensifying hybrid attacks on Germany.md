# 'Daily target': What we know so far about intensifying hybrid attacks on Germany

[Source](https://www.thelocal.de/20260904/daily-target-what-we-know-so-far-about-intensifying-hybrid-attacks-on-germany)

(U) BERLIN, Germany — German officials formally blamed Russia for an August 4 explosive-laden drone attack at Leipzig/Halle airport, with Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt stating that Germany is "a daily target of hybrid warfare" carried out by "low-level agents," a charge Moscow called "absurd." The Local Germany documented a surge of suspected sabotage in the week of August 31 to September 4, including improvised explosive devices at the Jänschwalde coal power plant in Brandenburg, targeted overhead power lines in Bergheim, an explosion that injured two people at Augsburg's central train station, and arson at a Munich construction site linked to a defense firm. Federal Criminal Police analysis cited by Suddeutsche Zeitung, NDR, and WDR recorded 165 suspected sabotage cases in 2026 as of early September, compared with 320 for all of 2025, while Foreign Minister Johann Wadephul announced tightened entry controls for Russian nationals, closure of the Russian consulate in Bonn, and cancellation of the Russian House cultural centre's contract in Berlin.
