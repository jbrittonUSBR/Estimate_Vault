# Saudi Pipeline Attack Risks ‘Disastrous’ Loss of Millions of Barrels of Oil

[Source](https://slashdot.org/firehose.pl?op=view&amp;id=185668358)

(U) RIYADH, Saudi Arabia — A New York Times report surfaced by the Slashdot aggregator said the continued closure of Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline risked keeping 4 percent of the world's oil supply from reaching international markets and driving energy prices even higher. Saudi Arabia scrambled to reroute roughly four million barrels of oil a day after temporarily shutting the 750-mile pipeline, which it said had been damaged by an Iran-backed militia. The report, by Lisa Friedman, noted that the pipeline had been the kingdom's primary export route since the war with Iran began in February, and that the Houthis had also seized a strategic Red Sea port, struck Saudi energy facilities and claimed to have downed a Saudi fighter jet.
