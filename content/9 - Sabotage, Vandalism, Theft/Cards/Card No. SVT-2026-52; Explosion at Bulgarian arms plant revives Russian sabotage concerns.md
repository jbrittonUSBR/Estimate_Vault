(U) An explosion and subsequent fire at a storage facility operated by the private Bulgarian arms manufacturer **EMCO** near Tryavna revived intense concerns over potential foreign sabotage and historical security breaches linked to Russian intelligence.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Incident:** The blast occurred at an ammunition storage site near the village of Belitsa. Roughly 300 employees were successfully evacuated, and no injuries, environmental pollution, or third-party property damage were reported.
- **Differing Perspectives on Cause:** While EMCO's management openly raised the specter of sabotage and ruled out human error, preliminary statements from Bulgarian authorities—including Interior Minister Ivan Demerdzhiev—suggested an internal or accidental cause, pointing to a vehicle fire that triggered the blaze.
- **History of Suspicious Incidents:** The blast recalled a long pattern of unexplained explosions at Bulgarian defense facilities over the past 15 years (including previous incidents at EMCO sites in 2011, 2022, and 2023). EMCO manufactures arms that have historically been exported to regions like Ukraine and Georgia.
- **The Russian Connection:** Past investigations by international investigative groups and local authorities previously linked several blasts at Bulgarian ammunition depots—as well as a 2015 poisoning attempt against EMCO owner Emilian Gebrev—to operatives from Russia's military intelligence service (the GRU).
