# Amidst fears of AI cyberattacks, Satya Nadella says lots of critical infrastructure is 'unpatchable' | TechCrunch

[Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/amidst-fears-of-ai-cyberattacks-satya-nadella-says-lots-of-critical-infrastructure-is-unpatchable/)

(U) New York, New York — Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella said much critical infrastructure software, including systems for building and transportation equipment, contained vulnerabilities that could not be patched, TechCrunch reported. He said AI was accelerating vulnerability discovery and exploitation, while rapid patch deployment could also create instability; he described a recent Outlook outage as resulting from a latent bug.
