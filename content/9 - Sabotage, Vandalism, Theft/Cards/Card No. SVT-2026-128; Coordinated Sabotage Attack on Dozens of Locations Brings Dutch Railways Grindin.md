# Coordinated Sabotage Attack on Dozens of Locations Brings Dutch Railways Grinding to a Halt

[Source](https://www.breitbart.com/europe/2026/09/15/coordinated-sabotage-attack-brings-dutch-railways-grinding-to-a-halt/)

(U) AMSTERDAM, Netherlands — Saboteurs placed metal objects on tracks at more than 30 locations across the Netherlands on September 15, 2026, bringing large parts of the national rail network to a standstill and disrupting road traffic and international train services. According to Breitbart News, the objects — 1.5-metre metal pipes and ordinary car jump-start cables — short-circuited the electrical track circuits railways use to detect trains, tricking the signalling system into showing occupied track and turning signals red. The rail operator reported that at several locations new obstacles were laid after engineers and police had cleared the original ones, and the AIVD domestic intelligence service assisted the police investigation. No group claimed responsibility, and Extinction Rebellion and a farmers' protest movement denied any knowledge of the action.
