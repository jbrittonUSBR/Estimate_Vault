# Batam-Bintan blackout: Police probe possible negligence, sabotage

[Source](https://en.antaranews.com/news/436809/batam-bintan-blackout-police-probe-possible-negligence-sabotage)

(U) October 20, 2026 — ANTARA reported that Indonesian police were investigating whether negligence or deliberate action caused an October 12 outage that blacked out Batam and Bintan. Police said initial findings pointed to a short circuit at the Sei Harapan substation, while investigators examined a repositioned CCTV camera and why the failure affected the wider grid. Authorities said five people had been named as suspects, but the cause and any role of sabotage remained under investigation.
