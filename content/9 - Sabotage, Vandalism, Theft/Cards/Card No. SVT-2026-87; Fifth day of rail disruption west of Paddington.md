# Fifth day of rail disruption west of Paddington

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — Rail services west of Paddington remain severely disrupted for a fifth day following a trackside fire that damaged electrical infrastructure. Great Western Railway, Elizabeth Line, and Heathrow Express services continue to face cancellations and reduced timetables as engineers work to restore full power. A man has been arrested on suspicion of arson in connection with the incident.
