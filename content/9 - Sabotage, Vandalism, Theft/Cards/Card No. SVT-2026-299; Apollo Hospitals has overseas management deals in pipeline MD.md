# Apollo Hospitals has overseas management deals in pipeline: MD

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/chennai/apollo-hospitals-has-overseas-management-deals-in-pipeline-md/articleshow/135545631.cms)

(U) Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India — The Times of India reported that Apollo Hospitals planned to expand overseas hospital-management contracts, with Managing Director Suneeta Reddy naming Iraq, Tanzania, and Indonesia as potential markets. The company said international-patient revenue rose 37% year over year in the quarter and reported plans to add 4,200 beds over five years; the article does not describe theft or sabotage.
