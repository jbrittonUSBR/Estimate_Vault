# How engineers controlled the Tangjiashan landslide-dammed lake after China’s 2008 earthquake

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/new-updates/dam-in-china-trapped-millions-of-tonnes-of-water-rising-7-feet-daily-and-threatening-millions-how-did-engineers-avert-disaster/articleshow/135072730.cms)

(U) October 20, 2026 — The Economic Times recounted how a landslide triggered by China’s 2008 Wenchuan earthquake blocked the Tongkou River, forming the Tangjiashan lake and creating a downstream flood risk. The article said authorities evacuated residents and used military helicopters to bring machinery to the site, where engineers cut a channel to release the water in a controlled manner. It reported that the drainage operation lowered the lake below a danger threshold without flood-related casualties and informed later landslide-dam response planning.
