# Two arrested in Stanislaus wire theft that caused power, phone, internet outages

[Source](https://www.modbee.com/news/local/crime/article318233225.html)

(U) TURLOCK, CALIFORNIA — The Stanislaus County Sheriff's Office said Ricky Lewis, 50, and Jaime Hernandez-Robles, 38, were booked November 18 on charges including felony grand theft, vandalism, conspiracy, damage to power and communication lines and possession of stolen property after a telecommunications wire theft investigation, according to the Modesto Bee. Investigators said suspects climbed utility poles, cut AT&T wire and used vehicles to pull it down in unincorporated Turlock and Denair, causing hundreds of thousands of dollars in damage and multiple power, telephone and internet outages. Both men remained in custody on $75,000 bail each.
