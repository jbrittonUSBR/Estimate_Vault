# Trump sends warning to ‘not nice’ Houthis after deadly Saudi Arabia attacks, suggests Turkey could help

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/10/13/us-news/trump-sends-warning-to-houthis-after-saudi-arabia-attacks-suggests-turkey-could-help/)

(U) WASHINGTON, DC — The New York Post reported that President Donald Trump warned Yemen’s Houthis against further attacks on Saudi Arabia and said he might ask Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan to help counter the group. Trump characterized the Houthis as “not nice” and suggested their attacks were linked to Iran. The article described his remarks amid escalating Houthi attacks and Saudi responses.
