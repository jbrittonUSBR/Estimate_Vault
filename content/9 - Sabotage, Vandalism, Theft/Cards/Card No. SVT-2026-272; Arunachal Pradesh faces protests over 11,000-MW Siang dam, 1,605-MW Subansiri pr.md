# Arunachal Pradesh faces protests over 11,000-MW Siang dam, 1,605-MW Subansiri project

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/india/arunachal-pradesh-faces-protests-over-11000-mw-siang-dam-1605-mw-subansiri-project/articleshow/135240109.cms)

(U) Arunachal Pradesh, India — According to The Economic Times reporting, protests were spreading in Arunachal Pradesh against large hydropower projects, including the 11,000-MW Siang Upper Multipurpose Project and the 1,605-MW Subansiri Upper Project, over concerns about displacement and environmental impact.
