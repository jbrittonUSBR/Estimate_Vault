(U) Two men were arrested within hours after stealing copper tubing from an outdoor air conditioning unit at St. Theresa Catholic Church in Corpus Christi, causing extensive damage and a freon leak that appeared as smoke. Joshua Serrera, 41, and Rick Gonzalez, 43, were charged with engaging in organized criminal activity after surveillance footage and prior police encounters helped identify them as the suspects. Father Joseph T. Nguyen discovered the theft around 6 a.m., immediately called 911, and confronted the men without fear while on the phone with authorities, later stating he felt reassured by the connection to police. The quick response, aided by U.S. Marshals, led to the suspects’ prompt arrest, highlighting effective coordination between church staff, surveillance, and law enforcement in addressing the copper theft.
