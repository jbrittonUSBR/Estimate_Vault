# Zero-Click Spyware Delivered via Targeted Ads: AdFection

[Source](https://citizenlab.ca/research/how-discover-tech-turned-targeted-ads-into-a-pipeline-for-spyware/)

(U) ONLINE — Citizen Lab researchers reported that Discover Technology used targeted online advertising to deliver spyware in what they named the AdFection campaign. Their investigation described a chain in which ad targeting directed potential victims to malicious content that could enable spyware installation without an ordinary click. The findings were based on Citizen Lab’s technical investigation and attribution; they should be understood as the researchers’ assessment.
