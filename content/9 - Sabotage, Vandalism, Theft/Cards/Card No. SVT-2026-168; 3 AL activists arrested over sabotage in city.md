# 3 AL activists arrested over sabotage in city

[Source](https://www.newagebd.net/post/country/314880/3-al-activists-arrested-over-sabotage-in-city)

(U) DHAKA — Police arrested three activists affiliated with the banned Awami League and its affiliated organizations over an alleged sabotage incident in the capital, New Age reported. The report named two of those held as Sakhawat Hossain Raihan, 32, a former organizing secretary of the banned Chhatra League's Shahjahanpur unit, and Md Saimun, 23, an active Jubo League member. Details of the alleged offense and the third suspect were not fully described in the available text.
