# NATO jets down drone over Lithuania and Russian ship fires flares at Danish helicopter

[Source](https://www.yahoo.com/news/world/articles/nato-jets-down-drone-lithuanian-081724369.html)

(U) VILNIUS — NATO fighter jets shot down a drone over Lithuania overnight, the first such shootdown in the country's airspace, the Associated Press reported. The drone came down near Pratkūnai, about 100 kilometers west of Vilnius near the Kaunas Reservoir, and was believed to have entered from Belarus after spending roughly 30 minutes in Lithuanian airspace. Defense Minister Robertas Kaunas said an explosive device was found in the wreckage and was neutralized. Separately, Denmark said a Russian frigate fired flares at one of its air force helicopters near Gedser; Lithuania summoned Russia's ambassador, and NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte and European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen commented on the incidents.
