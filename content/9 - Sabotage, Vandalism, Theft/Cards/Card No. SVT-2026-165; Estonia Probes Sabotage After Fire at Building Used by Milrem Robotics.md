# Estonia Probes Sabotage After Fire at Building Used by Milrem Robotics

[Source](https://united24media.com/world/estonia-probes-sabotage-after-fire-at-building-used-by-milrem-robotics-22775)

(U) TALLINN, Estonia — Latvian authorities handed three Latvian nationals suspected of setting fire to a Tallinn building used by defense company Milrem Robotics over to Estonia, where a court ordered all three detained, Estonian public broadcaster ERR reported. State Prosecutor Gardi Anderson said the suspects were being held on suspicion of damaging and destroying property and that sabotage remained one of several lines of inquiry, not an established finding. The fire broke out in August on Betooni Street in the Lasnamäe district; no one was injured.
