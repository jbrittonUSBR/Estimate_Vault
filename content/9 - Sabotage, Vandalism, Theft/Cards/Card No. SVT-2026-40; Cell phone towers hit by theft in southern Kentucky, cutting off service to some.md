(U) Thieves in southern Kentucky, particularly in Pulaski County, have been stealing copper wiring and equipment from cell phone towers, causing service disruptions and cutting off emergency 911 access for some residents. The thefts, including damage on Keno Road that affected multiple towers, have left roads and homes without reliable communication, prompting Pulaski County Sheriff’s Sgt. Cody Cundiff to warn of the severe safety risks both to the public—who may be unable to call for help—and to the thieves themselves due to high-voltage hazards. AT&T is actively investigating the incidents and has offered a $10,000 reward for information leading to arrests, while a recent Kentucky law aims to crack down on scrap yards that purchase such stolen materials. Authorities are urging residents to report suspicious activity or tips to the Pulaski County Sheriff’s Office, noting that while not all areas are affected, the targeted vandalism poses a growing threat to critical infrastructure and public safety in the region.
