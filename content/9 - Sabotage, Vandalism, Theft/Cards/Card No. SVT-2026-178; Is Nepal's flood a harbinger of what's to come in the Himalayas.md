# Is Nepal's flood a harbinger of what's to come in the Himalayas?

[Source](https://www.npr.org/2026/09/23/g-s1-144536/floods-climate-nepal-himalayas-dams-hydropower)

(U) RASUWA, Nepal — A glacier and its bedrock spanning more than 3,800 feet collapsed into the Trishuli River valley on August 26, sending 30-foot surges of water, ice and boulders downstream that swept away some 70 bridges, buried 12 hydropower plants and left 1,400 bodies recovered with more than 5,000 people still unaccounted for, according to the Nepali Army. In the NPR analysis, geoscientists and river experts said the disaster has revived questions about whether developers properly assessed seismic, glacial and cumulative risks before building dams in the Himalayas, and critics singled out World Bank financing for the Upper Trishuli-1 project. The piece is reported analysis and commentary rather than a findings document, and the risk characterizations are the views of the experts and advocates quoted.
