(TLP: Green)  An E-ISAC member reported that on Friday, May 8th, an employee reported a break-in at one of their yards. The incident is believed to have occurred between May 7th and 8th between the hours of 1630 and 0600 hours. The perimeter fence was found cut, and several pieces of equipment were tampered with, including Viper switches and capacitor banks, and wire was stolen from the equipment. The estimated value of the equipment and materials affected is approximately $150,000. A report was filed with law enforcement. This is the second reported incident at this location. On Saturday, May 2nd, at approximately 7:20 AM, two individuals gained access to the yard by cutting through the perimeter fence, creating a small entry point. Nothing of value was taken, other than old scrap wire from a junk Viper switch that is scheduled for recycling. There was also no damage identified and nothing else appeared to have been taken. A police report was not filed. Incident Category: Theft; Copper. Asset Affected: Warehouse. Incident State/Province: California . NERC Region: WECC. Geographical Location: Suburban.
