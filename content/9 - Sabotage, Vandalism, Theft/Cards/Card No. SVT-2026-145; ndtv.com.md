# 'Easy' Work Trap: How Russia Recruited Ukrainian Teens for Sabotage

[Source](https://www.ndtv.com/world-news/easy-work-trap-how-russia-recruited-ukrainian-teens-for-sabotage-12075553)

(U) KYIV — Ukrainian authorities report that Russia is recruiting Ukrainian teenagers, largely via Telegram and social media, to carry out sabotage and arson attacks inside Ukraine in exchange for small payments, presenting the work as easy money. At least 13 minors — including five girls — are now serving prison sentences in Ukraine in cases linked to such activities, part of a broader pattern in which more than 1,100 Ukrainians have been accused of pro-Russian activity. Ukrainian officials and independent reporting describe a Kremlin effort to rebuild its operational network inside the country by outsourcing low-level attacks to recruited amateurs and juveniles, including amateur saboteurs and, in some cases, contract hitmen targeting figures such as Ukrainian military recruiters. The practice extends across Europe, with Russian services recruiting ordinary people, often teenagers, through messaging platforms to conduct arson and sabotage against infrastructure and military-linked targets.
