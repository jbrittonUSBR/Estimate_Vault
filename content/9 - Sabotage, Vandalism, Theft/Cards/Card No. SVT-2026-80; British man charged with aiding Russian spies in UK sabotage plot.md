# British man charged with aiding Russian spies in UK sabotage plot

[Source](https://apnews.com/article/russia-britain-sabotage-arrest-cammidge-gru-088f7f415232de874c104a1ce56adf71)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — Prosecutors said a British man, Joshua Cammidge, 31, was charged with engaging in conduct to materially assist a foreign intelligence service in violation of the National Security Act, alleging he helped Russian military spies plan sabotage in the UK. Cammidge was arrested the previous week by counterterrorism police and was in contact with a suspected representative of the GRU Volunteer Corps, a group controlled by Russia's military intelligence agency that the UK deemed a national security threat in July. Authorities did not disclose what act of sabotage he allegedly planned; Security Minister Dan Jarvis said police acted against a potential threat, and Cammidge was scheduled to appear in Westminster Magistrates' Court. Supporters convicted of assisting the group face up to 14 years in prison.
