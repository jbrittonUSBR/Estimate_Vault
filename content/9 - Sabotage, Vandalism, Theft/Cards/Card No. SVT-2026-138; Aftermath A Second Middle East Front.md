# Aftermath: A Second Middle East Front

[Source](https://prospect.org/2026/09/17/aftermath-yemen-houthis-second-middle-east-front/)

(U) WASHINGTON — An analysis by The American Prospect's Nathan Thompson argued that the U.S. is being drawn into a second Middle East front as the Trump administration issued a September 4 FMS notice for over 10,000 bombs and JDAM kits to Saudi Arabia. CNN reported over 100 U.S. advisers in Saudi Arabia as Houthis seized Mokha and Perim Island, and a pipeline strike cut about 4% of global oil supply. With Oman-brokered talks ongoing, the risk of direct U.S. involvement in Yemen is rising.
