# Eels keep dying in hydro dam turbines. A new design could change that

[Source](https://www.cbc.ca/news/climate/what-on-earth-eels-hydro-dams-9.7381506)

(U) St. Lawrence River — CBC reported that American eels migrating downstream faced turbine-related mortality at hydroelectric dams, with one power plant’s survival rate reported at 74 percent. California-based Natel Energy said it had designed a turbine runner intended to reduce fish deaths without sacrificing power-generation efficiency. The article described the design as a proposed solution and noted that eel populations in the area had declined sharply since the 1970s.
