# Regional sediment characterization and curve reconstruction for hydropower infrastructure design and turbine wear management

[Source](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-80372-w.pdf)

(U) Rize, Türkiye — This peer-reviewed technical study examined 53 sediment grain-size curves from regulator sediments at 11 run-of-river hydropower plants to inform turbine-wear management and sampling protocols. The authors found no statistically significant winter-summer difference in sediment composition (p = 0.650), while principal component analysis and clustering identified four descriptive sediment groups. In leave-one-plant-out tests, logarithmic interpolation from five sieve sizes reconstructed missing grain-size values more accurately (R2 = 0.978; MAE = 1.706 percentage points) than the machine-learning models tested, which the authors offered as a basis for preliminary screening.
