# The Cyberattack Strategy of Tension

[Source](https://michelchossudovsky.substack.com/p/cyberattack-strategy-tension)

(U) WASHINGTON — In an opinion essay republished on Michel Chossudovsky's Substack, Kurt Nimmo argued that a recent wave of cyberattacks affecting Microsoft, JPMorgan Chase, the Federal Election Commission, media companies and retailers was a "strategy of tension" intended to frighten the public and build support for war with Iran, rather than the work of Iranian, Chinese or Russian hackers. He cited U.S. government attributions to Iran-affiliated actors and NATO and G7 exercises involving AI cyber operations, and speculated that U.S. intelligence and Israel's Unit 8200 could be responsible for false-flag attacks. Nimmo acknowledged that attribution for such operations was unlikely to ever be known with certainty; the piece was published as opinion.
