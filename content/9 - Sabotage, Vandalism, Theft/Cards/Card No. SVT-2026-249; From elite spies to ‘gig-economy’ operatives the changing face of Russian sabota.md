# From elite spies to ‘gig-economy’ operatives: the changing face of Russian sabotage

[Source](https://theconversation.com/from-elite-spies-to-gig-economy-operatives-the-changing-face-of-russian-sabotage-294285)

(U) Europe — Elena Grossfeld argued in The Conversation that Russian sabotage activity had shifted from elite intelligence operatives toward locally recruited, lower-level “gig-economy” actors. The analysis described recruitment and tasking patterns and their implications for attribution and deterrence; it reflected the author’s expert assessment, not an official intelligence finding.
