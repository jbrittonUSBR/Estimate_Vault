# Google claims it has seen the first fully autonomous cyberattack — and says a 'significant threshold' has been crossed in terms of cybersecurity

[Source](https://www.techradar.com/pro/security/google-says-it-just-saw-the-first-fully-autonomous-cyberattack-and-says-a-significant-threshold-has-been-crossed-in-terms-of-cybersecurity)

(U) United States — In vendor research reported by TechRadar, Google's Threat Intelligence Group and Mandiant said investigators found an unsecured staging server hosting a system called exploit_agent that compared code across patch releases, wrote exploits and launched them. The researchers said scripts on the machine also mapped internal networks, guessed passwords, planted webshells and removed webshells left by rival attackers, with configuration comments in Russian and victim data pointing to French media, entertainment and education sites. Greynoise reported that a campaign exploiting two PaperCut server flaws hit hundreds of organizations days after disclosure.
