# How Sabotage and Failed Scanners Are Turning Ghana into a Cartel Transit Hub

[Source](https://www.modernghana.com/news/1529654/the-port-paralysis-how-sabotage-and-failed-scanne.html)

(U) ACCRA, Ghana — In a Modern Ghana opinion column, columnist Atitso Akpalu argued that insider sabotage and broken cargo scanners had turned Ghana's ports into a transit hub for international drug cartels, citing the seizure of 3.9 tonnes of cocaine at Dunkirk. He said at least ten people, including four Customs Division officers, had been remanded and that containers scanned and flagged as abnormal were cleared anyway. Akpalu called for centralized remote image review, mandatory prosecution of negligent officers and ring-fenced funding for scanner maintenance; the views were his own.
