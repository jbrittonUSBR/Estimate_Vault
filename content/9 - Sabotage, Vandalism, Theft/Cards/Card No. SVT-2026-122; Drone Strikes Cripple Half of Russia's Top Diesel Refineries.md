# Drone Strikes Cripple Half of Russia's Top Diesel Refineries

[Source](https://oilprice.com/Latest-Energy-News/World-News/Drone-Strikes-Cripple-Half-of-Russias-Top-Diesel-Refineries.html)

(U) MOSCOW — Ukrainian drone strikes had shut three of Russia's six largest diesel refineries or left them operating at roughly a quarter of capacity, OilPrice reported, citing Reuters calculations. The Kirishi refinery was offline, while Volgograd and NORSI were running at about 25 percent, and the Taneco plant was struck on Sunday. The six plants account for roughly half of Russia's diesel output, and the report noted that the International Energy Agency recorded a refinery being hit about every three days between January and August 2026. The article also noted Russian restrictions on fuel exports.
