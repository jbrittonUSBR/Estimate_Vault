# German Power Grid Hit Twice in Latest Suspected Sabotage

[Source](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/09/02/883661.htm)

(U) BERLIN, Germany — Germany's power grid was hit by two suspected acts of sabotage on September 2, 2026, according to reporting by Insurance Journal and Bloomberg. In the west, a substation near Rommerskirchen operated by transmission system operator Amprion was targeted, taking five RWE lignite-fired units totaling 4.2 gigawatts offline. In the east, transmission lines were damaged near LEAG's Jänschwalde power plant using homemade projectiles. The resulting supply imbalance pushed intraday electricity prices as high as €5,000 per megawatt-hour, though the grid operator said supply and stability were maintained. Investigators examined both foreign-directed sabotage and far-left extremism.
