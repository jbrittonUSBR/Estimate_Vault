# Iran authorities say one dead after ship struck in Hormuz

[Source](https://www.rte.ie/news/2026/0913/1591336-iran-war/)

(U) STRAIT OF HORMUZ, Iran — Iran said one person was killed and four crew members wounded when an Iranian commercial vessel was struck by a projectile off its coast in the Strait of Hormuz, while the British maritime security agency UKMTO reported a separate vessel hit as it transited the strait, causing a fire and forcing the crew to evacuate. The incidents on September 12-13, 2026, came after drone strikes knocked out Saudi Arabia's 1,200-km East-West pipeline and Yemen's Houthis advanced along the Red Sea coast, threatening further disruption to wartime global energy supplies. Oil prices had surged above $100 a barrel the previous week, and the US retail diesel price hit an all-time high above $6.20 a gallon. Traders and Saudi buyers told Reuters that Riyadh had only five to seven days of stored oil at Yanbu if the pipeline remained shut.
