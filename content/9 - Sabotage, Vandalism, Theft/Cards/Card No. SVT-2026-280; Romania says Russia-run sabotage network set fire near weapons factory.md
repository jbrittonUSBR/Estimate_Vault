# Romania says Russia-run sabotage network set fire near weapons factory

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/27/romania-says-russia-run-sabotage-network-set-fire-near-weapons-factory)

(U) Brasov, Romania — Al Jazeera reported that Romanian authorities said a Russia-coordinated network set a 14 September fire near a state-owned weapons factory. Prosecutors said three people were involved; two were remanded in Germany and one remained at large in Poland. Russia had not commented in the report.
