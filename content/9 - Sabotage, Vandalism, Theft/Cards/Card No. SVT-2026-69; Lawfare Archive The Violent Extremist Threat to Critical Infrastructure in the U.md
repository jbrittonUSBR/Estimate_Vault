# Lawfare Archive: The Violent Extremist Threat to Critical Infrastructure in the United States

[Source](https://shows.acast.com/lawfare/episodes/lawfare-archive-the-violent-extremist-threat-to-critical-inf)

(U) — The Lawfare Podcast republished an archived October 24, 2022, episode in which managing editor Tyler McBrien discussed a George Washington University Program on Extremism report titled "Mayhem, Murder, and Misdirection: Violent Extremist Attack Plots Against Critical Infrastructure in the United States." McBrien spoke with program research fellow Ilana Krill and deputy director Seamus Hughes about the white supremacists and Salafi-jihadists behind such movements, the encrypted channels through which propaganda and plans spread, and what should be done to protect critical infrastructure in the United States.
