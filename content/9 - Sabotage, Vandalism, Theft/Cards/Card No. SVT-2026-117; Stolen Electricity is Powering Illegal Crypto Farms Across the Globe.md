# Stolen Electricity is Powering Illegal Crypto Farms Across the Globe

[Source](https://oilprice.com/Energy/Energy-General/Stolen-Electricity-is-Powering-Illegal-Crypto-Farms-Across-the-Globe.html)

(U) PUEBLA, Mexico — In an OilPrice.com roundup, Haley Zaremba cited Mexico's September 2026 raid on a 300-computer crypto mining farm in Puebla, which allegedly drew power from a federal hydroelectric complex, as one example of a global pattern of electricity theft to fuel cryptocurrency mining. The article reported that Mexican authorities logged about $817 million in electricity-theft losses in the January-to-July 2024 period alone, and that Malaysian police had uncovered some 14,000 illegal Bitcoin mining sites since 2020 with an estimated $1.1 billion in stolen power. It further noted that from Cambodia to Kyrgyzstan, illegal mining operations were increasingly tied to money laundering, forced labour, and organized crime.
