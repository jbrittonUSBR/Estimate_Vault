# NYPD Seeks Suspected Church Vandal in Brooklyn

[Source](https://brooklyneagle.com/416593/nypd-seeks-suspected-church-vandal/)

(U) NEW YORK — The NYPD is seeking the public's assistance in identifying a suspect who removed and vandalized a light fixture from the entranceway of a church on Summit Street in Brooklyn, releasing surveillance video of the man wanted in connection with the incident. The vandalism is the latest in a series of recent attacks on Brooklyn houses of worship, including damage to statues and glass at multiple churches that have drawn heightened police attention and patrols requested by the Diocese of Brooklyn. In a separate case, a suspect was arrested after throwing a Molotov cocktail at the Iglesia Bautista El Mesías church in Queens on July 8.
