# Finland’s Stubb warns Europe not to ‘overreact’ to Russian threats and sabotage

[Source](https://www.politico.eu/article/finland-alexander-stubb-warns-europe-overreaction-russia-threats-sabotage/)

(U) NEW YORK — Finnish President Alexander Stubb warned European partners not to "overreact" to Russian hybrid and sabotage attacks, telling POLITICO that Moscow aims to scare Europe into slowing support for Ukraine and that any overreaction amounts to a Russian win. Speaking on the sidelines of the U.N. General Assembly, Stubb said Russia lacks the interest and capacity for full-scale escalation with NATO while acknowledging Europe must prepare for continued attacks. He praised Germany's public attribution of the Leipzig drone incident to Russia as the right response.
