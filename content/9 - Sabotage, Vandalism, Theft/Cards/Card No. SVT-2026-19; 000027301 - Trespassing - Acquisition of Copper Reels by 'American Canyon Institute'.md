(TLP: Green) An E-ISAC member reported that on June 12th at 0830 hours, a Garage Crew Lead observed three males, estimated to be in their 40s-50s and possibly of East Asian or Middle Eastern descent, near an onsite building loading copper reels onto a trailer. When approached, the individuals claimed affiliation with a non-profit organization identified as American Canyon Institute (“American Indigenous / American Indian”) but declined to provide identification when requested. Upon inquiry regarding their entry to the site, they alleged that security granted them access and referenced a previous visit three years prior, during which employees had reportedly assisted with electrical wire for American Indian schools (this claim remains unverified). After being informed that removal of materials was not permitted, site crew members reclaimed the copper reels. Security was notified and subsequently escorted the individuals from the premises after all materials were returned. The individuals arrived in a white Chevrolet dually truck with a black trailer, bearing Kansas Plate LIS # E255636, dated 07/26/2026, and VIN: 1GC4KVEY5TF248351. It was later established that these individuals falsely asserted they had site approval and proceeded to the materials yard under false pretenses. They attempted to remove full reels of underground aluminum and two full reels of copper.
