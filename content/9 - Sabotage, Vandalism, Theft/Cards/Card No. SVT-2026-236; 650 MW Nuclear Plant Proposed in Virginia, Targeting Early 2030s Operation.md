# Developer proposed evaluating 650 MW nuclear project in Virginia

[Source](https://www.naturalnews.com/2026-10-19-nuclear-plant-proposed-virginia-targeting-2030-operation.html)

(U) Amherst County, Virginia — Natural News reported that developer Geenex Power planned to evaluate a proposed advanced nuclear project with capacity of up to 650 megawatts, with operation targeted for the early 2030s. The report said the project could create more than 200 permanent jobs, citing the company and local reporting; the facility remained a proposal, not an approved or operating plant.
