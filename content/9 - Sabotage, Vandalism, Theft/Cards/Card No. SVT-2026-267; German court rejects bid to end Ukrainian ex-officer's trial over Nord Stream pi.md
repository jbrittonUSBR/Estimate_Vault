# German court rejects bid to end Ukrainian ex-officer's trial over Nord Stream pipeline blasts

[Source](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/german-court-rejects-bid-end-ukrainian-officers-trial-137598323)

(U) Hamburg, Germany — A German court rejected a defense request to end the trial of former Ukrainian officer Serhii Kuznietsov, who was accused of involvement in the 2022 Nord Stream pipeline explosions, the Associated Press reported. The defense argued his military service restricted his ability to testify; the court found that this did not limit his defense under German law. The charges remained allegations.
