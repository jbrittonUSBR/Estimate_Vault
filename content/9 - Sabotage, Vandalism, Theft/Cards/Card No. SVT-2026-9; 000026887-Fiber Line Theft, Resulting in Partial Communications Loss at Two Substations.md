(TLP: Clear) On May 9th, an investigation into a partial communications loss at two substations revealed that the disruption was due to the severing of communications on a leased fiber path. An unidentified individual accessed a fiber vault outside one of the impacted substations and removed approximately 25 feet of fiber, resulting in this incident. While the act was intentional, the motive remains unclear; however, based on the amount of fiber removed, theft is considered more probable than deliberate sabotage. Notably, there were no customer outages as a result of the event. Communications were swiftly restored by rerouting the path, and arrangements for permanent repairs are underway. Local law enforcement has been notified. Incident Type: Theft / Other. Asset Affected: Communication. Incident State/Province: Wisconsin. NERC Region: MRO. 
Geographical Location: Urban.
