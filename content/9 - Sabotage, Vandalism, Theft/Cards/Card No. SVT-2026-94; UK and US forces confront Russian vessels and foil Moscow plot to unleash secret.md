# UK and US forces confront Russian vessels and foil Moscow plot to unleash secret new weapon to disable undersea cables

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16119893/UK-US-forces-confront-Russian-vessels-foil-Moscow-plot-unleash-secret-new-weapon-disable-undersea-cables.html)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — According to two Western officials speaking on condition of anonymity and cited by Reuters, British, Norwegian, and U.S. forces tracked and confronted Russian vessels in Arctic waters near the Svalbard archipelago in spring 2026, preventing Russia's GUGI deep-sea warfare directorate from completing an exercise with deep-sea submersibles and a secret technology designed to disable undersea cables without leaving attribution. No cables were damaged and the activity simulated deployment of the capability in a conflict with NATO, the officials said, adding that London and Oslo later presented their findings to Moscow to deter its use. The disclosure was the first public account of U.S. involvement in the confrontation and of the new weapon and its location; Russia's Defence Ministry did not respond to a request for comment, and the Kremlin has consistently denied conducting sabotage in NATO countries.
