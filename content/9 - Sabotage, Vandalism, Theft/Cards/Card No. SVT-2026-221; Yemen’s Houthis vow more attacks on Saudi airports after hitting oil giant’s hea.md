# Yemen’s Houthis vow more attacks on Saudi airports after hitting oil giant’s headquarters

[Source](https://www.irishtimes.com/world/middle-east/2026/10/14/yemens-houthis-vow-more-attacks-on-saudi-airports-after-hitting-oil-giants-headquarters/)

(U) SAUDI ARABIA — Reuters reported that Yemen’s Houthi movement threatened further attacks on Saudi airports and oil facilities after a strike hit a Saudi Aramco complex in Dhahran. Sources told Reuters the attack caused a fire that spread across several floors, while the Houthis described Saudi targets as legitimate and framed their campaign as retaliation for Saudi strikes in Yemen. The report noted that Riyadh’s airport had faced repeated attacks and flights had been disrupted.
