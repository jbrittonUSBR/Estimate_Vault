# Iraq seizes drone-launching platform used for targeting Saudi oil pipeline - Al Jazeera

[Source](https://slashdot.org/firehose.pl?op=view&amp;id=185632286)

(U) BAGHDAD, Iraq — Iraqi authorities seized a drone-launching platform used to target a Saudi oil pipeline, according to an Al Jazeera report surfaced via a Slashdot Firehose aggregation post. The seizure followed drone strikes on Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline that Iraq linked to launch sites on its territory.
