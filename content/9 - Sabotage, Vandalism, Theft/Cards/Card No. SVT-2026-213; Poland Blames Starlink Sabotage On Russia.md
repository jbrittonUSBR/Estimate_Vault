# Poland Blames Starlink Sabotage On Russia

[Source](https://www.huffpost.com/entry/poland-blames-starlink-sabotage-on-russia_n_6ace11c4e4b0649434775c30)

(U) Warsaw, Poland — On October 13, 2026, Polish officials blamed Russia for an alleged sabotage incident targeting Starlink satellite communications systems used in Poland. The accusation pointed to Russian actors as responsible for disrupting the service, though details of the incident and attributed impact remained limited in initial reporting.
