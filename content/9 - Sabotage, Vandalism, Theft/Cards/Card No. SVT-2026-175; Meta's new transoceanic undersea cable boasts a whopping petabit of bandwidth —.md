# Meta's new transoceanic undersea cable boasts a whopping petabit of bandwidth — Petal link between US and France will be twice as fast as the last

[Source](https://www.tomshardware.com/networking/metas-new-transoceanic-undersea-cable-boasts-a-whopping-petabit-of-bandwidth-petal-link-between-us-and-france-will-be-twice-as-fast-as-the-last)

(U) Menlo Park, California — Meta announced on September 23, 2026 its new transoceanic undersea cable project, dubbed "Petal," connecting the United States and France with capacity of a whopping petabit per second, twice as fast as its previous cable. The high-capacity fiber optic link is designed to meet growing data demands for Meta's services and represents a significant expansion of global internet infrastructure.
