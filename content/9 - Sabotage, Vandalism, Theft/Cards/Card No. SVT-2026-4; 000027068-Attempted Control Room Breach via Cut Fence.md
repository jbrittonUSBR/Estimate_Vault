(TLP: GREEN) On Thursday, May 21, an employee conducting a routine inspection identified a cut in the fence on the south side of the perimeter barrier, which was at the same location as a previous breach reported in early January. During the inspection of the elevated building, noticeable pry marks were observed on the battery room door. Minor marks found on the control room door appeared to be from an earlier incident. Both doors remained secure and could not be accessed without a key. It was subsequently confirmed that two SCADA alarms had been activated earlier that morning at 0444 and 0450 hours. The alarm for the battery room door was triggered due to a significant gap between the door and its frame. No interior damage to the control or battery rooms was found, and no evidence of unauthorized entry was determined. The Sheriff’s Office was notified of the incident for situational awareness, but no report was taken. Incident Type: Intrusion / Attempted. Asset Affected: Control Center. Incident State/Province: California. NERC Region: WECC. Geographical Location: Rural.
