# UK warns British firms supporting Ukraine of potential Russian sabotage attacks

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/europe/uk-warns-british-firms-supporting-ukraine-of-potential-russian-sabotage-attacks/articleshow/136133842.cms)

(U) LONDON — Britain's National Protective Security Authority, part of MI5, and the Ministry of Defence warned on November 24 that companies providing material support to Ukraine's armed forces could be targeted by Russian sabotage, according to The Times of India. The alert said Russia had increased attempts to sabotage European defence-related facilities in recent months and that the threat also included intimidation, espionage and information operations aimed at eroding support for Ukraine. Officials advised firms to limit online exposure, strengthen facility security, identify potential hostile observation points and train employees to spot suspicious activity.
