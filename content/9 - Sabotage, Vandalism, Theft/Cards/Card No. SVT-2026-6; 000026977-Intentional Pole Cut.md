(TLP: Green) An E-ISAC member reported that on Monday, May 18, at 0800 hours, employees located a damaged pole while conducting an outage patrol. Upon arrival at the location, they discovered that a utility pole had been cut through and was already lying on the ground. The location of the incident occurred on the property of a vacant home. No suspicious people or activity was observed in the area at the time of discovery. No outages were reported because of vandalism. The incident is believed to be intentionally done, as the cut to the pole was a “V” cut, a method that greatly reduces the integrity of the pole, particularly when the wind picks up, knocking it over. A similar incident occurred in the same area in late December 2023. A reciprocating saw (Sawzall) was used, which resulted in power outage. Incident Type: Vandalism; Property Damage. Asset Affected: Distribution. Incident State: California. NERC Region: WECC. Geographical Location: Rural. Result in customer outages? No.
