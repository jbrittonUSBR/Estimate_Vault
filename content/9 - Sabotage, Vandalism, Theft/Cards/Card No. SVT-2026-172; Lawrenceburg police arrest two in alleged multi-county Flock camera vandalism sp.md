# Lawrenceburg police arrest two in alleged multi-county Flock camera vandalism spree

[Source](https://www.lex18.com/news/covering-kentucky/lawrenceburg-police-arrest-two-in-alleged-multi-county-flock-camera-vandalism-spree)

(U) LAWRENCEBURG, Ky. — Lawrenceburg police arrested two Anderson County residents, 44-year-old Jim Thacker and 18-year-old Jovanna Grobe, in an investigation into the destruction and theft of Flock cameras across several central Kentucky counties. A witness told Versailles police that a woman got out of a black truck and shot a Flock camera with a shotgun on Kentucky 33 in Woodford County; officers later found the truck near Pumphouse Road in Lawrenceburg. Police recovered two shotguns and numerous boxes of ammunition and said additional charges could follow in other jurisdictions.
