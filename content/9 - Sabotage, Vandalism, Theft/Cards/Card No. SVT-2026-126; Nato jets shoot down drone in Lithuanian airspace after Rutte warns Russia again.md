# Nato jets shoot down drone in Lithuanian airspace after Rutte warns Russia against strikes near alliance territory

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/europe/nato-jets-shoot-down-drone-in-lithuanian-airspace-after-rutte-warns-russia-against-strikes-near-alliance-territory/articleshow/134257796.cms)

(U) VILNIUS — Lithuanian Air Force commander Antanas Matutis said wreckage from a drone shot down over Lithuania had been recovered and that the aircraft was "likely" carrying explosives, The Times of India reported. NATO fighter jets downed the drone in Lithuanian airspace, and NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte, speaking at NATO headquarters in Brussels on Monday, warned Russia against strikes near alliance territory. The report also noted Russian attacks on Kyiv and Taganrog and said Ukrainian forces had downed 222 Russian drones.
