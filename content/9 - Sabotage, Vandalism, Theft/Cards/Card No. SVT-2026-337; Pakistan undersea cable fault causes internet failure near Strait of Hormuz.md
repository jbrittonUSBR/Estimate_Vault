# Pakistan undersea cable fault causes internet failure near Strait of Hormuz

[Source](https://www.tomshardware.com/networking/pakistan-undersea-cable-fault-causes-internet-failure-near-strait-of-hormuz-fault-detected-offshore-weeks-after-iran-threatened-infrastructure-in-the-region)

(U) Offshore Pakistan, Gulf of Oman — Pakistan’s telecommunications authority attributed internet connectivity problems to a fault in the SEA-ME-WE 5 submarine cable, Tom’s Hardware reported. Transworld Associates was investigating the cause and repair timing, and traffic had been rerouted; the article did not establish a cause for the cable fault.
