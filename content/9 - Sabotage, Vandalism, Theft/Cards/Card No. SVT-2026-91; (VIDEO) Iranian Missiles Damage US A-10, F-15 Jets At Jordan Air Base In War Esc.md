# (VIDEO) Iranian Missiles Damage US A-10, F-15 Jets At Jordan Air Base In War Escalation After Tanker Strikes

[Source](https://www.ibtimes.com.au/iranian-missiles-damage-us-aircraft-jordan-1875315)

(U) AMMAN, Jordan — Iranian ballistic missiles damaged multiple U.S. military aircraft at a key air base in Jordan, marking an escalation in the conflict between the United States and Iran, IBTimes AU reported. An A-10 Thunderbolt II suffered severe damage to one wing and about eight F-15E Strike Eagles sustained lighter damage but remained serviceable, according to people familiar with the matter cited by Air & Space Forces Magazine, with the damage first reported by CBS News. The strikes hit Muwaffaq Salti Air Base in Zarqa governorate, with the Jordanian military saying it detected 20 ballistic missiles and intercepted or destroyed 18, and no U.S. casualties were reported. The report attributes some claims to Iranian state media and notes other assertions were not independently verified.
