# US Congressman Krishnamoorthi’s office receives suspicious package as FBI probes nationwide mail threats

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/us/us-congressmans-krishnamoorthis-office-receives-suspicious-package-as-fbi-probes-nationwide-mail-threats/articleshow/135022675.cms)

(U) Schaumburg, Illinois — A suspicious package containing unknown substances was delivered to Representative Raja Krishnamoorthi’s district office, prompting a response by local emergency services and federal law enforcement. Krishnamoorthi said in a social-media post that the package had been safely removed. The Times of India reported the incident occurred amid an FBI investigation into similar mailings to congressional offices and election officials nationwide.
