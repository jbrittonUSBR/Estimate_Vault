# Vandalism in varsities not Punjab’s character: Gargajj

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/chandigarh/vandalism-in-varsities-not-punjabs-character-gargajj/articleshow/134571685.cms)

(U) CHANDIGARH, India — Giani Kuldeep Singh Gargajj, the officiating jathedar of the Akal Takht, condemned recent violence and vandalism at two universities in Punjab as alien to the state's character and called for an investigation into who was responsible. The remarks, reported September 29, 2026, followed unrest rooted in student protests, including disturbances at Lovely Professional University in Jalandhar. Gargajj said the incidents had damaged Punjab's reputation and urged authorities to identify and punish those behind them. The statement carried weight given the Akal Takht's moral authority among Sikhs.
