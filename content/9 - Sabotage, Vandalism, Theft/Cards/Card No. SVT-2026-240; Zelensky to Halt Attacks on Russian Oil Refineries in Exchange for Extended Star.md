# Natural News reported Zelensky was open to pause in refinery strikes for Starlink coverage

[Source](https://www.naturalnews.com/2026-10-20-zelensky-to-halt-attacks-for-extended-starlink-coverage.html)

(U) October 20, 2026 — Natural News reported, citing a Wall Street Journal account, that Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy was open to a proposed 100-day pause in Ukrainian strikes on Russian oil refineries in exchange for extending Starlink coverage about 300 kilometers into Russia. The report described a proposal conveyed by US lawmakers, not a concluded agreement or an implemented halt to attacks.
