# From campus radicals to Beijing's AI sabotage: Rep Randy Fine says 'US fighting to save Western civilization'

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/campus-radicals-beijings-ai-sabotage-rep-randy-fine-says-us-fighting-save-western-civilization)

(U) WASHINGTON — In an interview with Fox News Digital published September 22, Rep. Randy Fine, R-Fla., argued that campus protests, opposition to AI data centers and antisemitism were tied to foreign influence operations by Iran and China, and framed the midterms as an existential contest for Western civilization. Fine, a staunch Trump ally, called for confronting pro-Palestinian campus activism and warned that China benefits when the United States blocks data-center construction; the characterizations were his own.
