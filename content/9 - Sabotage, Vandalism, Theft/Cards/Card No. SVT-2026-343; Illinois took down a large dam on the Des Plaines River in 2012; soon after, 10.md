# Illinois took down a large dam on the Des Plaines River in 2012; soon after, 10 fish species not recorded before appeared in the old pool, including channel catfish

[Source](https://m.economictimes.com/news/international/us/illinois-took-down-a-large-dam-on-the-des-plaines-river-in-2012-soon-after-10-fish-species-not-recorded-before-appeared-in-the-old-pool-including-channel-catfish/articleshow/136051186.cms)

(U) Cook County, Illinois — The Economic Times reported that the Hofmann Dam near Riverside and Lyons was breached in June 2012 by Cook County, the US Army Corps of Engineers and the Illinois Department of Natural Resources after it was judged to block fish movement, degrade water quality and no longer serve its original purpose. Fisheries specialist Stephen Pescitelli told a 2014 Illinois-Indiana Sea Grant workshop that surveys found 10 species in the former pool not previously recorded there, including channel catfish upstream of the dam for the first time. The article said the findings showed renewed fish passage and early habitat recovery, not a full ecological recovery or proof that dam removal alone caused every change.
