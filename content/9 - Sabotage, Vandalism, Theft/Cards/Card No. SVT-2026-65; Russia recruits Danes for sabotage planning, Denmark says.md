# Russia recruits Danes for sabotage planning, Denmark says

[Source](https://uk.news.yahoo.com/russia-recruits-danes-sabotage-planning-072516888.html)

(U) COPENHAGEN, Denmark — On September 3, 2026, Denmark's domestic intelligence service, PET, said Russia is recruiting Danish citizens to help plan sabotage against Danish defence companies with ties to Ukraine, according to Reuters. Emil Gresholm, head of PET's counter-espionage division, said the recruitment was taking place largely through social media and online gaming platforms, with recruits reportedly tasked with gathering photographs of firms and infrastructure. Russia's ambassador to Denmark, Vladimir Barbin, denied the accusation and said no evidence had been presented. Moscow has consistently denied engaging in hybrid warfare against European states.
