# UK and Germany launch joint-partnership to counter sabotage and cyber attacks

[Source](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-and-germany-launch-joint-partnership-to-counter-sabotage-and-cyber-attacks)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — The UK and German governments announced a new joint partnership to strengthen cooperation on countering sabotage, hybrid threats, and cyber attacks. The initiative, outlined by the Prime Minister's Office, aims to improve intelligence sharing, resilience, and coordinated response measures between the two NATO allies in the face of rising state-sponsored interference.
