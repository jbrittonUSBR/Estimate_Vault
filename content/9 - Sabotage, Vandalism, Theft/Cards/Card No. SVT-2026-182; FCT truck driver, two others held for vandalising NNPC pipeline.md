# FCT truck driver, two others held for vandalising NNPC pipeline

[Source](https://punchng.com/fct-truck-driver-two-others-held-for-vandalising-nnpc-pipeline/)

(U) DAFA, Kwali Area Council — The NSCDC FCT Command arrested three suspects, including a truck driver, over the alleged vandalism of an NNPC petroleum pipeline, recovering a loaded truck and 17 pieces of cut pipe; operatives said others linked to the network remained at large as investigations continued.
