# Russian Spies in Italy Sought Belarusians to Carry Out Sabotage — Media

[Source](https://reform.news/en/russian-spies-in-italy-sought-belarusians-to-carry-out-sabotage-media/)

(U) ROME, Italy — REFORM.news, citing the Italian outlet Il Riformista, reported that a network of Russian agents operating from Russia's embassies in Italy and at the Vatican had been uncovered, and that operatives sought Belarusian citizens who could be used to carry out sabotage against military or civilian infrastructure in Europe. The report said Russia's naval attaché in Rome and his deputy, both linked to the GRU, were expelled from Italy on July 9, 2026, after a Rome prosecutor's investigation into the transfer of confidential information, and that two former Italian intelligence officers were arrested and accused of passing classified material to Russia. It added that the network systematically collected information on vulnerabilities in digital infrastructure and that Italy appeared to have served as a transit hub for Russian and Belarusian citizens travelling to other European countries. The account rests on Il Riformista's reporting and was not independently corroborated in the REFORM.news article.
