(U) A series of intentional fiber-optic cable cuts caused widespread Verizon mobile and home internet outages across parts of Southern California, leaving thousands of users temporarily disconnected.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Incident:** Vandals intentionally cut multiple fiber-optic cables, triggering significant service disruptions for wireless and internet customers throughout the region, with heavy impacts reported in areas like the San Fernando Valley.
- **Scale of the Outage:** At the height of the disruption on Sunday afternoon, tracking platforms like Downdetector logged more than 12,000 user reports of lost cell signals and connection issues.
- **Rapid Repairs:** Verizon repair crews mobilized quickly to fix the damaged infrastructure, successfully restoring full mobile and internet service to affected customers by Sunday evening.
- **Security and Public Safety Concerns:** Verizon strongly condemned the acts, noting that cutting critical infrastructure is not just a property crime, but a dangerous disruption that risks cutting off vital lifelines for emergency responders and public safety officials. Law enforcement was notified to investigate the incidents.
