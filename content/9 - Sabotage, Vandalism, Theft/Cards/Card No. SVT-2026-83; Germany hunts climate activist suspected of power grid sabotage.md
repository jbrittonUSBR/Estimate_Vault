# Germany hunts climate activist suspected of power grid sabotage

[Source](https://www.thelocal.de/20260905/germany-hunts-climate-activist-suspected-of-power-grid-sabotage)

(U) BERLIN, Germany — German authorities were searching on September 5, 2026 for a militant climate activist suspected of a spate of attacks on the power grid, the latest sabotage targeting critical infrastructure. The 48-year-old man, named in German media as Daniel V., sent letters claiming responsibility, and investigators found "suspicious substances and suspected explosive materials or devices" during a search of his apartment in Gevelsberg, North Rhine-Westphalia. The attacks followed a common pattern, with firework-like devices used to shoot wires at overhead cables at substations to cause short circuits and cut power from nearby coal-fired plants; units at some plants were taken off the grid, though there were no major outages. Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt said authorities "must assume that this is a case of climate extremism," while North Rhine-Westphalia interior minister Herbert Reul stressed that "attacking critical infrastructure is a crime" with "no justification."
