# How did supermarket fridges become a target for hackers?

[Source](https://www.rte.ie/news/primetime/2026/1105/1598007-clarity-supermarket-cyberattack/)

(U) Europe — RTÉ reported that cyberattacks on refrigeration controls affected supermarkets in eight European countries, including 13 SuperValu stores in Ireland, where chilled and frozen food was removed from sale. The Irish incident was resolved the same evening; experts and authorities cited insecure remote access and known software weaknesses as possible exposure points, while responsibility for the attacks remained unconfirmed.
