# Schneider Electric SCADAPack x70 Products

[Source](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-26-258-04)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — CISA published an ICS advisory on September 15, 2026, warning of an insufficiently protected credentials vulnerability (CVE-2026-81861) affecting Schneider Electric's SCADAPack x70 products. The affected devices — SCADAPack 47x, 47xi, 47xd, 470R, 57x, 3xx, and 32 — are Remote Terminal Units used for remote monitoring and control in the critical manufacturing and energy sectors worldwide, and the flaw could allow unauthorized access to RTU configuration through the Secure Lock functionality. The vulnerability was assigned a CVSS v3 base score of 6.5 (MEDIUM) and was reported to CISA by Abhinav Agarwal. Schneider Electric recommended implementing Role-Based Access Control in place of the legacy Secure Lock feature, along with network segmentation and enabling the RTU firewall service as mitigations.
