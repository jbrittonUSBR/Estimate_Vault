# Iran rejects CNN report of 'dirty bomb' plot in Washington, calls claims 'fabricated'

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/middle-east/iran-rejects-cnn-report-of-dirty-bomb-plot-in-washington-calls-claims-fabricated/articleshow/135928096.cms)

(U) Tehran, Iran — Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei said on X that CNN and New York Times reports about an alleged dirty-bomb plot in Washington and possible missile facilities beneath dams were fabricated and intended to lay media groundwork for further military action. CNN had cited seven US officials saying Iran explored options to target senior Trump administration officials with a radioactive or chemical weapon, but those officials were divided on Tehran's capability and intent and the White House said there was no credible immediate threat. Baghaei also cited strikes on reservoirs in Khomeyn, Haftkel and Bamani and desalination facilities on Qeshm Island and elsewhere, arguing that civilian infrastructure near military sites is not a legitimate target under Article 56 of Additional Protocol I.
