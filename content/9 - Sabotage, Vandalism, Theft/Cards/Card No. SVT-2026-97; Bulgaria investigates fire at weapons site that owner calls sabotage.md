# Bulgaria investigates fire at weapons site that owner calls sabotage

[Source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cd94kj17zvwo)

(U) SOFIA, Bulgaria — Bulgaria investigated the cause of an overnight fire at an ammunition storage site run by private defense company EMCO near the village of Gorni Varpishta in central Bulgaria, the BBC reported. Interior Minister Ivan Demerdzhiev said authorities were "not ruling out outside interference," and no one was injured in the blaze, which triggered a series of explosions. EMCO, whose weapons are used by Ukraine, called the fire sabotage and said human error was "completely ruled out," following a similar fire and blasts at another EMCO warehouse in the area the previous month.
