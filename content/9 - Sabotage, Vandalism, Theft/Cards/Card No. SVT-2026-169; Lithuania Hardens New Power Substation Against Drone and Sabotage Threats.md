# Lithuania Hardens New Power Substation Against Drone and Sabotage Threats

[Source](https://www.arise.tv/lithuania-hardens-new-power-substation-against-drone-and-sabotage-threats/)

(U) VILNIUS, Lithuania — Lithuania is building the Gižai substation about 30 kilometers from Russia's Kaliningrad exclave with defenses against drones, explosives and sabotage, including reinforced fencing, tunnel sensors and concrete sarcophagi around transformers, Litgrid CEO Andrius Šemeškevičius said. The facility will be the main hub of the Harmony Link interconnection with Poland, and designers say a single Shahed strike should affect only limited equipment. The added security is expected to cost at least €48 million, and Energy Minister Lukas Savickas said Lithuania would ask the EU to cover at least half.
