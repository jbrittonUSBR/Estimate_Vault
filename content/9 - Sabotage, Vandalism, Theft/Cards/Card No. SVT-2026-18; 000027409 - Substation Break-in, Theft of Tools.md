(TLP: Green) On 6/16/2026, crews returned to a rural substation expansion construction site and found that the perimeter fence had been cut. A subsequent site inspection determined that unknown individual(s) forcibly entered two secured construction trailers within the substation perimeter and removed various tools and equipment. Substation operations were not affected. Local law enforcement responded, opened an investigation, and is conducting additional patrols in the area. The perimeter fence was repaired, and the substation was re-secured.
