# Field Report: Russia Strikes Kyiv, Odesa, and American-Owned Coca-Cola Plant in Overnight Barrage

[Source](https://www.thegatewaypundit.com/2026/09/field-report-russia-strikes-kyiv-odesa-american-owned/)

(U) Kyiv, Ukraine — Russia launched a night of drone and missile strikes across Ukraine on September 3, damaging critical infrastructure, an American-owned beverage plant and residential buildings from Kyiv to Odesa, according to a field report. A Russian drone struck a Coca-Cola Beverages Ukraine production facility in Velyka Dymerka near Kyiv, sparking a large fire; the company said all employees were safe. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said the barrage caused destruction in thirteen oblasts in a single night, and regional officials reported casualties including civilians wounded in Odesa, Kharkiv and Kherson, and one employee killed at a Nova Poshta logistics depot in Dnipro.
