# Iran Denies Role In Saudi Pipeline Attack

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/saudi/2026/saudi-260914-rferl01.htm)

(U) TEHRAN, Iran — Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmail Baqaei rejected suggestions that Tehran had prior knowledge of or played any role in the attack on Saudi Arabia's East-West oil pipeline, calling the accusation a "lie" and saying the Americans were "producing fake news at no cost whatsoever." Speaking at his weekly press briefing on September 14, Baqaei said actors outside the Middle East were seeking to increase tensions among regional states. The pipeline was struck by drones on September 10 and Saudi Arabia announced its temporary shutdown the next day; President Donald Trump named Iran on September 12 as the likely party responsible, while Riyadh said the drone was launched from Iraqi territory and no group had claimed responsibility.
