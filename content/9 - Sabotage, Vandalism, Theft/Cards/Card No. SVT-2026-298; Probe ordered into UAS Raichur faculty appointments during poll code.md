# Probe ordered into UAS Raichur faculty appointments during poll code

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/hubballi/probe-ordered-into-uas-raichur-faculty-appointments-during-poll-code/articleshow/135582257.cms)

(U) Raichur, Karnataka, India — The Times of India reported that the district deputy commissioner appointed a team to investigate allegations that the University of Agricultural Sciences approved professor appointments while an election code of conduct was in force. The inquiry was directed to review recruitment records and procedures; the claims were made in a complaint and had not yet been adjudicated.
