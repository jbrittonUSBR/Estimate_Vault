# Ukraine strikes 'one of the largest oil refineries' in southern Russia ahead of Putin-Trump meeting in China

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/world/ukraine-strikes-one-largest-oil-refineries-southern-russia-ahead-putin-trump-meeting-china)

(U) Krasnodar Krai, Russia — Ukraine’s military said it struck the Ilsky and Afipsky oil refineries, reporting explosions and damage to storage tanks at Ilsky and an oil-processing unit at Afipsky, Fox News reported. Ukraine’s General Staff said the facilities supplied fuel to Russian forces; the report attributed the claims to Ukrainian officials and did not independently confirm the damage.
