# Arrest warrants issued against four in Muzaffarpur gas pipeline blast case

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/patna/arrest-warrants-issued-against-four-in-muzaffarpur-gas-pipeline-blast-case/articleshow/136055210.cms)

(U) Muzaffarpur, India — A Chief Judicial Magistrate court issued arrest warrants for four people accused in the November 2 explosion on Gobarsahi-Dumri Road, which killed a scooter rider identified as Srinivas Singh after an earth-moving machine damaged an Indian Oil Corporation PNG pipeline during drain excavation. Sadar police station chief Navlesh Azad told The Times of India that investigators found evidence of ignored safety norms and negligence and that the four had been evading arrest. The victim's wife, Nibha Kumari, filed an FIR on November 7 alleging that officials failed to act after the leak; police said they would seek court permission to add negligence and attempted culpable homicide charges.
