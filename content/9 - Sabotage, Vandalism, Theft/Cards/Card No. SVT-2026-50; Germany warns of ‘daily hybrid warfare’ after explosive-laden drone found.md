(U) Following a suspected attack involving an explosive-laden drone at Leipzig/Halle Airport, German officials warned that the country is facing a constant, daily barrage of foreign-backed hybrid warfare.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Catalyst Incident:** The warning followed the discovery of a drone equipped with an explosive device near a runway at Leipzig/Halle Airport. Law enforcement used an explosives disposal robot to safely neutralize the device, with officials calling the security breach a "new quality of danger".
- **Daily Hybrid Threats:** German Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt stated in an interview that while the nation is not officially at war, it is a daily target for foreign powers seeking to instill fear and destabilize the country politically and socially.
- **Scope of Operations:** Dobrindt emphasized that espionage, sabotage, cyberattacks, and covert operations have become a "constant reality".
- **Broader Context and Suspicions:** While authorities investigate the Leipzig incident and additional drone sightings near military sites, various lawmakers have pointed toward Russia—which has been engaged in the war in Ukraine—though investigations remain ongoing.
