# What Does the Yanbu Outage Mean?

[Source](https://www.rigzone.com/news/what_does_the_yanbu_outage_mean-15-sep-2026-184616-article/)

(U) RIYADH — Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Energy said the East-West Pipeline was attacked on the morning of September 10 and was shut down "as a precautionary measure," with injuries reported, Rigzone reported. The article cited Rystad Energy analysis of scenarios in which loadings at Yanbu, at about 3 million barrels per day, could be disrupted for one, two or three months. Brent crude was trading near $108 a barrel at the time of reporting. The cause of the attack and the extent of the damage were not detailed in the account.
