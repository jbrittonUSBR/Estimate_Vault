# California Auditor: 57 Dams in Poor or Unsatisfactory Condition

[Source](https://www.naturalnews.com/2026-10-18-california-auditor-57-dams-in-poor-condition.html)

(U) California — A California State Auditor report released October 13 found 57 of 1,222 assessed dams were in poor or unsatisfactory condition, including 40 classified as high or extremely high hazard, according to Natural News. The outlet said the audit warned that failure of a high-hazard dam could endanger hundreds of thousands of residents and cause billions of dollars in property damage, citing the auditor’s report and state assessments. Natural News’ broader claims about dam risks were its characterization of those findings.
