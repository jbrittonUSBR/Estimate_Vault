# Fortinet Joins Project Watershed 250 to Strengthen National Water Cybersecurity Infrastructure

[Source](https://www.fortinet.com/blog/industry-trends/fortinet-joins-project-watershed-250-strengthen-national-water-cybersecurity-infrastructure)

(U) San Antonio, United States — Fortinet announced it joined Project Watershed 250, a six-month Texas pilot launched on August 31 alongside Governor Greg Abbott, White House National Cyber Director Sean Cairncross and Texas Cyber Command chief Adm. (Ret.) T.J. White, to strengthen the resilience of water systems across the state. The program unites federal and state agencies, water utilities and private-sector cybersecurity firms to provide participating utilities with red-team testing, system assessments, hardening support, threat intelligence and AI-enabled defensive capabilities. Fortinet cited CISA alerts on attacks against more than 100 U.S. water systems and noted more than 30 municipal water utilities in 12 states were targeted in a recent Iranian-backed campaign.
