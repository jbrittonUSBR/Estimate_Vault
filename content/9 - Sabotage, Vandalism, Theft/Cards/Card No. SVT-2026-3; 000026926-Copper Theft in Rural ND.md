(TLP: GREEN) Sometime between May 13th and 14th, copper grounds were stolen from a substation. It appears that the individual cut the barbwire fence on top of the gates and scaled the gate, not cutting any locks. Approximately, 60 feet of copper grounds were cut and stolen. Incident was reported to local law enforcement. NERC Region: MRO. Incident Location: Rural. Incident Type: Theft. Incident Sub-Type: Copper. Asset Affected: Substation. Substation Type: Distribution. Asset Sub-Type: Grounding.
