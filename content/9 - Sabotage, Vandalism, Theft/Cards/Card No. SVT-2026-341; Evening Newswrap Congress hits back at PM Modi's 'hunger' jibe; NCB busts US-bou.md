# Evening Newswrap: Congress hits back at PM Modi's 'hunger' jibe; NCB busts US-bound opioid pipeline & more

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/india/evening-newswrap-congress-hits-back-at-pm-modis-hunger-jibe-ncb-busts-us-bound-opioid-pipeline-more/articleshow/136026967.cms)

(U) New Delhi, India — This Times of India roundup summarized several November 20 stories, including Congress leaders Rahul Gandhi and Mallikarjun Kharge calling Prime Minister Narendra Modi's FICCI remarks on jobs and salaries an admission of government failure. It reported that the NCB and the US DEA arrested six people across Hyderabad, Delhi, Kolkata and Lucknow in an alleged opioid network that had handled nearly 3,000 parcels and a database of more than 59,000 customers. The roundup also described the arrest of 17 people in Gurgaon for an Apple gift-card scam targeting iPhone users in the United States and Canada, along with a Congress-BJP dispute over Mohammed Siraj's exclusion from India's cricket team.
