# Zelenskyy Says Latest Russian Missile And Drone Attacks Targeted Civilian Sites Across Ukraine

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/ukraine/2026/09/ukraine-260912-rferl02.htm)

(U) KYIV, Ukraine — President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said a fresh round of Russian strikes killed at least three people and wounded dozens more over 24 hours, targeting civilian sites and infrastructure across 10 regions. He said a Shahed-type drone strike on residential buildings in the Zaporizhzhia region killed two people and wounded six, a missile attack on critical infrastructure in Kryvyi Rih killed one and injured three, and 37 people were injured when a missile hit a high-rise residential building in Odesa. Rescue operations continued in Odesa, where two people remained unaccounted for.
