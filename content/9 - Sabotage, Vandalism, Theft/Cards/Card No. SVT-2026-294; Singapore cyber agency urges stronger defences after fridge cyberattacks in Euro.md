# Singapore cyber agency urges stronger defences after fridge cyberattacks in Europe

[Source](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/singapore/cybersecurity-agency-cyberattack-refrigeration-hack-6530431)

(U) Singapore — CNA reported that the Cyber Security Agency urged organizations to secure remote access to operational-technology systems after European supermarket refrigeration attacks. No similar incidents had been reported in Singapore; the agency cited weak or default credentials, outdated software, and exposed management interfaces as possible risks and recommended stronger access controls and monitoring.
