# Arunachal’s anti-dam protests spread as agitators oppose 1,605 MW Menga hydropower project

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/india/arunachals-anti-dam-protests-spread-as-agitators-oppose-1605-mw-menga-hydropower-project/articleshow/135218108.cms)

(U) Arunachal Pradesh, India — The Economic Times reported that protests against hydropower projects had expanded to the proposed 1,605-megawatt Subansiri Upper (Menga) project. A student-group bandh disrupted transport and other daily activity, while protesters called for cancellation and raised environmental and safety concerns; the article also reported clashes and property damage.
