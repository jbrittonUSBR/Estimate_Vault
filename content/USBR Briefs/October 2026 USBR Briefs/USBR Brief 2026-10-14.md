# USBR Brief 2026-10-14

DATAMINR ALERT REPORT

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Dataminr alerts indicate two separate lower priority incidents in the US Mid Atlantic region: a fire detected near Monongahela River Locks and Dam Two in Braddock, Pennsylvania, and drought driven water supply measures in Baltimore, Maryland, where officials began blending Susquehanna River water with reservoir water due to low levels at Prettyboy Reservoir and other locations.¹˒²

DETAIL

A fire was detected near Monongahela River Locks and Dam Two in Braddock, Pennsylvania, based on sensor reporting from NASA FIRMS.¹

Separately, Baltimore's Department of Public Works began blending water from the Susquehanna River with reservoir water to address drought conditions and low water levels at Prettyboy Reservoir and other locations.²

The two incidents are not linked in the reporting, but both point to environmental and infrastructure related pressures affecting water adjacent or water dependent locations in Pennsylvania and Maryland.¹˒²

IMPACT

The reported fire near Locks and Dam Two could pose a localized operational concern if it spreads toward nearby infrastructure, but the available reporting does not indicate damage, disruption, or response activity at this stage.¹

Baltimore's decision to blend Susquehanna River water with reservoir supplies shows that drought conditions and reduced reservoir levels have already prompted active water management measures.²

Taken together, the reporting suggests a need to watch for escalation in infrastructure stress tied to environmental conditions, though the current data is limited and does not support broader disruption claims. Outlook should focus on any follow on reporting of fire growth, containment, or impacts near the Braddock lock and dam area, as well as any additional water conservation measures, service changes, or worsening reservoir conditions in the Baltimore system.
