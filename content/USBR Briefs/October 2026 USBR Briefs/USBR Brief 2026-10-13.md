# USBR Brief 2026-10-13

DATAMINR ALERT REPORT

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

BLUF: Dataminr alerts indicate a mixed set of lower priority developments in the United States, led by drought driven water supply adjustments in Maryland, alongside separate missing person incidents in Minnesota and Utah and a newly disclosed high severity software vulnerability with immediate relevance for operational continuity and risk monitoring.¹˒²˒³˒⁴

## DETAIL

In Maryland, the Baltimore Department of Public Works began blending water from the Susquehanna River with reservoir water in response to drought conditions and low water levels at Prettyboy Reservoir and other locations, indicating active water management measures tied to environmental stress on supply infrastructure.¹

Separate public safety reporting points to two missing person incidents near reservoir and dam areas in different states. In Minnesota, a missing person was reported at Reservoir Woods Parking and Trailhead in St Paul.² In Utah, police are searching for 31 year old Corey Camp, who was last seen on September 28 near Yuba Dam in Juab County.³

In the cyber domain, government sourced reporting via CIRCL stated that Privasys attested TLS accepted evidence from any unrevoked TEE platform in a diversion attack scenario, assigned a CVSS score of 9.1, and said the issue was fixed in version 0.11.0.⁴

## IMPACT

The Maryland water blending measure suggests drought conditions have moved beyond monitoring into operational response, which may support decisions on water resource coordination, public messaging, and contingency planning if low reservoir levels persist.¹

The two missing person reports are geographically unrelated, but together they reinforce the need for localized situational awareness around reservoir and dam recreation areas where search activity or access controls could affect nearby operations.²˒³

The disclosed Privasys vulnerability has more immediate enterprise wide implications than the public safety incidents because the reported severity and named fix version support prompt review of exposure, patch status, and any dependencies on affected attested TLS implementations.⁴

Outlook will depend on whether additional reporting shows expanded drought response measures in Maryland, whether either missing person case develops into a recovery or extended search operation, and whether further technical reporting indicates active exploitation or broader downstream exposure tied to the Privasys issue.
