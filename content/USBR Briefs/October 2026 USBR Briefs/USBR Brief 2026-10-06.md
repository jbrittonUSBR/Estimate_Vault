# USBR Brief 2026-10-06

DATAMINR ALERT REPORT

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Bottom line up front, Dataminr alerts indicate a dispersed set of infrastructure and public safety incidents at or near dam and reservoir sites across multiple US states, led by several same day power outage and fire detections, with immediate significance for localized utility continuity, site safety, and environmental health monitoring.¹˒²˒³˒⁴˒⁵˒⁶˒⁷˒⁸˒⁹

## DETAIL

The most time-sensitive reporting centers on multiple power disruptions at dam related locations in California, New Mexico, and Illinois, including a Farmington Electric Utility outage affecting over 600 customers in Navajo Dam, New Mexico, and separate reported outages near Parker Dam, Napa, and Columbia Quarry Lake Dam.¹˒²˒³˒⁹

Sensor reporting also identified fires near Units 4 Bottom Ash Pond Dam in Waterford, Ohio, and near Inspiration Tailings Dam Number One in Miami, Arizona, indicating concurrent hazard activity at two separate dam adjacent industrial or mining related sites.⁴˒⁵

Separate public safety reporting points to a person being rescued after falling from a retaining wall near Silver Lake Dam in Rochester, Minnesota.⁶

Environmental health reporting indicates the Idaho Department of Environmental Quality issued a health alert for Henrys Lake and Island Park Reservoir after cyanobacteria was found in both bodies of water.⁸

Additional reporting references an ongoing fatality case tied to Dog River Reservoir in Douglasville, Georgia, with local media stating that the father of the man found dead there says he still has many questions.⁷

## IMPACT

The reporting suggests no single nationwide incident, but it does show a broad pattern of localized disruptions and hazards affecting dam and reservoir adjacent areas across several states. Power outages near dam infrastructure can complicate site operations, access, communications, and nearby community services, especially where customer impact is already quantified at more than 600 in Navajo Dam.¹˒²˒³˒⁹

The two fire detections raise the possibility of elevated operational risk at facilities associated with ash pond and tailings infrastructure, although the available reporting does not establish fire size, cause, or direct damage to those sites.⁴˒⁵ The Idaho health alert has immediate implications for recreation, water contact, and public messaging around affected water bodies.⁸

The rescue near Silver Lake Dam and the continued media attention on the Dog River Reservoir death underscore that these locations also carry ongoing life safety and reputational considerations beyond utility or environmental issues.⁶˒⁷

If additional outage reports, evacuation notices, utility restoration delays, or follow on fire detections emerge near the same sites, that would support a trajectory toward more sustained operational disruption. If instead utilities restore service quickly, no further fire indicators appear, and no expanded health warnings are issued, that would point to a series of contained local incidents rather than a widening infrastructure problem.
