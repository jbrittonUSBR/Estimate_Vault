# USBR Brief 2026-10-23

DATAMINR ALERT REPORT

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Dataminr alerts indicate active flood related emergencies in the US Southwest, centered on Dennehotso, Arizona, and Navajo Dam, Blanco, and Turley, New Mexico, with URGENT reporting on evacuations, rescues, and an ongoing flash flood warning, alongside localized infrastructure disruption and a separate lower priority fire detection near a dam in Montana.¹˒²˒³˒⁴

DETAIL

Emergency personnel were actively conducting evacuation and rescue efforts to reach individuals stranded in residences due to rising floodwaters in Dennehotso, Arizona, following an earlier dam failure.¹

In New Mexico, a flash flood warning remained in effect for Blanco, Navajo Dam, and Turley until 23:45, indicating continued flood risk across communities in the same broader area.²

The New Mexico flooding picture also included infrastructure disruption, with a Farmington Electric Utility power outage affecting over 100 customers in Navajo Dam.³

Separately, a fire was detected near Jessup Mill Pond Dam in Kalispell, Montana, based on sensor reporting, but the available information does not indicate any confirmed impact to the dam itself.⁴

IMPACT

The reporting points to an active life safety incident in Dennehotso, where rescue access to stranded residents remained a priority at the time of reporting.¹ The continued flash flood warning for Blanco, Navajo Dam, and Turley, combined with the outage affecting more than 100 customers in Navajo Dam, suggests flood conditions were also disrupting essential services in northwestern New Mexico.²˒³

Taken together, these events support near term decisions around emergency response posture, public warning, access restrictions, and utility coordination in the affected Arizona and New Mexico communities. The Montana fire detection is a separate monitoring issue and, based on the available information, does not yet show a direct dam emergency, though proximity to dam infrastructure warrants continued observation.⁴

Near term outlook will depend on whether rescue operations in Dennehotso expand or begin to wind down, whether the flash flood warning area sees renewed emergency messaging or extensions, and whether the Navajo Dam outage grows or starts to recover. Additional reporting of overtopping, road closures, further evacuations, or confirmed infrastructure damage would indicate a worsening trajectory, while warning expiration and restoration of access and power would point to stabilization.
