# USBR Brief 2026-11-23

Created 23 November 2026.

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Dataminr alerts indicate a mixed operating picture on 22 to 23 November, led by an urgent vehicle crash with reported entrapment in Buchanan Dam, Texas, alongside a continuing water rescue search in Alpine, Alabama, and a cyber alert involving a phishing site impersonating TD Bank; together, the reporting points to concurrent public safety and cyber risk issues in the United States.¹˒²˒³

## DETAIL

An urgent local source report said a vehicle crashed into a ravine on the 500 block of Lehne Loop in Buchanan Dam, Texas, with people reportedly trapped.¹

Separate local news reporting said police were searching for a missing 22 year old man after he fell from a boat on 21 November near Logan Martin Dam in Alpine, Alabama.²

In the cyber domain, a sensor detected a phishing website impersonating TD Bank at a tempurl.host address, indicating active malicious infrastructure targeting bank users or credentials.³

## IMPACT

The Texas crash may require immediate life safety response and could drive short term road access, rescue, and medical support demands in the Buchanan Dam area.¹ If the report of trapped occupants is accurate, the incident could escalate resource requirements quickly.¹

The Alabama incident reflects an ongoing search and rescue posture rather than a newly resolved event, which may continue to draw local law enforcement and emergency resources until the missing boater is located or the search is suspended.²

The phishing detection creates a separate but immediate risk of credential theft or fraud exposure for individuals who interact with the spoofed TD Bank site.³ This supports near term decisions to warn potentially affected users, block the identified domain and related infrastructure, and monitor for follow on impersonation activity.³

Outlook will depend on whether responders in Texas confirm extrication, casualties, or road closures, whether Alabama authorities report recovery or expanded search activity, and whether additional malicious domains or victim reports emerge tied to the TD Bank impersonation effort.
