# UK PM: IRGC 'almost certainly' behind RAF Fairford terror plot

[Source](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/434513)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — On October 13, 2026, Israel National News reported that UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham told Parliament intelligence assessments indicated Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) was almost certainly responsible for directing a suspected terrorist plot targeting RAF Fairford in Gloucestershire. Five British men were arrested in late September on terrorism and explosives-related offenses, though no viable explosive devices were found and those arrested were later released on bail; the UK also announced plans to sanction Iranian networks in response.
