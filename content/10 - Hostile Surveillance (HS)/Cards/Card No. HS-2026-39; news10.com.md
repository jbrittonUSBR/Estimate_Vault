# Unlawful Surveillance Added to List of Charges Against Brunswick Man

[Source](https://www.news10.com/news/rensselaer-county/unlawful-surveillance-added-to-list-of-charges-against-brunswick-man/)

(U) BRUNSWICK, N.Y. — A Brunswick man faces an added charge of unlawful surveillance in Rensselaer County, New York, according to NEWS10 ABC. The charge — which under New York law covers surreptitious recording or observation of a person in a place where they have a reasonable expectation of privacy — was added to a list of charges against the man, whose case is being handled by local authorities. The report highlights a local prosecution involving hidden-camera or peeping-style surveillance conduct.
