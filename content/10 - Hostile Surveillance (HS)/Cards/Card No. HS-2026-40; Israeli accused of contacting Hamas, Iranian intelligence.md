# Israeli accused of contacting Hamas, Iranian intelligence

[Source](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/433355)

(U) HAIFA, Israel — An indictment on security offenses will be filed against Sultan Abu al-Hija, a 31-year-old Israeli Arab resident of Tamra, following a joint investigation by the Shin Bet and the Israel Police Northern District Central Unit, the two agencies announced on September 18, 2026. Abu al-Hija was arrested late the previous month in an operation in which Northern National Guard Border Police officers carried out the arrest. The investigation found that he had contacted Hamas' Izz al-Din al-Qassam Brigades in the Gaza Strip several times seeking to secretly assist the terrorist organization, and had also established contact with Iranian intelligence operatives. According to the joint statement, he was arrested before he was able to carry out any missions on their behalf, and the indictment will be filed with the Haifa District Court.
