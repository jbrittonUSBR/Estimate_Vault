# Ex Nvidia CEO’s lover was in contact with Chinese intelligence for over a year, New York Times reports

[Source](https://en.protothema.gr/2026/11/23/ex-nvidia-ceos-lover-was-in-contact-with-chinese-intelligence-for-over-a-year-new-york-times-reports/)

(U) Washington, D.C., United States — US authorities investigating Singapore-based Megaspeed International over suspected shipments of advanced Nvidia AI chips to China in breach of export rules said they had not concluded that businesswoman Alice Huang spied for Beijing, as the inquiry remained open, Protothema reported. Citing people familiar with US intelligence reports, The New York Times said Huang, who had an affair with then-Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang before his November 12 resignation, communicated with a Chinese Ministry of State Security officer from spring 2025 to autumn 2026; Megaspeed said she left the company in August 2025 and denied wrongdoing. Republican Senator John Kennedy told NBC's "Meet the Press" that the affair itself was not the central issue, calling it "the most expensive sex since Helen of Troy."
