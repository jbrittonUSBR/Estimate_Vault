# UK police re-arrest two suspects in alleged Iran-linked base surveillance case

[Source](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/434923)

(U) October 21, 2026 — British counterterrorism police re-arrested two men under the National Security Act in an investigation into suspicious activity near RAF Fairford, a base used by US bombers, Israel National News reported. Police suspected the men of assisting Iranian intelligence, while Prime Minister Andy Burnham said he believed the activity was likely coordinated in Iran and almost certainly directed by the IRGC; Iran's ambassador denied the allegations.
