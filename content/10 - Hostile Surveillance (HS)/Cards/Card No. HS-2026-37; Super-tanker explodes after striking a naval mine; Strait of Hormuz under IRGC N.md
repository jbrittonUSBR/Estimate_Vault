# Super-tanker explodes after striking a naval mine; Strait of Hormuz under IRGC Navy control

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/iran/2026/09/iran-260914-irna01.htm)

(U) TEHRAN, Iran — Iran's IRGC Navy Command announced that the supertanker Algaya (IMO 9325336) exploded south of the Strait of Hormuz after striking naval mines while attempting to pass through a restricted zone, Iran's state-run IRNA news agency reported, as carried by GlobalSecurity.org. The command said efforts to contain the fire had failed and that the vessel was engulfed in flames, and that warnings had previously been issued about what it called the illegal route. The IRGC Navy declared that the Strait of Hormuz was blocked and remained under its surveillance, and Iranian naval authorities repeated warnings that vessels entering mined or prohibited areas, or sailing without coordination with the Persian Gulf Strait Authority, put crews and cargo at risk. The account reflects IRGC and Iranian state-media claims and was not independently verified in the report.
