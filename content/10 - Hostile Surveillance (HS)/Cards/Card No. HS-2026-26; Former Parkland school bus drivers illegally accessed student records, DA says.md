(U) Two former Parkland School District bus drivers, Shawn Levy and Tyler Young, have been charged with multiple felonies after allegedly breaking into the Orefield Middle School bus garage and illegally accessing protected student records, including disciplinary files and a student photo roster. Surveillance footage captured the pair arriving in Young’s Tesla around 10 p.m. on June 6, entering the secure facility without authorization, and spending nearly two hours attempting logins on the garage foreman’s computer and phone. Investigators later uncovered at least 55 instances of unauthorized access to student data from an off-site IP address linked to the men between March and June, prompting Lehigh County District Attorney Gavin Holihan to file charges of conspiracy to commit burglary, criminal trespass, and unlawful duplication. The school district has cooperated with police, launched an internal investigation, and stated it is strengthening cybersecurity measures to protect sensitive information and maintain community trust, while both former drivers face significant legal consequences for the breach.
