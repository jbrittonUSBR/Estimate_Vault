(TLP: Green) On Thursday, May 21, at approximately 1348 hours, a Security Officer (SO) observed an older white male adult walking along the outside perimeter of the substation, who appeared to be taking photos of the facility. The SO approached the individual and advised him that taking pictures of the substation was not allowed. The individual replied that he was taking the images for personal interest related to energy infrastructure. The subject left without incident.   Incident Type: Suspicious Activity Asset Affected: Substation / Distribution Incident State/Province: California NERC Region: WECC Geographical Location: Suburban
