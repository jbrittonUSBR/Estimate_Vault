(U) Newly unsealed federal court documents revealed that an undercover Department of Homeland Security (DHS) agent spying on a political activist group inside a Minneapolis church accidentally dropped a hidden audio recording device onto the gymnasium floor during a physical training session.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Incident:** As part of an ongoing DHS investigation dubbed "Operation Puppet Master" targeting anti-ICE activists, an undercover agent attended a martial arts and self-defense class hosted by a progressive group inside the gymnasium of **University Baptist Church** in Minneapolis.
- **The Dropped Wire:** According to internal investigative reports submitted in a federal conspiracy case, the agent's hidden recording device fell loose onto the floor "due to the physicality of the event." The agent left the gymnasium unaware it was missing, realized the drop shortly afterward, and returned a few minutes later to retrieve it.
- **Broader Surveillance Scope:** The court filings indicate that DHS agents surveilled at least five churches across Minnesota and Michigan since December, deploying undercover operatives inside and outside houses of worship hosting events organized by external activist coalitions.
- **Legal and Religious Backlash:** The revelations sparked immediate outrage from religious leaders and civil rights groups. Faith-based alliances argued that infiltrating sacred spaces and conducting warrantless surveillance on church grounds directly violates federal court injunctions and religious protections, prompting demands for legal accountability.
