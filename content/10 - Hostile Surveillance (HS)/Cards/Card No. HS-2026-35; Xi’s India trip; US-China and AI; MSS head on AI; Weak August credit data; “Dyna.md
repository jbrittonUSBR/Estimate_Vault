(U) WASHINGTON, United States — Bill Bishop's Sinocism newsletter of September 14, 2026, rounded up Chinese political, economic and technology developments, leading with Xi Jinping's roughly 24-hour visit to India for the BRICS summit and his bilateral meeting with Prime Minister Narendra Modi, at which Xi proposed five cooperation initiatives covering open-source AI, trade and investment, digital industry, intelligent manufacturing and sci-tech talent.
