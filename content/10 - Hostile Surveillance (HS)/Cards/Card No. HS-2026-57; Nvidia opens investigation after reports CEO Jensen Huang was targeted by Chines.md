# Nvidia opens investigation after reports CEO Jensen Huang was targeted by Chinese intelligence

[Source](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-investigation-rcna608148)

(U) Santa Clara, California, United States — NBC News reported that Nvidia's board opened an independent investigation after reports that CEO Jensen Huang was targeted in a Chinese intelligence effort involving his relationship with Megaspeed executive Alice Huang. Nvidia said it had no reason to believe company secrets or U.S. national security were compromised, and Huang said he would cooperate.
