# Candace Owens demands answers from Turning Point after Charlie Kirk statue vandalism

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/us-streamers/candace-owens-demands-answers-from-turning-point-after-charlie-kirk-statue-vandalism/articleshow/134249248.cms)

(U) PHOENIX, United States — Conservative commentator Candace Owens pressed Turning Point Action for details from its surveillance footage after an eight-foot memorial statue of Charlie Kirk outside the group's Phoenix headquarters was defaced with red paint, The Times of India reported. The statue had been unveiled on September 10, 2026, a year after Kirk was killed at a Turning Point event at Utah Valley University, and a nearby tribute sign was also damaged. Turning Point Action COO Tyler Bowyer called the perpetrators "radical criminals," and the organization said it was restoring the memorial and reviewing camera footage, while Phoenix police said they responded to the vandalism and had not publicly identified a suspect. Owens asked on X why video or a vehicle description had not been released, and the report noted that her questions focused on investigative details rather than political blame.
