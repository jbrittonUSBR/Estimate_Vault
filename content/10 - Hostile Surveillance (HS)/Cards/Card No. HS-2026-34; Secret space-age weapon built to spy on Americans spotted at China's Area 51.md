# Secret space-age weapon built to spy on Americans spotted at China's Area 51

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/sciencetech/article-16117493/china-area-51-wzx-drone-malan.html)

(U) BEIJING, China — Satellite imagery and leaked photographs indicated a giant, twin-engine stealth drone, believed to be called the WZ-X, was being developed at China's Malan Air Base, a secret military facility that analysts have compared to the U.S. Area 51. Aviation researcher Andreas Rupprecht, who posted the leaked images of the nearly 50-foot-long craft, said it likely has a range of about 12,000 miles and can fly above 60,000 feet, with an estimated 170-foot wingspan roughly the size of a U.S. B-2 bomber, and would be used for high-altitude, long-endurance intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance. Commercial satellites, including imagery from U.S. aerospace company Planet Labs, first captured the WZ-X at Malan in May 2025, but the People's Liberation Army Air Force had not officially confirmed the aircraft's existence, name or capabilities.
