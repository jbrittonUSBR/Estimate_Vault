# Iran sees ‘false flag’ operations after UK blames IRGC for airbase incident

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/13/iran-sees-false-flag-operations-after-uk-blames-irgc-for-airbase-incident)

(U) Tehran, Iran — On October 13, 2026, Iranian officials warned of potential "false flag" operations following UK allegations that the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) was involved in an incident at a UK airbase. Tehran dismissed the accusations as politically motivated and cautioned against escalating tensions based on unsubstantiated claims.
