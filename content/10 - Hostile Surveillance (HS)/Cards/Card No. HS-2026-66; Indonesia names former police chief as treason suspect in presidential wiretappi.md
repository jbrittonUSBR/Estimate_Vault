# Indonesia names former police chief as treason suspect in presidential wiretapping case

[Source](https://www.euronews.com/2026/11/24/indonesia-names-former-police-chief-as-treason-suspect-in-presidential-wiretapping-case)

(U) JAKARTA, INDONESIA — Indonesian police named former national police chief Listyo Sigit Prabowo among six current and former officers as treason suspects in an expanded investigation into alleged illegal surveillance of President Prabowo Subianto, according to Euronews. Criminal Investigation Agency head Asep Safrudin alleged Listyo ordered the wiretapping of the president and other public figures during 2025 and 2026, received surveillance reports and sought to weaken public confidence in the government to keep his post; some suspects were also accused of organizing anti-government protests. Listyo and two other senior officers were arrested on November 3, and police said the offences carried penalties including life imprisonment and the death penalty.
