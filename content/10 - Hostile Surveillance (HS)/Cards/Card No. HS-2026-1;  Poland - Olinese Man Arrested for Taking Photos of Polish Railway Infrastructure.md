(U) A Chinese national was detained in western Poland after he was spotted photographing critical railway infrastructure. Investigators said that they were analyzing the contents of the man's devices and awaiting a sworn interpreter before questioning the man. Poland has recently tightened rules on photographing critical infrastructure. Since April 2025, taking pictures or filming sites deemed important for national security- marked with special signs-has been banned, with violations punishable by fines, arrest or confiscation of equipment.
