(U) An international counterintelligence investigation involving the FBI and "Five Eyes" intelligence agencies revealed a sophisticated Chinese espionage operation that used fake consulting websites and AI-generated content to target and recruit Western defense and security experts.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Impersonation Scheme:** As part of a network of 13 seized domains, a fake consultancy website deliberately copied the corporate identity, name, and branding of **Horizzen**, a legitimate boutique consultancy based in Brisbane, Australia, to trick applicants into thinking it was a long-established, trusted firm.
- **The Recruitment Tactic:** Operating under fake corporate names and using AI-generated bios and photos, foreign intelligence agents posted lucrative job listings on professional and public job boards to attract individuals with defense, geopolitical, and security clearances.
- **The Escalation Trap:** Recruits were initially hired to write open-source reports for money. Over time, handlers gradually pushed for more sensitive or privileged state secrets, often moving targets to encrypted messaging apps.
- **International Crackdown:** The U.S. Department of Justice and the FBI seized the fraudulent domains in coordination with intelligence services from Australia, the UK, Canada, and New Zealand, following joint warnings over how foreign actors exploit AI and online platforms to infiltrate national security networks.
- **Local Fallout:** Leadership at Horizzen expressed shock and disbelief over discovering their company's digital identity had been hijacked to fuel a clandestine state-backed recruitment pipeline.
