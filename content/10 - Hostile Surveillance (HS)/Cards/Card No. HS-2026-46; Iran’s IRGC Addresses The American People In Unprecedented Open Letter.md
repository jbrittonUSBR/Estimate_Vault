# Iran’s IRGC Addresses The American People In Unprecedented Open Letter

[Source](https://dailycaller.com/2026/09/30/iran-irgc-donald-trump-war-israel-american-people-open-letter)

(U) WASHINGTON — The Daily Caller reported that Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps issued a 26-page open letter to the American people on September 29, 2026, claiming Tehran had won the war that U.S. President Donald Trump initiated on February 28, 2026, and asserting that more than 3,600 Iranians, mostly civilians, had been killed. The letter, addressed to U.S. scholars, students and media, accused the White House of representing "an elite cohort of powerful Zionist capitalists" and defended the "Death to America" chant as aimed at political leadership; the outlet noted that the casualty figures were unverified IRGC assertions and that the document omitted the Guard's own record, including the 2020 downing of a Ukrainian airliner. White House communications director Steven Cheung called the letter "a farcical and laughable attempt" by a "desperate group," and the report characterized the document as an apparent bid by the IRGC to assert control over Iran's on-again, off-again negotiations with Washington.
