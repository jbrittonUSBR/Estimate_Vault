# Germany summons Iran envoy following 'malicious' assassination plots

[Source](https://www.thelocal.de/20261019/germany-summons-iran-envoy-following-malicious-assassination-plots)

(U) Berlin, Germany — Germany summoned Iran's ambassador after a Hamburg court convicted two men of planning to assassinate prominent pro-Israel figures on behalf of Tehran, according to German authorities. The government described the alleged plot as “malicious”; the court convictions and the alleged Iranian connection were reported as part of the case, not as independently established state responsibility.
