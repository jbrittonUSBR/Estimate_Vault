(TLP: Green) An E-ISAC member reported that on May 23rd, a Hydro Operator observed a man in his 50s, heavyset and balding, taking photos of the utility property from his white truck with a camper shell. When approached, the man claimed to work for local new media and left soon after. The operator did not ask the subject which media outlet he worked for. Law enforcement was not contacted.   Incident Type: Suspicious Activity Asset Affected: Generation Incident State/Province: California NERC Region: WECC Result in customer outages? No
