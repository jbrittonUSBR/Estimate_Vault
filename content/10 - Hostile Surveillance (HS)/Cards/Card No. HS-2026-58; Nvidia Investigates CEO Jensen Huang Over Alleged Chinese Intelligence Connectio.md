# Nvidia Investigates CEO Jensen Huang Over Alleged Chinese Intelligence Connection

[Source](https://www.iphoneincanada.ca/2026/11/09/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-investigation/)

(U) Santa Clara, California, United States — iPhone in Canada reported that Nvidia's board commissioned an independent review after media reports alleged that a Chinese intelligence operation targeted CEO Jensen Huang through a relationship with Alice Huang, a former Megaspeed executive. Nvidia said the allegations had not established a compromise of company secrets or U.S. national security, and the company had not substantiated the espionage claims.
