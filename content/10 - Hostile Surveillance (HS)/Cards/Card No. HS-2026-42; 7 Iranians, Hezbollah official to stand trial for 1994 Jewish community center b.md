# 7 Iranians, Hezbollah official to stand trial for 1994 Jewish community center bombing that killed 85

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/09/22/world-news/argentine-court-orders-eight-suspects-to-trial-for-1995-jewish-community-bombing/)

(U) BUENOS AIRES, Argentina — An Argentine court ordered seven Iranians and a Lebanese Hezbollah official to stand trial in absentia for the July 18, 1994 AMIA Jewish community center bombing, which killed 85 people and injured more than 300. As reported by the Jewish News Syndicate via the New York Post, Judge Daniel Rafecas charged the eight suspects and Argentina has placed liens totaling $500 million on Iranian assets. Iran has consistently refused to extradite the suspects, leaving the long-delayed trial to proceed in absentia.
