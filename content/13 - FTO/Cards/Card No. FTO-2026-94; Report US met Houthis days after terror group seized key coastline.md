# Report: US met Houthis days after terror group seized key coastline

[Source](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/433258)

(U) MUSCAT, Oman — U.S. officials met representatives of Yemen's Houthi movement at the U.S. Embassy in Muscat over the weekend, five sources familiar with the matter told Reuters in a report published September 16, 2026. Two sources said Oman's government helped arrange the previously undisclosed talks, which followed the Houthis' seizure of Yemen's Red Sea coastline, including the port of Mocha and Perim Island. The Houthis said they did not intend to attack American vessels and remained committed to their 2025 ceasefire with Washington, while a Yemeni source said they pledged to spare Israeli and other commercial ships except Saudi vessels; the State Department did not confirm the meeting.
