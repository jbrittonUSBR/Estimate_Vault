# Saudi-led coalition dismisses ‘misleading’ Houthi claim of Riyadh attack

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/4/houthi-claims-of-targeting-riyadh-are-misleading-saudi-led)

(U) RIYADH, Saudi Arabia — The Saudi-led coalition dismissed Houthi claims of a successful strike on Riyadh as misleading, stating that no such attack had occurred and attributing the narrative to disinformation efforts by the Yemeni group.
