(U) The report examines mounting pressure and legislative scrutiny facing the **Middle East Children’s Alliance (MECA)**—a long-running, high-profile charity backed by prominent Hollywood figures and cultural icons—over its financial networks and alleged ties to organizations linked to terrorism.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Organization's Background:** Founded by activist Barbara Lubin, MECA has spent decades raising funds under the banner of providing humanitarian aid, schooling, medical care, and water projects for Palestinian children.
- **Star Power and Scrutiny:** While the charity historically leveraged high-profile celebrity endorsements and progressive donor networks to expand its reach, it has drawn intense investigations from Washington policymakers and independent researchers over where its funds ultimately land.
- **The Allegations:** Critics and investigators have raised alarms regarding MECA's partnering organizations and local grassroots distribution networks in the Middle East, questioning whether humanitarian funds have crossed paths with groups designated as terrorist organizations.
- **The Defense:** Representatives and defenders of MECA have strongly pushed back against the allegations, maintaining that every dollar is strictly accounted for and dedicated entirely to civilian aid, children's education, and essential medical infrastructure.
