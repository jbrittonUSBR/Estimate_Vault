# HC denies bail to ISIS sympathiser from Howrah

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/kolkata/hc-denies-bail-to-isis-sympathiser-from-howrah/articleshow/135809936.cms)

(U) Howrah, India — The Times of India reports that the Calcutta High Court denied bail to Saddam Mallick, who has been in custody since January 2023 and is charged under India’s anti-terrorism law. The court said the prosecution material indicated alleged active recruitment and radicalization, rather than merely possession of extremist material; Mallick’s lawyer disputed that possession alone supports the charges. The court also cited trial delays partly linked to a pending discharge application, noting that three of 52 prosecution witnesses had testified.
