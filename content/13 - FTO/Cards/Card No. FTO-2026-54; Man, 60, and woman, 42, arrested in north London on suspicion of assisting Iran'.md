# Man, 60, and woman, 42, arrested in north London on suspicion of assisting Iran's intelligence service

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16120809/Man-woman-arrested-north-London-Irans-intelligence.html)

(U) LONDON — A 42-year-old woman and a 60-year-old man were arrested by counter-terrorism police in London on September 9, 2026, on suspicion of assisting Iran's intelligence service under Section 3 of the National Security Act 2023, the Metropolitan Police said. The arrests followed two searches carried out at north and north-west London addresses on June 24, and a third property was searched after the pair were detained; both were released on bail with strict conditions until late October. Commander Helen Flanagan, head of Counter Terrorism Policing London, said police did not believe there was any imminent threat to the wider public and that the investigation was not linked to attacks on London venues tied to Jewish and Iranian communities.
