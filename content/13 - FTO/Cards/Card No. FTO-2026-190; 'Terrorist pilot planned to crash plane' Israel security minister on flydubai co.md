# 'Terrorist pilot planned to crash plane': Israel security minister on flydubai cockpit incident

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/middle-east/terrorist-pilot-planned-to-crash-plane-israel-security-minister-on-flydubai-cockpit-incident/articleshow/134592703.cms)

(U) TEL AVIV, Israel — On September 30, 2026, The Times of India reported that a flydubai flight from Dubai to Tel Aviv was diverted to Tabuk, Saudi Arabia after a pilot was stabbed with a pocketknife in the cockpit. According to the report, the aircraft declared squawks 7700 then 7500 and descended rapidly from roughly 34,000 to 17,000 feet in under a minute; Israeli officials, including National Security Minister Itamar Ben-Gvir and Transport Minister Miri Regev, called the incident terrorism and sought suspension of flydubai flights, though details about the pilot's nationality remained unconfirmed.
