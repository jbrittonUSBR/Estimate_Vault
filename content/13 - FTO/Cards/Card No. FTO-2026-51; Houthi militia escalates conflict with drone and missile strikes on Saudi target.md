# Houthi militia escalates conflict with drone and missile strikes on Saudi targets

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/houthi-attacks-saudi-oil-iran-conflict/)

(U) SANAA, Yemen — Yemen's Houthi militia launched a coordinated wave of drone and ballistic missile strikes across southern Saudi Arabia on September 8, 2026, hitting civilian areas and oil infrastructure in what Saudi officials called the gravest escalation of the Yemen conflict in years. The attacks injured 73 people and sparked fires at Saudi Aramco facilities, temporarily shutting operations, while Brent crude rose to $99 per barrel. Houthi spokesman Yahya Saree described the strikes as retaliation for prior Saudi airstrikes on Yemeni territory, and the Saudi government vowed retaliation, with reports documenting 54 Saudi airstrikes on September 9 and 10.
