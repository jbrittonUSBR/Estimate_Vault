# Egyptian Court Lists 35 Europe-Based Muslim Groups And 36 Individuals As Terrorist Entities

[Source](https://www.ibtimes.com.au/egypt-designates-european-muslim-brotherhood-linked-groups-1879007)

(U) Cairo, Egypt — The Cairo Criminal Court added 35 European-based Islamic organizations and 36 individuals to Egypt's terrorist lists for five years under Supreme State Security case No. 11108 of 2026, according to International Business Times. The designated entities included the Council of European Muslims, Musulmans de France, the Muslim Association of Britain and groups in Germany, Switzerland, Sweden and Italy. The court described the council as a Muslim Brotherhood front, an allegation the listed groups rejected.
