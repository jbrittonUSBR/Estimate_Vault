# UK sanctions Yemen’s Houthi group, four senior officials

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/24/uk-sanctions-yemens-houthi-group-four-senior-officials)

(U) LONDON, UNITED KINGDOM — The UK government imposed sanctions on Yemen's Houthi movement and four senior officials, accusing the Iran-backed group of undermining Middle East stability. Al Jazeera reported that the designated officials faced asset freezes and travel bans over alleged abuses, including torture and sexual violence against detainees. Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband said the measures held the Houthis accountable for targeting partners and civilians.
