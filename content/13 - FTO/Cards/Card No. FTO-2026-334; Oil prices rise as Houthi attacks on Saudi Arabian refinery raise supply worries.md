# Oil prices rise as Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabian refinery raise supply worries

[Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/28/oil-price-today-wti-brent-hormuz.html)

(U) Global — Oil prices rose as Houthi militants claimed an attack on Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery, raising supply concerns amid broader regional tensions. Reports also indicated the U.S. is preparing for potential renewed strikes on Iran after midterm elections, adding to geopolitical risk premia, according to CNBC.
