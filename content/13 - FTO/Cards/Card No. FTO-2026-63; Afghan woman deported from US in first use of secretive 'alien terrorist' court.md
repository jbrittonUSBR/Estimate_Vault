# Afghan woman deported from US in first use of secretive 'alien terrorist' court

[Source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c3eqpd9zgl9o)

(U) FORT WORTH, United States — Nazira Haji Zada, 47, an Afghan national with lawful US permanent residency living in Fort Worth, Texas, chose to leave the United States rather than continue arguing her case before the Alien Terrorist Removal Court, the first-ever use of the panel created in 1996. She was never charged with a crime; prosecutors alleged she was the "matriarch" of a family whose members plotted an ISIS-inspired mass shooting on Election Day 2024, and her son and son-in-law were convicted of that plot. The Justice Department said she conceded that she is an alien terrorist and waived appeal of the removal order, while her court-appointed lawyers, who said they were denied access to the evidence, called the proceedings a plain violation of due process.
