# Halima Salman convicted of receiving ISIS military training in Syria, faces 10 years in prison

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/us/halima-salman-convicted-receiving-isis-military-training-syria-faces-10-years-prison)

(U) Brooklyn, New York — A federal jury convicted U.S. citizen Halima Salman of knowingly receiving military training from ISIS while in Syria, where she traveled with her family as a teenager in 2016. Prosecutors said she married an ISIS fighter, received authorization to carry a rifle and completed training; she faces up to 10 years in prison, according to Fox News reporting.
