# FBI arrests Georgia man over laundering funds for ISIS

[Source](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/436686)

(U) Atlanta, US — The FBI's Atlanta field office said agents arrested Younes Moustafa Younes, a 34-year-old Lavonia resident, over alleged transfers of more than $26,000 to ISIS-linked recipients, Israel National News reported, citing CBS News Atlanta. The Justice Department said he used multiple phone numbers and accounts to move more than $16,000 between December 2025 and June 2026, with some funds intended for ISIS operations in Syria. Agents reportedly found shredded documents soaked in liquid and three phones submerged in an unknown substance, one of them smashed.
