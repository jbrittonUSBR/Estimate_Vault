# Riyadh airport resumes flights as Saudis fight Houthi missiles

[Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-25/riyadh-airport-resumes-flights-as-saudis-fight-houthi-missiles)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — Bloomberg reported that flights resumed at Riyadh airport after a closure following Houthi missile attacks, as Saudi air defences continued intercepting missiles. Reuters and other contemporaneous reporting said operations initially resumed at two terminals with limited traffic while the Houthis maintained warnings against using the airport.
