# Terrorist surrenders in Borno as soldiers arrest six kidnappers in Adamawa

[Source](https://punchng.com/terrorist-surrenders-in-borno-as-soldiers-arrest-six-kidnappers-in-adamawa/)

(U) MAIDUGURI, Nigeria — Troops of Operation Hadin Kai arrested six suspected kidnappers operating around Soktu Hill in Song Local Government Area of Adamawa State and recovered one Dane gun, while a Boko Haram/ISWAP fighter surrendered at Borgozo in Benisheikh, Kaga Local Government Area of Borno State. The acting military information officer for the North-East Joint Task Force, Captain Mohammed Goni, said in a statement that the six were detained after human intelligence and follow-up covert operations, and that the surrendered fighter had escaped from a terrorist enclave intending to abandon terrorism. Items recovered from the surrender included an AK-47 rifle, 14 rounds of 7.62mm ammunition, four magazines, an improvised explosive device and a bicycle, and all the suspects remained in military custody for investigation.
