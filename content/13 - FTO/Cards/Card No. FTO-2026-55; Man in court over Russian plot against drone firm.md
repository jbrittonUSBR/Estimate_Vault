# Man in court over Russian plot against drone firm

[Source](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c74e0ml3y8go?xtor=AL-72-%5Bpartner%5D-%5Byahoo.north.america%5D-%5Bheadline%5D-%5Bnews%5D-%5Bbizdev%5D-%5Bisapi%5D)

(U) LONDON — Joshua Cammidge, a 31-year-old father-of-one from Swindon, appeared at Westminster Magistrates' Court on September 10, 2026, charged with assisting a foreign intelligence service and preparing an act of sabotage, and was remanded in custody without entering a plea. Prosecutors told the court that Cammidge was prepared to "torch" a UK drone factory to show allegiance to Russia, having allegedly researched four drone manufacturing sites in Swindon and communicated 58 times with an individual linked to the GRU Volunteer Corps. He is due to appear at the Old Bailey on September 25.
