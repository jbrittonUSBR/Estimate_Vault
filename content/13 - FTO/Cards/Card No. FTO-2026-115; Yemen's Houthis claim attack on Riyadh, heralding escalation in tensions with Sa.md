# Yemen's Houthis claim attack on Riyadh, heralding escalation in tensions with Saudi Arabia

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/library/news/2026/09/mil-260920-pdo02.htm)

(U) Riyadh/Sanaa — Yemen’s Houthi group claimed large-scale attacks targeting Riyadh and the Saudi Red Sea hub of Yanbu with ballistic and cruise missiles and drones. The Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted a missile over Riyadh and thwarted other attacks, as tensions escalated between the Houthis and Saudi Arabia.
