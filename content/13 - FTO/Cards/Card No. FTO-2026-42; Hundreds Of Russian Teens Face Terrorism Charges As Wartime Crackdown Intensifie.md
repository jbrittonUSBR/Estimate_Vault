# Hundreds Of Russian Teens Face Terrorism Charges As Wartime Crackdown Intensifies

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/russia/2026/09/russia-260908-rferl02.htm)

(U) MOSCOW, Russia — An RFE/RL report detailed how hundreds of Russian teenagers have been charged with terrorism, sabotage, extremism or treason since Moscow's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022, citing cases such as 16-year-old Novosibirsk schoolgirl Vitalina Prening, placed on Russia's terrorist blacklist on August 10, and Arseny Turbin, sentenced to five years in a closed trial in 2024 at age 15 for distributing critical leaflets. The exiled rights group Department One said in a July report that at least 779 minors are on Russia's terrorist and extremist registry, nearly 97 percent of them added after 2022, while the banned group Memorial said at least 124 minors are currently facing criminal charges for political reasons. Rights campaigners quoted in the report accused Russian security agencies of using provocative tactics against minors and said the secrecy surrounding terrorism cases inflates conviction rates, with Department One head Dmitry Zair-Bek describing a broader "atmosphere of fear." The article was written by RFE/RL's Farangis Najibullah based on reporting by RFE/RL's Russian Service and Current Time.
