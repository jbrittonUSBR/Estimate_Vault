# US completes troop withdrawal from Iraq after 12 year anti-ISIS mission

[Source](https://www.business-standard.com/world-news/us-completes-troop-withdrawal-from-iraq-after-12-year-anti-isis-mission-126093000570_1.html)

(U) WASHINGTON — The U.S. military announced on September 30, 2026, that it had completed its troop withdrawal from Iraq and formally ended Operation Inherent Resolve, the 12-year campaign against ISIS, according to the Associated Press. Pentagon spokesman Sean Parnell said coalition forces and equipment had departed Erbil Air Base under a 2024 U.S.-Iraq agreement, leaving roughly 2,000 American troops in the country as of February 2026 after about 1,500 withdrew from Syria earlier in the year. Baghdad declared the date "Sovereignty Day" and began a three-day holiday, while critics warned that Iran-aligned militias and a thinner counterterrorism presence could allow ISIS to rebuild.
