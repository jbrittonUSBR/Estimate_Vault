# Doc Coming On Terrorist Plot At Swift Vienna Concerts

[Source](https://www.spinmagazine.com/2026/11/taylor-swift-vienna-documentary/)

(U) Vienna, Austria — SPIN reported that a documentary tentatively titled *Taylor Swift Wien* will examine the thwarted plot that led to cancellation of three Eras Tour concerts in August 2024 and the subsequent investigation. The outlet said the film is planned for German broadcaster ZDF in October 2027 and noted that the principal suspect pleaded guilty and received a 15-year sentence in 2026, while a second man received 12 years.
