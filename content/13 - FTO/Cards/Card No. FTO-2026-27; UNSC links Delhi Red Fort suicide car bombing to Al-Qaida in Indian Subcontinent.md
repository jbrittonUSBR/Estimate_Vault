(U) - **Attribution of the Attack:** The United Nations Security Council (UNSC), via its 38th Analytical Support and Sanctions Monitoring Team report, officially attributed the November 2025 suicide car bombing near Delhi's Red Fort to **Al-Qaeda in the Indian Subcontinent (AQIS)**.
- **Evolution of AQIS:** The report highlights that AQIS has transitioned from a fragmented organization into a more structured regional terrorist entity. Rather than operating in large units, the group relies on logistics networks and decentralized, small, scattered cells.
- **Broader Security Concerns:**
    - The monitoring team raised flags about AQIS attempting to exploit Bangladesh to set up new cells.
    - The assessment also noted ongoing concerns regarding terrorist groups pursuing chemical and biological weapons (such as ricin), as well as an increasing reliance on cryptocurrencies to fund operations.
