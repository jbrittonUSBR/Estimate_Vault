# Komnas HAM urges probe into suspected Papua office terror

[Source](https://en.antaranews.com/news/430727/komnas-ham-urges-probe-into-suspected-papua-office-terror)

(U) JAYAPURA, Indonesia — Indonesia's National Human Rights Commission (Komnas HAM) urged Papua police to conduct a thorough investigation into a suspected attack on its Jayapura office on Wednesday, September 9, 2026. Komnas HAM Monitoring and Investigation Commissioner Saurlin P. Siagian said an unidentified person threw a bottle containing flammable liquid with a fuse, commonly known as a Molotov cocktail, which struck at least three motorcycles parked at the office but did not cause a fire; the perpetrator fled immediately and staff did not see them. The object was thrown at the office in Dok V Bawah, North Jayapura Subdistrict, at about 10:15 a.m. local time, and Jayapura City Police Chief Commissioner Fredrickus Maclarimboen said police could not yet confirm the object was a Molotov cocktail, securing broken bottle fragments for forensic examination and questioning four witnesses.
