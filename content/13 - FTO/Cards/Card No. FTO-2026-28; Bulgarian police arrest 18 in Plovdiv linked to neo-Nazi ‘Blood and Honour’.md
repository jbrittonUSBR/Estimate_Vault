(U) Bulgarian police in Plovdiv arrested 18 individuals during a raid on a private club linked to the international neo-Nazi network **"Blood & Honour"**. The operation followed heightened public and law enforcement scrutiny sparked by a violent crime wave and the radicalization of local youth groups.
### **Key Details**

- **The Raids & Seizures:** Authorities searched the club premises and members' homes, uncovering flags with Nazi symbols (including swastikas) and literature promoting neo-Nazi ideology.
- **Charges Filed:** Out of the 18 detained, three individuals—including the tenant of the club and a father-and-son duo—faced formal charges for inciting discrimination, hatred, and violence based on race, national origin, or sexual orientation.
- **Broader Investigations:** The crackdown intersects with ongoing investigations into violent acts by local youth groups in Plovdiv, including attacks on foreign nationals and a high-profile murder case involving minors where neo-Nazi symbolism and videos surfaced.
