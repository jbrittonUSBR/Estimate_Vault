# Ontario man found guilty of terrorism charge related to neo-Nazi group

[Source](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/toronto/ontario-man-guilty-terrorism-charge-9.7337420)

(U) TORONTO, Canada — An Ontario Superior Court judge found Kristoffer Nippak, 33, guilty on Wednesday, September 9, 2026, of participating in the activities of a terrorist group, tied to the Atomwaffen Division (AWD), a designated neo-Nazi terrorist group in Canada. Nippak was arrested and charged in December 2023 following an 18-month RCMP investigation spanning central Quebec, eastern and northern Ontario and the Niagara Region, and was arrested alongside co-accused Matthew Althorpe, who was sentenced to 20 years in prison in March for three terrorism-related charges. Superior Court Justice Maureen D. Forestell found an AWD recruitment video "central" to the decision, ruling the Crown had proven beyond a reasonable doubt that Nippak knew AWD was a terrorist organization when he participated in filming the video.
