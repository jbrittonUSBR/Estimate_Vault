# IDF demolishes home of terrorist who murdered Netanel Shakron

[Source](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/434241)

(U) BINYAMIN, West Bank — IDF forces demolished the home of Qadri Samara, who murdered Netanel "Nati" Shakron, Israel National News reported on October 7, 2026. A powerful explosion was heard during the demolition in Givat Ze'ev and other nearby communities. Shakron, a 48-year-old resident of Alei Zahav, was shot dead at the Ein Raya spring near Neve Tzuf on the morning before Yom Kippur as he arrived with his eldest son to immerse ahead of the holy day. Security forces launched a manhunt; the attacker was wounded by a soldier during his escape, fled to a hospital in Ramallah, and was apprehended there about three hours later by soldiers from the Duvdevan Unit. Shakron is survived by his wife, Merav, and six children.
