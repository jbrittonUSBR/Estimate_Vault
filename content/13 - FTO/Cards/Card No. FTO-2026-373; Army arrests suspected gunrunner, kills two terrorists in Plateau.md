# Army arrests suspected gunrunner, kills two terrorists in Plateau

[Source](https://punchng.com/army-arrests-suspected-gunrunner-kills-two-terrorists-in-plateau/)

(U) Plateau State, Nigeria — The Punch reported that the Nigerian Army arrested a suspected gunrunner in Mangu and killed two people it described as terrorists during operations across three local government areas. The Army said it also cleared camps and recovered weapons, ammunition, motorcycles, and other supplies; these claims were based on its operational statement.
