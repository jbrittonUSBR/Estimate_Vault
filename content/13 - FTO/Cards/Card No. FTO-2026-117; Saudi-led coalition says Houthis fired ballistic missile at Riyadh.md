# Saudi-led coalition says Houthis fired ballistic missile at Riyadh

[Source](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/saudi-led-coalition-says-houthis-fired-ballistic-missile-at-riyadh-227101)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — The Saudi-led coalition said Yemen’s Houthis fired a ballistic missile at Riyadh at dawn, which was intercepted by Saudi air defenses. The coalition also reported thwarting attacks targeting Bisha, Taif, Farasan, and Yanbu, as a fire was reported near King Khalid International Airport.
