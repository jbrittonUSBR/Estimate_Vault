# Alarms Sound in Islam's Holiest City, Mecca, as Houthi Campaign Against Saudi Arabia Continues

[Source](https://www.breitbart.com/middle-east/2026/09/15/alarms-sound-in-islams-holiest-city-mecca-as-houthi-campaign-against-saudi-arabia-continues/)

(U) RIYADH — Breitbart reported that Saudi civil defense alerts sounded in several Saudi cities, including Mecca for the first time and Jeddah, while confirmed Houthi attacks struck Khamis Mushayt, Abha and Taif, injuring at least 13 civilians; Col. Turki al-Maliki, a Saudi military spokesman, condemned the "heinous and deliberate attacks" and vowed to take "all necessary operational measures." The Houthis claimed that 54 Saudi and Yemeni government air raids had been carried out, many with F-15 and Typhoon aircraft, and that three civilians, including two children, were killed in Taiz; those claims could not be independently verified. The article also reported that Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman met with Adm. Bradley Cooper, chief of U.S. Central Command, in Jeddah and later with Egyptian President Abdel Fattah el-Sisi, and that President Donald Trump had declined to commit U.S. air or ground forces, with Cooper offering only intelligence-sharing. The account drew on Saudi state statements, Al-Monitor reporting and Houthi media and reflected Breitbart's partisan framing of the conflict.
