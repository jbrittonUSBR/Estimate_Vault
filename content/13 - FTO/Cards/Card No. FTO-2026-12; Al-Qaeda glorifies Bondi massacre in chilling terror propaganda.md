(U) Al-Qaeda’s Yemeni affiliate has released chilling new propaganda that glorifies the December 2025 Bondi Beach massacre in Australia and urges followers in Western countries to carry out similar lone-wolf attacks. The material, published through its *Inspire Guide* series, presents the Bondi attack as a successful “blueprint” that security services cannot fully prevent, telling supporters that replicating such massacres is “the least they can do” to strike at the West. It provides tactical guidance drawn from the incident while emphasizing that lone-actor violence can bypass traditional counterterrorism measures. The propaganda effort is part of AQAP’s broader strategy to inspire self-radicalized individuals to commit high-impact attacks with minimal coordination. Experts warn that this could encourage copycat violence, particularly as major events like the 2026 World Cup approach and online radicalization networks continue to amplify such messages.
