# Iran-backed Houthi 'terrorist attack' hits Riyadh's tallest skyscraper and HQ of Saudi Public Investment Fund

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/world/iran-backed-houthi-terrorist-attack-hits-riyadh-tallest-skyscraper-hq-saudi-public-investment-fund)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — Saudi civil defense said a suspected Houthi missile damaged the 80-story Public Investment Fund Tower in the King Abdullah Financial District, breaking windows across up to eight floors but causing no injuries, according to Reuters as cited by Fox News. Coalition spokesman Col. Turki Al-Malki said air defenses intercepted four ballistic missiles launched toward the southern region and that a launch platform in Dhamar used in an attempt to strike Riyadh was destroyed. The U.S. Embassy had warned Americans earlier that week of heightened risk from Iran-backed Houthi attacks on airports and critical infrastructure.
