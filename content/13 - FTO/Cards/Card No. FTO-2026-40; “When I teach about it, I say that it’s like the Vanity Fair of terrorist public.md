# “When I teach about it, I say that it’s like the Vanity Fair of terrorist publications.”

[Source](https://www.niemanlab.org/reading/when-i-teach-about-it-i-say-that-its-like-the-vanity-fair-of-terrorist-publications/)

(U) CAMBRIDGE, Mass., United States — Nieman Lab's "What We're Reading" roundup highlighted a Washington Post report that CNN journalist Peter Bergen is donating a complete run of al-Jihad, an al-Qaeda-linked magazine that began publishing in 1984, along with much more of his reporting archive, to Georgetown University. Terrorism scholar Bruce Hoffman was quoted describing the publication as the "Vanity Fair of terrorist publications." The item, compiled by Nieman Lab's Joshua Benton, summarizes the underlying Washington Post story by Scott Nover.
