# Thirteen charged with terrorism offences related to Palestine Action protest

[Source](https://news.stv.tv/east-central/thirteen-charged-with-terrorism-offences-related-to-palestine-action-protest)

(U) EDINBURGH, SCOTLAND — STV News reported that Police Scotland charged a man over supporting a proscribed organisation, bringing to 13 the total charged with terrorism offences linked to a Palestine Action protest outside the US Consulate on October 31. The charge followed earlier cases against nine women and three men, and a report was to be submitted to the Procurator Fiscal. Police said extensive enquiries remained ongoing and further action would be taken as appropriate.
