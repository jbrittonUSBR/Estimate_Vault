# ISIS terrorist from Ontario gets additional 50 years in U.S. prison for stabbing guard in the eye

[Source](https://nationalpost.com/news/canada/isis-terrorist-from-ontario-gets-additional-50-years-in-u-s-prison-for-stabbing-guard-in-the-eye)

(U) October 20, 2026 — National Post reported that Abdulrahman El Bahnasawy received an additional 50-year federal sentence for a 2020 attack on two correctional officers at USP Allenwood, where he stabbed one officer in the head and face and another in the hand with a weapon fashioned from a desk. The first officer lost an eye; the new term was ordered to run consecutively to the 40-year sentence El Bahnasawy was serving for an ISIS-inspired New York terror plot. The Justice Department said El Bahnasawy pleaded guilty in 2025 to assault-related offenses and providing material support to ISIS.
