# Last UK and US troops leave Iraq as anti-Islamic State mission ends

[Source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c65ym1dyn92mo)

(U) ERBIL, Iraq — On September 30, 2026, the last remaining British troops left Iraq, bringing an end to the UK's 23-year military operation there as part of the U.S.-led coalition against Islamic State. According to BBC reporting by Jonathan Beale and David Gritten, the withdrawal was coordinated with the departure of U.S. forces, ending Operation Inherent Resolve in Iraq; the coalition's counter-ISIS taskforce was set to be based in Jordan going forward.
