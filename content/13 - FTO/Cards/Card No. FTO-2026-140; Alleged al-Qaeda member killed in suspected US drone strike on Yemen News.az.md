# Alleged al-Qaeda member killed in suspected US drone strike on Yemen

[Source](https://news.az/news/alleged-al-qaeda-member-killed-in-suspected-us-drone-strike-on-yemen)

(U) MUKALLA, Yemen — A suspected U.S. drone strike hit a vehicle carrying two suspected al-Qaeda members in Hadramout province's Brom Mayfa district, killing one and wounding the other, a local security official said. The official, speaking on condition of anonymity, said the vehicle was believed to belong to al-Qaeda in the Arabian Peninsula, according to Xinhua.
