# Turkish tourist arrested on Temple Mount on suspicion of incitement and support for terrorism

[Source](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/flashes/698168)

(U) Jerusalem, Israel — Israel National News reported that police arrested a Turkish tourist on the Temple Mount over the weekend after she allegedly handed money to worshippers and urged them to express support for Hamas. Investigators suspected she was also carrying inflammatory materials, according to the brief.
