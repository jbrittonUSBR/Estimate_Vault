# Trump left Saudi Arabia to face Houthis alone – Mearsheimer to RT (VIDEO)

[Source](https://www.rt.com/news/646322-trump-left-saudi-arabia-to-face-houthis-alone-mearsheimer/)

(U) MOSCOW — Political scientist John Mearsheimer told RT's "New Order" program, hosted by Afshin Rattansi, that President Donald Trump had left Saudi Arabia to face the Houthis alone, according to RT. Mearsheimer said the Houthis had seized Yemen's Red Sea coast and the Bab el-Mandeb strait while Washington focused elsewhere, and he characterized the situation as a strategic setback for Riyadh. RT is Russian state media; the interview presented Mearsheimer's analysis rather than reporting new facts.
