# Uzbek National Extradited to the United States to Face Charges for Providing Material Support to Foreign Terrorist Organizations

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/security/library/news/2026/09/sec-260924-doj01.htm)

(U) BROOKLYN, New York — Davud Ansariy, a citizen of Uzbekistan and naturalized U.S. citizen also known as “Kozimjon Gafurov” and “Kozim,” was extradited from Tbilisi, Georgia, on September 23 and arraigned in federal court in Brooklyn on an indictment charging him with conspiring to provide and providing material support to ISIS and the al-Nusrah Front, according to a Justice Department release carried by GlobalSecurity.org. Prosecutors allege Ansariy led a radicalized Brooklyn-based network members called “chayxona,” an Uzbek term for tea house, that raised money and arranged travel for would-be fighters and funded the travel of at least three individuals to Syria in 2013, all three of whom are believed to have been killed there. He had left the United States for Turkey on a one-way ticket in May 2016 and was arrested in Georgia in August 2026; the charges are allegations and he is presumed innocent.
