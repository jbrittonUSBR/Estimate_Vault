# Houston man gets 15 years for concealing support for ISIS

[Source](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/436960)

(U) HOUSTON, UNITED STATES — Israel National News, citing KPRC-TV and federal prosecutors, reported that Houston man Anas Said received a 15-year federal prison sentence for concealing material support for ISIS and lying to the FBI. Judge David Hittner imposed the term after Said pleaded guilty in July, with prosecutors saying he helped produce and distribute propaganda videos and posters. The court also ordered lifetime supervised release, and the outlet reported that Said had admitted contemplating an attack on as many people as possible.
