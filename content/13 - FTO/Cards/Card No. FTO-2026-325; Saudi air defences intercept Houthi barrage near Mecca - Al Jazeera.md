# Saudi air defences intercept Houthi barrage near Mecca - Al Jazeera

[Source](https://slashdot.org/firehose.pl?op=view&amp;id=186460304)

(U) Mecca province, Saudi Arabia — Slashdot’s firehose item linked to Al Jazeera’s report that shrapnel from two intercepted ballistic missiles fell in Jeddah, Taif, Rabigh, and Wadi Al-Mughammas.
