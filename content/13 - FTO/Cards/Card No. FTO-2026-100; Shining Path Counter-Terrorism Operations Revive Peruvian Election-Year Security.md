# Shining Path Counter-Terrorism Operations Revive Peruvian Election-Year Security Discourses

[Source](https://jamestown.org/shining-path-counter-terrorism-operations-revive-peruvian-election-year-security-discourses/)

(U) LIMA, Peru — A Jamestown Foundation Terrorism Monitor analysis by Jacob Zenn assessed that remnants of the Shining Path (Sendero Luminoso) remain active in Peru's VRAEM region but now function mainly as a narcoterrorist cartel rather than a nationwide Maoist insurgency. The report noted that security forces killed two alleged members, including a VRAEM Central Committee figure known as "David," in late March 2026, and that the government extended a state of emergency across 12 districts and eight population centers for 60 days on May 21. It argued that the timing and publicity of the March operation fed suspicions among outgoing officials that the security establishment was promoting counterterrorism operations to benefit conservative candidate Keiko Fujimori, who won narrowly on June 7. The brief concluded that Shining Path's remaining presence is militarily limited and increasingly intertwined with narcotics trafficking, even as small operations continue to shape national political discourse.
