# Taiz camps overflow with Yemenis fleeing fighting after Houthi offensive

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/features/2026/9/16/taiz-camps-overflow-with-yemenis-fleeing-fighting-after-houthi-offensive)

(U) TAIZ, Yemen — At least 100,000 people fled their homes in Yemen after a renewed Houthi offensive, according to the United Nations' refugee agency, with Taiz governorate absorbing most of the displacement. Ali al-Shumairi, director-general of the Taiz governorate camp management unit, told Al Jazeera that 65,845 people were displaced between August 10 and September 15, 2026, about 95 percent from five districts that saw the heaviest fighting. Camps around Taiz were overflowing, with more than 1,500 families arriving at the Mushrifa camp in six days and many living in the open, as Houthi forces pressed toward Taiz city after capturing the Red Sea coast.
