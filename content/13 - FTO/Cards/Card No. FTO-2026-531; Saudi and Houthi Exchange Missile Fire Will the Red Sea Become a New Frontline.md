# Saudi and Houthi Exchange Missile Fire: Will the Red Sea Become a New Frontline?

[Source](https://www.naturalnews.com/2026-11-24-saudi-houthis-missile-fire-red-sea-new-frontline.html)

(U) RIYADH, SAUDI ARABIA — Natural News, citing Middle East Eye and Reuters, reported that the Saudi-led coalition struck Hodeidah while Houthi forces fired missiles toward Riyadh and threatened Saudi Red Sea ports. Coalition spokesman Turki Al Malki said air defenses intercepted three ballistic missiles, while the Houthis claimed strikes on Jizan port and the Shuqaiq power station. The article said the exchange raised fears of a wider front along the Red Sea as Yemen's civil war reignited.
