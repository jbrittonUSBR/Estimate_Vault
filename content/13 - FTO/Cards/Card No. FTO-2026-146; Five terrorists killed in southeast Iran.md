# Five terrorists killed in southeast Iran

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/iran/2026/09/iran-260923-irna07.htm)

(U) Saravan, Iran — Iranian security forces, in a joint operation by the IRGC, Intelligence Ministry, and police on September 23, 2026, killed five members of a terrorist team in the southeastern city of Saravan following a public tip-off. Several team members were arrested, weapons and ammunition were seized from their hideout, and one member of the security forces was martyred during the clash. The IRGC's Quds Base stated the team was linked to the United States and Israel and intended to carry out a terrorist attack in Sistan-Baluchestan province.
