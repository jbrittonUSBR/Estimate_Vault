# Government upgrades the Prevention of Terrorism Act

[Source](https://english.gossiplankanews.com/2026/09/government-upgrades-prevention-of.html)

(U) Colombo, Sri Lanka — The Sri Lankan government published a bill on September 23, 2026 to repeal the Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act No. 48 of 1979 after 45 years and replace it with the "Bill to Protect the State from Terrorism." The new law defines terrorist offenses broadly, establishes safeguards including 48-hour production before a magistrate and audio-visual interrogation recording, limits detention to a maximum of one year with judicial oversight, and includes protections for peaceful protests, trade union actions, journalism, and academic activity.
