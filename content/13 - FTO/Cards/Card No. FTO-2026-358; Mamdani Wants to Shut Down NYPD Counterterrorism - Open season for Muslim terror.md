[Source](https://freerepublic.com/focus/f-chat/4401903/posts)

(U) New York City, USA — Sultan Knish argues that New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani has constrained the NYPD Strategic Response Group by requiring his approval before deployment, despite having campaigned to abolish the unit. The opinion piece describes the SRG as a rapid-response team with counterterrorism training and claims its reduced autonomy could weaken the city’s readiness for attacks and riots. Its assertions about Mamdani, terrorism trends, and the consequences of the policy are the author’s claims and are not independently substantiated in the piece.
