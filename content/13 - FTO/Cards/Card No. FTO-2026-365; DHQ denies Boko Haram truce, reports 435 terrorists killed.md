# DHQ denies Boko Haram truce, reports 435 terrorists killed

[Source](https://punchng.com/dhq-denies-boko-haram-truce-reports-435-terrorists-killed/)

(U) Abuja, Nigeria — The Punch reported that Defence Headquarters denied reports of a ceasefire with Boko Haram, ISWAP, or other armed groups and said military operations would continue. At a briefing, the military reported 588 operations in September and October, 435 people killed, 809 arrests, and 717 rescued captives; these figures and the denial are attributed to the military, while the reported truce came from separate media accounts.
