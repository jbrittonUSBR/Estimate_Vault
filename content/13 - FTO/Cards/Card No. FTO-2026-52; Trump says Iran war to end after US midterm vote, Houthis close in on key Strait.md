# Trump says Iran war to end after US midterm vote, Houthis close in on key Strait, sources say

[Source](https://biztoc.com/x/713b5842d4a2f4d7)

(U) CAIRO — President Donald Trump said he expected the war with Iran to end after the November U.S. midterm elections, after the two sides launched their biggest wave of attacks on shipping since the war began, according to a Reuters report. Trump also threatened again to strike Iran's Pickaxe Mountain site and claimed Tehran was trying to influence the midterm elections, while four Yemeni government military sources said the Houthis were edging closer to gaining leverage over the Bab el-Mandeb Strait. Brent crude hovered above $100 a barrel as supply routes through both the Strait of Hormuz and the Red Sea remained heavily disrupted.
