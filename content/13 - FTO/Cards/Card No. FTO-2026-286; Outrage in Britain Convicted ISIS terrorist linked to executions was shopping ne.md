# Protothema reports convicted ISIS member linked to executions was seen shopping near Windsor

[Source](https://en.protothema.gr/2026/10/18/outrage-in-britain-convicted-isis-terrorist-linked-to-executions-was-shopping-near-king-charless-castle/)

(U) Athens, Greece — Protothema reported that an Iraqi man convicted in Germany for membership in ISIS was living in Britain and had been seen shopping near Windsor. The report said court documents linked him to security roles at ISIS executions, while British authorities had not deported him because of concerns for his safety if returned to Iraq. The outlet framed his presence as an issue of public concern; the card reports its account and does not independently verify the allegations or legal rationale.
