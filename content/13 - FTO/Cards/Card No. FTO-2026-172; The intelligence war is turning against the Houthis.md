# The intelligence war is turning against the Houthis

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/opinions/2026/9/26/the-intelligence-war-is-turning-against-the-houthis)

(U) SANA'A, Yemen — In an opinion analysis attributed to Nawaf Obaid (Al Jazeera English), a Russia–Iran intelligence fusion system that had aided Houthi advances on Yemen's western front was reported to be under sustained attack, with Saudi-led forces degrading command-and-control nodes and correcting tactical vulnerabilities exposed during the Mokha breakthrough.
