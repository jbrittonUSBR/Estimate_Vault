# Saudi Arabia intercepts Houthi missiles targeting Riyadh, Eastern province

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/8/saudi-arabia-intercepts-houthi-missiles-targeting-riyadh-eastern-province)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — Al Jazeera reported that the Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted missiles aimed at Riyadh, the Eastern Province, and southern areas, as well as a Houthi drone. Saudi media said debris from an attempted attack damaged an Aramco administrative building in Riyadh without casualties; the Houthis separately claimed attacks on Saudi sites.
