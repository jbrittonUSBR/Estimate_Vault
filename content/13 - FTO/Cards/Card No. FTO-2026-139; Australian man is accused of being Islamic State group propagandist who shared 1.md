# Australian man is accused of being Islamic State group propagandist who shared 10,000 files online

[Source](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/australian-man-accused-islamic-state-group-propagandist-shared-136675405)

(U) WELLINGTON, New Zealand — Authorities in Melbourne accused a 65-year-old Australian citizen of pledging allegiance to the Islamic State group and spreading about 10,000 pieces of extremist propaganda online between 2015 and 2020, including guides on planning mass casualty attacks. He was the seventh man arrested in Victoria state since May over allegations of possessing or distributing IS content, and police did not link the cases.
