# Iran insists no talks with the US until its 7 conditions are met, and other Mideast news

[Source](https://www.newsday.com/news/nation/mideast-iran-gaza-israel-houthis-libya-november-24-2026-v21527)

(U) MIDDLE EAST — The Associated Press reported that Iran's chief negotiator Mohammad Bagher Qalibaf said Tehran would not hold talks with the United States until seven conditions were met, according to Iranian state television. A Yemeni military official said Houthi forces had advanced about 60 kilometers toward Aden, where pro-government troops dug in on a defensive line, and Iran's Revolutionary Guard claimed three oil tankers caught fire after striking mines in the Strait of Hormuz. Gaza officials reported at least nine Palestinians killed in Israeli strikes, including a Hamas interior ministry security official whose death Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu confirmed.
