# Russian Investigators Open Terrorism Case Over Ukrainian Attacks on Moscow, Moscow Region

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/ukraine/2026/09/ukraine-260920-sputnik05.htm)

(U) Moscow, Russia — Russia’s Investigative Committee opened a criminal case under Article 205 (terrorism) over Ukrainian drone attacks on civilian infrastructure in Moscow and the Moscow Region, according to Russian officials. The committee said people were killed and injured and that residential and industrial/logistics facilities were damaged.
