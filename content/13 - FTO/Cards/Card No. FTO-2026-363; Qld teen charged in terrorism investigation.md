[Source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-07/gladstone-teen-charged-with-planning-to-cause-death/107359128)

(U) South Gladstone, Queensland, Australia — ABC News reported that Queensland police charged an 18-year-old man with planning to cause death or grievous bodily harm after a public tip to the national security hotline. Police opposed bail and said there were no ongoing direct threats to the community; the charge remains an allegation.
