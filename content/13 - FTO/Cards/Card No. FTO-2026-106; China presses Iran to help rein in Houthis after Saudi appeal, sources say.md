# China presses Iran to help rein in Houthis after Saudi appeal, sources say

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/world-news/china-presses-iran-to-help-rein-in-houthis-after-saudi-appeal-sources-say/articleshow/134311640.cms)

(U) BEIJING, China — China privately urged Iran to help rein in Yemen's Houthis after an appeal from Saudi Arabia, following the group's rapid military advance along the Red Sea coast, according to three Iranian sources cited by Reuters. The sources said Riyadh turned to Beijing after Houthi gains around the Bab el-Mandeb strait left Saudi oil exports and shipping more exposed, and that China's private message went further than its public calls for restraint, dialogue and safe navigation. Tehran's response was that stability and peace in the region depended on ending the U.S.-Israeli war on Iran, and Chinese officials issued no explicit threats or indications of economic pressure, the sources said. China's foreign ministry told Reuters it did not wish to see regional tensions spill further into Yemen and the Red Sea and said facilities vital to people's livelihoods must not be targeted.
