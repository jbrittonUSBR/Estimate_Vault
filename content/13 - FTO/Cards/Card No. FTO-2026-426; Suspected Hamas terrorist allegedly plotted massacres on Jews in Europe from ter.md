# Suspected Hamas terrorist allegedly plotted massacres on Jews in Europe from terraced home in Birmingham

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16281587/Suspected-Hamas-terrorist-allegedly-plotted-massacres-Jews-Europe-terraced-home-Birmingham.html)

(U) Birmingham, United Kingdom — The Daily Mail reports that Mohammed Alfatih Naim is accused by German prosecutors of helping transport firearms and ammunition as part of an alleged Hamas network suspected of planning attacks on Jewish or Israeli targets in Europe. The article says weapons and ammunition were allegedly moved from Berlin to a Vienna storage unit and that a claim-of-responsibility video was recovered by British police; these allegations have not been adjudicated. Naim was separately convicted in a UK court of wounding, weapon possession, and criminal damage in an assault on a bailiff, and extradition proceedings are expected after his UK sentence.
