# Judge tells prosecutors that terrorism case defendant can read violent religious passages

[Source](https://abcnews.com/US/wireStory/judge-tells-prosecutors-terrorism-case-defendant-read-violent-138304850)

(U) New York, US — Judge Colleen McMahon told prosecutors that the government had to allow Mohammad Baqer Saad Dawood al-Saadi access to a religious biography despite passages prosecutors said justified violence, citing comparable texts in the Bible. Al-Saadi, who pleaded not guilty to a rewritten indictment alleging coded messages about an attack on RAF Fairford in England, was removed from the courtroom after repeated outbursts. McMahon adjourned the case to February 10 so his family could seek new lawyers.
