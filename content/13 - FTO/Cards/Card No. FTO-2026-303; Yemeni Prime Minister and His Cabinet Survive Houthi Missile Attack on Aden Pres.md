# Natural News reported Houthi missiles struck Aden palace complex; Yemen's cabinet was unharmed

[Source](https://www.naturalnews.com/2026-10-21-yemeni-pm-cabinet-survive-houthi-missile-attack.html)

(U) October 21, 2026 — Natural News reported that Houthi missiles struck the Maashiq presidential palace complex in Aden on October 15 while Prime Minister Shaya al-Zindani and cabinet members were present; Yemeni officials said they were unharmed. The article cited officials who reported two security personnel killed, and separately cited Al Arabiya for a toll of five dead and 16 wounded; the Houthis' spokesman characterized the site as a Saudi operations room.
