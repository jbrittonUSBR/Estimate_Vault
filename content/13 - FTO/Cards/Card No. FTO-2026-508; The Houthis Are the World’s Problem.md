# The Houthis Are the World’s Problem

[Source](https://www.theatlantic.com/international/2026/11/houthis-saudi-arabia-war/689532/?utm_source=feed)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — In an Atlantic opinion essay, Robert F. Worth described meeting a Yemeni military officer at a Riyadh mall in late October when an incoming-missile alert interrupted their conversation, and noted that a mid-October strike on the capital's main airport killed at least 14 people with few flights resumed a month later. He wrote, citing Saudi officials who spoke off the record, that the kingdom had brought in thousands of Pakistani and Syrian soldiers and that one official attributed the September Houthi seizure of Bab el-Mandeb to Iranian coordination, including a Mahan Air flight carrying technicians and weapons. The essay argued that the Houthis now control about a third of Yemen, can disrupt a waterway carrying roughly 15 percent of global trade, and that Saudi-backed forces withdrew from the Bab el-Mandeb area on November 15 while Aden remained weakly defended.
