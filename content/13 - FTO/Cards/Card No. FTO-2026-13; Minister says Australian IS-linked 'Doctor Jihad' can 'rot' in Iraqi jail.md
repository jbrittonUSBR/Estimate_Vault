(U) **Australia's 'Doctor Jihad' Tareq Kamleh reportedly found alive in Iraqi jail** (ABC News, Holly Tregenza, July 22, 2026) Islamic State-linked Perth paediatrician Tareq Kamleh, known as “Doctor Jihad,” is reportedly alive and being held in a Baghdad prison, according to *The Australian*, despite earlier beliefs that he had died in the 2017 final siege of Raqqa. He travelled to Syria in 2015 to join Islamic State and is wanted in Australia on multiple terrorism-related charges, including joining and recruiting for the group. Iraqi Supreme Court documents from June reportedly indicate that investigations into Kamleh are ongoing and that his activities “were not limited to providing medical services.” He is accused of adopting IS propaganda and advocating that terrorist activity should not be confined to conflict zones.

**Political reaction**:
- Opposition Leader Angus Taylor said Kamleh “shouldn’t come back” and urged the government to refuse him a passport and issue a temporary exclusion order (TEO) to prevent his return.
- Housing Minister Clare O’Neil stated the government does “not want this person back in Australia” and that he can “rot in jail.”
- The government maintains it is legally obligated to allow Australian citizens to return and cannot permanently deny passports, but it will not assist the return of those who joined terrorist organisations and says returning individuals would face the full force of the law. TEOs can only temporarily delay return to allow security preparations.

(U) Kamleh is among a group of Australian men linked to Islamic State held in Iraqi prisons who could potentially seek to return if released. The government has previously allowed the return of some women and children (including “ISIS bride” Hodan Abby under strict conditions), with some returnees later charged with terrorism or slavery offences.
