# Houthi Attack Damaged Buildings in Riyadh Financial District

[Source](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/11/20/893242.htm)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — The state-run Saudi Press Agency said a Houthi strike damaged several floors of a tower in Riyadh's financial district and nearby buildings but caused no casualties. Bloomberg News cited an eyewitness who identified the damaged building as the headquarters of Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund; the fund did not immediately respond to a request for comment. The King Abdullah Financial District had been under remote-work orders since deadly Houthi strikes on Riyadh's main airport in October.
