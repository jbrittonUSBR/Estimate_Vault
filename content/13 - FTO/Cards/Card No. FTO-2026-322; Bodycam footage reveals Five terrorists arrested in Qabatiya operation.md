# Bodycam footage reveals: Five terrorists arrested in Qabatiya operation

[Source](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/435171)

(U) Qabatiya and Binyamin, West Bank — Israel National News reported that Israeli forces arrested five people in Qabatiya, identifying three as affiliated with Hamas and two with Palestinian Islamic Jihad. Separately, it said Paratroopers Battalion 202 raided more than 50 locations in the Binyamin area and searched for weapons; the descriptions and affiliations were attributed to the Israeli report.
