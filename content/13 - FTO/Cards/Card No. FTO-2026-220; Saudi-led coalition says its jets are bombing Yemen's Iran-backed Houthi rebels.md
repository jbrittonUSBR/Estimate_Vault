# Saudi-led coalition says its jets are bombing Yemen's Iran-backed Houthi rebels

[Source](https://www.yahoo.com/news/world/articles/iran-backed-houthis-seize-another-072001134.html)

(U) ADEN, Yemen — Saudi-led coalition aircraft carried out airstrikes against Iran-backed Houthi rebel targets across Yemen as fighting intensified around the strategic Bab el-Mandeb Strait. The strikes came after Houthi forces made territorial gains and launched attacks on targets in Saudi Arabia, prompting a major coalition offensive to reclaim key coastal areas.
