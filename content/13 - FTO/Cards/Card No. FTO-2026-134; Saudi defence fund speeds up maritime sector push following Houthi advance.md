# Saudi defence fund speeds up maritime sector push following Houthi advance

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/defence/saudi-defence-fund-speeds-up-maritime-sector-push-following-houthi-advance/articleshow/134408932.cms)

(U) BEIRUT, Lebanon — Saudi Arabia's first defence-focused venture capital fund, MASNA Ventures, asked the Capital Markets Authority to raise its capacity to $150 million from $100 million and was accelerating investment in autonomous maritime systems, including uncrewed surface and underwater vehicles. The push followed Houthi advances near the Bab el-Mandeb strait that increased threats to Red Sea shipping.
