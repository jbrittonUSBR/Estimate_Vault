# We need to better combat terrorism. The first step? Remove religion from the definition

[Source](https://theconversation.com/we-need-to-better-combat-terrorism-the-first-step-remove-religion-from-the-definition-297132)

(U) Canberra, Australia — In an opinion essay, three Australian legal academics argued that the federal criminal definition of a "terrorist act" should drop "religious" and instead define "ideology" broadly. They cited Independent National Security Legislation Monitor Jake Blight's report to federal parliament, which proposed seven changes, and noted the Commonwealth Office of Public Prosecutions said religiously motivated cases could have been prosecuted as ideologically motivated ones. The authors cited the Bondi Junction inquest and terrorism charges over the Wakeley church stabbing as examples of inconsistent treatment, and said Home Affairs Minister Tony Burke had signaled the government would not pursue the change.
