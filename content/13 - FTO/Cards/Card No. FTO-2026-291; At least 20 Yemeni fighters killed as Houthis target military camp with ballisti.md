# At least 20 Yemeni fighters killed as Houthis target military camp with ballistic missiles

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/middle-east/at-least-20-yemeni-fighters-killed-as-houthis-target-military-camp-with-ballistic-missiles/articleshow/135047351.cms)

(U) SOUTHERN YEMEN — The Times of India reported that a Houthi ballistic-missile strike on a government military camp killed at least 20 Yemeni fighters. The report attributed the casualty figure to local officials and described damage at the camp; the count remained source-specific amid differing reports of the toll.
