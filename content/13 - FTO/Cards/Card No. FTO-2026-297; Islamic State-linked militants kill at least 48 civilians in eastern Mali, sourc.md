# Islamic State-linked militants kill at least 48 civilians in eastern Mali, sources say

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/defence/islamic-state-linked-militants-kill-at-least-48-civilians-in-eastern-mali-sources-say/articleshow/135069061.cms)

(U) Ménaka region, Mali — Islamic State Sahel Province fighters killed at least 48 people across several localities, Reuters reported, citing two security sources and two local officials who spoke anonymously. The sources said the attacks appeared to follow a pro-government operation against suspected informants; the reported casualty total was still being determined and Reuters did not independently verify the claims.
