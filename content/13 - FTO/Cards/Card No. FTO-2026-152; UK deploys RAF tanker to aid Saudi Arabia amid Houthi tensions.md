# UK deploys RAF tanker to aid Saudi Arabia amid Houthi tensions

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/uk-deploys-raf-tanker-to-aid-saudi-arabia-amid-houthi-tensions/)

(U) London, United Kingdom — The UK Ministry of Defence deployed an RAF Voyager tanker to Saudi Arabia on September 23, 2026 as part of time-limited defensive support amid escalating Houthi drone and missile attacks. The deployment aims to bolster Saudi air defenses and protect critical Red Sea shipping lanes, with observers noting increased risks to Saudi energy infrastructure including the East-West oil pipeline.
