# EXCLUSIVE: ICE ‘Delta Team’ Captures Twice-Deported MS-13 Terrorist in Pre-Dawn Houston Raid

[Source](https://www.breitbart.com/border/2026/11/21/exclusive-ice-delta-team-captures-twice-deported-ms-13-terrorist-in-pre-dawn-houston-raid/)

(U) Houston, US — Breitbart Texas reported that ICE Enforcement and Removal Operations officers arrested Samuel Jeremias Grande-Caminos, a 39-year-old Salvadoran national, outside his apartment around 5:48 a.m. after roughly three weeks of surveillance. Houston field office director Brett Bradford said he had been deported twice and convicted of felony illegal re-entry, and Salvadoran authorities had documented his MS-13 membership. He was transferred to ICE custody for federal prosecution, while Bradford criticized a Houston police directive limiting cooperation with ICE.
