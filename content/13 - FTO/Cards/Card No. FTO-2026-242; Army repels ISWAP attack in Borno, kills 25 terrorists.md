# Army repels ISWAP attack in Borno, kills 25 terrorists

[Source](https://punchng.com/army-repels-iswap-attack-in-borno-kills-25-terrorists/)

(U) Borno, Nigeria — Nigerian Army troops under Operation Hadin Kai repelled a coordinated Islamic State West Africa Province attack in Kukawa, killing more than 25 terrorists and recovering six AK-47 rifles and ammunition, according to a military statement; some soldiers were killed in the fighting.
