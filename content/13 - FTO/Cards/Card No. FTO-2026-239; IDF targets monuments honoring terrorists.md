# IDF targets monuments honoring terrorists

[Source](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/434228)

(U) NABLUS, West Bank — The IDF launched a large-scale overnight operation across Judea and Samaria to demolish monuments honoring terrorists, Israel National News reported on October 7, 2026. Troops entered a number of Palestinian villages and cities and began demolishing dozens of monuments, with a main focal point in Nablus and another in the town of Ya'bad, southwest of Jenin, where a monument to Yasser al-Hamadouni was destroyed. The operation followed a mapping effort led in recent weeks by Finance Minister Bezalel Smotrich and MK Tzvi Sukkot, who catalogued dozens of monuments and demanded their demolition. Soldiers left messages warning residents that glorifying terrorism would not be tolerated and that the campaign would not be a one-time action. Smotrich and Sukkot welcomed the operation, saying it was cutting off "the infrastructure of incitement to terrorism."
