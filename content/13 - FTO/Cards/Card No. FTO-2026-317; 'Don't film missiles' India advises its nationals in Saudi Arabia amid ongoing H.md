# 'Don't film missiles': India advises its nationals in Saudi Arabia amid ongoing Houthi strikes

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/india/dont-film-missiles-india-advises-its-nationals-in-saudi-arabia-amid-ongoing-houthi-strikes/articleshow/135187788.cms)

(U) Saudi Arabia — The Times of India reported that India’s embassy urged nationals not to film or share information about missile and drone interceptions, citing potential legal liability under local rules. It also said Air India services to Riyadh and Dammam were suspended through November 7 and Jeddah flights through October 31, while the Indian consulate assisted travelers affected by disruptions.
