# Saudi coalition says two pilots killed in chopper crash near Yemen

[Source](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/world/saudi-arabia-helicopter-crash-houthis-yemen-6469396)

(U) Najran Region, Saudi Arabia — The Saudi-led coalition said two officers died when an AH-6i reconnaissance helicopter crashed inside Saudi territory near the Yemen border. Coalition spokesman Turki al-Malki attributed the crash to a technical malfunction, while Houthi spokesman Yahya Saree claimed the group had shot down a Saudi helicopter in the same area. The cause was therefore reported differently by the two parties.
