# Saudi Arabia urges EU to designate Houthis as terrorists amid renewed attacks

[Source](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/436118)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — Israel National News, citing six European officials quoted by Reuters, reports that Saudi Arabia is urging the EU to designate Yemen’s Houthi movement as a terrorist organization after weeks of near-daily attacks on the kingdom. Some EU governments reportedly oppose the move, arguing it could disrupt diplomatic contacts and peace efforts; any listing requires unanimous approval by all 27 member states. The Houthis are already subject to EU sanctions, while the United States, Canada, and Australia have designated the group as terrorist.
