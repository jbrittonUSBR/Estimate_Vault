# Houthis claim missile and drone strikes on Saudi Aramco facilities and Khamis Mushait airbase

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/houthis-claim-strikes-saudi-aramco-airbase/)

(U) SANAA, Yemen — Yemen's Houthi movement claimed responsibility on September 8, 2026 for coordinated ballistic missile and drone strikes on Saudi Aramco energy facilities and King Khalid Air Base in Khamis Mushait, with claimed targets also including Abha, Najran and Jizan. Saudi authorities said the attacks injured at least 73 civilians, including women and children, and caused fires and temporary operational halts at energy sites, while Aramco declined to detail damage. Houthi military spokesman Yahya Saree described the operation as retaliation for a Saudi-led air campaign, and independent verification of the group's damage claims was not available.
