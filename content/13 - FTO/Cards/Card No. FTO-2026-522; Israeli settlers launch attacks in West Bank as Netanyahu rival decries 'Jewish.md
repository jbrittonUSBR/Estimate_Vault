# Israeli settlers launch attacks in West Bank as Netanyahu rival decries 'Jewish terrorism'

[Source](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/22/middleeast/west-bank-attacks-eisenkot-intl)

(U) Ramallah, West Bank — CNN reported that residents said settlers set fire to at least a dozen vehicles and spray-painted "revenge" on homes in Kafr Malik, Deir as-Sudan and Deir Abu Masha'al, with no injuries reported. Israeli authorities said they opened an investigation, and police said they would enter the villages under military escort; no arrests had been announced. Gadi Eisenkot, who was seeking to form a government against Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, said the attacks were "acts of Jewish terrorism" and blamed far-right ministers Itamar Ben-Gvir and Bezalel Smotrich, while the UN counted 410 settler attacks linked to the olive harvest since September. Netanyahu issued no public statement, and CNN said it had contacted his office.
