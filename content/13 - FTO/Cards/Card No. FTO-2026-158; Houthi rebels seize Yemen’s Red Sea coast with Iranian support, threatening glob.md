# Houthi rebels seize Yemen’s Red Sea coast with Iranian support, threatening global shipping chokepoint

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/houthi-rebels-seize-yemen-red-sea-coast/)

(U) MOCHA, Yemen — Houthi forces captured the Red Sea port city of Mocha and roughly 5,400 square kilometers of territory across Yemen’s western coast in under 48 hours during an offensive that began around September 3 and culminated on September 10, according to an account published by Crypto Briefing citing observers. The outlet reported that IRGC advisers provided tactical guidance and intelligence, that Houthi control extended to Perim and the Hanish Islands positions dominating the Bab el-Mandeb Strait, and that a Houthi spokesman claimed government forces were expelled from six districts. It also reported drone attacks on a Saudi east-west oil pipeline and Brent crude rising above $105 a barrel; the casualty and territorial claims were not independently verified and the outlet is a financial news aggregator.
