# Gaza terrorists heard joking during Oct. 7 attack in newly released intercepted calls [Al-Muhtadi US visa fraud]

[Source](https://freerepublic.com/focus/f-news/4396541/posts)

(U) Kfar Aza, Israel — According to newly released intercepted phone calls from a US federal investigation into Mahmoud Amin Ya’qub al-Muhtadi, a member of the Democratic Front for the Liberation of Palestine's National Resistance Brigades, Gaza terrorists were heard joking and celebrating during the October 7, 2023 Hamas-led attack as they prepared to cross into Israel. The calls showed them scrambling to join the invasion, discussing hostages and expecting regional escalation, while al-Muhtadi later lied on his US visa application to enter the country.
