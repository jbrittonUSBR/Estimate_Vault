# WSE Shareholder Alert: September 29, 2026 Lead Plaintiff Deadline in Wise Group plc Securities Class Action - Contact SueWallSt

[Source](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/wse-shareholder-alert-september-29-2026-lead-plaintiff-deadline-in-wise-group-plc-securities-class-action---contact-suewallst-302868870.html)

(U) NEW YORK — A press release issued via PR Newswire publicized a securities class action against Wise Group plc (NASDAQ: WSE) and reminded investors of a September 29, 2026 deadline to move to be appointed lead plaintiff in the case. The notice stated that the class period runs from May 11, 2026 to July 23, 2026, and that the suit alleges the company's regulatory disclosures shifted from reassurance to reversal within weeks, with the stock falling on June 1, 2026 and June 2, 2026. The release was distributed by the law firm's marketing service SueWallSt and directs affected shareholders to contact the firm; it is a press release and not a judicial ruling.
