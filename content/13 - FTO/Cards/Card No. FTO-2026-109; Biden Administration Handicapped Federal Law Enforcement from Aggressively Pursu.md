# Biden Administration Handicapped Federal Law Enforcement from Aggressively Pursuing Terrorist Cartels, Says South Texas U.S. Attorney

[Source](https://www.breitbart.com/border/2026/09/16/biden-administration-handicapped-federal-law-enforcement-from-aggressively-pursuing-terrorist-cartels-says-south-texas-u-s-attorney/)

(U) SOUTH TEXAS — U.S. Attorney Aaron Reitz told Breitbart News that the Biden administration had handicapped federal law enforcement from aggressively pursuing terrorist-designated cartels. Following President Trump's designation of several cartels as Foreign Terrorist Organizations, prosecutors have moved to file terrorism charges against cartel members, including in connection with plots targeting law enforcement and officials. The partisan outlet Breitbart reported Reitz's claims as the Trump administration ramped up FTO-based enforcement actions.
