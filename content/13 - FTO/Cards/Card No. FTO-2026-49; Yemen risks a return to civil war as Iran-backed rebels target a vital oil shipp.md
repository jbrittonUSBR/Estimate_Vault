# Yemen risks a return to civil war as Iran-backed rebels target a vital oil shipping route

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/defence/yemen-risks-a-return-to-civil-war-as-iran-backed-rebels-target-a-vital-oil-shipping-route/articleshow/134014289.cms)

(U) CAIRO, Egypt — In a September 10, 2026 Associated Press report, Yemen was described as moving closer to a return to civil war as Iran-backed Houthi rebels threatened a crucial shipping alternative to the Strait of Hormuz. Residents said the Houthis had entered the key Red Sea port of Mokha, which had been held by Yemen's internationally recognized government, putting the rebels about 80 kilometers (50 miles) from the Bab el-Mandeb Strait and endangering another entry point for food and aid. The 2022 ceasefire had begun to buckle in recent weeks after the Houthis attacked several oil facilities and utilities in Saudi Arabia, wounding dozens, and after Saudi airstrikes on Sanaa's airport. Analysts cited, including the International Crisis Group's Ahmed Nagi, noted the Houthis now control a much larger stretch of coastline; the report said crude oil again traded above $100 a barrel amid the risk to another global shipping lane that usually carries about $1 trillion of goods a year.
