(U) Al-Qaeda’s Yemeni affiliate, Al-Qaeda in the Arabian Peninsula (AQAP), has published a new issue of its *Inspire Guide* series that glorifies the December 2025 Bondi Beach massacre in Australia and explicitly calls on followers living in Western democracies to carry out similar lone-wolf attacks. The guide frames the Bondi attack as a successful “blueprint” that security services cannot fully prevent, urging supporters to replicate it as “the least they can do” to strike at the West. It provides tactical guidance drawn from the incident while promoting the idea that such attacks can bypass traditional counterterrorism measures. The propaganda effort reflects AQAP’s ongoing strategy of inspiring low-tech, high-impact violence by self-radicalized individuals rather than centrally directed operations. Experts and officials have noted the risk of copycat attacks, particularly amid heightened global tensions and the approach of major events like the 2026 World Cup, as the group seeks to exploit grievances and online radicalization networks.
