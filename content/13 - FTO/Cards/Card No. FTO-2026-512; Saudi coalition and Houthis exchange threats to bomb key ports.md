# Saudi coalition and Houthis exchange threats to bomb key ports

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/20/saudi-coalition-and-houthis-exchange-threats-to-bomb-key-ports)

(U) Sanaa, Yemen — The Saudi-led coalition began a "targeted operation" against Hodeidah port and Houthi missile and drone sites in Sanaa, Saada and Taiz after urging civilians to evacuate the port area. Houthi spokesman Yahya Saree said Jeddah and other Saudi Red Sea ports would become targets and told airlines to avoid Saudi airspace. Al Jazeera's correspondent in Sanaa reported coalition strikes on military sites and workshops and described them as the heaviest air strikes since the conflict began; the Houthis were advancing on Aden while continuing to besiege Taiz.
