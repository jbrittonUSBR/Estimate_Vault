# Parents warned extremist groups are targeting a growing number of kids online across Prairies

[Source](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/manitoba/parents-warned-extremist-groups-9.7412854)

(U) Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada — CBC reported that police and researchers say violent extremist groups are grooming and recruiting children through social media, online games and messaging platforms, sometimes coercing them toward self-harm or real-world violence. The Evolve support program reported 10 online-extremism cases so far in 2026, compared with four in 2025 and two in 2024; police and researchers cautioned that online targeting can increase the risk of offline violence.
