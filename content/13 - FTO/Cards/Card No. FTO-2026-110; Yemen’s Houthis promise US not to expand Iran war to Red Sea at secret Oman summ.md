# Yemen’s Houthis promise US not to expand Iran war to Red Sea at secret Oman summit

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/09/16/world-news/yemens-houthis-promise-us-not-to-expand-iran-war-to-red-sea-at-secret-oman-summit/)

(U) WASHINGTON — At a secret summit in Muscat, Yemen's Houthis promised the United States they would not expand the Iran war into the Red Sea, agreeing to limit attacks to Saudi shipping in exchange for de-escalation. The Houthis have seized Mocha and several Bab al-Mandab islands, bombed oil pipelines, and previously disrupted Red Sea shipping; since an Oman-brokered truce in May 2025, attacks have subsided, though oil markets remain sensitive to any renewed escalation.
