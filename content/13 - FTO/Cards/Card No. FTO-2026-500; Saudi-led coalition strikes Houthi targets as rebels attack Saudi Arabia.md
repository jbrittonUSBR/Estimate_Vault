# Saudi-led coalition strikes Houthi targets as rebels attack Saudi Arabia

[Source](https://www.business-standard.com/world-news/saudi-led-coalition-strikes-houthi-targets-as-rebels-attack-saudi-arabia-126112100022_1.html)

(U) Sanaa, Yemen — The Associated Press reported that the coalition said its Friday strikes hit Hodeidah port facilities used to move weapons and ballistic missiles, along with missile and drone sites in Sanaa, Saada and Taiz, after Houthi missiles and drones were fired at Saudi Arabia. Spokesman Turki al-Malki said air defenses destroyed five drones and one ballistic missile, and Saudi civil defense reported material damage but no injuries at the Public Investment Fund tower in Riyadh's financial district. Houthi spokesman Yahya Saree warned that Jeddah and other Red Sea ports would be targeted and told airlines to avoid Saudi airspace.
