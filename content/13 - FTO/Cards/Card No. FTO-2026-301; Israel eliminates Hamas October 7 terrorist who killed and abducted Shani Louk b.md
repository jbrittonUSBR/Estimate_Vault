(U) October 21, 2026 — The Daily Mail reported that an Israeli airstrike killed Tamer Muhammad Ibrahim al-Sabbagh in Jabalia, northern Gaza, on Monday. The outlet, citing the Israeli military, identified him as a Hamas member involved in the October 7, 2023, abduction of Shani Louk and said he was the last member of the cell shown with her; the claim was attributed to Israeli sources.
