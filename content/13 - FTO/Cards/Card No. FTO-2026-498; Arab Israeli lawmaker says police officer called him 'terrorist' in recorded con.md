# Arab Israeli lawmaker says police officer called him 'terrorist' in recorded confrontation

[Source](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-crime/2026-11-19/ty-article/.premium/arab-israeli-mk-says-cop-called-him-terrorist-in-recorded-confrontation/000001a1-f4b4-d059-a5ff-f6fede310000)

(U) Southern Israel — Joint List MK al-Nasasra confronted police on Thursday after he saw officers allegedly mistreating a handcuffed Bedouin man, Haaretz reported. A recording captured him asking why the man was being humiliated, and he said an officer called him a "terrorist" after he returned to his car, an exchange that was not recorded.
