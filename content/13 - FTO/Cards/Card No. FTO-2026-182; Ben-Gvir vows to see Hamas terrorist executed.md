# Ben-Gvir vows to see Hamas terrorist executed

[Source](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/433735)

(U) Jerusalem, Israel — Israeli National Security Minister Itamar Ben-Gvir met in prison with Hassan Salameh, a senior member of Hamas' military wing convicted of orchestrating a series of deadly suicide bombings in the 1990s, and said he intends to work toward having him executed. Salameh was convicted of responsibility for three suicide attacks — including two bombings on Jerusalem's Route 18 in February and March 1996 — in which 46 people were killed and about 90 wounded, and was sentenced to 46 cumulative life sentences plus 20 additional years. Ben-Gvir said that meeting Salameh decades after protesting against his attacks, and now as national security minister, represented a full-circle moment.
