# Houthi offensive in Yemen adds to fear that Iran war could escalate into a wider global conflict

[Source](https://theconversation.com/houthi-offensive-in-yemen-adds-to-fear-that-iran-war-could-escalate-into-a-wider-global-conflict-286816)

(U) LANCASTER, United Kingdom — In a commentary for The Conversation, Simon Mabon, a professor of international relations at Lancaster University, argued that the Houthis' rapid advance down Yemen's Red Sea coast gave the Iran-backed group the potential to control traffic through the Bab al-Mandab strait. He wrote that the seizure of strategically important positions, combined with aerial attacks on Saudi Arabia, had already helped force Riyadh to close its East-West oil pipeline and could cut off the kingdom's ability to export oil. Mabon framed the crisis through the security-dilemma concept, warning that the U.S.-Iran war and the Russia-Ukraine war increasingly overlap and that Gulf states such as Saudi Arabia risk being drawn into a wider confrontation. The piece is expert analysis rather than a news report.
