# Panamanian authorities investigate bombing attack on investigative reporter's home

[Source](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/panamanian-authorities-investigate-bombing-attack-investigative-reporters-home-138147790)

(U) Panama City, Panama — The Associated Press reports that authorities are investigating an explosion at investigative journalist Rolando Rodríguez’s home as possible arson; no one was injured, although the property was damaged. La Prensa reported that a person arrived by taxi and threw an object toward the residence, while the Attorney General’s Office said the inquiry could expand if other crimes are identified. Rodríguez said he was preparing to publish reporting about alleged irregularities involving former officials and a government contractor; investigators had not identified a motive or perpetrator in the report.
