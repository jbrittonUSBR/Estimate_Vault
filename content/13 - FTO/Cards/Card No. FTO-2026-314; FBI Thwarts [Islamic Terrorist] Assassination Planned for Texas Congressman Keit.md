# FBI Thwarts Assassination Planned for Texas Congressman Keith Self

[Source](https://texasscorecard.com/federal/fbi-thwarts-assassination-planned-for-texas-congressman-keith-self/)

(U) McKinney, Texas — Texas Scorecard reported that a 17-year-old McKinney resident was arrested and charged in an alleged plot to assassinate U.S. Representative Keith Self and carry out an ISIS-inspired attack. The outlet said investigators believed the suspect obtained a firearm and planned an October 17 attack; the case remained an allegation, not a finding of guilt.
