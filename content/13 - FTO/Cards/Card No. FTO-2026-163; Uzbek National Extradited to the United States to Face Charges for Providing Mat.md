# Uzbek National Extradited to the United States to Face Charges for Providing Material Support to Foreign Terrorist Organizations

[Source](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/uzbek-national-extradited-united-states-face-charges-providing-material-support-foreign)

(U) WASHINGTON — The Justice Department’s Office of Public Affairs announced that Davud Ansariy, a Uzbek national and naturalized U.S. citizen, was arraigned in federal court in Brooklyn on an indictment charging him with conspiring to provide and providing material support to ISIS and the al-Nusrah Front, following his extradition from Tbilisi, Georgia, where he was arrested in August 2026. The release states that Ansariy led a Brooklyn-based network its members called “chayxona,” meaning tea house, that collected money to send fighters to Syria and to support their families, funding the travel of at least three people in 2013, all believed killed, and that he left the United States for Turkey in May 2016 after a co-conspirator’s arrest. The announcement reflects the department’s charges against the defendant, who is presumed innocent.
