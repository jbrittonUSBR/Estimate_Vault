# Civil Defense: One Killed, Two Injured by Intercepted Houthi Drone Debris in Taif

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/saudi/2026/saudi-260917-spa01.htm)

(U) RIYADH, Saudi Arabia — The General Directorate of Civil Defense said its teams in Taif Governorate responded on September 17, 2026, to debris from the interception and destruction of a drone launched by the Houthi movement, which the Saudi statement described as a terrorist militia. The directorate said the falling debris killed a Yemeni resident, injured a Saudi woman and a Pakistani resident whose condition was critical, and caused material damage to civilian buildings and vehicles. It said standard emergency procedures for such incidents had been implemented. The account was carried by the state-run Saudi Press Agency and republished by GlobalSecurity.org.
