# Morocco's king expresses solidarity with Saudi Arabia after Houthi attacks

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/saudi/2026/saudi-260928-azertac01.htm)

(U) RABAT — King Mohammed VI of Morocco sent telegrams to Saudi King Salman and Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman expressing solidarity after Houthi attacks on the kingdom, according to AZERTAC via GlobalSecurity.org. The king said that "any attack on Saudi Arabia is an attack on Morocco" and affirmed Rabat's support for Saudi security and territorial integrity. The messages were reported on September 28, 2026, amid escalating Houthi strikes on Saudi infrastructure.
