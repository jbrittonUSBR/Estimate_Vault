# U.S. military declares end of anti-ISIS Operation Inherent Resolve in Iraq

[Source](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/us-military-iraq-withdrawal-isis-operation-inherent-resolve-over/)

(U) WASHINGTON — The U.S. military declared an end to Operation Inherent Resolve in Iraq on September 30, 2026, closing out a 12-year campaign against ISIS, CBS News reported. Pentagon spokesman Sean Parnell announced that coalition personnel and equipment had left Erbil Air Base, completing a withdrawal set in motion by a 2024 agreement between Washington and Baghdad. About 2,000 U.S. troops had remained in Iraq as of February 2026, after roughly 1,500 left Syria earlier that year. Iraqi officials cast the announcement as an assertion of sovereignty, even as observers cautioned that ISIS cells and Iran-backed militias still pose a threat.
