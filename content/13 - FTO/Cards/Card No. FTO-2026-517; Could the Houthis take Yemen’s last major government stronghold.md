# Could the Houthis take Yemen’s last major government stronghold?

[Source](https://rt-english.com/news/649124-houthis-yemen-saudi-coalition/)

(U) Sanaa, Yemen — RT reported that Houthi forces advanced to within 60–100 km of Aden after Saudi-backed forces withdrew from positions near Bab el-Mandeb, citing military and security sources quoted by international media. UNICEF warned that more than 330,000 people, including 174,000 children, had been displaced by the renewed fighting, while Lloyd's List Intelligence counted 1,360 vessels transiting Bab el-Mandeb in October, 46% below pre-crisis levels. Political scientist Karim Sader told France 24 that encirclement of Aden was more likely than an assault, and RT noted the Yemeni government designated the Houthis a terrorist organization on Wednesday.
