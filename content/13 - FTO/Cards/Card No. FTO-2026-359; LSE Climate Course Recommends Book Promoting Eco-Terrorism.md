# LSE Climate Course Recommends Book Promoting Eco-Terrorism

[Source](https://wattsupwiththat.com/2026/11/04/lse-climate-course-recommends-book-promoting-eco-terrorism/)

(U) London, United Kingdom — Watts Up With That, republishing a Daily Sceptic commentary, criticized a London School of Economics first-year course reading list that includes *Property Will Cost Us the Earth*, an essay collection on climate direct action. The opinion article characterized parts of the book as sympathetic to sabotage and eco-terrorism; it is a polemical interpretation of the course materials, not an LSE finding or endorsement of unlawful acts.
