# Mother of ISIS terrorist who plotted to ‘create the next 9/11’ is running for public office in Canada, she says she wants ‘safe and welcoming’ schools

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/global-trends/mother-of-isis-terrorist-who-plotted-to-create-the-next-9/11-is-running-for-public-office-in-canada-she-says-she-wants-safe-and-welcoming-schools/articleshow/133979357.cms)

(U) MILTON, Canada — Dr. Khdiga R. Metwally, the mother of a convicted ISIS terrorist, was running for a school board trustee position in Canada, campaigning for the Halton District School Board Ward 1 and 2 trustee seat in Milton, about 30 miles outside Toronto. Her son, Abdulrahman El Bahnasawy, was serving a 40-year U.S. prison sentence after pleading guilty to terrorism charges over a 2016 ISIS-inspired plot to "create the next 9/11" that targeted Times Square, the New York City subway and concert venues. Metwally told the Daily Mail that her son, whom she described as mentally ill, was serving his sentence, and said she "rejects violence and extremism in all forms" and that her candidacy should be judged on her education experience and plans for students and families.
