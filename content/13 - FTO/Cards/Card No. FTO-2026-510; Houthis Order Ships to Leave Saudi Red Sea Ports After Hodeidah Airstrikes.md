# Houthis Order Ships to Leave Saudi Red Sea Ports After Hodeidah Airstrikes

[Source](https://gcaptain.com/houthis-order-ships-to-leave-saudi-red-sea-ports-after-hodeidah-airstrikes/)

(U) Hodeidah, Yemen — The Saudi-led coalition said it struck Hodeidah port on Friday to destroy capabilities it said were used to smuggle weapons and ballistic missiles, while Yemen's information minister said the port remained fully operational and goods continued to flow. Houthi military spokesman Yahya Saree urged shipping companies to evacuate Saudi Red Sea ports and warned airlines to avoid Saudi airspace, except near Mecca and Medina, saying the group would also target a power station at Shuqaiq. The coalition said it separately struck missile and drone capabilities in Sanaa, with operations continuing in Saada and Taiz.
