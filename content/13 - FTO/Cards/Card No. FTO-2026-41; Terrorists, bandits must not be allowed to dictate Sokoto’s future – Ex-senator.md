# Terrorists, bandits must not be allowed to dictate Sokoto’s future – Ex-senator

[Source](https://punchng.com/terrorists-bandits-must-not-be-allowed-to-dictate-sokotos-future-ex-senator/)

(U) SOKOTO, Nigeria — Former Senator and Niger Delta Development Commission member Abdullahi Gobir backed the renewed military offensive against terrorists, bandits and other criminal elements operating in Sokoto East and other vulnerable parts of Sokoto State, saying persistent attacks on rural communities had become a serious threat to lives, agriculture, livelihoods and economic activities. In a statement issued on Tuesday, Gobir said the operations by the Nigerian Army and other security agencies should be sustained until criminal networks responsible for killings, abductions and displacement were dismantled, and warned that individuals who harbour, finance, shield or provide logistical support to terrorists and bandits are a serious threat to ongoing operations. He commended the Nigerian Army, Nigeria Police Force, Department of State Services, Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps and Community Guards Corps, and stressed that military force alone could not end the crisis without effective intelligence gathering and community cooperation. The statement was reported by Nigeria's Punch newspaper.
