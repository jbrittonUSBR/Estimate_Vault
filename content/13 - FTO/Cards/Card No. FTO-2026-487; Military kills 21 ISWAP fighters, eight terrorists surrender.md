# Military kills 21 ISWAP fighters, eight terrorists surrender

[Source](https://punchng.com/military-kills-21-iswap-fighters-eight-terrorists-surrender/)

(U) Borno, Nigeria — Maj. Gen. Michael Onoja said air strikes near Monguno and Arinna killed 21 ISWAP fighters and wounded 10 others between November 13 and 19, including a Shura Council member and an IED expert. He said 26 people, comprising eight fighters and 18 family members, surrendered across five local government areas, and troops rescued four displaced people in Gwoza. Onoja also reported arrests of suspected collaborators, weapons recoveries and the rescue of kidnapped victims in several Nigerian states.
