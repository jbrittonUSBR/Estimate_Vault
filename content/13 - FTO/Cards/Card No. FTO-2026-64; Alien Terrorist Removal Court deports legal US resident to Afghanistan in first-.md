# Alien Terrorist Removal Court deports legal US resident to Afghanistan in first-ever case

[Source](https://www.npr.org/2026/09/12/nx-s1-5967476/alien-removal-court-deportation-afganistan)

(U) FORT WORTH, United States — The Justice Department brought the first-ever case before the Alien Terrorist Removal Court, created by Congress in 1996 and never previously used, to deport Nazira Haji Zada, 47, a lawful permanent resident who arrived from Afghanistan in 2018 and lived in Fort Worth, Texas. Haji Zada was arrested at her home in July; in filings unsealed on 11 September, Senior US District Judge Joan Ericksen ordered her removal on 20 August after she waived her right to challenge detention and conceded she was an "alien terrorist," with the supporting evidence remaining classified. Her court-appointed attorneys, Matthew Farley and Mary Manning Petras, argued that the secretive court's use of undisclosed evidence violated due process and said her consent to removal should not be read as an endorsement of its legitimacy.
