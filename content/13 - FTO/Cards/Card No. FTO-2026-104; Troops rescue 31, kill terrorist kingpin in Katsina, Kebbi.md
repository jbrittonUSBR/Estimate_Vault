# Troops rescue 31, kill terrorist kingpin in Katsina, Kebbi

[Source](https://punchng.com/troops-rescue-31-kill-terrorist-kingpin-in-katsina-kebbi/)

(U) KATSINA, Nigeria — The Joint Task Force North-West, Operation Fansan Yamma, said troops rescued 31 kidnapped victims and killed two terrorists, including a suspected kingpin, in separate operations in Katsina and Kebbi states on September 14 and 15, 2026. Media information officer Lt. Col. Aliyu Danja said in a statement issued on September 16 that 10 victims were rescued in Funtua Local Government Area of Katsina and 21 in Kangiwa Local Government Area of Kebbi. Troops killed Abbah Alhassan, described as a known terrorist kingpin, during an operation in the Matazu and Kankara areas of Katsina, and arrested two suspects said to be associated with a kingpin named Mannori. Items recovered included ammunition, a motorcycle, a cutlass, camouflage clothing and cannabis.
