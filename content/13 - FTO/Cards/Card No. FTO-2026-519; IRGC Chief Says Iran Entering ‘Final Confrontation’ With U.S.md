# IRGC Chief Says Iran Entering ‘Final Confrontation’ With U.S.

[Source](https://legalinsurrection.com/2026/11/irgc-chief-says-iran-entering-final-confrontation-with-u-s/)

(U) Tehran, Iran — Legal Insurrection reported that IRGC commander Maj. Gen. Ahmad Vahidi, quoted by Iran's WANA news agency, described the conflict with the United States and its allies as a "final confrontation" spanning military, political and media spheres. The post said the IRGC had stepped up drone strikes on Bahrain and Kuwait, both of which host U.S. troops, and cited CNN reporting that U.S. intelligence believed Tehran was developing plans for a dirty-bomb or chemical attack on Washington. It also quoted President Donald Trump telling Reuters he would decide on renewed strikes against Iran after the beginning of the year.
