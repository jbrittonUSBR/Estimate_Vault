# Red Sea in Turmoil: Houthi Attacks Undermine Saudi Vision 2030 and Regional Trade

[Source](https://www.naturalnews.com/2026-11-22-houthi-attacks-undermine-saudi-vision-2030-trade.html)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — NaturalNews.com, a commentary site, reported that Houthi spokesman Yahya Saree said on November 20 that the Shuqaiq power station and Riyadh's financial district were legitimate targets and urged people to leave, citing Houthi-run Al-Masirah TV. The article said the warnings followed a coalition call for civilians to evacuate Hodeidah port and came a day after the Public Investment Fund Tower, Riyadh's tallest skyscraper, was damaged in a suspected strike. It noted the reported damage could not be independently verified, and contrasted the Saudi energy ministry's description of limited damage from an earlier Aramco Tower strike with BBC Verify footage that it said showed more extensive damage.
