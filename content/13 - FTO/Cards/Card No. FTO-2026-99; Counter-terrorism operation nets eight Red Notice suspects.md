# Counter-terrorism operation nets eight Red Notice suspects

[Source](https://www.interpol.int/News-and-Events/News/2026/Counter-terrorism-operation-nets-eight-Red-Notice-suspects)

(U) LYON, France — A three-week INTERPOL-coordinated border operation across Algeria, Jordan, Morocco, Palestine and Tunisia resulted in 48 arrests, including eight people wanted under INTERPOL Red Notices and one under a Wanted Persons Diffusion, the organization announced on September 16, 2026. Operation Phoenix generated more than 700,000 cross-checks against INTERPOL databases and 246 hits, and seizures included 11 luxury vehicles, 1.8 kilograms of gold, 13,000 Captagon pills, 530 mobile phones and 1,525 cartons of smuggled cigarettes. INTERPOL said it was the first cross-Middle East and North Africa operation in which female police officers had full operational responsibility, and the arrests included a suspected mastermind of a 2022 shooting in Toulouse, France, detained at Algiers airport.
