# Saudi-led coalition says Houthis attempted to attack dozens of Saudi facilities

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/defence/escalating-conflict-houthis-launch-multiple-attacks-on-saudi-facilities-casualties-reported/articleshow/134971126.cms)

(U) SAUDI ARABIA — Reuters reported that the Saudi-led coalition in Yemen said the Houthis had attempted to attack dozens of Saudi airports, ports and oil facilities. The coalition’s spokesman described the alleged attacks as targeting civilian infrastructure, while reporting separately on the Houthi missile strike on a government site in Aden. The coalition’s account of the intended targets was an official claim and was not independently confirmed in the report.
