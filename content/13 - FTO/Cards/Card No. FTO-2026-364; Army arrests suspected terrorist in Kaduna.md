# Army arrests suspected terrorist in Kaduna

[Source](https://punchng.com/army-arrests-suspected-terrorist-in-kaduna/)

(U) Kagarko, Kaduna State, Nigeria — The Punch reported that troops arrested Auta Maikyala at a hospital where he was seeking treatment for a gunshot wound, in an operation involving the Department of State Services. The military said preliminary inquiries linked him to attacks, kidnapping, and cattle rustling; the report also described separate clearance operations in Zamfara State.
