# Houthis target Saudi oil facility as hostilities escalate in Yemen

[Source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-04/houthi-saudi-yemen-iraq-middle-east-hostilities/107226524)

(U) SANAA, Yemen — Yemen’s Iran-aligned Houthi forces launched ballistic missiles and drones at a Saudi Aramco oil facility south of Riyadh in retaliation for Saudi airstrikes, as renewed fighting over Yemen’s Red Sea coast escalated hostilities and worsened the country’s humanitarian crisis.
