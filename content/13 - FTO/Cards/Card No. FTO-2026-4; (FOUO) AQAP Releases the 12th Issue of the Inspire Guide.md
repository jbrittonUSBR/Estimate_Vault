(U//FOUO) On 23 March 2026, al-Qaeda in the Arabian Peninsula (AQAP) released their twelfth iteration of Inspire Guide. a This spring issue released a 16-page issue, titled “The Night Bullets Spoke.” This issue focuses on the recent attack in Australia and includes narrative reporting, ideological framing, and instructional guidance intended to inspire individuals to carry out independent attacks without direct organizational support.

(U//FOUO) The 12th issue highlighted the Bondi Beach, Australia, attack, and called for individuals to conduct similar actions in the United States, Israel, and other Western countries. The publication repeatedly directs readers, particularly those living in Western countries, to take action on their own. It states that individuals should not wait for instructions or coordination and instead act based on their own initiative. This approach aligns with the concept of “lone jihad,” and frames these types of actions as a responsibility. They emphasize that individuals can act without any need for training or support from any organization.

(U//FOUO) Previous issues of Inspire Guide give instructions, including visual aids on how to construct homemade explosive devices. Inspire Guide 12 focused on creating “Timed Hand Grenades," giving instructions on how to design the circuit, prepare the ignitor, and produce an operational grenade.
