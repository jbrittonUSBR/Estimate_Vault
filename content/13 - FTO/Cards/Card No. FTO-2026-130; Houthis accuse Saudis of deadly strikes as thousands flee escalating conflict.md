# Houthis accuse Saudis of deadly strikes as thousands flee escalating conflict

[Source](https://www.yahoo.com/news/world/articles/g7-leaders-condemn-houthi-strikes-040401302.html)

(U) SANAA, Yemen — Yemen's Houthi movement accused Saudi Arabia of a series of deadly strikes that killed civilians and prison detainees as Riyadh sought to repel a Houthi offensive that seized strategic Red Sea territory. The United Nations warned that displacement could rise from more than 130,000 people to over 230,000 in the coming months.
