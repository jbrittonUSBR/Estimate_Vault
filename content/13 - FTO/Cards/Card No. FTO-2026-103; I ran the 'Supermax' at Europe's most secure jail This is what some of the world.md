# I ran the 'Supermax' at Europe's most secure jail: This is what some of the world's most dangerous inmates told me over cups of tea... and why terrorists MUST be locked up in their own unit

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/crime-desk/article-16107819/Supermax-unit-Europe-secure-jail.html)

(U) VUGHT, Netherlands — Writing for the Daily Mail's Crime Desk, Arthur Parashar gave a first-person account of visiting PI Vught, which he described as Europe's most secure prison, including its 18-cell "Supermax" unit and a separate terrorist wing. The feature quoted former prison director Yola Wanders, who ran the high-security units from 2009 to 2020, on the isolation regime applied to organized crime leaders and murderers, the constant supervision of inmates outside their cells, heavy monitoring of outside contact and a 22-million-euro on-site courtroom. Wanders argued that terrorist inmates should be concentrated together because the main risks are radicalization, recruitment and influence over others, while cautioning that "if you treat them like animals, then you create animals," and said such prisoners fall into ideological leaders and a larger group of followers. The piece is a personal feature that advocates the Dutch model as a possible example for the United Kingdom's overcrowded prison system, not a news report.
