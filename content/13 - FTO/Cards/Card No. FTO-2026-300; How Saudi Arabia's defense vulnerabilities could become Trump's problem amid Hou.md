# Houthi attacks expose challenges for Saudi air defenses and US policy

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/saudi-arabia-defense-vulnerabilities-trump-problem-houthi-onslaught)

(U) October 21, 2026 — Fox News analysis reported that Saudi Arabia sought additional allied support as Houthi missile and drone attacks targeted cities, airports, and energy infrastructure. Experts quoted by Fox argued that the kingdom's size, limited interceptor stocks, and difficulty detecting lower-flying threats constrained its defenses; the article framed whether Washington should expand its role as a policy dilemma for President Donald Trump.
