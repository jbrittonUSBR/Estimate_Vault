# Terrorist Attack? Two German Passport Holders Held Over Starlink Antenna Fire Near Sofia.

[Source](https://freerepublic.com/focus/f-news/4400424/posts)

(U) Plana, Bulgaria — Novinite reported that two German-passport holders, aged 20 and 21, were detained over the deliberate burning of 11 Starlink antennas at a facility near Sofia on October 9–10. Bulgarian authorities were investigating the fire as a possible terrorist attack; the report attributed that characterization to investigators and did not establish motive or responsibility in court.
