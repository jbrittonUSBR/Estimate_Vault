# DSS arraigns seven over terrorism, kidnapping in Kebbi

[Source](https://punchng.com/dss-arraigns-seven-over-terrorism-kidnapping-in-kebbi/)

(U) BIRNIN KEBBI, NIGERIA — The Department of State Services arraigned seven people before a Federal High Court on charges including supporting terrorism, withholding information and unlawful firearms possession. Punch reported that prosecutors accused five defendants of buying and selling cattle rustled by the Lakurawa group, another of relaying information about a suspected terrorist leader, and a third of holding a revolver and ammunition. All seven pleaded not guilty, and Justice Emmanuel Gakko adjourned the cases to December 3, 2026.
