# US Military Accused of “Weaponizing” El Niño Against Somali Civilians

[Source](https://www.motherjones.com/politics/2026/10/us-military-africom-weaponizing-el-nino-somali-civilians-women-children-al-shabaab-airstrikes-drones-flooding-usaid/)

(U) Jilib, Somalia — Mother Jones, republishing a Guardian investigation, reported residents' allegations that U.S. airstrikes against al-Shabaab were pushing civilians toward flood-prone areas as El Niño rains approached. AFRICOM denied targeting civilians or using weather as a method of warfare; the article also reported aid shortages and displacement risks. The alleged strategy is disputed, not an established finding.
