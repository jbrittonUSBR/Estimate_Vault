(U) London, UK — NaturalNews alleges that a compulsory first-year course at the London School of Economics includes a collection on direct climate action and sabotage, edited by Jessie Kindig and introduced by Andreas Malm. The article cites a Daily Wire investigation claiming Malm’s book *How to Blow Up a Pipeline* is promoted at multiple universities and quotes pro-sabotage language attributed to Malm and Kindig. It argues that the course materials may encourage eco-sabotage; that conclusion is the article’s commentary, not evidence of an actual planned attack.
