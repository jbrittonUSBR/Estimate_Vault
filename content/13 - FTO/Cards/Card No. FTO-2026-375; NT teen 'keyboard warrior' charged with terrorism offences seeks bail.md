# NT teen 'keyboard warrior' charged with terrorism offences seeks bail

[Source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-09/nt-teen-terrorism-offences-bail-application-keyboard-warrior/107364124)

(U) Darwin, Northern Territory, Australia — ABC News reported that a 14-year-old charged with collecting documents likely to facilitate terrorist acts, possessing violent extremist material, and possessing firearm- or explosives-making material applied for bail. Prosecutors opposed release; the judge reserved a decision and sought information about specialist support in custody, while the defence argued the boy was troubled but not dangerous.
