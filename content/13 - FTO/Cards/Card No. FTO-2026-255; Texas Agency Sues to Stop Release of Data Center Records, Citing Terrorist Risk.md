# Texas Agency Sues to Stop Release of Data Center Records, Citing Terrorist Risk

[Source](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/southcentral/2026/10/08/888504.htm)

(U) AUSTIN, Texas — A Texas state agency filed suit to block release of records related to a data center, citing potential terrorist risk to critical infrastructure as grounds for exemption under state law; the legal action frames the dispute as a policy matter rather than a reported attack.
