# Iran dismantles three terrorist operational cells

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/iran/2026/09/iran-260920-irna08.htm)

(U) Tehran, Iran — Iran’s Ministry of Intelligence said it had killed or arrested members of three terrorist cells in West Azerbaijan, Alborz, and Kerman provinces, alleging the cells were affiliated with “terrorist groups acting as mercenaries for the American-Zionist enemy” and planned assassinations and sabotage of vital infrastructure.
