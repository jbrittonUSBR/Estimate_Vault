# Pentagon insider smears antifascists who unmasked Nazi terrorists later convicted by DOJ

[Source](https://www.rawstory.com/raw-investigates/antifascist/)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — A Raw Story investigation reported that Jennica Pounds, a Pentagon special government employee known online as Data Republican, built a searchable database of antifascist websites hosted on the Noblogs platform days after the State Department designated their provider, Autistici/Inventati, as a terrorist entity. Raw Story reported that Pounds said she scraped more than 40,000 NoBlogs articles and indexed 1,717 items containing personally identifiable information, describing the sites as an elaborate doxxing scheme. The article noted that several of the targeted sites had previously helped unmask violent extremists, including Terrorgram Collective co-leader Dallas Humber, who is serving a 30-year prison sentence, and that Raw Story could not independently verify the figures Pounds cited. The piece is an investigative report by a correspondent specializing in right-wing extremism and presents critical characterizations of Pounds's conduct.
