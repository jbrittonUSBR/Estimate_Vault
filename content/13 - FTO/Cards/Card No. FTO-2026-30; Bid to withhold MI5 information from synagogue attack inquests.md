# Bid to withhold MI5 information from synagogue attack inquests

[Source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c87vp8v8vdeo)

(U) MANCHESTER, United Kingdom — The UK Government applied to block certain MI5 information relating to the fatal Manchester synagogue attack from being heard in public, a pre-inquest hearing at Manchester Coroner's Court heard. The Home Office sought to withhold the information from the inquests into the deaths of Adrian Daulby, 53, and Melvin Cravitz, 66, who were killed when Jihad Al-Shamie, 35, drove into the gates of the Heaton Park Hebrew Congregation Synagogue on 2 October last year and attacked with a knife while wearing a fake suicide belt. Home Office counsel Neil Sheldon KC said disclosure would harm national security, while lawyers for the victims' families said the Public Interest Immunity application contradicted public statements that the attacker was not known to counter-terror policing or the security services.
