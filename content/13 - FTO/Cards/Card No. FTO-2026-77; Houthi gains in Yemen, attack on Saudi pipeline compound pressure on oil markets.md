# Houthi gains in Yemen, attack on Saudi pipeline compound pressure on oil markets

[Source](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/show/houthi-gains-in-yemen-attack-on-saudi-pipeline-compound-pressure-on-oil-markets)

(U) WASHINGTON — A PBS NewsHour segment reported that Houthi forces had seized the Greater and Lesser Hanish islands, Mokha port and Mayun island, giving the group control of Yemen's west coast and the Bab al-Mandab strait, a chokepoint for about 10 percent of global trade. The segment said Saudi Arabia's 745-mile East-West pipeline had been out of service for weeks after an attack blamed on Iran-backed Iraqi militias, while a mosque in Jazan was damaged and two people wounded and the Khamis Mushait air base was hit. More than 85,000 people had been displaced in Yemen since July, with more than 2,000 crossing into Djibouti, and oil prices traded near $110 a barrel. President Donald Trump said the United States was holding direct talks with the Houthis and had not conducted strikes, and former U.S. Ambassador Michael Ratney told PBS the situation had significantly raised risks to regional oil exports.
