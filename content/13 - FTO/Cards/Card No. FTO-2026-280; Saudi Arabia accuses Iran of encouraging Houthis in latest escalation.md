# Saudi Arabia accuses Iran of encouraging Houthis in latest escalation

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/world-news/saudi-arabia-blames-iran-for-houthi-escalation-amid-intensifying-conflict/articleshow/134971264.cms)

(U) SAUDI ARABIA — AFP reported that Saudi Arabia accused Iran of encouraging the Houthis amid escalating attacks on Saudi targets. A Saudi foreign ministry official said Tehran had encouraged the escalation, while Riyadh also accused Iran of arming, training and supporting the group in violation of UN Security Council resolutions. These were Saudi allegations and were not independently verified in the report.
