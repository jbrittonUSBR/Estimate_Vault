# Pennsylvania man accused of plotting ISIS attack arrested with rifle and 190 rounds of ammunition: DOJ

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/us/pennsylvania-man-accused-plotting-isis-attack-arrested-rifle-190-rounds-ammunition-doj)

(U) PITTSBURGH — Jonathan Hunter Kramer, 21, of Valencia, Pennsylvania, was arrested at a Cranberry Township hotel and charged with receiving a firearm and ammunition intended for ISIS, the Justice Department said, according to Fox News. Investigators seized a semiautomatic rifle, 190 rounds of ammunition, five rifle magazines (three capable of holding 30 rounds), a scope, a bipod, an entrenchment tool, pepper spray and about 30 knives, and witnesses at the store and hotel said the bag Kramer carried made a metallic clicking sound. Prosecutors said an anonymous tip on March 19, shortly after his release from juvenile detention, led to the renewed investigation and that no specific target had been identified; Kramer was booked into the Butler County Jail and made his first court appearance Monday, with prosecutors seeking to hold him without bond. The charge is an allegation and has not been proven.
