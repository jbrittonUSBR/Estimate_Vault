# All U.S. B-1 Bombers Return to U.S. From British Terrorism Alert Air Base

[Source](https://www.breitbart.com/europe/2026/10/05/all-u-s-b-1-bombers-return-to-u-s-from-british-terrorism-alert-air-base/)

(U) FAIRFORD, United Kingdom — All U.S. B-1 bombers stationed at RAF Fairford returned to the United States following a heightened terrorism alert at the British air base. The move came as British authorities maintained increased security measures amid ongoing concerns over potential terrorist threats in the region.
