# ‘I Like to Kill Jews’: Columbia Student Facing Deportation has History of Supporting Terrorism, Antisemitism

[Source](https://legalinsurrection.com/2026/09/i-like-to-kill-jews-columbia-student-facing-deportation-has-history-of-supporting-terrorism-antisemitism/)

(U) NEW YORK — A 100-page investigation by journalists David Collier and Rachel Feldman reported that Mohsen Mahdawi, a Palestinian Columbia University student facing deportation over his role in the anti-Israel campus protest movement, had a previously undocumented history of statements supporting terrorists, armed violence, and antisemitism. According to the report, Mahdawi promised to "annihilate the Jews," praised Hamas, honored dead terrorists, and bragged that Palestinians "have the capacity and conviction to resist, to kill and to wound," with some postings published as recently as 2024 while he was on US soil. Mahdawi, who graduated from Columbia and is believed to be a graduate student there, was briefly in immigration detention and released in April of last year; his case has continued in the courts. The findings, reported by Legal Insurrection citing the Washington Free Beacon, were presented as undermining portrayals of Mahdawi as a peaceful activist.
