# Yemen’s Houthis move to control crucial Waterway – our Research maps the Countries most at Risk

[Source](https://www.juancole.com/2026/09/waterway-research-countries.html)

(U) SANAA, Yemen — On September 29, 2026, analysis published by The Conversation and republished on Juan Cole's website examined the Houthis' efforts to consolidate control over key Red Sea shipping lanes. The researchers mapped the countries most exposed to disruptions along the vital maritime corridor, warning that continued Houthi influence over those waterways carried significant economic and security implications for regional and global actors.
