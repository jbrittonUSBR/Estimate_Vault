# Mike Huckabee responds to [jihad] stabbing attack, slamming world hypocrisy

[Source](https://freerepublic.com/focus/f-news/4394548/posts)

(U) JERUSALEM, Israel — U.S. Ambassador to Israel Mike Huckabee condemned a terrorist stabbing attack at Ma'oz Yehuda Farm in Samaria on Monday, September 7, 2026, saying such attacks receive far less international attention than attacks on Palestinians. "This won't get the international attention that attacks on Palestinians receive but it's a daily occurrence that Israelis have to live with," Huckabee wrote, offering prayers for the "Peace of Jerusalem." Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu also condemned the attack and praised the IDF soldiers who engaged and eliminated the terrorist, vowing Israel would continue to eliminate terrorists, hunt down those who dispatch them, and destroy terrorist infrastructure in Judea and Samaria.
