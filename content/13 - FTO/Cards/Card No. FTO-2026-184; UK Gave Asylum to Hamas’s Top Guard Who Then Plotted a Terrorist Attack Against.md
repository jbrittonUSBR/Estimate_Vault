# UK Gave Asylum to Hamas’s Top Guard Who Then Plotted a Terrorist Attack Against Jews

[Source](https://freerepublic.com/focus/f-chat/4397062/posts)

(U) LONDON — The Sunday Times reported that a former top bodyguard to Hamas leader Ismail Haniyeh, identified only as "Mr. X" for legal reasons, obtained asylum in Britain and lived there for more than a decade while allegedly plotting an attack on European Jews, according to Mediaite. The report said the married father in his forties, an IT consultant in a large English city, was arrested by National Crime Agency officers in London in November after Mossad and German intelligence learned of the plan, and that seven suspects were detained in coordinated operations across Austria, Denmark and the UK. Mr. X faced extradition to Germany over an alleged plot to attack Jewish community centers, Israeli diplomats and pro-Israel demonstrations; Mediaite's account rested on the newspaper's reporting and the claims had not been independently verified.
