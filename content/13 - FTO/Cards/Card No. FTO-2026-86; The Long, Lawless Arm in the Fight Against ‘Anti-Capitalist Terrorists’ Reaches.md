# The Long, Lawless Arm in the Fight Against ‘Anti-Capitalist Terrorists’ Reaches Into Europe as Autistici/Inventati Is Debanked, Hacked, and Has Its Domain Shut Down

[Source](https://www.nakedcapitalism.com/2026/09/the-long-lawless-arm-in-the-fight-against-anti-capitalist-terrorists-reaches-into-europe-as-autistici-inventati-is-debanked-hacked-and-has-its-domain-shut-down.html)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — In a commentary piece for naked capitalism, Conor Gallagher argued that the U.S. State Department's August 26, 2026, designation of the Italian digital collective Autistici/Inventati as part of a "transnational far-left terrorist network" set a dangerous precedent for the digital commons and free speech in Europe. According to the account, Italy's Banca Etica closed the collective's accounts, the Public Interest Registry shut down its domain, and the group was hit by a cyberattack before announcing on September 6 that it would discontinue its services to protect its engineers and users. Gallagher wrote that no evidence of terrorism had been presented in court, linked the action to the 2025 National Security Presidential Memorandum 7, and criticized European officials for failing to respond. The piece is advocacy commentary rather than a straight news report.
