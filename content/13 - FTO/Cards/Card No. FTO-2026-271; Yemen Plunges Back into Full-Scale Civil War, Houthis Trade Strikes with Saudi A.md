# Yemen Plunges Back into "Full-Scale Civil War," Houthis Trade Strikes with Saudi Arabia

[Source](https://www.democracynow.org/2026/10/13/houthis_strike_saudi)

(U) RIYADH, Saudi Arabia — On October 13, 2026, Democracy Now! reported that Yemen had plunged back into full-scale civil war as Iran-backed Houthi forces traded strikes with Saudi Arabia, launching missiles and drones at Riyadh’s King Khalid International Airport in attacks that killed at least 13 people and injured over 300. The escalation came as Houthis captured key Red Sea coastline areas near the Bab al-Mandab Strait and fought Saudi-backed government forces on multiple fronts, with humanitarian conditions deteriorating across Yemen.
