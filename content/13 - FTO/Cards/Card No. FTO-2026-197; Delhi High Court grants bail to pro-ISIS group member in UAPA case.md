# Delhi High Court grants bail to pro-ISIS group member in UAPA case

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/india/delhi-high-court-grants-bail-to-pro-isis-group-member-in-uapa-case/articleshow/134614176.cms)

(U) NEW DELHI, India — The Delhi High Court on October 1, 2026 granted bail to Mohd Shahzad Kamal, an alleged member of the pro-ISIS group Harkat-ul-Harb-E-Islam, in a case under the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act, with a bench of Justices Navin Chawla and Arun Bhardwaj ordering simply, "Bail granted." According to the National Investigation Agency, Kamal and co-accused entered into a criminal conspiracy to commit terrorist acts and provided funds for "jihadi activities" by the ISIS-affiliated group, and he was arrested on August 31, 2021 in an NIA case registered in 2018. He approached the High Court after the trial court rejected his bail application on May 29, 2024, and a copy of the High Court's verdict was awaited, PTI reported.
