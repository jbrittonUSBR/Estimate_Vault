# Saudi-led coalition says Riyadh financial district skyscraper targeted in 'terrorist attack'

[Source](https://m.economictimes.com/news/international/world-news/saudi-led-coalition-says-riyadh-financial-district-skyscraper-targeted-in-terrorist-attack/articleshow/136134742.cms)

(U) RIYADH, SAUDI ARABIA — Reuters reported that the Saudi-led coalition described the targeting of a skyscraper in Riyadh's financial district as a "terrorist attack," while Saudi Civil Defense said 11 people were injured. The coalition provided no further details, according to the report. Reuters noted that the Houthis had intensified attacks on Saudi airports and oil infrastructure since Yemen's civil war reignited in July and had made territorial gains against the Saudi-backed government.
