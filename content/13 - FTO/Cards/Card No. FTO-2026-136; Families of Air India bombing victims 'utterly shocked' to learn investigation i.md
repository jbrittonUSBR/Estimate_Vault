# Families of Air India bombing victims 'utterly shocked' to learn investigation is inactive

[Source](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/air-india-investigation-dead-9.7352244)

(U) TORONTO, Canada — Families of the victims of the 1985 Air India bombing sent letters to the RCMP and the prime minister expressing dismay after being told at a September 12 meeting that the investigation had been deemed inactive, though the RCMP said the case remained open and would be pursued if new information emerged.
