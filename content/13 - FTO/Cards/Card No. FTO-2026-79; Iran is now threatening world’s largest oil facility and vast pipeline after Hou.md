# Iran is now threatening world’s largest oil facility and vast pipeline after Houthi attacks escalate

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/09/15/world-news/how-the-houthis-attacks-on-saudi-arabia-dramatically-increase-oil-supply-risk/)

(U) DUBAI — The New York Post reported that Houthi attacks had increasingly threatened Saudi Arabia's Abqaiq processing facility, the world's largest, and the 745-mile East-West pipeline that carries crude to the Red Sea. Saudi Red Sea exports fell to about 2.5 million barrels per day last month from 4.6 million in July, according to the International Energy Agency, while the pipeline had been carrying about 4 million barrels per day, roughly 4 percent of world supply. The report said the Yanbu export terminal had only five to seven days of export capacity and that Brent crude traded above $109 a barrel and the U.S. benchmark above $105, with the Bab al-Mandab strait only 20 miles wide. Kelly Campa of the Institute for the Study of War was quoted on the vulnerability, and the article noted that Trump and Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman had spoken but that no U.S. strikes had followed.
