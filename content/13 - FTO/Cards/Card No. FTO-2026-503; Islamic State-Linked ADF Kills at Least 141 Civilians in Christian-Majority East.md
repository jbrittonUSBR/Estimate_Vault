# Islamic State-Linked ADF Kills at Least 141 Civilians in Christian-Majority Eastern Congo

[Source](https://www.thegatewaypundit.com/2026/11/islamic-state-linked-adf-kills-least-141-civilians/)

(U) Mambasa, Democratic Republic of the Congo — The Gateway Pundit reported that the Islamic State-affiliated Allied Democratic Forces killed at least 75 civilians and abducted about 40 people in Mambasa territory in the week ending November 14, and struck Cantine-Aloya in North Kivu on November 5–6, where the WHO and Africa CDC put the toll at 66 including three health workers. Citing local civil society figures and Africanews, the article said victims were hacked or shot, more than 50 houses were burned and three health facilities were looted, prompting 180 patients to flee amid an Ebola outbreak. It attributed to FDD's Long War Journal the claim that most victims counted as killed by the group's Central Africa branch were Christian villagers, and noted North Kivu's military governor urged stronger Congolese-Ugandan joint operations on November 20.
