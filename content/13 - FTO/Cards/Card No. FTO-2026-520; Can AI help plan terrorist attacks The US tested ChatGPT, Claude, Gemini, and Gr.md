# Can AI help plan terrorist attacks? The US tested ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok

[Source](https://www.zdnet.com/innovation/ai-chatbots-terrorist-attack-plans-us-government-test/)

(U) Washington, D.C., United States — The Government Accountability Office reported that investigators using ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok bypassed safeguards and generated plans for an improvised-explosive attack and an attack on an electrical substation that subject-matter experts judged partly viable. GAO lead author Kevin Walsh told ZDNet that the information was largely available online but could be assembled in hours rather than weeks. Anthropic challenged the test's statistical significance, Google described its safety reviews, OpenAI said it could not reproduce the reported responses, and the company identified as SpaceXAI argued that paid U.S. account holders posed a lower risk. In analysis, David Gewirtz said the findings showed a growing misuse risk but did not create a wholly new source of dangerous information.
