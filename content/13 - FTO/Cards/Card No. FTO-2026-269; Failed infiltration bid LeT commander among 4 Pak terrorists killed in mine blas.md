# Failed infiltration bid: LeT commander among 4 Pak terrorists killed in mine blast in J&K's Poonch

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/india/failed-infiltration-bid-let-commander-among-4-pak-terrorists-killed-in-mine-blast-in-jks-poonch/articleshow/134896260.cms)

(U) POONCH, India — On October 12, 2026, The Times of India reported that four Pakistan-occupied Kashmir residents, including a Lashkar-e-Taiba commander, were killed in a suspected improvised explosive device blast in the Hajira tehsil area near the Line of Control in Poonch district during what appeared to be a failed infiltration attempt. Security forces on the Indian side heightened alert and vigilance along the LoC following the incident.
