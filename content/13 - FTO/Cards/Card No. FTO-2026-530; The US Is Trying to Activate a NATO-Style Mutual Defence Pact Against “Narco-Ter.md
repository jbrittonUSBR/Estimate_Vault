# The US Is Trying to Activate a NATO-Style Mutual Defence Pact Against “Narco-Terrorism” for the American Continent

[Source](https://www.nakedcapitalism.com/2026/11/the-us-is-trying-to-activate-a-nato-style-mutual-defence-pact-against-narco-terrorism-on-the-american-continent.html)

(U) WASHINGTON, D.C. — Naked Capitalism guest writer Nick Corbishley reported that 12 OAS member states, including the United States, pushed through a resolution to invoke the 1947 Rio Treaty against groups Washington labels "narco-terrorists." He wrote that the vote, backed by 14 of 18 members, sent the issue to foreign ministers within 75 days but did not authorize force. Corbishley noted that Brazil voted against the move and Mexico objected, and argued that it extended US military influence across the hemisphere.
