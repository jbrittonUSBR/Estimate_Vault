# CENTCOM Says Hormuz 'Remains Open' As Houthi-Saudi Fighting Threatens Another Key Waterway

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/library/news/2026/09/mil-260916-rferl04.htm)

(U) WASHINGTON — U.S. Central Command said on September 16, 2026 that the Strait of Hormuz "remains open for transit," asserting that traffic was flowing and momentum building despite what it called Iranian attempts to intimidate civilian crews. CENTCOM said thousands of commercial ships and nearly one billion barrels of oil had transited the strait in recent months, though commercial traffic has fallen sharply since the Iran war began in February. The statement came as Houthi forces captured ground overlooking the Bab el-Mandeb Strait and Saudi warplanes struck Yemen while Houthis fired drones and missiles at Saudi cities, threatening a second regional energy chokepoint.
