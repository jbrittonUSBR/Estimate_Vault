(U//LES) Since at least 2023, Tren de Aragua (TdA) members within the United States have organized parties that last from several hours to multiple days across various states. These gatherings frequently involve and facilitate criminal activity, including planning and coordination. Such events are typical within TdA’s strongholds in Latin America, where they serve as mechanisms to enable and advance criminal operations. Parties have been held in vacation rentals, warehouses, Latin bars and lounges, and private residences.

(U//LES) TdA uses parties to recruit, fundraise, and social network: 

- (U//LES) Recruit: TdA members bring potential recruits as guests to showcase the gang’s easy access to drugs, sex, and money. In parties in Salt Lake City, Utah, each TdA member brought a guest they would attempt to recruit.
- (U//LES) Fundraise: TdA members sell tusi (pink cocaine), unrefined ketamine, marijuana, cocaine, stolen liquor, gold, and sex. TdA recruits women specifically for prostitution at parties and coordinates a driver to bring them to party locations. At reported parties in Florida, TdA members skimmed and cloned credit cards used by attendees to purchase drugs, alcohol, and prostitutes. The stolen card information was then used by TdA members to buy goods, which were subsequently resold for profit.
- (U//LES) Network: At parties, attendees wanting criminal services from TdA—primarily jackpotting, prostitutes, or drugs— meet and socialize with TdA members providing those services. Criminal activity is initiated and coordinated.

(U//LES) As of 2025, TdA carefully organizes its parties by advertising via private social media chats and withholding details, such as time and place, until hours before the event. Prospective attendees are vetted prior to admission, and the gang maintains heightened security measures, including deploying lookouts to monitor for law enforcement activity nearby. In response to enhanced U.S. immigration enforcement and deportations, TdA has adapted its parties, and will probably continue to do so. In Texas, TdA has started using large ranch-style properties along the outskirts of major cities instead of renting vacation rentals.
