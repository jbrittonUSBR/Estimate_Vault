# ISIS-K Terrorist Sentenced to 20 Years in Prison for Role in Abbey Gate and Other Terror Attacks

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/security/library/news/2026/09/sec-260916-doj01.htm)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — The U.S. Department of Justice announced that Mohammad Sharifullah, an Afghan national and ISIS-K member, was sentenced to 20 years in prison for his participation in a nearly decade-long conspiracy to provide material support to a designated foreign terrorist organization. According to court records, Sharifullah conducted surveillance of the road to Kabul's Abbey Gate on August 26, 2021, and reported it clear before a fellow ISIS-K operative detonated a body-worn bomb that killed 13 U.S. service members and about 160 civilians. Prosecutors said he also supported the 2016 attack on Nepali security guards at the Canadian embassy in Kabul and shared instructions on AK-style rifles with the gunmen who killed more than 100 people at Crocus City Hall near Moscow in March 2024. The FBI Washington Field Office investigated the case, which was prosecuted in the Eastern District of Virginia. The account derives from a Department of Justice press release reproduced by GlobalSecurity.org.
