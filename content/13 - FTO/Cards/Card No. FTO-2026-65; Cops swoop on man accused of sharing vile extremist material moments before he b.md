# Cops swoop on man accused of sharing vile extremist material moments before he boarded plane to Afghanistan

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16129305/Melbourne-arrest-extremist-isis.html)

(U) MELBOURNE, Australia — Australian Federal Police arrested Mohammad Emad uddin Ahmadzai, 30, at Melbourne Airport on 12 September, shortly before he was due to board an Etihad flight to Kabul, and charged him with one count of possessing violent extremist material and another of contravening a requirement under the Crimes Act. The arrest was the sixth made in connection with an alleged extremist group chat; devices seized in a 7 May raid on a home in Roxburgh Park allegedly contained footage of shootings, executions and beheadings as well as ISIS flags and logos. Ahmadzai told officers in a recorded interview that he was taking his family to visit his sick father, and the matter was adjourned to Melbourne Magistrates' Court.
