# Saudi Arabia turns to Syria for help as fighting with Houthis escalates

[Source](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/saudi-arabia-turns-syria-fighting-houthis-escalates-137956922)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — The Associated Press, carried by ABC News, reported that Syria was preparing to send an initial force of about 600 fighters to Saudi Arabia, while Syrian military advisers had already deployed. Officials speaking anonymously said the force would be stationed in Saudi Arabia and that no fighters had been sent to Yemen; Saudi officials had not publicly confirmed the plan.
