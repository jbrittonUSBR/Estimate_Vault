# EU warns airlines to avoid parts of Saudi airspace amid Houthi attacks

[Source](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/433917)

(U) BRUSSELS, Belgium — The European Union Aviation Safety Agency on September 30, 2026 issued a new warning advising airlines not to fly through a designated area of Saudi Arabian airspace at any altitude, citing a "highly volatile" security environment amid the U.S.-Iran conflict, with the advisory valid through November 16 unless revised earlier. EASA said Houthi attacks on Saudi maritime, energy and infrastructure targets expanded significantly in frequency and geographic reach during September, with missiles and drones reaching farther into the kingdom, including the Riyadh, Tabuk and Yanbu areas, in addition to southern and southwestern regions. The agency warned of risks including misidentification of civilian aircraft, interception activity and falling debris, and urged operators to maintain contingency plans; Israel National News reported that the restricted zone lies within the Jeddah Flight Information Region.
