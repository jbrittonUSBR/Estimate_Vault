# Portland teen faces terrorism, attempted murder charges after school cafeteria chemical scare

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/us/portland-teen-faces-terrorism-attempted-murder-charges-after-school-cafeteria-chemical-scare)

(U) PORTLAND, Oregon — A 15-year-old Benson Polytechnic High School student was charged with domestic terrorism, attempted murder and multiple weapons counts after allegedly mixing chemicals in the school cafeteria and releasing toxic hydrogen sulfide gas. The incident left at least one student in critical condition and prompted prosecutors to seek adult trial on 19 counts.
