(U//FOUO) Harakat Ashab al-Yamin al-Islamia Movement, also known as Ashab alYamin, issued an Arabic-language statement addressed to POTUS using various derogatory terms asking, "do you know where we start?". The statement continued: "from the heart of the event, where your daughter (POTUS' daughter), that (derogatory term)", and listed nine individuals reportedly associated with POTUS' daughter including POTUS' son-in-law and the U.S. Special Envoy for Peace Missions. The statement continued, "O barbarian! Your daughter was on the brink of death thirteen days ago, but our men do not kill (derogatory term)". The statement further threatened, "retribution will pursue you, your children, and your in-laws. This is a promise from (Harakat Ashab al-Yamin al-Islamia)". The statement also directed a message to the American public, particularly "security services, the Secret Service, and the victims of Epstein (DECEASED) Island, where the blood of the captives was shed and the souls of children were killed". The statement continued: "Avenge this shame and kill this arrogant, criminal, accursed (POTUS)". The statement also included a Quranic quote on legal retribution and the group's logo, which depicts a hand holding a rifle with a globe in the background. The post received 7.78k views, and 147 emoji reactions.
