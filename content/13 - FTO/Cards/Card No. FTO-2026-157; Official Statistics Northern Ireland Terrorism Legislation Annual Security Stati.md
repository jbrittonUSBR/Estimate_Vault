# Official Statistics: Northern Ireland Terrorism Legislation Annual Security Statistics: 2025

[Source](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/northern-ireland-terrorism-legislation-annual-security-statistics-2025)

(U) LONDON — The UK government published official statistics on the use in Northern Ireland of powers under the Terrorism Act 2000, the Terrorism (Northern Ireland) Act 2006, the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 and the Justice and Security (Northern Ireland) Act 2007 for January to December 2025. The bulletin reported that 26 people were detained under section 41 of the Terrorism Act 2000, 25 of them for 48 hours or less, with 11 charged with a total of 25 offences including five for preparation of terrorist acts, and that 30 premises were searched under warrant by the Police Service of Northern Ireland while section 47A stop-and-search powers were not used. Five people became subject to notification requirements, and as of April 28, 2026, all 11 charged after section 41 detention were still awaiting prosecution; the figures are the government’s official statistics as published.
