# Yemeni forces say they repel Houthi attacks in Taiz as fighting rages

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/24/yemeni-forces-claim-repelling-houthi-attacks-in-taiz-as-fighting-rages-on)

(U) TAIZ, Yemen — Government-aligned Yemeni forces said they repelled coordinated Houthi attacks overnight Wednesday in the Wadi al-Maqatirah area of Taiz, with spokesman Col. Majid al-Nuzaili claiming dozens of fighters were killed and wounded in clashes in Al Ahkum and that the attacks aimed at a key road linking Aden, Lahj and Taiz, Al Jazeera reported. There was no immediate Houthi comment on the government’s account. The UN human rights office said it had documented the killing of 32 civilians, including 15 children, in Yemen since August without attributing blame and three Houthi attacks on displacement sites, while the World Health Organization reported 674 killed and almost 3,000 injured since August 6 and 158,000 displaced.
