# 'Terrorist' pilot 'dreamed of crashing plane into Tel Aviv's airport' and had planned to hijack flight weeks ago 'but was friends with a crew member on board', sources claim

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16182531/Terrorist-pilot-dreamed-crashing-plane-Tel-Avivs-airport-planned-hijack-flight-weeks-ago-friends-crew-member-board-sources-claim.html)

(U) TEL AVIV, Israel — According to unverified sources cited by the Daily Mail, a pilot with alleged terrorist intentions had dreamed of crashing a plane into Tel Aviv's Ben Gurion Airport and had planned to hijack a flight weeks earlier but was deterred after discovering a crew member was a friend. The claims remain unconfirmed by official Israeli sources at the time of reporting.
