# US Embassy in Saudi Arabia warns citizens of possible Houthi attacks, airport disruptions amid rising tensions

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/world-news/us-embassy-in-saudi-arabia-warns-of-houthi-attacks-and-airport-disruptions-amid-rising-tensions/articleshow/135481224.cms)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — The Economic Times reported that the U.S. Embassy warned citizens of possible Houthi attacks, airport disruption, and rapid escalation, advising caution and direct confirmation of flight status with airlines. The alert said Riyadh airport had partially reopened and other major airports remained operational, though delays and cancellations were reported; it also warned against sharing images of interceptions or impact sites under Saudi rules.
