# Apprehended Venezuelan Tren de Aragua Leader on FBI’s 10 Most Wanted Fugitives List Appears in Nebraska Court Following Homeland Security Task Force Investigation

[Source](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/apprehended-venezuelan-tren-de-aragua-leader-fbis-top-10-most-wanted-list-appears-nebraska)

(U) OMAHA, Nebraska — A Venezuelan leader of the Tren de Aragua transnational criminal organization who was on the FBI’s Ten Most Wanted Fugitives list was apprehended and appeared in Nebraska federal court following a Homeland Security Task Force investigation.
