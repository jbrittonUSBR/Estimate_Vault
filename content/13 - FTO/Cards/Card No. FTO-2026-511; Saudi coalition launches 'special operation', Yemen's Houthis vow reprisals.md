# Saudi coalition launches 'special operation', Yemen's Houthis vow reprisals

[Source](https://m.economictimes.com/news/defence/saudi-coalition-launches-special-operation-yemens-houthis-vow-reprisals/articleshow/136031547.cms)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — The Saudi-led coalition said its "special operation" on Friday hit smuggling and weapons-transport operations at Hodeidah port and military targets including missile and drone facilities in Sanaa, Saada and Taiz. Houthi spokesman Yahya Saree said Jeddah and other Saudi Red Sea ports would be targeted, warned that Saudi airspace was unsafe and told people to avoid Riyadh's King Abdullah Financial District. The Houthis also accused the Saudis of striking a civilian boat and killing 19 people.
