# Iran Threatens South Korea with ‘Serious Consequences’ for Deploying to Strait of Hormuz

[Source](https://www.breitbart.com/national-security/2026/09/07/iran-threatens-south-korea-with-serious-consequences-for-deploying-to-strait-of-hormuz/)

(U) TEHRAN, Iran — Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baqaei threatened South Korea with "serious consequences" on Monday, September 7, 2026, if it deployed ships, aircraft, or personnel to help secure the Strait of Hormuz. In a statement posted to X, Baqaei said any country maintaining a military presence or participating in operations in the Persian Gulf and the Strait of Hormuz would be regarded as directly supporting aggression against Iran. The South Korean government had said the previous Friday that "nothing has been decided" about a possible deployment but that options, including troops, maritime patrol aircraft, and a support ship, were under discussion. Iranian academic Mohammad Marandi separately told Al Mayadeen that South Korea's economic assets across the Persian Gulf would be vulnerable to Iranian attack.
