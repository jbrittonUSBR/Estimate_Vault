# Houthi group targets jet-fuel storage in air attack on Riyadh

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/houthi-air-attack-riyadh-jet-fuel/)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — Yemen’s Houthi group claimed to have targeted jet-fuel storage facilities at or near Riyadh in an air attack, as part of a broader escalation that saw missiles and drones launched toward the Saudi capital and other sites; Saudi air defenses intercepted the threats.
