# Far-left activist arrested over arson attack that destroyed town hall during French riots is prosecuted for 'attempting to murder a policeman'

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16230001/Far-left-activist-arson-murder-charges-France-riots.html)

(U) Limoges, France — The Daily Mail reported that an activist was arrested in connection with a town-hall arson during riots and faced prosecution on an allegation of attempting to murder a police officer. The article said prosecutors linked the defendant to the fire and violence; the accusations remained allegations pending court proceedings.
