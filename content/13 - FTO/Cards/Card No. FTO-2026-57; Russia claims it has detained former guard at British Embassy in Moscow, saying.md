# Russia claims it has detained former guard at British Embassy in Moscow, saying he was part of Ukrainian plot to stage 'false flag attack on UK diplomats'

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16119915/Russia-claims-detained-former-guard-British-Embassy-Moscow-saying-Ukrainian-plot-stage-false-flag-attack-UK-diplomats.html)

(U) MOSCOW — Russia's FSB claimed on September 10, 2026, that it had detained a former security guard at the British Embassy in Moscow who was allegedly part of a Ukrainian plot to stage a false-flag attack on UK diplomats and blame Russia. According to the FSB, the suspect collected personal data on diplomatic personnel at the direction of a Ukrainian blogger working with Ukraine's SBU, with the alleged aim of drawing Britain into direct conflict with Russia. The British Embassy did not comment on the allegations, and Moscow's claims could not be independently verified.
