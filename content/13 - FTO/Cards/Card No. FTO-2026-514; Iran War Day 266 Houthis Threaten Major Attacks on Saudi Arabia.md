# Iran War Day 266: Houthis Threaten Major Attacks on Saudi Arabia

[Source](https://www.theamericanconservative.com/iran-war-day-266-houthis-threaten-major-attacks-on-saudi-arabia/)

(U) Washington, D.C. — The American Conservative reported that Senate Republicans rejected a war powers resolution 51–48 on Thursday that would have required President Donald Trump to end the Iran war or seek congressional authorization, with three Republicans joining nearly all Democrats. The daily brief said Brent crude opened at $92.38 a barrel and cited analysts noting reduced traffic through the Strait of Hormuz after Iran said two tankers struck mines. It also reported that the Houthis damaged Riyadh's tallest skyscraper on Thursday and that Iraq's government reached a compromise with Kataib Hezbollah.
