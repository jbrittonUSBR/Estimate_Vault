# The UK expands military support for Saudi Arabia against Houthis to include 'defensive' air patrols

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/defence/the-uk-expands-military-support-for-saudi-arabia-against-houthis-to-include-defensive-air-patrols/articleshow/135069753.cms)

(U) London, United Kingdom — Britain announced an expansion of military support for Saudi Arabia that included RAF Typhoon defensive air patrols, according to the Associated Press. Defence Secretary Wes Streeting said the aircraft would help protect Saudi cities, civilians, and critical infrastructure from Houthi missiles and drones, and would not strike Houthi targets.
