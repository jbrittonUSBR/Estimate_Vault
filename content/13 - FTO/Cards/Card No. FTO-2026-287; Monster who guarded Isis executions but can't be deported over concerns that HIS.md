# Daily Mail reports Iraqi ISIS member linked to executions was seen shopping near Windsor

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16217337/Monster-Isis-executions-deported-life-danger-spotted-Kings-op.html)

(U) London, United Kingdom — The Daily Mail reported that Iraqi national Mohammed Yaseen, whom it described as a former ISIS member, was seen shopping near Windsor after entering Britain by small boat. The paper said German court records linked him to guarding ISIS executions and that British authorities had not deported him because of concerns about risks to his life in Iraq. These descriptions and claims are attributed to the tabloid's reporting and the cited court material; the article used highly charged language.
