# Al-Qaeda Supporters Online Discuss Installing Read-Only Memory (ROM) On Mobile Devices, Improvising Faraday Bags to Block Mobile Signals

[Source](https://www.memri.org/cjlab/al-qaeda-supporters-online-discuss-installing-read-only-memory-rom-mobile-devices-improvising)

(U) ONLINE — On 10 September 2026, users of a pro-al-Qaeda platform discussed installing custom read-only memory (ROM) on mobile devices and recommended a mobile operating system for privacy, anonymity, and security. Users also advised improvising Faraday bags from materials such as tin foil and foam or a steel ammunition can to block electromagnetic signals.
