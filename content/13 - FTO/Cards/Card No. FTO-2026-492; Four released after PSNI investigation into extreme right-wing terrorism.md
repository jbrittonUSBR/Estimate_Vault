# Four released after PSNI investigation into extreme right-wing terrorism

[Source](https://www.thejournal.ie/psni-arrests-right-wing-terrorism-7233017-Nov2026/)

(U) Belfast, Northern Ireland — The PSNI arrested four men aged 33 to 35 under the Terrorism Act on Thursday in Bangor, east Belfast and south Belfast as part of an investigation into extreme right-wing terrorism, TheJournal.ie reported. All four were released after questioning, and the PSNI said the investigation continued and appealed for information.
