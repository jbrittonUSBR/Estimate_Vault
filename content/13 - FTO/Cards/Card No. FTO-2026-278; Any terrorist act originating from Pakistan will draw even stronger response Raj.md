# Any terrorist act originating from Pakistan will draw even stronger response: Rajnath Singh

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/defence/any-terrorist-act-originating-from-pakistan-will-draw-even-stronger-response-rajnath-singh/articleshow/134970450.cms)

(U) NEW DELHI, INDIA — PTI reported that Indian Defence Minister Rajnath Singh said any future terrorist act originating from Pakistan would draw an even stronger response. Speaking at a defence conclave in New Delhi, Singh cited Operation Sindoor and earlier Indian military actions as evidence of a shift toward proactive responses. These were Singh’s remarks and did not constitute an independently verified assessment of terrorist activity.
