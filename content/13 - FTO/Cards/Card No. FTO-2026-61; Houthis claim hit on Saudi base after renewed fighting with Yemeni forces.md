# Houthis claim hit on Saudi base after renewed fighting with Yemeni forces

[Source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-13/houthis-hit-saudi-base-amid-renewed-fighting-with-yemen/107147690)

(U) SANAA, Yemen — Houthi fighters claimed to have attacked a military base in southern Saudi Arabia with drones and missiles, targeting what a spokesman described as weapons depots and command-and-control centres. The claim came as renewed fighting between the Iran-allied Houthis and Yemen's Saudi-backed government forces killed more than 500 people, according to sources on both sides, and as the Houthis cemented their hold on the Bab el-Mandeb Strait. The date of the claimed operation was not specified.
