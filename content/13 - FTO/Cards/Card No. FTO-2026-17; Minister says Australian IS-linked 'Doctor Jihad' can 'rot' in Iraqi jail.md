(U) Perth paediatrician Tareq Kamleh, known as “Doctor Jihad,” who travelled to Syria in 2015 to join Islamic State, is reportedly alive and being held in a Baghdad prison. He had previously been presumed dead in the 2017 fall of Raqqa. Iraqi court documents indicate ongoing investigations and allege that his activities went beyond medical services to include promoting extremist propaganda. He faces terrorism-related charges in Australia for joining and recruiting for IS. Opposition Leader Angus Taylor called for him to be barred from returning (via passport refusal or a temporary exclusion order). Housing Minister Clare O’Neil said the government does not want him back and that he can “rot in jail.” The government stated it will not assist the return of Australians who joined terrorist organisations and that any returnees would face the full force of the law, while noting legal limits on permanently denying citizens entry.
