# Far-left streamer Hasan Piker says US commits ‘pound for pound’ more terrorism than Al Qaeda

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/09/17/us-news/far-left-streamer-hasan-piker-says-us-commits-pound-for-pound-more-terrorism-than-al-qaeda/)

(U) WASHINGTON — Twitch streamer Hasan Piker claimed on-air that the United States commits "pound for pound" more terrorism than Al Qaeda, citing the 2022 U.S.-backed Saudi airstrike on a girls' school in Yemen as an example. Appearing as a surrogate for Democratic candidate Abdul El-Sayed in Michigan's Senate race, Piker's comments drew a sharp rebuke from Axios as "clickbait," as polls showed El-Sayed trailing Republican Mike Rogers by 1.8 points ahead of the November 3 midterms.
