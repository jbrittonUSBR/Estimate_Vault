# Iraq: Dire Prospects for Alleged ISIS-Linked Men, Boys

[Source](https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/10/05/iraq-dire-prospects-for-alleged-isis-linked-men-boys)

(U) BAGHDAD, Iraq — Human Rights Watch reported that Iraqi authorities were holding thousands of men and boys allegedly linked to ISIS in harsh conditions, many without adequate access to fair trials or rehabilitation. The NGO warned that prolonged detention, harsh sentences and lack of reintegration programs posed serious risks to due process and long-term stability in Iraq.
