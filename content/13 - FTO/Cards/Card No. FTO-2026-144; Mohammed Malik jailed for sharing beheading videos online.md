# Mohammed Malik jailed for sharing beheading videos online

[Source](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cw33k2yedrzko)

(U) Gillingham, UK — Mohammed Malik, 24, of Burnt Oak Terrace, Gillingham, Kent, was jailed for eight years at Winchester Crown Court on Monday after being convicted of two counts of disseminating a terrorist publication and two counts of expressing support for a proscribed organisation. Police said Malik shared videos of prisoners being beheaded, originally produced by the media wing of Daesh, across several social media platforms and that his home was raided on 14 January 2025, where officers seized a phone, hard drives, swords, knives and a homemade Daesh flag. Counter-terrorism officers said the material posed a risk of radicalising others. On release Malik will serve a year on licence and was made subject to a Serious Crime Prevention Order for 15 years.
