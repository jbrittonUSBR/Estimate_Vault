# Two men charged with terrorism offences in Manchester

[Source](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cqx29nke55kno)

(U) MANCHESTER, UK — Two Iranian nationals who arrived in the UK by small boat were charged with terrorism offences over an alleged plot to target Manchester's Jewish community, the BBC reported on October 2, 2026. Rahman Salehi, 34, of Salford, and Salam Ahmadyan, 36, of Liverpool, were accused of preparing to commit or assist an act of terrorism on or before September 20. They were arrested in Manchester's Northern Quarter on the eve of Yom Kippur. Counter Terrorism Police alleged the men obtained instructions to build a chlorine bomb, acquired components, researched targets and conducted reconnaissance, and were in contact with a third party overseas who may be in Iran. Searches were carried out at addresses in Liverpool and Salford, and the men were due to appear at Westminster Magistrates' Court.
