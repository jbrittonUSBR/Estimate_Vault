# US rushes bombers out of UK base over new terrorist threat – NYT

[Source](https://www.rt.com/news/646718-us-rushes-bombers-out-uk/)

(U) LONDON, UK — The United States withdrew all 12 of its B-1 bombers from RAF Fairford in the UK after receiving new threat information about an alleged terrorist plot against the base, RT reported on October 5, 2026, citing the New York Times and anonymous US officials. The move came a week after five British men in their early 20s were arrested near the base and subsequently released on bail. UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham said London had "strong indications" Iran could have been behind the incident, an allegation Tehran denied, with its London embassy accusing Britain of promoting "Iranophobia." Two anonymous US officials said commanders acted after new warnings about a possible Iranian-backed plot, and that the withdrawal was deemed so urgent some bombers departed without aerial refueling support. US European Command confirmed the bombers were redeploying to home stations. (Note: RT is Russian state media; the underlying claims are attributed to unnamed sources and remain unverified.)
