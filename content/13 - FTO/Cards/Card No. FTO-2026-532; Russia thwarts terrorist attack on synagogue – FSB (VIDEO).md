# Russia thwarts terrorist attack on synagogue – FSB (VIDEO)

[Source](https://rt-english.com/russia/649286-fsb-synagogue-bombing-thwarted/)

(U) KISLOVODSK, RUSSIA — RT reported, citing Russia's FSB, that security officers detained a man in his early 20s and foiled a plot to bomb a synagogue. The agency said he had built an explosive device, pledged allegiance to an international terrorist group and intended to flee to Syria after the attack. RT noted that the FSB released surveillance and arrest footage and has reported similar plots against Jewish sites in recent years.
