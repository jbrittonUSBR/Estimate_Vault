# UK air base 'terrorism' plot probe continues after suspects released on bail

[Source](https://abcnews.com/International/uk-air-base-terrorism-plot-probe-continues-after/story?id=136844465)

(U) LONDON — Five British nationals from London, aged 23 to 25, who were arrested near RAF Fairford on suspicion of terrorism offenses were released on bail, ABC News reported on September 29, 2026. Police said no improvised explosive devices were found, though petrol was recovered, and Assistant Commissioner Laurence Taylor said the investigation was continuing. The men, arrested on Sunday and bailed on Monday, were detained near the Gloucestershire base used by U.S. bombers; Iran's embassy denied any link and Secretary of State Marco Rubio cited a "foreign actor."
