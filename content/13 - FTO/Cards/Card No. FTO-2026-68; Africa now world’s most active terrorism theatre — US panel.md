# Africa now world’s most active terrorism theatre — US panel

[Source](https://punchng.com/africa-now-worlds-most-active-terrorism-theatre-us-panel/)

(U) WASHINGTON — The US House Committee on Homeland Security released a report ahead of the 25th anniversary of the 11 September 2001 attacks concluding that Africa has become the world's most active terrorism theatre, with more than two-thirds of ISIS's global activity and seven of the ten countries with the highest terrorism-related death tolls located on the continent. The report, titled "25th Anniversary Report: National Security Improvements and the Domestic Threat Landscape 25 Years After 9/11/01," said US and partner resources in Africa remained limited and intelligence coverage uneven, and warned that lone actors and inspired attackers required a shift from organisation-centric counterterrorism to behaviour-based prevention. It made more than 20 recommendations covering intelligence sharing, aviation and border security, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence and biodefence, and committee chairman Andrew Garbarino urged Congress and the executive branch to implement them quickly and on a bipartisan basis.
