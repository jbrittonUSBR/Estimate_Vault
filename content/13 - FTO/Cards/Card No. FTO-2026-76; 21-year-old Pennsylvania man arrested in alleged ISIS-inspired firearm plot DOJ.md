# 21-year-old Pennsylvania man arrested in alleged ISIS-inspired firearm plot: DOJ

[Source](https://abcnews.com/US/21-year-pennsylvania-man-arrested-alleged-isis-inspired/story?id=136450719)

(U) PITTSBURGH — Jonathan Hunter Kramer, 21, of Valencia, Pennsylvania, was arrested at a hotel and charged with receiving a firearm and ammunition in support of ISIS after FBI agents approached him outside a sporting goods store and searched his person and room, the Justice Department said. According to ABC News, investigators seized a semiautomatic rifle, five rifle magazines, a bipod, a scope, 190 rounds of ammunition, an entrenchment tool, a prayer rug, pepper spray and about 30 knives, along with a handwritten note. Kramer, who allegedly used the alias "Hamza Al Rashid," had been on the FBI's radar since 2023, when Pennsylvania authorities adjudicated him delinquent for an alleged role in a plot to commit a mass-casualty incident; a tipster recently contacted the FBI about him. The charge is an allegation and has not been proven in court.
