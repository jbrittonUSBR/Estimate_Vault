# Security officials warn the US aviation industry of recent calls for attack by Al Qaeda

[Source](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/20/politics/security-officials-warn-us-aviation-al-qaeda)

(U) Washington, DC — The FBI, DHS and TSA sent airlines, cargo carriers and airports an advisory saying Al Qaeda affiliates had praised a September attempt to hijack a Flydubai flight to Tel Aviv and urged similar attacks, according to a copy obtained by CNN. A law enforcement source told CNN there were no known specific, credible threats, and the advisory listed warning signs such as attempts to evade screening and unexplained losses of badges or uniforms. AAA projected about six million domestic air passengers during the November 24–30 travel week.
