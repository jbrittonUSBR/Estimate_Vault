# Le Pen's National Rally Party Vows to Classify Antifa as a Terrorist Group in France

[Source](https://www.breitbart.com/europe/2026/11/08/le-pen-party-vows-to-classify-antifa-as-a-terror-group-in-france/)

(U) Paris, France — Breitbart reported that National Rally leader Jordan Bardella said his party would seek to have Antifa-affiliated militias and other far-left groups listed as terrorist organizations if it entered government. The proposal is a political pledge ahead of the 2027 election, not a current French or European Union designation; Bardella’s claims about links to political parties were presented as his allegations.
