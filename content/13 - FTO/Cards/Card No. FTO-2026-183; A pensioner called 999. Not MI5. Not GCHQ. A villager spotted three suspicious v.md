# Farmer's 999 Call Led to Five Arrests Near RAF Fairford in Terrorism Probe

[Source](https://freerepublic.com/focus/f-chat/4397092/posts)

(U) FAIRFORD — A farmer near RAF Fairford in Gloucestershire called 999 at about 00:45 after spotting three suspicious vans, leading police to arrest five men near the U.S. bomber base, according to a Free Republic repost of an X thread by @InTheTrenchesUK. The men were initially arrested under the Explosives Act and later under Section 5 of the Terrorism Act; about 85 households were evacuated and a 400-meter cordon was established while a bomb-disposal robot was deployed. The account said the arrests were not intelligence-led, and it included commentary from the original X user rather than independent reporting.
