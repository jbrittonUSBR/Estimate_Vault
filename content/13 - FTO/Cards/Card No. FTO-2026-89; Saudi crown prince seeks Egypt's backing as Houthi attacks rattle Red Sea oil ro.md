# Saudi crown prince seeks Egypt's backing as Houthi attacks rattle Red Sea oil routes

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/world-news/saudi-crown-prince-seeks-egypts-backing-as-houthi-attacks-rattle-red-sea-oil-routes/articleshow/134277311.cms)

(U) CAIRO, Egypt — Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman met Egyptian President Abdel-Fattah el-Sissi in Cairo to seek backing as Houthi attacks on Saudi shipping and infrastructure helped drive a surge in global oil prices, the Associated Press reported. Both sides said they affirmed a shared commitment to freedom and security of maritime navigation in the Red Sea and the Bab el-Mandeb Strait, though neither announced concrete steps and Egypt is unlikely to offer military assistance. The report noted that Houthi fighters had seized the Red Sea port of Mokha and three strategic islands in and near the strait over the previous week, and that a drone attack attributed to Iran-aligned militias in Iraq had damaged the East-West Pipeline, which regional officials said would be largely shut down for weeks. Saudi Arabia has so far appeared reluctant to launch a major military campaign against the Houthis.
