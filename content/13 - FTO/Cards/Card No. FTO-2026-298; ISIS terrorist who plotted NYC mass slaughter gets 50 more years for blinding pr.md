# ISIS-inspired New York plotter received 50-year sentence for prison assault

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/10/19/us-news/isis-terrorist-who-plotted-nyc-mass-slaughter-gets-50-more-years-for-blinding-prison-guard/)

(U) Williamsport, Pennsylvania — Abdulrahman El Bahnasawy, already serving a 40-year sentence for plotting ISIS-inspired attacks in New York City, received an additional 50-year federal sentence for attacking correctional officers at USP Allenwood in 2020. The Justice Department said one officer lost an eye; the new sentence was ordered to run consecutively to his existing term.
