# Crude oil futures gain on reports of Houthi attack in Riyadh

[Source](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/markets/commodities/crude-oil-futures-gain-on-reports-of-houthi-attack-in-riyadh/article71723090.ece)

(U) Mangaluru, India — BusinessLine reported that at 10:01 a.m. Friday January Brent crude futures rose 0.50% to $92.44 a barrel and WTI futures gained 0.56% to $82.90 after reports of a strike on a tower in Riyadh's financial district. Saudi civil defense, quoted by SPA, said the incident caused material damage to several floors and no injuries, and the Foreign Ministry said the kingdom reserved the right to defend its territory and urged the UN Security Council to condemn the attacks. December crude futures on the Multi Commodity Exchange fell 0.69% to ₹7,753 in early trading.
