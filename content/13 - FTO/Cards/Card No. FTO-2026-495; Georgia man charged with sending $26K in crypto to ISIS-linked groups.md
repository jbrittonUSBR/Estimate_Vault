# Georgia man charged with sending $26K in crypto to ISIS-linked groups

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/georgia-man-charged-crypto-isis-transfers/)

(U) Atlanta, US — FBI agents arrested Younes Moustafa Younes, 34, of Lavonia, Georgia, on November 18 on federal charges of international money laundering and destruction of evidence, Crypto Briefing reported. Federal authorities alleged he sent about $9,206 in 11 transfers from December 2021 to April 2022 and another $16,807 from December 2025 to June 2026 to ISIS-linked recipients. Investigators said agents found three water-damaged phones and shredded documents during the arrest.
