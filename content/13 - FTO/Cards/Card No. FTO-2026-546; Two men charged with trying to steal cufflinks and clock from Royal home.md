# Two men charged with trying to steal cufflinks and clock from Royal home

[Source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cx5y1987dyp6o)

(U) NORWICH, ENGLAND — The BBC reported that Mirgen Doku, 34, and Arilen Ndoci, 32, appeared at Norwich Magistrates' Court charged with entering a dwelling with intent to steal and trespassing on a protected site after police were called to Anmer Hall on the Sandringham estate. Prosecutors accused them of trying to take six pairs of cufflinks, including a pair described as the Duke of Edinburgh's, and a clock; both were remanded in custody without entering pleas, and a third suspect remained at large. Police said the property was unoccupied and that dogs, drones and a helicopter were used in the search, while Kensington Palace declined to comment on security matters.
