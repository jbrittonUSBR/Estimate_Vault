# Butler County Man Charged with Receiving a Firearm and Ammunition With Intent to Commit Terrorism on Behalf of ISIS

[Source](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/butler-county-man-charged-receiving-firearm-and-ammunition-intent-commit-terrorism-behalf)

(U) PITTSBURGH, United States — Jonathan Hunter Kramer, 21, of Valencia, Pennsylvania, was charged by federal criminal complaint with receiving a firearm and ammunition knowing or having reasonable cause to believe it would be used to commit a felony or a federal crime of terrorism, including providing material support to a designated foreign terrorist organisation. FBI agents interdicted Kramer on 12 September as he returned to a hotel in Cranberry Township carrying three 30-round rifle magazines and five boxes of rifle ammunition, then seized a semiautomatic rifle, five magazines, a bipod and scope, 190 rounds of ammunition, an entrenchment tool and about 30 knives, the Justice Department said. The affidavit alleged that Kramer had used the online persona "Hamza Al Rashid," claimed affiliation with ISIS and spoken of preparing for his "mission"; he made his initial appearance on 14 September and prosecutors sought to detain him without bond.
