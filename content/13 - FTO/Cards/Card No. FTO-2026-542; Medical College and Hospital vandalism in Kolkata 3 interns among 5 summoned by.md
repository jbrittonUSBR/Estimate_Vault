# Medical College and Hospital vandalism in Kolkata: 3 interns among 5 summoned by KP

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/kolkata/medical-college-and-hospital-vandalism-in-kolkata-3-interns-among-5-summoned-by-kp/articleshow/136112103.cms)

(U) KOLKATA, INDIA — The Times of India reported that Kolkata Police summoned five people, including three Medical College and Hospital interns, for questioning over vandalism and unrest on the campus last week, with Bowbazar police issuing notices requiring them to appear before an investigating officer within three days and no arrests made. A senior officer said police had reviewed CCTV footage to establish the roles of certain individuals, while the interns told TOI they were consulting lawyers. The unrest had followed show-cause notices to eight interns over their remarks about moving Chorbagan Durga Puja pandal stampede victims to a private medical facility, and the health department formed a three-member probe panel.
