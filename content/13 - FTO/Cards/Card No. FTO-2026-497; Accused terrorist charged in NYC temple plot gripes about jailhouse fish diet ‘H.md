# Accused terrorist charged in NYC temple plot gripes about jailhouse fish diet: ‘He’s famished’

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/11/19/us-news/accused-terrorist-charged-in-nyc-temple-plot-gripes-about-jailhouse-fish-diet-hes-famished/)

(U) New York, US — Mohammad Baqer Saad Dawood al-Saadi pleaded not guilty to expanded terrorism charges in Manhattan federal court while his lawyer told Judge Colleen McMahon that solitary confinement at the Metropolitan Detention Center left him relying on mackerel because halal chicken was unavailable. Prosecutors accused him of plotting a Manhattan temple bombing, an Amsterdam bank firebombing and attacks on a British air base using coded calls to relatives in Iraq, the New York Post reported. He was escorted out after demanding to fire his public defenders.
