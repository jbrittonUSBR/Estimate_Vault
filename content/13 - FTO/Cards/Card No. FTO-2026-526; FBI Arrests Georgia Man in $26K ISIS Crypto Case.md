# FBI Arrests Georgia Man in $26K ISIS Crypto Case

[Source](https://legalinsurrection.com/2026/11/fbi-arrests-georgia-man-in-26k-isis-crypto-case/)

(U) Macon, Georgia, United States — Legal Insurrection reported that federal prosecutors charged Younes Moustafa Younes, 34, of Lavonia, Georgia, with international money laundering and destroying evidence after an FBI search found damaged phones soaking in liquid and shredded papers in a bathtub. The complaint alleges he sent more than $26,000 in cryptocurrency to people and groups tied to ISIS between 2021 and 2026, including funds intended to support ISIS in Syria; the article noted the allegations had not been proven in court. He appeared before a magistrate judge and remained in custody ahead of hearings on November 23 in Macon.
