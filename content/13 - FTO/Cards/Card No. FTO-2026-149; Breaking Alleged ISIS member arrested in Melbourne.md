# Breaking: Alleged ISIS member arrested in Melbourne

[Source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-23/counter-terrorism-police-arrest-melbourne-man-alleged-isis-link/107185494)

(U) Melbourne, Australia — Australian Federal Police arrested Mohamed Bakit in Carlton on September 23, 2026 on terrorism charges after a multi-year investigation revealed he allegedly acted as an online propagandist for the Islamic State group. Police allege Bakit sent approximately 10,000 files of violent extremist material and ISIS propaganda between November 2015 and July 2020, pledged allegiance to the group, and shared content depicting executions, torture, and information about carrying out mass casualty attacks.
