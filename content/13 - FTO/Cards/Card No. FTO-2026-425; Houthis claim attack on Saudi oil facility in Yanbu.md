# Houthis claim attack on Saudi oil facility in Yanbu

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/houthis-claim-attack-on-saudi-oil-facility-in-yanbu/)

(U) Yanbu, Saudi Arabia — Crypto Briefing reports that Yemen’s Houthi movement claimed an attack on an oil facility in Yanbu, a major Saudi oil hub. The article says Saudi authorities had not confirmed damage or casualties at publication and separately reported intercepting missiles and drones directed at other areas. Thus, the attack claim was not independently confirmed in the linked report.
