# Israeli Arab firefighter arrested for terror plot

[Source](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/433154)

(U) KAFR QASSEM, Israel — A covert investigation by the Sharon District Police Central Unit and the Shin Bet led to the arrest of a 19-year-old Kafr Qassem resident who performed National Service as a firefighter at a central Israel fire station, on suspicion he sought to form a terror cell and planned a series of attacks. Investigators said the suspect exploited the access and knowledge gained as a firefighter to strategic sites, marking at least one sensitive facility as a preferred target, and that he had been in contact with Hamas terrorists and Iranian intelligence agents, obtaining combat manuals and bomb-making instructions and attempting to build explosives. Police said he also tried to recruit two acquaintances, who were arrested but released without indictment for lack of evidence. A raid on his home found a large quantity of training and airsoft equipment; an indictment was to be filed the next day.
