# Moscow arrests British embassy security guard

[Source](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/moscow-arrests-british-embassy-security-guard-226703)

(U) MOSCOW — Russia's FSB security service said on September 10, 2026, that it had detained a Russian security guard working at Britain's embassy in Moscow, alleging he was recruited by Ukraine for a false-flag attack on the UK ambassador. The FSB claimed the individual was tasked by a Ukrainian blogger working with Ukraine's SBU to track events and personnel at the embassy and note their security system, which it said was intended to prepare sabotage and terrorist attacks on British diplomats that would then be blamed on Russia. Kyiv and London did not respond to the allegations.
