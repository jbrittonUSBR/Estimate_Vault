# UK base plot 'coordinated by handler' on behalf of Iran

[Source](https://www.rte.ie/news/uk/2026/1027/1596944-raf-fairford-investigation/)

(U) RAF Fairford, England — Irish broadcaster RTÉ reported that two men were charged in connection with an alleged plot involving vans carrying petrol to the U.S.-operated RAF Fairford base. Prosecutors said a handler known as “Ali,” whom police believed worked for Iran, directed the men; the allegations had not been proven in court.
