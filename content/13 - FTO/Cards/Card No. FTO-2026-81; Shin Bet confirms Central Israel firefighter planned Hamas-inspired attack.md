# Shin Bet confirms: Central Israel firefighter planned Hamas-inspired attack

[Source](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/433179)

(U) TEL AVIV — Shin Bet confirmed the arrest of Mustafa Issa, 19, an Israeli citizen from Kafr Qassem who worked as a National Service firefighter at a station in central Israel, on suspicion of planning a Hamas-inspired attack. According to Israel National News, Issa had marked at least one sensitive facility, was in contact with Hamas and Iranian intelligence, possessed bomb-making manuals and had attempted to manufacture explosives. He also allegedly tried to recruit two acquaintances from Kafr Qassem, who were arrested and held for about two weeks but not indicted, and security forces seized airsoft and training equipment. An indictment was expected to be filed the following day; the allegations have not been proven in court.
