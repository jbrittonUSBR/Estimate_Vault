# Syria arrests 6 suspected members of 'terrorist cell' in Idlib

[Source](https://www.anews.com.tr/middle-east/2026/11/25/syria-arrests-6-suspected-members-of-terrorist-cell-in-idlib)

(U) IDLIB, SYRIA — Anadolu Agency reported that Syria's Interior Ministry said security forces arrested six suspected members of an extremist cell in Idlib on Wednesday and freed a captive, Karim Musa al-Nader, during a raid in Kafr Jales where a weapon and money were seized. The ministry said the cell was suspected of kidnappings, killings, terrorizing civilians and ransom demands, and that the detainees were referred for investigation before court. It also cited earlier operations against ISIS cells, including the arrest of four suspects in November and a government claim that 235 militants had been detained and seven attacks foiled in three months.
