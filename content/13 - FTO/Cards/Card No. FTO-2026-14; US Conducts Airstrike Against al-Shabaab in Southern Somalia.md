(U) **US Conducts Airstrike Against al-Shabaab in Southern Somalia** (Horseed Media, July 20, 2026) The United States military carried out an airstrike targeting the Islamist militant group al-Shabaab in southern Somalia, according to U.S. Africa Command (AFRICOM). The strike occurred on July 15, 2026, near Welmaro, approximately 100 kilometres north of the port city of Kismayo, and was conducted in coordination with Somalia’s Federal Government. AFRICOM stated that the operation aimed to degrade al-Shabaab’s ability to threaten U.S. forces and American citizens overseas. The command said it would continue working with the Somali Federal Government and the Somali Armed Forces against the group.

(U) No further details were released about the units or assets involved (citing operational security), and AFRICOM did not immediately provide information on casualties or an assessment of the strike’s impact. Independent verification was not available at the time of reporting.
