# From Saddam's execution to the rise and fall of ISIS: Stunning images illustrate America's two decades in Iraq as final US troops leave today

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16171791/Saddam-Hussein-US-Iraq-invasion.html)

(U) BAGHDAD — A Daily Mail photo retrospective published September 30, 2026, traced the U.S.-led invasion of Iraq from the predawn strikes of March 20, 2003, through Saddam Hussein's capture and execution, the rise and fall of ISIS, and the final departure of American troops. The feature placed the two-decade war in visual context, including the toppling of Saddam's statue and the militants' 2014 sweep across northern Iraq. It noted that about 5,000 coalition personnel died during the war, including 179 British service members, and that the conflict reshaped Iraq's politics, security forces and regional alignments.
