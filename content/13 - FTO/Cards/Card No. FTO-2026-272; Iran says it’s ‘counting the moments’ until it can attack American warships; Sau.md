# Iran says it’s ‘counting the moments’ until it can attack American warships; Saudi Aramco facilities attacked as Houthi attacks escalate

[Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/14/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-saudi.html)

(U) Tehran, Iran — On October 14, 2026, Iranian officials warned they were "counting the moments" until they could attack American warships, as regional tensions escalated following Houthi attacks on Saudi Aramco facilities. The rhetoric came amid growing fears of broader confrontation involving Iran, its proxies, and U.S. forces in the Gulf.
