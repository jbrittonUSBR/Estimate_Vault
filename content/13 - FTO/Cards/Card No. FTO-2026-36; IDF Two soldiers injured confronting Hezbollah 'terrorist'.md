# IDF: Two soldiers injured confronting Hezbollah 'terrorist'

(U) NORTHERN ISRAEL — Two Israeli soldiers were injured during an encounter with a Hezbollah operative near the Lebanon border. The Israel Defense Forces said troops confronted a "terrorist" affiliated with Hezbollah, with the exchange resulting in minor injuries to the soldiers. The incident underscores ongoing tensions along the Israel-Lebanon border despite recent ceasefire efforts.
