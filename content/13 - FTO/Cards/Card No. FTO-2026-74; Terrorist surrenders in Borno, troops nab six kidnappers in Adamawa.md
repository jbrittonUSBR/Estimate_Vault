# Terrorist surrenders in Borno, troops nab six kidnappers in Adamawa

[Source](https://punchng.com/terrorist-surrenders-in-borno-troops-nab-six-kidnappers-in-adamawa/)

(U) MAIDUGURI, Nigeria — A repentant Boko Haram/ISWAP fighter surrendered to Nigerian Army troops under Operation Hadin Kai on September 13, 2026, at Borgozo in Benisheikh, Kaga Local Government Area of Borno State, handing over an AK-47 rifle, 14 rounds of 7.62mm ammunition, four magazines, an improvised explosive device, a magazine pouch and a bicycle. In a separate operation, troops arrested six suspected kidnappers operating around Soktu Hill in Song Local Government Area of Adamawa State in a raid and follow-up operations, recovering a locally made Dane gun, according to a statement by Capt. Mohammed Goni. The military presented the surrender and arrests as part of continuing clearance and stabilization operations in northeastern Nigeria; the suspects' guilt has not been established in court.
