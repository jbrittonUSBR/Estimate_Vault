# Prabowo calls forest fires ‘environmental terrorism,’ orders probe

[Source](https://en.antaranews.com/news/437077/prabowo-calls-forest-fires-environmental-terrorism-orders-probe)

(U) October 21, 2026 — Indonesian President Prabowo Subianto described forest and land fires in Sumatra and Kalimantan as “environmental terrorism” and ordered an investigation, according to state news agency Antara. The characterization was Prabowo's; Antara reported that he called for authorities to determine responsibility for the fires.
