# California Man Pleads Guilty to Bombing Santa Barbara County Courthouse in Retaliation for Law Enforcement Seizing His Firearms

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/security/library/news/2026/09/sec-260914-doj01.htm)

(U) SANTA MARIA, United States — Nathaniel James McGuire, 22, of Santa Maria, California, pleaded guilty to one count of use of a weapon of mass destruction and one count of malicious destruction of a building using an explosive for detonating a bomb at the Santa Maria courthouse on 24 September 2024, an attack that injured three people and caused about $35,000 in damage. According to the Justice Department, McGuire threw a backpack bomb past security screening toward Santa Barbara County sheriff's deputies in retaliation for the earlier seizure of his firearms, then went to his vehicle for two long guns and 10 Molotov cocktails intending to return and kill deputies and a judge before a security guard and officers stopped him. Five people were hospitalised and released the same day, and sentencing was set for 1 March 2027, with a maximum penalty of life in prison.
