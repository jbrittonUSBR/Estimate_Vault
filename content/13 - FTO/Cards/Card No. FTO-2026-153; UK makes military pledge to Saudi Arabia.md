# UK makes military pledge to Saudi Arabia

[Source](https://www.rt.com/news/646107-uk-saudi-military-support-houthis/)

(U) London, UK — RT, a Russian state outlet, reported that the UK pledged military assistance to Saudi Arabia as hostilities with Yemen's Houthi movement intensified, with Britain set to deploy an RAF Voyager air-to-air refuelling aircraft to the region following a request from Riyadh. The UK Ministry of Defence said the aircraft would provide refuelling support for Saudi "defensive activity" in Yemen. The report said the Houthis had launched missile and drone attacks on Saudi territory and made gains along Yemen's Red Sea coast, and that Riyadh had sought military help from several countries as its stocks of missile interceptors came under pressure.
