# Coalition: Six Ballistic Missiles Launched by Terrorist Houthi Militia Intercepted, Destroyed

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/saudi/2026/saudi-260924-spa01.htm)

(U) RIYADH, Saudi Arabia — Maj. Gen. Turki Al-Malki, official spokesman for the Saudi-led Coalition to Support Legitimacy in Yemen, said coalition forces intercepted and destroyed six ballistic missiles launched by the Houthi militia and that air defenses foiled an attempt to target Taif and Yanbu, according to the Saudi state news agency SPA. Al-Malki said the militia’s “escalation and violations” would be dealt with firmly. The statement was republished by GlobalSecurity.org and reflects the coalition’s account; no independent verification or Houthi response was included.
