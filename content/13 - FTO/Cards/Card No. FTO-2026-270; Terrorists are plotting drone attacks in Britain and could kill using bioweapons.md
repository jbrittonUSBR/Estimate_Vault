# Terrorists are plotting drone attacks in Britain and could kill using bioweapons as AI 'supercharges' threats, warns the head of MI5

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16204517/Terrorists-drone-attacks-Britain-kill-using-bioweapons-AI-supercharges-threats-head-MI5.html)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — On October 13, 2026, the Daily Mail reported that MI5 Director-General Sir Ken McCallum warned artificial intelligence is “supercharging” the terrorist threat, with terrorists plotting AI-enabled drone attacks in Britain and potentially seeking to use AI to create novel pathogens or bioweapons. He said MI5 was devoting more resources to state threats than during the Cold War while also flagging rising extremist intent linked to anti-immigration and anti-Muslim grievances.
