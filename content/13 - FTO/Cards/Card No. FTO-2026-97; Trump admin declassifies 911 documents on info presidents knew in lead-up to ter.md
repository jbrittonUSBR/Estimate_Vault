# Trump admin declassifies 9/11 documents on info presidents knew in lead-up to terrorist attacks

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/09/11/us-news/trump-admin-declassifies-9-11-documents-on-info-presidents-knew-in-lead-up-to-terrorist-attacks/)

(U) WASHINGTON — The Trump administration released 71 previously classified Presidential Daily Briefs on September 11, 2026, the 25th anniversary of the 9/11 attacks, documents that showed Presidents Bill Clinton and George W. Bush had been warned that Osama bin Laden sought to hijack U.S. aircraft. A December 4, 1998, briefing prepared for Clinton was titled "Bin Laden Preparing To Hijack US Aircraft," and a September 10, 1998, item warned that al Qaeda might fly an explosive-packed airplane into a U.S. city; the release also included the July 25, 2001, memo "Bin Laden Determined to Strike in US." CIA Director John Ratcliffe announced the release, and former national security adviser Condoleezza Rice apologized to victims' families for the government's failure to prevent the attacks, which killed nearly 3,000 people.
