# Children make up 40 per cent of active counterterrorism cases, AFP warns

[Source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-11/afp-warns-of-sharp-spike-in-minor-counter-terrorism-case/107369962)

(U) Canberra, Australia — AFP Counterterrorism Commander Paula Hudson said minors account for 40 percent of active joint counterterrorism cases in 2026. She told ABC that 32 of 37 youths prosecuted since 2020 were arrested and prosecuted in the past two years, and said all 55 cases involving 12–17-year-olds had an online radicalisation component.
