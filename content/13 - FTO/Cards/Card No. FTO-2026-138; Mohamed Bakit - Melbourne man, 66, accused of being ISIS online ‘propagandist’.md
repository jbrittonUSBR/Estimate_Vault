# Mohamed Bakit: Melbourne man, 66, accused of being ISIS online ‘propagandist’

[Source](https://www.theaustralian.com.au/news/latest-news/melbourne-man-65-allegedly-served-as-isis-online-propagandist-police/news-story/eeb71218122f25b37efb2e419fc2f370)

(U) MELBOURNE, Australia — Mohamed Bakit, 66, appeared in Melbourne Magistrates' Court after counterterrorism police arrested him at his Carlton home and charged him with membership of a terrorist organisation and transmitting violent extremist material. Police alleged he pledged allegiance to ISIS and shared about 10,000 files as an online propagandist between 2015 and 2020; his lawyer said he denied the charges.
