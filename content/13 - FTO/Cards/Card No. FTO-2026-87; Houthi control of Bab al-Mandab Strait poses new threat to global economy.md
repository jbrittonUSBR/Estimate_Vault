# Houthi control of Bab al-Mandab Strait poses new threat to global economy

[Source](https://freerepublic.com/focus/f-news/4395694/posts)

(U) SANAA, Yemen — Yemen's Iran-aligned Houthi movement seized the Red Sea port city of Mokha and several islands in the Bab al-Mandab Strait in mid-September 2026, consolidating control over the shortest maritime route between Asia and Europe, according to a Le Monde report republished by the Free Republic aggregator. Drones launched from Iraq damaged Saudi Arabia's East-West oil pipeline on September 10, forcing its shutdown the following day. The outlet reported that the combination of pipeline attacks and Houthi control of the strait raised fears of higher oil prices and shipping costs, even as pressure from the blockade of the Strait of Hormuz had begun to ease.
