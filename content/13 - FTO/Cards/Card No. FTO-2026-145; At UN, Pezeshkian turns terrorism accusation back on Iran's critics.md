# At UN, Pezeshkian turns terrorism accusation back on Iran's critics

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/iran/2026/09/iran-260923-unnews01.htm)

(U) New York, United States — Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian, speaking at the UN General Assembly on September 23, 2026, rejected US President Donald Trump's accusation that Iran was the "number one sponsor of terror" and instead accused critics of being terrorists themselves. Pezeshkian cited the February 2026 US-Israeli strike that killed former supreme leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei as an act of terrorism and claimed Iran was a victim rather than a perpetrator, while also condemning what he called double standards over Israel's nuclear weapons program outside the Non-Proliferation Treaty.
