# U.S. Tip Leads Russia to Arrest Minor for Alleged Moscow Terrorist Plot

[Source](https://www.breitbart.com/asia/2026/11/02/u-s-tip-leads-russia-arrest-minor-alleged-moscow-terrorist-plot/)

(U) Moscow, Russia — Breitbart reported that Russia’s Federal Security Service said it detained a minor after receiving information from the United States about an alleged threat at the Tsaritsyno museum and park. The FSB cited social-media posts and searches on the suspect’s phone; the site was temporarily closed, and the allegations were not independently verified in the report.
