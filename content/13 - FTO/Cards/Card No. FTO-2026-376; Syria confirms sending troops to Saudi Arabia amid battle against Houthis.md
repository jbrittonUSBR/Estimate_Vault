# Syria confirms sending troops to Saudi Arabia amid battle against Houthis

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/features/2026/11/9/syria-confirms-sending-troops-to-saudi-arabia-amid-battle-against-houthis)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — Al Jazeera reported that Syrian officials said a small number of military advisers had gone to Saudi Arabia to help train Yemeni forces, while a larger contingent was being discussed. The report cited anonymous officials who said roughly 600 Syrian special-forces personnel could deploy to Saudi Arabia; it said no Syrian fighters had been sent into Yemen and Syria’s public position emphasized training and military cooperation.
