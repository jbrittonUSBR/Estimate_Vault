# UK to deploy Typhoon jets to help defend Saudi Arabia from Houthi attacks

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/20/uk-to-deploy-raf-fighter-jets-to-defend-saudi-arabia-from-houthi-attacks)

(U) London, United Kingdom — The UK announced it would deploy RAF Typhoon jets to conduct defensive counter-air missions over Saudi Arabia amid Houthi missile and drone attacks. The Ministry of Defence said the jets would protect Saudi cities and critical infrastructure and would not conduct offensive strikes against the Houthis.
