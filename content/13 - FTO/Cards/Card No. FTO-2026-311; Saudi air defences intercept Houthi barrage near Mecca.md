# Saudi air defences intercept Houthi barrage near Mecca

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/25/saudi-air-defences-intercept-houthi-barrage-near-mecca)

(U) Mecca province, Saudi Arabia — Al Jazeera reported that Saudi Civil Defence said shrapnel from two intercepted ballistic missiles, suspected to have been fired by the Houthis, fell in Jeddah, Taif, Rabigh, and Wadi Al-Mughammas. A drone and ballistic missile were also reported intercepted over Riyadh; the Houthis denied targeting the holy cities of Mecca and Medina.
