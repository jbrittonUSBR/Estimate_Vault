# Iran’s UNGA Moment

[Source](http://foreignpolicy.com/2026/09/23/iran-unga-speech-pezeshkian-trump-israel-terrorist-nuclear/)

(U) NEW YORK — In Foreign Policy’s Situation Report newsletter, reporters Rishi Iyengar and John Haltiwanger wrote that Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian used his UN General Assembly speaking slot on September 23 to retaliate rhetorically against President Donald Trump’s threat to “annihilate” Iran, calling the U.S. government “terrorists” and declaring that Iranians were “victims of terrorism.” Per the column, Pezeshkian cited the killing of former Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei and strikes on a school and sports complex, said “those who are terrorists and train and protect terrorists describe us as such,” and criticized Israel’s nuclear arsenal while defending Iran’s nuclear program.
