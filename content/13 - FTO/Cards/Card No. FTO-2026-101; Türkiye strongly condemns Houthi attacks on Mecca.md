# Türkiye strongly condemns Houthi attacks on Mecca

[Source](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/turkiye-strongly-condemns-houthi-attacks-on-mecca-226989)

(U) ANKARA, Türkiye — Türkiye's Foreign Ministry said in a written statement on September 17, 2026, that it strongly condemned the Houthi drone attack targeting the holy city of Mecca and reaffirmed its support for Saudi Arabia's sovereignty, territorial integrity and security. The statement followed Saudi Arabia's announcement that it had shot down a drone heading toward Mecca, and it called for an immediate halt to attacks that Ankara said also threatened regional stability. Foreign Minister Hakan Fidan discussed the escalation by telephone with Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi, while Communications Director Burhanettin Duran said any attempted attack against the Two Holy Mosques "wounds our collective conscience." The Defense Ministry separately warned that the Houthi threat in the Red Sea and Bab el-Mandeb harmed international maritime trade and global stability.
