(U) PALMA, Mozambique — On September 28, 2026, the Daily Mail reported on the three-day "Battle of Palma," in which ISIS-linked militants besieged a hotel packed with foreign nationals in the northern Mozambican town. The account detailed both the horror of the assault and acts of heroism by those trapped inside as they fought for survival during the prolonged siege.
