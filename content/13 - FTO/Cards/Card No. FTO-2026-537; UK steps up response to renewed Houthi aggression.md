# UK steps up response to renewed Houthi aggression

[Source](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-steps-up-response-to-renewed-houthi-aggression)

(U) LONDON, UNITED KINGDOM — The UK government announced sanctions against the Houthi movement and four top officials, citing renewed aggression inside Yemen and strikes on Saudi civilian infrastructure. Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband said the measures held the Houthis accountable for attacks on commercial shipping in the Bab el-Mandeb Strait that had raised cost-of-living pressures in Britain. The Foreign Office said the designations imposed asset freezes, travel bans and director disqualifications and targeted officials accused of serious human rights abuses including torture and sexual violence.
