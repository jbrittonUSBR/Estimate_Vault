# Antifa will be designated a banned terrorist group under plans drawn up by Marine Le Pen's National Rally party

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16277681/Antifa-designated-banned-terrorist-group-plans-drawn-Marine-Le-Pens-National-Rally-party.html)

(U) Paris, France — The Daily Mail reported that National Rally leader Jordan Bardella said his party would seek to place Antifa and other far-left groups on terrorist lists if it forms the next government. The article described this as a future political proposal and quoted Bardella’s claims about protest-related violence; no such designation had been made at the time of publication.
