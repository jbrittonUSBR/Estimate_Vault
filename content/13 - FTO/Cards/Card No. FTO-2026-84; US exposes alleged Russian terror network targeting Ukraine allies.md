# US exposes alleged Russian terror network targeting Ukraine allies

[Source](https://www.politico.eu/article/russia-intelligence-network-us-europe-murder-terrorism-charges/)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — U.S. prosecutors unsealed charges against five people allegedly working for Russian intelligence, accusing them of conspiracy to finance terrorism and, in three cases, conspiracy to commit murder for hire, POLITICO reported. The indictment alleged that the network recruited operatives to conduct surveillance and killings in the United States and to attack civilian and military infrastructure in countries aligned, or perceived to be aligned, with Ukraine, and that all five defendants remained at large. Prosecutors said the group most recently offered a U.S.-based individual $40,000 to "eliminate" or "disappear" a prominent Russian dissident, while a separate alleged intelligence officer offered $25,000 to kill a dissident in Lithuania and proposed attacks on a warehouse or electrical substation in "all the countries that are helping Ukraine." The network also allegedly coordinated operations in Prague and Lithuania in 2024, and Russia's Foreign Ministry and embassy in Washington did not respond to requests for comment.
