# Murder of Netanel Shakron: Terrorist's interrogation reveals how he planned attack

[Source](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/437005)

(U) NEVE TZUF, WEST BANK — Israel National News reported that a prosecutor's declaration said the man who killed Netanel Shakron near Neve Tzuf had scouted the spring a day earlier and returned after a failed first attempt. The outlet said investigators found the September 20 attack was planned, that the shooter discussed it with a friend who declined to join, and that he told interrogators he wanted to become a martyr. Israeli police, the ISA and the IDF arrested the suspect in Ramallah and later detained the friend.
