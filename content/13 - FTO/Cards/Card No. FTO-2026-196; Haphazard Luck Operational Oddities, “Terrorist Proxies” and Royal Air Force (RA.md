# Haphazard Luck: Operational Oddities, “Terrorist Proxies” and Royal Air Force (RAF), Fairford

[Source](https://www.globalresearch.ca/operational-oddities-terrorist-proxies-raf-fairford/5942134)

(U) FAIRFORD, England — In an opinion analysis published September 29, 2026, Dr. Binoy Kampmark examined the arrest of five British nationals, aged 23 to 25, near RAF Fairford in Gloucestershire after police found chemicals in three white vans. The piece described how a farmer's 999 call at about 01:36 BST triggered a response that evacuated 85 households to a leisure centre in Cirencester and imposed a 400-metre cordon, and it noted that investigators later concluded the materials were not viable explosives and released the suspects on bail. Kampmark argued that the case showed how quickly ordinary circumstances can be reframed through the language of terrorism and foreign proxies, citing Counter Terrorism Policing Assistant Commissioner Laurence Taylor's warning about operatives acting on behalf of a foreign state. The article also noted that Iran's embassy rejected the allegations and that President Donald Trump commented publicly on the arrests.
