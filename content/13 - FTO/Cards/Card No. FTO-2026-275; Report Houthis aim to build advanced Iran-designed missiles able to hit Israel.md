# Report: Houthis aim to build advanced Iran-designed missiles able to hit Israel

[Source](https://www.haaretz.com/middle-east-news/2026-10-14/ty-article/report-houthis-aim-to-build-advanced-iran-designed-missiles-able-to-hit-israel/000001a1-39bc-d25f-a1b3-7dbc367a0000)

(U) Sanaa, Yemen — On October 14, 2026, a report indicated that Yemen's Houthi forces were working to develop advanced, Iran-designed missiles capable of reaching Israel. The reported effort pointed to continued Iranian support for the Houthis and an expansion of their long-range strike capabilities amid regional escalation.
