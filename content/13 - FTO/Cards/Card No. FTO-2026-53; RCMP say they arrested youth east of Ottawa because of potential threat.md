# RCMP say they arrested youth east of Ottawa because of potential threat

[Source](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/ottawa/glengarry-ontario-rcmp-national-security-arrest-threat-youth-9.7338605)

(U) GLENGARRY, Canada — The RCMP said on September 10, 2026, that a national security team arrested a youth in the North and South Glengarry area of eastern Ontario on August 21 because of information about "a potential threat to the population." The force said it executed two search warrants as a "necessary enforcement action" and invoked the Youth Criminal Justice Act, indicating the person arrested was under 18. The RCMP said there was no known threat to public safety at that time and declined further comment because the matter is before the courts.
