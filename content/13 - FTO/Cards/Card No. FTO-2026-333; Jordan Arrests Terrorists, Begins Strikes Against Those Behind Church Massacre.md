# Jordan Arrests Terrorists, Begins Strikes Against Those Behind Church Massacre

[Source](https://dailycaller.com/2026/10/27/jordan-air-strikes-church-massacre-karak-terrorists)

(U) Karak, Jordan — Jordan arrested three armed militants suspected of carrying out a deadly shooting at a Greek Orthodox church funeral that killed nine people and injured 34. The Jordanian Air Force also conducted strikes on suspected terrorist hideouts in southern Syria linked to the attack; King Abdullah pledged a harsh response, according to The Daily Caller.
