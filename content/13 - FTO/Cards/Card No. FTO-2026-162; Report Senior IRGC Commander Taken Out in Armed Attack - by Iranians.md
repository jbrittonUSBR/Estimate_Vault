# Iranian State Media: IRGC Brigadier General Hossein Zarifi Killed in Armed Attack in Sistan-Baluchestan

[Source](https://freerepublic.com/focus/f-news/4396688/posts)

(U) SARAVAN, Iran — Iran’s Tasnim news agency reported that Brig. Gen. Hossein Zarifi, commander of the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps’ Shahid Sajjad operational base in Saravan County in southeastern Sistan-Baluchestan province, was killed in clashes with armed men, according to a RedState account aggregated by Free Republic. The IRGC’s Quds Base said five members of the armed group were killed and others arrested and described those targeted as “terrorists” without identifying the organization; Iran International, citing the Baloch rights group Haalvsh, reported at least 13 security personnel seriously wounded, a figure Gulf News said it could not verify. The claims and casualty figures could not be independently verified, and the account is a partisan aggregation of Iranian state media reporting.
