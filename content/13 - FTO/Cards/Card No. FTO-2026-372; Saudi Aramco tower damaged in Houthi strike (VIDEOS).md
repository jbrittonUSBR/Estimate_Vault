# Saudi Aramco tower damaged in Houthi strike (VIDEOS)

[Source](https://rt-english.com/news/648584-saudi-aramco-tower-damaged/)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — RT reported that an Aramco office tower in the King Abdullah Financial District suffered visible damage during a Houthi-claimed missile and drone attack. Saudi Arabia’s Energy Ministry said shrapnel from an intercepted missile caused minor damage and no casualties; the coalition also reported intercepting other projectiles aimed at the kingdom.
