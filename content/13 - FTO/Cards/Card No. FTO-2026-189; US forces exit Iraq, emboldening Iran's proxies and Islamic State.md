# US forces exit Iraq, emboldening Iran's proxies and Islamic State

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/defence/us-forces-exit-iraq-emboldening-irans-proxies-and-islamic-state/articleshow/134576835.cms)

(U) BAGHDAD, Iraq — On September 30, 2026, Reuters analysis republished by The Times of India reported that the U.S. withdrawal from Iraq, completed on September 30, 2026 after 23 years following the 2003 invasion, risked emboldening Iran's proxies and Islamic State. The piece, citing analysts including Jassim al-Bahadli, Ahmed Younis, and Gen. Mohammed al-Jubouri, noted the withdrawal followed a 2024 agreement under President Biden and was executed under President Trump after over 4,500 U.S. deaths in more than two decades of operations.
