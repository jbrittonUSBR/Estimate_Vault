# Houthis target Saudi base as Iran denies involvement in Yemen war

[Source](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/world/houthis-target-saudi-base-iran-denies-yemen-war-involvement-6383191)

(U) SANAA, Yemen — Yemen's Houthis said Saudi Arabia conducted 54 airstrikes across western Yemen over 24 hours after the group fired dozens of drones and missiles at Saudi military facilities, prompting civil defence alerts in the southern cities of Khamis Mushait and Abha and later in Najran. Houthi military spokesman Yahya Saree said the strikes, using F-15 and Typhoon aircraft, hit Taiz, Lahj, Al-Jawf, Marib and Hodeida; the group's Al-Masirah TV reported two civilians killed when a bridge in Taiz was destroyed, while two military sources told AFP that Houthi missile attacks in western Yemen killed eight troops of the Saudi-backed government forces. Iran's foreign ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baqaei denied that Tehran interferes in Yemeni affairs and described the Houthis as "entirely independent actors," as the UN migration agency reported that 93,864 people had fled the renewed fighting.
