# Department of Justice Removes Alien Terrorist in First Use of United States Alien Terrorist Removal Court

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/security/library/news/2026/09/sec-260911-doj01.htm)

(U) WASHINGTON — The Justice Department announced the first-ever removal under the Alien Terrorist Removal Court, saying Nazira Haji Zada, 47, an Afghan national formerly residing in Fort Worth, Texas, was removed after conceding that she is an alien terrorist for supporting her family's ISIS-inspired Election Day 2024 mass-shooting plot. According to the department's release, the court issued its removal order on 20 August and unsealed it on 11 September after her return to Afghanistan, leaving her permanently inadmissible to the United States. The government said it relied on classified information while providing her and her two federal public defenders with roughly half a terabyte of documents, and noted that her son Abdullah Haji Zada was sentenced to 15 years in prison while son-in-law Nasir Ahmad Tawhedi awaited sentencing.
