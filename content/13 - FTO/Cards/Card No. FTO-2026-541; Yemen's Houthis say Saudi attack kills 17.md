# Yemen's Houthis say Saudi attack kills 17

[Source](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/world/yemen-houthis-saudi-strikes-kill-seventeen-6575956)

(U) SANAA, YEMEN — CNA reported that Yemen's Houthis said Saudi-led coalition airstrikes killed 17 people and destroyed several buildings in Hodeida on November 23, with the group's telecommunications ministry saying 17 workers were killed, 11 wounded and services disrupted for tens of thousands. The Houthi military spokesman also said an attack in Lahj governorate struck buses and transport vehicles and caused casualties. Saudi Arabia did not immediately comment, and AFP said it could not independently verify the claims.
