# Saudi-led coalition says intercepted Houthi missile in western region

[Source](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/saudi-led-coalition-says-intercepted-houthi-missile-in-western-region-229699)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — Coalition spokesman Turki al-Malki said on November 22 that air defenses destroyed a ballistic missile launched toward the kingdom's Red Sea coast, where the Yanbu crude-export terminal and Jazan refinery are located. Citing Saudi authorities, the report said Houthi attacks had killed 26 people in the kingdom since September, including 23 in strikes on airports. It noted that a tower housing the Public Investment Fund was damaged in Riyadh's financial district on November 19 and that an Aramco skyscraper had been hit earlier in the month.
