# Saudi Arabia confirms Yemen’s Houthi rebels tried to attack its capital with ballistic missile

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/09/20/world-news/saudi-arabia-confirms-yemen-houthi-terrorists-attempted-to-strike-capital-riyadh/)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — Saudi Arabia’s coalition confirmed that Yemen’s Houthi rebels attempted to strike the capital with a ballistic missile, the first such targeting of Riyadh since tensions escalated; the missile was intercepted with no immediate reports of casualties or damage.
