(U) A UK court convicted Mohamed Mohamoud, a 22-year-old resident of Bristol, for preparing acts of terrorism after he traveled to Somalia in an attempt to join the Islamic State (ISIS).
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Radicalization Path:** Court proceedings revealed that Mohamoud—who was born in Somalia and moved to Bristol in 2020—became radicalized after viewing extremist content online, specifically via YouTube and TikTok.
- **The Plot and Travel:** Mohamoud admitted that he flew to Somalia with the explicit intent of joining the terrorist organization and achieving "martyrdom" on the battlefield.
- **Disruption by Counter-Terrorism:** Investigators noted that while he successfully traveled back to East Africa, his attempts to directly integrate into the militant group's core ranks were ultimately thwarted by ongoing UK and international counter-terrorism operations.
- **Legal Outcome:** Following an investigation by Counter Terrorism Policing, Mohamoud was prosecuted and found guilty at Winchester Crown Court for preparation of terrorist acts under the UK Terrorism Act.
