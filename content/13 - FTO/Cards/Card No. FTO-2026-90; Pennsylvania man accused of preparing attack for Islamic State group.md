# Pennsylvania man accused of preparing attack for Islamic State group

[Source](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/nation/2026/09/15/pennsylvania-man-charged-alleged-isis-attack-plot/91783241007/)

(U) PITTSBURGH, United States — A Pennsylvania man was charged in federal court with obtaining a semiautomatic rifle and ammunition to carry out an attack in support of the Islamic State group, USA TODAY reported. Jonathan Hunter Kramer, 21, was arrested after FBI agents descended on a hotel room in Cranberry Township and was charged with receiving a firearm with the intent to use it in a felony or act of terrorism. Court documents described a history of plotting a mass-casualty attack as a minor, discussions of targeting non-Muslims and engagement with ISIS propaganda, and FBI Director Kash Patel said the subject had repeatedly expressed extremist rhetoric while acquiring weapons. The charges followed an FBI investigation that authorities said thwarted the planned attack.
