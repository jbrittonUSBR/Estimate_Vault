# Iran President Tells U.N. Iran Will Not Bow Down to Trump’s Terrorism

[Source](https://newrepublic.com/post/215765/iran-president-un-speech-trump-terrorism)

(U) NEW YORK — In a New Republic column, writer Malcolm Ferguson reported that Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian used his UN General Assembly speech to reject President Donald Trump’s characterization of Iran as a terrorist threat, saying “We have been the victims of terrorism” and that “those who are terrorists … describe us as such,” as the U.S. delegate walked out. According to the column, Pezeshkian refused to reopen the Strait of Hormuz and vowed Iran would not “bow our head” on its nuclear right, stating “no nuclear weapons, and no limitations to peaceful nuclear technology.” The piece is opinionated political commentary, and its framing and characterizations are the author’s.
