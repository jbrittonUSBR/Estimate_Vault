# Taylor Swift ISIS concert plot foiled with Israel’s help, ex-Mossad chief says

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/world/taylor-swift-isis-concert-plot-foiled-israels-help-ex-mossad-chief-says)

(U) Vienna, Austria — Fox News reported that former Mossad director David Barnea said at a Jerusalem conference that Austrian intelligence first detected the 2024 threat and asked Israel to help identify the network behind a planned attack on Taylor Swift's concerts. Barnea said the operation prevented mass casualties; Austrian authorities arrested a 19-year-old suspect on August 7, 2024, one day before the first scheduled show, and three sold-out concerts were canceled. Fox News noted that U.S. officials had also shared intelligence with Austria and that Barnea separately described alleged Iranian and Hamas networks in Europe.
