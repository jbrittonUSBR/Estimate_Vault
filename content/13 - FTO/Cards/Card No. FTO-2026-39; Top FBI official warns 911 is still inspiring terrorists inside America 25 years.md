# Top FBI official warns 9/11 is still inspiring terrorists inside America 25 years later

[Source](https://abcnews.com/US/top-fbi-official-warns-911-inspiring-terrorists-inside/story?id=136258064)

(U) WASHINGTON, D.C., United States — In an interview with ABC News ahead of the 25th anniversary of the September 11, 2001 attacks, FBI co-deputy director Chris Raia warned that the threat from foreign terrorist organizations has hardly gone away and is in some ways harder to stop, because people are now radicalized "from the comforts of their own home, through social media, the internet." Raia said the FBI is "way better postured now in 2026" to stop large-scale plots but that al-Qaeda and ISIS are relentlessly seeking to develop "lone offenders" inside the United States, and officials said the bureau had arrested at least 16 people in the past year and a half for allegedly plotting or supporting plots on behalf of those groups. He said 9/11 remains a motivating factor for online radicalization, calling it "their Super Bowl," and cited the recent arrest of 35-year-old Jessica Bowie of Albany, New York, for allegedly plotting to bomb the state capitol. The account is drawn from FBI statements reported by ABC News.
