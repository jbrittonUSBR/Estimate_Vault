# Why are Saudi defences struggling to rebuff Houthi attacks?

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/defence/why-are-saudi-defences-struggling-to-rebuff-houthi-attacks/articleshow/134875724.cms)

(U) RIYADH, Saudi Arabia — Analysts cited by AFP attributed Saudi air defenses' struggles to rebuffing Houthi attacks to factors including the use of low-cost drones and missiles, saturation tactics, and gaps in coverage, following recent deadly strikes on Riyadh airport.
