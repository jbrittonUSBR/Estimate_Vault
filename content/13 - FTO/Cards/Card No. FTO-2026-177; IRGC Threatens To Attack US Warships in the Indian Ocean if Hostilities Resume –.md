# IRGC Threatens To Attack US Warships in the Indian Ocean if Hostilities Resume – Warning Comes as UK Probes Iran Links in Foiled Terrorist Attack at Fairford Airbase

[Source](https://www.thegatewaypundit.com/2026/09/irgc-threatens-attack-us-warships-indian-ocean-if/)

(U) TEHRAN — Ali Mohammadi, deputy political commander of the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps Navy, said on September 27, 2026, that Iran would strike U.S. warships "even in the Indian Ocean" if hostilities resumed, according to Fars News via The Gateway Pundit. The report said the warning followed the rejection of an Iranian proposal for a seven-day truce in the Strait of Hormuz and coincided with British counterterrorism inquiries into five men arrested near RAF Fairford, a base used by U.S. bombers. The Gateway Pundit is a partisan outlet, and the statements were reported without independent confirmation of operational intent.
