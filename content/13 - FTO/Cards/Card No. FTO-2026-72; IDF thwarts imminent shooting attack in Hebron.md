# IDF thwarts imminent shooting attack in Hebron

[Source](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/433116)

(U) HEBRON, West Bank — Israeli troops arrested a Palestinian man in Hebron over the Rosh Hashanah holiday who, according to the military, was planning to carry out an imminent shooting attack against Israeli civilians. The arrest was carried out on Shabbat under Shin Bet direction, with the IDF saying the suspect, named as Abd al-Rahman, was detained based on intelligence indicating he planned to open fire in the immediate future. The operation came after Central Command raised readiness across Judea and Samaria ahead of the Jewish New Year, conducting drills and arresting more than 220 wanted suspects in the preceding days. Following the arrest, the IDF said its soldiers would continue to operate to thwart terrorism throughout the region.
