# Houthi missile damages Saudi Aramco Tower in Riyadh in fresh attack

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16276239/Houthi-missile-damages-Saudi-Aramco-Tower-Riyadh-fresh-attack.html)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — The Daily Mail reported that debris from an intercepted missile damaged windows in an Aramco office tower, with Saudi officials reporting limited damage and no casualties. The Houthis claimed responsibility, while Saudi authorities also reported intercepting other missiles; responsibility for the tower damage was described through competing official accounts.
