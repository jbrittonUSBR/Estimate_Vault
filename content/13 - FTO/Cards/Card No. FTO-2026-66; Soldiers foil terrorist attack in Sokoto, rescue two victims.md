# Soldiers foil terrorist attack in Sokoto, rescue two victims

[Source](https://punchng.com/soldiers-foil-terrorist-attack-in-sokoto-rescue-two-victims/)

(U) SOKOTO, Nigeria — Troops of Sector 2, Joint Task Force North-West Operation FANSAN YAMMA, responded to a distress call after terrorists attacked Unguwan Lalle in Sabon Birni Local Government Area of Sokoto State on 13 September, engaging the assailants and forcing them to withdraw while rescuing two kidnapped victims. The troops later re-established contact after intelligence indicated the attackers were regrouping and recovered 157 rounds of PKT ammunition and phone batteries. In a separate operation on 12 September, troops on stop-and-search along the Kaduna–Birnin Gwari Road intercepted a suspected illicit drug peddler identified as Yohana Musa, according to a statement from the task force's media information officer, Lt. Col. Aliyu Danja.
