(U) U.S. Africa Command (AFRICOM) reported that the U.S. military carried out an airstrike against al-Shabaab on July 15, 2026, near Welmaro in southern Somalia (about 100 km north of the port city of Kismayo). The strike was conducted in coordination with Somalia’s Federal Government and aimed at degrading the group’s ability to threaten U.S. forces and American citizens overseas. AFRICOM said it would continue working with Somali forces against the militants but provided no further details on the assets used, casualties, or the strike’s impact, citing operational security. Independent verification was not immediately available.
