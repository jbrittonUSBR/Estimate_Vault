# Jordan acknowledges civilian casualties in Syria airstrike against 'Islamic State'

[Source](https://www.dw.com/en/jordan-acknowledges-civilian-casualties-in-syria-airstrike-against-islamic-state/a-79775653)

(U) Syria — Jordan acknowledged that civilians were killed in an airstrike it said targeted Islamic State militants, and a government spokesperson expressed regret, DW reported. The strike circumstances and exact civilian toll were not detailed in the available report.
