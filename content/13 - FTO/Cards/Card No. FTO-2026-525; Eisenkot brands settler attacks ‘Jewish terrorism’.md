# Eisenkot brands settler attacks ‘Jewish terrorism’

[Source](https://rt-english.com/news/649227-eisenkot-jewish-terrorism-west-bank/)

(U) Hebron, West Bank — RT reported that settlers raided several Palestinian communities early Sunday, burning vehicles and daubing racist graffiti, with no injuries reported and Israeli security forces saying they opened an investigation. Gadi Eisenkot, leader of the Yashar Party and tasked with forming a new government, wrote on X that the arson attacks were "Jewish terrorism" and accused National Security Minister Itamar Ben-Gvir and Finance Minister Bezalel Smotrich of fomenting the violence; other Israeli lawmakers warned of a third intifada or international sanctions. RT cited the Palestinian Wall and Settlement Resistance Commission as counting 1,369 settler attacks in October, up from 647 in September.
