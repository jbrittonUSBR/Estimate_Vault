# Saudi-led coalition intercepts Houthi missile launched towards Riyadh

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/14/saudi-led-coalition-intercepts-houthi-missile-launched-towards-riyadh)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — On October 14, 2026, the Saudi-led coalition intercepted a Houthi missile launched toward Riyadh. No casualties or major damage were immediately reported, but the attack underscored continued Houthi efforts to strike targets in the Saudi capital amid escalating regional tensions.
