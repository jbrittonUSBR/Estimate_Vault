# Stabbing victim recounts: “I fought the terrorist - Then I saw I was covered in blood"

[Source](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/432847)

(U) PETAH TIKVA, Israel — Netanel Jacobs, wounded in a terrorist stabbing attack in Samaria on the morning of Monday, September 7, 2026, recounted from his hospital bed at Beilinson Hospital his struggle with the assailant. Jacobs said he fought the attacker with his bare hands, punching and pushing him, and continued the struggle even after the knife broke, choking the man before he escaped toward his car. He chased the fleeing attacker with a rock before realizing he had been covered in blood and had suffered stab wounds to his head, neck and hand. His parents, Avdiel and Penina, said their son was in good and stable condition.
