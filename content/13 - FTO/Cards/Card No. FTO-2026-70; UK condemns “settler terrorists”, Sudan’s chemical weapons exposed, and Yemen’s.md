# UK condemns “settler terrorists”, Sudan’s chemical weapons exposed, and Yemen’s war escalates: The Cheat Sheet

[Source](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news/2026/09/11/uk-condemns-settler-terrorists-sudan-chemical-weapons-yemen-war-cheat-sheet)

(U) GENEVA — The New Humanitarian's weekly Cheat Sheet reported that UK Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband told parliament on 8 September that Britain would recognise Israel's occupation of the West Bank as illegal, condemn "settler terrorists," ban goods from Israeli settlements, sanction companies and individuals involved in settlement expansion and refuse to export weapons that support the occupation, with 11 other countries including Canada, France, Ireland, Spain and Sweden announcing similar moves. The roundup also cited New York Times and Washington Post reporting on an intelligence dossier describing a Sudanese military chemical weapons programme in 2024 that used chlorine gas against the Rapid Support Forces, and noted that Israeli strikes in south Lebanon on 7 September reportedly killed 12 people and forced around 20,000 to flee the Nabatieh district. It further reported that the Houthis seized the Red Sea port of Mokha and an island in the Bab al-Mandeb strait, with 46,000 people displaced across Yemen in a week.
