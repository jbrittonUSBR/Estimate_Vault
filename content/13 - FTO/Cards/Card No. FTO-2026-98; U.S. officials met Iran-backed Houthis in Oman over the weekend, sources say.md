# U.S. officials met Iran-backed Houthis in Oman over the weekend, sources say

[Source](https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/u-officials-met-iran-backed-110356019.html)

(U) MUSCAT, Oman — U.S. officials held previously undisclosed talks with Houthi representatives in Oman over the weekend, five sources told Reuters, in a meeting one source said took place on Sunday at the U.S. Embassy in Muscat. The Houthis told the U.S. side they had no intention of attacking American vessels and were committed to the 2025 ceasefire, and a Yemeni source said they also pledged not to target Israeli or other commercial shipping except vessels belonging to Saudi Arabia. The Houthi delegation included sanctioned senior official Mohammad Abdulsalam and Abdelmalik al-Ajiri, according to sources, while the State Department said it had "no comment on this specific reported meeting"; the talks followed the Houthis' seizure of Yemen's entire Red Sea coastline.
