# Saudi-led coalition intercepts third Houthi drone in Eastern Province

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/saudi-led-coalition-intercepts-third-houthi-drone-in-eastern-province/)

(U) Eastern Province, Saudi Arabia — Crypto Briefing reports that coalition spokesperson Turki al-Maliki said a drone was intercepted and destroyed in the Eastern Province, the third interception announced in a short period. The outlet says officials reported no damage or casualties. It characterizes the interceptions as part of heightened Saudi defensive activity amid Houthi attacks, while noting that the article also includes prediction-market analysis.
