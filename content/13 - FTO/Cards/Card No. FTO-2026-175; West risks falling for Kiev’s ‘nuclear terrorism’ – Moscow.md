# West risks falling for Kiev’s ‘nuclear terrorism’ – Moscow

[Source](https://www.rt.com/russia/646349-nuclear-terrorism-ukraine-miroshnik/)

(U) Moscow, Russia — RT, a Russian state outlet, reported that Russian Ambassador-at-Large Rodion Miroshnik warned the West risks becoming a victim of the "nuclear terrorism" it enables by backing Ukraine, pointing to an escalation in Ukrainian attacks on the Zaporozhye Nuclear Power Plant and nearby Energodar. Miroshnik told TASS that Western governments understand Kiev's "increasingly provocative tactics" but continue to "turn a blind eye," and linked the escalation to the EU's 90 billion euro loan package for Ukraine. The report said the plant's main external power line was severed in March and restored only recently, and that attacks on the plant and its personnel had killed at least 13 people since the spring, according to site officials.
