# UK sends refuelling plane to Saudi Arabia as Houthi advance threatens global oil routes

[Source](https://www.naturalnews.com/2026-09-23-uk-refuelling-plane-saudi-arabia-houthi.html)

(U) London, United Kingdom — According to the Natural News report (a conspiracy-oriented outlet), the British government agreed on September 23, 2026 to deploy an RAF Voyager tanker to Saudi Arabia for time-limited air-to-air refuelling operations in support of Saudi defenses against Iran-backed Houthi attacks. The deployment comes as Houthi forces have seized Mokha and Perim Island in the Bab el-Mandeb strait, threatening a route carrying roughly 12 percent of global trade and contributing to Brent crude prices near $110 per barrel and UK inflation rising to 3.1 percent.
