# Saudi Arabia, Bahrain report new attacks as Iran says it remains open to a deal with U.S.

[Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/22/saudi-houthi-bahrain-iran-attacks-araghchi-talks.html)

(U) Manama, Bahrain — CNBC reported that Saudi Arabia said it intercepted a Houthi drone aimed at Riyadh and a ballistic missile aimed at its western region, while Bahrain's Interior Ministry said an Iranian attack set fire to a civilian industrial facility and injured two people. Iranian state media claimed the Revolutionary Guard struck U.S. military facilities in Bahrain and downed an MQ-9 drone; U.S. Central Command did not immediately comment. Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi told the Iran newspaper that the June Islamabad memorandum was no longer in effect but that Tehran remained open to a negotiated settlement.
