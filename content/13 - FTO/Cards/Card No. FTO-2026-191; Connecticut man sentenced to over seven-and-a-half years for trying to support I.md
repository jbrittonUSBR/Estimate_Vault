# Connecticut man sentenced to over seven-and-a-half years for trying to support ISIS

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/09/30/us-news/connecticut-man-ahmad-elshazly-sentenced-for-trying-to-support-isis/)

(U) NEW HAVEN, Connecticut — On September 30, 2026, the New York Post reported that Ahmad Khalil Elshazly, 29, of West Haven, Connecticut, was sentenced to 92 months in prison followed by lifetime supervised release after pleading guilty on November 30, 2022 to attempting to provide material support to ISIS. According to the report, Elshazly had paid $500 to an undercover agent, was arrested on December 15, 2019 in Stonington, Connecticut, and the Department of Justice had sought a 15-year sentence.
