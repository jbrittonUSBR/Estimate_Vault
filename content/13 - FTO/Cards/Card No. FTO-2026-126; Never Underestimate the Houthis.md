# Never Underestimate the Houthis

[Source](https://www.rand.org/pubs/commentary/2026/09/never-underestimate-the-houthis.html)

(U) SANTA MONICA, United States — A RAND commentary by political scientist Alexandra Stark, published September 21, 2026, and originally in the New York Times on August 21, argues that Yemen's Houthi movement should be treated as a pragmatic, self-interested actor rather than a mere Iranian proxy. Stark writes that the Houthis, who control much of northern Yemen, are positioned to shape the next phase of the US-Iran conflict through their ability to disrupt traffic in the Bab al-Mandab Strait, through which 12-15 percent of maritime trade travels. She notes the group resumed attacks on Red Sea shipping in mid-2026, striking Saudi-linked tankers and Saudi energy and transport infrastructure, and that with Iran effectively closing the Strait of Hormuz, the Red Sea-Suez route has become even more important. The piece concludes that de-escalating regional tensions will be difficult without confronting the Houthi threat directly, since no deal with Iran can be assumed to bind the group's actions.
