# Operation 'Yemen Dawn': Saudi-led coalition joins fight to wrest Red Sea from Houthis

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/middle-east/operation-yemen-dawn-saudi-led-coalition-joins-fight-to-wrest-red-sea-from-houthis/articleshow/134700138.cms)

(U) RIYADH, Saudi Arabia — The Saudi-led coalition launched Operation 'Yemen Dawn' with massive air support to help Yemeni government forces reclaim territory along Yemen's Red Sea coast from Iran-backed Houthi rebels. The offensive aimed to secure the strategic Bab el-Mandeb Strait after Houthi advances threatened critical shipping lanes.
