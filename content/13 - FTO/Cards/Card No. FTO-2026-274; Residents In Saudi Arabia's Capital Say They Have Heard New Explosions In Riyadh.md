[Source](https://www.huffpost.com/entry/riyadh-explosions-houthi-rebels_n_6acf5645e4b08a0fe85498f8)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — On October 14, 2026, residents in Riyadh reported hearing new explosions in the Saudi capital amid renewed Houthi attacks. Saudi air defenses were active, and authorities worked to confirm details of the incidents as part of the ongoing escalation between the Houthis and the Saudi-led coalition.
