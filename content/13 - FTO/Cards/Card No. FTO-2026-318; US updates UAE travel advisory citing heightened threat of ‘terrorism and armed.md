# US updates UAE travel advisory citing heightened threat of ‘terrorism and armed conflict’ by Iranian-supported Houthis

[Source](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/world/us-updates-uae-travel-advisory-citing-heightened-threat-of-terrorism-and-armed-conflict-by-iranian-supported-houthis/article71622512.ece)

(U) United Arab Emirates — BusinessLine, citing ANI and the U.S. advisory, reported that the UAE remained at Level 3 (“Reconsider Travel”) while the advisory summary was updated to note an increased potential for Houthi armed attacks. It also cited restrictions on U.S. personnel travel to military bases and energy infrastructure and warned of rapid escalation; the advisory level itself did not change.
