# Iraq: US troops withdraw from their Iraqi US-led Operation Inherent Resolve mission against ISIS after 12 years as Baghdad delays militia disarmament

[Source](https://www.dw.com/en/iraq-us-troops-withdraw-from-their-iraqi-us-led-operation-inherent-resolve-mission-against-isis-after-12-years-as-baghdad-delays-militia-disarmament/a-79462926)

(U) BAGHDAD — The United States completed the withdrawal of its remaining 2,500 troops from Iraq by September 30, 2026, ending the 12-year Operation Inherent Resolve against ISIS, Deutsche Welle reported. Iraqi Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi declared October 1 a national "Independence Day," while Baghdad extended a deadline for militia disarmament to June 30, 2027. Iran-backed groups including Kata'ib Hezbollah rejected disarmament, raising concerns about the durability of the security gains.
