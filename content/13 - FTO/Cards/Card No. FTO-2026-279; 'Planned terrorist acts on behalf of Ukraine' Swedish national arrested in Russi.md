# 'Planned terrorist acts on behalf of Ukraine': Swedish national arrested in Russia

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/europe/planned-terrorist-acts-on-behalf-of-ukraine-russia-arrests-swedish-man-charges-him-with-illegal-border-crossing/articleshow/134964299.cms)

(U) PSKOV REGION, RUSSIA — The Times of India reported that Russia’s Federal Security Service (FSB) said it had arrested a Swedish national in the Pskov region and accused him of illegally crossing the border and planning sabotage and terrorist attacks on behalf of Ukraine. The FSB alleged that the man had served in a Ukrainian drone unit and released video it said showed him describing his activities. These remained Russian state allegations; the claims about a planned plot and the man’s role were not independently verified in the report.
