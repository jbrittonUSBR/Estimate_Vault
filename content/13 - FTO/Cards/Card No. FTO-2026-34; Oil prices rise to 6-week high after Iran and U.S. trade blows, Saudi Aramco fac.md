# Oil prices rise to 6-week high after Iran and U.S. trade blows, Saudi Aramco facilities reportedly hit

[Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/07/oil-prices-rise-to-6-week-high-after-iran-and-us-trade-blows-saudi-aramco-facilities-reportedly-hit.html)

(U) NEW YORK, United States — Oil prices rose to a six-week high on Monday, September 7, 2026, as the United States and Iran traded strikes over the weekend. Brent crude futures climbed 1.5% to $97.73 per barrel, the highest since July 23, while West Texas Intermediate rose 1.8% to $93.10. U.S. Central Command said American forces struck three Iranian oil tankers on Saturday after Iran launched ballistic missiles at two Navy warships, and Saudi Aramco oil facilities in Jizan were reportedly hit in fresh attacks that same day, with responsibility not immediately clear. Iranian Parliament Speaker Mohammad Baqer Qalibaf and U.S. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth exchanged threats over the escalating hostilities.
