# Indian Embassy in Saudi Arabia issues safety advisory amid Houthi attacks

[Source](https://www.business-standard.com/world-news/indian-embassy-in-saudi-arabia-issues-safety-advisory-amid-houthi-attacks-126101800198_1.html)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — The Indian Embassy advised Indian nationals to follow Saudi authorities’ emergency alerts and move to an interior room away from windows when danger alerts are issued. It urged people to remain indoors, avoid open areas and rooftops, and wait for the threat to pass; the advisory followed Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia. The instructions were presented as safety guidance, not as a claim about the cause or details of any individual attack.
