# Saudi strikes on Yemen's Taiz kill dozens: Houthis

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/middle-east/saudi-strikes-on-yemens-taiz-kill-dozens-houthis/articleshow/134528810.cms)

(U) TAIZ — Saudi airstrikes hit Houthi-held areas of Taiz in southwestern Yemen, and Houthi military spokesman Yahya Saree said the strikes killed or wounded nearly 50 people, according to Agence France-Presse via The Times of India. Saree did not provide a breakdown of dead and wounded, and the figures could not be independently verified. The strikes were reported on September 27, 2026, as fighting between the Saudi-led coalition and the Houthis intensified.
