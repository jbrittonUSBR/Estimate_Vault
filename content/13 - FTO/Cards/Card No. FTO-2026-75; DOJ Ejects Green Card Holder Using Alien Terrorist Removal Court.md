# DOJ Ejects Green Card Holder Using Alien Terrorist Removal Court

[Source](https://newrepublic.com/post/215394/justice-department-ejects-green-card-holder-afghanistan-alien-terrorist-removal-court)

(U) WASHINGTON — The Justice Department used the Alien Terrorist Removal Court for the first time to deport Nazira Haji Zada, a 47-year-old Afghan lawful permanent resident from Fort Worth, Texas, Attorney General Todd Blanche said, according to The New Republic. Zada was removed to Afghanistan last month; her son, Abdullah Haji Zada, and son-in-law Nasir Ahmad Tawhedi pleaded guilty in 2024 to attempting to buy two AK-47-style rifles and 500 rounds of ammunition from an undercover FBI agent for a planned attack in Oklahoma. The article said Zada had no knowledge of their plans, and that her public defenders raised due-process concerns about the specialized removal court, including the government's refusal to show them classified evidence despite their security clearance. The account relied on Justice Department statements and court filings and included legal-advocacy criticism of the procedure.
