# Saudi Arabia Partly Reopens Riyadh Airport Despite Renewed Houthi Threats

[Source](https://www.huffpost.com/entry/saudi-arabia-reopens-airport-houthi-attacks_n_6ade1d89e4b0320e45d2c1e7)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — Reuters reported that King Khalid International Airport partially reopened two terminals after a Houthi-claimed attack killed 13 people on October 10, while the group continued to call the airport a military target. Saudi authorities reported intercepting missiles and drones; debris injured two people and damaged homes and vehicles, and flight-tracking data showed little airport traffic after reopening.
