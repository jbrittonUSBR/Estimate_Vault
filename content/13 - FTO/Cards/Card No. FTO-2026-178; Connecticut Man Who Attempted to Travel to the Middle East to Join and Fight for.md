# Connecticut Man Who Attempted to Travel to the Middle East to Join and Fight for ISIS Sentenced to Over Seven Years in Prison

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/security/library/news/2026/09/sec-260928-doj01.htm)

(U) WASHINGTON — The U.S. Department of Justice announced on September 28, 2026, that Ahmad Khalil Elshazly, 29, of West Haven, Connecticut, was sentenced to 92 months in prison and lifetime supervised release for attempting to provide material support to ISIS. U.S. District Judge Victor A. Bolden imposed the sentence; Elshazly was arrested in Stonington on December 15, 2019, and pleaded guilty on November 30, 2022. GlobalSecurity.org reproduced the department's press release, which said Elshazly had sought to travel to the Middle East to join the group.
