# PA governor honors terrorists involved in deadly 2004 Tel Aviv bombing

[Source](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/436873)

(U) Nablus, West Bank — Israel National News reported, citing JNS, that Palestinian Authority governor Ghassan Daghlas met Yahya Zahran and Hassan Ahmad after their release from Israeli prison, where they had served 22 years for involvement in the 2004 Carmel Market suicide bombing in Tel Aviv that killed three Israelis and wounded more than 30. The Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine claimed responsibility for that bombing. A statement on the PA's Nablus Governorate Facebook page praised the men's "patience, sacrifice and steadfastness" and said the "joy of freedom" would be incomplete until all prisoners were freed.
