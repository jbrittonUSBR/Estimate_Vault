# Court holds N.L. man over fear he will commit terrorist acts after threats against mosque

[Source](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/newfoundland-labrador/gander-resident-matthew-collins-accused-of-making-threat-against-mosque-police-say-9.7433124)

(U) GANDER, NEWFOUNDLAND AND LABRADOR — CBC reported that Matthew Collins appeared by video in provincial court while remaining in custody awaiting a bail hearing, facing eight charges including possessing explosives, uttering threats and intimidation regarding a religious property. The RCMP said police had reasonable grounds to fear Collins might commit terrorism offences and had filed a court application used when there is a "fear of terrorism offense." CBC reported that the investigation began after the Royal Newfoundland Constabulary sought help locating someone posting online threats toward a mosque, and that the application would be heard after a decision on bail.
