# Saudi-led coalition intercepts Houthi missiles targeting Riyadh and south

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/19/saudi-led-coalition-intercepts-houthi-missiles-targeting-riyadh-and-south)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — Saudi Arabia said Houthi attacks damaged the Public Investment Fund tower and nearby buildings, but caused no injuries. The Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted missiles directed at Riyadh and the south, including four ballistic missiles aimed at the southern region. Saudi Civil Defence issued warnings for Khamis Mushait and Abha before declaring the danger had passed, according to Al Jazeera.
