# Oil up as Houthi attacks on Riyadh raise supply concerns

[Source](https://www.rte.ie/news/business/2026/1120/1599964-world-oil-prices/)

(U) Dublin, Ireland — RTÉ reported that Brent crude fell 0.7% to $91.31 a barrel and West Texas Intermediate eased to $82.85 on November 20, putting both benchmarks on track for a third straight weekly decline as Middle East export flows recovered. The report said the Houthis had fired missiles at Riyadh and warned shipping firms to evacuate Saudi Red Sea ports, while Iran's Revolutionary Guards Navy said through state media that two tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz. BNP Paribas strategist Aldo Spanjer and other analysts cited in the article attributed the drop to strong exports and expectations of a supply surplus, with OPEC+ due to meet on November 29 to set 2027 output.
