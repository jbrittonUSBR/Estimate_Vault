# Houthis said to have seized island in Red Sea

(U) SANA'A, Yemen — Yemen's Houthi movement reportedly seized control of an island in the Red Sea, according to reports. The move would expand the Iran-backed group's operational reach in a strategically vital maritime corridor already disrupted by its attacks on shipping. The reported seizure raises further concerns over freedom of navigation and regional security in the Bab al-Mandab approaches.
