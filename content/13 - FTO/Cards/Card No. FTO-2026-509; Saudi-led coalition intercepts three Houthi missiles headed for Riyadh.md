# Saudi-led coalition intercepts three Houthi missiles headed for Riyadh

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/21/saudi-led-coalition-intercepts-three-houthi-missiles-headed-for-riyadh)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — Al Jazeera reported that the coalition said it intercepted three Houthi ballistic missiles aimed at Riyadh early on November 21, after Saudi civil defense issued eight emergency alerts in 24 hours and a day after the Public Investment Fund tower in the financial district was damaged. Pakistan's Foreign Minister Mohammad Ishaq Dar condemned the attack on the district as a threat to regional peace and security. The report noted a coalition "special operation" against Hodeidah port and Sanaa, Houthi threats against Jeddah and other Red Sea ports, U.N. concern over humanitarian supply routes, and more than 350,000 people displaced, with Taiz receiving the largest number.
