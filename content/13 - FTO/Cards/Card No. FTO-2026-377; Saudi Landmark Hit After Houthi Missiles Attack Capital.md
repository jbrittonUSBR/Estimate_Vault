# Saudi Landmark Hit After Houthi Missiles Attack Capital

[Source](https://dailycaller.com/2026/11/08/saudi-arabia-aramco-tower-hit-houthi-conflict)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — The Daily Caller reported that falling shrapnel from an intercepted missile caused minor damage to an Aramco office tower, with no injuries reported by Saudi officials. The article said Saudi authorities did not identify the source of the attempted attack and noted that the Houthis had not claimed responsibility at publication.
