# Oil Price Today (November 20): Brent crude rises to $93 as fresh Houthi attacks heighten worries. What are experts saying?

[Source](https://m.economictimes.com/markets/commodities/news/oil-price-today-november-20-brent-crude-rises-to-93-as-fresh-houthi-attacks-heighten-worries-what-are-experts-saying/articleshow/136010579.cms)

(U) Mumbai, India — The Economic Times reported that Brent crude rose 0.55% to $92.60 a barrel on November 20 and WTI gained 0.38% to $83.53, as missile strikes near Riyadh and reported tanker disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz renewed concern about regional supply. Both benchmarks were still heading for a third weekly decline because crude shipments through the waterway had recovered, according to the article. It cited Goldman Sachs raising its December Brent forecast to $105 from $90 and flagging a possible $140 level in 2027, and noted the IEA's warning that global demand could fall more than 4% by 2035, with the author stating the views were those of the cited analysts.
