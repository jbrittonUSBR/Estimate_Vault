# Indonesia arrests 11 over suspected links to terrorist networks

[Source](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/asia/indonesia-arrests-11-suspected-terrorists-densus-88-6432196)

(U) JAKARTA, Indonesia — Indonesian counterterrorism police arrested 11 suspects linked to alleged terrorist networks in a series of raids across the country. The arrests by Densus 88 were part of ongoing efforts to disrupt plots and dismantle cells affiliated with extremist groups operating in Indonesia.
