# October 7: Terrorism, Enablers and Intelligence Failures

[Source](https://goodmenproject.com/featured-content/october-7-terrorism-enablers-intelligence-failures-sjbn/)

(U) Jerusalem, Israel — In an opinion interview published on the anniversary of the October 7 attacks, Scott Douglas Jacobsen spoke with Amos Guiora, Irina Tsukerman and Giovanni Giacalone about definitions of terrorism, institutional "enablers" and intelligence failures. Guiora criticized Israeli political leaders and said warnings from female surveillance soldiers were ignored before the 2023 attack, while Giacalone argued European states had underestimated Islamist networks and Tsukerman described how extremist groups exploit smaller attacks and outside backing. The panel also disagreed about deradicalization, with Giacalone calling it ineffective and Tsukerman saying early intervention could still disrupt radicalization.
