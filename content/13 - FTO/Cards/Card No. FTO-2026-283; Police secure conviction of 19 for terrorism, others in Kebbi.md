# Police secure conviction of 19 for terrorism, others in Kebbi

[Source](https://punchng.com/police-secure-conviction-of-19-for-terrorism-others-in-kebbi/)

(U) KEBBI STATE, NIGERIA — Punch Newspapers reported that Kebbi State Police said 19 people had been convicted of terrorism, illegal firearm possession and vandalism of critical infrastructure following a 17-month investigation and prosecution. The cases were heard at the Federal High Court in Birnin Kebbi, after intelligence-led operations in several areas of the state. The report attributed the account of the convictions and investigation to the police command.
