# J&K: Police bust LeT-linked OGW network, arrest 3 for aiding terrorists

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/india/jk-police-bust-let-linked-ogw-network-arrest-3-for-aiding-terrorists/articleshow/134249990.cms)

(U) NEW DELHI — Jammu and Kashmir Police in Rajouri district arrested three alleged overground workers linked to Lashkar-e-Taiba — Zakir Husaain, Mohd Azam and Mohd Mustaq — accusing them of providing logistics, safe transit and guides to militants. Police said an improvised explosive device was recovered on the disclosure of one suspect and had allegedly been entrusted to the group for safekeeping ahead of a targeted attack. Investigators were also examining the network's suspected support to high-profile attacks, including one against civilians, The Times of India reported. The accusations have not been tested in court.
