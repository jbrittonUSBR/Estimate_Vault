# Yemeni government forces claim 816 operations targeting Houthi militia in 24 hours

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/yemeni-government-forces-claim-816-operations-targeting-houthi-militia-in-24/)

(U) Aden, Yemen — Crypto Briefing, a prediction-market analysis site, reported that Colonel Majed Abdullah al-Nazili, spokesman for Yemen's internationally recognized government, claimed forces carried out 816 artillery, drone, rocket-launcher and air operations against Houthi positions across Taiz, Lahij, Dhale, Abyan, al-Jawf and Marib in 24 hours. The article said Houthi forces were nevertheless reported to have advanced in parts of Lahij and toward Aden, and noted that its related contract on Houthi entry into Aden by December 31 had been priced lower after the report. It described the piece as interpretive analysis of publicly available information rather than verified reporting.
