# Troops kill eight terrorists, rescue three victims in N’West operations

[Source](https://punchng.com/troops-kill-eight-terrorists-rescue-three-victims-in-nwest-operations/)

(U) Northwest Nigeria — The Punch reported that Nigerian troops killed eight suspected militants and rescued three kidnapped people during coordinated operations in Katsina and Kaduna states. The casualty and rescue figures were attributed to the military; the report did not independently verify them.
