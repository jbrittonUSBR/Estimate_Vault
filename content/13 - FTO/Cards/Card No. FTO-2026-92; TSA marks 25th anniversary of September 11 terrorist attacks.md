# TSA marks 25th anniversary of September 11 terrorist attacks

[Source](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/tsa-marks-25th-anniversary-of-september-11-terrorist-attacks-302876489.html)

(U) WASHINGTON — The Transportation Security Administration marked the 25th anniversary of the September 11, 2001, terrorist attacks on September 11, 2026, honoring the nearly 3,000 people killed and its roughly 60,000 employees. In a release issued through PRNewswire, the agency pointed to its Horizon 25 strategy and to the Aviation and Transportation Security Act, which Congress passed on November 19, 2001, creating TSA. The statement framed the anniversary as a moment to reaffirm the agency's screening mission.
