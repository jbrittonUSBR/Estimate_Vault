# Mother of ISIS jihadist convicted in New York terror plot to 'create the next 9/11' runs for public office to create 'safe and welcoming' environments in woke city

[Source](https://freerepublic.com/focus/f-news/4394845/posts)

(U) MILTON, Canada — Dr. Khdiga R. Metwally, the mother of a convicted ISIS terrorist, was running for public office in Canada, appearing on the ballot for Halton District School Board Ward 1 and 2 trustee, about 30 miles outside Toronto. Her son, Abdulrahman El Bahnasawy, was serving a 40-year U.S. prison sentence for plotting an ISIS terrorist attack in New York City during the summer of 2016; prosecutors said he planned to detonate bombs in Times Square and the subway system and to shoot civilians at concert venues, and he admitted to being inspired to "create the next 9/11." Metwally told the Daily Mail that her son is mentally ill and that she "rejects violence and extremism in all forms," saying "I have never supported any violent act committed by my son or anyone else"; the item was republished on Freerepublic.com from the UK Daily Mail.
