# Yemen govt forces fortify Aden defences as Houthis advance

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/defence/yemen-govt-forces-fortify-aden-defences-as-houthis-advance/articleshow/135815279.cms)

(U) Aden, Yemen — AFP reports that Yemen’s internationally recognized government is strengthening positions west and north of Aden as Houthi forces advance east after taking strategic hilltops in Al-Madaribah wal Ara district. Three military sources told AFP that the defenses include sandbags and tunnels, while government forces have struggled to retake the Red Sea coast near the Bab al-Mandab. The report says fighting has intensified since a 2022 truce collapsed over the summer, with casualties reported on both sides.
