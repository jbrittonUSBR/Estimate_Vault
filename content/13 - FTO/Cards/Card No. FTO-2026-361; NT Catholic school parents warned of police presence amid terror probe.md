# NT Catholic school parents warned of police presence amid terror probe

[Source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-08/nt-catholic-schools-increased-police-terrorism-related-charges/107359480)

(U) Northern Territory, Australia — ABC News reported that Catholic Education NT advised families at 18 schools to expect a precautionary police presence after two teenagers were charged with terrorism-related offences within a week. Authorities said the visits were part of an ongoing investigation, not a response to a specific or imminent threat, and school leaders urged families not to speculate about the allegations.
