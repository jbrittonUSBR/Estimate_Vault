# Intercepted Houthi missiles cause damage in Saudi Arabian city

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/10/26/intercepted-houthi-missiles-cause-damage-in-saudi-arabian-city)

(U) Khamis Mushait, Saudi Arabia — Al Jazeera reported that Saudi state television showed damage to buildings and vehicles from debris after Saudi forces intercepted Houthi missiles. The video report described damage from falling debris; it did not independently verify the launch attribution or provide a confirmed casualty count.
