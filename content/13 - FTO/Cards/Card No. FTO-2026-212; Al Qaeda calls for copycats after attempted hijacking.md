# Al Qaeda calls for copycats after attempted hijacking

[Source](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/434067)

(U) DUBAI, UAE — Al Qaeda praised the Omani man who attempted to hijack a Flydubai flight carrying more than 150 Israelis and called on others to follow his example, according to a report cited by Israel National News on October 4, 2026. In an Arabic-language statement, the group said aircraft carrying "Zionists, Americans and all the other infidels" remained viable targets, invoking the September 11 attacks. On Wednesday, 29-year-old co-pilot Hamam Al-Hamami stabbed Indian captain Smit Machchhar and tried to seize the Israel-bound plane. An Emirati official alleged Al-Hamami intended to crash the aircraft into a high-rise building in Israel — reportedly the Azrieli Towers in Tel Aviv — modeled on the September 11 plot. Machchhar fought back and opened the cockpit door, allowing passengers to subdue the attacker and prevent a crash.
