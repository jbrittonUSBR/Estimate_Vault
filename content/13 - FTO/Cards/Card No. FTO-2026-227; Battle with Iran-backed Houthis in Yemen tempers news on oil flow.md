# Battle with Iran-backed Houthis in Yemen tempers news on oil flow

[Source](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-trump-us-oil-prices-yemen-red-sea-houthis-bab-el-mandeb/)

(U) ADEN, Yemen — Renewed fighting between Saudi-led coalition forces and Iran-backed Houthi rebels in Yemen raised concerns over oil shipping through the Bab el-Mandeb Strait. As coalition forces launched major offensives to reclaim coastal territory, Houthi missile and drone attacks on regional targets heightened risks to global energy flows.
