# Saudi Arabia intercepts more Yemen Houthi missiles and drones, and other Mideast news

[Source](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/saudi-arabia-intercepts-yemen-houthi-missiles-drones-mideast-138314659)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — The Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted five drones and one ballistic missile targeting the kingdom on Friday morning, including Riyadh, and Saudi Civil Defense issued 13 emergency alerts since Thursday. The Houthis did not immediately claim the Friday attacks but had said a day earlier that they targeted Saudi airports, air bases and Riyadh's financial center. The Associated Press roundup also reported that Iraqi security forces deployed outside Jurf al-Sakhar south of Baghdad after a deal between the government and Kataib Hezbollah facilitated by Iran's Quds Force commander Esmail Qaani.
