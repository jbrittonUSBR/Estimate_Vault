# What help can Egypt offer Saudi Arabia in the war against the Houthis?

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/18/what-help-can-egypt-offer-saudi-arabia-in-the-war-against-the-houthis)

(U) Cairo, Egypt — Al Jazeera analyzed what military assistance Egypt might provide Saudi Arabia after President Abdel Fattah el-Sisi sought parliamentary authorization for possible defensive support amid Houthi attacks. The article described Egypt's prior naval and air contributions to Red Sea maritime security and cited analysts who said future aid could include air and missile defense, counter-drone measures, naval escort, and intelligence sharing. These were expert assessments of possible assistance, not confirmation that Egypt had deployed forces.
