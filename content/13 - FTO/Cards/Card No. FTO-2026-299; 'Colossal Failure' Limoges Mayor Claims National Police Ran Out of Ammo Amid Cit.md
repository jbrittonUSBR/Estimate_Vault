# 'Colossal Failure': Limoges Mayor Claims National Police Ran Out of Ammo Amid City Hall Arson Attack

[Source](https://www.breitbart.com/europe/2026/10/20/limoges-mayor-claims-national-police-ran-out-of-ammo-amid-city-hall-arson-attack/)

(U) October 20, 2026 — Limoges Mayor Guillaume Guérin said police had run out of ammunition during violent unrest in the city, which culminated in an arson attack that severely damaged the historic city hall. Breitbart reported Guérin's criticism of national police preparedness; the ammunition shortage and his characterization of the response as a “colossal failure” were claims attributed to the mayor. Authorities arrested suspects in connection with the fire.
