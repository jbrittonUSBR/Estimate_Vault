# Houthi missile attack on Yemeni presidential palace kills five, minister says

[Source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm62y6lp73zdo)

(U) ADEN, YEMEN — BBC News reported that five people were killed and 16 injured in a Houthi missile attack targeting al-Maashiq presidential palace in Aden, citing Yemeni officials. Information Minister Moammar al-Eryani told the BBC that the prime minister and other ministers were unharmed. Houthi spokesman Yahya Sarea said the group had targeted a Saudi military operations headquarters in Aden.
