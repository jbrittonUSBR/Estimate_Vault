# Melbourne man facing charges linked to extremist group chat, AFP say

[Source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-13/melbourne-man-charged-violent-extremist-material-airport-arrest/107148034)

(U) MELBOURNE, Australia — Australian Federal Police charged a 30-year-old Roxburgh Park man, Mohammad Ahmadzai, with possessing violent extremist material after he was arrested at Melbourne Airport. A Melbourne court heard that a seized phone allegedly contained videos depicting shootings, mass casualties, beheadings and throat cuttings, and that he was allegedly part of a chat group sharing such material. He was remanded in custody, with a bail application expected later in the week.
