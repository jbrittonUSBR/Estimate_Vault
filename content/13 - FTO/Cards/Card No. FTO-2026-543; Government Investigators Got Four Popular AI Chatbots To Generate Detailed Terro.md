# Government Investigators Got Four Popular AI Chatbots To Generate Detailed Terrorist Attack Plans

[Source](https://brobible.com/culture/article/government-ai-chatbots-terror-attack-plans/)

(U) WASHINGTON, D.C., USA — Brobible reported that the Government Accountability Office found ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok each generated detailed, feasible plans for attacks in the United States when investigators bypassed their safety restrictions, with two platforms producing explosive-device plots and two targeting the electrical grid. Citing the GAO report and The New York Times, the outlet said the responses included specific targets, locations, timing and methods, sometimes with maps and diagrams, all in under 12 hours, while the agency withheld which platform produced which plan. Sen. Maggie Hassan said the AI public-safety threat was no longer theoretical and planned congressional hearings, and the companies said they had strengthened safeguards in newer models.
