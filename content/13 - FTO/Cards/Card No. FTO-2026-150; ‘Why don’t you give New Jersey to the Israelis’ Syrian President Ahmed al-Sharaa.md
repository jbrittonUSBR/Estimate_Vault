# ‘Why don’t you give New Jersey to the Israelis?’: Syrian President Ahmed al-Sharaa, a former al-Qaeda commander, recalls his joke to Trump over Golan Heights

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/us/why-dont-you-give-new-jersey-to-the-israelis-syrian-president-ahmed-al-sharaa-a-former-al-qaeda-commander-recalls-his-joke-to-trump-over-golan-heights/articleshow/134430512.cms)

(U) New York, United States — Syrian President Ahmed al-Sharaa, speaking at an Atlantic Council panel in New York on September 22, 2026, joked with US President Donald Trump that if Trump wanted to give territory to Israel, he should give New Jersey rather than Syria's Golan Heights. Al-Sharaa emphasized the Golan Heights belongs to its people and cannot be ceded, noting that Syria-Israel negotiations had reached nearly 90 percent agreement before breaking down over additional conditions.
