# Egypt approves military support for Saudi Arabia after Houthi attacks escalate

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/world-news/egypt-approves-military-support-for-saudi-arabia-after-houthi-attacks-escalate/articleshow/135213170.cms)

(U) Cairo, Egypt — Reuters reported that Egypt’s parliament authorized President Abdel Fattah el-Sisi to provide military support to Saudi Arabia if requested, amid escalating Houthi attacks. Egyptian officials described the authorization as defensive and limited to protecting Saudi territory; the specific support to be provided had not been announced.
