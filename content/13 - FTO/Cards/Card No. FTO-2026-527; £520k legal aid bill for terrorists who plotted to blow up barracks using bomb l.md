# £520k legal aid bill for terrorists who plotted to blow up barracks using bomb launched from toy car as High Court to rule on ringleader's parole bid

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16313135/520k-legal-aid-bill-terrorists-plotted-blow-barracks-using-bomb-launched-toy-car-High-Court-rule-ringleaders-parole-bid.html)

(U) Luton, United Kingdom — The Daily Mail reported, based on a Freedom of Information request, that four men convicted in 2013 for preparing an attack on a Territorial Army barracks in Luton received £521,611.03 in state-funded legal aid. The article said Zahid Iqbal, who was recorded discussing a remote-controlled toy car to push explosives under the barracks gate, was recalled to prison in 2023 after his release and would remain in custody pending a High Court review of a Parole Board decision. The Ministry of Justice said ministers referred the case to the High Court and that offenders did not receive legal-aid funds directly.
