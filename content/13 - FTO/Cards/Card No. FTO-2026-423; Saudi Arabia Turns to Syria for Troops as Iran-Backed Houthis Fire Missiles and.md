# Saudi Arabia Turns to Syria for Troops as Iran-Backed Houthis Fire Missiles and Drones at Riyadh

[Source](https://www.thegatewaypundit.com/2026/11/saudi-arabia-turns-syria-troops-as-iran-backed/)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — The Gateway Pundit reports that the Houthis claimed missile and drone attacks on Riyadh on November 8; Saudi Arabia said falling shrapnel caused minor damage to an Aramco administrative building and reported no casualties. The outlet says Saudi air defenses intercepted two ballistic missiles and coalition forces destroyed two launchers, while the Houthis also claimed attacks on oil facilities, the airport, and troops. It further reports conflicting accounts about a possible Syrian deployment: unnamed sources described preparations, while Syria’s foreign minister denied plans to send troops to Yemen.
