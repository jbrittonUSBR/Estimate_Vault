# Justice Department announces actions against alleged North Korean remote IT-worker schemes

[Source](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-announces-coordinated-actions-combat-north-korean-remote-it-worker)

(U) UNITED STATES — The Justice Department announced coordinated law-enforcement actions targeting what it described as North Korean remote IT-worker schemes affecting more than 70 U.S. companies. The department said the actions included an indictment, two arrests, searches of suspected “laptop farms” in Texas and seizure of about 187 computer devices. DOJ alleged the schemes generated revenue for the North Korean government and exposed employers to data-security and identity-theft risks; these characterizations came from the department's press release.
