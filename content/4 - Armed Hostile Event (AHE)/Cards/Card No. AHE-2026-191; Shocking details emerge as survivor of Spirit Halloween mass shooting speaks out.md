# Shocking details emerge as survivor of Spirit Halloween mass shooting speaks out

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/11/20/us-news/fairfield-spirit-halloween-shooting-survivor-speaks-out/)

(U) Fairfield, California — Anthony Barlow said a .45-caliber bullet shattered his elbow during the Oct. 30 shooting at a Spirit Halloween store and that he later learned his young son had also been struck, in the stomach. The shooting killed 31-year-old Constance Hages, who was 13 weeks pregnant, and wounded six others; 17-year-old Monte Smith, arrested Nov. 2, pleaded not guilty to two counts of murder and other charges, and prosecutors have asked that he be tried as an adult.
