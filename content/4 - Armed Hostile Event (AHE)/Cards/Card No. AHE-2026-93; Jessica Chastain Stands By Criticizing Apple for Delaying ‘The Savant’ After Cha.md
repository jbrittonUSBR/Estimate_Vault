# Jessica Chastain Stands By Criticizing Apple for Delaying ‘The Savant’ After Charlie Kirk’s Assassination: ‘I Had to Go on Record That I Thought It Was a Mistake’

[Source](https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/jessica-chastain-the-savant-delayed-release-apple-mistake-1236903631/)

(U) Los Angeles, United States — Actress Jessica Chastain publicly reiterated her criticism of Apple TV for postponing the release of her domestic-terrorism thriller *The Savant* following the assassination of Charlie Kirk, telling Net-a-Porter she felt it was a mistake and that she had a responsibility to go on record about it; Apple has since rescheduled the series for Spring 2027.
