# B.C. government to sue OpenAI after Tumbler Ridge mass shooting

[Source](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/british-columbia/bc-government-announce-update-openai-legal-action-9.7352395)

(U) VANCOUVER — British Columbia is suing OpenAI in California over the February 10 Tumbler Ridge mass shooting, Attorney General Niki Sharma announced Monday, alleging the company failed to notify law enforcement about alarming ChatGPT interactions with the shooter. The claim, filed jointly with the Peace River South School District and naming CEO Sam Altman, seeks to recover costs including rebuilding the demolished school and asks for injunctive relief; six children and two adults were killed. Sharma said OpenAI refused to disclose the chats, and the province pointed to Altman's April apology acknowledging the company did not alert police to an account banned in June.
