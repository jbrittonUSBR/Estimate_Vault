# FBI investigation thwarts "mass casualty attack" from Pennsylvania man accused of pledging ISIS allegiance

[Source](https://freerepublic.com/focus/f-news/4395723/posts)

(U) PITTSBURGH, United States — In a CBS News report republished by Free Republic, federal law enforcement officials said a Pennsylvania man planned an ISIS-inspired "mass casualty attack" before an FBI investigation thwarted him. Officials said Jonathan Kramer, 21, of Valencia, had pledged allegiance to the Islamic State and was arrested on Saturday outside a hotel in Cranberry Township, about 25 miles north of Pittsburgh, with a rifle, ammunition and dozens of knives in his possession. Richard Evanchec, special agent in charge of the FBI's Pittsburgh field office, described the suspect as having shared a "radical ideology."
