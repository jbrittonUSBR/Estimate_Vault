# Ukraine's Starlink access restricted without warning, Zelensky adviser says

[Source](https://kyivindependent.com/this-cannot-be-a-coincidence-ukraines-starlink-access-restricted-without-warning-zelensky-adviser-says/)

(U) October 14, 2026 — The Kyiv Independent reported that SpaceX imposed a 100-mph speed restriction on Starlink systems used by Ukraine’s military without advance notice. Mykhailo Podolyak, an adviser to President Volodymyr Zelensky, called the timing “not a coincidence,” framing the restriction as a political concern; the article reported his characterization and did not establish a motive for SpaceX’s action.
