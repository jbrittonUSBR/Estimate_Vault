# Chilling 911 audio shows dad pleading for help before rushing into daughters' school with gun to stop active shooter

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16267021/Kaylub-Giamanco-911-call-Silver-Rail.html)

(U) Bend, Oregon, USA - The Daily Mail reported on released 911 audio from the October 15 shooting at Silver Rail Elementary School, in which father Kaylub Giamanco called for help after seeing an armed teenager enter the school where his daughters were present. The outlet said Giamanco then entered the school and shot the suspect, who had already killed three children and a teacher; its account is attributed to the article and law-enforcement reporting.
