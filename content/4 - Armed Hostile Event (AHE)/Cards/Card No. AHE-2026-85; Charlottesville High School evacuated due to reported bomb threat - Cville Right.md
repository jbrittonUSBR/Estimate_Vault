# Charlottesville High School evacuated due to reported bomb threat

[Source](https://cvillerightnow.com/news/208802-charlottesville-high-school-evacuated-due-to-reported-bomb-threat/)

(U) CHARLOTTESVILLE, United States — Charlottesville High School was evacuated around noon on October 2, 2026 after police received a bomb threat against the school at 11:38 a.m., with about 50 staff members and others in the building moved to the nearby football stadium while a dozen police vehicles and bomb-detecting K-9 units responded. Chief of Police Michael Kochis told Cville Right Now the incident was being investigated as a potential hoax or "swatting" case, and a preliminary sweep by 12:55 p.m. found no danger; no students were in class because the day was designated for family-teacher conferences.
