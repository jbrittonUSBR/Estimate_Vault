(U) A 45-year-old man was arrested after fatally shooting six employees at a youth welfare facility in Stade, northern Germany, on June 29, 2026, in what police believe was a family-related attack tied to a custody matter. The victims—four women and two men, all adult staff members—were killed during the incident, with five dying at the scene and one later in hospital, while several others were injured. The suspect, a Turkish citizen born in Germany, had an appointment at the facility regarding custody of his three-month-old daughter, who was present along with her 34-year-old mother; the child was placed in the custody of the youth welfare office, and the mother remains subject to police measures. A 65-year-old woman with close family ties, who drove a getaway vehicle, was also taken into custody. Authorities confirmed the area has been cleared with no ongoing threat to the public and warned against spreading unverified information online, noting that mass shootings are rare in Germany due to strict gun ownership laws.
