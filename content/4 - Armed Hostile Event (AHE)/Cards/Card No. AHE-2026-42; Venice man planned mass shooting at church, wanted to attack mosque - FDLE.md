(U) The Florida Department of Law Enforcement (FDLE) arrested a 19-year-old Venice resident who allegedly planned and prepared to carry out mass shootings and bomb attacks targeting local houses of worship.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Suspect:** 19-year-old Giovanni Pimienta was taken into custody and booked into the Sarasota County Jail.
- **The Targets and Plot:** According to investigators, Pimienta discussed plans to conduct a mass shooting at a mosque and carry out an attack on a local church. Authorities stated that he visited the targeted church ahead of time, took photos, and interviewed people there about their beliefs. His plans reportedly involved taking hostages and using a firearm recovered from his home.
- **Charges:** Pimienta faces multiple felony counts, including writing threats to kill and threatening to conduct a bomb attack or mass shooting.
