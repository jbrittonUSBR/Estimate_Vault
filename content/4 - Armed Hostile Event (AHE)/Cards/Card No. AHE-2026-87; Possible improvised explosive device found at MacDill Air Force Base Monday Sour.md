# Possible improvised explosive device found at MacDill Air Force Base Monday: Sources

[Source](https://abcnews.com/US/improvised-explosive-device-found-macdill-air-force-base/story?id=131197338)

(U) TAMPA, United States — A suspicious package found outside the MacDill Air Force Base visitor centre on March 16, 2026 contained what appeared to be an improvised explosive device, multiple sources told ABC News, though it was not immediately known whether the device would have worked. The FBI's Tampa office said field screening identified "possible energetic materials" and that final laboratory analysis was not complete; the base raised its force protection level to FPCON Charlie the following night and ordered a shelter-in-place on March 18 after "a threat was made," lifting it a couple of hours later.
