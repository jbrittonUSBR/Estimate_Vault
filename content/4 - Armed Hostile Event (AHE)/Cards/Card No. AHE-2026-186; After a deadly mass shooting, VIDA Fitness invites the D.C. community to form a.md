# After a deadly mass shooting, VIDA Fitness invites the D.C. community to form a human chain of support

[Source](https://www.advocate.com/news/vida-fitness-community-gathering)

(U) Washington, D.C. — VIDA Fitness scheduled a public gathering to honor James Stuart, who was killed at the Logan Circle gym, and support people affected by the shooting. The Advocate reported that organizers planned a community procession between two gym locations and that regular operations were expected to resume after safety clearance.
