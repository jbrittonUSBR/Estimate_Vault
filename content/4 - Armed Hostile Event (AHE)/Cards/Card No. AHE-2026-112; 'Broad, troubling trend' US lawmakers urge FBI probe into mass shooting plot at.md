# 'Broad, troubling trend': US lawmakers urge FBI probe into mass shooting plot at Illinois Hindu temple

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/us/broad-troubling-trend-us-lawmakers-urge-fbi-probe-into-mass-shooting-plot-at-illinois-hindu-temple/articleshow/135006991.cms)

(U) Aurora, Illinois — The Times of India reported that U.S. lawmakers urged the FBI to investigate an alleged planned mass shooting targeting the Sri Venkateswara Swami (Balaji) Hindu temple as a possible hate crime. The report said the suspect was arrested on October 11 after a colleague alerted authorities to suspicious electronic communications, and that investigators recovered an alleged attack-plan document, firearms, ammunition, tactical equipment, and a manifesto. The allegations remain under investigation, and the lawmakers' characterizations of a broader trend are attributed to them.
