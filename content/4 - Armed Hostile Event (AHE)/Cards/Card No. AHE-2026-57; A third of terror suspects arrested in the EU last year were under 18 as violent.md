# A third of terror suspects arrested in the EU last year were under 18 as violent extremist networks grow online

[Source](https://fortune.com/2026/09/08/eu-terror-suspects-under-18-online-extremism/)

(U) BRUSSELS, Belgium — EU authorities warned that violent extremist networks are growing online and increasingly involve young people, reporting 45 terrorist attacks across 10 EU countries in the previous year that killed six people and included no mass-casualty plots. Police arrested 486 people across 21 countries, about one third of them under 18. Europol and EU Counter-Terrorism Coordinator Bartjan Wegter cited 'nihilistic violent extremism' and groups such as 'The COM' and '764,' and warned that artificial intelligence is amplifying propaganda and recruitment.
