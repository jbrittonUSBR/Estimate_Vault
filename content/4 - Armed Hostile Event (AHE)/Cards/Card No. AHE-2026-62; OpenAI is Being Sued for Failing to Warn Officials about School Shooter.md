# OpenAI is Being Sued for Failing to Warn Officials about School Shooter

[Source](https://dianeravitch.net/2026/09/09/openai-is-being-sued-for-failing-to-warn-about-school-shooter/)

(U) TUMBLER RIDGE, Canada — OpenAI faced 30 new lawsuits from victims of a school shooting in Tumbler Ridge, British Columbia, who accused the company of providing "substantial assistance and encouragement" to the shooter and failing to alert police to her ChatGPT interactions, according to the education blog Diane Ravitch's Blog. The blog cited Futurism in reporting that the shooter, 18-year-old Jesse Van Rootselaar, killed her mother and stepbrother before attacking a school in February, killing five students and a teacher and wounding 27 others. The account added that OpenAI debated whether to alert local officials and decided against it, deactivating her account, after which she obtained a new one under a different name. The summary reflects an opinion blog relaying another outlet's reporting.
