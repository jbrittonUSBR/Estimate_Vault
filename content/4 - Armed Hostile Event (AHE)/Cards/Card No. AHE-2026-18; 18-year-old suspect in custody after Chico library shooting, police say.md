(U) An 18-year-old Chico resident, Bradley Scott Sayer, was taken into custody after fatally shooting two adults and injuring a child at the Butte County Library’s Sherman Avenue branch in Chico, California, on June 22, 2026. Sayer, who reportedly expressed a desire to commit a “Columbine High School massacre type of shooting” and was obsessed with mass shootings, acted alone with no prior connection to the victims or the library. The incident was reported at 5:12 p.m., and Chico police officers responded quickly, hearing gunshots and screams over an open 911 line before apprehending Sayer as he exited the building. The victims included 46-year-old Jacob Hull and a 74-year-old man whose next of kin was still being located, while the injured child was taken to Enloe Hospital with non-life-threatening wounds. The shooting prompted the closure of First Avenue, all Butte County Library branches the following day, and a family reunification center at a local church, with the investigation involving federal, state, and local partners.
