# Minneapolis man charged over alleged threats to bomb Mall of America and schools

[Source](https://alphanews.org/another-suspect-charged-after-threatening-to-bomb-mall-of-america-minneapolis-schools/)

(U) October 20, 2026 — Alpha News, citing the criminal complaint, reported that Niman Ali Ahmed, 24, was charged with felony threats of violence after allegedly telling a 911 operator he planned to bomb the Mall of America and Minneapolis schools. Police reportedly found no explosives during a search; Ahmed remained in custody, and the outlet reported that the court ordered a competency evaluation. The allegations had not been adjudicated.
