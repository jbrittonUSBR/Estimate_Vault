# Eight dead, one injured in Turkey mass shooting

[Source](https://punchng.com/eight-dead-one-injured-in-turkey-mass-shooting/)

(U) Afyonkarahisar Province, Turkey — Local officials said eight people were killed and one was seriously injured in shootings across three villages in west-central Turkey. The Punch, citing Anadolu, reported that police found the suspect's red minivan near Eskisehir after he fled on foot, prompting a major manhunt.
