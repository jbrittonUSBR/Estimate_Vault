# Two killed, dozens hospitalized in mass shooting at homecoming party in Georgia

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/10/04/us-news/two-killed-dozens-hospitalized-in-mass-shooting-at-homecoming-party-in-georgia/)

(U) VIENNA, Georgia — A mass shooting during a homecoming block party in Dooly County left two people dead and dozens injured. The attack occurred around 1:25 a.m. as over 1,000 people gathered on Pine Street, with victims ranging in age from 14 to 51 transported to regional hospitals with injuries ranging from stable to critical.
