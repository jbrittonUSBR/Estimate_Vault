# Dozens Fall Ill On Two Condor Flights To Frankfurt, Triggering Mass Casualty Response

[Source](https://onemileatatime.com/news/dozens-fall-ill-condor-flights-frankfurt-mass-casualty-response/)

(U) Frankfurt, Germany — Dozens of passengers on two Condor Airbus A330-900neo flights from La Romana, Dominican Republic, fell ill over the weekend, prompting a "MANV 25" mass-casualty response at Frankfurt Airport in which one person from each flight was taken to a hospital, according to the travel blog One Mile at a Time, citing aeroTELEGRAPH. The blog reported the roughly 620 passengers had all disembarked from the same AidaBella cruise ship and said a norovirus outbreak was suspected as the cause, though the cause remained under investigation.
