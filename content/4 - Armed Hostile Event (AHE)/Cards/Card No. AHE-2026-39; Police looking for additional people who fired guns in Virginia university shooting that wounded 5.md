(U) A late-night shooting on the campus of Virginia State University (VSU) left five people wounded just as students were returning for the start of the academic year, triggering a temporary campus lockdown and an intensive police manhunt.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Incident:** The shooting occurred around 1:30 a.m. outside a campus residence hall (near the Quad Annexes). When campus police and Chesterfield County officers arrived, they discovered five victims—ranging in age from 17 to 23—suffering from gunshot wounds.
- **The Victims:** Four of the injured individuals were from surrounding communities, while one victim was confirmed to be a VSU student. One victim was initially listed in critical condition, while the others sustained non-life-threatening injuries.
- **The Suspect:** Authorities arrested 19-year-old Camron Harris of Henrico, Virginia, who is not a student at the university. Following a search, Harris was discovered hiding inside a campus dormitory closet and was taken into custody without bond. He faces multiple felony charges, including malicious wounding and using a firearm in the commission of a felony.
- **Ongoing Investigation:** Chesterfield County police stated that investigators are actively looking into whether other individuals who fired weapons during the altercation were aggressors or acting in self-defense.
