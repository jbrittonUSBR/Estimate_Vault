# Teen accused of plotting mass shooting at Chicago-area Hindu temple

[Source](https://abcnews.com/US/wireStory/teen-accused-plotting-mass-shooting-chicago-area-hindu-137275418)

(U) PALATINE, Illinois — The Associated Press reported that police arrested 19-year-old Alexandros Paporakis on charges of making a terrorist threat and unlawfully possessing weapons, after coworkers shared concerning digital messages and an alleged attack plan. Police said they believed he may have planned to target a Hindu place of worship, possibly the largest temple in nearby Aurora, though the intended location remained under investigation. Prosecutors alleged the plan included an eight-page manifesto and tactical-equipment photographs; authorities said a home search recovered a rifle, ammunition and other items. Paporakis was ordered held pending trial, and the allegations remained unproven.
