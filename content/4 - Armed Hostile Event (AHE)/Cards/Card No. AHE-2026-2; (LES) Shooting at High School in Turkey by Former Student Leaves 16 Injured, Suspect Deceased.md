(U) On April 14, 2026, Omer Ket, 19, equipped with a pump action shotgun, opened fire at a high school in south eastern Turkey, injuring 16, including 10 students, four teachers, one staff member, and one officer, before fatally succumbing to a self-inflicted gunshot would after being cornered by responding officers. The school, Ahmet Koyuncu Vocational and Technical Anatolian High School, is located in the Sanliurfa Province, approximately 590 miles from Istanbul. According to open sources, the suspect is a former student who had been expelled but continued his education through a distance learning program. 
(U//LES) Online forums circulating unverified screenshots of the suspected shooter's social media posts indicate the attack may have been inspired by the Columbine High School shooting in association with the more recently established True Crime Community (TCC).
