(U) Muscatine, Iowa (KWQC) - Police say Ryan McFarland was responsible for killing six people between three locations on Monday. TV6 Investigates dug into his criminal charges and found several notable crimes. Iowa court records show McFarland was charged in 2011 in connection with the death of a child. He pleaded guilty to child endangerment, an aggravated misdemeanor, in 2012. Information on the situation surrounding the child’s death isn’t available online due to the age of the case. McFarland got hit with several charges for fraudulent business practices in 2016. The original complaint starts with McFarland lying about how many miles were on a car he was selling by tampering with the odometer but ended with payouts to more than ten people and a guilty plea to two felonies of fraudulent practice which tag him as a habitual offender. He had to pay more than $58,000 in restitution. Court records list 210 Park Avenue as McFarland’s home at one point in time. That is the same address where police responded to on Monday where they found four shooting victims. TV6 Investigates does not know who owned the home at the time of Monday’s shooting.
