# Ex-Marine Nigel Edge facing death penalty for killing three in North Carolina mass shooting: prosecutors

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/10/07/us-news/ex-marine-facing-death-penalty-for-killing-three-in-north-carolina-mass-shooting/)

(U) SOUTHPORT, United States — A Brunswick County prosecutor has announced he will seek the death penalty against former Marine Nigel Max Edge, who is charged with opening fire from a small boat at the American Fish Company dockside bar on September 27, 2025, killing three people and wounding several others. Edge faces three counts of first-degree murder and multiple attempted murder and weapons charges; the court is also considering questions about his mental capacity after a prior traumatic brain injury.
