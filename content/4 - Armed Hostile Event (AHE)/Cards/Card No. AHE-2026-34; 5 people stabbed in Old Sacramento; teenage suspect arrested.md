(U) - **Incident Overview:** Early Saturday morning (around 3:00 a.m.), a violent altercation broke out near the intersection of K and Front Streets in Old Sacramento.
**Victims:** Five men suffered stab wounds during the incident. Three victims were found at the scene by responding police officers, while two additional victims independently arrived at local hospitals. All five victims survived and were listed in stable condition.
**Arrests:** Police detained three individuals as persons of interest following the attack. An 18-year-old man, Ezekiel Griffis, was arrested and booked into the Sacramento County Jail on assault charges. A second suspect was subsequently taken into custody.
**Investigation:** Authorities launched an investigation—reviewing surveillance footage and gathering evidence—to determine what triggered the early-morning altercation.
