A Shasta County man who had reportedly been [active in local right-wing politics](https://www.sfgate.com/politics/article/controversial-california-proposal-returns-prop-50-21146593.php) was arrested last week with a bunker and large cache of illegal weapons, according to authorities. California Highway Patrol officers arrested Michael Jay Kamfolt, 40, on 11 weapons charges on Thursday, including possession of a stolen firearm and armor-penetrating ammunition, [according to a press release](https://www.chp.ca.gov/news-alerts/news-list/illicit-cannabis-cultivation-operation-leads-to-discovery-of-underground-bunker-and-large-cache-of-illegal-firearms/). Authorities said they found an underground bunker accessed through a 100-foot-long tunnel and approximately 10,000 rounds of ammunition. The operation also allegedly found supplies for illicit cannabis operation, but CHP Northern Division Chief John Pinoli said in a press release that the weapons were a larger concern. Shasta County has become a hotbed of conservative activism tied to President Donald Trump’s Make American Great Again movement, with resistance to COVID-19 restrictions and swirling election conspiracy theories that have led to chaotic local meetings. Kamfolt was reportedly involved in MAGA activism, including monitoring local elections staff with Supervisor Kevin Crye, according to [previous reporting by the Shasta Scout](https://shastascout.org/as-tensions-mount-in-shasta-county/).
