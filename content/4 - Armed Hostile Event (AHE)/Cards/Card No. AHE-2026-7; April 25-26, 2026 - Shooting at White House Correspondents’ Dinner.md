(U) **Suspect’s note:** Investigators are [combing through a message](https://www.cnn.com/2026/04/26/us/white-house-correspondents-dinner-shooter-teacher-invs) sent by the man accused of [charging through a security checkpoint](https://www.cnn.com/2026/04/26/politics/white-house-correspondents-dinner-security-protocol) and opening fire at a press gala in Washington, DC. The suspect “clearly stated” he [wanted to target administration officials](https://cnn.com/2026/04/26/us/white-house-correspondents-dinner-shooter-teacher-invs), the White House said.

(U) **How events unfolded:** President Donald Trump and top officials were rushed to safety after [gunfire was heard](https://www.cnn.com/2026/04/26/politics/video/shooting-oz-pearlman-secret-service-trump-digvid) outside the ballroom at the Washington Hilton. Authorities believe the [suspect fired one or two times](https://www.cnn.com/2026/04/25/politics/live-news/trump-white-house-correspondents-dinner?post-id=cmogacepe00083b6sfkmon4mv), according to a law enforcement source. Secret Service agents shot at the suspect but [did not hit him](https://www.cnn.com/2026/04/25/politics/live-news/trump-white-house-correspondents-dinner#cmogacepe00083b6sfkmon4mv) before he was taken into custody [near a staircase](https://www.cnn.com/2026/04/26/politics/map-white-house-correspondents-dinner-trump-vis) leading to the ballroom, the source said.

(U)  **Arraignment tomorrow:** The suspect has been identified as Cole Tomas Allen, a 31-year-old teacher from California, sources said. He’s expected to [appear in court Monday](https://www.cnn.com/2026/04/25/politics/live-news/trump-white-house-correspondents-dinner?post-id=cmog1xvqt00003b6ssceg5v57), according to the US attorney for DC.
