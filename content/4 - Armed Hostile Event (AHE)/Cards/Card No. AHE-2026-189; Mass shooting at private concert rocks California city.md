# Mass shooting at private concert rocks California city

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/11/22/us-news/9-wounded-in-shooting-at-private-concert-in-central-california/)

(U) Strathmore, California — Nine people were wounded Saturday night when gunfire erupted at a private concert attended by more than 100 people at a home in this Central California community, the Tulare County Sheriff's Office said. Two victims were listed in critical condition and one was airlifted to a hospital; investigators said the incident appeared gang-related, and no arrests had been announced.
