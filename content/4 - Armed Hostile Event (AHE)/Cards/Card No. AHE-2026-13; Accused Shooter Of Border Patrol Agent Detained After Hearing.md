(U) Accused Shooter Of Border Patrol Agent Detained After Hearing: A still image from video surveillance at the port of entry in Pittsburg was included in a court motion filed Wednesday by federal prosecutors, who seek the continued detention of a Manchester resident accused of firing a handgun at a Border Patrol agent at the U.S.-Canadian border at about the time the image was captured on Feb. 22.After pleading not guilty to charges of firing a gun at a U.S. Border Patrol agent at the U.S.-Canada border in Pittsburg in February, the accused shooter continues to be detained without bail, following a detention hearing at federal court on Monday. In May, Blu Zeke Daly, 26, of Manchester, and also known as Cullan Zeke Daly, was indicted by a grand jury on one count of attempted murder of a federal officer and one count of assaulting a federal officer with a dangerous weapon. A preliminary hearing scheduled for Thursday at federal court has been canceled as the suspect accused of firing at a Border Patrol agent at the U.S.-Canada border in Pittsburg in February continues to be detained and a grand jury delivered indictments on Thursday.
