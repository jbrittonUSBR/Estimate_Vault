# Third arrest made in Crown Heights hookah lounge mass shooting, NYPD says

[Source](https://gothamist.com/news/third-arrest-made-in-crown-heights-hookah-lounge-mass-shooting-nypd-says)

(U) BROOKLYN, United States — The New York Police Department arrested a third person, 34-year-old Brooklyn resident Haile Cummings, in connection with a mass shooting at a hookah lounge in Crown Heights in August 2025, according to Gothamist. Cummings faced a murder charge in the attack at Taste of the City Lounge on Franklin Avenue, in which three people were killed and 11 others injured. Police had previously arrested Timothy Spence, 21, and Elijah Roy, 25, and Spence pleaded guilty in federal court to assault in aid of racketeering and discharging a firearm during a violent crime; federal prosecutors said the shooting grew out of a dispute between rival gangs.
