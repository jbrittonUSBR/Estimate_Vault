# (VIDEO) 3 Dead, Including Shooter, As Gunfire Wounds Officers In Downtown Minneapolis Apartment Building

[Source](https://www.ibtimes.com.au/tragic-shooting-downtown-minneapolis-three-dead-1874906)

(U) MINNEAPOLIS — Three people, including the suspected shooter, died and three police officers were wounded in a shooting at a downtown Minneapolis apartment building on September 2, 2026, according to the International Business Times. The shooting occurred just after 4:30 p.m. at Loring Towers on East Grant Street; officers responded to a 911 call at 4:36 p.m. and arrived three minutes later. Two civilians and the shooter were killed, and three officers were injured — one shot in the stomach and one in the leg, both reported stable. Authorities had not released a motive, and it was unclear how the shooter died.
