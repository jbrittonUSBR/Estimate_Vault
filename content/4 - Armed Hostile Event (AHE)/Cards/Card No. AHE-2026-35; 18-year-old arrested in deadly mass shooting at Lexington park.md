(U) An 18-year-old man was arrested and charged in connection with a fatal mass shooting at a public park in Lexington, Kentucky, during a community celebration.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Incident:** The shooting occurred shortly before 7 p.m. at Charles Young Park during the annual East End Day community event.
- **Victims:** A 16-year-old boy, Di'Zhon Robertson, was killed. Four other individuals—including two children (ages 4 and 14) and two adults—were wounded with non-life-threatening injuries.
- **The Suspect & Charges:** 18-year-old Jametrius M. Griffin was taken into custody and charged with murder, multiple counts of first- and second-degree assault, and tampering with physical evidence. He entered a plea of not guilty, and a judge set his bond at $1 million.
- **Community Impact:** Local advocacy and violence-prevention groups, such as One Lexington, mobilized support services and grief counseling for families and students affected by the tragedy.
