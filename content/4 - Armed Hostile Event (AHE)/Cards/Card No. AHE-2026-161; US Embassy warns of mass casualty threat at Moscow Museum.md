# US Embassy warns of mass casualty threat at Moscow Museum

[Source](http://nationalpost.com/news/u-s-embassy-warns-of-mass-casualty-threat-at-moscow-museum/)

(U) Moscow, Russia - The National Post reported that the U.S. Embassy warned of a credible mass-casualty threat at the Tsaritsyno Museum Reserve and advised U.S. citizens to avoid the location, stay alert and avoid crowds. The alert did not identify a suspect or describe an attack; the warning was attributed to the embassy.
