# Inside Thailand's school shooter drills following recent attacks

(U) BANGKOK, Thailand — Following a series of deadly attacks, Thai schools have begun conducting active shooter drills to prepare students and staff for armed intruder scenarios. The drills reflect growing concern over school violence in the country and a push to institutionalize emergency response procedures, mirroring practices adopted in other nations facing similar threats.
