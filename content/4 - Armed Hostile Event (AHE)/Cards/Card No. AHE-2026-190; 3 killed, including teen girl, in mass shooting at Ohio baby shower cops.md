# 3 killed, including teen girl, in mass shooting at Ohio baby shower: cops

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/11/21/us-news/multiple-killed-including-teen-girl-after-gunmen-open-fire-on-ohio-baby-shower/)

(U) Parma, Ohio — A 19-year-old expectant father and a 29-year-old man exchanged gunfire around 6 p.m. during a baby shower at Pandora's Magical Events Venue, leaving the father, the pregnant woman's 45-year-old father and a 17-year-old girl dead, the Parma Police Department said. A 39-year-old woman was shot in the leg and expected to survive; the 29-year-old man was taken into custody, and officials said witnesses reported he may have acted in self-defense.
