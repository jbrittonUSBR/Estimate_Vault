# Authorities investigated suspicious-substance letters sent to congressional, election offices

[Source](https://abcnews.com/Politics/wireStory/authorities-investigate-letters-suspicious-substances-congressional-election-offices-137290377)

(U) WASHINGTON — The Associated Press reported that federal authorities were investigating letters containing suspicious substances mailed to congressional and election offices in multiple locations. Authorities urged recipients to treat the mail as potentially hazardous, while election offices in Louisiana and Oklahoma had been evacuated after receiving powdery packages. The AP said the Senate had been briefed and the House was monitoring the matter; the substances and responsible parties had not been identified.
