# How dispute over hotel lease escalated into mass shooting at Guwahati's Bilas Inn, with leaseholder killing 4 linked to owner's family

(U) Guwahati, India — The Times of India reported that a hotel lease dispute preceded a shooting at Bilas Inn that killed five people and injured one. Police said leaseholder Ghanakanta Gogoi was arrested within an hour; the investigation was ongoing.
