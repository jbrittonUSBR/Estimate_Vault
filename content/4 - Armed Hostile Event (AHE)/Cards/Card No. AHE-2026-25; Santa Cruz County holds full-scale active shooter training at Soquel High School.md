(U) Santa Cruz County held its annual full-scale active shooter training exercise at Soquel High School on June 24, 2026, involving approximately 150 law enforcement personnel, 80 fire responders, and about 100 volunteers simulating a chaotic school shooting scenario with lifelike mock victims and realistic screams. Organized by the Santa Cruz County Sheriff’s Office and held for the sixth consecutive year as part of more than a decade of similar drills across the county, the training focused on coordinated response tactics, including treating realistic wounds and managing high-stress situations that responders described as among the most difficult calls. Ashley Keehn from the sheriff’s office stressed that such hands-on exercises are essential preparation that cannot be replicated otherwise. Volunteer mock victim Mace Crowbear, who has participated for six years and experienced a real school lockdown involving her son, noted the emotional intensity but said the training has reassured her about the preparedness and capability of local law enforcement and first responders.
