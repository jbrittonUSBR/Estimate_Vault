(U) The victims of a [mass shooting in Sandy, Ore.,](https://people.com/multiple-people-dead-after-traumatic-mass-shooting-in-oregon-11987659) have been named. According to [charging documents](https://www.clackamas.us/news/2026-06-01/bryan-andrew-moore-arraigned-on-murder-charges-attempted-murder-of-a-police-officer) from the Clackamas County Sheriff's Office, Bryan Moore, 38, was arraigned on June 1 on three counts of first-degree murder at Clackamas County Circuit Court. This was in relation to the deaths of Jenna Mary Overson, 37, Mary Beth Overson, 70, and Kobyn McClure, 16, and the attempted aggravated murder of Sandy Police Department Sergeant Garrett Thornton. Moore, who, per local news outlet Oregon Public Broadcasting ([OPB](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/06/01/sandy-oregon-shooting-kidnapping-victims/)), was married to Jenna Mary Overson, is also charged with felon in possession of a firearm and two counts of first-degree kidnapping in relation to his own 3-year-old child and one other person. Per the charging documents and j[ail records](https://www.clackcosheriff.us/adultsInCustody/cb86bddb3314aaa676c9ac8b51329165), Moore is being held without bail at the Clackamas County jail. His next court appearance is at 2 p.m. local time on June 8. According to witnesses, Moore's third victim, teenager McClure, just happened to be near the shooting, which began as a domestic violence incident, per OPB. A [GoFundMe](https://www.gofundme.com/f/help-lay-jenna-overson-and-mary-overson-to-rest-with-dignity?attribution_id=sl:b56216d3-16e1-4cb7-952c-d313fdc13e38&lang=en_US&ts=1780368885&utm_campaign=fp_sharesheet&utm_content=amp17_td-amp20_control&utm_medium=customer&utm_source=copy_link8BIM) set up by Becca Zaloznik identifies Jenna Mary Overson as her cousin and Mary Beth Overson as her aunt and calls the killings “senseless” and “cruel,” adding that it has “left our entire family reeling in grief.” The fundraiser notes that Jenna was a “devoted mother who was loved by all who knew her,” adding that Mary had a great sense of humor. The GoFundMe, which aims to raise $8,000, adds that funds raised will go toward funeral costs as well as expenses for Jenna's “two boys.”
