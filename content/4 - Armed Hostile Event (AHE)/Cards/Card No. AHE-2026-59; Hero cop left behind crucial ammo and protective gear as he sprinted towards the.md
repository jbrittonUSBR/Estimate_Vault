# Hero cop left behind crucial ammo and protective gear as he sprinted towards the Bondi Beach terror attack

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16119301/bondi-beach-massacre-police-officer-hero-barraza.html)

(U) SYDNEY, Australia — Detective Senior Constable Cesar Barraza, the police officer credited with stopping the Bondi Beach terror attack, said he raced to the scene on December 14 without a bulletproof vest or spare ammunition in his haste to stop the bloodshed, according to a new book, Bondi Terror. The book describes how Barraza took cover about 40 metres from two armed men and fatally shot gunman Sajid Akram, 50, before exchanging fire with accused gunman Naveed Akram, learning afterward he had only three bullets left in his magazine. The attack left 15 people dead and Naveed Akram, charged with 78 offences including 15 counts of murder, is due to return to court on October 28.
