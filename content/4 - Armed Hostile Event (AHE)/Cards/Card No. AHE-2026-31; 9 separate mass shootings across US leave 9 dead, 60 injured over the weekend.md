(U) **9 separate mass shootings across US leave 9 dead, 60 injured over the weekend** (ABC News, Bill Hutchinson and Megan Fahrney, July 20, 2026) At least nine mass shootings occurred over the weekend across eight states, leaving nine people dead and at least 60 wounded. The incidents took place in Colorado, Delaware, Maryland, North Carolina, Oklahoma, Arizona, Tennessee, and two in Minnesota.

**Key incidents:**

- **Denver, Colorado** (early Monday): One dead, eight wounded in the parking lot of Ultra Lounge nightclub around 1 a.m. Evidence suggested at least two weapons; possible link to an earlier confrontation between groups. No suspects identified.
- **Oklahoma City, Oklahoma** (early Sunday): Two dead, three injured during a party at Dos Cinco restaurant around 12:31 a.m. Altercation escalated into gunfire. No arrests announced.
- **Jackson, Tennessee** (Sunday night): Three dead, nine wounded at Savannah Williams Park around 11 p.m. (more than 100 people present). Sheriff described it as appearing gang-related; one shooting involved a rifle fired from a vehicle. No arrests.
- **Tucson, Arizona** (early Sunday): Nine critically injured around 2 a.m. in a busy downtown entertainment district. Officers on foot patrol confronted and shot the suspected gunman (hospitalized in life-threatening condition). Believed to stem from a conflict between groups; possible gang ties under investigation.
- **Baltimore, Maryland** (Sunday night): Six injured (ages 17–21, non-life-threatening) around 9:45 p.m. in the Mt. Winans neighborhood. Two gunmen exited a car and opened fire on a group under a tree. No arrests; motive under investigation.
- **Asheville, North Carolina** (early Sunday): Two dead, seven wounded around 2:20 a.m. in a parking lot near downtown after bars closed. Possibly related to a prior gun violence incident. No arrests.
- **Minneapolis, Minnesota** (two separate shootings on Sunday): Combined one dead and 14 injured. One around 8:20 p.m. (one dead, four wounded); earlier around 12:27 a.m. outside Reign Event Center (nine injured, ages 18–27) following an altercation. No connection found between the two; no arrests.
- **Newark, Delaware** (early Saturday): Six injured (including three teenagers; non-life-threatening) around 12:10 a.m. in a Waffle House parking lot when an unidentified person opened fire on a crowd. No arrests announced.

Most cases remained under active investigation with limited suspect identifications or arrests at the time of reporting.
