(U) A gunman identified as Seth Hatfield from Alberta killed a Jewish civilian and a Montreal police officer while seriously wounding another in the heavily Jewish Côte-des-Neiges neighborhood of Montreal on June 22, 2026, before being neutralized by police. The civilian victim, Michael Moshe Mizrahi, a 68-year-old Chabad member originally from Lebanon with family in Israel, was caught in the crossfire near the Hilton Hotel and kosher establishments; the fallen officer was 34-year-old Constable Mohamed Lamine Benredouane, with the second officer later reported in stable condition. Hatfield, believed linked to the incel movement, drove several hours to the scene and left behind a 104-page manifesto advocating violent revolution against modern capitalist society and targeting police, Jews, women, and pornography companies. The attack, which involved 20-30 shots near the site of the 1989 École Polytechnique massacre, prompted a shelter-in-place alert for Jewish institutions that was later lifted, with Montreal police confirming no other suspects and an ongoing investigation. Condolences came from Israeli officials, local leaders, and Jewish organizations, noting Mizrahi’s family’s prior experience with terror and the 24-year gap since the last Montreal police officer was killed in the line of duty.
