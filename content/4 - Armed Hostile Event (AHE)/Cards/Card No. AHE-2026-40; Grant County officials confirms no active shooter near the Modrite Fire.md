(U) Local officials clarified and resolved security concerns, confirming there was **no active shooter** near the active Modrite Fire zone.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Source of Alarm:** Panic and reports of potential gunfire near the fire lines prompted concerns for emergency personnel.
- **The Investigation:** According to [Colville Tribal Broadcasts, News and Information](https://kxly920.com/2026/08/08/grant-county-officials-confirm-no-active-shooter-near-the-modrite-fire/), authorities determined that the sounds of gunfire were the result of a hunter shooting at a bear in the vicinity—not at firefighters or firefighting operations.
- **Resolution:** Tribal law enforcement quickly addressed and resolved the situation. Officials confirmed that all fire personnel were safely accounted for, and active wildfire suppression efforts resumed without further delay.
