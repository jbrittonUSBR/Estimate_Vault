(U) A suspected shooter opened fire at JAT Partner Logistics, a trucking company in Midland, Texas, on June 22, 2026, killing an employee who confronted him outside before entering a meeting area, firing on employees, and then taking his own life. Deputies responding to reports of shots fired at the 5400 block of East Highway 80 discovered three people dead from gunshot wounds, including 45-year-old Jeff Waugh, a father of two; witnesses reported the suspect had planned to set the building on fire. The Midland County Sheriff’s Office and Texas Department of Public Safety are investigating the incident, with the *motive remaining unclear and the suspect’s identity and employment status not publicly confirmed.* This marks the second active shooter event in Midland in recent days, following one on June 12. Authorities stated there was no ongoing risk to the public, though the scene remained active with road closures in place.
