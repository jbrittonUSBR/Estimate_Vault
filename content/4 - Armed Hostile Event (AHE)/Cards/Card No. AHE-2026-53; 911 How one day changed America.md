(U) SYDNEY, Australia — Twenty-five years after the September 11, 2001 attacks, the ABC published a long-read reflection on how the day reshaped the United States and the world. The attacks killed 2,977 people from more than 100 countries, excluding the 19 hijackers, after al-Qaeda operatives flew hijacked airliners into the World Trade Center, the Pentagon and a field in Pennsylvania. The piece examined the enduring political legacy of 9/11 in September 2026.
