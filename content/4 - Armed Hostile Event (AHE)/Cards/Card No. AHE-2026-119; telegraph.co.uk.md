# Moscow Stoked Weekend Riots in Europe, Italy Says

[Source](https://www.telegraph.co.uk/world-news/2026/10/21/moscow-stoked-weekend-riots-in-europe-italy-says/?msockid=25aa5b6aaa4864a02aa84c83abf465f9)

(U) ROME — Italy accused Russia of stoking the violent unrest that produced looting, arson, and pitched battles with police across Europe over the weekend, as cost-of-living and energy-price protests spread across France, Italy, Spain, Portugal, and Belgium. The unrest included the arson destruction of the Limoges town hall in France and rioting in northern Italian cities such as Milan and Bologna, with a U.S. Embassy Rome demonstration alert warning of further protests and gatherings across Italy in the following days. The Italian government's allegation that Moscow fueled the riots adds an external-manipulation dimension to the wave of European unrest, which has put pressure on governments across the EU.
