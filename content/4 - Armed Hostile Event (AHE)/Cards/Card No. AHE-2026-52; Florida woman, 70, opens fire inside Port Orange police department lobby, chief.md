# Florida woman, 70, opens fire inside Port Orange police department lobby, chief says

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/us/florida-woman-70-opens-fire-port-orange-police-department-lobby-chief-says)

(U) PORT ORANGE, United States — A roughly 70-year-old Florida woman was confronted by law enforcement after she allegedly opened fire inside the lobby of the Port Orange Police Department on the morning of Thursday, September 3, 2026. Police Chief Manuel Marino said the woman entered the main lobby shortly after 8 a.m. holding a handgun, brandished the weapon at civilian staff in the front records section, and ultimately fired a round in the direction where they were working. She exited the lobby and was confronted by responding officers in an officer-involved shooting; she was transported to the hospital with her condition not immediately clear. Three officers were involved and no officers or civilian staff were injured. The Florida Department of Law Enforcement investigated the shooting.
