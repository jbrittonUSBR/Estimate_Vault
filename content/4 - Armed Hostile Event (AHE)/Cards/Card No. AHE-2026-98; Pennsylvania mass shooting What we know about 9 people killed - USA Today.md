# Pennsylvania mass shooting: What we know about nine people killed

[Source](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/crime/2026/10/10/multiple-people-killed-erie-mass-shooting/92189555007/)

(U) ERIE, Pennsylvania — A gunman killed eight people, including two women found outside and six others inside a home on Perry Street, before dying of an apparent self-inflicted gunshot wound. Among the victims were two children, a pregnant woman, and several family members; a family friend was also killed. Erie police responded after a ShotSpotter alert and found the suspect dead inside with a registered firearm, bringing the total to nine dead in what police described as the city's worst mass shooting in recent memory.
