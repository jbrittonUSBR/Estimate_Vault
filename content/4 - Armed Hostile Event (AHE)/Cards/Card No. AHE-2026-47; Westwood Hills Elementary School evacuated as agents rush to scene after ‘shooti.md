# Westwood Hills Elementary School evacuated as agents rush to scene after ‘shooting incident’ reported

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/09/02/us-news/westwood-hills-elementary-school-in-waynesboro-evacuated-after-shooting-incident-report/)

(U) WAYNESBORO, Va. — Westwood Hills Elementary School in Waynesboro, Virginia was evacuated on the morning of September 2, 2026 after a shooting incident was reported, according to the New York Post. One adult was injured and all children were reported safe; the school is about 95 miles northwest of Richmond. The Augusta County Sheriff described the scene as "contained and secured," and the ATF's Washington division responded. Reunification was established at Westminster Presbyterian Church, all Waynesboro schools were locked down, and Governor Abigail Spanberger was monitoring the situation.
