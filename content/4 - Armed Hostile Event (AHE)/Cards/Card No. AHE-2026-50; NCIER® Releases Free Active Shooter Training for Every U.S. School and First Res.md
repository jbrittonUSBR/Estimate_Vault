# NCIER® Releases Free Active Shooter Training for Every U.S. School and First Responder

[Source](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ncier-releases-free-active-shooter-training-for-every-us-school-and-first-responder-302866570.html)

(U) ORLANDO, Fla. — In a press release dated September 1, 2026, the National Center for Integrated Emergency Response (NCIER®), a division of C3 Pathways Inc., announced the release of a free online active-shooter and school-safety training course called "SSAVEIM Foundations." The self-paced course comprises 13 video modules, takes under one hour to complete and has no prerequisites, and was developed alongside the Standard Response Protocol and Standard Reunification Method. The announcement included a statement from NCIER chief executive Bill Godfrey. (Note: this is a company press release.)
