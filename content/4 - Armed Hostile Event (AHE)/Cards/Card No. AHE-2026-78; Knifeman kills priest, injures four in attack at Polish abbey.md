# Knifeman kills priest, injures four in attack at Polish abbey

[Source](https://www.reuters.com/world/one-dead-four-injured-polish-abbey-knife-attack-local-media-report-2026-09-24/)

(U) JAROSLAW, Poland — A man armed with a knife killed a priest and injured four other people during an attack on clergy and worshippers at a Benedictine abbey in southern Poland on Thursday, authorities said. The assailant used a knife taken from a kitchen at the site to attack one woman and four men, including a priest in a confessional, at a Benedictine Sisters monastery on the abbey grounds in Jaroslaw, and a 31-year-old Ukrainian man was arrested, prosecutor Bozena Harasz-Woloszyn of the District Prosecutor's Office in Przemysl said. Three victims were clergy and two were laypeople, with one dead and two in critical condition; the motive was not immediately clear and police were investigating, while Jaroslaw's mayor declared mourning through the end of the week.
