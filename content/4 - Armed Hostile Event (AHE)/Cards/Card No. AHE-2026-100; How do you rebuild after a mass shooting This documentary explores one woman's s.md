# How do you rebuild after a mass shooting? This documentary explores one woman's story

[Source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-13/aftermath-harmful-conspiracies-documentary/107257376)

(U) TASMANIA, Australia — On October 13, 2026, ABC News reported on the documentary *Aftermath*, which explores how Port Arthur massacre first responder Wendy Scurr fell into conspiracy theories after struggling with PTSD and a lack of mental health support in the wake of Australia’s worst mass shooting. The film also examines how co-director Jared Nicholson became drawn into similar online spaces before reckoning with his own past.
