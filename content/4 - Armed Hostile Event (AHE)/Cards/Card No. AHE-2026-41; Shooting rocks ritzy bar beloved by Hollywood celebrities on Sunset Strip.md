(U) A Sunday night shooting at a high-end rooftop bar along the Sunset Strip in West Hollywood left one man wounded and prompted a heavy emergency response.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Incident:** The shooting occurred at approximately 8:55 p.m. in the 8400 block of W. Sunset Boulevard (near La Cienega Boulevard). Witnesses and local reports indicated the gunfire took place at Harriet’s Rooftop bar, located on the ninth floor of the 1 Hotel West Hollywood.
- **The Victim:** An adult male was shot once in the leg. Los Angeles County firefighters transported him to a nearby hospital, where he was subsequently listed in stable condition.
- **Investigation & Search:** Deputies from the Los Angeles County Sheriff’s Department (LASD) West Hollywood Station responded to the scene, secured the area, and recovered a bullet casing near where the victim had been sitting.
- **Suspect Status:** Authorities launched an active investigation to determine a motive, and no immediate arrests were announced.
