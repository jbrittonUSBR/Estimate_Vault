# FBI Arrests Alleged ISIS-Inspired Attacker Planning Minnesota Mass Shooting at Mall of America

[Source](https://www.breitbart.com/2nd-amendment/2026/10/07/fbi-arrests-alleged-isis-inspired-attacker-planning-minnesota-mass-shooting-at-mall-of-america/)

(U) BLOOMINGTON, United States — The FBI arrested 18-year-old Sheikhdoon Abdullahi Mohamud in Minnesota on allegations that he was planning an ISIS-inspired mass shooting at the Mall of America. According to Breitbart reporting citing the Justice Department, Mohamud had communicated with an undercover informant for nearly two years, expressed intent to travel to fight for ISIS or al-Shabaab, and later planned to kill 30-60 people at the mall; he was taken into custody after attempting to purchase firearms from an undercover agent.
