(U) Kickapoo Lucky Eagle Casino in Eagle Pass, Texas, issued an official statement on June 28, 2026, denying online reports of an active shooter at the property. The casino’s Facebook post stated, “There is no active shooter at Kickapoo Lucky Eagle Casino Hotel. Please rely on official information. Thank you,” in response to circulating rumors. As of 5 p.m. that Sunday, no law enforcement agency had confirmed or announced any active shooter situation at the casino, according to the Eagle Pass News Leader. The statement urged the public to depend on verified sources amid the unconfirmed social media reports, with local authorities in Eagle Pass and Maverick County contacted for further clarification but providing no additional details at the time.
