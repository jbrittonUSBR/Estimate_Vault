# Russian satellite shadowed ICEYE radar-imaging spacecraft, The War Zone reported

[Source](https://www.twz.com/news-features/russias-satellite-unprecedented-shadowing-of-a-very-particular-radar-imaging-satellite-is-a-big-deal)

(U) October 19, 2026 — The War Zone reported that Russian satellite Kosmos 2612 maneuvered close to ICEYE-X36 after weeks of unusual orbital adjustments. The outlet described the shadowing as unprecedented and significant, noting the spacecraft’s radar-imaging capability and reported links to Ukraine; those judgments were the publication’s analysis, not an official determination of intent.
