# How FBI tracked an ISIS-linked teen, stopped him from carrying out Mall of America mass shooting

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/us/how-fbi-tracked-an-isis-linked-teen-stopped-him-from-carrying-out-mall-of-america-mass-shooting/articleshow/134788836.cms)

(U) BLOOMINGTON, United States — The FBI arrested an 18-year-old Minnesota man, Sheikhdoon Abdullahi Mohamud, after a nearly two-year undercover investigation into an alleged ISIS-inspired plot to carry out a mass shooting at the Mall of America. According to court documents, Mohamud had expressed interest in joining ISIS or al-Shabaab and, when unable to travel abroad, allegedly planned to kill 30 to 60 people at a Hmong Arts Celebration event at the mall in late October. An undercover agent sold him an AK-47, ammunition and magazines for $600 before his arrest.
