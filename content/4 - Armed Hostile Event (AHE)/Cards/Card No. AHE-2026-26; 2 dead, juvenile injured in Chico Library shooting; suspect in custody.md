(U) Chico police responded to reports of an active shooter at the Butte County Library on Sherman Avenue around 5:12 p.m. on June 22, 2026, where 18-year-old Bradley Scott Sayer opened fire, killing two adults—46-year-old Jacob Hull and a 74-year-old man—and injuring a juvenile before being taken into custody as he fled out the back of the building. Officers quickly formed an entry team, entered the library upon hearing gunshots and screams over an open 911 line, and apprehended Sayer without exchanging fire; all other Butte County Library branches were closed the following day as the investigation continued with assistance from multiple agencies. Police confirmed no ongoing threat to the public, and the suspect acted alone with no apparent connection to the victims or the library. The incident prompted street closures and a large emergency response in the area.
