# Multiple Victims Reported in Downtown Portland Assault; Suspect Killed in Officer-Involved Shooting

[Source](https://www.kgw.com/article/news/crime/multiple-victims-reported-downtown-portland-incident/283-545f6008-bd81-4668-8b3a-523d42756ce7)

(U) PORTLAND, Ore. — Portland police responded to an assault involving a weapon in downtown Portland at about 4:34 p.m. on Thursday, October 15, 2026, near Southwest Broadway, where a man cut multiple people with a box cutter before a Portland police officer shot and killed the suspect. Portland Fire & Rescue reported at least three victims at the scene; two people suffered non-life-threatening injuries in the stabbing. The incident prompted a large police response and an officer-involved-shooting investigation.
