# False flag fever: Who is itching for a ‘mass casualty event’ to start a war with Russia?

[Source](https://rt-english.com/news/649269-russia-eu-false-flag/)

(U) EUROPE — In an RT opinion column, historian Tarik Cyril Amar argued that comments by Anne Applebaum on the Bulwark podcast — saying European leaders privately discuss escalating the war with Russia and that a "mass casualty event" would be needed to convince the public — amounted to hinting that a pretext for war should be manufactured. Amar cited the 2022 Nord Stream pipeline sabotage and the 2014 Kyiv sniper killings as historical examples of false-flag operations and claimed Western establishments had sought to blame Russia for the Nord Stream attack. RT labeled the piece opinion and stated the author's views did not necessarily represent the outlet.
