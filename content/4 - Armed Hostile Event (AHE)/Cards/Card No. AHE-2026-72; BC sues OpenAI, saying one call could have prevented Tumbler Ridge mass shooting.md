# BC sues OpenAI, saying one call could have prevented Tumbler Ridge mass shooting

[Source](https://www.nationalobserver.com/2026/09/22/news/bc-sues-openai-saying-one-call-could-have-prevented-tumbler-ridge-mass-shooting)

(U) VANCOUVER — The British Columbia government filed a lawsuit in California against OpenAI over the February 10 Tumbler Ridge mass shooting, asserting that "one telephone call to the RCMP could have prevented the tragedy." The claim, announced by Attorney General Niki Sharma and filed with the Peace River South school district, says OpenAI flagged and banned shooter Jesse Van Rootselaar's ChatGPT account in 2025 but did not alert police, and alleges negligence, aiding and abetting a mass shooting and defective product liability. The province is seeking a jury trial, damages and injunctive relief; OpenAI has said it is committed to working with authorities and called the shooting an unspeakable tragedy.
