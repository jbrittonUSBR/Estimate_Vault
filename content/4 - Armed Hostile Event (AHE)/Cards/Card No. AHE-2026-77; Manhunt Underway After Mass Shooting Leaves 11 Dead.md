# Manhunt Underway After Mass Shooting Leaves 11 Dead

[Source](https://www.newser.com/story/396911/gunmen-kill-11-at-south-african-house-party.html)

(U) DURBAN, South Africa — Three gunmen stormed a house party in the Ziko area of KwaMakhutha south of Durban on Tuesday night and opened fire, killing 11 people — 10 at the scene and one at a hospital — and wounding three others, South African police said, according to Newser. Among the dead was a woman believed to have been pregnant, and police were examining a car set on fire near the home for possible links to the attack. Acting National Police Commissioner Puleng Dimpane said investigators were pursuing a “promising lead”; no motive has been identified, though authorities say a feud between rival groups is possible and some victims had previously drawn Crime Intelligence attention.
