# Mass shooting rocks California city as cops swoop on home

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/10/18/us-news/mass-shooting-rocks-california-city-as-cops-swoop-on-home/)

(U) October 18, 2026 — The New York Post reported that Riverside County sheriff’s deputies responded to a reported shooting at a home in Eastvale, California, shortly before 11:15 p.m. Saturday. Deputies found seven people with gunshot wounds; one was pronounced dead at the scene and six were transported to nearby trauma centers. Authorities had not publicly identified the deceased, provided updates on the survivors’ conditions, or announced an arrest, and the investigation was ongoing.
