# New York court rules social media firms not liable in Buffalo Tops mass shooting lawsuit

[Source](https://freerepublic.com/focus/f-news/4404139/posts)

(U) BUFFALO, NEW YORK — New York's Court of Appeals ruled 4-3 that Google, YouTube, Meta's Instagram, Reddit and Snapchat could not be held liable for the 2022 Buffalo grocery-store shooting that killed 10 Black people, upholding a lower court's dismissal, per WGRZ reporting carried by Free Republic and Reuters. The majority found no proof that the platforms' algorithms made the white supremacist gunman's attack a foreseeable consequence, while a dissenting judge argued juries should decide whether the platforms' design and failure to warn triggered the violence. The case was brought by survivors and families of victims.
