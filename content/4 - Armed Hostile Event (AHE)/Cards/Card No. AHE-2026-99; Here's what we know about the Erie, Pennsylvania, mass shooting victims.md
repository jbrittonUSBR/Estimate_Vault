# Here's what we know about the Erie, Pennsylvania, mass shooting victims

[Source](https://www.cbsnews.com/pittsburgh/news/erie-mass-shooting-shooter-victims-identified/)

(U) ERIE, Pennsylvania — Erie County Coroner Lyell Cook identified Matthew Stone Sr., 45, as the gunman in the October 9, 2026 mass shooting at a Perry Street home that left nine people dead, including seven of his relatives, before he died by suicide. Among the victims were his pregnant daughter Angelina Stone and her two young children, as well as his wife Michelle Stone and two other relatives; two women outside the home were also killed when Stone shot them as they arrived.
