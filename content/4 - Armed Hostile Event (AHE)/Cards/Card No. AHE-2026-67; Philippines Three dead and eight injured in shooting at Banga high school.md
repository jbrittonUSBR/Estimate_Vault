# Philippines: Three dead and eight injured in shooting at Banga high school

[Source](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c6x2z4dlzyr0o)

(U) BANGA, Philippines — A 16-year-old gunman shot two students dead and injured eight others at Banga National High School in the southern province of Mindanao on the afternoon of Friday, September 18, 2026, before killing himself. The two deceased victims were 14-year-old pupils, and the local mayor, Albert Palencia, told the BBC that four of the injured were in critical condition. Palencia told Reuters the gunman had warned friends at lunchtime that he would be "executing [his] plan," but they laughed because he was a known joker; he then shot 10 students before turning the gun on himself. Classes were suspended across the entire municipality of Banga, and it marked the third deadly school shooting in the Philippines since June.
