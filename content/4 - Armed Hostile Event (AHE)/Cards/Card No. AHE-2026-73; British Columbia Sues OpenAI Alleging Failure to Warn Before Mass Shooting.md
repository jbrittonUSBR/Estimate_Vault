# British Columbia Sues OpenAI Alleging Failure to Warn Before Mass Shooting

[Source](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/09/22/886361.htm)

(U) VANCOUVER — British Columbia filed a lawsuit in California against OpenAI alleging the company could have used ChatGPT logs to warn police and avert the February 10 Tumbler Ridge mass shooting, according to Bloomberg's account of the filing. The suit says 18-year-old Jesse Van Rootselaar killed five students and a teacher at Tumbler Ridge Secondary School, as well as her mother and half-brother, before taking her own life, and that OpenAI internally flagged and banned one of her accounts in 2025 without escalating a warning to police. The province is seeking a jury trial, damages and injunctive relief, while OpenAI said its thoughts remain with the victims and families.
