# Search turns up no threats, students return to class

[Source](https://www.cbs19news.com/news/investigation-into-alleged-bomb-threat-underway-at-chs/article_12b35004-1cd9-474e-ac0f-270c75ae6441.html)

(U) CHARLOTTESVILLE, United States — A secondary search of Charlottesville High School and its surroundings by Charlottesville police found no threats, and the school returned to its regular schedule on October 2, 2026, CBS 19 News reported at 1:45 p.m. The announcement followed a bomb threat called in around 11:40 a.m. that prompted evacuation and a sweep by city, county, university and state police with bomb-detecting K-9s; the incident remained under investigation.
