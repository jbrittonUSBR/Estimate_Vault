# EU warns of a chilling new terror menace 25 years after 9/11 as online youths seek violent thrills

[Source](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/eu-warns-chilling-new-terror-menace-25-years-136264885)

(U) BRUSSELS, Belgium — The European Union's counterterrorism coordinator warned of a new terror menace 25 years after the September 11 attacks, describing online youths who seek violent thrills, according to the Associated Press. The official said this emerging threat is developing alongside the persistent danger posed by established jihadist networks. The warning came as Europe marked the anniversary of the 2001 attacks.
