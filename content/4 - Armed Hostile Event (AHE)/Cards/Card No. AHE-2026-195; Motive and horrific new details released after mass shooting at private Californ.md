# Motive and horrific new details released after mass shooting at private California concert

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/11/23/us-news/mass-shooting-at-private-california-concert-linked-to-gang-violence/)

(U) STRATHMORE, CALIFORNIA — Gunfire at a private Norteño rap concert headlined by Bay Area artist Lazy-Boy at Candyman Ranch wounded nine people on November 21, per the New York Post. The Tulare County Sheriff's Office said more than 150 people attended and investigators believed the shooting may have been gang-related; one victim was airlifted, three required surgery, and a 26-year-old man shot in the chest remained in critical condition. One victim had been released from the hospital, according to ABC7.
