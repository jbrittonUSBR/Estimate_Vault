# Arizona man accused of plotting mass casualty attack on religious site with 'cookbook' tied to OKC bombing

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/us/arizona-man-accused-plotting-mass-casualty-attack-religious-site-cookbook-tied-okc-bombing)

(U) October 18, 2026 — Fox News reported that Maricopa County prosecutors accused Matthew Bjornholt, 27, of threatening an attack on a Jewish or Muslim house of worship and alleged that the plan involved explosives, firearms, and an accomplice. Prosecutors said he possessed *The Anarchist Cookbook* and sought a $500,000 bond; his defense attorney disputed the allegations, describing them as hearsay and saying federal investigators had found no evidence of terrorism or bomb-making materials. A judge set bond at $100,000 and found probable cause to proceed, according to Fox News’ account of local reporting.
