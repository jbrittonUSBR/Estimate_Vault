# Election debate cancelled in Brazil, bomb scare outside Supreme Court

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/2/election-debate-cancelled-in-brazil-bomb-scare-outside-supreme-court)

(U) BRASILIA, Brazil — President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva and opposition candidate Flavio Bolsonaro both skipped a final TV Globo debate on October 1, 2026, ahead of the first round of the presidential election, prompting the broadcaster to cancel the event after rulings by the electoral court and the Supreme Court created what it called "legal uncertainty." The same day, police ruled out a bomb threat outside the Supreme Court after arresting a suspect who tried to jump the court's fences and threw a backpack onto a bus stop; the bag contained only personal belongings, a military police spokesman said.
