# Massive police response amid reports of active shooter at Minneapolis apartment building

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16100729/Minneapolis-apartment-shooting.html)

(U) MINNEAPOLIS — Police mounted a massive response to reports of an active shooter at a downtown Minneapolis apartment building on the afternoon of September 2, 2026, according to the Daily Mail. Officers arriving at the Loring Towers building encountered a "haze" that limited visibility as they confronted the gunman. The incident killed two civilians and the suspected shooter and left three officers wounded. Nearby locations including the University of St Thomas and the Hyatt Regency were placed on lockdown during the response.
