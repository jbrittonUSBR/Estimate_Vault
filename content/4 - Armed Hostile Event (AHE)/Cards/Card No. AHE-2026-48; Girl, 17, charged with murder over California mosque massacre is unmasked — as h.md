# Girl, 17, charged with murder over California mosque massacre is unmasked — as her ties to shooters revealed

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/09/01/us-news/female-san-diego-mosque-shooting-accomplice-idd-mugshot-released/)

(U) SAN DIEGO — A 17-year-old North Carolina student, identified as Sarah Lindsey Santiago, was charged with three counts of first-degree murder and one count of conspiracy in connection with the May 18, 2026 massacre at the Islamic Center of San Diego, the New York Post reported on September 1, 2026. Prosecutors pursued an aiding-and-abetting theory, alleging she agreed to livestream the attack and recorded and disseminated footage along with the gunmen's manifesto. Three people were killed by a 17-year-old and an 18-year-old gunman, both of whom were later found dead in a vehicle near the mosque. Forsyth County District Attorney Jim O'Neill announced the charges.
