# Police officers shot among multiple victims in downtown Minneapolis shooting

[Source](https://freerepublic.com/focus/f-news/4394014/posts)

(U) MINNEAPOLIS — A gunman opened fire in a downtown Minneapolis apartment building on September 2, 2026, killing two civilians and wounding several police officers before the shooter was killed, according to a Fox News report aggregated by Free Republic. Three officers were injured in the incident at Loring Towers, and roughly six to seven people were reported hurt in total. A large multi-agency response included the Minneapolis Police Department, ATF, the Hennepin County Sheriff's Office, the Minnesota Bureau of Criminal Apprehension and the FBI. Governor Tim Walz, Attorney General Keith Ellison and Mayor Jacob Frey commented on the response.
