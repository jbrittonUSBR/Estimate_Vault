# Wichita police respond to ‘mass casualty’ incident involving vehicle at Stryker Sports Complex

[Source](https://freerepublic.com/focus/f-news/4399548/posts)

(U) Wichita, Kansas — Wichita police responded to a vehicle incident at Stryker Sports Complex during a youth soccer game and described it as a mass-casualty incident. Reporting by KCTV5 said a child and an adult died and multiple people were injured after a vehicle drove onto the fields; police said the driver was in custody and there was no ongoing public threat. The Free Republic link is an aggregator post pointing to KCTV5 coverage, not the primary police account.
