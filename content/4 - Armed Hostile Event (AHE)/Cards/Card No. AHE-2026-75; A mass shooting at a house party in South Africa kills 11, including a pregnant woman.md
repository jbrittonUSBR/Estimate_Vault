# A mass shooting at a house party in South Africa kills 11, including a pregnant woman

[Source](https://apnews.com/article/south-africa-house-party-shooting-91416626e0cf789bf2f801061bb8b46a)

(U) CAPE TOWN, South Africa — Three gunmen entered a house in KwaMakhutha township, about 30 kilometers south of Durban, and opened fire on a party late Tuesday, killing at least 11 people including a pregnant woman and wounding three others, police said. Ten died at the scene and an 11th at a hospital; no arrests had been made, and the acting national police commissioner ordered a 72-hour operation to track down the suspects. Police said the motive was unknown but that some victims were known to authorities in criminal investigations and a feud between rival groups could not be ruled out.
