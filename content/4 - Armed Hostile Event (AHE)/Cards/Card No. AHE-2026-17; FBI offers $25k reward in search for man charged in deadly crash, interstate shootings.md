(U) The FBI is offering a reward of up to $25,000 for information leading to the arrest of 22-year-old Oscar Sanchez-Munoz, who is wanted in connection with a series of shootings and a deadly crash in the Kansas City metro area. On June 11, Sanchez-Munoz was involved in a shooting in Wyandotte County, Kansas, that injured an adult and a child inside a vehicle. Five days later, on June 16, five people—including an Uber driver transporting fans to a World Cup game—were shot along I-70 and I-670 in a 30-minute span, with one victim in life-threatening condition. He is also believed to be linked to a fatal crash around 6:30 p.m. that same day at Truman Road and Bennington Avenue, where a truck slammed into a traffic signal box and flipped over. Multi-agency searches, including at homes in Kansas City and a standoff in Independence that resulted in a house fire, have been conducted as Sanchez-Munoz, described as armed and dangerous, remains at large. Kansas City police and the FBI are urging the public to provide tips to help locate him.
