# 3 sentenced to death for deadly church mass shooting in Jordan

[Source](https://www.christianpost.com/news/3-sentenced-to-death-for-church-mass-shooting-in-jordan.html)

(U) Karak governorate, Jordan — Jordan’s Armed Forces said three men were sentenced to death by hanging after being convicted of terrorism-related offenses for the October 23 shooting at St. George’s Church. The Christian Post reported that the attack killed nine people and wounded 34, and that security forces captured the suspects three days later.
