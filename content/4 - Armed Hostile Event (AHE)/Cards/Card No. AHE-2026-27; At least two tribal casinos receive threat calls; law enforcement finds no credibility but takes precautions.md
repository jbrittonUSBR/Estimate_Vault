(U) Tribal casinos in at least three states received threat calls or faced online reports of violence on June 28, 2026, prompting law enforcement responses even as authorities assessed the threats as not credible. At Downstream Casino Resort in northeast Oklahoma, Quapaw Nation marshals responded to a threat of violence and maintained increased presence as a precaution after similar threats were reported at other tribal properties in the state. In Michigan’s Upper Peninsula, Hannahville Tribal Police locked down and searched Island Resort & Casino following reports of explosives and weapons in a vehicle on the property before issuing an all-clear later that evening. In south Texas, Kickapoo Lucky Eagle Casino addressed online rumors of an active shooter by stating there was no such threat and urging reliance on official information. While no connections between the incidents across more than 1,600 miles were confirmed and law enforcement found no evidence of credible threats, precautions were taken and public reassurances issued at the affected properties.
