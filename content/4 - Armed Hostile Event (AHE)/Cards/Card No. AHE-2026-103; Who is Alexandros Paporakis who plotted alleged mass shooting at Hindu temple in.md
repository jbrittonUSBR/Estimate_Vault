# Who is Alexandros Paporakis? Alleged plot targeted Hindu temple in Aurora

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/us/who-is-alexandros-paporakis-who-plotted-alleged-mass-shooting-at-hindu-temple-in-aurora-what-his-manifesto-targeting-indians-revealed/articleshow/134967765.cms)

(U) AURORA, Illinois — The Times of India reported that 19-year-old Alexandros Paporakis was arrested over an alleged plan to attack a Hindu place of worship; the intended temple had not been identified. The article said prosecutors alleged that coworkers received an eight-page manifesto describing a mass-casualty plan and photos of tactical equipment, and that Paporakis had spoken of killing Indian people. Police and prosecutors reportedly said a search recovered an unserialized AR-style rifle, loaded magazines, ammunition, a gas canister and items bearing Nazi symbolism. The allegations had not been adjudicated.
