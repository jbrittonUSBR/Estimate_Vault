# Two Macon mass shooting suspects linked to 2023 shooting, drug and firearm bust

[Source](https://freerepublic.com/focus/f-news/4397481/posts)

(U) MACON, United States — A WGXA report reposted by the Free Republic aggregator linked two suspects in a Macon mass shooting, identified in the post as David Vincent Pitts and Jaquez Bailey, to a prior drug and firearm bust and a 2023 shooting. According to the excerpt, the pair and 28 others trespassed and held an unauthorised pool party at the Bowman Station Apartments, where more than a dozen guns were recovered after deputies received reports of vandalism, trespassing and drug use at several apartment complexes; the earlier shooting at Carolyn Crayton Park wounded 12 teenagers at a birthday celebration.
