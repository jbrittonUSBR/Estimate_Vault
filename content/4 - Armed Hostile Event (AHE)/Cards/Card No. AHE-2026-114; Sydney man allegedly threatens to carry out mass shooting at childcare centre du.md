# Sydney man allegedly threatens to carry out mass shooting at childcare centre during conversation with AI chatbot

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16219607/hurstville-man-ai-chatbot.html)

(U) October 18, 2026 — The Daily Mail reported that David Lan Thackray, 29, was arrested and charged after Interpol flagged chatbot conversations in which he allegedly threatened to shoot children at a childcare center near his Hurstville home. Prosecutors told Sutherland Local Court the potential consequences were catastrophic, while his lawyer argued the comments were a stress-induced tirade with no weight behind them. The article said Thackray was due to return to court pending a mental-health assessment; the alleged threat had not been adjudicated.
