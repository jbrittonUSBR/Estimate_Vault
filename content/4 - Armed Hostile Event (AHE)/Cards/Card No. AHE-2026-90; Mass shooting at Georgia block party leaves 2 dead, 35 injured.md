# Mass shooting at Georgia block party leaves 2 dead, 35 injured

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/us/mass-shooting-georgia-block-party-leaves-2-dead-35-injured)

(U) VIENNA, Georgia — A mass shooting at a neighborhood block party attended by more than 1,000 people left two men dead and at least 35 others injured. Most of the wounded suffered gunshot wounds, while others were struck by vehicles or injured while fleeing as multiple shooters opened fire from different locations.
