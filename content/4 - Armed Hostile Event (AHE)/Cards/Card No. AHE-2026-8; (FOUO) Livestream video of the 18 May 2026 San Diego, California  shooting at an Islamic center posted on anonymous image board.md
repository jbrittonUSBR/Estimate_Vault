(U//FOUO) Source created a thread on an anonymous image board platform titled "SAN DIEGO ISLAMIC CENTER SHOOTING - LIVESTREAM CONFIRMED". Source first posted a short video clip, stating "The video attached is confirmation" that the two individuals who "shot up the mosque in Clairemont livestreamed the entire shooting". Source later replied in the same thread with a link to download the 10 minute, 24-second "Full livestream" from a video sharing site. Source claimed the video was "Livestreamed on Discord" and "Recorded on some kids phone".

(U//FOUO) The video shows that one individual screen-recorded a Discord call with a second individual. During the call, the second individual used their mobile device camera to show another device engaged in a separate Discord call. In that separate call, another participant provided the video feed of the attack (Collector Comment: It remains unclear whether the call showing the attack video feed involved only the two participants, or if there were additional attendees. It is also unclear whether the video feed was live during the attack, or a "live" viewing of a recorded video).

(U//FOUO) The 10-minute and 24-second video feed of the shooters depicted two individuals wearing tactical gear. The footage showed them driving to the location, exiting the vehicle, entering the building and opening fire. The video captured them hovering over a victim, walking around while experiencing a possible gun jamming issue, switching between weapons, exiting the building, and re-entering the vehicle. After re-entering the vehicle, the driver shot the other individual and then themselves.

(U//FOUO) The Shooter's tactical gear displayed a Sonnenrad symbol and an Atomwaffen Division logo. Their weapons featured a swastika, an SS Schutzstaffel symbol, the phrases "Race War Now", "Battle of [illegible]", "[illegible] were promised years ago", and "TND" (Collector Comment: TND may be an abbreviation for the phrase "Total [derogatory term for African Americans] Death").
