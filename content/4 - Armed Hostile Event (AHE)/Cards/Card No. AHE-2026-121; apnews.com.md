# California State Workers Strike over Wages and Healthcare

[Source](https://apnews.com/article/california-state-workers-strike-newsom-a3a4f547a612a68360e75e7feaaffb96)

(U) SACRAMENTO, Calif. — Thousands of California state workers walked off the job in a one-day strike on October 21, 2026, urging Gov. Gavin Newsom's administration to agree to a new contract, in the first strike in SEIU Local 1000's 44-year history. The union, the largest in state government, reported roughly 100,000 members walking off the job and pressed for a 20% salary increase over three years, higher state healthcare contributions, and changes to a return-to-office mandate after more than 96% of members voted to approve an unfair labor practice strike. State services were affected by the walkout, which marked an escalation in the contract dispute.
