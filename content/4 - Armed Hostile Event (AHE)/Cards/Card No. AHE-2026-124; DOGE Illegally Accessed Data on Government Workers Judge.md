# Judge ruled OPM unlawfully disclosed federal workers' data to DOGE

[Source](https://www.theepochtimes.com/us/doge-illegally-accessed-data-on-government-workers-judge-6106938)

(U) United States — The Epoch Times reported that a federal judge ruled the Office of Personnel Management unlawfully disclosed federal employees’ personal data to the Department of Government Efficiency, finding violations of the Privacy Act. The ruling concerned OPM’s provision of access to personnel records; it did not find that DOGE itself unlawfully accessed the data.
