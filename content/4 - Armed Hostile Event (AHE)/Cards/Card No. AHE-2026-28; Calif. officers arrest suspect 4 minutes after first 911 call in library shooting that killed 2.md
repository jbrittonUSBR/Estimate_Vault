(U) Chico police arrested 18-year-old Bradley Scott Sayer less than four minutes after the first 911 call reporting an active shooter at the Butte County Library on Sherman Avenue on June 22, 2026, where he killed two men and injured a child before fleeing out the back and being taken into custody. Sayer, a recent Chico High School graduate and self-described longtime fan of the Columbine massacre who wore a “natural selection” T-shirt mimicking the 1999 shooters, conducted a walk-through of the library, retrieved a shotgun from his vehicle, shot 46-year-old Jacob Hull in the leg and head outside, then fired multiple shots inside, killing 74-year-old Robert Johnson; a 7-year-old girl with Hull suffered minor injuries while witnessing the attack. Officers heard gunshots and screams over an open 911 line, quickly entered the building, and apprehended Sayer without exchanging fire, recovering a shotgun and two other firearms registered to his family. Police Chief Billy Aldridge praised the rapid response for limiting further loss of life, while the suspect was booked on two counts of murder with arraignment scheduled; all Butte County Library branches closed the following day as authorities planned enhanced security measures.
