# Leaseholder kills Guwahati hotel co-owner, her son, daughter in law, brother and caretaker: How a rent dispute led to mass shooting in Hotel Bilas Inn

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/new-updates/tenant-kills-guwahati-hotel-co-owner-her-son-daughter-in-law-brother-and-caretaker-how-a-rent-dispute-led-to-mass-shooting-in-hotel-bilas-inn/articleshow/135229638.cms)

(U) Guwahati, India — The Economic Times reported that a shooting at Hotel Bilas Inn left five people dead and one injured on Monday night. Police suspected a dispute over the hotel lease and arrested leaseholder Ghanakanta Gogoi within an hour, seizing a licensed pistol, according to the report; the motive remained under investigation.
