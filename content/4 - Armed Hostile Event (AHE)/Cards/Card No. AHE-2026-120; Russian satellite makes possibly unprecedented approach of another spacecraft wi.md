# Russian satellite makes possibly unprecedented approach of another spacecraft without permission

[Source](https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/19/science/russian-satellite-iceye-x36-approach)

(U) October 19, 2026 — CNN reported that Russian satellite Kosmos 2612 maneuvered unusually close to ICEYE-X36, a spacecraft operated by a U.S. company and carrying radar-imaging capabilities. The article described the encounter as possibly unprecedented and reported tracking observations; it did not establish that the approach was hostile or that it caused damage.
