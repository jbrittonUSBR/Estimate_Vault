# Victims of Pennsylvania mass shooting included pregnant daughter of gunman

[Source](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/victims-pennsylvania-mass-shooting-included-pregnant-daughter-gunman-rcna603037)

(U) ERIE, Pennsylvania — The nine victims killed in the October 9, 2026 mass shooting at a Perry Street residence in Erie included the gunman's pregnant daughter, Angelina Stone, and her two young children, according to NBC News. The shooter, identified as Matthew S. Stone, was found dead inside the home from an apparent self-inflicted gunshot wound.
