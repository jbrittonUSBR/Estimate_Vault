(U) A daytime shooting spree in northeast Houston left three motorists injured after a gunman opened fire on moving vehicles from a porch. The suspect was subsequently shot and wounded by law enforcement.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Incident:** Around 11 a.m., 45-year-old Demetri O'Neal Thomas allegedly armed himself with a rifle and began firing at passing vehicles along North Wayside Drive near St. Louis Street.
- **Casualties:** Three people driving past were struck by gunfire and sustained injuries; all three were later reported to be in stable condition at local hospitals.
- **Law Enforcement Response:** Houston Police Department (HPD) officers and a Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS) trooper arrived after being flagged down by bystanders. Officers located Thomas on a nearby residential porch, where he refused verbal commands to drop his rifle and raised the weapon.
- **Suspect Wounded & Charged:** Three HPD officers and the DPS trooper discharged their weapons, striking Thomas. He was hospitalized in critical condition and subsequently charged with multiple counts, including aggravated assault against a public servant and aggravated assault with a deadly weapon.
