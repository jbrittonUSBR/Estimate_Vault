# 11 wounded in mass shooting at Georgia park - WSB-TV

[Source](https://slashdot.org/firehose.pl?op=view&amp;id=185882360)

(U) Macon, USA — A mass shooting at Carolyn Crayton Park in Macon, Georgia, left 11 teenagers wounded during a late-night gathering, according to the Bibb County Sheriff's Office. Reporting aggregated through Slashdot and WSB-TV described the incident as a shootout at a birthday party at the park. The Slashdot Firehose entry linked to several news reports, including coverage by 13WMAZ, WAVY.com, FOX 5 Atlanta and CBS News, with some accounts citing 12 teens wounded.
