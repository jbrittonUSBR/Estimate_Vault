(U) At least nine mass shootings occurred across eight states over the weekend of July 18–20, 2026, leaving nine people dead and at least 60 injured. Incidents took place in Colorado, Delaware, Maryland, North Carolina, Oklahoma, Arizona, Tennessee, and two in Minnesota.

(U) Notable cases included:

- Denver nightclub parking lot: 1 dead, 8 injured
- Oklahoma City restaurant party: 2 dead, 3 injured
- Jackson, Tennessee park: 3 dead, 9 injured (reportedly gang-related)
- Downtown Tucson: 9 injured (gunman shot by police)
- Baltimore neighborhood: 6 injured
- Asheville, North Carolina parking lot: 2 dead, 7 injured
- Two separate Minneapolis shootings: 1 dead, 14 injured combined
- Newark, Delaware Waffle House parking lot: 6 injured (including teens)

(U) Most remain under investigation with few or no arrests announced at the time of reporting.
