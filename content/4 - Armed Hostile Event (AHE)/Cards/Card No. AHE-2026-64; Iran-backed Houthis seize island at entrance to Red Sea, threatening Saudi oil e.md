# Iran-backed Houthis seize island at entrance to Red Sea, threatening Saudi oil exports

[Source](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/show/iran-backed-houthis-seize-island-at-entrance-to-red-sea-threatening-saudi-oil-exports)

(U) BAB EL-MANDEB STRAIT, Yemen — PBS NewsHour reported that Houthi rebels in Yemen captured the strategic island of Mayun after Saudi-backed Yemeni government forces withdrew, extending the group's hold on both shores of the Bab el-Mandeb Strait. The advance followed the Houthis' seizure of the Red Sea port of Mokha, and a source told PBS that Saudi Arabia had formally requested US airstrikes; satellite data showed black smoke near the kingdom's East-West Pipeline, which it shut down as a precautionary measure.
