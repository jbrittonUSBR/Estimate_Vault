# Online 'wild west' is radicalising young people to carry out nihilistic violence, Southport inquiry hears

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16114977/Online-wild-west-radicalising-young-people-carry-nihilistic-violence-Southport-inquiry-hears.html)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — The second phase of the Southport Inquiry, chaired by Sir Adrian Fulford, heard that an online 'wild west' is radicalising young people toward nihilistic violence and mass-casualty attacks, with a focus on 'violence-fixated individuals' not primarily driven by ideology. Dr Richard Taylor of the Counter-Terrorism Clinical Consultancy Service said that nearly one in ten of 1,534 referrals showed a fascination with extremist violence, about a third of them under 15. Professor Laura Smith of the University of Bath described online gateway communities centred on self-harm, eating disorders and gore; the inquiry follows the July 2024 murders by Axel Rudakubana.
