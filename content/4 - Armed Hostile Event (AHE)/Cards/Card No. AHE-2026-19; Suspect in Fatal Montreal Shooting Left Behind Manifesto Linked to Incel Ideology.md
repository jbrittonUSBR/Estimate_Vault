(U) A suspect in a fatal shooting in Montreal’s Côte-des-Neiges neighborhood on June 22, 2026, left behind a 104-page manifesto espousing incel ideology before killing a police officer, a civilian bystander, and himself while injuring another officer. The manifesto, reviewed by local media, blames feminism, liberalism, and capitalism for male suffering and calls for terror to establish a “new order,” ending with the exhortation to “Be unflinching, go forth, and KILL THEM ALL!” It lists numerous targets including pornography companies such as Aylo (parent of Pornhub), politicians, business leaders, real-estate brokerages, influential Zionists, private equity firms, bankers, oil and health-care executives, cryptocurrency leaders, plastic surgeons, and “pickup artists.” Police have not publicly confirmed the suspect’s identity—reported by media as Seth Hatfield of Lethbridge, Alberta—or a specific motive, but issued warnings to Canadian law enforcement about potential copycat attacks targeting police. The civilian victim, 68-year-old Michael Mizrahi, was caught in the crossfire, while the injured officer was reported in stable condition.
