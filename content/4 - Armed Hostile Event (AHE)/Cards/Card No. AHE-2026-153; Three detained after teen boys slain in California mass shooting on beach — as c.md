# Three detained after teen boys slain in California mass shooting on beach — as cops raid home

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/11/06/us-news/three-detained-after-teen-boys-slain-in-california-mass-shooting-on-beach-as-cops-raid-home/)

(U) Greenfield and Moss Landing, California, USA - The New York Post reported that Monterey County investigators detained three people after searching a Greenfield home in connection with a shooting near Moss Landing State Beach. Two teenage boys, aged 13 and 15, had been killed; the article described the detainees as persons of interest and did not report charges.
