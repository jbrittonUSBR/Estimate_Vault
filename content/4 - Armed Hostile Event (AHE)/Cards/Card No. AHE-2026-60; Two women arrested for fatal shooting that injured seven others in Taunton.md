# Two women arrested for fatal shooting that injured seven others in Taunton

[Source](https://www.thesunchronicle.com/news/local_news/two-women-arrested-for-fatal-shooting-that-injured-seven-others-in-taunton/article_78efcf7a-f834-485f-ae4d-545fb350d433.html)

(U) TAUNTON, United States — Two 25-year-old New Bedford women were arrested and charged in connection with a shooting at a house party that killed one person and wounded seven others in Taunton, Massachusetts, according to Bristol County District Attorney Thomas Quinn. Sienna Barboza and Malkiah Cunningham each faced one count of murder and seven counts of armed assault with intent to murder, with their arrests announced Wednesday and Thursday respectively. Zirovic-k Depina, 22, of Brockton, was pronounced dead early Sunday morning after police responded to reports of shots fired near Weir and Sumner streets.
