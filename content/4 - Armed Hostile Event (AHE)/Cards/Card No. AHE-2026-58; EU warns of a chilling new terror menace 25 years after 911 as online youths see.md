# EU warns of a chilling new terror menace 25 years after 9/11 as online youths seek violent thrills

[Source](https://www.yahoo.com/news/world/articles/eu-warns-chilling-terror-menace-050858368.html)

(U) BRUSSELS, Belgium — EU Counter-Terrorism Coordinator Bartjan Wegter warned ahead of the 25th anniversary of the September 11, 2001 attacks that a new "nihilistic violent extremism" driven largely by young people online poses a growing security threat in Europe. Investigators from nine European countries identified about 4,340 web addresses linked to the online community known as "The COM" during a disruption campaign in June and July. Europol recorded 45 terrorist attacks across 10 EU countries in the previous year, none of them mass-casualty events, along with 486 arrests; jihadist networks were responsible for 24 of the attacks and more than 70 percent of arrests, and about one third of those detained were under 18.
