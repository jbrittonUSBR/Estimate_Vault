# The SBU detained a 16-year-old agent of the Russian Federation who manufactured explosives for a terrorist attack in Khmelnytskyi

[Source](https://korabelov.info/en/2026/10/621671/the-sbu-detained-a-16-year-old-agent-of-the-russian-federation-who-manufactured-explosives-for-a-terrorist-attack-in-khmelnytskyi/#google_vignette)

(U) KHIMELNYTSKYI, Ukraine — The Security Service of Ukraine's counterintelligence detained a 16-year-old Russian agent from Chernivtsi who, on the orders of Russian handlers, manufactured an improvised explosive device intended for an attack on a Ukrainian serviceman, Ukrainian outlet Novosti Nikolaeva reported on October 2, 2026. According to the investigation, the teenager travelled to Khmelnytskyi, bought components, collected TNT with an electric detonator from a stash, reinforced the device with metal nuts, left it in a cache and installed a remote-access phone camera to monitor it before being detained at the bus station. He was charged with high treason under part 2 of Article 111 of Ukraine's Criminal Code and faces life imprisonment with confiscation of property; the outlet's account derives from SBU statements.
