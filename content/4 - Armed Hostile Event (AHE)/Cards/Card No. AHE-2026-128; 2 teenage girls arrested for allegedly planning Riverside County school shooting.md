# Two teen girls arrested for allegedly planning shooting at Corona High School

[Source](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/local/riverside-county-school-shooting-plan-arrests/3958704/)

(U) October 20, 2026 — NBC Los Angeles reported that two 15-year-old girls who attended Corona-Norco Unified School District schools were arrested on suspicion of conspiring to attack Corona High School. Police said the FBI had alerted them to threatening social-media communications and that the teens researched school shootings and discussed obtaining weapons; no weapons were recovered, and prosecutors were reviewing the case. The allegations remained under investigation.
