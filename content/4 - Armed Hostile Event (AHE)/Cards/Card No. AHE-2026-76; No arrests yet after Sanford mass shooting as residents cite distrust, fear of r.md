# No arrests yet after Sanford mass shooting as residents cite distrust, fear of retaliation

[Source](https://www.clickorlando.com/news/local/2026/09/23/no-arrests-yet-after-sandford-mass-shooting-as-residents-cite-distrust-fear-of-retaliation/)

(U) SANFORD, Fla. — Three days after a mass shooting at a memorial gathering left eight people hurt, Sanford police had announced no arrests and named no suspects, with investigators saying witnesses would not talk. Residents told WKMG that distrust of law enforcement and fear of retaliation were keeping people from coming forward, citing unsolved cases and the 2012 killing of Trayvon Martin nearby. A national nonprofit planned community patrols and at least a dozen churches were set to gather Friday near the park where the shooting occurred.
