# Trans neo-Nazi teen pleads not guilty over thwarted Hindu temple mass shooting plot

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/11/19/us-news/trans-neo-nazi-teen-pleads-not-guilty-over-thwarted-hindu-temple-mass-shooting-plot/)

(U) Aurora, Illinois — Alexandros Paporakis, 19, pleaded not guilty Thursday to a nine-count indictment including terrorism and armed violence over an alleged plan to attack a Hindu temple here on Oct. 11, the first day of Navaratri. Court documents say he drove to the temple parking lot and tried to fire at a person outside, but police say his gun jammed; prosecutors say he wrote that he intended the attack to terrorize Indian immigrants, and he remains detained pending a Jan. 7, 2027 court date.
