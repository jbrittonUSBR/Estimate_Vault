# Trump Cites Iran's 'Drug Factories' As Another Target For Bombing

[Source](https://sg.news.yahoo.com/trump-cites-irans-drug-factories-105749925.html)

(U) DURANT, United States — At a rally in Durant, Oklahoma, on October 1, 2026, President Donald Trump said the June 2025 "Operation Midnight Hammer" bombing of Iran's nuclear facilities also struck "drug factories" because Iran "was doing drugs," describing the targets as "drug factories/nuclear factories." HuffPost, republished by Yahoo News Singapore, reported that the remark appeared to be a new justification for the ongoing war with Iran and noted that Trump also appeared to misstate the chronology of the conflict, which began in February 2026, months after the single 2025 bombing mission.
