# EU Agency Warns Websites May Need to Be Taken Offline as PHP Prepares Patches for Critical Vulnerabilities

[Source](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/eu-agency-warns-websites-may-need-be-taken-offline-php-prepares-patches-2026-10-21/)

(U) BRUSSELS — The EU's cybersecurity agency, CERT-EU, warned that organizations unable to apply patches for two critical vulnerabilities in the widely used PHP programming language should consider taking affected websites offline, as cybersecurity agencies in Europe, Australia, Japan, and Britain urged PHP users to update immediately. Several government, school, and hospital websites across Europe were taken offline on Wednesday, October 21, 2026, as organizations rushed to protect their systems ahead of PHP's release of fixes. The flaws affect a language underpinning a large share of the web, prompting broad emergency-patching guidance worldwide.
