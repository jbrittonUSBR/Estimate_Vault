# Poland treats fatal mass shooting as 'terrorist offence'

[Source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-09/poland-treating-mass-shooting-as-terrorist-offence/107362106)

(U) Kraków, Poland — Polish prosecutors treated the mass shooting in the city's Main Square as an act constituting a terrorist offence, ABC News reported. The attack killed six people and injured at least 15; the suspect remained in critical condition after surgery. Police said two officers who stopped the gunman were hospitalized, and the country entered two days of national mourning.
