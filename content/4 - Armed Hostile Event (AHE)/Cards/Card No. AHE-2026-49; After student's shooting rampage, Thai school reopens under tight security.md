# After student's shooting rampage, Thai school reopens under tight security

[Source](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/asia/after-students-shooting-rampage-thai-school-reopens-under-tight-security-6354856)

(U) NONTHABURI, Thailand — Debsirin Nonthaburi School reopened under tight security on September 1, 2026, three weeks after a student's deadly shooting rampage at the school, according to a Reuters report carried by CNA. The school resumed classes with an increased police presence and heightened security measures in place.
