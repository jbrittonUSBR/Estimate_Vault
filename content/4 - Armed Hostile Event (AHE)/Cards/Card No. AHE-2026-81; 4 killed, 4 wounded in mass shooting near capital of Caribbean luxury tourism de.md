# 4 killed, 4 wounded in mass shooting near capital of Caribbean luxury tourism destination

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/travel/4-killed-4-wounded-mass-shooting-near-capital-caribbean-luxury-tourism-destination)

(U) KINGSTOWN — Four people were killed and four wounded in a mass shooting in the Green Hill community near Kingstown, St. Vincent and the Grenadines, at about 10:30 p.m. on Friday, Fox News reported. Acting Commissioner of Police Trevor Bailey said the shooting was under investigation, and the report noted that the country had recorded 37 murders so far in 2026. The attack occurred near the capital of a Caribbean destination known for luxury tourism.
