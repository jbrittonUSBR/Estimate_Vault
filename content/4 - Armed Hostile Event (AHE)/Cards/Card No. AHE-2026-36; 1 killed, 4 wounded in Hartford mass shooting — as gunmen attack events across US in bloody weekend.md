(U) An early morning shooting on Wethersfield Avenue in Hartford left one person dead and four others wounded, prompting local concerns about community safety and police resources.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Incident:** Police responded to the scene shortly after 1:40 a.m. following reports of gunfire. Officers arrived to find a large crowd and a victim who was unresponsive.
- **Casualties:** One individual was pronounced dead at the scene (identified in local follow-up reports as a 32-year-old man), while four others sustained injuries and were transported to local hospitals.
- **Victim Conditions:** Among the four surviving victims, individuals were listed in stable or critical-but-stable conditions.
- **Investigation:** Authorities launched an active investigation into the shooting, appealing to the public and witnesses who were gathered in the area to come forward with any information or surveillance footage.
