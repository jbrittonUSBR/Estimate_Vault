(U) The Beaufort County Sheriff’s Office in South Carolina appealed to the public for assistance in identifying a person of interest linked to the July 4th mass shooting at Coligny Beach on Hilton Head Island.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Appeal:** Authorities released surveillance footage seeking information on an unidentified male seen in the area during the holiday incident. He was described as wearing a white T-shirt, black shorts, black socks, black Nike Air Force 1 Low sneakers, and carrying a small black backpack.
- **The Incident Context:** The investigation stems from a chaotic fight between two groups that broke out on the crowded public beach on July 4, resulting in an exchange of gunfire that left eight people with non-fatal injuries.
- **Arrests & Indictments:** Law enforcement previously arrested multiple suspects and secured grand jury indictments against several individuals on charges ranging from assault and weapons offenses to attempted murder. Investigators are continuing to comb through evidence and track down additional participants.
