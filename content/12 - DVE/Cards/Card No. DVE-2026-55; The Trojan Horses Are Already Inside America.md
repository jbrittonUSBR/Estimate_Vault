# The Trojan Horses Are Already Inside America

[Source](https://discernreport.com/the-trojan-horses-are-already-inside-america/)

(U) WASHINGTON — In a September 15, 2026 opinion column, the Discern Report's Candace O'Donnell described a House Foreign Affairs subcommittee hearing titled "Trojan Horses: Covert Foreign Malign Influence Operations in America," chaired by Rep. Bill Huizenga, which examined foreign funding of U.S. advocacy groups. The column focused on the network of Neville Roy Singham, citing 223 transactions totaling $591 million between 2017 and 2025, including roughly $278 million to $285 million routed to six nonprofit organizations such as the People's Support Foundation ($167.5 million), the Justice and Education Fund ($68.7 million), The People's Forum ($22.44 million), Tricontinental ($16.76 million) and CodePink ($1.33 million). It also cited a CodePink protest at the Institute of World Politics and claimed that the Party for Socialism and Liberation had participated in 21 anti-data-center campaigns across 14 states, delaying, shrinking or blocking about $23.6 billion in projects. The column said, citing the New York Post, that a Manhattan federal grand jury was examining the funding network; its characterizations of the groups and of a "covert influence" operation reflected the author's opinion and hearing testimony rather than established findings.
