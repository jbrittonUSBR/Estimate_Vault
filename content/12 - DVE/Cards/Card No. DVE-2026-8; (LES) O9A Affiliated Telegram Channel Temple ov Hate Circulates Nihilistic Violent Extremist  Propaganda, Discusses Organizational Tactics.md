(U//LES) Throughout December 2025 and January 2026, Temple ov Hate (TovH)—a Nihilistic Violent Extremist (NVE) chat group affiliated with the pseudo-Satanic, neo-Nazi movement Order of Nine Angles (O9A)—engaged in renewed activity in a newly established online messaging channel, following a period of dormancy. The new channel is comprised of several separate compartments including an “Announcements” section, a “Literature” collection, a “Sinister Music” library, and a “TovH Chatroom” with approximately 130 members, 1 in which users discuss NVE topics such as self-harm, extortion, animal torture, and share knowledge with one another relating to the pseudo-Occult ideological teachings of O9A. Consistent with other O9A cells, or “nexions,” the leader of TovH has also promoted neoNazi ideas, including calling for anti-government violence and targeted racial violence. In addition TovH’s leader has promoted a Roblox channel called “Satanic Revolt” in which users display O9A insignia on their avatars, and has previously claimed an alliance with NVE group No Lives Matter (NLM). 4 Based on analysis of the content shared within the TovH channel and discussions that have taken place in the associated Chatroom, the NYPD Intelligence and Counterterrorism Bureau (ICB) assesses that TovH is likely to attract individuals aligned with NVE spaces—such as the Com extortion network—and that ideological materials circulated by TovH could resonate with like-minded individuals who may be further radicalized and mobilized into acts of violence or sexual extortion.

- (U//LES) Members of the TovH Chatroom have discussed organization tactics for the purposes of carrying out acts of violence in furtherance of sowing societal chaos for chaos’ sake, consistent with O9A’s nihilistic ideology. On 25 January 2026, one user discussed wanting to “trample” on “mundane dross”—O9A terminology referring to average people who follow society’s moral norms—while carrying firearms. Earlier, on 30 December 2025, a different user discussed the benefits of organizing O9A nexions in densely populated urban areas, arguing that operational security is more difficult in rural locations. Members have also made reference to past REMVE attacks as inspiration; for example, one user asked “why there has been no meaningful work done since mcvey”—referring to 1995 Oklahoma City bomber Timothy McVeigh.
- (U//LES) A section of the TovH Telegram channel titled “Literature” contains PDFs of manifestos and manuals offering guidance on potentially violent/criminal activities such as armed insurrection and Leaderless Resistance (i.e., the formation of autonomous cells), and encourages terroristic bombings, attacks on critical infrastructure, and the assassination of perceived political enemies. Many of these texts contain references to neo-Nazi ideology and symbolism. For example, one manifesto titled “A Practical Guide to the Strategy and Tactics of Revolution”—which purports to be a re-issue of a text authored by former O9A leader David Myatt in the 1990s—begins by stating that “Aryan culture” and the “Aryan race” are in danger, and calls for “organized insurrection” in addition to assassination of Jewish people, judges, police officers, government officials, and media personalities. 6 TovH also circulates ideological texts associated with groups responsible for the sexual extortion of minors. Texts in this section were purportedly authored by Joshua Caleb Sutter, who is the founder of O9A nexion Temple ov Blood (ToB) in addition to two publishing houses—Martinet Press and Agony’s Point Press—through which he has distributed NVE literature depicting the rape and murder of children.
- (U//LES) The channel’s inaugural announcements claimed a “partnership” with NLM, and encouraged TovH members to join an NLM channel. On 5 December 2025, TovH circulated recruitment graphics referring to the partnership as “Brothers in Hate” and stating “F*ck Your Gods, F*ck Your Laws, We are the Deccian, International Terror.” Further inaugural posts presented TovH as a movement aiming to restore the nihilism at core of O9A’s ideology, and referenced 2026 as a year in which the group hopes to accomplish its “Revolutionary Satanism.”
- (U//LES) Members of TovH frequently discuss engaging in an O9A tactic known as an “Insight Role”—i.e., roleplaying or infiltrating a political ideology, or another cultural group, for the purposes of gaining knowledge and sowing discord. For example, in a group discussion on 1 December 2025, one user stated that his “main method” of sowing O9A’s influence “is to gain leadership in as many spaces as possible, and make them work for us without even knowing it.” Other users expressed agreement with this tactic, noting that “you have to be a natural spook to successfully undertake (an insight role).”
