# Four imprisoned Palestine Action activists appeal ‘terrorism’ sentences

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/3/four-imprisoned-palestine-action-activists-appeal-terrorism-sentences)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — Four Palestine Action activists — Charlotte Head, Samuel Corner, Leona Kamio, and Fatema Zainab Rajwani — who were sentenced as "terrorists" announced an appeal against their convictions and sentences. The four were found guilty of criminal damage "with a terrorist connection" following a raid claimed by Palestine Action on an Israeli arms factory in August 2024 in Filton, near Bristol, in the first categorization of property damage as "terrorism" in British courts. Head and Kamio received five-year sentences, Corner received seven years and eight months, and Rajwani, at 21 the youngest, received four years and eight months. Human rights groups condemned the original ruling, with supporters saying the sentences set a dangerous precedent.
