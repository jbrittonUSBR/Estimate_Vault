(U) An anonymous tip submitted through Florida's **FortifyFL** safety reporting platform led the St. Johns County Sheriff’s Office and federal law enforcement to arrest two middle school students who had amassed a cache of homemade firearms, explosive components, and white supremacist materials while conspiring to commit an armed robbery.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Investigation & Arrests:** Following a tip regarding communications on the Telegram app, deputies searched the homes of two students attending Switzerland Point Middle School—14-year-old Julian Davis and 13-year-old Charles Williams.
- **Weapons and Explosives Seized:** Working alongside the ATF, investigators recovered makeshift firearms constructed from hardware store items, ammunition, and raw chemical components (including homemade black powder, ETN, and urea nitrate) capable of producing high explosives.
- **Ideology and Manifestos:** Searches of the older teen's residence and digital devices uncovered a written manifesto, a fascination with mass shooters, and a tactical vest covered in neo-Nazi and white supremacist imagery, alongside literature promoting racist conspiracy theories like the "Great Replacement."
- **Charges Filed:** The 14-year-old was charged with multiple felonies, including conspiracy to commit robbery with a firearm, and is being tried as an adult. The 13-year-old faced a related conspiracy charge. While school officials noted there was no direct indication of a planned attack on the school itself, authorities credited the quick reporting tool with preempting a potentially violent incident.
