(U//FOUO) Ideological accelerationism promotes violence and disruption with the aim of accelerating the perceived inevitable demise of our current social, political, and economic systems, often by exacerbating social divisions, engaging in mass violence, and/or targeting critical infrastructure. Accelerationism does not exclusively align with a specific ideology and can be better understood as a collection of tactics aimed at strategic societal collapse. Often, accelerationism is associated with white supremacy groups who seek to tear down modern progressive society, incite what they perceive as an inevitable race war, and re-build the US as a white ethnostate. While white supremacist ideology and action has focused a lot on accelerationism in recent years, core principles of accelerationism can be traced back to Marxism and are thus embraced by some insurrectionary anarchists who may similarly pursue accelerationism to start a revolution and rebuild the US without the influence of capitalism, imperialism, and colonialism. 9,10 Most recently however, the rise of NVE groups who seek violence for the sake of violence has corresponded with an adoption of the use of accelerationism tactics not in pursuit of rebuilding society but in the interest of total societal collapse and human extinction. The pursuit of accelerationism goals and tactics by a variety of DVE groups represents the complexity of the current threat environment and emphasizes the risk of threat actors adopting nontraditional terrorist targets such as critical infrastructure and nontraditional tactics such as wildfires.
