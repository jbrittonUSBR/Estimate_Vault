(U) A California elementary school special education teacher faced a fierce public backlash and a formal police investigation after uploading social media videos of herself handling a firearm and talking about forming a "Black militia."
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Video and Statements:** The educator, identified as Kenisha Daily, posted clips online in which she appeared to load a gun, challenged viewers to "come find me," and stated she was starting a "Black militia" to rob white people.
- **School District Response:** Following public outrage, school officials swiftly placed the teacher on administrative leave while cooperating with local law enforcement to evaluate the potential security threat.
- **Police Investigation:** Local police opened an inquiry and visited the teacher's home.
- **The Teacher's Defense:** In subsequent interviews with local media, Daily defended the posts by claiming the video was intended as "entertainment." However, she later acknowledged her error in judgment, admitting, "I understand how it could be hurtful or concerning."
