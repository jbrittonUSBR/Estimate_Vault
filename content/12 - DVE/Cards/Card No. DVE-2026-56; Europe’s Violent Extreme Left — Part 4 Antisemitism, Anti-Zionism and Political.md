# Europe’s Violent Extreme Left — Part 4: Antisemitism, Anti-Zionism and Political Violence

[Source](https://www.counterextremism.com/blog/europes-violent-extreme-left-part-4-antisemitism-anti-zionism-and-political-violence)

(U) BERLIN — A Counter Extremism Project "CounterPoint" analysis by Alexander Ritzmann examined antisemitism and eliminationist anti-Zionism within violence-oriented extreme-left milieus in France, Germany, Greece, Italy and Spain, and the pathways by which such narratives can justify violence or contribute to target selection. It cited 1,320 antisemitic acts recorded in France in 2025, including 126 violent acts; in Germany, police recorded 437 Israel/Palestine-related left-wing offenses in 2024, up from 129 in 2023, while the RIAS monitoring group documented 501 left-wing anti-imperialist antisemitic incidents in its 2025 report, 94 percent of them Israel-related; Italy's CDEC recorded 963 incidents in 2025, and Spain's FCJE reported 221. The analysis noted the U.S. Treasury's August 2026 sanctions on Masar Badil and recommended that governments adopt a dedicated "violence-oriented antisemitic extremism" threat category and improve monitoring, prosecution and prevention. The findings and recommendations were the author's analysis for the Counter Extremism Project, a non-profit policy organization, rather than official government findings.
