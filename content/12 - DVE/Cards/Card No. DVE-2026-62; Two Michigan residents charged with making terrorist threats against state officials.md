# Two Michigan residents charged with making terrorist threats against state officials

[Source](https://michiganadvance.com/2026/09/21/two-michigan-residents-charged-with-making-terrorist-threats-against-state-officials/)

(U) LANSING, Michigan — Michigan Attorney General Dana Nessel announced charges against two residents in unrelated cases for making terrorist threats against public officials, including the mayor of Dearborn, the governor, the secretary of state, and members of the Michigan Supreme Court.
