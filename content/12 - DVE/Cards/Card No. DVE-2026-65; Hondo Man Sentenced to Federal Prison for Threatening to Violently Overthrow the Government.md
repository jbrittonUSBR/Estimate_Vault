# Hondo Man Sentenced to Federal Prison for Threatening to Violently Overthrow the Government

[Source](https://www.justice.gov/usao-wdtx/pr/hondo-man-sentenced-federal-prison-threatening-violently-overthrow-government)

(U) SAN ANTONIO — In a U.S. Department of Justice press release, the U.S. Attorney’s Office for the Western District of Texas announced that Jay Anthony Villarreal, 25, of Hondo, was sentenced to 27 months in federal prison followed by three years of supervised release for threatening a violent attack to overthrow the government. According to the release, Villarreal left Hondo on June 28, 2025, bound for Washington, D.C., with an AR-15-style rifle, a .45-caliber pistol and a 9mm pistol plus loaded magazines and ammunition, and used social media to try to recruit military veterans; a friend who saw the posts alerted the FBI, and he turned back near Beaumont, was arrested on July 2, 2025, and was convicted by a federal jury in June 2026. The account reflects the government’s characterization of the case and is not an independent finding.
