# The Accelerationist Case for Frontier Pacing

[Source](https://www.oreilly.com/radar/the-accelerationist-case-for-frontier-pacing/#BlogPosting)

(U) SAN FRANCISCO, United States — In an essay published on O'Reilly Radar and republished from Contraptions Substack, Venkatesh Rao argues that even self-identified accelerationists should favor "frontier pacing" over unrestricted acceleration of AI capabilities. Rather than relying on a lab cartel or export controls alone, he contends that competitive market and customer pressure can help pace frontier development. The essay frames the debate as a strategic question about governance rather than a binary choice between acceleration and deceleration.
