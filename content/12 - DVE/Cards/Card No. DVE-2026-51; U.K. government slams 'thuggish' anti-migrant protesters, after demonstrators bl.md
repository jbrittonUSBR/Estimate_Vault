# U.K. government slams 'thuggish' anti-migrant protesters, after demonstrators block port

[Source](https://www.cbc.ca/news/world/uk-migrant-crossing-protests-9.7334741)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — On September 7, 2026, the U.K. government condemned the "intimidating and thuggish behavior" of anti-immigrant demonstrators after a weekend of disruptive protests at ports and migrant-arrival sites. Hundreds of masked protesters in Portsmouth on September 6 blocked lifeboats and buses, while demonstrators in Dover blocked roads on September 5, causing a traffic jam of roughly 6.4 kilometers. Reporting by CBC News and the Associated Press stated that the demonstrations appeared to be led by Daniel Thomas, an ally of activist Tommy Robinson. The report also noted that small-boat crossings were down more than 40 percent compared with 2025, and that Reform UK leader Nigel Farage voiced support for peaceful protest while criticizing the masked, paramilitary-style dress of some participants.
