(U//FOUO) Overview: The 137th anniversary of Adolf Hitler's birthday will take place on 20 April 2026, a day celebrated by various RMVEs, including local white supremacist and neo-Nazi groups. In the past, Hitler's birthday has served as inspiration for white supremacist e vents, actions, and even mass casualty attacks to include one of the deadliest school shooting in history, the 1999 Columbine High School massacre. Although no specific local events have been planned for 2026, events celebrating 20 April have occurred locally and nationally over the past several years. The Anti-Defamation League (AOL) recorded an estimated 170 incidents of both antisemitic and white supremacist reports in Pennsylvania in 2025 and preliminary data indicates an additional 76 antisemitic incidents in the stale from January to June 2026. 
- (U) On 17 April 2025, a Florida State University political science student opened fire on campus using an old service weapon, killing two and injuring six. While officials have not publicly disclosed any motives for the attack, their peers stated the shooter has a fascination with Hitler and frequently espoused radical conspiracy theories and hateful ideas, even being removed from a student politics group as a result. Two days later on 19 April, university police uncovered a gun case containing three empty AK-style magazines, two grips, and a magazine loader in a men's restroom garbage can on campus, as well as a shopping bag of clothes underneath. Elsewhere in the same building they located additional hidden bags containing various electronics and books, including Hitler's Mein Kampf believed to belong to another subject.
- (U) On 21 April 2024, a Delaware-based RMVE group posted a video of a small group of people holding white supremacist banners and flags on a sidewalk in Wilkes-Barre, PA. The post claimed that 20 individuals from "all over the tristate" had gathered on 20 April to celebrate "the most important day of the year."
- (U) On and around 20 April 2024, several national and Pennsylvania-based RMVE groups posted videos and photos on Telegram showcasing activity to commemorate Hitler's birthday, including public banner hanging, flag waving, and flyer distribution.
- (U) On 20 April 2024, a series of racist digital billboards celebrating Hitler and containing white supremacist dog-whistle statements were displayed in several towns near Detroit, MI According to a local news outlet, a Michigan-based RMVE group wrote to them and claimed credit for the billboards.
- (U) In 2025, several international RMVE groups observed Hitler's birthday. Members of the far-right group Northwest British Movement hosted a gathering featuring Nazi memorabilia at a pub in Oldham, England, following which rune people were arrested on suspected public order offenses. Similarly, an unknown individual or group hung Nazi party banners over a tunnel on 20 April in  Stockholm, Sweden.

(UI/FOUO) **Analyst Comment:** At this time, the DVIC is unaware of any specific or credible threats of RMVE activity if the Philadelphia area on and around 20 April 2026. However, RMVEs may engage in demonstrative activity and/or criminal acts to pay tribute to neo-Nazism and Adolf Hitler on his birthday. In recent years,
RMVEs have remained active throughout the Philadelphia area by engaging in demonstrations, vandalisms, and extensive propaganda to promote the white race. Local RMVE groups and individuals have increasingly collaborated on actions, increasing numbers at events and their overall capability. Past incidents highlight the
potential for counter activity at RM-VE-organized events and demonstrations, potentially leading to confrontation and cross-ideological violence. Additionally, RMVEs- long actors in particular-have been known to carry out attacks on dates of significance to increase the psychological consequences associated with an attack. Attacks have varied from swatting, harassment, and assault to potential mass casualty attacks that feature the use of firearms, improvised explosive devices (IEDs), and vehicle ramming. RMVEs continue to express interest in targeting the Jewish community on social media platforms highlighting individuals, businesses, community centers, schools, cemeteries, and synagogues as potential targets.
