# Vestal man arrested on federal charge after alleged threats to kill Muslims to the FBI

[Source](https://www.wbng.com/2026/11/24/vestal-man-arrested-federal-charge-after-alleged-threats-kill-muslims-fbi/)

(U) VESTAL, NEW YORK — Robert John Lucas, 65, was arrested on November 21 on a federal charge of making threatening statements after allegedly calling the FBI and threatening to kill Muslims, according to WBNG. Court documents cited by the outlet said Lucas claimed to have explosives in his vest during calls to the FBI's National Watch Operations Center; CNY Central reported the complaint described threats involving a sword and dynamite. CAIR-NY said the threats targeted the Johnson City mosque and welcomed the arrest.
