# Florida Man Charged After FBI Says He Posted Videos Threatening to Attack Jewish People

[Source](https://www.tampafp.com/flamethrower-guns-and-hate-florida-man-busted-by-fbi-over-chilling-threat-videos/)

(U) SARASOTA, Florida — Federal prosecutors charged Duncan MacDonald of Sarasota with interstate transmission of a threat to injure after the FBI examined videos he posted to his Instagram story feed between September 16 and 17, according to a criminal complaint unsealed in the U.S. District Court for the Middle District of Florida and reported by the Tampa Free Press. The complaint states that the recordings showed MacDonald in a residential “hardware room” displaying tactical gear, ammunition and racks of firearms while saying “we’re over here gonna hunt Jews,” and that an acquaintance made screen recordings and reported them to law enforcement. Court records did not yet list defense counsel for MacDonald, and the allegations had not been tested at trial.
