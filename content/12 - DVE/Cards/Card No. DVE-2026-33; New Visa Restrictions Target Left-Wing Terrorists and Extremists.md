(U) Secretary of State Marco Rubio announced a new State Department visa-restriction policy aimed at foreign nationals affiliated with, financing, recruiting for, or otherwise enabling “far-left terrorist and aligned groups.” The policy seeks to block entry of individuals linked to left-wing extremist or terrorist networks that the administration says use organized violence and intimidation as political tools. Rubio argued that counterterrorism efforts have long overlooked political violence from the left while focusing on Islamist threats, and that this “blind spot” must end. The article frames the move as part of broader Trump administration efforts to address domestic left-wing extremism and foreign funding of it, citing past and recent incidents of political violence. Commentators quoted in the piece described the policy as a sensible prioritization of existing visa authorities to keep out those who would recruit, fundraise, or incite violence in the United States.
