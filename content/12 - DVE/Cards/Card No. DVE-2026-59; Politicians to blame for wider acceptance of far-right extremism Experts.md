# Politicians to blame for wider acceptance of far-right extremism: Experts

[Source](https://nltimes.nl/2026/09/23/politicians-blame-wider-acceptance-far-right-extremism-experts)

(U) THE HAGUE, Netherlands — Dutch experts warned that politicians bore responsibility for the growing acceptance of far-right extremism, arguing that failing to challenge terms such as "remigration" or "population replacement" had helped shift the boundaries of what was considered normal in public debate.
