# Dutch intelligence service warns far-right extremists are normalizing their ideas

[Source](https://nltimes.nl/2026/09/22/dutch-intelligence-service-warns-far-right-extremists-normalizing-ideas)

(U) THE HAGUE, Netherlands — The Dutch General Intelligence and Security Service (AIVD) and the National Coordinator for Security and Counterterrorism (NCTV) warned that far-right extremists were attempting to make their ideas more acceptable to a wider audience by presenting a "softer version" of their views, a strategy that could reduce resistance and potentially lead to justification for violence.
