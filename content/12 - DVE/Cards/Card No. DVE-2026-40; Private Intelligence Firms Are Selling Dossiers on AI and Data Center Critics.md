(U) A growing corporate surveillance trend involves private intelligence and security firms compiling detailed dossiers on grassroots activists, environmentalists, and local community leaders who oppose the rapid expansion of AI infrastructure and massive power-hungry data centers.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The New Surveillance Target:** As Big Tech corporations face mounting community pushback over the water consumption, grid strain, and land use of data centers, major tech players and real estate developers have increasingly turned to private intelligence contractors to monitor opposition.
- **Corporate Espionage Tactics:** Private intelligence firms deploy corporate tradecraft—ranging from monitoring social media and tracking activist networks to infiltrating local community meetings—to profile critics and map out potential legal or public relations vulnerabilities.
- **Silencing Local Resistance:** Critics and civil liberties advocates warn that these intelligence-gathering efforts are designed to intimidate local organizers, derail grassroots opposition, and preemptively counter community pushback against controversial industrial-scale tech projects.
- **The Privatization of Surveillance:** The report highlights a troubling convergence where private corporate entities utilize intelligence-gathering methods traditionally reserved for state security, bypassing public accountability and blurring the lines between corporate interests and civil liberties.
