(U) Political journalist John Fund, speaking with Sky News Digital Presenter Gabriella Power, criticized legacy media for disproportionately focusing on “right-wing violence” and “right-wing extremism” while making excuses for, or giving far less coverage to, left-wing extremism and violence. Fund argued that this double standard exists across countries, with left-wing acts of violence often downplayed, excused, or receiving delayed recognition of political motives (citing examples such as the murder of a former British cabinet minister and the killing of the UnitedHealthcare CEO). He said the left-wing side of the problem is “almost never noticed” by mainstream coverage.
