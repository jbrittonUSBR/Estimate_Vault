(U) The piece defends Secretary of State Marco Rubio’s focus on left-wing political violence and the July 2026 ministerial conference on the “Resurgence of Political Terrorism.” Fernandez argues that left-wing terrorism is not new and has occurred in historical waves:

- Late 19th/early 20th-century anarchist violence
- State-sponsored “Red Terror” under communist regimes
- 1970s revolutionary groups (Weather Underground, Red Brigades, Baader-Meinhof, Shining Path, etc.), often backed by foreign regimes

(U) He contends a third wave may be emerging, fueled by recent left-wing activism (including post-October 7 protests), with cross-pollination between radical leftists and anti-Western actors (e.g., American leftists attending events in Iran). He cites rising left-wing attacks/plots in the U.S., the growth of groups like the Democratic Socialists of America, and historical patterns in which idealistic agitation later produced violence. Fernandez concludes that the U.S. government is right to take the threat seriously now—working with allies to disrupt, defund, and prosecute far-left violent extremists—rather than waiting until it escalates further.
