(U) Newly unsealed court documents revealed that the Department of Homeland Security (DHS) conducted a sweeping, undercover surveillance operation—internally dubbed "Operation Puppet Master"—targeting progressive political organizations, non-profits, and major labor unions in Minnesota following protests against federal immigration enforcement.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Investigation's Scope:** Initiated shortly after high-profile immigration enforcement operations and subsequent protests in the Minneapolis–Saint Paul area, the DHS targeted groups it accused of supporting "violent opportunists and agitators."
- **Tactics Used:** Federal investigators deployed administrative subpoenas to pull years of banking and wire-transfer data from prominent entities, including the **Service Employees International Union (SEIU)**, the **Communications Workers of America (CWA)**, and the **Sunrise Movement**. Undercover agents also infiltrated community meetings in churches, libraries, and parks, and monitored private encrypted chat groups.
- **The "Conspiracy" Narrative:** Prosecutors and investigators linked several mainstream labor federations, socialist groups, and community non-profits into an overarching diagram presented to grand juries as an "antifa" network.
- **Backlash and Pushback:** Labor leaders and civil liberties advocates condemned the probe as an illegal, politically motivated overreach designed to intimidate organizations exercising their First Amendment rights. Legal pushback has also mounted, with some defense attorneys successfully challenging search warrants as lacking evidence of actual criminal conduct.
