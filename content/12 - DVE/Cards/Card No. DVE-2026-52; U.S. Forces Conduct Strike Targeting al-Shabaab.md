# U.S. Forces Conduct Strike Targeting al-Shabaab

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/library/news/2026/09/mil-260908-africom01.htm)

(U) QUUMBI, Somalia — In coordination with the Federal Government of Somalia, U.S. Africa Command (AFRICOM) conducted an airstrike targeting al-Shabaab on Sept. 5, 2026. The airstrike occurred in the vicinity of Quumbi, Somalia, approximately 75 km north-northwest of Kismayo. AFRICOM stated that, alongside the Federal Government of Somalia and Somali Armed Forces, it continues to take action to degrade al-Shabaab's ability to threaten U.S. forces and citizens abroad. The command said specific details about units and assets would not be released to ensure continued operations security. The information was released in an AFRICOM public affairs announcement.
