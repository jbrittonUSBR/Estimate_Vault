# From Al-Qaeda To Lone Wolves And AI: How Terrorism Has Changed Since 9/11

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/security/library/news/2026/09/sec-260908-rferl01.htm)

(U) WASHINGTON, D.C., United States — In an RFE/RL analysis marking 25 years since the September 11, 2001 attacks, terrorism experts described how the threat landscape has shifted from organized groups such as al-Qaeda toward lone actors radicalized online and the growing use of drones, AI and social media. The article cited the case of 25-year-old Afghan Farhad N., who was sentenced to life imprisonment for driving a car into a crowd in Munich, killing two people and injuring dozens, and who had no affiliation with a specific group. Former US diplomat and retired General Joseph Votel noted that "the environment is much more complex now for security experts," while experts also flagged a loss of counterterrorism capacity, with Tom Sanderson of CSIS citing a "reduction in focus on terrorism, the budgets, the personnel." The piece was written by RFE/RL's Ray Furlong and quotes multiple counterterrorism specialists.
