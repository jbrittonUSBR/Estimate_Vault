# Somalia announces killing of 285 al-Shabab fighters, including founding commanders

[Source](https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2026/Nov/206847/somalia_announces_killing_of_285_alshabab_fighters_including_founding_commanders__.aspx)

(U) MOGADISHU, SOMALIA — Outgoing Defence Minister Ahmed Moallim Fiqi said at a November 23 news conference that Somali forces had killed 285 al-Shabab fighters, including 12 senior commanders, in operations across Bay, Middle Shabelle, Lower Shabelle and Hiiraan regions, according to Hiiraan Online. He said Danab special forces and other army units, backed by air support from international partners, killed founding members of the group and commanders responsible for military operations, communications, logistics and intelligence. The casualty figures were government claims that had not been independently verified.
