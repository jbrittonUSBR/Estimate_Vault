(U) Drawing on German court proceedings and official police statistics (PMK-left), this report highlights a significant quantitative rise and qualitative shift in left-wing politically motivated violence across Germany.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **Surge in Violent Offenses:** Police-recorded left-wing violent crimes rose by **42.7%** in a single recent reporting year (reaching 1,087 offenses, up from 762), indicating a sharp escalation in aggressive tactics.
- **Targeting Law Enforcement & Political Opponents:** A substantial portion of the spike involved direct assaults and bodily-injury offenses targeting police officers (up 45.4%) as well as members and representatives of the right-wing populist party **Alternative for Deutschland (AfD)**.
- **Infrastructure Sabotage:** Clandestine networks utilizing labels like _Vulkangruppen_ have continued executing high-impact arson and sabotage attacks against critical infrastructure (such as power grids and heating lines), causing widespread disruption to transport, communications, and public services.
- **Transnational Networks:** Judicial actions against groups like _Antifa Ost_ revealed that decentralized extremist factions can organize into durable criminal organizations capable of cross-border coordination, targeted surveillance, and premeditation.
- **Converging Mobilizations:** German authorities note that extreme-left milieus have increasingly intersected with anti-capitalist, anti-militarist, climate activist, and anti-Israel/pro-Palestinian protest mobilizations following major geopolitical events.
