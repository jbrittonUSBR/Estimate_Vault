(U) **Legacy media blasted over making excuses for left-wing extremism** (Sky News Australia video/opinion segment, around July 22, 2026) In a Sky News Australia opinion segment, political journalist John Fund criticized legacy media for what he described as a pattern of making excuses for left-wing extremism while heavily covering right-wing violence. Speaking with Sky News Digital Presenter Gabriella Power, Fund said: “The legacy media in every country constantly refers to right-wing violence, right-wing extremism. There are lots of excuses made and lots of coverage that never occurs when a left-winger does something.” The short video segment (available on Sky News Australia and mirrored on platforms such as YouTube and Facebook) focuses on this perceived double standard in media coverage of political extremism and violence.
