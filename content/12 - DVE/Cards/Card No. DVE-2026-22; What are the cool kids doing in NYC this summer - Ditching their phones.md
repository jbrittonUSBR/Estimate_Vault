(U) The piece reports on a "burgeoning Luddite renaissance" in New York — an informal coalition of phone-free event organizers, self-described "attention activists," aligned nonprofits, and a few "not-smart phone" companies pushing back against pervasive smartphone/tech use. It centers on comedian Dan Fox, described as the figure behind the "Summer of Ludd" festival, a series of events aimed at getting people to disconnect from smartphones. The article quotes Fox saying he's running for president with "no platform," describing himself as wanting to be "the first IRL president of all time," and traces his motivation to a 2015 concert where he reportedly became disillusioned watching an audience experience a show through their phone screens rather than directly.
