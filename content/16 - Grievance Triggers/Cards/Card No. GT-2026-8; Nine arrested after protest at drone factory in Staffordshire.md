# Nine arrested after protest at drone factory in Staffordshire

[Source](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c699d2ry0p34o)

(U) Shenstone, UK — Nine people opposed to Israel's military action in Gaza were arrested after a protest at UAV Engines, a subsidiary of Israeli firm Elbit Systems, in Shenstone, Staffordshire, at about 06:40 BST on Monday. Four were held on suspicion of the charge of "lock on to person/object/land to cause serious disruption," and a 55-year-old man of no fixed address was arrested on suspicion of wilful obstruction on a highway, while four others were questioned on suspicion of both offences, Staffordshire Police said. The force said protest activity in the area had finished and inquiries were continuing.
