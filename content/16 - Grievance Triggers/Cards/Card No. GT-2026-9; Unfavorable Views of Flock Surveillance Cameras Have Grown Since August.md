# Unfavorable Views of Flock Surveillance Cameras Have Grown Since August

[Source](https://www.dataforprogress.org/blog/2026/9/23/unfavorable-views-of-flock-surveillance-cameras-have-grown-since-august)

(U) WASHINGTON — Data for Progress, a progressive polling firm, reported on September 23, 2026, that 52 percent of likely voters viewed Flock Safety automated license plate readers unfavorably, a net negative margin that widened from 19 points in August to 29 points. The survey of 1,194 likely voters, conducted September 18–20 with a margin of error of plus or minus 3 percentage points, also found awareness had risen, with 24 percent saying they had not heard enough to form an opinion, down from 37 percent. The group said the findings came as Flock faced a Senate Judiciary Committee hearing and after more than 200 cities and counties canceled or deactivated their contracts.
