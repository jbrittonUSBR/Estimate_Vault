# Why a mysterious Russian satellite is threatening to spark a space war

[Source](https://www.independent.co.uk/voices/russia-nuclear-bomb-starlink-space-war-weapons-ukraine-b3068139.html)

(U) October 19, 2026 — The Independent’s missile-defense expert Sidharth Kaushal wrote that Russia’s Cosmos 2612 satellite had approached within about three miles of the U.S.-Finnish ICEYE-X36 radar satellite, which supported Ukraine, according to satellite trackers. Kaushal argued that Russia’s perceived vulnerability to U.S. precision-strike and space-based surveillance could make counterspace operations central to its deterrence planning. He warned that a nuclear detonation in orbit was a possible, highly indiscriminate option, while noting it would violate the Outer Space Treaty and damage satellites across national and commercial systems.
