# US Data Centers to Burn More Natural Gas Than Most Nations

[Source](https://economiccollapse.report/us-data-centers-to-burn-more-natural-gas-than-most-nations/)

(U) NEW YORK — A September 15, 2026 Economic Collapse Report post, adapted from a Zero Hedge "Tyler Durden" item, cited a BloombergNEF outlook projecting that U.S. data centers will add about 15 billion cubic feet per day of natural gas demand over the decade to 2035, more than any country except China, Russia, Iran and the United States. The forecast was more than double BloombergNEF's December estimate of 6.9 bcf/d. The post said gas would supply 69 percent of the power for new grid-connected data centers, that power-sector gas demand would reach 54 bcf/d by 2035 — 18 bcf/d above 2025 — and that LNG exports would grow by 21 bcf/d, requiring producers to supply an additional 11 bcf/d. It also cited Wood Mackenzie's warning of the "end of cheap Henry Hub" and analyst Henry Eaton's caution about the forecast's wide error bars; the item was a curated aggregation of energy-industry analysis rather than original reporting.
