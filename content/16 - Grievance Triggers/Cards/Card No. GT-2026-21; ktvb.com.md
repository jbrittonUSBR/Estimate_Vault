# Cyberattack Forces Jerome School District to Cancel Monday and Tuesday Classes

[Source](https://www.ktvb.com/article/news/education/cyberattack-forces-jerome-school-district-cancel-monday-classes/277-8ac0744c-f888-4433-838e-6c36c050c65a)

(U) JEROME, Idaho — The Jerome School District canceled classes for all students on Monday, October 19, 2026, and again on Tuesday after being targeted by a cyberattack over the weekend that compromised its technology infrastructure. Reporting by KMVT indicated hackers gained control of some district networks and issued a ransom demand. The district reopened schools on Wednesday, October 20, following the weekend intrusion, which disrupted instruction and forced a multi-day closure.
