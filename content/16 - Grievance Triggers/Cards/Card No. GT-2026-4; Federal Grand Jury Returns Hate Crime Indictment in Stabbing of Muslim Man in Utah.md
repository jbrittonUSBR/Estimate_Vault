(U) A federal grand jury returned a hate crime indictment against **Peter Larsen**, a 48-year-old resident of Orem, Utah, following a targeted stabbing of a Muslim man at a mall.
### **Key Details of the Case**

- **The Attack:** The incident occurred on July 13 at the Valley Fair Mall (West Valley Mall) in West Valley City, Utah. Investigations revealed that Larsen harbored anti-Muslim views, went to the mall specifically to target Muslims, purchased a knife on-site, and questioned employees about their religion before attacking the victim.
- **The Victim's Injuries:** The victim, a Muslim mall worker, suffered multiple severe stab wounds (including to his neck, heart, and lungs) and required extensive surgeries. His life was saved when heroic bystanders intervened and subdued Larsen until police arrived.
- **Legal Charges:** Larsen was indicted federally for "willfully causing bodily injury to a victim because of his actual or perceived religion". Having been held in state custody since his initial arrest, he now faces severe federal charges that carry up to a life sentence upon conviction.
### **Broader Context**

(U) Justice Department officials emphasized a zero-tolerance policy for bias-motivated violence. Civil rights advocates and Muslim advocacy organizations highlighted the attack as a stark reminder of the real-world dangers tied to rising Islamophobia and anti-Muslim rhetoric.
