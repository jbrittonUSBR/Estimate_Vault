# Police Shooting of 1-Year-Old Kohen Wiley Was Justified, Mississippi Attorney General Says

[Source](https://www.mississippifreepress.org/police-shooting-of-1-year-old-kohen-wiley-was-justified-mississippi-attorney-general-says/)

(U) Senatobia, Mississippi — The Mississippi Free Press reported that Attorney General Lynn Fitch concluded a Senatobia police officer’s fatal shooting of one-year-old Kohen Wiley was justified. The child was shot while sitting in his mother’s lap during a police response, according to the report; the family’s attorney criticized the finding and called for release of body-camera footage. The conclusion was the attorney general’s official determination, which the family disputed.
