# Could Putin Use a Low-Yield Nuclear Bomb at Chernobyl to End the War with Ukraine?

[Source](https://blogs.timesofisrael.com/could-putin-use-a-low-yield-nuclear-bomb-at-chernobyl-to-end-war-with-ukraine/)

(U) JERUSALEM — A Times of Israel Blogs opinion piece examines the speculative scenario that Russian President Vladimir Putin could detonate a low-yield nuclear weapon at the Chernobyl site in an effort to force an end to the war with Ukraine. The commentary argues such a strike — against a location already contaminated by the 1986 reactor disaster — could be intended to signal nuclear resolve and generate radiological disruption without the full escalation of a strategic nuclear attack. The item is a blog/opinion contribution rather than a report of an operational event.
