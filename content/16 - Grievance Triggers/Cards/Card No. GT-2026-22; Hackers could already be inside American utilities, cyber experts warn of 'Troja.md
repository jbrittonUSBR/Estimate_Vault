# Hackers could already be inside American utilities, cyber experts warn of 'Trojan Horse' threat

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/tech/hackers-already-inside-american-utilities-cyber-experts-warn-trojan-horse-threat)

(U) October 18, 2026 — Fox News reported that unknown attackers remotely raised refrigeration temperatures at hundreds of French supermarkets, causing food losses, and cited cybersecurity experts who said the incident illustrated risks to U.S. critical infrastructure. Experts interviewed by Fox said older operational-technology equipment in utilities and food, pharmaceutical, and oil facilities could be exposed; John Walsh of IGEL warned that adversaries might already have access to U.S. systems. The article presented these as expert assessments and did not report evidence of a current compromise at American utilities.
