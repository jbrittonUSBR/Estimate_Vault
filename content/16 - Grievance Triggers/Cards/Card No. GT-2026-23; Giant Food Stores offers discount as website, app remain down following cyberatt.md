# Giant Food Stores offers discount as website, app remain down following cyberattack

[Source](https://lancasteronline.com/news/local/giant-food-stores-enters-second-week-with-website-app-still-down-following-cyberattack/article_e967ec80-b4ab-4566-b11a-b1bb1b56b157.html)

(U) October 19, 2026 — LancasterOnline reported that Giant Food Stores offered customers a 10% discount, up to $10, while its website and app remained unavailable during the second week of a cyber incident affecting parent company Ahold Delhaize. Company spokesperson Ashley Flower said the offer was being sent by email, expiring points would be extended, and digital coupons were being automatically activated; Ahold Delhaize said it had reported the incident to Dutch police and had no evidence that customer, associate, or partner data had been taken. Stores remained open, while the company said restoration work continued.
