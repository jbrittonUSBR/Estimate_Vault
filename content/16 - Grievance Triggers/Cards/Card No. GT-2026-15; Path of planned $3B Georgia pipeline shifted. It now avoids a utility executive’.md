# Path of planned $3B Georgia pipeline shifted. It now avoids a utility executive’s house.

[Source](https://www.ajc.com/business/2026/09/path-of-planned-3b-georgia-pipeline-shifted-it-now-avoids-a-utility-executives-house/)

(U) ATLANTA — Southern Company shifted the route of its planned $3 billion Southern System Expansion 4 gas pipeline so that it no longer crosses property owned by Chris Lane, a 25-year veteran manager at a Southern Co. power plant, the Atlanta Journal-Constitution reported September 30, 2026. The revised path crosses only land belonging to Donnie Peek in Fortson, Georgia, adding about 1,400 feet of pipe and millions of dollars in cost. Peek has a rehearing request pending before the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, and Southern Natural Gas denies that the change was made to favor an executive. The episode highlighted how routing decisions for major energy infrastructure can become entangled with the personal interests of utility employees.
