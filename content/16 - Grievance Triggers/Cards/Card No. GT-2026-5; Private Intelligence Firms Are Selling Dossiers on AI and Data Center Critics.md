# Private Intelligence Firms Are Selling Dossiers on AI and Data Center Critics

[Source](https://prospect.org/2026/08/10/private-intelligence-firms-selling-dossiers-on-ai-data-center-critics/)

(U) WASHINGTON — The American Prospect reported that private intelligence firms were selling dossiers on critics of artificial intelligence and the data centers powering it, including pitches to federal regulators. According to documents obtained by the magazine, the corporate intelligence firm RANE Network offered the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission a report on the threat posed by anti-technology sentiment, and Kroll and the Securitas subsidiary Liferaft marketed similar "risk intelligence" on activism and online hostility toward AI companies. The reporting noted the activity coincided with federal agencies circulating surveillance memorandums on a new domestic extremism category termed "anti-tech extremism."
