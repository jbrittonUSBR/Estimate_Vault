# Big Tech’s Plans for AI Are Bringing Nuclear Power Back to New England

[Source](https://www.nakedcapitalism.com/2026/10/big-techs-plans-for-ai-are-bringing-nuclear-power-back-to-new-england.html)

(U) LOWELL, United States — New England governors met at UMass Lowell on October 1, 2026, to develop a regional strategy for advanced nuclear power, as supporters of Massachusetts' 1982 voter-approved nuclear referendum law protested outside; Governor Maura Healey has proposed repealing the law's requirement for a statewide vote before a new plant is built. In a reposted Common Dreams opinion essay, guest author Tom Valovic argued that the push is driven by Big Tech's demand for AI data-centre power and aligns with the Trump administration's $17.5 billion programme to speed reactor construction, calling the "zero carbon" framing a marketing claim rather than a safety case.
