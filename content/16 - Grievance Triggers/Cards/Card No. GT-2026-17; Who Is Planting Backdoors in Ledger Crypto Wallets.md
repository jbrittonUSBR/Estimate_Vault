# Who Is Planting Backdoors in Ledger Crypto Wallets?

[Source](https://www.zetter-zeroday.com/who-is-planting-backdoors-in-ledger-crypto-wallets/)

(U) SAN FRANCISCO, United States — Security researchers reported discovering suspected backdoors in firmware or update mechanisms tied to Ledger crypto wallets, prompting an investigation into the source and scope of potential compromises affecting user funds. The incidents raised concerns about supply-chain attacks targeting hardware wallet manufacturers, though the identity of those responsible had not been publicly confirmed at the time of reporting.
