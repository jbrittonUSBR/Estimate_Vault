# US federal court work hit after BAE Systems cyberattack affects bankruptcy court systems

[Source](https://www.yahoo.com/news/us/articles/us-federal-court-hit-bae-151500369.html)

(U) October 15, 2026 — The Administrative Office of the U.S. Courts said a cybersecurity incident affecting BAE Systems’ information technology disrupted the Bankruptcy Noticing Center, which produces and transmits notices for federal bankruptcy courts nationwide. The office said BNC systems, websites, and the related noticing process were unavailable and that it was coordinating with affected courts and BAE Systems. BAE was investigating and working to restore operations; the notice did not identify the attack method or report data exposure.
