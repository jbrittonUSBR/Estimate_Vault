# Cyberattack on French supermarkets tells us more about food security than AI safety

[Source](https://www.politico.eu/article/cyberattack-french-supermarkets-food-security-ai-safety/)

(U) October 19, 2026 — POLITICO’s Sam Clark and Ketrin Jochecová reported that attackers remotely raised refrigerator temperatures at hundreds of French supermarkets, spoiling food, while their identities and motives remained unknown. The journalists cited experts who said exposed operational-technology devices and basic cybersecurity failures—not necessarily advanced AI—were likely central to the incident; they also noted that AI could make cyberattacks easier to scale. The analysis described the attack as a warning for food security and other critical sectors, while quoting industry representatives who said supply chains remained resilient.
