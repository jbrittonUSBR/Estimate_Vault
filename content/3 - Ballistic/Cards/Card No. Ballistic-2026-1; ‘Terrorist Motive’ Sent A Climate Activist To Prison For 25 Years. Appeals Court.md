# ‘Terrorist Motive’ Sent A Climate Activist To Prison For 25 Years. Appeals Court Says Not So Fast

[Source](https://dailycaller.com/2026/09/02/eighth-circuit-cameron-smith-sentence-climate-terrorism-25-years)

(U) ST. LOUIS — A three-judge panel of the Eighth Circuit Court of Appeals on September 2, 2026 vacated the 25-year sentence of Canadian national Cameron Smith, who had been convicted of shooting energy infrastructure in South Dakota and North Dakota, according to The Daily Caller. The panel found that the lower court improperly applied a "terrorist motive" sentencing enhancement when it sentenced Smith in March 2025 to 150 months per count by US District Judge Daniel Traynor. Smith's attacks included a 2022 strike on a Keystone pipeline transformer and pump station and a 2023 attack on a substation near Ray, North Dakota, each causing more than $100,000 in damage. The case was remanded for resentencing.
