# Zaporizhzhia nuclear plant loses external power supply

[Source](https://www.rte.ie/news/2026/1108/1598404-ukraine-russia/)

(U) Zaporizhzhia region, Ukraine — RTÉ reports that the Russian-installed management of the Zaporizhzhia nuclear plant said its external supply was lost after a damaged power line was disconnected by automatic protection systems. The management said backup diesel generators were powering equipment needed for safe operation and that radiation readings at the site and monitoring zone were normal; RTÉ also reported separate Russian strikes had caused outages in the Dnipropetrovsk and Kharkiv regions.
