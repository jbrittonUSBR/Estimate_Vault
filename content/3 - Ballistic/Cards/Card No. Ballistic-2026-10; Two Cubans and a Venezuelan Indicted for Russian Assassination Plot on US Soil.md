# Two Cubans and a Venezuelan Indicted for Russian Assassination Plot on US Soil

[Source](https://military.com/two-cubans-and-a-venezuelan-indicted-for-russian-assassination-plot-on-us-soil)

(U) NEW YORK — A federal grand jury in Brooklyn indicted five individuals for a Russian intelligence-backed plot to assassinate a U.S.-based dissident on American soil. Prosecutors said Russian operatives Yuri Khrameev and Kirill Pak recruited Cuban and Venezuelan nationals, offering $1,500 upfront with a promise of $40,000, and the network had also offered money for similar plots in Lithuania. All defendants remain at large in Russia and face up to 20 years in prison if convicted.
