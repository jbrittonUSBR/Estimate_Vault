# Houthis blame Saudi Arabia for another strike on Sanaa's airport

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/middle-east/houthis-blame-saudi-arabia-for-another-strike-on-sanaas-airport/articleshow/134872073.cms)

(U) SANA'A, Yemen — Houthi-aligned media reported multiple airstrikes by the Saudi-led coalition on Houthi-controlled areas in Yemen, including Sanaa International Airport and the Harf Sufyan district of Amran governorate. No immediate comment came from Saudi Arabia, though the coalition had vowed a firm response to a prior October 10 strike on Sanaa airport that it blamed on the Houthis and described as a war crime. The Houthis renewed warnings against use of Saudi airspace by airlines and travelers except for Mecca and Medina airports, signaling a tit-for-tat escalation between the parties.
