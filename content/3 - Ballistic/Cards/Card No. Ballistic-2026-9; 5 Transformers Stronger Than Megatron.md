# 5 Transformers Stronger Than Megatron

[Source](https://screenrant.com/transformers-stronger-megatron/)

(U) NEW YORK, United States — An entertainment feature by Emedo Ashibeze published by Screen Rant on September 10, 2026, ranks five Transformers characters that it argues outclass Megatron in power. The opinion piece lists Metroplex, a Titan-class Autobot whose alternate form is a city; Quintessa, the self-proclaimed "Prime of Life" and creator of Cybertron who manipulates events and brainwashed Optimus Prime; Optimus Prime, the Autobot leader who has defeated Megatron repeatedly; Unicron, a planet-devouring god of chaos; and Primus, the creator deity of the Transformers. The article asserts that Megatron's strength is eclipsed by these godlike beings, from the creator god of Cybertron to planet-devouring entities and elite Primes.
