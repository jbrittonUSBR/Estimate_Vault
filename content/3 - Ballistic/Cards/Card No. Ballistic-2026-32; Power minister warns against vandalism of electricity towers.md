# Power minister warns against vandalism of electricity towers

[Source](https://punchng.com/power-minister-warns-against-vandalism-of-electricity-towers/)

(U) Bauchi, Nigeria — Nigeria's Minister of Power, Joseph Tegbe, said Friday at the commissioning of an upgraded transmission substation in Bauchi that people were "pulling down towers and blowing up towers," imposing costs through interrupted trade and electricity supply. The facility was converted from a 132/33kV to a 330/132/33kV station; Transmission Company of Nigeria managing director Sule Abdulaziz said the World Bank-funded project was completed in June 2026 and would benefit about 64,800 additional people.
