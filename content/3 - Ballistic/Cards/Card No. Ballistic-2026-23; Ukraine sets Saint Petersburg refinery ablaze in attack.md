# Ukraine sets Saint Petersburg refinery ablaze in attack

[Source](https://www.rte.ie/news/ukraine/2026/1018/1595862-ukraine-russia-conflict/)

(U) SAINT PETERSBURG, Russia — Ukraine hit Saint Petersburg with a large-scale drone attack, causing fires at an oil refinery and depot, Moscow said, RTÉ reported on October 18, 2026. Saint Petersburg governor Alexander Beglov said one person was killed and five hospitalized, and that authorities were fighting fires at the city's oil terminal, the Ruchyi oil depot and industrial warehouses; 12 housing blocks were damaged and flights were restricted at Pulkovo airport. Leningrad region head Alexander Drozdenko said air defenses shot down 144 Ukrainian drones, with state media calling it the largest attack on the city since the 2022 war began, and said 20,000 people were left without power in the town of Yanino after a substation was hit. Russian outlets said the VKontakte social network's data center and a logistics center of retailer Ozon were also hit. The strike came amid strain in Kyiv's ties with Washington over U.S. President Donald Trump's "energy ceasefire," which collapsed; Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky accused Russia of striking Ukrainian energy facilities 76 times since the ceasefire was announced.
