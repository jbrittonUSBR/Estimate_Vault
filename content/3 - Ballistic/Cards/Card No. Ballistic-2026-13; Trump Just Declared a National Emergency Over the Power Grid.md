# Trump Just Declared a National Emergency Over the Power Grid

[Source](https://activistpost.com/trump-just-declared-a-national-emergency-over-the-power-grid/)

(U) WASHINGTON — President Donald Trump signed Executive Order 14420 on August 26, 2026, declaring a national emergency over foreign-produced bulk-power system equipment, including transformers, circuit breakers, inverters and industrial control systems. The order, issued under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act and the National Emergencies Act, lets the energy secretary block imports and require the identification, isolation, removal or replacement of equipment already installed if it comes from a "Covered Foreign Entity." The Activist Post, an alternative site that often promotes conspiracy theories, presented the measure as a revival of a 2020 grid order that was later suspended and warned that it did not name China explicitly.
