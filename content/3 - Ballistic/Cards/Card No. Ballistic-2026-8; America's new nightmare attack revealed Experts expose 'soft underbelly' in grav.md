# America's new nightmare attack revealed: Experts expose 'soft underbelly' in grave danger and unveil 'Everything, Everywhere, All at Once' assault more devastating than 9/11

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16085301/america-cyberattack-infrastructure-new-york.html)

(U) NEW YORK — Cybersecurity and infrastructure experts warned that a coordinated attack combining hidden malware against water and power systems with drone strikes on electrical equipment could produce cascading chaos across the United States, in a Daily Mail analysis published on September 12, 2026. Annie Fixler of the Foundation for Defense of Democracies told the outlet that cities such as New York and Los Angeles were highly vulnerable and that the loss of water and wastewater systems could make a city uninhabitable within hours. The report cited a July and August 2026 wave of cyber-physical attacks on water utilities in at least a dozen states, the Volt Typhoon campaign attributed to China, and physical sabotage including the 2013 Metcalf substation shooting and the 2022 Duke Energy attacks in North Carolina. Former Federal Energy Regulatory Commission chairman Jon Wellinghoff warned that disabling nine critical high-voltage substations could trigger a nationwide blackout lasting up to 18 months.
