# Power restored to Lagos, Ogun, four others after vandalism — Minister

[Source](https://punchng.com/power-restored-to-lagos-ogun-four-others-after-vandalism-minister/)

(U) Lagos, Nigeria — The Punch reports that Power Minister Joseph Tegbe said electricity had been restored to affected areas in Lagos, Ogun, Oyo, Kwara, and the Federal Capital Territory after repair of the vandalized Benin–Egbin 330-kilovolt line. Tegbe said supply to the Lagos Complex had risen above 1,000 megawatts, while repairs to another damaged transmission route were still planned; the report notes that generation and gas constraints continued to affect reliability.
