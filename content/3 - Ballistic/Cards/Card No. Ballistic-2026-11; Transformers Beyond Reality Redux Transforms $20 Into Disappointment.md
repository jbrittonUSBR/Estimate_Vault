# Transformers: Beyond Reality Redux Transforms $20 Into Disappointment

[Source](https://www.uploadvr.com/transformers-beyond-reality-redux-transforms-20-into-disappointment/)

(U) SAN FRANCISCO — Quest VR port Transformers: Beyond Reality Redux, developed by META4 Interactive and released September 10, 2026 for $19.99, drew a tepid 2.5/5 review from critics who called it visually downgraded from its original version and bland across its six stages. Reviewers said the $20 title offered little replay value, transforming a modest purchase into a disappointment for fans of the franchise.
