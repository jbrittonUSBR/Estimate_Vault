# Warning that mammoth nationwide blackout could last 18 MONTHS as power grid's appalling weak spots exposed

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16062409/nationwide-blackout-18-months-power-grid.html)

(U) WASHINGTON — Former Federal Energy Regulatory Commission chairman Jon Wellinghoff warned in an August 19, 2026 Daily Mail report that a coordinated attack on the US power grid could cause a nationwide blackout lasting up to 18 months. Wellinghoff cited a power-flow analysis finding that disabling nine critical substations — the top ten in each of the three national interconnections — could black out the entire country. The report referenced the 2013 sniper attack on a Pacific Gas & Electric substation in San Jose, California. Mark Lauby of the North American Electric Reliability Corporation called a large cascading blackout "unlikely."
