# Ukraine Provoking Nuclear Incident at Zaporozhye Plant - Rosatom CEO

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/ukraine/2026/09/ukraine-260913-sputnik05.htm)

(U) MOSCOW, Russia — Rosatom CEO Alexey Likhachev, in remarks carried by Sputnik and aggregated by GlobalSecurity.org, accused Ukraine of deliberately targeting fuel tankers delivering emergency diesel to the Zaporozhye Nuclear Power Plant in strikes on September 11. He said two Russian servicemen were killed and several wounded near the plant's perimeter, and demanded that the IAEA firmly condemn the attack and name those responsible. The statement is a Russian state-media claim.
