# NSCDC nabs suspect with stolen cables, transformer oil

[Source](https://punchng.com/nscdc-nabs-suspect-with-stolen-cables-transformer-oil/)

(U) Janguza, Kano State, Nigeria — The Punch reports that the Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps arrested a 30-year-old man suspected of vandalizing a transformer at a checkpoint in Janguza, Tofa Local Government Area. Citing the Kano command, the paper says officers recovered cable, tools, and 25 litres of transformer oil, with the damaged components valued at about ₦2 million; the suspect remained in custody for investigation and prosecution.
