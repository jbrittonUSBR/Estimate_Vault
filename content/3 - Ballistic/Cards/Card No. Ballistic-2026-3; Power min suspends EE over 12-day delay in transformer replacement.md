# Power min suspends EE over 12-day delay in transformer replacement

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/lucknow/power-min-suspends-ee-over-12-day-delay-in-transformer-replacement/articleshow/133014808.cms)

(U) LUCKNOW, India — Uttar Pradesh energy minister Arvind Kumar Sharma ordered disciplinary action on August 6, 2026 over delays in replacing damaged transformers, The Times of India reported. The executive engineer of the Ghazipur Gahmar division was suspended after a burnt transformer went unreplaced for 12 days, and an adverse entry was ordered against the chief engineer of Gorakhpur II over a 10-day delay in Deoria. Sharma ordered a review of transformer replacement across Deoria, Ghazipur, Shravasti, Bahraich and Fatehpur, and also reviewed preparations for the Kanwar Yatra.
