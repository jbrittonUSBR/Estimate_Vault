# Transformer-aided guard: A hybrid spatio-temporal framework for confidence-aware intrusion detection in cyber-physical systems

[Source](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0361820)

(U) No specific location — Abeer Aljohani’s PLOS ONE study proposes Transformer-Aided Guard, a system combining transformer-based temporal modeling, graph-based spatial analysis, and Bayesian uncertainty estimates to detect intrusions in cyber-physical systems. The paper reports evaluation across four benchmark datasets and an average accuracy of 98%, presenting the framework as a potential approach for monitoring critical infrastructure and IoT environments.
