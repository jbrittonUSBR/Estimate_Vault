# Houthis are testing the limits of collective defense

[Source](https://www.wnd.com/2026/10/houthis-are-testing-limits-collective-defense/)

(U) October 18, 2026 — In this opinion article, republished by WND from RealClearDefense, researcher Saima Afzal argued that a reported drone strike on an electricity facility in Medina exposed uncertainty about the Saudi-Türkiye-Pakistan defense framework. Afzal noted that the Saudi-led coalition attributed damage to a transformer supplying the Prophet’s Mosque to the strike, while the Houthis denied responsibility. She contended that alliance members needed to define how far collective-defense commitments extended before defensive cooperation or maritime protection could draw them into Yemen’s conflict.
