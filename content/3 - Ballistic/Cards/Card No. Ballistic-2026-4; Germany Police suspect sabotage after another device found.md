# Germany: Police suspect sabotage after another device found

[Source](https://www.dw.com/en/germany-police-suspect-sabotage-after-another-device-found/live-78896707)

(U) BERLIN, Germany — German police investigated a series of suspected acts of sabotage against the country's power grid in early September 2026, including the discovery of suspicious objects near substations. On Friday, September 4, authorities said further explosive devices had been found close to two electrical substations in the eastern state of Saxony, with police securing four objects at two locations for defusal. Earlier that day, armed officers from Germany's elite SEK unit stormed a truck in the village of Niederzier, in North Rhine-Westphalia, believed to belong to a suspect who had fled on foot. Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt said authorities suspected "climate extremism," citing confession letters from a 48-year-old suspect who cited the "fight against fossil fuels" as his motive.
