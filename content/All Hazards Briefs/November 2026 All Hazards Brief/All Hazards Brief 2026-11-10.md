# All Hazards Brief 2026-11-10

DATAMINR ALERT REPORT

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Dataminr alerts indicate two separate US public safety incidents on 9 November, both at ALERT priority, involving a possible magnetic explosive device found in Orosi, California and a second detention near Lafayette Park in Washington, DC, underscoring localized but potentially sensitive security concerns in transportation and government-adjacent areas.¹˒²

## DETAIL

In California, police responded to a possible magnetic explosive device found on a stop sign at the intersection of CA-63 and Avenue 419 in Orosi, indicating a potential explosive hazard at a roadside location.¹

In Washington, DC, the US Secret Service said a second individual was detained after jumping a barricade near the northwest corner of Lafayette Park around the same time as an incident involving an individual in a vehicle, suggesting a concentrated security response near a protected area.²

The two incidents are not linked in the reporting, but both involve rapid law enforcement action in publicly accessible spaces with possible implications for perimeter security and public safety.¹˒²

## IMPACT

The California incident could disrupt local movement and require explosive ordnance or investigative follow-up if the device is determined to be viable.¹ The Washington incident points to continued sensitivity around access control and perimeter enforcement near Lafayette Park.²

Taken together, the reporting supports heightened awareness for short notice security incidents in both routine transit corridors and high visibility government-adjacent locations. Multiple reports do not exist here, so the broader pattern remains limited and should be treated cautiously.

Near term outlook will depend on whether authorities confirm the nature of the device in Orosi and whether additional official details emerge on the circumstances of the detentions in Washington. Indicators to watch include road closures, bomb squad activity, further Secret Service statements, and any notice of expanded security perimeters.
