# Bihar gas pipeline leak: One dead, six injured as PNG leak triggers blast in Muzaffarpur

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/india/bihar-gas-pipeline-leak-one-dead-six-injured-as-png-leak-triggers-blast-in-muzaffarpur/articleshow/135461393.cms)

(U) Muzaffarpur, Bihar, India - The Times of India reported that a piped-natural-gas leak caused an explosion in the Gobarsahi-Dumri area, killing one person and injuring six. Witnesses said a construction excavator had dug near the underground pipeline; the blast damaged a four-storey building and shattered nearby windows.
