# Natural gas pipeline fire near Ward, Alabama, prompts shutdown with no injuries reported, operator says - CBS News

[Source](https://slashdot.org/firehose.pl?op=view&id=186834593)

(U) Ward, Alabama — Southern Natural Gas Company said a natural gas release and fire on a pipeline segment near Ward occurred shortly before 11:30 p.m. on November 21, sending flames dozens of yards above the treeline and drawing reports of the glow from across the Mississippi state line. The company said it shut down and depressurized the affected section, that responders extinguished the blaze, and that no one was injured and customer deliveries were unaffected while the cause remained under investigation. The Pleasant Hill Volunteer Fire Department said several volunteer crews responded after midnight to what it described as a major pipeline explosion that sparked a secondary brush fire, and the mayor of nearby York urged residents to stay clear of the area.
