# Possible pipe bomb found at Irvine gas station, 2 arrested

[Source](https://ktla.com/news/orange-county/possible-pipe-bomb-found-at-irvine-gas-station-2-arrested/)

(U) IRVINE, Calif. — Irvine police officers responding to a suspicious-circumstances report at a Chevron gas station on Portola Parkway arrested two men on drug possession and an outstanding warrant, then found what appeared to be a pipe bomb in their car, the department said. The Orange County Sheriff's Department bomb squad spent about two hours examining and rendering the device safe, and a nearby shopping center was evacuated as a precaution. No injuries were reported.
