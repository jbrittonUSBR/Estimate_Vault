(U) Manhattan Beach police and emergency response teams investigated a residential neighborhood following the discovery of a potential explosive device inside a home.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **Location:** The police activity and investigation centered on the 1100 block of Chestnut Avenue, located near Manhattan Beach Boulevard.
- **The Response:** Authorities deployed the Los Angeles County Sheriff’s Department bomb squad to the scene to examine the suspicious item.
- **Precautions:** As a safety measure, police implemented partial evacuations of nearby residents and shut down portions of local roadways—including westbound Manhattan Beach Boulevard at Meadows Avenue and adjacent residential streets—while crews cleared and investigated the area.
- **Resolution:** The active threat investigation and road closures were lifted later that afternoon after authorities secured the scene.
