(U) An explosion occurred Monday morning outside 26 Federal Plaza in lower Manhattan (which houses FBI offices and an ICE field office). Authorities said a man threw or set off an incendiary device (described as fireworks in a cart with an “ICE out of our streets” sign), causing a fire and smoke. Three people were reported injured (two required hospital treatment). The suspect, identified as Andrew Arrabaca (a U.S. Army veteran), was taken into custody. The FBI described him as coming “armed with bad intentions” and recovered multiple weapons (axes, knives, hammer, machete, and additional incendiary devices). The Joint Terrorism Task Force is investigating. The incident took place amid heightened tensions over immigration enforcement and recent protests against ICE.
