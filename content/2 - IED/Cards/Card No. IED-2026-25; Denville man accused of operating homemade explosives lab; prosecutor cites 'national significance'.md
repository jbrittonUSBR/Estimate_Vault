(U) Joseph Rizos, a 37-year-old Denville, New Jersey, resident, was arrested on June 24, 2026, and charged with one count of recklessly risking widespread injury and four counts of possession of destructive devices after a months-long investigation revealed he had been operating a homemade explosives lab at his Avondale Road home. Authorities executed a search warrant following a vehicle stop, uncovering a laboratory with explosive precursors, blasting caps, cell phones, firearms, and ammunition, plus approximately 109 containers of suspected homemade explosives stored in a shed without the required permit from the New Jersey Department of Labor and Workforce Development. Nearby homes were temporarily evacuated as a precaution during the operation, which involved multiple local, state, and federal agencies including the FBI and DEA. Morris County Prosecutor Robert Carroll described the case as involving “matters of national significance” in Northern New Jersey, though investigators have not disclosed the defendant’s alleged intent while the probe continues. Rizos remains jailed pending a detention hearing and is presumed innocent unless proven guilty.
