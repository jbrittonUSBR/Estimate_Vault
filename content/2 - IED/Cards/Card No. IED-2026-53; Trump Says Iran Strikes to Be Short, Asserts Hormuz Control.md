# Trump Says Iran Strikes to Be Short, Asserts Hormuz Control

[Source](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/09/03/883847.htm)

(U) WASHINGTON — President Donald Trump said on September 3, 2026 that renewed US strikes on Iran would be "not too long" and asserted that the United States controls the Strait of Hormuz, according to a Bloomberg report published by Insurance Journal. The remarks followed a second round of US strikes in three days that targeted radar and mine-laying sites along Iran's southern coast. Iran retaliated with drone and missile volleys against US bases, and Tehran accused the US of striking a wedding in Sirik, where the Red Crescent reported at least four dead and about 67 injured. Brent crude slipped 0.6% to $95.10.
