(U//FOUO) DHS. "Honduran National Emplaced Improvised Explosive Devices at National Monument and Future World Cup Fan Festival Location in Missouri, February 2026." Serial: IIR 4 007 S0253 26, March 2026. HSIN. Accessed April 3, 2026. 

(U//FOUO) On 23 February 2026 at 1115 hours central time, Honduran national Owen Isaac ((Canizalas Argueta)), DOB 03 September 2003, emplaced 20 M-device style improvised explosive devices at the National World War I memorial in Kansas City, Missouri. Canizalas Argueta arrived in the vicinity approximately 45 minutes prior, loitered in the area before moving their vehicle to the west side of the parking. Canizalas Argueta then exited their vehicle with a red-pink plastic bag containing the explosive devices, placed them in the trash can on the west side of the parking lot, and left the area in their vehicle. At the time when Canizalas Argueta emplaced the explosive devices, multiple school-aged children were present outside of the memorial, and at least one school bus passed directly by the trash can. Immediately after leaving the World War One memorial Canizalas Argueta traveled to a fast-food restaurant then traveled by car out of state to Utah (NFI). After traveling to Utah, Canizalas Argueta returned to Missouri on 3 March 2026 and was taken into custody.
(U//FOUO) The explosive devices were found by janitorial staff at approximately 1435 on 23 February 2026, and safely recovered without incident by law enforcement. 
(U//FOUO) The 20 explosives were m-device style explosives, constructed with heavy red cardboard tubes and cardboard end caps. Each device measured 6 inches in length and approximately 1.25 inches in diameter and had a dark colored piece of pyrotechnic hobby fuse protruding from a centered priming hole.
(U//FOUO) The Kansas City World War One memorial is the future site for the Kansas City 2026 FIFA World Cup Fan Festival. Prior to this incident, Canizalas Argueta's vehicle traveled to Miami, Dallas, and Los Angeles, which are cities involved with the 2026 FIFA World Cup games.
