(U) Prosecutors in Morris County, New Jersey, announced that more than 100 homemade explosive devices were discovered during a search of a home on Avondale Road in Denville on Wednesday, following an investigation that began in March into suspect Joseph Rizos, 37. Rizos was arrested during a traffic stop before the search, which uncovered a home laboratory, materials for making explosives, blasting caps, cellphones, firearms, ammunition, and 109 individual containers of suspected explosives stored in a shed without a permit. He faces one count of recklessly risking widespread injury and four counts of possession of destructive devices, with the devices described as capable of causing massive destruction. The Morris County Prosecutor’s Office, in collaboration with the FBI, DEA, and other agencies, had been investigating Rizos for allegedly operating a destructive device manufacturing business in 2025 and 2026. Prosecutor Robert Carroll praised the investigators’ persistence in shutting down the operation, and Rizos remains held in county jail pending a hearing.
