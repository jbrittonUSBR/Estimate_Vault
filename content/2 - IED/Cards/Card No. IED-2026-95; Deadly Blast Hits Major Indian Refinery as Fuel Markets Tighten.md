# Deadly Blast Hits Major Indian Refinery as Fuel Markets Tighten

[Source](https://oilprice.com/Latest-Energy-News/World-News/Deadly-Blast-Hits-Major-Indian-Refinery-as-Fuel-Markets-Tighten.html)

(U) MANGALURU, India — A high-pressure cold separator ruptured in the Coker Hydrotreater Unit at Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Ltd.'s 15-million-tonne-per-year refinery around noon on September 30, 2026, killing one worker and injuring at least eight others and starting a fire that took about two and a half hours to extinguish. The blast was heard more than 10 km away and damaged nearby buildings; MRPL isolated the unit but had not disclosed how long it would be offline, a disruption of note because the state-controlled refiner had planned to run the plant above 100 percent of capacity through March 2027 amid tight fuel supplies, OilPrice.com reported.
