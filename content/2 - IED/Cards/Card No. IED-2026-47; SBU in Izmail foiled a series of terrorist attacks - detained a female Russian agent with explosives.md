(U) The Security Service of Ukraine (SBU) successfully foiled a planned series of terrorist attacks in Izmail, Odesa region, detaining a female Russian agent who was caught in possession of homemade explosives.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Suspect:** The detained individual is a 27-year-old resident from the Zhytomyr region who was recruited by Russian special services via Telegram channels offering "easy earnings".
- **The Plot:** After renting an apartment in Izmail, the suspect manufactured homemade explosive devices (IEDs) equipped with mobile phones for remote detonation and packed with metal nuts to maximize casualties. Her target was to blow up Defense Forces vehicles and strike near military and law enforcement administrative buildings.
- **Reconnaissance & Arrest:** Before her arrest, she conducted surveillance by photographing and recording military vehicles. SBU counterintelligence agents intercepted and detained her just as she had finished preparing a disguised explosive device and was awaiting final placement instructions.
- **Legal Consequences:** Law enforcement seized mobile phones, ready-made explosives, and bomb-making components. She has been charged with preparing a terrorist act and is being held without bail, facing up to 12 years in prison.
