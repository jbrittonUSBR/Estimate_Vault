# Germany news: Police find arms depot near highway

[Source](https://www.dw.com/en/germany-news-police-find-arms-depot-near-highway/live-79273324)

(U) BERLIN — Police in the western German state of Rhineland-Palatinate found a depot containing rifles, pistols and other firearms near a highway, Deutsche Welle reported in its September 15, 2026 live news blog. The blog's summary did not specify the number of weapons, whether anyone was arrested or whether the cache was linked to a criminal or extremist network. The discovery was reported alongside other German news that day, and further details were not immediately available.
