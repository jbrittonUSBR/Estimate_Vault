# Omagh Bombing Inquiry: Officer says investigations didn't follow guidelines

[Source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6vgjpr3rz1ko)

(U) Omagh, Northern Ireland — A police officer told the Omagh Bombing Inquiry that investigations into the 1998 bombing did not follow relevant guidelines and that shortcomings in data entry may have led to missed opportunities. The BBC reported the testimony as evidence heard by the inquiry, which is examining whether the bombing could have been prevented. The account reflects the officer's evidence, not a final inquiry finding.
