# Belgium’s Intelligence Agency: Iran Behind Synagogue Bombing, Arson Campaign

[Source](https://legalinsurrection.com/2026/09/belgiums-intelligence-agency-iran-behind-synagogue-bombing-arson-campaign/)

(U) BRUSSELS, Belgium — On September 28, 2026, Legal Insurrection reported that Belgium's intelligence agency (CUTA) had concluded Iran was behind a synagogue bombing and an arson campaign targeting Jewish sites. According to the report citing Ynet and The Brussels Times, a blast occurred around 04:00 on March 9 at a synagogue in Liège (Rue Léon Frédéricq), with additional arson attacks targeting the Jewish quarter in Antwerp; no casualties were reported, and CUTA chief Gert Vercauteren was cited in attributing the operations to Iran-linked cells.
