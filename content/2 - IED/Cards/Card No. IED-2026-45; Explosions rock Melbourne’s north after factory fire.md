(U) A massive fire accompanied by explosions engulfed an electrical substation on Sydney Road in Fawkner (a northern suburb of Melbourne, Australia), prompting an emergency response and disrupting the surrounding area.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Incident:** Fire Rescue Victoria (FRV) responded to the scene in the early hours (around 3:16 AM) after a fire indicator panel was triggered, quickly escalating the response as thick smoke filled the vicinity.
- **The Response:** Firefighters arrived to find a double-brick substation building engulfed in flames. Crews had to carefully manage the scene, cooling the building with water while waiting for the high-voltage power to be officially isolated before completely extinguishing the blaze.
- **Impact and Safety:** Emergency services, including police and ambulance crews, secured the area. Thermal imaging cameras were used to check for remaining hotspots, and the fire was brought under control within roughly an hour, though it caused localized disruptions and power concerns for the community.
