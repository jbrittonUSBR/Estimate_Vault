# 16th Street Baptist Church marks bombing anniversary with a new visitor center

[Source](https://abcnews.com/Politics/wireStory/16th-street-baptist-church-marks-bombing-anniversary-new-136475525)

(U) BIRMINGHAM, Ala. — Sixteenth Street Baptist Church marked the 63rd anniversary of the 1963 bombing on September 15, 2026 with a memorial service and the opening of a new Education & Visitor Center adjacent to the historic sanctuary. The center includes immersive exhibits, oral history initiatives, artist residencies, community quilting projects and a restaurant named Koinonia, and its signature installation, The River Wall, was inspired by the stained-glass window Wales gave the church after the bombing. The Rev. Arthur Price Jr. and center director Terrence Brown said the facility was intended to turn remembrance into engagement, and the service featured an address by Equal Justice Initiative founder Bryan Stevenson honoring Addie Mae Collins, Denise McNair, Carole Robertson and Cynthia Wesley.
