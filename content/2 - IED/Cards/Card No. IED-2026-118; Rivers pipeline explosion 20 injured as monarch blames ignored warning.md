# Rivers pipeline explosion: 20 injured as monarch blames ignored warning

[Source](https://punchng.com/rivers-pipeline-explosion-20-injured-as-monarch-blames-ignored-warning/)

(U) Eleme, Nigeria — An explosion involving a gas pipeline in Rivers State injured about 20 people, according to The Punch. The King of Eleme, HRM Phillip Obelle, accused the pipeline operator of failing to respond to an earlier leak warning. The report attributed the warning allegation to the monarch; it did not establish responsibility for the explosion.
