(U) The Chattanooga Police Department bomb squad responded to a suspicious package left in front of the Goodwill store on East Brainerd Road, prompting area closures and the temporary evacuation of nearby businesses, including a local restaurant. Rather than executing a controlled explosion, the bomb squad performed a "disruption," which involves mechanically breaking apart the contents of the package to safely neutralize any potential threat.
