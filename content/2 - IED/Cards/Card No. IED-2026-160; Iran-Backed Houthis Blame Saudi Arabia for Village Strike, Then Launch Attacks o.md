# Iran-Backed Houthis Blame Saudi Arabia for Village Strike, Then Launch Attacks on Riyadh

[Source](https://www.thegatewaypundit.com/2026/11/iran-backed-houthis-blame-saudi-arabia-village-strike/)

(U) Sanaa, Yemen — Airstrikes hit homes in al-Shariya, a farming village about 15 kilometers northeast of Sanaa, on November 7, and the Houthi-run Health Ministry reported 44 villagers killed, including 21 children, with 40 wounded. The article said those casualty figures came from Houthi officials and had not been independently verified, and that Saudi Arabia neither claimed nor denied the strike while a report by Sheba Intelligence carried by the Saudi outlet Sabq argued damage patterns suggested an internal explosion rather than an airstrike. Houthi military spokesman Yahya Saree announced six attacks on Saudi targets, including King Khalid International Airport and oil facilities in Riyadh, which the Houthis said were retaliation for al-Shariya and a separate market strike in Taiz.
