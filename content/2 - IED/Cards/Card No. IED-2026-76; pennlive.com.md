# FBI Joins Investigation After Fourth Bomb Threat to Pa. School District

[Source](https://www.pennlive.com/news/2026/09/fbi-joins-investigation-after-fourth-bomb-threat-to-pa-school-district.html)

(U) NEW SEWICKLEY TOWNSHIP, Pa. — The FBI joined the investigation into a series of bomb threats targeting the Freedom Area School District in Beaver County, Pennsylvania, which has received four bomb threats since the start of the school year, prompting evacuations so police could conduct searches. An FBI spokesperson said the agency was working closely with New Sewickley Township Police to identify and bring to justice those responsible for the recent threats. The repeated threats have disrupted instruction in the district.
