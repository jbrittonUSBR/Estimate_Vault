# Four PoK residents killed in IED blast; security heightened in J&K's Poonch

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/india/at-least-4-pok-residents-killed-after-powerful-explosion-jolts-line-of-control/articleshow/134896260.cms)

(U) POONCH, India — On October 12, 2026, The Times of India reported that four residents of Pakistan-occupied Kashmir were killed in a suspected improvised explosive device blast in the Hajira tehsil area near the Line of Control in Poonch district. The explosion occurred as a group was reportedly laying water pipes, and Indian security forces heightened alert and vigilance along the LoC following the incident.
