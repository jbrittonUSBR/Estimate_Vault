# Scene cleared following bomb squad presence at Estero post office

[Source](https://www.winknews.com/news/lee/scene-cleared-following-bomb-squad-presence-at-estero-post-office/article_febe9db0-4abb-4888-b645-953051f32ff5.html)

(U) ESTERO, United States — The Lee County Sheriff's Office Bomb Squad investigated an unattended container left in the lobby of the Estero post office on October 1, 2026, and determined it was non-hazardous. The scene was cleared after the package was found to contain nothing, and no injuries or arrests were reported.
