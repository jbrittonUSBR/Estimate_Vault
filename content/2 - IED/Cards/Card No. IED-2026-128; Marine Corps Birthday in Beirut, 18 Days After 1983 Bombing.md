# Marine Corps Birthday in Beirut, 18 Days After 1983 Bombing

[Source](https://military.com/beirut-marines-cut-their-birthday-cake-18-days-after-the-barracks-bombing)

(U) Beirut, Lebanon - Military.com recounts how Marines at the 24th Marine Amphibious Unit held a low-key Marine Corps birthday cake-cutting on November 10, 1983, 18 days after the barracks bombing killed 241 U.S. service members. The article draws on Marine Corps histories and describes the ceremony amid memorial observances and heightened security.
