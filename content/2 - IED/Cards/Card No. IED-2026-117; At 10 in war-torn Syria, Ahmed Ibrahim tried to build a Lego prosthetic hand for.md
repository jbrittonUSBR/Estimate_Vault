# At 10 in war-torn Syria, Ahmed Ibrahim tried to build a Lego prosthetic hand for a friend injured in a bombing; now in Canada, he has won funding to develop an AI-powered robotic hand

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/science/discovery/at-10-in-war-torn-syria-ahmed-ibrahim-tried-to-build-a-lego-prosthetic-hand-for-a-friend-injured-in-a-bombing-now-in-canada-he-has-won-funding-to-develop-an-ai-powered-robotic-hand/articleshow/134983651.cms)

(U) Hamilton, Canada — The Times of India profiled Ahmed Ibrahim, who as a child in Syria tried to build a Lego-and-motor prosthetic hand for a friend who had lost a hand in a bombing. The improvised device did not work, but the experience informed Ibrahim's later work developing an AI-powered robotic hand using muscle signals. The article said he had received funding in Canada to advance that project.
