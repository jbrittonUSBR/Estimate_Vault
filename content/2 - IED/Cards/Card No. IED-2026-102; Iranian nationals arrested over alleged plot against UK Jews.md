# Iranian nationals arrested over alleged plot against UK Jews

[Source](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/434028)

(U) MANCHESTER, UK — Two Iranian nationals were arrested in the United Kingdom on suspicion of plotting an attack against Manchester's Jewish community during Yom Kippur, Israel National News reported on October 3, 2026. British police said Rahman Salahi, 35, and Salameh Ahmadian, 36, allegedly sought components and equipment for an improvised explosive device and a chlorine bomb and conducted surveillance of potential targets. According to police, the two were in ongoing contact with individuals in Iran who directed and assisted the planning. The suspects were due to appear before a London court on Saturday as authorities sought to extend their detention. The UK Home Office said one man had been granted asylum status and the second had a pending application.
