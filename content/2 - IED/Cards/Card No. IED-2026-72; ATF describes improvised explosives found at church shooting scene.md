# ATF describes improvised explosives found at church shooting scene

[Source](https://www.abc12.com/news/grand-blanc/atf-describes-improvised-explosives-found-at-church-shooting-scene/article_bdd160e5-c092-4392-9670-8c4cb6494b85.html)

(U) GRAND BLANC TOWNSHIP, United States — ATF investigators said they found four crude improvised explosive devices in the pickup truck that crashed into the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints on McCandlish Road, describing them as little more than consumer fireworks taped together. Special Agent in Charge James Deir said the devices were "fairly basic" and "juvenile in nature," adding that they would have caused no damage in a crowd. Authorities said the suspect, 40-year-old Thomas Sanford, rammed the church, opened fire inside, poured gasoline and set the building alight while dozens of parishioners were present; four people died, eight were wounded by gunfire and two were hospitalized with smoke inhalation. A Grand Blanc Township police officer and a Michigan Department of Natural Resources conservation officer shot and killed Sanford behind the church.
