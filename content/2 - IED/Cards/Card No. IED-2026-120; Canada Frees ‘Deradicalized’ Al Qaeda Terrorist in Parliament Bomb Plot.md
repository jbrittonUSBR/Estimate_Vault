# FrontPage Magazine: Toronto 18 plot ringleader granted full parole

[Source](https://www.frontpagemag.com/canada-frees-deradicalized-al-qaeda-terrorist-in-parliament-bomb-plot/)

(U) Toronto, Canada — The FrontPage Magazine report carried by Free Republic said Zakaria Amara, a ringleader of the thwarted Toronto 18 plot, was granted full parole after the Parole Board of Canada found he no longer showed signs of holding extremist beliefs. Amara had helped recruit members for a group that planned to attack Parliament Hill and other targets in Toronto. The aggregator headline's phrase “Al Qaeda Terrorist” is not a precise description of the reported parole decision, and the account did not say Canada freed him from custody without conditions.
