# Troops rescue eight victims, apprehend six ISWAP members in Borno, Adamawa

[Source](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/09/troops-rescue-eight-victims-apprehend-six-iswap-members-in-borno-adamawa/)

(U) MAIDUGURI, Nigeria — Troops rescued eight abductees and arrested six suspected ISWAP members and collaborators in separate operations across Borno and Adamawa states between September 11 and 13, according to an operational report made available to the News Agency of Nigeria. The report said soldiers of the 82 Division Task Force Battalion rescued the eight at the fringes of Armuda and Gava villages in Gwoza, while troops of the 23 Brigade arrested three suspected terrorists at Gaya in Hong and six suspected kidnappers at Soktu Hill in Song. The operations also disrupted improvised explosive devices, led to the arrest of suspected logistics suppliers at Koppa in Konduga and of illegal miners, and recovered mobile phones, bicycles and cash.
