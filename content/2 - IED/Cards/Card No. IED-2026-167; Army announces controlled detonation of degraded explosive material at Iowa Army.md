# Army announces controlled detonation of degraded explosive material at Iowa Army Ammunition Plant

[Source](https://www.army.mil/article/297069/army_announces_controlled_detonation_of_degraded_explosive_material_at_iowa_army_ammunition_plant)

(U) MIDDLETOWN, IOWA — The U.S. Army announced on November 24 that it planned a controlled, in-place detonation of about 68 drums holding more than 9,700 pounds of degraded, crystallized lead azide at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant near Middletown on Wednesday morning. The Army said a multi-agency review concluded the material had become ultra-sensitive to friction, heat and shock, making neutralization or transport too risky, and Acting Secretary of the Army Adam Telle said a mitigated blast was preferable to an uncontrolled one. The announcement said FEMA was supporting a recommended relocation of Middletown residents and areas within a five-mile southwest cone, with air monitoring, soil testing and government-funded cleanup planned after the detonation.
