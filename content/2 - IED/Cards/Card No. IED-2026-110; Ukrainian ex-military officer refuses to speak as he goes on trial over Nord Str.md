# Ukrainian ex-military officer refuses to speak as he goes on trial over Nord Stream pipeline bombing

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16208233/Ukrainian-ex-military-officer-refuses-speak-goes-trial-Nord-Stream-pipeline-bombing.html)

(U) BERLIN, Germany — A Ukrainian ex-military officer went on trial in Germany for his alleged role in the September 2022 Nord Stream pipeline bombings, but refused to testify during proceedings. Prosecutors were presenting evidence linking him to the explosions, though key details about command responsibility and broader involvement remained contested as the trial began.
