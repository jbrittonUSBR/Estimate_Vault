# ISIS Blows Up Power Transmission Tower in Iraq - Iran

[Source](https://www.europesays.com/iran/298289/)

(U) ANBAR PROVINCE, Iraq — An ISIS attack destroyed an electricity transmission tower on the main power line linking Haditha in Anbar province with Baiji in Salahaddin province, a security source said on the evening of September 18, 2026. Saad Ghazi Mohammed, head of the Security Committee at the Anbar Provincial Council, said early on Saturday, September 19, that the main electricity line connecting the two cities had been cut and that preliminary information confirmed a tower had been blown up. On the same night, a second security source said security forces thwarted an attempt by ISIS militants to target another transmission tower between Baiji and Haditha, disrupting the attack before the militants reached their target; the attempted attack resulted in an armed clash. The account is a secondary aggregation of a Kurdistan24 report.
