# JCPD: Bomb Threat Led to Large Response at Johnson City Medical Center

[Source](https://www.wjhl.com/news/local/heavy-first-responder-presence-at-johnson-city-medical-center/)

(U) JOHNSON CITY, Tenn. — A heavy first responder presence converged on Johnson City Medical Center after a patient allegedly made a bomb threat, according to a Johnson City Police Department news release. Medical center staff reported to police that the patient threatened to have "a bomb," prompting a large law enforcement and fire response at the Ballad Health regional referral center. The JCPD investigated the threat at the facility, which is home to the region's largest dedicated emergency air rescue fleet.
