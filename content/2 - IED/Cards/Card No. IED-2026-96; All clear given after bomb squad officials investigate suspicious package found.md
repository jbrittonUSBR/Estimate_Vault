# All clear given after bomb squad officials investigate suspicious package found near businesses in Wilton Manors

[Source](https://wsvn.com/news/local/broward/all-clear-given-after-bomb-squad-officials-investigate-suspicious-package-found-near-businesses-in-wilton-manors/)

(U) WILTON MANORS, United States — The Fort Lauderdale Police Bomb Squad gave the all clear on October 2, 2026 after examining suspicious packages left on the front steps of a Starbucks and a Truist Bank on the 1500 block of Northeast 26th Street in Wilton Manors. A technician entered the bank past a bicycle and blue backpack, and all packages were confirmed non-explosive; the area was deemed safe and roadway closures were lifted.
