(U) **Photo: The SBU foiled a double terrorist attack in Ivano-Frankivsk targeting rescue workers** (UA.NEWS, July 21, 2026) Ukraine’s Security Service (SBU) Counterintelligence Directorate prevented an attempted double terrorist attack planned by Russian intelligence in central Ivano-Frankivsk. The plot targeted a residential complex courtyard and was designed to maximize casualties among first responders. According to the SBU, the plan involved two homemade explosive devices, each containing about six kilograms of explosives, detonators, and hundreds of metal bolts as shrapnel, disguised as kitchen pots. The first device was to be remotely detonated in a parking lot among civilian vehicles. After residents, rescue workers, medical personnel, and law enforcement arrived, a second device nearby would be detonated remotely.

(U) A local unemployed man was allegedly recruited by Russian intelligence via Telegram channels while seeking “easy money.” Following instructions, he rented an apartment overlooking the target site so handlers could monitor and coordinate. He also secretly installed a phone connected to a power bank in the courtyard to help select the optimal placement for the explosives and later purchased the components to build the devices. SBU counterintelligence documented the full preparation process, stopped the attack before it occurred, and identified the Russian intelligence officers involved in coordinating it, according to the agency.

The report notes prior cases in which Ukrainian courts sentenced individuals for similar Russia-linked terrorist plots.
