# MRPL shares tumble 5% after explosion at Mangaluru plant, thick smoke seen from refinery unit

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/stocks/news/mrpl-shares-tumble-5-after-explosion-at-mangaluru-plant-thick-smoke-seen-from-refinery-unit/articleshow/134589058.cms)

(U) MANGALURU, India — An explosion at the Mangaluru Refinery and Petrochemicals Ltd plant near Jokatte sent thick smoke over the facility on September 30, 2026, driving the company's shares down more than 5% to 162.50 rupees on the National Stock Exchange. One worker suffered burns over about 35% of his body and was in intensive care, while six others sustained minor injuries; police said there were no deaths and the fire was under control. Police Commissioner Sudheer Kumar Reddy said the cause had not been determined, and the blast was audible at Mangaluru airport. The incident raised fresh questions about safety at one of India's largest coastal refineries.
