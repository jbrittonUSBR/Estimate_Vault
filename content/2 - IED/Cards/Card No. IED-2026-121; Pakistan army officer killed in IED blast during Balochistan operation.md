# Pakistan army officer killed in IED blast during Balochistan operation

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/pakistan/pakistan-army-officer-killed-in-ied-blast-during-balochistan-operation/articleshow/135041355.cms)

(U) October 19, 2026 — The Times of India reported that Major Wajahat Waheed was killed when an IED struck security forces during a clearance operation in the Shadi Kaur area of Gwadar district, Balochistan. The military’s Inter-Services Public Relations said forces separately killed four militants in Jattakabad, Kalat district, and recovered arms and ammunition. The Army said clearance operations would continue; the report attributed those operational details to the military.
