# IED scars of Bastar: Survivors to get prosthetic limbs

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/raipur/ied-scars-of-bastar-survivors-to-get-prosthetic-limbs/articleshow/134716473.cms)

(U) Bastar, India — On October 5, 2026, authorities announced a program to provide prosthetic limbs to survivors of improvised explosive device (IED) blasts in the Bastar region, where decades of Maoist insurgency have left many civilians maimed by landmines and IEDs.
