# NIA makes second arrest in the improvised explosive device in Manipur

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/india/nia-makes-second-arrest-in-the-improvised-explosive-device-moirang-in-manipur/articleshow/134570149.cms)

(U) IMPHAL, India — The National Investigation Agency arrested Thangmang Haokip in Churachandpur as the second accused in the April 7, 2026, improvised explosive device attack at Tronglaobi Awang Leikai in Bishnupur district, Manipur, according to a September 29 report. The blast killed two minor children and injured another person, and investigators recovered an unexploded improvised rocket-propelled grenade at the scene. Haokip was arrested on September 25 and remanded to 12 days of police custody, following the earlier arrest of S. Palal Thadou Kuki on August 12. The case underscored the continued use of improvised explosives in Manipur's ethnic conflict.
