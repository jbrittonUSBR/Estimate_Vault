# ‘We will not allow that’: Trump warns Iran over ‘rumour’ of Tehran's dirty bomb plot

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/middle-east/we-will-not-allow-that-trump-warns-iran-over-rumour-of-tehrans-dirty-bomb-plot/articleshow/136044456.cms)

(U) Washington, United States — At a White House press conference on November 20, President Donald Trump said the United States would determine whether Iran was trying to build a dirty bomb and would stop it, calling reports of such a plot a "rumour." The article noted that CNN had reported U.S. intelligence indicated Iran might try to build and detonate such a weapon in Washington, that Reuters said it could not independently verify the report, and that Iran's Foreign Ministry had dismissed the allegations as "baseless" on Tuesday. Trump also said Iran would "never have a nuclear weapon" and claimed the U.S. Navy had imposed a blockade on the Strait of Hormuz with "total control" over the waterway.
