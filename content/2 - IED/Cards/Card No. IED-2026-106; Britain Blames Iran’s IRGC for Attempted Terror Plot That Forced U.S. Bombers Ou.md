# Britain Blames Iran’s IRGC for Attempted Terror Plot That Forced U.S. Bombers Out of the UK

[Source](https://www.breitbart.com/europe/2026/10/12/britain-blames-irans-irgc-attempted-terror-plot-forced-u-s-bombers-uk/)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — On October 12, 2026, Breitbart News reported that British Prime Minister Andy Burnham told Parliament that intelligence assessments indicated Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) was almost certainly responsible for directing a suspected terrorist plot targeting RAF Fairford in Gloucestershire, a base used by U.S. bombers. Five men were arrested in late September on terrorism and explosives-related offenses, no viable explosives were found, and the U.S. subsequently withdrew bombers from the base while the UK announced plans to sanction Iranian networks.
