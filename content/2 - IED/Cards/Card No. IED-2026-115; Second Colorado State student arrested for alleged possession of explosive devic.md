# Second Colorado State student arrested for alleged possession of explosive device parts

[Source](https://www.cbsnews.com/colorado/news/second-colorado-state-student-arrested-alleged-possession-explosive-parts/)

(U) FORT COLLINS, COLORADO — CBS News reported that a second Colorado State University student was arrested on suspicion of possessing parts that could be used to make explosive devices. The report described the arrest as part of an ongoing investigation and noted that the allegations had not been proven in court.
