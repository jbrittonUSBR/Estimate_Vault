# 'How to Become an Assassin' to 'Lone Mujahid' Pocket Book: NIA chargesheet reveals how extremist literature was used to plot Red Fort blast

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/defence/how-to-become-an-assassin-to-lone-mujahid-pocket-book-nia-chargesheet-reveals-how-extremist-literature-was-used-to-plot-red-fort-blast/articleshow/133968723.cms)

(U) NEW DELHI, India — A National Investigation Agency chargesheet in the 2025 Red Fort blast case alleged that a terror module used an al-Qaeda in the Arabian Peninsula-linked manual, the 'Lone Mujahid Pocket Book' and 'How to Become an Assassin,' containing instructions on explosives, improvised explosive devices and covert tactics. The November 10, 2025 car blast killed 11 people and injured 29 at the Red Fort area, and the vehicle was used as a vehicle-borne improvised explosive device. Investigators found traces of TATP and ammonium nitrate, and named Umer Un Nabi as the mastermind; he died in the blast and proceedings against him were abated.
