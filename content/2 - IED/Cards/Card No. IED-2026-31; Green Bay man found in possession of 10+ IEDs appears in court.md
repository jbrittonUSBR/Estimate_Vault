(U) **UPDATE: Green Bay man found in possession of 10+ IEDs appears in court** (WFRV / wearegreenbay.com, latest update July 20, 2026) A 31-year-old Green Bay, Wisconsin man, Kenneth Seay, faces 18 charges after authorities allegedly found more than 10 improvised explosive devices (IEDs) and drugs at his residence.

**Key details from the criminal complaint (February 2026):**
- On February 20, authorities learned of a package shipped to Seay’s home from California. A K9 later indicated narcotics.
- The package contained about 170 grams of a white crystalline substance and 12 grams of white powder that tested positive for methamphetamine and cocaine.
- On February 24, a search of the residence led the bomb squad to recover 13 improvised explosive devices. One contained nails; another had “Boomstick” written on it. The devices were determined to be homemade, not commercial. One door had been modified for barricading.
- The FBI and ATF were contacted. Seay requested a lawyer and did not speak with authorities.
- An acquaintance told investigators that Seay sold drugs, received shipments about twice a week, and was afraid of the cartel.

**Charges include:**
- Maintaining a drug trafficking place (felony)
- Possession with intent to deliver cocaine (felony)
- Possession with intent to deliver methamphetamine (felony)
- Possession of THC (misdemeanor)
- Possession of drug paraphernalia (misdemeanor)
- Possession of improvised explosives (13 counts, each a felony)

(U) Cash bond was set at $75,000. Seay has pleaded not guilty. The case has progressed through multiple court appearances (status conferences, waiver of preliminary hearing, arraignment). As of the July 20, 2026 update, he remained in custody; the defense filed a motion to suppress evidence from the search, bond was continued, and a motion hearing was set for September 4, 2026.
