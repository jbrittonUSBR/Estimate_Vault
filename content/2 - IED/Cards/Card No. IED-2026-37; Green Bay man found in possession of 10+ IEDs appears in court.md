(U) In February 2026, authorities searching a Green Bay residence (following a drug package intercept) discovered 13 improvised explosive devices (IEDs), along with methamphetamine, cocaine, and related items. One device contained nails; another was labeled “Boomstick.” A door had been modified for barricading. The FBI and ATF were notified. 31-year-old Kenneth Seay faces 18 charges, including:

- Maintaining a drug trafficking place
- Possession with intent to deliver cocaine and methamphetamine
- Multiple counts of possession of improvised explosives

(U) Bond was set at $75,000. Seay has pleaded not guilty. The case has continued through multiple court appearances, with a motion hearing scheduled for September 4, 2026.
