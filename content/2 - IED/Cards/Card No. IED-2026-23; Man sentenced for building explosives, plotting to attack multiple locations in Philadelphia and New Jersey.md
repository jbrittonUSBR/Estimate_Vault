(U) A 20-year-old Philadelphia man, Muhyyee-Ud-din Abdul-Rahman, was sentenced to 20 to 40 months in prison after being convicted in September for building and possessing explosives and plotting mass casualty attacks on multiple targets in the Philadelphia and New Jersey areas. The planned targets included the Philadelphia Pride Parade, a Black university, the Army-Navy Football game, Valley Forge Military Academy, nuclear power plants in both states, and other unspecified locations. Abdul-Rahman, who was 17 at the time of his arrest in August 2023, built and possessed the explosives as part of the plot. The district attorney’s office has expressed concern that the sentence is too short given the severity of the offenses. The case highlights efforts to prevent attacks on high-profile events and critical infrastructure.
