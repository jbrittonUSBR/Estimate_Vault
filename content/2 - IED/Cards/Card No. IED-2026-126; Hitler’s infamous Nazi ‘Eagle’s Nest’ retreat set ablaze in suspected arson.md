# Hitler’s infamous Nazi ‘Eagle’s Nest’ retreat set ablaze in suspected arson

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/10/24/world-news/hitlers-infamous-nazi-eagles-nest-retreat-set-ablaze-in-suspected-arson/)

(U) Berchtesgaden, Bavaria — The New York Post, citing Der Spiegel and Bavarian police, reported that a fire broke out on 22 October at Kehlsteinhaus, known as the Eagle’s Nest. Authorities treated it as suspected arson and arrested an unidentified person of interest. The extent of damage was not clear in the report.
