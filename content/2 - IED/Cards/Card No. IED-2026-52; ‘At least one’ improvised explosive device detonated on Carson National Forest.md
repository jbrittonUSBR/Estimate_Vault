(U) The Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives (ATF), alongside local law enforcement and the U.S. Forest Service, is investigating multiple improvised explosive and incendiary devices discovered within the **Carson National Forest** in northern New Mexico. Authorities are offering a **reward of up to $10,000** for information leading to the arrest and conviction of those responsible.
### **Key Details**

- **Timeline and Locations:**
    - Between **April 2 and May 8**, multiple destructive devices were recovered near the **Tres Piedras Ranger Station** in Tres Piedras. At least one of these devices had detonated, causing minor damage.
    - On **August 10**, an additional device was found by a concerned citizen and turned in to the **Canjilon Ranger Station** in Canjilon.
- **Investigation:** ATF Certified Explosives Specialists and Fire Investigators have collected evidence from the scenes, which are undergoing forensic analysis at the ATF Forensic Science Laboratory.
- **Public Safety Warning:** Officials stress that these devices were intentionally placed and are extremely unstable. The public is strongly advised **never to touch, move, or handle suspicious objects**, but rather to immediately leave the area and contact local law enforcement.
