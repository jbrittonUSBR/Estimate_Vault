(U) A vigilant shop clerk in New Mexico helped foil a violent bomb plot after a former Navy SEAL, Gregory Vandenberg, stopped at a travel center and openly detailed plans to use explosives against police officers at a "No Kings Day" protest in San Diego. While purchasing large mortar fireworks and firecrackers, Vandenberg boasted about his special operations background and his intent to harm law enforcement, prompting alert store employees to record his license plate and notify the authorities. Law enforcement tracked and arrested Vandenberg the following day, subsequently uncovering neo-Nazi and extremist paraphernalia in his vehicle, ultimately leading to his federal conviction for transporting explosives with intent to injure.
