# Gujarat chemical plant blast kills 5, injures 5 in Bharuch district

[Source](https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/gujarat-chemical-plant-blast-kills-5-injures-5-in-bharuch-district-126101700247_1.html)

(U) Bharuch district, India — A blast at SynZeal Pharmachem's facility in the Saykha industrial area killed five workers and injured five others, according to the report. It occurred around 10 p.m. during a hydrogenation process. Authorities were investigating the incident; the report did not establish a cause beyond its connection to the industrial process.
