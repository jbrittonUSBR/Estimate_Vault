# Explosion near German train station injures 2 Ukrainian citizens

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/world/explosion-near-german-train-station-injures-2-ukrainian-citizens)

(U) AUGSBURG, Germany — An explosion at the main train station in Augsburg injured two people, both Ukrainian citizens, early on September 2, 2026, prompting police to close the station after finding a suspicious package. Police spokesperson Timo Küfner said the package was ultimately found to be harmless and trains resumed at 10 a.m. local time, adding it was "too early to say anything about a possible motive." The victims were a 17-year-old girl and an 18-year-old man who suffered only minor injuries, and the incident came a day after Germany accused Russia of a failed drone sabotage operation at Leipzig/Halle Airport.
