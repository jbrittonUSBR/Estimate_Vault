# No bomb squad dispatched, Omagh Bombing Inquiry hears

[Source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cwr50gyln104o)

(U) OMAGH, United Kingdom — The Omagh Bombing Inquiry heard on September 30, 2026 that British Army bomb disposal experts were not deployed to Omagh before the August 1998 blast, despite an "accredited" codeword warning received 35 minutes before the explosion that killed 29 people. Colonel Peter MacMullan testified that ammunition technical officers at Lisnelly Barracks, about 1 km from the town centre, were placed on "notice to move" at 14:40 BST but never deployed because the RUC did not request them, and said the army's response was "exactly in line with policies and procedures" of the time; he also acknowledged gaps and timing issues in army logbooks and the loss of a communications tape.
