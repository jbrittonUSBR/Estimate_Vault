# WATCH: Shelter in Place Order Issued After Massive Explosion at Mississippi Chemical Plant

[Source](https://www.thegatewaypundit.com/2026/11/watch-shelter-place-order-issued-after-massive-explosion/)

(U) Greenville, Mississippi, USA - The Gateway Pundit reported a shelter-in-place order after an explosion and fire at the Loveland chemical plant. Local officials said preliminary information from plant management linked the fire to a herbicide chemical; reports said the fire was contained and no injuries had been reported.
