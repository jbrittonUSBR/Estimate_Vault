(U) Sierra Vista police and Fort Huachuca K9 units responded to a hoax bomb threat called in around 8:40 a.m. on June 24, 2026, to the Oscar Yrun Community Center targeting an undisclosed park, leading to the temporary clearing of parks and facilities before they were safely reopened with no explosives found. This marks at least the fourth such threat in a week for the Sierra Vista Police Department, following recent incidents at the local mall, a salon, and a Food City, with similar hoax calls reported in Tucson; authorities suspect the same perpetrator based on voice alteration and vague details consistent with swatting-style calls. Lt. Justin Dannels noted the pattern, while residents like Scotland Gearhart expressed concern over the disruption to daily activities such as park visits with children. The incidents highlight ongoing community impacts from what appear to be attention-seeking hoaxes, amid a prior swatting case in the region that left a man paralyzed.
