(U) A joint multi-agency investigation targeting a major drug distribution network operating across the Denver metropolitan area resulted in 17 arrests and the seizure of a substantial cache of illegal firearms, narcotics, and explosives.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Operation:** The sweeping bust was the culmination of a months-long investigation conducted jointly by the Denver District Attorney's Office, the FBI's Violent Criminal Enterprise Task Force, and the Denver Police Department.
- **Arrests:** Authorities arrested and charged 17 suspects accused of conspiring to traffic and distribute narcotics in both bulk and retail quantities throughout the region.
- **Seizures:** During the raids, law enforcement recovered a massive inventory of contraband, which included:
    - Approximately 46 firearms
    - 15 pounds of cocaine
    - **Five pipe bombs**
    - Nine vehicles, roughly $100,000 in jewelry, and $35,000 in cash
- **Law Enforcement Response:** Officials highlighted the collaborative nature of the cross-jurisdictional task force—which integrated local police departments alongside federal assets—emphasizing that targeting these high-level networks remains a top priority for removing dangerous weapons and drugs from local streets.
