# ‘Unsubstantiated' bomb threat delays Aragon Ballroom concert

[Source](https://www.nbcchicago.com/news/local/unsubstantiated-bomb-threat-delays-aragon-ballroom-concert/3996127/)

(U) CHICAGO, United States — A bomb threat called in to Chicago police shortly before 7 p.m. on September 30, 2026 temporarily delayed a Steve Lacy concert at the Aragon Ballroom, prompting officers to clear and inspect the venue while patrons waited outside. Investigators concluded the threat was "not bonafide" and allowed the show to proceed, and the FBI said it was aware of an "unsubstantiated threat," warning that hoax threats and swatting carry federal and state criminal penalties.
