(U) Ukraine’s Security Service (SBU) prevented a planned double bombing in the center of Ivano-Frankivsk. According to the SBU, Russian intelligence recruited a local unemployed man via Telegram. He was instructed to prepare two improvised explosive devices (each with about 6 kg of explosives, detonators, and metal bolts as shrapnel), disguised as kitchen pots. The plan: detonate the first device in a residential complex parking lot, then remotely detonate the second once rescue workers, medics, police, and residents arrived, maximizing casualties. The suspect rented an apartment overlooking the site for monitoring and installed a phone for coordination. SBU counterintelligence documented the preparation, stopped the attack before it occurred, and identified the Russian handlers involved.
