# Former IRGC commander, sanctioned by Canada, to address London Islamophobia conference

[Source](https://freerepublic.com/focus/f-news/4403939/posts)

(U) London, United Kingdom — A Jerusalem Post report, excerpted on Free Republic, said Saied Reza Ameli was scheduled to deliver a remote keynote at the Islamic Human Rights Commission's 13th conference, "Artificial Intelligence and Islamophobia," on December 13. The report said Ameli served as secretary of Iran's Supreme Council of the Cultural Revolution from 2019 to 2023, an institution involved in developing compulsory hijab policy during the 2019–2022 crackdowns, and listed Shereen Fernandez and Sandew Hira as the other keynote speakers. Justice for Iran was cited as identifying him as a former deputy IRGC brigade commander in the 1980s.
