# Inside Iran's ruthless Quds Force unit tasked with attacks on foreign soil and overseen by 'Shadow Commander' boss - as UK probes regime link to RAF Fairford bomb plot

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16165951/Inside-Irans-ruthless-Quds-Force-unit-tasked-attacks-foreign-soil-overseen-Shadow-Commander-boss-UK-probes-regime-link-RAF-Fairford-bomb-plot.html)

(U) LONDON — The Daily Mail profiled the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps' Quds Force and its Unit 840, described as responsible for attacks and plots on foreign soil under the direction of the unit's "shadow commander," as British police investigated a possible Iranian link to the RAF Fairford bomb plot. The report said Iran had been linked to 218 plots since 1979, about half of them in Europe, and cited MI5 Director General Ken McCallum's statement that more than 20 Iran-backed plots had been disrupted in a year. The article's characterization of Iranian intent and its speculation about a Fairford connection were not independently substantiated.
