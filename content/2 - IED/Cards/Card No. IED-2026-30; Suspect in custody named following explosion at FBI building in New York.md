(U) **Suspect in custody named following explosion at FBI building in New York** (The Guardian, July 20, 2026) Authorities investigated an explosion Monday morning outside 26 Federal Plaza in lower Manhattan, a building that houses FBI offices and an ICE field office. A suspect who allegedly deployed an incendiary device was taken into custody and later identified as Andrew Arrabaca.

(U) FBI Assistant Director James Barnacle said Arrabaca “came armed with bad intentions” and listed multiple weapons recovered from him. Video showed smoke and flames outside the building, with law enforcement responding quickly. Local reporting indicated the fire was started by fireworks loaded into a wagon bearing a sign reading “ICE out of our streets.” Three people were reported injured, with two requiring hospital treatment. The FBI confirmed an individual deployed an incendiary device, was taken into custody, and that the Joint Terrorism Task Force is investigating. NBC New York reported that Arrabaca may have emotional issues and had previously served in the U.S. Army and National Guard. House Democrat Hakeem Jeffries praised the swift law-enforcement response and said the perpetrator must be prosecuted fully.

(U) The article places the incident in the context of recent protests against ICE and the Trump administration’s immigration enforcement, noting increased deaths involving federal immigration authorities and ongoing hardline policies.
