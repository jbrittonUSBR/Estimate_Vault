# MICHAEL RUIZ: What is a dirty bomb? Explosive terror threat explained

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/us/michael-ruiz-what-is-dirty-bomb-explosive-terror-threat-explained)

(U) Washington, D.C. — A dirty bomb combines conventional explosives with radioactive material to disperse contamination, but does not produce a nuclear detonation. Experts cited by Fox News said the blast would pose the greatest immediate danger, while contamination, evacuations, cleanup costs, and public fear could cause wider disruption. U.S. officials said they had not seen evidence of an imminent plot.
