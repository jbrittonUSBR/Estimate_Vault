(U) A bomb threat phoned in at 4:27 p.m. on June 23, 2026, from an unknown number claiming that 20 pipe bombs were hidden inside Springfield Town Center in Fairfax County prompted authorities to evacuate the entire shopping mall as a precaution. The Fairfax County Police Department, including its Explosive Ordnance Disposal unit, responded by clearing shoppers and employees from the premises and conducting a detailed search of the property. Police are investigating the source of the threat and urged the public to avoid the area while the operation continued. No injuries were reported, and the incident remained an active situation with limited additional details released at the time of reporting.
