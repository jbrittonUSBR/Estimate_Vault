# How A Resurgent Al-Qaeda Is Helping Militant Attacks In Pakistan

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/pakistan/2026/pakistan-260908-rferl01.htm)

(U) ISLAMABAD, Pakistan — A report carried via RFE/RL described how a resurgent Al-Qaeda is aiding militant attacks in Pakistan, according to GlobalSecurity.org. The article cited a May explosion at a police checkpoint that killed 21 people and referenced a United Nations report on the group's support to the Pakistani Taliban. It also noted Al-Qaeda in the Indian Subcontinent's backing of suicide bombings and vehicle-borne improvised explosive devices.
