# UK Terror Police Investigate Potential Iranian Link over Suspected Bombing Plot on Base Used by U.S. Air Force

[Source](https://www.breitbart.com/europe/2026/09/27/uk-terror-police-investigate-potential-iranian-link-over-suspected-bombing-plot-on-base-used-by-u-s-air-force/)

(U) LONDON — Counterterrorism police investigated a possible Iranian link to a suspected bombing plot at RAF Fairford after five men were arrested near the base, Breitbart News reported, citing The Times. The report noted that four U.S. bombers were stationed at Fairford and that the IRGC had described bases used to strike Iran as legitimate targets. It quoted police commander Vicki Evans on the 400-meter cordon; the Iranian connection remained under investigation and was not confirmed.
