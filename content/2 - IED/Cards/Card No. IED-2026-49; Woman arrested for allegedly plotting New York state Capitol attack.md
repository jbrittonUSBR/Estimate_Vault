(U) The FBI and federal prosecutors thwarted an alleged ISIS-inspired terrorist plot targeting the New York State Capitol building in Albany, arresting a 35-year-old local resident.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Suspect & Charges:** Jessica Bowie of Albany, New York, was arrested and charged in a federal criminal complaint with attempting to provide material support for a foreign terrorist organization. If convicted, she faces up to 20 years in prison.
- **The Plot:** According to court documents, Bowie plotted between July and August to use an explosive device to target the Capitol building and kill state senators while they were in session. Investigators stated she intended to smuggle the device inside using a delivery bag and hoped to inflict mass casualties.
- **The Sting Operation:** The FBI uncovered the plot after tracking Bowie's online activity and radicalization. Undercover agents and informants posing as ISIS sympathizers coordinated with her, eventually arresting her as she met to retrieve a purported explosive device and a firearm.
- **Official Response:** Federal and state leaders—including Governor Kathy Hochul and federal prosecutors—praised law enforcement for intercepting the plot early, assuring the public that there was no active ongoing threat to the Capitol.
