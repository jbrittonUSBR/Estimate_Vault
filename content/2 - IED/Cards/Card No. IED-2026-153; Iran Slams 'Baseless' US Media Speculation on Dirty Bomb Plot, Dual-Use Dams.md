# Iran Slams 'Baseless' US Media Speculation on Dirty Bomb Plot, Dual-Use Dams

[Source](https://www.commondreams.org/news/speculative-us-media-reports-iran-war)

(U) Iran — Iranian officials rejected reports that Tehran was considering a chemical or “dirty bomb” attack on Washington and disputed speculation that dams were linked to missile sites. The CNN report cited unnamed U.S. officials and described the alleged planning as an effort to preserve options, not evidence of an imminent attack; the White House separately said there were no credible threats to the homeland. Iranian officials called the reporting baseless.
