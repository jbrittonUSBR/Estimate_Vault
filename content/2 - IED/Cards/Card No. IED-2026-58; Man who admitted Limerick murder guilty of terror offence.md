# Man who admitted Limerick murder guilty of terror offence

[Source](https://www.rte.ie/news/courts/2026/0908/1590771-guilty-plea-terror-offence/)

(U) LIMERICK, Ireland — RTÉ reported that a man who had admitted the murder of Geila Ibram in Limerick was re-arraigned and pleaded guilty to a terrorist offence. The court accepted the plea in the same case arising from the April 2023 killing. Sentencing was listed for November 6, 2026.
