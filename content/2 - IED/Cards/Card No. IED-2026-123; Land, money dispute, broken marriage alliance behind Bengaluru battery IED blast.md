# Land, money dispute, broken marriage alliance behind Bengaluru battery IED blast; 3 held

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/bengaluru/land-money-dispute-broken-marriage-alliance-behind-bengaluru-battery-ied-blast-3-held/articleshow/135194563.cms)

(U) Bengaluru, India — The Times of India reported that police arrested three people in connection with a battery-powered improvised explosive device blast and attributed the suspected motive to a land and money dispute involving a broken marriage alliance. The motive and suspects’ roles were presented as the police investigation’s findings and allegations, not as established court findings.
