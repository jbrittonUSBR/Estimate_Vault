# Colombia: Car bomb attack injures at least 23

[Source](https://www.dw.com/en/colombia-car-bomb-attack-injures-at-least-23/a-80033956)

(U) VILLAVICENCIO, COLOMBIA — DW reported that a car bomb exploded outside the Meta provincial governor's office in Villavicencio on Monday night, injuring at least 23 people and severely damaging nearby buildings, while President Abelardo de la Espriella said no deaths had been reported "so far." No group claimed the attack, which came on the eve of the tenth anniversary of Colombia's FARC peace accord; DW noted De la Espriella had denounced the deal as "false peace" shortly before the blast. De la Espriella blamed "narco-terrorists" and named FARC-EMC commander Antonio Medina as responsible.
