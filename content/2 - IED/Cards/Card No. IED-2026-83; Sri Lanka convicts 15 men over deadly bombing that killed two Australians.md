# Sri Lanka convicts 15 men over deadly bombing that killed two Australians

[Source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-22/sri-lanka-convicts-15-men-over-deadly-easter-bombing/107183542)

(U) Colombo, Sri Lanka — A Sri Lankan court convicted 15 men on September 22, 2026 over the 2019 Easter Sunday bombings that killed more than 260 people, including two Australians. The coordinated attacks on churches and hotels were carried out by Islamic State-inspired suicide bombers, and the convictions come as part of long-running prosecutions related to one of Sri Lanka's deadliest terrorist attacks.
