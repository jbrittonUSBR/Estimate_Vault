# Dirty Bomb Blast Radius: How Far Could the Damage Spread?

[Source](https://modernsurvivalblog.com/nuclear/dirty-bomb-blast-radius/)

(U) City-specific — A dirty bomb could cause blast injuries near the explosion and spread radioactive contamination farther, but the author noted that there was no universal blast radius or safe-distance estimate. Citing the Nuclear Regulatory Commission, the article said contamination could reach several blocks or miles depending on the material and conditions, while detectable contamination did not necessarily mean a dangerous radiation dose. It described cleanup, restricted access, and economic disruption as potential consequences.
