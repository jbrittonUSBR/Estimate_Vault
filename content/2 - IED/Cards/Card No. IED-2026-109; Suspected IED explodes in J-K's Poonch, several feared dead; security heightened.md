# Suspected IED explodes in J-K's Poonch, several feared dead; security heightened

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/india/suspected-ied-explodes-in-j-ks-poonch-several-feared-dead-security-heightened/articleshow/134895103.cms)

(U) POONCH, India — On October 12, 2026, The Times of India reported a suspected improvised explosive device explosion near the Line of Control in Poonch district, with several feared dead on the Pakistan-occupied Kashmir side. Security was heightened along the LoC as Indian forces went on alert following reports of the blast, which was later confirmed to have killed four PoK residents.
