(U) RTD Transit Police K-9 units were honored by the National Police Canine Association on June 17, 2026, for their role in explosives and firearm detections during two investigations that assisted other Denver metro area law enforcement agencies. On December 21, 2025, Officer Corey Averill and K-9 Milo helped the Jefferson County Sheriff’s Office locate a firearm inside a residence within three minutes, earning a quarterly award for firearm detection. Five days later, Officer Jesse Robinson and K-9 Reign assisted the Cherry Hills Police Department in an arson investigation along the Highline Canal Trail by detecting a powdery substance that led to the identification of explosive materials, prompting ATF involvement and the discovery of significant explosives at a suspect’s home, resulting in an arrest; Reign received the NPCA Northwest Region Detection Case of the Quarter Award for Q4 2025. The four RTD K-9 units, funded by FEMA and trained to detect explosives, provide patrol coverage across the agency’s 2,349-square-mile district seven days a week and undergo 10 weeks of initial certification plus ongoing weekly training. RTD Chief of Police and Emergency Management Steve Martingano praised the teams’ success as a testament to strong interagency partnerships that enhance community safety.
