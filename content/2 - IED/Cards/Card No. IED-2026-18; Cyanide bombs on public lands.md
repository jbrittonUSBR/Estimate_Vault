(U) The June 25, 2026, edition of The Denver Post includes three letters to the editor. Dennis Shannon of Parker opposes the use of M-44 sodium cyanide devices, known as “cyanide bombs,” on public lands, arguing there is no justification for placing such lethal devices where hikers, campers, hunters, mountain bikers, children, and pets may encounter them and urging Congress to enact a permanent ban after reports of inadequate signage and warnings. Myra Louise Bender of Denver responds to a column on age as a factor in choosing leaders by noting its discussion of President Joe Biden while omitting any mention of 80-year-old President Donald Trump. Charles Bradley Sutton of Loveland criticizes Colorado Attorney General Phil Weiser for defending express-lane civil penalties, asserting that state agencies lack statutory authority for lanes not using shoulders, that courts have deferred to the AG’s arguments despite this, and questioning whether the AG’s duty is to shield agencies or protect the public given more than a million dollars in penalties collected amid budget shortfalls.
