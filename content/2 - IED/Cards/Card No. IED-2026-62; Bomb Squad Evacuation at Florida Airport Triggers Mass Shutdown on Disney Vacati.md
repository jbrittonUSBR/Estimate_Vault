# Bomb Squad Evacuation at Florida Airport Triggers Mass Shutdown on Disney Vacations

[Source](https://insidethemagic.net/2026/09/miami-airport-disney-world-travel-security-emd1/)

(U) MIAMI, United States — Reports described a bomb-squad response and a partial evacuation at Miami International Airport after a suspicious package was found, with no effect on Walt Disney World operations reported. The account, attributed to an 'OC Scanner' social media source, was not confirmed by MIA or the Miami-Dade Sheriff's Office and should be treated as unverified. The report followed a fatal Amazon Prime Air Boeing 767 cargo crash at the airport on September 6 that killed five people.
