# Gas pipeline explodes in Syria, knocks three power plants offline

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/30/gas-pipeline-explodes-in-syria-knocks-three-power-plants-offline)

(U) DAMASCUS, Syria — An explosion and fire at a gas pipeline in eastern Ghouta near Damascus on September 30, 2026 forced the Deir Ali, al-Nasiriyah and Tishreen power plants offline and ignited a blaze at Tishreen, the third blast to hit Syria's gas network in six weeks. Energy Minister Mohammed al-Bashir said the cause remained unclear and could be a technical malfunction, an attack or sabotage, while the Syrian Electricity Company warned of longer power rationing; hours later, state media reported that gunmen killed at least seven people on a bus in Homs province.
