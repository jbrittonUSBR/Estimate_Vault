# Amritsar police bust 6 drug & arms modules; 2 held in IED blast case

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/chandigarh/amritsar-police-bust-6-drug-arms-modules-2-held-in-ied-blast-case/articleshow/134924244.cms)

(U) AMRITSAR, India — Punjab police dismantled six drug and arms modules and arrested two suspects in connection with an IED blast case, seizing weapons and narcotics during coordinated raids. The operations pointed to cross-linked criminal networks involving explosives and trafficking, though further investigations were ongoing.
