(U) What began as a shelter-in-place over the weekend in unincorporated Boulder County has now shifted into an active investigation. The Boulder County Communications Center got a call reporting a suspicious item just after 8 a.m. Sunday, from a passerby near Ponderosa Way. Authorities say the caller had opened a metal case near the road and found five suspected improvised explosive devices or IEDs inside. A shelter-in-place order remained in effect from noon until about 7 p.m. while bomb technicians from the Boulder County Regional Bomb Squad worked to secure the scene. By Sunday evening, the devices had been transferred to a containment trailer and moved to a secure location. Technicians will continue examining and disposing of the devices before investigators can confirm whether they had the capability of exploding. The case leaves several unanswered questions this week, including who left the case near the roadway, how long it had been there and whether the devices were intentionally placed or abandoned. “We appreciate the community’s patience during this incident,” said the Boulder County Sheriff’s Office in a press release. “Bomb Squad technicians needed to adjust tactics during the operation for safety reasons, which extended the time needed to secure the devices. The safety of the community remains our highest priority.” No injuries were reported.
