# Pakistan teen arrested after Claude AI chats reveal bomb plot targeting college

[Source](https://indianexpress.com/article/world/lahore-teen-arrested-claude-ai-bomb-plot-fbi-anthropic-alert-10976701/)

(U) LAHORE, PAKISTAN — The Indian Express reported that Pakistan's National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency arrested a 17-year-old first-year college student at his home on Bedian Road in Lahore on Monday after his chats with Anthropic's Claude chatbot allegedly discussed making an explosive from household chemicals and attacking a government college. The outlet said the FBI and Anthropic alerted Pakistani authorities, and NCCIA Additional Director Atif Ameer said the agency acted on information about the student's online conversations. Investigators found no explosives, chemicals or weapons at the home, seized electronic devices for examination and registered a case under the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act and Pakistan Penal Code, according to the report.
