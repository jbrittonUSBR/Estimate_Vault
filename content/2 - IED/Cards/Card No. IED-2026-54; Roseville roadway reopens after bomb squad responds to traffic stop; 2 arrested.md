# Roseville roadway reopens after bomb squad responds to traffic stop; 2 arrested

[Source](https://www.kcra.com/article/roseville-bomb-squad-investigates-possible-explosive-device/73636100)

**Query:** `manual-url`

(U) Roseville police said a roadway has reopened after the bomb squad was called to investigate a possible explosive device found during a traffic stop on Monday night. One man was arrested on suspicion of driving under the influence and drug sale charges, police said. Another man, a passenger, was arrested for outstanding arrest warrants.
