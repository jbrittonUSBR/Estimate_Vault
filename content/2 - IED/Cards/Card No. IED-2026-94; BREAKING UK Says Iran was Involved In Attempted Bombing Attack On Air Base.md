# UK Says Iran Was Involved in Attempted Bombing Attack on Air Base

[Source](https://www.mediaite.com/media/news/breaking-uk-says-iran-was-involved-in-attempted-bombing-attack-on-air-base/)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — Prime Minister Andy Burnham said on September 30, 2026 that there were "strong indications" Iran played a part in an incident at R.A.F. Fairford, a base used by U.S. bombers, over the preceding weekend. Five men in their twenties were arrested near the base on suspicion of explosives and terrorism offences and later released on bail, after the village of Whelford was evacuated when three vans raised suspicions; Burnham defended the bail decision as subject to the "strictest possible bail conditions" while President Donald Trump criticised it.
