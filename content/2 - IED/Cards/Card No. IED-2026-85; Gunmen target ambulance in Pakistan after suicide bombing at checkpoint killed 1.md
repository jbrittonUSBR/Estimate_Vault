# Gunmen target ambulance in Pakistan after suicide bombing at checkpoint killed 12

[Source](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/asia/pakistan-suicide-bombing-police-checkpoint-ambulance-6412666)

(U) DERA ISMAIL KHAN, Pakistan — A suicide bombing at a police checkpoint killed at least 12 people and wounded about 35, after which gunmen opened fire on an ambulance evacuating victims, killing a rescue worker; the Pakistani Taliban (TTP) claimed responsibility for the attack in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province.
