# Retired Engineer Jailed 15 Years for Blowing Up ULEZ Camera with Homemade Bomb

[Source](https://news.sky.com/story/retired-engineer-jailed-for-blowing-up-ulez-camera-using-homemade-bomb-13594406)

(U) LONDON — Kevin Rees, 64, a retired electrical appliances engineer from Sidcup, southeast London, was sentenced at the Old Bailey on October 2, 2026 to 15 years' imprisonment (with a further year on licence) for using a homemade bomb to destroy a newly installed Ultra Low Emissions Zone (ULEZ) camera in Willersley Avenue, Sidcup, on December 6, 2023. Rees placed the device — a metal cylinder filled with black powder — next to the camera, lit the fuse, and ran before the explosion, which propelled shrapnel up to 100 metres into cars, a van, and the window frame of a child's bedroom; no one was injured. He was convicted at Woolwich Crown Court on January 28, 2026 of causing an explosion likely to endanger life or cause serious injury to property, and three counts of possessing a prohibited weapon after three working homemade stun guns were found at his home. The judge ruled the attack had a terrorism connection, and prosecutors noted the "force of the explosion was such that it could have caused multiple deaths." Rees was a committed anti-ULEZ campaigner whose social media posts celebrated damage to cameras.
