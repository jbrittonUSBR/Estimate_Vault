# Gardaí arrest two more men after Waterford arson attack that killed five

[Source](https://www.irishtimes.com/crime-law/2026/11/23/gardai-arrest-two-more-men-after-waterford-arson-attack-that-killed-five/)

(U) Waterford, Ireland — Gardaí arrested two additional suspects on Monday in the investigation into the October 13 fire that killed Philip and Kim English and their sons Callum, 12, and Jayden, 9, at the family home in the Birchwood estate outside Waterford city. The Irish Times reported the men, a Waterford man in his 20s and an adult teenager, were questioned about alleged links to a local drugs gang that detectives believe carried out the attack as part of a campaign of drug-related intimidation. A third child, six-year-old Elliot English, died in hospital on November 6, leaving the couple's 14-year-old daughter as the sole survivor, still in treatment; Monday's arrests brought to eight the number of people detained, two of whom have been charged.
