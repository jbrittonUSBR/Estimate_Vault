(U) MANSON, N.C. (WBTV) - State officials have said what caused an [**explosion**](https://www.wbtv.com/2026/05/26/several-workers-hurt-explosion-north-carolina-manufacturing-plant-officials-say/ "https://www.wbtv.com/2026/05/26/several-workers-hurt-explosion-north-carolina-manufacturing-plant-officials-say/") that left multiple workers hurt at a manufacturing plant in North Carolina last week. The explosion happened at International Paper on Highway 1 in Warren County just before 4 a.m. on Tuesday, May 26. The Warren County Sheriff’s Office [**said**](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/14eKucQgYqx/ "https://www.facebook.com/share/p/14eKucQgYqx/") three workers were hurt in the explosion. None of their injuries were life-threatening. Multiple fire and hazmat trucks were called to the facility. The North Carolina fire marshal’s office was investigating the explosion, and said it happened in a boiler room when there was a malfunction in the boiler system. The cause was said to have been *accidental*. International Paper’s facility in Warren County is one of nine in North Carolina. According to the company’s [**website**](https://www.internationalpaper.com/locations "https://www.internationalpaper.com/locations"), the plant makes packaging products, specifically boxes. Warren County is on the North Carolina-Virginia border, about an hour and a half north of Raleigh.
