# Australian man charged with ISIS-inspired bomb plot, downloading terror group material

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/11/20/world-news/australian-bomb-suspect-nowroz-rayed-amin-charged-with-isis-inspired-plot/)

(U) Sydney, Australia — Australian Federal Police arrested Nowroz Rayed Amin, 35, of Ingleburn, on November 20 and charged him with collecting documents likely to facilitate a terrorist act and possessing violent extremist material downloaded online. Deputy Commissioner Hilda Sirec told reporters police alleged he researched building a vehicle-borne improvised explosive device, but said investigators saw no link to the approaching first anniversary of the December 14 Bondi Beach attack, which killed 15 people and wounded 40. Amin appeared in Campbelltown Local Court the same day, entered no pleas and did not seek bail, and was remanded in custody; two other men were arrested on separate weapons and hindering charges during 10 search warrants.
