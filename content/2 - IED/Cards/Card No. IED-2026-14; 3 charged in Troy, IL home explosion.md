(U) ST. LOUIS, Mo. (First Alert 4) -Three people have been charged in connection with a home explosion that resulted in the deaths of three people in April. Madison County Sheriff’s Office has charged Colton Cissell, 22, with three counts of involuntary manslaughter, one count of possession of explosives or explosive incendiary devices, one count of endangering the life or health of a child and one count of possession of explosive material without a license. Kenneth Cissell, 48, was charged with one count of endangering the life or health of a child. Roberta Cissell, 43, is being charged with one count of endangering the life or health of a child. The home explosion occurred in the late hours of April 5 on West Kirsch Road in Troy, Illinois. The explosion claimed the lives of Madeleine Maxeiner, 18, Ayden Hendrickson, 19, and Paisley Nishwitz, 12. Madison County Sheriff’s Office had said their initial investigation shows there were multiple explosions that caused the fire and deaths. The initial explosion was due to the mishandling of commercial-grade energetic material. Colton Cissell had been sued in April by one of the family members of the victims. Colton Cissell has been arrested and is in custody in Madison County Jail. Kenneth Cissell and Roberta Cissell were later taken into custody after Colton Cissell was arrested.
