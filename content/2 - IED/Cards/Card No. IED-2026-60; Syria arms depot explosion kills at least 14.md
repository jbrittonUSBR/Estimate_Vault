# Syria arms depot explosion kills at least 14

[Source](https://www.dw.com/en/syria-arms-depot-explosion-kills-at-least-14/a-79203314)

(U) SAMARDA, Syria — An explosion at a temporary weapons storage site near Sarmada in Idlib province killed at least 14 people and wounded 11, Syria's Defense Ministry said, noting that weapons were being stored there temporarily. Secondary explosions hampered the response. It was the second fatal incident of its kind in Idlib that month, after an explosion in Binnish on September 1 killed five people.
