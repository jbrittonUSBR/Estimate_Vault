# Mississippi Chemical Plant Explosion Linked to Herbicide Fire

[Source](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/southeast/2026/11/05/891719.htm)

(U) Greenville, Mississippi, USA - Insurance Journal reported that an explosion and fire at the Loveland Products chemical plant on November 4 involved a herbicide chemical, according to preliminary information from plant management. Nearby residents were told to shelter in place because of smoke; no injuries were reported, and the order was lifted after the fire was contained.
