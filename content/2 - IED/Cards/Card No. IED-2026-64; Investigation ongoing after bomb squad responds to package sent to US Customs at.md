# Investigation ongoing after bomb squad responds to package sent to US Customs at Shannon

[Source](https://www.thejournal.ie/shannon-airport-suspicious-package-7156849-Sep2026/)

(U) SHANNON, Ireland — Gardaí were investigating after a suspicious package arrived at Shannon Airport addressed to US customs, prompting the Army's Explosive Ordnance Disposal team to attend the scene on the morning of August 25, 2026. A garda spokesperson said the package was contained and removed to an alternate location that would not impact airport operations, and bomb disposal personnel made it safe before handing over the scene. The Shannon Airport Group declined to comment during the active investigation.
