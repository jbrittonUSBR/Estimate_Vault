# How the wife of Sydney terror plot accused was jailed for translating bomb manuals for extremists in Bangladesh

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16313797/How-wife-Sydney-terror-plot-accused-jailed-translating-bomb-manuals-extremists-Bangladesh.html)

(U) DHAKA, BANGLADESH — The Daily Mail reported that Sadia Amin, a former pre-school teacher, was arrested in Dhaka in 2017 and charged with translating explosive manuals for a commander of Neo-Jama'at Mujahideen Bangladesh, an Islamic State offshoot also known as Islamic State Bangladesh, according to the Sunday Telegraph. Citing Bangladeshi authorities, the outlet said she admitted the charges in court and was incarcerated for an undisclosed period, while a local police major said she translated English instructions for making explosive devices into Bengali. The report noted that Nowroz Amin, described as her estranged husband, was arrested in Sydney on Friday on terrorism-related charges and that police said there was no information linking the two cases.
