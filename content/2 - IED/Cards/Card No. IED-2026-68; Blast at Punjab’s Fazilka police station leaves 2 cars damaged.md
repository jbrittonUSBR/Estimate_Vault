# Blast at Punjab’s Fazilka police station leaves 2 cars damaged

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/chandigarh/blast-at-punjabs-fazilka-police-station-leaves-2-cars-damaged/articleshow/134214299.cms)

(U) FAZILKA, India — An early Sunday explosion rocked the City Police Station in Punjab's Fazilka, shattering the window panes of two cars parked on the premises as case property, with no casualties reported. Superintendent of Police Satinder Kumar said the blast occurred around 2 a.m., after which a forensic team examined the site and investigators reviewed CCTV footage from the station and nearby areas to establish the cause. Shiromani Akali Dal chief Sukhbir Singh Badal blamed the Aam Aadmi Party government for the incident, calling it a "total collapse of law and order" in the state.
