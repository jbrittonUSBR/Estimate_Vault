(U) An explosion and subsequent fire occurred inside a garage at a home in the 900 block of Calville Estates Court in Henderson, Nevada, near Lake Mead Parkway and Athens Avenue, around 11:30 a.m. on June 24, 2026, prompting a large emergency response from the Henderson Fire Department and police. Two adults, four children, and two dogs were present; one adult man sustained serious injuries and was hospitalized, while the others were safely evacuated, and a police officer self-transported to a hospital for smoke inhalation. As a precaution, a nearby shopping center including the Learning Experience Academy of Early Education childhood education facility was temporarily evacuated, with parents picking up children and the order lifted by 1:40 p.m.; a portion of East Athens Avenue was also closed during the response. The incident caused significant damage to the home, rendering it uninhabitable, though no other nearby homes or the shopping center were affected, and crews had the scene under control by Wednesday afternoon. The cause of the explosion remains under investigation.
