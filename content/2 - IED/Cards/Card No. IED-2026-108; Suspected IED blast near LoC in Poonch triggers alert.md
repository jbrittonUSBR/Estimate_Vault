# Suspected IED blast near LoC in Poonch triggers alert

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/india/massive-ied-blast-near-loc-in-poonch-security-forces-on-high-alert/articleshow/134896196.cms)

(U) POONCH, India — On October 12, 2026, The Economic Times reported a suspected improvised explosive device blast near the Line of Control in Poonch district, prompting security forces to go on high alert. Indian officials heightened vigilance along the LoC as reports emerged of casualties on the Pakistan-occupied Kashmir side of the border; subsequent reports indicated four PoK residents were killed in the explosion.
