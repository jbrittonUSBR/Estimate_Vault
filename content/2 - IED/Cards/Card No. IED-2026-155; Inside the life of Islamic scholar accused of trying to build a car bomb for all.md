# Inside the life of Islamic scholar accused of trying to build a car bomb for alleged terror attack - before his arrest at a Sydney home

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16310583/sydney-terror-plot-car-bomb.html)

(U) Sydney, Australia — Counter-terrorism police raided 10 homes across Sydney just before 6 a.m. on November 20 and arrested three men, including 35-year-old Nowroz Rayed Amin in Ingleburn. AFP Deputy Commissioner Hilda Sirec said detectives alleged Amin researched how to build a vehicle-borne improvised explosive device for a possible attack but found no identified target, no materials obtained and no imminent threat, and said the case was not linked to the approaching Bondi Beach attack anniversary. Amin was charged with collecting terrorism-related documents and possessing violent extremist material, while the other two men faced hindering-police and firearms charges.
