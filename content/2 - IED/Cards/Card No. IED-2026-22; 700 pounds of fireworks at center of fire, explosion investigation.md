(U) A fire and explosion at a home on Smugglers Cove Road in Greenbank, Washington, on Wednesday, June 25, 2026, centered on approximately 700 pounds of fireworks stored inside, leveling the residence at 3670 Smugglers Cove Road, causing significant damage to a neighboring home, and blast damage to a third. The incident, reported around 2:45 p.m., injured three firefighters from Central Whidbey Island Fire and Rescue and South Whidbey Fire/EMS—who were hospitalized but released by Friday morning, with one nearly losing a finger—and possibly two residents, while firefighters rescued dogs amid popping fireworks. The cause remains under investigation by the Region 3 Arson Task Force in coordination with the ATF, with a possible cigarette ignition noted but unconfirmed; four pallets of fireworks had been delivered recently, and some unexploded items remained at the scene. Displaced residents, including Tanya Hernandez and her family who spent the night in a truck, received aid from the Red Cross and community fundraising via GoFundMe, as witnesses described loud explosions, shaking ground, and a large black smoke cloud. This echoes a prior 2025 fireworks-related explosion on Whidbey Island that killed a resident.
