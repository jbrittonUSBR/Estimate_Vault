# From the Lockerbie bombing to 9/11: How attacks on flights defined US air travel

[Source](https://abcnews.com/Business/wireStory/lockerbie-bombing-911-attacks-flights-defined-us-air-136267276)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — An AP report traced how attacks on commercial flights, from the 1988 Lockerbie bombing to the September 11, 2001 hijackings, shaped the modern U.S. air travel experience and its security regime. The article recounted the bombings and hijackings that prompted measures such as reinforced cockpit doors and expanded screening by the Transportation Security Administration. It examined how decades of aviation terrorism left a lasting mark on how Americans fly.
