# Intel chief warns of 'evolving' threats as terror groups now 'recruit their agents online'

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/media/intel-chief-warns-evolving-threats-terror-groups-recruit-agents-online)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton said on Fox News' "Special Report" that terrorist groups now "recruit their agents online" and radicalize them domestically, describing an evolving threat 25 years after the September 11, 2001 attacks. Clayton spoke after host Bret Baier cited United Nations figures estimating al Qaeda's global network at roughly 25,000 potential fighters, and pointed to the March attempt by two Pennsylvania men to detonate explosives outside Gracie Mansion in New York as evidence that foreign training camps are no longer required. He said coordination among intelligence and law enforcement agencies had improved substantially since 9/11.
