# Dissident republican was like 'spider at centre of web' in plotting Omagh bombing, inquiry hears

[Source](https://www.thejournal.ie/omagh-bombing-inquiry-2-7168778-Sep2026/)

(U) BELFAST — The Omagh Bombing Inquiry opened evidential hearings on Monday, where lead counsel Paul Greaney KC said dissident republican Colm Murphy "sat like a spider at the centre of the web" of the 1998 plot. Greaney described a group of "experienced terrorists" who stole a Vauxhall Cavalier in Carrickmacross and fitted it with cloned plates before the Real IRA bomb killed 29 people in the County Tyrone town on August 15, 1998. The inquiry, chaired by Lord Turnbull, is examining whether UK authorities could have prevented the attack, the worst single atrocity of the Troubles.
