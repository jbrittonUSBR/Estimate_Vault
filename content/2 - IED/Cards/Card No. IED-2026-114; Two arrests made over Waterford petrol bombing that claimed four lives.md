# Two arrests made over Waterford petrol bombing that claimed four lives

[Source](https://www.irishtimes.com/crime-law/2026/10/15/two-arrests-made-over-waterford-petrol-bombing-that-claimed-four-lives/)

(U) WATERFORD, IRELAND — The Irish Times reported that a man and a woman were arrested in connection with a petrol-bomb arson attack in Waterford that killed four members of a family. The arrests followed searches across the city and came two days after the attack at a home in the Birchwood estate. The investigation was ongoing, and the report did not identify the suspects.
