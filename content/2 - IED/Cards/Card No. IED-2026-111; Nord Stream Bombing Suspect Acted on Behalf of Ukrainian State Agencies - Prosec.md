# Nord Stream Bombing Suspect Acted on Behalf of Ukrainian State Agencies - Prosecutor

[Source](https://sputnikglobe.com/20261014/nord-stream-bombing-suspects-acted-on-behalf-of-ukrainian-state-agencies---prosecutor-1124879818.html)

(U) BERLIN, Germany — A German prosecutor stated that the main suspect in the 2022 Nord Stream pipeline bombings had acted on behalf of Ukrainian state agencies, according to statements made as the trial proceeded. (U) The claim, reported by state-affiliated Sputnik, remained contested and was one of several competing narratives about responsibility for the explosions.
