(U) Bab-el-Mandeb, Yemen — On October 5, 2026, Saudi-backed Yemeni forces expelled Houthi rebels from several areas around the strategic Bab-el-Mandeb Strait, according to officials; the advance aimed to secure the key maritime chokepoint amid ongoing fighting and repeated Houthi attacks on shipping.
