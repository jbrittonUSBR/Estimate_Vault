# Car bomb injures 20 in Colombia ahead of 10th anniversary of peace deal with FARC rebels

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/rest-of-world/car-bomb-injures-20-in-colombia-ahead-of-10th-anniversary-of-farc-peace-deal/articleshow/136125201.cms)

(U) VILLAVICENCIO, COLOMBIA — The Times of India, citing AP, reported that around 20 people were injured when a car bomb exploded in Villavicencio on Monday, destroying the Meta governor's office and damaging nearby buildings. President Abelardo de la Espriella wrote on X that "narco-terrorists" were responsible and confirmed no fatalities, while no group claimed the attack. TOI reported the blast came on the eve of the tenth anniversary of the FARC peace deal and noted that De la Espriella, who took office in August, had called the accord "false peace" minutes earlier.
