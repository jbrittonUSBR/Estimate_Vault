(U) Los Angeles County District Attorney Nathan J. Hochman on June 24, 2026, highlighted multiple recent prosecutions involving large quantities of illegal fireworks and explosives ahead of the July 4 holiday, vowing aggressive enforcement against those who manufacture, distribute, store, or sell such devices. In one case, four individuals were charged with 10 felony counts each after authorities seized 8,500 pounds of illegal fireworks and improvised explosive devices from an East Los Angeles storage facility on June 23. Other cases include a South Los Angeles family charged in connection with over 37,000 pounds of fireworks found in a home where a child lived, and a major 2024 seizure of 75 tons of mortar-style fireworks from a Gardena storage unit. Hochman cited recent deadly explosions in the county, including incidents in Jefferson Park, Bell Gardens, and Pacoima that killed several people and injured others, warning that illegal fireworks are not harmless and can cause catastrophic harm. He emphasized interagency collaboration and urged the public to report violations and attend professional fireworks displays instead.
