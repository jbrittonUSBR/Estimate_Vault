# 'Iran bomb plot' at RAF base foiled by farmer's 999 call: Five arrested in early hours after three vans spotted near air base used by US bombers

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16165239/Iran-bomb-plot-RAF-base.html)

(U) FAIRFORD — A farmer spotted three vans about 350 yards from RAF Fairford and called police at 12:45 a.m., leading to the arrest of five men in what British officials investigated as an Iranian-backed bomb plot, the Daily Mail reported. About 85 households were evacuated and the vans reportedly carried fake "Fuel2U" livery; the base is used by U.S. bombers. The report said Iran was suspected, but no charges or confirmed Iranian operational link had been announced at the time of publication.
