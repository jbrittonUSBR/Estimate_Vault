(U) Israeli prosecutors formally charged a 20-year-old man from a southern Bedouin town with planning a car-bomb attack against a popular nightclub in Beersheba, following a joint investigation by the police and the Shin Bet security agency.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Target & Plot:** The suspect, Abdullah Gergawi from Segev Shalom, allegedly took concrete steps to manufacture an explosive device to target Beersheba's Forum nightclub.
- **Radicalization & Connections:** According to authorities, Gergawi was heavily exposed to Islamic State (ISIS) propaganda on social media. Investigators found he participated in Telegram chat groups dedicated to the terrorist organization and communicated with Islamic State enthusiasts, as well as an individual who presented himself as a Palestinian terror operative.
- **Weapons Cache:** During the investigation, authorities discovered that Gergawi had access to a substantial arsenal in 2025—including machine guns (MAG and Negev) and assault rifles (Kalashnikovs and M16s)—and was additionally charged with illegal weapons possession and firing an M-16 knockoff in an open area earlier in the year.
- **Broader Security Context:** The case follows a pattern of arrests involving local citizens suspected of plotting attacks on behalf of the Islamic State, highlighting ongoing counterterrorism vigilance in Israel against home-grown radicalization.
