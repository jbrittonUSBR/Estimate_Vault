# Chaos Reigns as Army Plans to Explode Toxic Lead “Dirty Bomb” Over Populated Area

[Source](https://futurism.com/science-energy/us-army-delay-explosion-lead)

(U) MIDDLETOWN, IOWA — Futurism reported that the U.S. Army repeatedly delayed its planned controlled detonation of about 9,700 pounds of unstable lead azide in 68 drums at an Iowa munitions plant, moving it from Monday to Tuesday and then Wednesday, while Middletown residents waited to evacuate; the outlet cited a White House source, quoted by the New York Post, calling it a "dirty bomb." The Army said, as quoted by Army Times, that chemical neutralization would have required seven to 22 days of hands-on drum handling and was deemed too risky. Futurism reported that environmental groups sued the EPA after it authorized the blast, that Trump on Monday ordered a wait for the courts and governor-elect Rob Sand, and that the Army cited "more optimal weather conditions" for the delay.
