# Massive blast tears through Syria weapons depot, 14 killed in Idlib - video

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/middle-east/massive-blast-tears-through-syria-weapons-depot-14-killed-in-idlib-video/articleshow/133972344.cms)

(U) SAMARDA, Syria — An explosion at a weapons warehouse in Syria's Idlib province near Sarmada killed at least 14 people and injured 11, according to state television and the health ministry. The cause of the blast was not immediately established. The site was reported to have stored weapons and remnants of war.
