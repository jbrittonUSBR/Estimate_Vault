(U) A man set off fireworks and ignited a small fire outside 26 Federal Plaza in New York City (home to an immigration court, ICE offices, and other federal agencies) around 9 a.m. on July 20. One federal employee and one civilian were injured; both were treated and released. The suspect, identified as 43-year-old Andrew Arrabaca (a U.S. Army veteran with a reported history of anti-ICE activism), was arrested. Investigators recovered axes, knives, a hammer, a machete, multiple incendiary devices/fireworks, and anti-ICE literature from him. The FBI described him as having “bad intentions” and labeled him an anti-government extremist. He had not yet been charged as of the article’s update. The incident caused road closures, transit disruptions, and a large emergency response. The FBI’s Joint Terrorism Task Force is investigating.
