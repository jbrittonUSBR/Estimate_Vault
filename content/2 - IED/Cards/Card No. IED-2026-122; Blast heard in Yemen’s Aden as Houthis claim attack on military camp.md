# Blast heard in Yemen’s Aden as Houthis claim attack on military camp

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/21/blast-heard-in-yemens-aden-as-houthis-claim-attack-on-military-camp)

(U) October 21, 2026 — Al Jazeera reported that a blast and smoke were observed near Aden airport, while Houthi spokesman Yahya Saree claimed the group had fired a ballistic missile at a Saudi-led coalition operations room at Badr camp. Yemen’s military separately accused the Houthis of attacks on airports in Aden and Mukalla and a factory in Taiz; those claims were not independently confirmed in the report. The Yemeni government and Saudi-led coalition had not immediately commented on the Houthi claim.
