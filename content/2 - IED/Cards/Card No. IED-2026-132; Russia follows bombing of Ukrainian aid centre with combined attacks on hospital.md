# Russia follows bombing of Ukrainian aid centre with combined attacks on hospitals and hospices

[Source](https://www.irishtimes.com/world/europe/2026/11/04/russia-follows-bombing-of-ukrainian-aid-centre-with-combined-attacks-on-hospitals-and-hospices/)

(U) Kharkiv and Dnipro, Ukraine - The Irish Times reported that a Russian strike on a Kharkiv hospital injured 73 people and led to the evacuation of 226 patients, according to local officials. It also described a missile strike on a Dnipro hospital and hospice that displaced more than 100 patients, including terminally ill people; officials reported two deaths and 20 injuries in the wider Dnipro strikes.
