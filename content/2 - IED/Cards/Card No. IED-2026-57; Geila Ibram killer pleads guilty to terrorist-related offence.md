# Geila Ibram killer pleads guilty to terrorist-related offence

[Source](https://www.irishtimes.com/crime-law/courts/2026/09/08/geila-ibram-killer-pleads-guilty-to-terrorist-related-offence/)

(U) BELFAST, United Kingdom — Habib Shah Shamel, 29, pleaded guilty at Belfast Crown Court to a terrorist-related offence, the Irish Times reported. He had already admitted the murder of Geila Ibram, who was killed in Limerick in April 2023. A tariff hearing in the case was listed for November 6, 2026.
