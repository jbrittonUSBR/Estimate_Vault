# Crude bomb explodes behind Khilgaon police station, one held

[Source](https://tob.news/crude-bomb-explodes-behind-khilgaon-police-station-one-held/)

(U) DHAKA, BANGLADESH — The Times of Bangladesh reported that a crude bomb exploded behind Khilgaon Police Station in Dhaka on Tuesday evening, beside a Bata showroom a few buildings from the station, and that police detained Mazharul Islam, 30, after reviewing CCTV footage. Khilgaon station Officer-in-Charge Md Shafiqul Islam confirmed the detention and said Mazharul claimed to be a rickshaw puller but had no rickshaw, while preliminary information suggested he had carried out crude bomb explosions at multiple Dhaka locations. The officer said police were also investigating a motorcycle suspected of having brought him to the scene and were seeking its rider.
