# Afghan man tied to Abbey Gate bombing sentenced to 20 years for supporting ISIS

[Source](https://abcnews.com/International/afghan-man-tied-abbey-gate-bombing-sentenced-20/story?id=136501479)

(U) ALEXANDRIA, Va. — Mohammad Sharifullah, an Afghan man arrested in 2025 in connection with the Abbey Gate suicide bombing, was sentenced on September 16, 2026 to 20 years in prison for providing material support to ISIS-K. A federal jury convicted Sharifullah in April after he admitted in FBI interviews that he helped the group plot dozens of attacks over roughly a decade, but the jury deadlocked on whether his support was directly responsible for the 13 American service members and about 160 Afghan civilians killed at Kabul's Hamid Karzai International Airport in August 2021. U.S. District Judge Anthony Trenga imposed the maximum sentence, saying there was "no doubt" Sharifullah was a "dedicated terrorist," after victims' relatives testified during a 2 1/2-hour hearing.
