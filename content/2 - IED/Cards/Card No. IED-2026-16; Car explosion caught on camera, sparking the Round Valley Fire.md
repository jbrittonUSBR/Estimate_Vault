(U) A vehicle fire on State Route 87 near Fountain Hills, Arizona, exploded on June 24, 2026, sparking the Round Valley Wildfire that burned about 30 acres of brush on the southbound side of the highway south of Snowflake in the Tonto National Forest. The incident prompted the closure of both directions of SR 87 for roughly two hours in the afternoon, with hotshot crews responding quickly to stop the fire’s forward progress. Video footage captured thick black smoke rising from the vehicle before a large explosion sent parts flying, leaving the surrounding land scorched, as confirmed by Arizona Department of Transportation reports and Tonto National Forest statements. The cause of the initial car fire and whether anyone was injured remain unknown. Fire crews were still on scene into the evening as the blaze was contained.
