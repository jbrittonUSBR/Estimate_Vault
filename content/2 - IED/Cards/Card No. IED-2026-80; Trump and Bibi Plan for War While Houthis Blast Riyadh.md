# Trump and Bibi Plan for War While Houthis Blast Riyadh

[Source](https://www.globalresearch.ca/trump-bibi-plan-war-houthis-blast-riyadh/5941228)

(U) RIYADH — Houthi forces launched ballistic and cruise missiles and drones at targets in Saudi Arabia on Saturday, setting a fuel storage facility ablaze and temporarily disrupting flights at King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh, according to Houthi military spokesman Yahya al-Sarea. Saudi Civil Defense issued alerts for a "hostile aerial threat" after residents and journalists reported explosions. Global Research, a conspiracy-oriented opinion site, framed the strike as evidence that the Houthis had entered a dangerous new phase of the wider regional confrontation and warned of further attacks on Saudi energy infrastructure.
