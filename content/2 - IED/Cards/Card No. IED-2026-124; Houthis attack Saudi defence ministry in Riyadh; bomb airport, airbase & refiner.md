# Houthis attack Saudi defence ministry in Riyadh; bomb airport, airbase & refinery

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/middle-east/houthis-attack-saudi-defence-ministry-in-riyadh-bomb-airport-airbase-refinery/articleshow/135236826.cms)

(U) Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — Yemen’s Houthis claimed they attacked Saudi Arabia’s Defense Ministry in Riyadh and struck other targets, including an airport, airbase and refinery, The Times of India reported. The Houthi claims were not independently confirmed in the article, and the outlet described the attack as a possible escalation in the conflict.
