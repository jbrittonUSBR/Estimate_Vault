# Red Fort blast suicide bomber stored urine in polythene: NIA finds PDF with instruction on how to make a bomb using urine

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/india/red-fort-blast-suicide-bomber-stored-urine-in-polythene-nia-finds-pdf-with-instruction-on-how-to-make-a-bomb-using-urine/articleshow/134005855.cms)

(U) NEW DELHI — India's National Investigation Agency chargesheet said Umar-un-Nabi, the accused mastermind of the November 2025 Red Fort car blast, stored his urine in a polythene bag while hiding in a house in Nuh, prompting a foul-smell complaint from the homeowner. The chargesheet cited a PDF recovered from the accused's pen drives that described preparing explosives using urea nitrate and mentioned extracting urea from urine as an alternative source. The NIA alleged Nabi, who died in the blast, was the operational head of a revived Ansar Ghazwat-ul-Hind module and that the vehicle-borne device killed 11 people and injured 29 near the Red Fort.
