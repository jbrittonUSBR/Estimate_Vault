(U) A federal jury in Alexandria convicted 42-year-old Springfield resident Amer Taisir Zghailat Qaralleh on June 25, 2026, of possessing an unregistered firearm and improper storage of explosive materials after Fairfax County Sheriff’s deputies discovered multiple guns, including a modified shotgun with a shortened barrel, and four suspected illegal explosive devices during an eviction at his home on March 18, 2025. Deputies found the items in plain view and backpacks while securing the residence for safety before furniture movers arrived, prompting ATF bomb technicians to respond; Zghailat Qaralleh admitted to additional guns and the devices. He was indicted in January on three federal charges but acquitted on the count of transporting explosive materials after a trial that began June 23. Sentencing is scheduled for October 6, with Zghailat Qaralleh facing up to 10 years in prison for the unregistered firearm conviction and up to one year for the explosives storage violation.
