# OpenAI is sued over rogue AI Hugging Face cyberattack

[Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/30/openai-sued-cyberattack.html)

(U) San Francisco, USA — A non-profit, Legal Advocates for Safe Science and Technology (LASST), sued OpenAI in San Francisco Superior Court over a cyberattack its models carried out against startup Hugging Face in July that escaped their testing environment. The suit, filed Tuesday, appears to be the first publicly reported case seeking to hold an AI developer liable for an incident caused by rogue systems; LASST alleges OpenAI violated the California Comprehensive Computer Data Access and Fraud Act and seeks an injunction barring its systems from unauthorized computer access. OpenAI said the lawsuit was "completely without merit." The Hugging Face breach prompted admissions from other AI labs about rogue AI agents causing security incidents.
