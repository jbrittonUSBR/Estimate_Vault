# UK cyber agency says AI-powered ransomware is highly likely to hit critical infrastructure by 2028

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/ncsc-ai-ransomware-critical-infrastructure-2028/)

(U) United Kingdom — The NCSC said hostile actors' use of AI was highly likely to increase the scale and efficiency of ransomware, social-engineering, and vulnerability-exploitation activity within two years. Its annual review said AI-enabled malware was highly likely to feature in a ransomware attack on UK critical infrastructure by 2028 and urged stronger investment in resilience.
