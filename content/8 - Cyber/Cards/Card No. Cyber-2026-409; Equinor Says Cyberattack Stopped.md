# Equinor Says Cyberattack Stopped

[Source](https://www.rigzone.com/news/equinor_says_cyberattack_stopped-20-nov-2026-185131-article/)

(U) Stavanger, Norway — Rigzone reported that Equinor said on November 20 that a cyberattack it had disclosed three days earlier was 'stopped,' with production running normally and no indication that data had been stolen or altered. Equinor had said on Thursday that the attack was still ongoing and did not appear to specifically target the company, and had ruled out a virus attack the day before. The company said it reported the matter to police. Rigzone also reported that Norwegian utility Skagerak Energi disclosed an attempted cyberattack a day after Equinor's initial disclosure and later said its operations remained unaffected.
