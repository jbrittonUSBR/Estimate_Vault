# LiteLLM Compromised in Fresh Supply Chain Attack

[Source](https://www.securityweek.com/litellm-compromised-in-fresh-supply-chain-attack/)

(U) International — SecurityWeek reported that attackers used stolen GitHub credentials to publish malicious LiteLLM 1.107.0 packages on PyPI and Docker on 21 October. The packages, online for about three hours, installed an information stealer and remote-access component.
