# Britain on notice: AI-powered ransomware attacks to arrive by 2028

[Source](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/11/03/britain-on-notice-ai-powered-ransomware-attacks-to-arrive-by-2028/5307200)

(U) United Kingdom — The Register reported that the NCSC assessed AI-powered malware was highly likely to be part of a ransomware attack on UK critical infrastructure by 2028. The agency warned that AI could scale attacks exploiting longstanding weaknesses and also described defensive work to identify vulnerabilities in government systems.
