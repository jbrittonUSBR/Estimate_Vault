# Caught cold: fridge cyberattack highlights internet gadget weaknesses

[Source](https://www.digitaljournal.com/article/caught-cold-fridge-cyberattack-highlights-internet-gadget-weaknesses/)

(U) Europe — AFP reported that refrigeration cyberattacks raised temperatures at supermarket chains in Belgium, Estonia, Finland, Ireland, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, and Sweden, forcing stores to discard spoiled food. Experts cited internet-exposed controllers and weak or compromised credentials as risks and said investigations had not established who was responsible.
