# French supermarkets face millions in losses after cyberattack hits refrigerators nationwide

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/tech/french-supermarkets-face-millions-losses-cyberattack-hits-refrigerators-nationwide)

(U) FRANCE — Fox News reported that a cyberattack affected refrigeration systems at multiple French supermarket chains, causing some refrigerators to heat to about 122°F and requiring stores to discard fresh food. Carrefour said roughly 80 stores were affected, while Monoprix reported impacts at about 5% of its stores. The article cited experts’ estimate of losses exceeding €10 million, while noting the total had not been officially calculated.
