# ShinyHunters expose 6.4M in attack on medical supplier McKesson

[Source](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/09/10/shinyhunters-expose-64m-in-attack-on-medical-supplier-mckesson/5295550)

(U) SAN FRANCISCO, United States — The cyberattack on medical and pharmaceutical supplier McKesson affected roughly 6.4 million individuals, according to the breach notification service Have I Been Pwned, The Register reported. HIBP added data leaked by the ShinyHunters extortion group, which had initially claimed to steal 284 million documents from the company in August and issued a $55.2 million extortion demand that went unpaid. The exposed records included names, contact details, dates of birth, employer details and sensitive health information, though HIBP did not include Social Security numbers in its analysis; McKesson had not publicly confirmed the scale of the breach.
