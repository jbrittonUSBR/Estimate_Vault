(U) Qilin ransomware claimed 1,358 victims in the period tracked by Black Kite (a 443% increase from the prior year). The group operated across more than 50 countries and accounted for roughly one in every five to six disclosed ransomware victims. Overall ransomware activity also rose: Black Kite recorded 7,551 publicly disclosed victims between April 2025 and March 2026 (up 24.9% year-over-year), with March alone hitting a record 861 victims. Manufacturing was the most targeted sector, followed by professional/scientific/technical services. Mid-sized firms ($50–100 million revenue) saw growing pressure. Many organizations remained vulnerable after recovery—43.5% still had critical unpatched issues and 30.8% had known exploited vulnerabilities. The report emphasizes ongoing exposure through credentials, third-party connections, and unpatched systems.
