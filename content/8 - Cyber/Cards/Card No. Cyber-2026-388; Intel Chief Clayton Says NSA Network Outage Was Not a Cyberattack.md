# Intel Chief Clayton Says NSA Network Outage Was Not a Cyberattack

[Source](https://www.breitbart.com/tech/2026/11/18/intel-chief-clayton-says-nsa-network-outage-was-not-a-cyberattack/)

(U) Maryland — Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton told lawmakers the NSA network outage was caused by maintenance and technical changes, not a cyberattack or coordinated state-sponsored action. The NSA described a temporary degradation during scheduled maintenance, while the report said the classified NSANet outage affected communications and prompted staff to work from home. Officials said mission-critical collection systems remained operational and the network was restored by Wednesday evening.
