# #StopRansomware: SafePay Ransomware

[Source](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories/aa26-293a)

(U) WASHINGTON, DC — CISA, the FBI and the U.S. Secret Service, alongside Germany's Landeskriminalamt Rheinland-Pfalz and UK authorities, released a joint advisory on October 20, 2026, detailing the tactics, techniques, procedures and indicators of compromise of the SafePay ransomware group. The advisory stated that SafePay actors have been highly active since August 2024, targeting businesses and critical infrastructure across North America, South America, Europe and Australia, and were among the most active ransomware groups in 2026, impacting at least 600 separate victims across multiple critical infrastructure sectors. It described initial access through stolen or guessed credentials, brute-force attacks, phishing and exploitation of known vulnerabilities in edge devices, followed by account manipulation, use of living-off-the-land binaries, security-tool impairment, data exfiltration and encryption using a hybrid ChaCha20/RSA scheme with the ".SafePay" extension. The advisory recommended prioritizing patching of known exploited vulnerabilities, implementing identity and access management with multifactor authentication, maintaining immutable offline backups, network segmentation, and reporting incidents to the FBI and CISA.
