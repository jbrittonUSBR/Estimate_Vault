# Burnaby school district warned student and family data may have been compromised

[Source](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/british-columbia/buranby-schools-cyberattack-update-9.7381425)

(U) BURNABY, British Columbia — CBC News reported that Burnaby School District warned families that information may have been compromised in a September cyberattack. The district said potentially exposed data included identity documents, student assessments, specialized-support and medical information, and bus-routing details. The district had notified parents and guardians; the report did not identify an attacker or confirm which specific records had been accessed.
