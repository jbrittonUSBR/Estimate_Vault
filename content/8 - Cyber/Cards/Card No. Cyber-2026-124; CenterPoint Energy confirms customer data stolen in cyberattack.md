# CenterPoint Energy confirms customer data stolen in cyberattack

[Source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/centerpoint-energy-confirms-customer-data-stolen-in-cyberattack/)

(U) HOUSTON, United States — CenterPoint Energy disclosed a breach compromising some customers' personal information after a threat actor leaked data allegedly stolen from the Houston-based utility, which serves about 7 million metered electric and natural gas customers in Indiana, Minnesota, Ohio and Texas. The investigation began after the company found an online post from an actor using the alias "4d722e4d656f77" claiming to have stolen 7.49 million customer records containing names, phone numbers, service and billing addresses, account numbers, billing amounts and partial Social Security numbers; the actor said the data was exfiltrated by iterating through millions of IDs on a public API that lacked rate limiting and a web application firewall. In a filing with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, CenterPoint confirmed an unauthorized third party obtained personal information through an external-facing system but did not name the actor, the number of affected customers or the data types, and said electric and gas services were not impacted. Class actions have since been filed alleging the breach occurred between August 17 and September 1.
