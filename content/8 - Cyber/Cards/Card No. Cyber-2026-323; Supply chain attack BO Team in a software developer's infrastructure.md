# Supply chain attack: BO Team in a software developer's infrastructure

[Source](https://ics-cert.kaspersky.com/publications/reports/2026/11/05/supply-chain-attack-bo-team-in-a-software-developers-infrastructure/?utm_source=ics-cert.kaspersky.com&utm_mediumrss&utm_campaignmain)

(U) Russia — Kaspersky ICS CERT reported that the BO Team had maintained access to an unnamed Russian software developer's network for months and compromised its development and code-signing infrastructure. Kaspersky said the access created a potential software supply-chain risk, but investigators detected the intrusion before the attackers achieved that apparent objective.
