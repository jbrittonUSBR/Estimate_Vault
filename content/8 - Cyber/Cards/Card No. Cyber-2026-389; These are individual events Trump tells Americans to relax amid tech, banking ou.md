# "These are individual events": Trump tells Americans to "relax" amid tech, banking outages, dismisses cyberattack fears

[Source](https://m.economictimes.com/news/international/world-news/these-are-individual-events-trump-tells-americans-to-relax-amid-tech-and-banking-outages-dismisses-cyberattack-fears/articleshow/135976836.cms)

(U) Shenzhen — President Donald Trump urged Americans not to panic over recent technology and banking disruptions, describing them as individual events rather than a coordinated cyberattack. Trump said he would alert the public if there were a genuine crisis. The Economic Times reported that administration officials said they had found no evidence connecting the outages or indicating a systemic cyber threat; the named organizations separately attributed incidents to technical causes or maintenance.
