# Cyberattack on service provider exposes payee data at 40 Irish credit unions

[Source](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/11/19/cyberattack-on-service-provider-exposes-payee-data-at-40-irish-credit-unions/5310979)

(U) Ireland — Attackers exploited a vulnerability in Wellington IT’s online banking platform in October, exposing saved payee details associated with around 40 Irish credit unions, The Register reported. The potentially exposed fields included payee names, IBANs, BICs, payment references and addresses; affected credit unions said login credentials and funds were not exposed. Ireland’s minister responsible for credit unions criticized the delay in notifying institutions, while Wellington IT said it notified customers after confirming a potential data risk.
