# European Commission confirms AI-generated cyberattack disrupted its systems

[Source](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/11/20/european-commission-ai-generated-cyberattack-publicly-available-tool/)

(U) Brussels, Belgium — European Commission spokesperson Thomas Regnier confirmed at a November 19 press briefing that the institution detected an 'AI generated cyber campaign' on October 22 and temporarily took several applications offline as a preventive measure, with most later restored. Regnier said the attackers misused a publicly available AI tool but declined to name it or attribute the campaign, and said investigators had found no evidence of a staff data breach or ransomware demand. Politico first reported the incident and reported that CERT-EU had observed use of an AI penetration-testing framework called ARTEX; Regnier said the Commission had notified CERT-EU, the European Data Protection Supervisor and affected services.
