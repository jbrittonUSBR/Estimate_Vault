# Trump thinks there may be upside to reported cyberattack on critical FBI database: ‘Russia, Russia, Russia hoax’

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/11/21/us-news/trump-talks-reported-hacking-attempt-of-critical-fbi-database-russia-russia-russia/)

(U) Washington, D.C. — President Donald Trump said in a Fox News interview on Saturday that a reported cyberattack on the FBI's National Crime Information Center database could have a "positive" side by revealing information about the Russia investigation he calls a hoax. CNN reported Friday that the FBI was investigating a suspected hack of the database, the New York Post reported. Trump also repeated claims of widespread 2020 voter fraud and referenced his impeachment over a phone call with Ukraine's president; the article noted the fraud claims were rejected by courts.
