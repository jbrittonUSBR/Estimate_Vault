# North Korea’s Lazarus Operates Through Six Distinct Cyber Clusters

[Source](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/north-korea-lazarus-six-cyber/)

(U) — Researchers from Sekoia and Kudelski Security reported that North Korea's Lazarus umbrella operates as six distinct cyber clusters — TEMP.Hermit, Citrine Sleet, CryptoCore, Jade Sleet, Moonstone Sleet and Famous Chollima — under the country's main military intelligence bureau, the GRIB. The analysis, based on tactics, techniques and procedures, found clusters divided between espionage, financially motivated operations and sanctions evasion, with the former APT38 unit likely split into CryptoCore and Jade Sleet. It also described thousands of North Korean IT workers operating under false identities whose salaries were remitted to the regime and whose employment access supported financial theft and espionage.
