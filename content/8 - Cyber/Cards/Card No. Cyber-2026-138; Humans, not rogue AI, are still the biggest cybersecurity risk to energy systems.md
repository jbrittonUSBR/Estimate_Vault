[Source](https://www.theverge.com/science/997834/ai-cyberattack-energy-critical-infrastructure)

(U) United States — Cybersecurity experts said aging energy infrastructure, limited patching capacity, and AI-empowered adversaries make human-driven attacks the primary risk to power systems, while fully autonomous AI-driven attacks remain a secondary concern for now.
