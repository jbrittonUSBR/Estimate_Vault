# Week in review: Linux rootkit deployed on F5 BIG-IP APM devices, Cisco FMC bugs exploited

[Source](https://44.231.18.133/2026/09/13/week-in-review-linux-rootkit-deployed-on-f5-big-ip-apm-devices-cisco-fmc-bugs-exploited/)

(U) ZAGREB, Croatia — Help Net Security's weekly roundup reported that a Linux rootkit found on compromised F5 BIG-IP APM devices hid a web shell in memory rather than writing it to disk, according to research by Sophos. The digest also covered active exploitation of CVE-2026-20079, a critical authentication bypass in Cisco Secure Firewall Management Center, by state-sponsored and financially motivated attackers. It further noted Google's fix of an actively exploited Chrome zero-day (CVE-2026-87491) and a joint CISA, NSA and FBI advisory warning that China-based AI firms are conducting large-scale knowledge-distillation campaigns against U.S. AI models.
