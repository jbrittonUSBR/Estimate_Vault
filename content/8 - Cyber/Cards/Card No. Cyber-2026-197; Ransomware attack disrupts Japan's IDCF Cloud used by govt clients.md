# Ransomware attack disrupts Japan's IDCF Cloud used by govt clients

[Source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ransomware-attack-disrupts-japans-idcf-cloud-used-by-govt-clients/)

(U) TOKYO, Japan — A ransomware attack disrupted IDCF Cloud, a Japanese cloud service provider used by government clients. The incident affected service availability as the company worked to contain the attack and restore systems, highlighting the risks posed to public sector infrastructure through third-party providers.
