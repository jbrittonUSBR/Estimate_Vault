# From supermarket fridges to passenger flights, a wave of threats is testing Europe's defenses

[Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/09/europe-fridge-hacks-bomb-threats-cyberattack.html)

(U) Europe — CNBC reported that refrigeration cyberattacks spoiled food at supermarkets in several European countries, while a separate wave of suspected hoax bomb threats disrupted dozens of flights and prompted diversions and fighter-jet escorts. The report said authorities had not established who was responsible or whether the incidents were connected.
