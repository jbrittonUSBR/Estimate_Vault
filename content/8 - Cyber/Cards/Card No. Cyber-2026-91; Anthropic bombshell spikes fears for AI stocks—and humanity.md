(U) one [Anthropic researcher](https://www.barrons.com/articles/anthropic-researcher-quit-ai-ipo-099f5801) has taken it to the next level—warning that those building artificial intelligence believe it could “kill us all by the end of the decade.” A current Anthropic researcher replied [on X](https://x.com/EvanHub/status/2097497037956891126?s=20), giving such a scenario a greater than 10% chance. The company has not responded to a request for comment. Regardless of how likely that is, it’s not a good look ahead of Anthropic and OpenAI’s IPOs. Maybe it’s time to appreciate Apple, which has been seen as an anti-AI trade in recent months, still relying on its tried and tested products. Speaking of which, it’s expected to unveil a [foldable iPhone today](https://www.barrons.com/livecoverage/apple-event-iphone-stock-john-ternus). It won’t come cheap, but nothing does these days. Especially the costs of running AI data centers, though Google has found a [cool way](https://www.barrons.com/articles/google-stock-ai-energy-fix-31d9fe50) to reduce energy costs. Gas definitely won’t be getting cheaper. [Oil prices hit $100](https://www.barrons.com/articles/oil-price-bond-yield-brent-47b9404a) early Wednesday for the first time since July. The latest [trade tit-for-tat](https://www.barrons.com/articles/trump-ban-canada-dairy-alcohol-62a82396) between the U.S. and Canada will also affect a range of goods. The problem for investors is deciding whether the market’s hottest tech stocks are also getting too expensive.
