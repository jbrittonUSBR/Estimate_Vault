# Health intel-sharing group warns industry in wake of cyberattack on French blood bank

[Source](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/06/nx-s1-6032602/cyberattack-blood-bank-healthcare)

(U) France — NPR reported that a cyber incident at a vendor serving the French Blood Establishment altered temperature and defrost settings at blood-storage facilities. Staff moved products before they were damaged, and the blood service said the incident did not disrupt supplies; Health-ISAC subsequently warned members about refrigeration-system attacks.
