# JPMorgan says third-party vendors targeted in cyberattack

[Source](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/business/jpmorgan-says-third-party-vendors-targeted-in-cyberattack-6566201)

(U) New York — JPMorgan Chase said outside service providers were targeted and that attackers may have accessed marketing data containing some customer names and email addresses. The bank said it would contact affected customers directly and that the incident was not connected to an earlier transaction-processing outage. CNA also reported claims by the group n0n involving JPMorgan, Visa and Mastercard; Reuters could not independently verify the posted data, and the payment firms said their payment systems were unaffected.
