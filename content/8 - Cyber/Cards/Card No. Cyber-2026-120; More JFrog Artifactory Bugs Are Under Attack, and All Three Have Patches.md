# More JFrog Artifactory Bugs Are Under Attack, and All Three Have Patches

[Source](https://devops.com/more-jfrog-artifactory-bugs-are-under-attack-and-all-three-have-patches/)

(U) SAN FRANCISCO, United States — Attackers were actively exploiting three JFrog Artifactory vulnerabilities, all of which had patches available, in a campaign that exposed how slow remediation can turn artifact repositories into software supply chain attack paths, DevOps.com reported. According to a Wiz analysis summarized by SecurityWeek, the operators chained the three CVEs to gain administrative privileges in under five minutes, install malicious Groovy plugins and plant persistent Rust backdoors during a 24-day campaign. Wiz estimated that as many as 62 percent of exposed Artifactory instances were vulnerable.
