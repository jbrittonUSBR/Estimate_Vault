(U) Suisun City, a Northern California municipality, declared a local state of emergency after a severe malware attack disrupted critical public safety networks, including 911 routing and police/fire dispatch.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Incident:** The cyber incident began when malicious software infiltrated the city's network, forcing officials to proactively shut down their entire IT infrastructure to contain the threat and preserve forensic evidence.
- **Public Safety Continuity:** Although standard local dispatch systems were affected, emergency response operations were successfully rerouted through the Solano County dispatch center to ensure police and firefighters could continue responding to calls.
- **Emergency Declaration:** The City Council unanimously voted to declare a state of emergency, which fast-tracks access to state and federal resources, streamlines funding, and aids recovery efforts.
- **Investigation:** Federal and state agencies—including the FBI, the Department of Homeland Security, and the California Office of Emergency Services—stepped in to assist local experts with the ongoing investigation into the origin of the malware.
