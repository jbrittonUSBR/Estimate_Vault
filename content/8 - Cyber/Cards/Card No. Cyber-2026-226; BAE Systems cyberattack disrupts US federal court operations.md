# BAE Systems cyberattack disrupts US federal court operations

[Source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/bae-systems-cyberattack-disrupts-us-federal-court-operations/)

(U) October 16, 2026 — A cyberattack on BAE Systems disrupted the Bankruptcy Noticing Center systems and websites that the contractor operated for the U.S. Judiciary, halting bankruptcy notice production and distribution. The Administrative Office of the U.S. Courts said it was coordinating with BAE Systems, which was investigating and working to restore operations. The affected services included the National Creditor Registration Service.
