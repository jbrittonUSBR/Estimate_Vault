# Arizona’s court system hit by cyberattack

[Source](https://www.abajournal.com/news/article/arizonas-court-system-hit-by-cyberattack)

(U) PHOENIX, Arizona — Arizona's court system was hit by a cyberattack that disrupted online services. The Arizona Supreme Court and Administrative Office of the Courts said they were working to restore systems while investigating the incident, with some court operations affected as a precautionary measure.
