# Inside the LiteLLM 1.107.0 supply chain compromise: Credential theft through Python startup hooks

[Source](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/10/23/inside-the-litellm-1-107-0-supply-chain-compromise-credential-theft-through-python-startup-hooks/)

(U) Online — Microsoft researchers detailed a compromise of LiteLLM version 1.107.0 in which malicious Python startup hooks were used to steal credentials. Microsoft said the compromised package was distributed through the software supply chain and outlined indicators and remediation steps; the account was a vendor’s technical analysis.
