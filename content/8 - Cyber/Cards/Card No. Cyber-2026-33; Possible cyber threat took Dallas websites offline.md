(U) **Dallas city, police websites shut down over possible threat** (The Dallas Morning News, Everton Bailey Jr., July 21, 2026) Dallas officials said a possible cyber threat caused a daylong outage of the city’s main government and police websites on Monday. City spokesman Rick Ericson explained that the city’s digital security systems detected a threat from outside its network and automatically took offline dallascityhall.com, dallas.gov, and dallaspolice.net. “The threat did not result in a network intrusion or a loss of city data,” he said.

(U) The city’s IT department was notified that the sites were down at 9:55 a.m. Monday. An internal notice to employees at 1:11 a.m. Tuesday confirmed that service had been restored. The outage did not affect 911 or 311 phone and virtual services. Other systems, including DallasGo (the city’s online bill-payment platform) and DallasNow (its online permitting portal), remained accessible. The affected sites provide public information about city departments, elected officials, online services, and police resources.
