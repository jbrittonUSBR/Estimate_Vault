# Japan's Keio confirms ransomware attack disrupted business systems

[Source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/japans-keio-confirms-ransomware-attack-disrupted-business-systems/)

(U) TOKYO, Japan — On September 28, 2026, BleepingComputer reported that Keio Corporation had confirmed a ransomware attack detected in the early hours of September 26, 2026 that disrupted business systems. According to the report, Keio shut down its network in response; the attack affected only its hospitality business while train operations remained unaffected across its 85 km line with 69 stations, 25 hotels, and about 2,200 staff generating roughly $2.6 billion in revenue; separately, Tokyo Metro disclosed that approximately 59,000 member emails had been exposed.
