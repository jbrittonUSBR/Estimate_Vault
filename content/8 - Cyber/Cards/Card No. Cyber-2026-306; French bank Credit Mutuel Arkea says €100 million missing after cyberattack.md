# French bank Credit Mutuel Arkea says €100 million missing after cyberattack

[Source](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/business/french-bank-credit-mutuel-arkea-says-eu100-million-missing-after-cyberattack-6527446)

(U) Brittany, France — Reuters, carried by CNA, reported that Credit Mutuel Arkea said about 100 million euros had been stolen through fraudulent transfers from an account belonging to the bank. The lender said customer assets were unaffected, temporarily suspended selected transactions, and notified French authorities while seeking to recover funds.
