# FBI Confirms Data Was Breached in Cyber Attack on Springfield Schools

[Source](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/east/2026/09/16/885307.htm)

(U) SPRINGFIELD, Massachusetts — The FBI confirmed that data was breached in a cyberattack on Springfield Public Schools, potentially exposing staff and student information including some Social Security numbers. The district, which serves about 27,000 students across 66 schools, reopened on September 14 following the incident. A ransom demand has not been publicly confirmed, and the investigation remains ongoing.
