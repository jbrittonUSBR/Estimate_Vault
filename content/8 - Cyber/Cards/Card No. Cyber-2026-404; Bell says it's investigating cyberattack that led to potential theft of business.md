# Bell says it's investigating cyberattack that led to potential theft of business customer data

[Source](https://www.cbc.ca/news/business/bell-agentic-cyber-attack-9.7430468)

(U) Montreal, Canada — Bell said an "agentic" threat actor accessed internal systems on November 17 and stole business customer data including names, corporate email addresses, phone numbers and IP addresses, according to CBC News. The company said files and PDFs were not affected, that customer services were not disrupted, and that it had contained the incident; no group had claimed responsibility. Bell said it notified affected customers, shared indicators of compromise with enterprise customers and the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security, and engaged the RCMP's cybercrime unit.
