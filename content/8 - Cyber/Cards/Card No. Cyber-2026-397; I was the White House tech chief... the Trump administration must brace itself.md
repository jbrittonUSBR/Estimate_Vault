# I was the White House tech chief... the Trump administration must brace itself. A new, more devastating cyber crisis is looming and this is where America's enemies will hit next

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/sciencetech/article-16308573/white-house-tech-advisor-cyberattack-warning.html)

(U) Washington, D.C. — In an opinion piece, former White House chief information officer Theresa Payton criticized the government's response to a recent nationwide flight disruption and said hostile actors could exploit weaknesses exposed by such failures. Payton argued that companies were rushing technology updates without adequate testing, redundancy or rollback plans, and cited French authorities' attribution of an October attack on refrigeration controllers at about 700 sites to an Iranian Revolutionary Guard unit. The article noted that Senator Mark Warner said there was no indication the recent U.S. outages resulted from a cyberattack, foreign interference or a coordinated operation.
