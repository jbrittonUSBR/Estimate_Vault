(U) Dallas city and police websites (dallascityhall.com, dallas.gov, and dallaspolice.net) went offline Monday after the city’s digital security systems detected a possible cyber threat from outside the network and automatically took the sites down as a precaution. City spokesman Rick Ericson said there was no network intrusion or loss of city data. The outage was detected around 9:55 a.m. Monday and service was restored early Tuesday. Critical services such as 911, 311, online bill payment (DallasGo), and permitting (DallasNow) remained unaffected.
