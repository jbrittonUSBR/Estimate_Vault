# UK Must Expect AI-Powered Attack on Critical Infrastructure Within Two Years: Report

[Source](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/11/03/891295.htm)

(U) United Kingdom — Reuters reported that the NCSC judged a ransomware attack involving AI-powered malware on UK critical infrastructure by 2028 to be highly likely. NCSC chief Richard Horne said the concern was that AI would help attackers find and exploit neglected vulnerabilities at greater scale, not that it had created entirely new attack methods.
