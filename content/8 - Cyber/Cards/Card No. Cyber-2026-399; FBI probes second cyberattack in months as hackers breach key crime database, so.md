# FBI probes second cyberattack in months as hackers breach key crime database, sources say

[Source](https://freerepublic.com/focus/f-news/4403861/posts)

(U) Washington, D.C. — A Free Republic post reproduced a Fox News report saying three sources familiar with the matter told the outlet that hackers breached the FBI's National Crime Information Center, used to track fugitives and monitor sex offenders, in the second such attack on the bureau in months. The report said the FBI was investigating the entry point and it was unclear what data was compromised. It also noted that CNN first reported the hack and that ShinyHunters had claimed in September to have stolen records on nearly every FBI agent.
