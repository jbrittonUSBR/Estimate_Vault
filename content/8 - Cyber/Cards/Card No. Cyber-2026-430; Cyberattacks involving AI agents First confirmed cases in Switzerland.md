# Cyberattacks involving AI agents: First confirmed cases in Switzerland

[Source](https://www.bacs.admin.ch/en/26-ki-agenten-1-en)

(U) BERN, SWITZERLAND — Switzerland's National Cyber Security Centre said it had received its first reports of cyberattacks in which AI agents were used against Swiss organizations, affecting operators of critical infrastructure in at least two sectors and compromising victims' IT systems. The NCSC said attackers used "agentic offensive frameworks" that independently find vulnerabilities, select tools and adapt to defenses, exploiting weak passwords and known internet-facing flaws much faster than humans, and noted that publicly available tools such as ARTEX and CyberStrikeAI had nearly doubled since October 2026. It urged organizations to reduce internet exposure, patch within 24 hours, require MFA or passkeys and conduct regular penetration tests.
