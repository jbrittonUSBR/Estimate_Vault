# Survey: Lack of Confidence in Software Supply Chain Security Runs High

[Source](https://devops.com/survey-lack-of-confidence-in-software-supply-chain-security-runs-high/)

(U) NEW YORK, United States — On September 28, 2026, DevOps.com reported on a Cloudsmith survey of 400 platform and security engineers in the United States and United Kingdom finding widespread lack of confidence in software supply chain security. According to the survey, 73 percent were only moderately or not confident in their organization's software supply chain security (58 percent moderately, 15 percent not confident), 48 percent said they needed to quarantine packages manually, and only 37 percent could automatically detect and block malicious packages within minutes.
