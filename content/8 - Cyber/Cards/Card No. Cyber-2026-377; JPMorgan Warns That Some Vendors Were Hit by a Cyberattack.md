# JPMorgan Warns That Some Vendors Were Hit by a Cyberattack

[Source](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/national/2026/11/19/893138.htm)

(U) New York — JPMorgan said attackers targeted outside vendors and may have accessed marketing data, including some customer names and email addresses. The bank said the incident was unrelated to its earlier service outage and that services were not disrupted. Insurance Journal also reported Visa and Mastercard disclosures about separate third-party incidents; both companies said payment data and their payment networks were unaffected.
