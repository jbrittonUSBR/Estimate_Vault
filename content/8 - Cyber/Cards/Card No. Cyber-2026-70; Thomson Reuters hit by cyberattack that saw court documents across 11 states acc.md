# Thomson Reuters hit by cyberattack that saw court documents across 11 states accessed by a hacker

[Source](https://www.techradar.com/pro/security/thomson-reuters-hit-by-cyberattack-that-saw-court-documents-across-11-states-accessed-by-a-hacker)

(U) NEW YORK, United States — Thomson Reuters confirmed that an unauthorized party accessed certain files in its C-Track court case management system in a breach later determined to have occurred in March 2026, with activity detected in one of its cloud environments on June 30. The incident affected courts in 11 U.S. states (Alabama, Pennsylvania, Kentucky, Montana, Nevada, North Dakota, South Carolina, Tennessee, Ohio, New Hampshire, and Wyoming), the U.S. Virgin Islands, and Ontario, Canada, exposing court records and personal information; Ontario's three Chief Justices said the company notified the province's Ministry of the Attorney General on July 23. Thomson Reuters said C-Track remained operational with no disruption and that there was no evidence of identity theft, though investigators had not yet determined exactly what information was accessed or how many people were affected.
