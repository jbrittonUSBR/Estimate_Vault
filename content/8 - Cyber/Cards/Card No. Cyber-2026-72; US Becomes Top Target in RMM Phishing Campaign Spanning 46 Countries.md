# US Becomes Top Target in RMM Phishing Campaign Spanning 46 Countries

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/us-becomes-top-target-in-rmm-phishing.html)

(U) — An RMM phishing campaign initially associated with Canadian targeting through its use of Canada Revenue Agency tax-form lures was found to be part of a broader operation spanning 46 countries, with roughly 45 percent of observed activity tied to the United States, making it the campaign's top geographic target. ANY.RUN research connected 601 cases to the operation, which uses fake documents — including shipping and UPS communications, Adobe PDFs, tax notices, U.S. Social Security Administration themes, and invoices — to trick victims into installing legitimate remote monitoring and management software. Researchers identified 425 kit URLs across 240 hosts, 94 percent of which were observed for only a single day, using rapidly rotated infrastructure on Vercel, GitHub Pages, Netlify, and compromised websites. Education, technology, and government were among the top targeted industries, with banking, finance, and manufacturing also prominently affected.
