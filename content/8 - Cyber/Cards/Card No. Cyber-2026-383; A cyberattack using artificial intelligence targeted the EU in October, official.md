# A cyberattack using artificial intelligence targeted the EU in October, official says

[Source](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/cyberattack-artificial-intelligence-targeted-eu-october-official-138284407)

(U) Brussels — An official said an AI-assisted cyberattack targeted the European Union in October, and the Commission detected the incident on October 22. Commission spokesperson Thomas Regnier said authorities launched an investigation and temporarily took several applications offline, most of which were later restored. He said the Commission had found no evidence of a data breach affecting staff as a whole, while the attacker’s identity and the full effects remained unclear.
