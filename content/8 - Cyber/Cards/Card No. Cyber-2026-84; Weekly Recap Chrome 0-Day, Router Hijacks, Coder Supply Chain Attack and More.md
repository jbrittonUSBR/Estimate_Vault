# ⚡ Weekly Recap: Chrome 0-Day, Router Hijacks, Coder Supply Chain Attack and More

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/weekly-recap-chrome-0-day-router.html)

(U) — The Hacker News weekly recap reported active exploitation of a Chrome zero-day (CVE-2026-85046, CVSS 8.8), a type-confusion flaw in the V8 engine addressed in Google's latest update and the sixth actively exploited Chrome zero-day of the year. It also detailed the MikroTrick exploit chain against two MikroTik RouterOS zero-days used to hijack devices without authentication, and a supply-chain compromise in which attackers altered Coder's Cloudflare infrastructure to deliver malicious Terraform modules that stole credentials and secrets. Additional items covered N-able N-central hotfixes for critical authentication-bypass and maximum-severity RCE flaws and an unpatched Magento/Adobe Commerce zero-day used to backdoor online stores.
