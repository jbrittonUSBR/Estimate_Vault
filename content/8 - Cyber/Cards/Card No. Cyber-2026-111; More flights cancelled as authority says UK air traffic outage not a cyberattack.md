# More flights cancelled as authority says UK air traffic outage not a cyberattack

[Source](https://punchng.com/more-flights-cancelled-as-authority-says-uk-air-traffic-outage-not-a-cyberattack/)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — Nearly 400 flights were cancelled across the United Kingdom as Britain's air traffic control service recovered from a technical glitch it ruled out as a cyberattack, The Punch reported, citing AFP. The head of National Air Traffic Services, Martin Rolfe, said the agency had "ruled out cyber" activity as the cause, while Transport Secretary Heidi Alexander said she did not believe the issue was "unavoidable" and ordered an independent review. More than 330,000 passengers were affected over two days of disruption, with British Airways cancelling nearly 550 flights.
