# McKesson Narrows Confirmed Data Theft from Its Cyberattack to Oncology and Multispecialty Customers, Promises Patient Notices

[Source](https://www.medicaldaily.com/mckesson-data-breach-oncology-multispecialty-patient-notifications-478987)

(U) IRVING, Texas — McKesson narrowed the confirmed scope of its August cyberattack to certain third-party applications serving a subset of oncology and multispecialty customers, stating that data from its pharmaceutical distribution and CoverMyMeds businesses showed no sign of involvement; affected individuals are to be notified as required by law, though the total number impacted remains undetermined.
