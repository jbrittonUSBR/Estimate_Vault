# Group of bipartisan lawmakers ask US government to ban several hack-for-hire firms

[Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/09/group-of-bipartisan-lawmakers-ask-us-government-to-ban-several-hack-for-hire-firms/)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — Democratic senators Ron Wyden and Sheldon Whitehouse and Republican congressman Pat Harrigan sent a letter urging Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick to add three Indian companies—BellTroX, CyberRoot, and Sunkissed Organic Farms (formerly Appin)—to the department's economic sanctions "entity list." The lawmakers accused the hack-for-hire firms of conducting cyberattacks and targeted espionage against Americans, business owners, and their lawyers for over a decade to manipulate ongoing litigation, and of using foreign courts to suppress reporting on their activities. It was not clear whether the Commerce Department would act on the request; the companies and the Qatari government did not respond to TechCrunch's requests for comment.
