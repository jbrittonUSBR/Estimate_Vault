# AI Insiders Expect a Cyberattack on Banks, Power or Water Within 12 Months

[Source](https://offgridsurvival.com/ai-insiders-expect-a-cyberattack-on-banks-power-or-water-within-12-months/)

(U) WASHINGTON, D.C. — According to an Axios report citing unnamed AI industry insiders, executives at OpenAI, Anthropic, and other AI companies are privately planning for a catastrophic cyberattack that could cut off access to banks, the internet, power, or water, and many expect such an event within the next six to 12 months. The planning is largely focused on shaping Washington's political response rather than public preparedness, even as AI tools are already being used in attacks against financial institutions.
