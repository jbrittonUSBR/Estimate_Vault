# Cyberattack Exposes Chase, Mastercard Customer Data

[Source](https://www.newser.com/story/400348/cyberattack-exposes-chase-mastercard-customer-data.html)

(U) New York, United States — Newser, an AI-assisted aggregator citing the Washington Post and AP, reported that JPMorgan Chase and Mastercard said a compromised outside vendor may have exposed customer names and email addresses. Visa said it was aware of similar claims but had not confirmed any exposure of its customer data. The companies said card numbers and payment details were unaffected and services continued normally; Mastercard described a 'limited' exposure involving a small group in one program, while Chase said marketing data held by third-party partners was affected and that impacted customers would be notified. Newser said the incident was not linked to a separate Chase banking outage earlier that week.
