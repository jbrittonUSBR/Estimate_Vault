# M&S profit rebounds from cyberattack but clothing a drag

[Source](https://www.rte.ie/news/business/2026/1104/1597916-marks-and-spencer-quarterly-results/)

(U) London, United Kingdom — Reuters reported that Marks & Spencer's first-half adjusted profit before tax more than doubled from the previous year as food sales recovered after the 2025 cyberattack. The company said fashion, home, and beauty results still lagged pre-attack levels because of lingering product-availability problems and a difficult apparel market.
