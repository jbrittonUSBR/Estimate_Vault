# Chinese Rare-Earth Suppliers Halt U.S. Shipments, Sources Say

[Source](https://www.naturalnews.com/2026-09-07-chinese-rare-earth-suppliers-halt-us-shipments.html)

(U) — Natural News, citing a Reuters report relayed by ZeroHedge, reported that a handful of Chinese rare-earth suppliers had refused to ship material to U.S. companies since early August out of fear of retaliation from Beijing. Sources said the refusals began after China imposed sanctions on the U.S.-based Responsible Business Alliance and amid concern that material sold to U.S. firms could be resold to banned end users. The report said the exact number of blocked suppliers and shipments was unknown, and analysts warned the move could tighten U.S. supplies of critical minerals used in defense, aerospace, semiconductor and energy sectors.
