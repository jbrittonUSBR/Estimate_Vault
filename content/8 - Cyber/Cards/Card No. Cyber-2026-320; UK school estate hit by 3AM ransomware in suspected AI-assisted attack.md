# UK school estate hit by 3AM ransomware in suspected AI-assisted attack

[Source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/uk-school-estate-hit-by-3am-ransomware-in-suspected-ai-assisted-attack/)

(U) United Kingdom — BleepingComputer reported that a 3AM ransomware affiliate encrypted about 37 machines in a UK school estate after reaching roughly 50 systems. Bitdefender said command patterns appeared consistent with possible AI assistance but could not determine whether an AI model was used or whether a person copied and pasted instructions.
