# UK Flight Chaos Spills Into Second Day After Tech Outage

[Source](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/09/09/884450.htm)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — Air travelers in the UK faced a second day of severe disruption after a technical issue at National Air Traffic Services (NATS) forced Heathrow and other hubs to cancel flights. About 8,000 flights use UK airspace on average each day, and the spillover left aircraft and crew out of position, requiring extra flights to clear the backlog of stranded passengers. Heathrow said operations were recovering but warned of knock-on impacts as craft and crew repositioned, while Gatwick said it would take time for operations to fully return to normal; the government ruled out a cyber attack.
