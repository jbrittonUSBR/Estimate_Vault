# Cyberattack or Outage? CFOs Face the Same Cash Flow Problem

[Source](https://www.pymnts.com/back-office/cfo/2026/cyberattack-or-outage-cfos-face-the-same-cash-flow-problem/)

(U) United States — In a PYMNTS analysis, the publication argued that a payment outage can become a liquidity problem for chief financial officers even without a cyberattack, because technology failures can block access to funds while the cause is still unknown. It cited a week of disruptions affecting banks, airlines, Apple and Microsoft, including reported ransomware leaks of customer data from Visa, Mastercard and JPMorganChase on November 19. PYMNTS said CFOs must weigh the risk of delaying legitimate payments against releasing funds through potentially compromised systems, and pointed to its own research on third-party vendor exposure.
