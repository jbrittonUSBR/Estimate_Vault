# Eastern States’ Grocers Shut Online Systems After Cyberattack

[Source](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/east/2026/10/14/888832.htm)

(U) UNITED STATES — Andrew G. Simpson reported that Ahold Delhaize took websites, apps, and online ordering systems for its Stop & Shop, Hannaford, Food Lion, and Giant Food chains offline after a cybersecurity incident. The company described the shutdown as precautionary; stores, pharmacies, checkout, and refrigeration systems remained operational, and it said it had found no evidence of customer or associate data theft while the investigation continued.
