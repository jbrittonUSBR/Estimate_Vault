# Ransomware gets personal: 'We-know-where-you-are kind of stuff'

[Source](https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/10/14/ransomware-gets-personal-we-know-where-you-are-kind-of-stuff/5302939)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — Security researchers reported a shift in ransomware tactics toward highly personalized extortion, with attackers leveraging geolocation and personal data to increase pressure on victims. The trend indicated growing sophistication in social engineering and data collection methods used by ransomware groups.
