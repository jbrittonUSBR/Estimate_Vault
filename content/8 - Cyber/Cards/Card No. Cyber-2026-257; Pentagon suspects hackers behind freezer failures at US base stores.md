# Pentagon suspects hackers behind freezer failures at US base stores

[Source](https://thenextweb.com/news/pentagon-military-base-freezer-failures-suspected-cyberattack)

(U) Arlington, Virginia — The Pentagon is investigating suspected cyberattacks that caused freezers and refrigerators to defrost at military commissaries on at least six U.S. bases starting in late August, spoiling food. The probe follows similar refrigeration system tampering at hundreds of French supermarkets in mid-October and focuses on potential exploitation of vulnerabilities in refrigeration control systems.
