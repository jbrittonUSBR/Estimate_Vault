# Italy’s Foreign Ministry Under Cyberattack as Embassy Sites Come Under Review

[Source](https://securityaffairs.com/200650/cyber-warfare-2/italys-foreign-ministry-under-cyberattack-as-embassy-sites-come-under-review.html)

(U) ROME, Italy — Italy’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs reported it was under a cyberattack targeting its website, prompting checks of embassy and consulate sites abroad and increased coordination with the Polo Strategico Nazionale and other authorities. The ministry said mitigation measures were in place and no major disruption had been reported at the time. Foreign Minister Antonio Tajani announced Italy would work with Romania and other EU states to push for proposals to designate those responsible for such attacks, with analysts noting the pattern matches pro-Russian hacktivist activity seen in past Italian targets.
