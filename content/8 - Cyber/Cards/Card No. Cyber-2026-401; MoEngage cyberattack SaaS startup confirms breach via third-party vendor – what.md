# MoEngage cyberattack: SaaS startup confirms breach via third-party vendor – what it said and how many customers affected

[Source](https://www.livemint.com/companies/news/moengage-cyberattack-saas-startup-confirms-breach-via-third-party-vendor-what-it-said-and-how-many-customers-affected-11795265588243.html)

(U) Bengaluru, India — MoEngage said a breach in late October, which it attributed to compromised credentials at a third-party provider, affected less than 1% of customer accounts and was contained after detection. Crypto exchange ZebPay told customers that user contact details, device information and recent account activity were exposed through MoEngage, but said its own servers, wallets and funds were not affected. Livemint reported that MoEngage told Moneycontrol there was no service disruption, while people familiar with the matter told the Economic Times some customers lost platform access for nearly a week.
