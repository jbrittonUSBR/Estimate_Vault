# National Crime Database Reportedly Hacked, Prompting FBI Investigation Of Potential Cyberattack

[Source](https://dailycaller.com/2026/11/21/national-crime-database-hack-federal-bureau-investigation-probe-cyberattack/)

(U) Washington, D.C. — The Daily Caller reported, citing Fox News and three sources familiar with the matter, that the FBI was investigating an apparent breach of its National Crime Information Center database, which tracks fugitives and monitors sex offenders. The article said it was unclear what information was compromised and that the FBI did not immediately respond to a request for comment. It noted that ShinyHunters attacked the FBI's job portal in September and that Director Kash Patel announced the arrest of one suspect on October 9.
