# Ex-employee sues Sheppard Mullin over cyberattack exposing sensitive personal data

[Source](https://www.abajournal.com/news/article/ex-employee-sues-sheppard-mullin-over-cyberattack-exposing-sensitive-personal-data)

(U) LOS ANGELES, California — A former paralegal at Sheppard, Mullin, Richter & Hampton filed a putative class action lawsuit after a reported August cyberattack that the firm disclosed to California's attorney general affected at least 1,000 people. The suit alleges the attack stemmed from a social engineering incident in which an attorney provided documents to an unknown third party, and that the firm failed to adequately train employees, maintain safeguards, or provide timely notification as required by California law; stolen data reportedly included names, Social Security numbers, and government ID numbers.
