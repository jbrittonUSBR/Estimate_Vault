# NCSC Chief Warns of Imminent AI Ransomware Threat to UK Critical Infrastructure

[Source](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/ncsc-ai-ransomware-threat-uk-cni/)

(U) London, United Kingdom — Infosecurity Magazine reported that NCSC chief Richard Horne warned AI could help attackers discover and exploit existing weaknesses at unprecedented speed and scale. The NCSC assessed that AI-powered malware was highly likely to be used in ransomware against UK critical infrastructure by 2028 and urged organizations to address underinvestment and weak security fundamentals.
