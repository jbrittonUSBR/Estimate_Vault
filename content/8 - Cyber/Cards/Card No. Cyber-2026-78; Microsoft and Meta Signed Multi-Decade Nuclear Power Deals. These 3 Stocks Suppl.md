# Microsoft and Meta Signed Multi-Decade Nuclear Power Deals. These 3 Stocks Supply That Power

[Source](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/09/03/microsoft-and-meta-signed-multi-decade-nuclear-power-deals-these-3-stocks-supply-that-power/)

(U) — A 24/7 Wall St. investment analysis reported that multi-decade nuclear power agreements signed by hyperscale technology firms, including Microsoft's deal with Constellation Energy to restart the Three Mile Island Unit 1 reactor (Crane Clean Energy Center) and Meta's 20-year power purchase agreement for the Clinton plant, have established a template for long-dated corporate offtake of nuclear generation. The article stated that Constellation Energy signed roughly 920 megawatts of long-term nuclear contracts at an average term of 18.5 years during the second quarter. It highlighted three suppliers positioned to benefit: Constellation Energy (CEG), BWX Technologies (BWXT), whose backlog rose 40% year over year to $8.4 billion, and GE Vernova (GEV), with a reported backlog of $176 billion. The piece is an investing-oriented commentary rather than a news report.
