# Major cyberattack on the European Commission: Fears of personal data leaks – Which systems were affected?

[Source](https://en.protothema.gr/2026/11/19/major-cyberattack-on-the-european-commission-fears-of-personal-data-leaks-which-systems-were-affected/)

(U) Brussels — Politico reported that a cyberattack disrupted European Commission systems for more than ten days, affecting payroll, leave and medical-reimbursement applications. The Commission said it had contained most of the attack and temporarily took several applications offline as a precaution. CERT-EU reportedly identified ARTEX, an AI-enabled testing tool, as a possible tool used; the full damage and any personal-data exposure had not been confirmed.
