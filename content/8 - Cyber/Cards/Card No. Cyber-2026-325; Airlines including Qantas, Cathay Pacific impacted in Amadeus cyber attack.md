# Airlines including Qantas, Cathay Pacific impacted in Amadeus cyber attack

[Source](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/business/companies/airlines/airlines-including-qantas-cathay-pacific-impacted-in-amadeus-cyber-attack/AUGWJ3XXLNDBNNI67ITNPKB6IQ/)

(U) Global — The New Zealand Herald reported that Qantas, Cathay Pacific, Korean Air, Japan Airlines, and other carriers investigated possible booking-data exposure after unauthorized access to a system at travel-technology provider Amadeus. The airlines said their own systems remained secure, while Amadeus said it had contained the incident and had no evidence that payment data were accessed.
