# Red Hat Survey Shows Blind Spots in Swiss Enterprise Software Supply Chains

[Source](https://www.redhat.com/en/about/press-releases/red-hat-survey-shows-blind-spots-swiss-enterprise-software-supply-chains)

(U) Zurich, Switzerland — Red Hat said a survey of CIOs found that 95% of Swiss respondents believed their AI agents operated securely, while only 38% reported a complete view of direct and indirect software dependencies. The release is vendor research, not reporting of a specific incident.
