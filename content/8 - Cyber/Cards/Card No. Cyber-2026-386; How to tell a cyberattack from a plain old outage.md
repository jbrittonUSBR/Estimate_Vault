# How to tell a cyberattack from a plain old outage

[Source](https://slashdot.org/firehose.pl?op=view&id=186791746)

(U) United States — WIRED’s Andy Greenberg reported that recent disruptions at Microsoft, the FAA, JPMorgan Chase, United Airlines and an NSA network had not been shown to be connected or caused by hackers. Network researcher Doug Madory said only about one in a thousand outages he monitored was caused by hackers, and advised starting with accidental causes such as faulty updates, configuration changes, cable cuts or power failures. The article recommended checking organizations’ status updates and seeking concrete evidence before concluding an outage was a cyberattack.
