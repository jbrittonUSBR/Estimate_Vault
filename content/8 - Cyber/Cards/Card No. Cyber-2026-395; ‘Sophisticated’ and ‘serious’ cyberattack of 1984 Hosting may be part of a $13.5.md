# ‘Sophisticated’ and ‘serious’ cyberattack of 1984 Hosting may be part of a $13.5 million crypto heist.

[Source](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1039547/sophisticated-and-serious-cyberattack-of-1984-hosting-may-be-part-of-a-13-5-million-crypto-heist)

(U) Reykjavik, Iceland — Privacy-focused web host 1984 Hosting announced on Friday that it was temporarily shutting down services after a security incident. Cryptocurrency exchange Wagyu blamed the host for a hack that drained $13.5 million in tokens from the site; The Verge reported the host's statement did not mention Wagyu or disclose whether customer data had been taken.
