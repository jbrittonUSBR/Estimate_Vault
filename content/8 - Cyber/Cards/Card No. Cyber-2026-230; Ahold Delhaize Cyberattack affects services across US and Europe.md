# Ahold Delhaize cyberattack disrupted services across US and Europe

[Source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ahold-delhaize-cyberattack-affects-services-across-us-and-europe/)

(U) October 20, 2026 — Ahold Delhaize said a cyberattack forced it to take some systems offline, disrupting websites, apps, and e-commerce services at brands in the United States and Europe while stores remained open. The company said most Central and Southeastern European sites were back online, restoration work continued elsewhere, and investigators had found no evidence that customer, employee, or partner data was stolen.
