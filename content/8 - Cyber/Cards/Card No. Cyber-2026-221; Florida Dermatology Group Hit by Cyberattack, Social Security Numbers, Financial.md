# Florida Dermatology Group Hit by Cyberattack, Social Security Numbers, Financial Details, and Medical Records of 85,585 Patients at Risk

[Source](https://dailyhodl.com/2026/10/17/florida-dermatology-group-hit-by-cyberattack-social-security-numbers-and-medical-records-of-85585-patients-at-risk/)

(U) October 17, 2026 — Mid Florida Dermatology & Plastic Surgery said an unauthorized party may have copied patient information from its systems between March 26 and 30, affecting about 85,585 people. The Daily Hodl reported that the exposed records could include personal, financial, Social Security, and medical information, with details varying by patient. The outlet said the ransomware group Qilin claimed responsibility online; the practice reported the breach to federal health authorities on May 1.
