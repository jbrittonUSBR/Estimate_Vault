# Is China Really a Cyber Threat to Taiwan?

[Source](https://thediplomat.com/2026/09/is-china-really-a-cyber-threat-to-taiwan/)

(U) TAIPEI, Taiwan — In a commentary published by The Diplomat on September 8, 2026, analyst Andrew Faulhaber argued that China's cyber capabilities do not define the totality of its threat to Taiwan and that the cyber threat is currently modest. The piece noted that in July Taiwan suffered a first-of-its-kind sophisticated AI-enabled intrusion against its government and private-sector networks that was suspected to have been carried out by Chinese actors. Applying the formula of intent, opportunity, and capability, Faulhaber assessed that environmental factors currently limit China's intent and opportunity to use cyber in an existential way, but cautioned the threat could change rapidly as conditions shift.
