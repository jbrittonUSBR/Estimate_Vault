# Chase Bank announces new cyberattack may have stolen customer data as tech nightmare continues

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/sciencetech/article-16305985/chase-bank-cyberattack-customer-data.html)

(U) New York — JPMorgan Chase said attackers targeted third-party vendors that held marketing data, and that customer names and email addresses may have been exposed. The bank did not specify the number of affected customers and said it would contact them and provide support, including credit monitoring where appropriate. Visa and Mastercard also reported vendor-related data incidents; each said payment card data was not affected.
