# FBI to Springfield: cyber attack on schools did include data breach, maybe Social Security numbers

[Source](https://www.nepm.org/regional-news/2026-09-15/fbi-to-springfield-cyber-attack-on-schools-did-include-data-breach-maybe-social-security-numbers?_amp=true)

(U) SPRINGFIELD, United States — Officials in Springfield, Massachusetts, said the district's data had been breached as a result of a cyberattack around Labor Day, and that the compromised information could include Social Security numbers for staff, though that was not confirmed. The FBI contacted officials at Massachusetts's second-largest school district on the afternoon of September 15, 2026, to report the breach, a day after schools reopened following a sudden four-day closure caused by the incident. Officials said student information may also have been exposed, though they noted student Social Security numbers are not commonly included in student data files, and said the full extent of what was breached and where it was leaked remained under investigation. The district said it was expediting free credit monitoring for all personnel.
