# CISA Issues Cyber Decoy Guidance for Critical Infrastructure

[Source](https://www.executivegov.com/articles/cisa-cyber-decoy-guidance-infrastructure)

(U) WASHINGTON — The Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency released its first detailed guidance on defensive cyber decoys, titled "Using Cyber Decoys to Strengthen Detection and Response," describing how critical infrastructure operators can deploy realistic decoy systems and information assets to detect and observe intruders. CISA said the approach can counter "living off the land" activity and should be paired with zero trust, and the guide draws on MITRE's ATT&CK and Engage frameworks. The document is agency guidance, not a report of a specific intrusion.
