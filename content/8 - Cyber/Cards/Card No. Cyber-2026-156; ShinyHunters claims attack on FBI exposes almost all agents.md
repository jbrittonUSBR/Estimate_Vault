# ShinyHunters claims attack on FBI exposes almost all agents

[Source](https://cyberscoop.com/shinyhunters-claims-fbi-attack/)

(U) Washington, USA — The FBI said it was investigating unauthorized activity affecting its FBIjobs.gov site after the cybercrime group ShinyHunters claimed responsibility for breaching FBI systems and temporarily defacing the jobs portal. The group claimed it stole "very sensitive data on almost all FBI agents and individuals who filed an application with the FBI for a job," and said it acted in response to a May FBI public service announcement about the group. The site's apply.fbijobs.gov and Special Agent Application Portal remained unavailable, and experts told CyberScoop the targeting of a law enforcement agency could backfire by inviting a takedown of the group.
