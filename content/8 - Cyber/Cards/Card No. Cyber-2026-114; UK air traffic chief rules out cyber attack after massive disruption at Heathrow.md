# UK air traffic chief rules out cyber attack after massive disruption at Heathrow

[Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/09/uk-airport-disruption-heathrow-air-traffic-control.html)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — The head of UK air traffic control services said he did not believe a cyberattack caused the software issue that disrupted hundreds of thousands of passengers with flight cancellations, CNBC reported. National Air Traffic Services chief Martin Rolfe told BBC Radio 4's Today program that a thorough investigation would follow and that the agency had "at this point ruled out cyber." Flight tracking website Flightradar24 said 1,300 flights were cancelled to and from UK airports on Tuesday, nearly all at London's Heathrow, and airlines criticised NATS over a third major failure in three years.
