# Japanese internet giant GMO tells customers to shut down and disconnect PCs from the internet following data breach and ransomware threat

[Source](https://www.techradar.com/pro/security/japanese-internet-giant-gmo-tells-customers-to-shut-down-and-disconnect-pcs-from-the-internet-following-data-breach-and-ransomware-threat)

(U) Japan — TechRadar reported that GMO told Onamae Desktop Cloud customers to back up data, disconnect PCs, and shut them down after attackers installed malware on some hosted environments and threatened ransomware. The company said it would not pay a reported ransom demand and later warned that some MetaTrader credentials were compromised.
