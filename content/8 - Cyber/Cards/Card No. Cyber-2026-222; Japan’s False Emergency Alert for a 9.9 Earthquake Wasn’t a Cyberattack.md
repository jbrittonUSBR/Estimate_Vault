# Japan’s False Emergency Alert for a 9.9 Earthquake Wasn’t a Cyberattack

[Source](https://gizmodo.com/japans-false-emergency-alert-for-a-9-9-earthquake-wasnt-a-cyberattack-2000827000)

(U) Japan — Japan’s Meteorological Agency issued and withdrew a nationwide warning of a magnitude 9.9 earthquake after an offshore magnitude 6.9 quake near Russia’s Kamchatka Peninsula. Gizmodo reported the alert was canceled eight minutes later and said the agency was investigating a possible error in applying a magnitude formula designed for domestic earthquakes. An automatic precautionary shutdown temporarily disrupted at least one train line; the article cited no evidence of a cyberattack.
