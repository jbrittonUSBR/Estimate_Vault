# Scaling cyber protection for US critical infrastructure: How Elastic supports the Community Cyber Resilience Foundation

[Source](https://www.elastic.co/blog/how-elastic-supports-the-community-cyber-resilience-foundation)

(U) United States — Elastic reported that Accenture, Dragos, and the SANS Institute launched the Community Cyber Resilience Foundation to assist under-resourced water, electric, and natural-gas utilities. The first phase was planned to provide free tools, equipment, deployment services, and training to as many as 500 utilities, supported by an initial commitment of about $10 million.
