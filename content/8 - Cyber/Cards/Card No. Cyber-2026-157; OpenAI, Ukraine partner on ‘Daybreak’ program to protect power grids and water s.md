# OpenAI, Ukraine partner on ‘Daybreak’ program to protect power grids and water systems

[Source](https://cyberscoop.com/openai-ukraine-cybersecurity-critical-infrastructure/)

(U) NEW YORK — OpenAI and the Ukrainian government announced a partnership under which Kyiv's cybersecurity officials will receive access to advanced AI models through OpenAI's Daybreak program, along with more than $1 billion in subsidized tokens, to help protect power grids, water systems and other critical infrastructure. Ukrainian Consul General Dmytro Kushneruk said the tools would automate incident response, threat triage, login analysis, code review and vulnerability validation at machine speed while keeping humans in charge. Ukraine faces roughly 6,000 cyberattacks a year, he said.
