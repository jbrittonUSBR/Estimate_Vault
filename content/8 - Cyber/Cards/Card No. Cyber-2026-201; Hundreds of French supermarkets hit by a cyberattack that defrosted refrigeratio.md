# Hundreds of French supermarkets hit by a cyberattack that defrosted refrigeration systems

[Source](https://www.dw.com/en/hundreds-of-french-supermarkets-hit-by-a-cyberattack-that-defrosted-refrigeration-systems/a-79646097)

(U) FRANCE — Hundreds of French supermarkets were hit by a cyberattack that disrupted refrigeration systems, causing food to spoil and leading stores to dispose of perishable goods, according to Deutsche Welle.
