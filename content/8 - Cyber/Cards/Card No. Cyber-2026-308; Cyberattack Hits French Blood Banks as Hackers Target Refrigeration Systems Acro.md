# Cyberattack Hits French Blood Banks as Hackers Target Refrigeration Systems Across Europe

[Source](https://securityaffairs.com/202431/hacking/cyberattack-hits-french-blood-banks-as-hackers-target-refrigeration-systems-across-europe.html)

(U) France — Security Affairs reported that a cyber incident disrupted refrigeration monitoring and control systems at three French Blood Establishment facilities. French authorities said staff moved blood products to unaffected refrigerators, no blood supplies were affected, and investigators were examining how access occurred.
