# Container Shipping’s Red Sea Comeback Is Outpacing the Risk Warnings

[Source](http://wwd.com/sourcing-journal/logistics/red-sea-return-suez-canal-container-shipping-comeback-risk-houthis-1239347017/)

(U) RED SEA — WWD reported that container carriers were increasingly resuming Red Sea and Suez Canal transits despite continuing security warnings related to Houthi attacks. The article described carriers’ route decisions and the commercial pressure to shorten voyages, while noting that renewed traffic did not remove the threat to vessels. The report focused on shipping-industry activity and risk, rather than confirming a new attack.
