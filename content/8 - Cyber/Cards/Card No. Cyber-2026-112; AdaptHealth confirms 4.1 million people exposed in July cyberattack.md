# AdaptHealth confirms 4.1 million people exposed in July cyberattack

[Source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/adapthealth-confirms-41-million-people-exposed-in-july-cyberattack/)

(U) SAN FRANCISCO, United States — Healthcare company AdaptHealth confirmed that data of 4.1 million people was exposed in a cyberattack discovered in July and attributed to the ShinyHunters threat group, BleepingComputer reported. The company provides home medical devices and services including sleep-apnea and respiratory equipment and oxygen therapy, and first disclosed the incident to the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on July 2, 2026. AdaptHealth said the breach occurred through a social engineering ploy that compromised the privileged account of a third-party contractor and that it found no evidence of identity theft or misuse; a submission to the Department of Health and Human Services cited 4,115,802 affected individuals.
