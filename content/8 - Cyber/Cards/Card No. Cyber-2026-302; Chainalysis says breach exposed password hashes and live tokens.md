# Chainalysis says breach exposed password hashes and live tokens

[Source](https://cointelegraph.com/news/chainalysis-cyberattack-password-hashes-live-tokens-exposed)

(U) New York, United States — Cointelegraph reported that Chainalysis disclosed exposure of names, usernames, password hashes, and live authentication tokens in a cyber incident. The company said it contained the attack vector and had no evidence that investigation data or attribution data were accessed.
