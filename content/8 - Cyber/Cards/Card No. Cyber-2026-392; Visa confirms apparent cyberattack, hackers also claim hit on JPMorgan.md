# Visa confirms apparent cyberattack, hackers also claim hit on JPMorgan

[Source](https://www.techradar.com/pro/security/visa-confirms-apparent-cyberattack-hackers-also-claim-hit-on-jpmorgan)

(U) United States — Visa acknowledged a security incident involving a third-party provider in Asia-Pacific, while saying its own systems were not affected and there was no indication payment card numbers were compromised. The group n0n claimed on its leak site to have stolen data from Visa and JPMorgan Chase and threatened to publish it, but TechRadar said the claims and the scope of any stolen data remained unverified. JPMorgan said a separate service disruption was caused by a software bug, not a cyberattack.
