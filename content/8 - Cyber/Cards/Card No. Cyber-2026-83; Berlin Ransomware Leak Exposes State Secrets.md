# Berlin Ransomware Leak Exposes State Secrets

[Source](https://securityaffairs.com/198545/cyber-crime/berlin-ransomware-leak-exposes-state-secrets.html)

(U) Berlin, Germany — The Rhysida ransomware group published nearly six terabytes of data stolen from Berlin's state administration on its dark-web leak site after the city-state refused a 30 Bitcoin ransom demand. The gang claimed the dataset comprised roughly 1.44 million files, including personal information on 12,076 individuals, credentials, personnel and court records, and documents alleged to contain state secrets and national-defense material, including a folder on chemical, biological, radiological, and nuclear planning. Berlin's state government said it launched a central crisis unit to review and assess the leaked data, while the group's claims had not been independently verified.
