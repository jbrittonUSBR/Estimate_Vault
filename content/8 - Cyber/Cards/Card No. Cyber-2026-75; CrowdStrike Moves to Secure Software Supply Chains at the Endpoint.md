# CrowdStrike Moves to Secure Software Supply Chains at the Endpoint

[Source](https://devops.com/crowdstrike-moves-to-secure-software-supply-chains-at-the-endpoint/)

(U) — CrowdStrike announced a Real-Time Supply Chain Attack Protection capability at its Fal.con 2026 conference, designed to block malicious open-source packages at the endpoint before the embedded code can execute. The feature is intended for both human developers and AI coding agents and is to run across Windows, macOS, and Linux. The company cited findings from its 2026 Threat Hunting Report, including that North Korea's STARDUST CHOLLIMA crew poisoned 131 trusted AI framework packages and that ALTERED SPIDER compromised more than 300 dependencies in a single day, to argue that endpoint-level enforcement is needed to keep pace with accelerating software supply chain compromise.
