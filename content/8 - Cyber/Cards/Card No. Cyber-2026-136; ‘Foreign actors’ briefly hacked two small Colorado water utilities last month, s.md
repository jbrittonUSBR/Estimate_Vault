# ‘Foreign actors’ briefly hacked two small Colorado water utilities last month, state says

[Source](https://www.denverpost.com/2026/09/18/colorado-water-systems-hacking-iran-concerns/)

(U) DENVER — Two small private Colorado water utilities, each serving fewer than 200 customers, were briefly hacked by "foreign actors" in late August, with attackers changing settings, disabling alarms and remote access, and altering pumping cycles. No water-quality impacts were reported, and the utilities were not named. The incident follows a July multi-state campaign targeting water utilities and has raised concerns about potential Iran-backed involvement.
