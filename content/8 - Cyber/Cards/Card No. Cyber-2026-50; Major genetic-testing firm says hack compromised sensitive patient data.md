(U) Baylor Genetics, a Houston-based clinical diagnostic genomics and genetic-testing company, disclosed a data breach after an unauthorized third party infiltrated its network and accessed sensitive patient and employee files.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Timeline:** Suspicious activity was first flagged within the network on or around June 15, 2026. Investigators later determined that the unauthorized access occurred between **June 11 and June 17, 2026**. Following an extensive data review that concluded at the end of July, formal notification letters began going out to impacted individuals in August.
- **Compromised Data:** The exposed information varied by individual but included names, dates of birth, medical testing details, laboratory results, and health insurance information. For a limited subset of patients and employees, sensitive records such as **Social Security numbers**, government IDs, and financial data were also compromised.
- **Operations & Misuse:** Baylor Genetics stated that its actual laboratory testing operations and patient care pipelines were **not disrupted** by the incident, and there was no evidence that test results were altered. Furthermore, the company reported having no confirmed cases of identity theft or fraud tied to the breach at the time of disclosure.
- **Response:** In response, the company secured its systems, enhanced its access management and security controls, coordinated with law enforcement and state regulators, and began offering complimentary credit monitoring services to affected individuals.
