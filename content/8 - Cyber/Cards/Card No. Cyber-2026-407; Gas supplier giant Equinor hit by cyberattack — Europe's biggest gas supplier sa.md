# Gas supplier giant Equinor hit by cyberattack — Europe's biggest gas supplier says it has now restored services

[Source](https://www.techradar.com/pro/security/gas-supplier-giant-equinor-hit-by-cyberattack-europes-biggest-gas-supplier-says-it-has-now-restored-services)

(U) Stavanger, Norway — Equinor said in a November 17 statement that it had detected an 'ongoing cyber-attack against certain IT systems' and had mobilized resources to contain it. TechRadar reported that the company said the three-day incident did not affect energy production, involved no ransomware or evidence of data theft, and was reported to police. In a November 20 update, Equinor said the attack had been stopped, production was running normally and there was no indication of consequences for employees, partners or suppliers. The company did not disclose the attack method, and TechRadar said the attacker remained unidentified.
