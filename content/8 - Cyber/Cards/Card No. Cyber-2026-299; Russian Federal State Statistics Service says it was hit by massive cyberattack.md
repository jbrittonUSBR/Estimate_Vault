# Russian Federal State Statistics Service says it was hit by massive cyberattack — hackers steal 23TB of data, including Ukraine war casualty figures

[Source](https://www.techradar.com/pro/security/russian-federal-state-statistics-service-says-it-was-hit-by-massive-cyberattack-hackers-steal-23tb-of-data-including-ukraine-war-casualty-figures)

(U) Moscow, Russia — TechRadar reported that Rosstat acknowledged a cyberattack over the Halloween weekend but said it had found no evidence of database compromise or protected data leakage. The hacking group Silent Crow claimed it stole about 23 terabytes and published military casualty figures, which the report said were not independently verifiable.
