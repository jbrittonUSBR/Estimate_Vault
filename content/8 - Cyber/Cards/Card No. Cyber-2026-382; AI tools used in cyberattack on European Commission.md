# AI tools used in cyberattack on European Commission

[Source](https://www.rte.ie/news/technology/2026/1119/1599850-eu-cyberattack/)

(U) Brussels — The European Commission confirmed that an AI-assisted cybercampaign targeted it, with the incident detected on October 22. Spokesperson Thomas Regnier said several applications were temporarily taken offline as a precaution and most had since been restored. The Commission said it had found no evidence of a data breach affecting staff as a whole; it did not identify who was responsible.
