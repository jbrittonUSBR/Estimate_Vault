# Cyberattack hits Trump administration as hackers' latest strike against the US's critical systems takes down the Department of Agriculture

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16310465/Cyberattack-hits-Trump-administration-hackers-latest-strike-against-USs-critical-systems-takes-Department-Agriculture.html)

(U) Washington, D.C. — The Daily Mail reported, citing NBC News and two former USDA officials, that the U.S. Department of Agriculture was hit by a cyberattack this week that affected a system used to pay farmers. The department reset passwords and restarted applications, causing multi-day outages, and critical Farm Service Agency systems were down for more than a week ahead of a December 11 enrollment deadline. The article said responsibility and any data theft were unknown; Under Secretary Richard Fordyce said cybersecurity measures prevented data loss, and President Trump said the recent outages were separate incidents rather than a mass attack.
