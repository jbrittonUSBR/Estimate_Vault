# Security's 30-Year Habit of Layering Around the Problem

[Source](https://www.cybersecuritydive.com/spons/securitys-30-year-habit-layering-around-the-problem/830105/)

(U) WASHINGTON — A Cybersecurity Dive sponsored article examines how cybersecurity practitioners have spent roughly three decades "layering" controls around underlying problems rather than resolving root causes, producing increasingly complex, hard-to-manage security stacks. The piece argues that this accrual of overlapping tools and compensating controls has driven up operational cost and complexity without proportionally reducing risk, and is vendor-sponsored guidance rather than reporting of a specific incident.
