# UK summons air traffic boss after major outage as flights resume

[Source](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/world/uk-air-traffic-major-outage-flights-resume-6373391)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — Flights resumed at major UK airports after an hours-long outage led to the cancellation of about 2,000 flights and intensified pressure on air traffic control provider NATS and its chief executive, Martin Rolfe. Aviation analytics company Cirium reported that 30 percent of scheduled departures were grounded on Tuesday and 5 percent on Wednesday; Ryanair said its losses reached 3 million pounds after cancelling 260 flights affecting 48,000 passengers, and British Airways said 190 flights had been cancelled as disruption continued. NATS, a public-private partnership partially owned by airlines, pension funds, and the government, paid its owners dividends of 175 million pounds in 2025; Ryanair is suing the provider over a 2023 outage.
