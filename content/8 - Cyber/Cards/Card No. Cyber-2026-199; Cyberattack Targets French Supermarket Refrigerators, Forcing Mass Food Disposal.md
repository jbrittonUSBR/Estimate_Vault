# Cyberattack Targets French Supermarket Refrigerators, Forcing Mass Food Disposal

[Source](https://www.cnet.com/tech/services-and-software/cyberattack-hackers-target-french-supermarket-refrigerators/)

(U) FRANCE — A cyberattack disrupted refrigeration systems at French supermarkets operated by Ahold Delhaize, causing spoilage of perishable goods and forcing stores to discard large amounts of food, according to CNET.
