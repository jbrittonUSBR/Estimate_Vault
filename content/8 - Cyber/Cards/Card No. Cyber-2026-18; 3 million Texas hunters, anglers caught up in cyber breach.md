(U) More than 3 million Texas hunting and fishing license holders may have had personal information exposed in a cybersecurity breach involving the Texas Parks and Wildlife Department’s license system vendor, according to Texas Cyber Command. The unauthorized access potentially compromised driver’s license information, passport numbers (where provided), email addresses, phone numbers, and residential addresses, though sensitive data such as Social Security numbers, dates of birth, and financial or credit card details were not obtained. There is no evidence that minors were affected or that any specific group was targeted. TPWD has implemented additional security measures and enhanced monitoring while offering affected customers one year of free credit monitoring through Kroll, with enrollment available until September 14, 2026, via a dedicated call center. The department emphasized that license sales continue as normal and urged customers to monitor their accounts and consider placing fraud alerts or credit freezes as precautions.
