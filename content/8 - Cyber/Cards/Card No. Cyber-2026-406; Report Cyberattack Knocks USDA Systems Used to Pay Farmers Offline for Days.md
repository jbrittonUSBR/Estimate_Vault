# Report: Cyberattack Knocks USDA Systems Used to Pay Farmers Offline for Days

[Source](https://www.breitbart.com/tech/2026/11/20/cyberattack-knocks-usda-systems-used-to-pay-farmers-offline-for-days/)

(U) Washington, D.C., United States — Breitbart News, citing Nextgov/FCW and three people familiar with the matter, reported that USDA reset database passwords across all environments after its Cybersecurity and Privacy Operations Center uncovered a security incident in November 2026, causing application outages that lasted several days. An internal email from acting assistant CIO Chris Rottler said the resets affected farmer payment, crop-insurance and disaster-aid systems. A Thursday technical bulletin warned of intermittent outages through the week, and staff reportedly still had VPN problems and could not access client-facing loan and aid applications. Breitbart said the intruder's identity and whether any data was taken remained unclear.
