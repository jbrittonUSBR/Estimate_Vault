# Black Kite's Manufacturing & Distribution Ransomware Report 2026 Confirms Manufacturing Remains #1 Target

[Source](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/black-kites-manufacturing--distribution-ransomware-report-2026-confirms-manufacturing-remains-1-target-302879058.html)

(U) BOSTON — A press release from Black Kite reported that manufacturing and distribution remained the top ransomware target for a fourth consecutive year, with 1,183 victims in the first seven months of 2026, a 39.7% increase year-over-year. The sector accounted for 22% of 7,551 disclosed victims, with the U.S. share dropping from 52.3% to 34.8% as European attacks surged 85.4%. The group "The Gentlemen" was the most active with 142 victims, and the median revenue of targeted manufacturers was $42.9 million.
