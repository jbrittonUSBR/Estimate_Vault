# Hackers Leak User Data From JPMorgan, Visa, Mastercard, Ransomware Group Claims

[Source](https://www.naturalnews.com/2026-11-24-hackers-leak-user-data-jpmorgan-visa-mastercard.html)

(U) NEW YORK — A ransomware group calling itself n0n claimed responsibility for leaking marketing data on thousands of Visa, Mastercard and JPMorgan Chase customers obtained through third-party service platforms, according to Natural News citing the Wall Street Journal. The firms said no card numbers or funds were compromised, and JPMorgan said vendors' marketing data including some names and email addresses may have been accessed; reports indicated records on more than 6,000 Visa premium cardholders and about 16,000 JPMorgan customers were exposed. Natural News is a commentary site, and its claims about the leak and its scope were attributed to other outlets.
