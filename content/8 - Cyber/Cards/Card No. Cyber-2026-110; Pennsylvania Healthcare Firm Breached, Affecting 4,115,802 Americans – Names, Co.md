# Pennsylvania Healthcare Firm Breached, Affecting 4,115,802 Americans – Names, Contact Details, Health Records and More at Risk

[Source](https://dailyhodl.com/2026/09/10/pennsylvania-healthcare-firm-breached-affecting-4115802-americans-names-contact-details-health-records-and-more-at-risk)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — Pennsylvania-based healthcare provider AdaptHealth notified millions of patients about a data breach exposing personal and health information, according to The Daily Hodl. U.S. Department of Health and Human Services data showed the hacking incident affected 4,115,802 individuals, with an unauthorized entity gaining access to cloud-based systems and stealing files that may have included names, contact and demographic information, and health insurance and health information. AdaptHealth said the files did not include Social Security numbers or financial and bank account information, and the breach was linked to the ShinyHunters threat group.
