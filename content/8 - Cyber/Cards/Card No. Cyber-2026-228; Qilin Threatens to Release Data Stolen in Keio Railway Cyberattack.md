# Qilin Threatens to Release Data Stolen in Keio Railway Cyberattack

[Source](https://newsonjapan.com/article/151076.php)

(U) TOKYO, Japan — The international hacking group Qilin claimed responsibility for a ransomware attack on subsidiaries of Keio Railway and threatened to release stolen data, News On Japan reported on October 19, 2026. The attack occurred in the early hours of September 26, when several companies within the Keio Railway Group were targeted by ransomware, disrupting operations and leaving reservation services at some hotels and certain email systems unavailable. According to a cybersecurity company, Qilin published a statement claiming responsibility, confirmed on the morning of October 16, including 27 images purporting to show internal company documents and a countdown believed to indicate a deadline for releasing stolen data. Keio Railway acknowledged the group claimed to have released some subsidiary information but said it had not yet established whether the published material was genuine, and that it was continuing to investigate.
