# Cyberattack shuts down internet fan fiction library

[Source](https://www.digitaljournal.com/article/cyberattack-shuts-down-internet-fan-fiction-library/)

(U) Online — AFP reported that Archive of Our Own had been offline since October 29 after a cyberattack disrupted the fan-fiction site, which hosted nearly 18 million works and had more than 11 million registered users. The nonprofit said it had no evidence of database loss but could not rule out theft, and it estimated restoration could take up to a month.
