# Russian hacker group claims to have posted B.C. school district information on dark web

[Source](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/british-columbia/qilin-burnaby-school-ransomware-9.7409539)

(U) Burnaby, British Columbia, Canada — CBC reported that the Qilin ransomware group posted screenshots it said came from data stolen in a September breach of Burnaby School District systems. The district had confirmed that personal files were stolen but said it hired an outside expert to verify whether the material was published and determine the scope; CBC said it did not download the files.
