# Vigilance — Catch software supply chain attacks anywhere in the SDLC

[Source](https://vigihq.com/)

(U) FREDERICTON, Canada — Vigilance is a software supply chain security tool, built in Canada, that compares what a system runs against the update about to replace it and reports only the files that gained a new capability, such as reaching the internet or reading keys. The vendor states that over 70 percent of software supply chain attacks do not use known CVEs, and positions the tool to catch attacks such as NotPetya, SolarWinds, Kaseya, and 3CX. Vigilance installs as a single file with no agent, runs on Mac, Linux, Windows, and the BSDs, reads inside deb, rpm, npm, pip, container, MSI, and ISO packages, and never blocks a program. The company offers a free tier for up to 50 machines and a Pro tier for larger fleets, and the page is the product's marketing site rather than a news report.
