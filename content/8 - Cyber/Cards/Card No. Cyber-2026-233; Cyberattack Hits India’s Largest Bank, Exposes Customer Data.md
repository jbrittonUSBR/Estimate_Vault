# Cyberattack Hits India’s Largest Bank, Exposes Customer Data

[Source](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/10/21/889809.htm)

(U) October 21, 2026 — Insurance Journal reported that State Bank of India began investigating a suspected cyberattack after a preliminary review found nearly 4,000 records with customer information exposed on the dark web. The bank said the records included personal and account-related details and that a fuller investigation was underway to determine the source and scope. SBI said the incident had no material effect on its core operations or business continuity.
