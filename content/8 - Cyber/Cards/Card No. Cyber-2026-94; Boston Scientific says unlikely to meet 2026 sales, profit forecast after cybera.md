# Boston Scientific says unlikely to meet 2026 sales, profit forecast after cyberattack

[Source](https://finance.yahoo.com/healthcare/articles/boston-scientific-says-cyberattack-likely-104232391.html)

(U) MARLBOROUGH, United States — Reuters reported on September 8, 2026 that Boston Scientific said it no longer expects to meet its previously issued third-quarter and full-year 2026 sales and adjusted profit forecasts after a cybersecurity incident disrupted the medical device maker's global operations. The company said unauthorized activity on some of its IT systems, first identified on August 25, caused a network outage that affected access to operating systems and business applications and disrupted manufacturing and the processing and shipment of customer orders. It stated that major distribution centers were again processing and shipping orders at or above normal levels and that manufacturing had resumed at most facilities, but that the full financial impact remained uncertain.
