# The SOC Files 5: Ghost in the pipeline, or a fileless SharePoint breach by Storm-2603

[Source](https://securelist.com/storm-2603-fileless-toolshell-attack/121467/)

(U) Worldwide — Kaspersky's retrospective report attributed a 2025 on-premises SharePoint intrusion to activity consistent with Storm-2603 and described access maintained in the web-server process without a file-based implant. Kaspersky said the incident involved later post-compromise activity and a ransomware deployment attempt, and distinguished its findings from other actors' SharePoint campaigns.
