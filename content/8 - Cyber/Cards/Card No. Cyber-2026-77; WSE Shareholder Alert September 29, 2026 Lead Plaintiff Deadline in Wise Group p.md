# WSE Shareholder Alert: September 29, 2026 Lead Plaintiff Deadline in Wise Group plc Securities Class Action - Contact SueWallSt

[Source](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/wse-shareholder-alert-september-29-2026-lead-plaintiff-deadline-in-wise-group-plc-securities-class-action---contact-suewallst-302868870.html)

(U) — In a press release distributed via PRNewswire, the law firm Levi & Korsinsky LLP, operating the SueWallSt alert service, announced a securities class action against Wise Group plc (NASDAQ: WSE) filed in the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of New York on behalf of investors who purchased shares between May 11 and July 23, 2026. The release stated that WSE shares closed at $10.72 on June 3, 2026, down 16.05% across three trading sessions, and fell to $11.33 on July 24, 2026, and attributed the declines to a June 1 Reuters report of a Brussels money-laundering probe into more than €500 million ($582.5 million) in suspicious transactions and a July 24 Wall Street Journal report that the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency denied Wise's national trust bank license citing "long-standing deficiencies" in anti-money-laundering and counter-financing-of-terrorism controls. The lead plaintiff deadline was set at September 29, 2026. The claims are allegations contained in a press release and have not been adjudicated.
