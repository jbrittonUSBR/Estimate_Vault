# The Same Old CVEs Keep Getting Exploited: Patching's Enduring Blind Spot

[Source](https://www.cybersecuritydive.com/spons/more-cves-than-ever-the-same-old-ones-keep-getting-exploited/830366/)

(U) WASHINGTON — A Cybersecurity Dive sponsored analysis notes that despite a record volume of newly published CVEs, threat actors continue to exploit a relatively small set of older, well-known vulnerabilities that organizations have failed to patch. The piece examines why longstanding remediation gaps persist — including asset-management blind spots, patch fatigue, and legacy systems — and argues that prioritizing exploitation-in-the-wild data over raw CVE counts is more effective at reducing risk. The content is vendor-sponsored guidance rather than reporting of a specific incident.
