# Cyberattack Diverted Ambulances From New Mexico's Only Level I Trauma Center as Hackers Claim Patient Data Theft

[Source](https://www.medicaldaily.com/unm-health-cyberattack-ambulance-diversion-data-claims-480515)

(U) Albuquerque, New Mexico — According to Medical Daily reporting, a cyberattack on UNM Health diverted ambulances from New Mexico’s only Level I Trauma Center for about two hours and limited new patients, while hackers claimed to have stolen patient data.
