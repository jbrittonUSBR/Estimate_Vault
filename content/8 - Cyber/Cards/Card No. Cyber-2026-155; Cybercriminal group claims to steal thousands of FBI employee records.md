# Cybercriminal group claims to steal thousands of FBI employee records

[Source](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/09/22/shinyhunters-fbi-cyber-hack-01088494)

(U) WASHINGTON — The FBI said it was investigating a possible breach of its FBIjobs.gov portal after the cybercriminal group ShinyHunters claimed to have stolen "very sensitive" data on "almost all" FBI agents and job applicants. ShinyHunters provided 404 Media with a sample covering 5,000 purported agents that included names, home addresses, phone numbers and information on agents' spouses. The FBI National Press Office confirmed the investigation but did not verify the group's claims.
