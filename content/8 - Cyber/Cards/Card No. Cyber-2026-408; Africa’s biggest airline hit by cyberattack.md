# Africa’s biggest airline hit by cyberattack

[Source](https://rt-english.com/africa/649148-ethiopian-airlines-reports-cyber-attack-customer-data/)

(U) Addis Ababa, Ethiopia — RT, the Russian state-funded broadcaster, reported that state-owned Ethiopian Airlines notified ShebaMiles members on November 18 that a 'limited' incident may have exposed customer data, while saying no financial information appeared to have been accessed. Chief Commercial Officer Lemma Yadecha told Addis Fortune that hackers accessed thousands of frequent-flier accounts but took or altered no data or miles, and that suspicious activity was detected on November 17 after several days of access. The airline said flights were not disrupted and that it was investigating with cybersecurity partners and authorities; ShebaMiles had more than 5.38 million members as of October 2025.
