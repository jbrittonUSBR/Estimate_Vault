# Energy Sector Threat Analysis Report

[Source](https://www.cyber.nj.gov/threat-landscape/sector-threat-analysis-reports/energy-sector-threat-analysis-report)

(U) TRENTON, United States — The New Jersey Cybersecurity and Communications Integration Cell (NJCCIC) published a sector threat analysis report on the energy sector on September 9, 2026, assessing it as a critical risk due to high exposure to state-sponsored cyber threats and its foundational role in powering other critical infrastructure. The report stated that Chinese state-sponsored advanced persistent threat groups such as APT41 and Volt Typhoon have demonstrated the capability to persist within industrial control system and SCADA environments, often pivoting from exposed IT assets into operational technology domains. It warned that unpatched systems, weak IT/OT segmentation, and third-party vendor access compound the risk, and recommended measures including strict segmentation, credential hygiene, and deep packet inspection of industrial protocols.
