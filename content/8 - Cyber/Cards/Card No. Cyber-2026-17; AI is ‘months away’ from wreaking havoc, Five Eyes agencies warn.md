(U) Five Eyes intelligence agencies from the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand have jointly warned that advanced AI models are only months away from enabling catastrophic cyberattacks on businesses and governments, particularly those relying on outdated or unsupported software. The agencies stated that frontier AI systems will dramatically accelerate the speed, scale, and sophistication of offensive cyber operations while lowering barriers for malicious actors, urging leaders to treat cyber risk as a core organizational and societal responsibility rather than a purely technical issue. They emphasized the need for immediate action, including integrating AI tools into defensive security operations, warning that “the timeline is not years, it is months” and that failure to adapt will leave critical systems highly vulnerable. Experts such as Gary Barlet of Illumio reinforced that AI-driven attacks will make breaches an inevitability, as defenders struggle to keep pace with adversaries who face fewer constraints. The warning comes amid growing concerns over frontier models, with some advanced systems already facing access restrictions due to national security risks.
