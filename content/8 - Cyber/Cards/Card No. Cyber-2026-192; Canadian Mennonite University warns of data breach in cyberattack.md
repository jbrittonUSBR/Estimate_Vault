# Canadian Mennonite University warns of data breach in cyberattack

[Source](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/manitoba/cyber-hack-canadian-mennonite-university-9.7373948)

(U) WINNIPEG, Canada — Canadian Mennonite University warned of a data breach following a cyberattack on its systems. The university advised affected individuals to remain vigilant for potential phishing attempts, though specific details about the scope of compromised data were limited in initial reports.
