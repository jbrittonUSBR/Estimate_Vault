(U) The House Intelligence Committee advanced its fiscal 2027 Intelligence Authorization Act, which includes several cyber- and AI-related provisions. Key elements:

- Authorizes a pilot program for the Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI) to provide monthly unclassified cyber-threat briefings to one selected state (with a follow-up report on expanding the effort).
- Requires ODNI to develop a strategy for better information sharing with state and local governments, plus a GAO assessment of current sharing practices and obstacles (including security clearances).
- Election-security amendments (adopted with Democratic support) requiring an unclassified assessment of foreign threats to the 2026 midterms, partial funding holds until overdue election reports are delivered, and protections for analysts working on foreign-influence products.
- Increased funding and support for the intelligence community’s use of frontier AI models for cyber and other missions, plus expansion of the NSA’s Artificial Intelligence Security Center.

(U) The bill is expected to be incorporated into the broader National Defense Authorization Act later in the year.
