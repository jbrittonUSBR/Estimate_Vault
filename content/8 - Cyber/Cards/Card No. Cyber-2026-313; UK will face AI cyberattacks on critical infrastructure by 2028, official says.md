# UK will face AI cyberattacks on critical infrastructure by 2028, official says

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/11/03/world-news/uk-will-face-ai-cyberattacks-on-critical-infrastructure-by-2028-official-says/)

(U) United Kingdom — The National Cyber Security Centre assessed that AI-powered malware was highly likely to be used in a ransomware attack against UK critical infrastructure by 2028. NCSC chief Richard Horne said AI could accelerate discovery and exploitation of longstanding weaknesses and urged organizations to invest in basic security and resilience.
