# Warhorse Studios hit by cyberattack, company systems and data affected

[Source](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/warhorse-studios-hit-by-cyberattack-company-systems-and-data-affected)

(U) Czech Republic — Warhorse Studios confirmed it had suffered a cyberattack involving unauthorized access to company systems and data, according to contemporaneous coverage. The game developer said it was working with cybersecurity experts and authorities to secure its systems and assess the impact; it had not disclosed what information, if any, was taken.
