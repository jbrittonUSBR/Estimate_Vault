# FG builds new control centre to strengthen power grid

[Source](https://punchng.com/fg-builds-new-control-centre-to-strengthen-power-grid/)

(U) OSOGBO, Nigeria — The Federal Government began construction of a new National Control Centre for the Nigerian Independent System Operator in Osogbo, Osun State, with a groundbreaking ceremony on September 30, 2026 presided over by Vice-President Kashim Shettima's representative and attended by Minister of Power Joseph Tegbe. The facility will replace a 64-year-old control centre and include modern SCADA systems, large-format control displays, communications and data systems and enhanced security, intended to improve monitoring, fault detection and restoration across the national grid and support integration of renewables and the West African Power Pool; the details came in a statement from the minister's media aide.
