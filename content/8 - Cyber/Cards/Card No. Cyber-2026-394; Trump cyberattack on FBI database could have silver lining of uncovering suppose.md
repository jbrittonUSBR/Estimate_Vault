# Trump cyberattack on FBI database could have silver lining of uncovering supposed 'Russia hoax'

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16311557/donald-trump-cyberattack-fbi-database-russia-hoax.html)

(U) Washington, D.C. — President Donald Trump said in a Fox News interview on Saturday that the reported breach of the FBI's National Crime Information Center (NCIC) might have an upside by exposing information about the Russia investigation he calls a hoax. The FBI had opened an urgent probe into the second cyberattack on the agency in months; the article said it was unclear what NCIC data, which law enforcement uses for warrants and background checks, was targeted or who was responsible. A former FBI official quoted by CNN warned that the larger concern was whether attackers could compromise the accuracy of information officers rely on.
