# ASOS Plc Shares Slump After App Users Receive Hack Notification

[Source](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/10/06/888095.htm)

(U) London, United Kingdom — ASOS Plc shares fell sharply after users of its shopping app received a push notification claiming a breach of its Snowflake instance by a group calling itself “Xuanye,” with the company saying personal data including names and contact details may have been accessed via a third-party communication platform while payment details and passwords were not believed compromised; Snowflake said it found no compromise of its platform.
