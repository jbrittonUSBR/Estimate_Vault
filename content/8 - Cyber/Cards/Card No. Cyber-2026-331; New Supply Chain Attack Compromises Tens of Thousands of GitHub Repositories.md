# New Supply Chain Attack Compromises Tens of Thousands of GitHub Repositories

[Source](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/supply-chain-attack-thousands/)

(U) Global — Infosecurity Magazine reported that Socket researchers found a malicious GitHub Actions workflow in tens of thousands of repositories, including repositories owned by Uber. The researchers said compromised maintainer accounts had inserted a fake security-audit workflow to collect credentials, and they had not observed malicious package releases on PyPI or crates.io in the report's initial findings.
