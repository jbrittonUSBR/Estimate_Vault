# Houston-Based Genetics Firm Suffers Data Breach, Potentially Exposing Medical Data of 2,810,878 Patients and Staff

[Source](https://dailyhodl.com/2026/09/06/houston-based-genetics-firm-suffers-data-breach-potentially-exposing-medical-data-of-2810878-patients-and-staff/)

(U) HOUSTON, Texas — A Houston-based genetics laboratory disclosed a data breach that a U.S. Department of Health and Human Services filing listed as affecting 2,810,878 patients and staff. The company said it detected suspicious activity on approximately June 15, 2026, and confirmed unauthorized access to its systems between June 11 and June 17. Exposed information included names, dates of birth, laboratory test results, medical details, and limited health insurance and Social Security numbers. The firm said its review was completed around July 30, 2026, that it found no evidence the data had been misused, and that laboratory operations were not interrupted.
