# Grocery Giant Ahold Delhaize Estimates Financial Toll of October Cyberattack

[Source](https://www.securityweek.com/grocery-giant-ahold-delhaize-estimates-financial-toll-of-october-cyberattack/)

(U) Zaandam, Netherlands — SecurityWeek reported that Ahold Delhaize estimated its October cyberattack would reduce fourth-quarter sales by 200 million to 250 million euros and cut its 2026 earnings outlook. The retailer said the disruption mainly affected online services in the United States and Benelux and that restoration work continued after most disruption was contained within two weeks.
