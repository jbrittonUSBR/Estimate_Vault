# Infostealer Logs Expose Replayable AI Tokens That Can Bypass MFA

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/infostealer-logs-expose-replayable-ai.html)

(U) SAN FRANCISCO, United States — Identity services provider Okta reported that an analysis of a 7 GB infostealer dump released on Telegram on August 2, 2026, found data belonging to 5,871 infected machines across 162 countries, including thousands of unexpired authentication tokens for AI services such as Google, Microsoft, Anthropic, Amazon, and OpenAI. Of 44,791 unique JSON web tokens in the dataset, 555 were likely related to AI service authentication, and a tool analysis found 24 still-valid API keys for four AI-related services. Okta warned that session tokens and API keys are sought by threat actors because they can often be replayed to bypass credential-based authentication and multi-factor authentication, enabling abuse such as LLMjacking.
