# Hackers gain brief control over LNG vessel safety systems near Gibraltar

[Source](https://cybernews.com/news/fbi-fuel-tanker-attacks-hackers-gain-control-critical-systems/)

(U) GIBRALTAR — Crew members of the Liberian-flagged LNG tanker Vivit Africa claimed attackers temporarily took control of tank pressure control systems and pressure relief valves and disrupted the boil-off gas management cycle while the vessel transited the Strait of Gibraltar, according to a report by shipping publication Splash247. The crew said the disruption raised the risk of tank rupture and explosion, while the Italian Coast Guard described only a malfunction in cargo-monitoring systems. The incident is the third reported tanker cyberattack in recent weeks, and the FBI is investigating possible Iran-linked involvement.
