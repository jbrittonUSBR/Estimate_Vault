# How FBI busted Russian plot to kill US-based dissident, target Ukraine supporters

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/us/how-fbi-busted-russian-plot-to-kill-us-based-dissident-target-ukraine-supporters/articleshow/134278231.cms)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — The FBI disrupted an alleged Russian intelligence operation to assassinate a prominent Russian dissident believed to be living in Washington, DC, after operatives recruited a person in the US to conduct surveillance on the target, according to a Times of India report citing the Associated Press. Five people were charged after a Manhattan federal court unsealed an indictment alleging a Russian intelligence network had recruited people in the US and elsewhere since at least 2024 to surveil and kill Russian dissidents; a Venezuelan citizen living in Brooklyn was allegedly sent to two locations linked to the intended victim, paid $1,000 and $1,500 for surveillance assignments, and then offered $40,000 to "eliminate" or "disappear" the target, which the recruit rejected. The Justice Department said network members had also commissioned associates to carry out or attempt acts of terrorism against civilian and military infrastructure in European countries aligned with or perceived to be aligned with Ukraine. The five defendants — Yuri Khrameev, Kirill Khrameev, Oemis Romagoza Durruthy, Yaidel Delgado Suarez and Angel Eduardo Castro — remain at large, and the charges are allegations.
