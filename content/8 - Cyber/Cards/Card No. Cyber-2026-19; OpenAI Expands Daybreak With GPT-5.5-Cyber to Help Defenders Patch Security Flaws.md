(U) OpenAI has expanded its Daybreak initiative with the release of an improved GPT-5.5-Cyber model and an updated Codex Security plugin designed to help security teams and open-source maintainers more effectively identify, validate, and patch software vulnerabilities across large codebases. The model supports deeper analysis to detect issues, trace attack paths, generate reports with severity ratings and remediation guidance, and develop/test patches, while the plugin enables triaging of findings from scanners, advisories, and bug reports, along with large-scale patch generation. In parallel, OpenAI launched “Patch the Planet” in partnership with Trail of Bits to bolster security in critical open-source projects such as cURL, Python, and the Go project by providing tools that reduce the burden on maintainers. These developments come amid warnings from Five Eyes agencies and cybersecurity experts that advanced AI models are accelerating both defensive patching and offensive exploitation, compressing response windows and lowering barriers for less-skilled attackers. OpenAI emphasizes that the tools include appropriate governance and human oversight to empower defenders while preserving their control over changes to shared infrastructure.
