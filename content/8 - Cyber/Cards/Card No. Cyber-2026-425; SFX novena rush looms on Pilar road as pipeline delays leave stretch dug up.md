# SFX novena rush looms on Pilar road as pipeline delays leave stretch dug up

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/goa/sfx-novena-rush-looms-on-pilar-road-as-pipeline-delays-leave-stretch-dug-up/articleshow/136112929.cms)

(U) PANAJI, INDIA — The nine-day novena for St Francis Xavier's feast beginning at Old Goa was expected to worsen traffic on the Pilar–Carambolim road, where delayed pipeline work and other excavation had left a 7-km stretch potholed and lined with open trenches, according to The Times of India. Residents said the road had been degraded for more than three years by underground power cabling work begun in 2023 and subsequent pipeline laying and testing, raising accident risks and dust pollution. The article reported no cyberattack or industrial-control-system incident.
