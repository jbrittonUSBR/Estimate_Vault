# SCADA to put Chandigarh power grid on real-time watch

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/chandigarh/scada-to-put-chandigarh-power-grid-on-real-time-watch/articleshow/134601769.cms)

(U) CHANDIGARH, India — The Chandigarh administration has approved a ₹29-crore Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) system to be implemented by Power Grid Corporation of India Ltd for the newly notified State Load Dispatch Centre, providing real-time visibility of voltage, power flows and equipment status across the UT power grid to enable faster fault detection, improved dispatch and cyber-resilient grid monitoring.
