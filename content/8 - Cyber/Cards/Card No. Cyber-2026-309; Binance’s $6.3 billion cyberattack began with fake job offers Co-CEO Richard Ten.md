# Binance’s $6.3 billion cyberattack began with fake job offers: Co-CEO Richard Teng reveals details

[Source](https://www.livemint.com/companies/news/binances-6-3-billion-cyberattack-began-with-fake-job-offers-co-ceo-richard-teng-reveals-details-11793816141115.html)

(U) Global — Livemint reported that Binance co-CEO Richard Teng said attackers used fake recruitment approaches and malicious developer tools to compromise employee devices over several months. Binance attributed the campaign to North Korea, said it rebuilt affected systems and revoked credentials, and said it was working with law enforcement to trace funds.
