# Bell investigates cyberattack that exposed customer information

[Source](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/company-news/2026/11/23/bell-investigating-cyberattack-that-exposed-customer-information-last-week/)

(U) Canada — Bell Canada said an "agentic threat actor" accessed an internal system on 17 November and obtained personal information about residential and business customers of Bell, Bell Aliant, Bell MTS, Virgin Plus and Lucky Mobile. Spokesperson Ellen Murphy said names, mailing addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, account numbers and payment details may have been exposed, but no credit card or banking information was accessed and no customer services were affected. Bell said it removed the intruder, engaged the RCMP cybercrime unit and shared indicators of compromise with the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security. A separate, concurrent attack on the third-party provider of Bell MTS email service caused account lockouts and password resets and may have exposed some email accounts.
