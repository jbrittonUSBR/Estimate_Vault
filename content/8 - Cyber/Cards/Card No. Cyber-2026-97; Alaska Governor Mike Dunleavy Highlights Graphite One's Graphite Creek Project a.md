# Alaska Governor Mike Dunleavy Highlights Graphite One's Graphite Creek Project as Strategic to U.S. Critical Mineral Security

[Source](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/alaska-governor-mike-dunleavy-highlights-graphite-ones-graphite-creek-project-as-strategic-to-us-critical-mineral-security-302871641.html)

(U) VANCOUVER, Canada — In a press release issued on September 8, 2026, Graphite One Inc. highlighted comments by Alaska Governor Mike Dunleavy identifying the company's Graphite Creek Project, north of Nome, as a potentially significant domestic source of graphite as the United States seeks to strengthen critical mineral supply chains. The release said Dunleavy made the comments in an August 31 Fox News op-ed and a subsequent television interview, stating that the deposit could provide enough graphite to meet the entire nation's needs for a century. According to the U.S. Geological Survey, Graphite Creek is the largest known graphite deposit in the United States, and the release noted that the United States remained 100 percent net import reliant for natural graphite in 2025.
