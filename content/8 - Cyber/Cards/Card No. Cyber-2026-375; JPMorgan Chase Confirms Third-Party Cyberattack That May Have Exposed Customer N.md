# JPMorgan Chase Confirms Third-Party Cyberattack That May Have Exposed Customer Names and Emails

[Source](https://cybersecuritynews.com/jpmorgan-third-party-data-breach/)

(U) New York — JPMorgan Chase said attackers targeted outside service providers and may have accessed marketing data that included customer names and email addresses. The bank said it had not determined how many customers were affected and reported no disruption to banking services. It said it would contact affected customers and offer support, including credit monitoring where appropriate.
