# How we can prepare for future cyberattacks on critical infrastructure

[Source](https://theconversation.com/how-we-can-prepare-for-future-cyberattacks-on-critical-infrastructure-296214)

(U) Canada — Researchers Alvi Jawad and Jason Jaskolka argued that cyber risk to critical infrastructure should be assessed by the consequences of an attack, not only by whether systems can be breached. They described simulations in which attacks on a municipal wastewater facility could disrupt equipment, increase chemical use or lead to under-treated discharge. The authors recommended preparing safeguards and response plans for operational, physical, safety, environmental, economic, societal and reputational impacts.
