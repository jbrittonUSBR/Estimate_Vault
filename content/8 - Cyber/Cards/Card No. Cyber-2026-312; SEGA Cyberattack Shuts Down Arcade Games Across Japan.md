# SEGA Cyberattack Shuts Down Arcade Games Across Japan

[Source](https://news.sankakucomplex.com/n/C19Mm7542i-N7lWG9W1M2g)

(U) Tokyo, Japan — Sega Fave said a third party had accessed servers connected to its ALL.Net arcade network and Aime player-card services, prompting a suspension that disrupted connected arcade games across Japan. The company said its initial investigation had found no confirmed customer-data leak, while it continued investigating and had not set a full restoration date.
