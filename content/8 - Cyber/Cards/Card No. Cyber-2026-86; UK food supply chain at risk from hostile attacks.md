# UK food supply chain at risk from hostile attacks

[Source](https://biztoc.com/x/26fb5f95321450e9)

(U) London, United Kingdom — A National Audit Office report named cyberattacks among the major threats to the UK food supply chain and called on the Department for Environment, Food & Rural Affairs to work closely with industry to prevent severe shocks. The report cited increased operating costs and disruptions following 2025 attacks on retailers including Marks & Spencer, which estimated its incident would cost around £136 million, and the Co-op, which confirmed data on 6.5 million members was stolen. NAO head Gareth Davies said risks were increasing in likelihood and severity, and the report found Defra was less confident businesses could withstand shocks without government intervention over the next five to ten years.
