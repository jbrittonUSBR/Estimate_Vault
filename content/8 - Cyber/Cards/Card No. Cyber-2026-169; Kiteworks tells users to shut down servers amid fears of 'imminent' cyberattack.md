# Kiteworks tells users to shut down servers amid fears of 'imminent' cyberattack

[Source](https://www.techradar.com/pro/security/kiteworks-tells-users-to-shut-down-servers-amid-fears-of-imminent-cyberattack)

(U) SAN MATEO, California — On September 28, 2026, TechRadar reported that Kiteworks told users to shut down their servers amid fears of an "imminent" cyberattack. The warning followed prior advisories after the company identified credible federal threat intelligence and urged a precautionary shutdown of Kiteworks SFTP servers; in subsequent reporting consistent with earlier advisories, Kiteworks indicated no known breach had occurred, the advisory was lifted on September 27, 2026, and customers were advised to update to a secure version (v9.5.1).
