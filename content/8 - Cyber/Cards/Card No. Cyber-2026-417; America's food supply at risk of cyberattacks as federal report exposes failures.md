# America's food supply at risk of cyberattacks as federal report exposes failures amid USDA security breach

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/sciencetech/article-16309017/americas-food-supply-cyberattack-usda-breach.html)

(U) Washington, D.C., United States — The Daily Mail, a British tabloid, reported on a Government Accountability Office audit released November 19 that found USDA was not giving CISA continuous, real-time awareness of threats to the food sector and had fallen short on cybersecurity emergency exercises. The GAO warned that weaknesses such as unchanged default passwords could let attackers alter food quality, contamination-testing or traceability data, and said USDA's homeland security office lacked the funding, authority and expertise to meet some responsibilities. The audit, covering July 2025 to November 2026, said its findings did not establish the cause of a separate incident that knocked USDA farm-loan and aid systems offline earlier that week. The responsible party and whether data was stolen remained unknown, according to the article.
