# Middle East cyber threats enter a new phase as ransomware surges and AI joins the attacker’s toolkit

[Source](https://www.digitaljournal.com/article/middle-east-cyber-threats-enter-a-new-phase-as-ransomware-surges-and-ai-joins-the-attackers-toolkit/)

(U) DUBAI — A CloudSEK report found Middle East ransomware activity surging, with feeds rising from 17 in April 2025 to 357 in June 2026, with Israel accounting for about 38% of incidents. Threat actors including MuddyWater used Gemini to obfuscate PowerShell, while a group dubbed Nimbus Manticore deployed AI-assisted malware. The UAE (2,588) and Saudi Arabia (1,880) saw the most indicators, with Turkey the most targeted overall as AI becomes integrated into regional cyber operations.
