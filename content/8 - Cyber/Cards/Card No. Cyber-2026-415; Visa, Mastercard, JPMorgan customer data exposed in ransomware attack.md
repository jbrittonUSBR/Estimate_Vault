# Visa, Mastercard, JPMorgan customer data exposed in ransomware attack

[Source](https://www.business-standard.com/world-news/visa-mastercard-jpmorgan-customer-data-leak-ransomware-attack-us-outage-126112000197_1.html)

(U) New York, United States — The original Business Standard report could not be retrieved, but its search excerpt and other reporting indicated that ransomware group n0n published data it said came from vendors of JPMorgan Chase, Visa and Mastercard after its deadline passed. A Reuters review cited in the excerpt found what appeared to be 124 bank employee email addresses and more than 16,000 customer email addresses, some with phone numbers and postal codes. JPMorgan said attackers reached marketing data held by vendors that may include names and email addresses, Visa blamed a service provider in Asia Pacific and said no card numbers were affected, and Mastercard said only a small group of cardholders in one benefit program had email addresses exposed; all three said their payment systems were unaffected.
