(TLP: Amber+Strict) On 5/08/2026, an E-ISAC member was aware of an individual who had been aggressively commenting and communicating with their employees and had started being even more aggressive in his attempts. The individual uses social media (primarily LinkedIn) and email but has also shown up in-person to voice his perspectives. None of his actions to-date have been overtly threatening, but the increasing frequency of his communications and attempts at getting attention in external forums has led the member to put preventative and detection measures in place.
