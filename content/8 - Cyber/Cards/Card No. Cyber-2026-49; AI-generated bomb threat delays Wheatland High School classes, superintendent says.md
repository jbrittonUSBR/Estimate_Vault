(U) A series of anonymous, AI-generated bomb threats severely disrupted classes and triggered multi-agency law enforcement responses at schools in Wheatland, California.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Threats:** Wheatland Union High School and the nearby Bear River Middle School were targeted by automated calls using artificial intelligence-generated voices. The threats forced classes to be delayed or entirely canceled over consecutive days.
- **Law Enforcement Response:** Local police, along with K-9 explosive-detection units, the Yuba County Sheriff's Office, and other regional and federal agencies, swept the campuses. Authorities emphasized that despite being suspected hoaxes or AI-generated, every threat must be treated as a real emergency.
- **Impact and Security Shifts:** The repeated disruptions strained local resources and caused widespread concern among parents and students. In response, school districts and law enforcement implemented heightened security protocols, including overnight campus monitoring, extra video reviews, and increased patrols.
