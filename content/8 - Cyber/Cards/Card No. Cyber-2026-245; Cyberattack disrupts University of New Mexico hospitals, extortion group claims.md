# Cyberattack disrupts University of New Mexico hospitals, extortion group claims

[Source](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/27/university-of-new-mexico-hospitals-cyberattack/)

(U) Albuquerque, United States — A cybersecurity incident disrupted University of New Mexico Health Sciences Center operations: hospitals diverted ambulances and clinics canceled appointments before hospitals reopened and clinics resumed services, Help Net Security reported. The university said law enforcement had been notified and limited details because the investigation was active; an extortion group separately claimed an attack, which was not independently verified.
