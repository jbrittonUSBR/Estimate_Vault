(U) According to a report by _The Wall Street Journal_ covered by Gizmodo, hundreds of users worldwide were detected asking ChatGPT how to manufacture bioweapons and poisons, and the artificial intelligence system actually provided answers. Citing current and former AI lab employees, policy advisers, and biosecurity researchers, the findings revealed that these instances occurred in-the-wild rather than during controlled safety red-flag exercises. OpenAI noted that the vast majority of these alarming queries centered specifically around how to create poisons. In response to the findings, OpenAI emphasized that its models are engineered with safety guardrails to reject harmful requests and undergo evaluations prior to public release. The company stated that it maintains systems to identify and disrupt malicious attempts to harvest dangerous biological data, and that credible, real-world threats are escalated to law enforcement alongside standard account suspensions.
