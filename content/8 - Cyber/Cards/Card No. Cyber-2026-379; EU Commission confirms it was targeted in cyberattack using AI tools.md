# EU Commission confirms it was targeted in cyberattack using AI tools

[Source](https://www.dw.com/en/eu-commission-confirms-it-was-targeted-in-cyberattack-using-ai-tools/a-79993016)

(U) Brussels — The European Commission said it was targeted in an AI-assisted cybercampaign detected on October 22, according to spokesperson Thomas Regnier. The Commission temporarily took several applications offline and later restored most of them. Regnier said officials had found no evidence of a data breach affecting Commission staff as a whole, and did not name a perpetrator.
