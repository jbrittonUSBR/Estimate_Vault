# America’s Biggest Oil Export Port Faces Thousands of Cyberattack Attempts

[Source](https://oilprice.com/Energy/Energy-General/Americas-Biggest-Oil-Export-Port-Faces-Thousands-of-Cyberattack-Attempts.html)

(U) CORPUS CHRISTI, TEXAS — The Port of Corpus Christi, which handles about 60% of U.S. crude exports, faced hundreds or thousands of intrusion attempts daily, CEO Kent Britton told the Financial Times, per a ZeroHedge report republished by OilPrice.com. The report cited an August cyberattack that forced North Carolina ports onto manual gate processing, FBI and Coast Guard boardings of two foreign-flagged tankers in the Gulf of Mexico over suspected network compromise, and more than 120 million cyberattack attempts blocked at the Port of Los Angeles in August. A Coast Guard cybersecurity rule took effect in July 2025, but port cybersecurity plans were not due for approval until July 16, 2027.
