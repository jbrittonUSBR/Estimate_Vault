# Tower Records Hit by Cyberattack, 11 Million Customer Records Potentially Leaked

[Source](https://newsonjapan.com/article/151491.php)

(U) Tokyo, Japan — Tower Records said a cyberattack on 19 November may have exposed about 11 million records from its membership database, including roughly 2.78 million records with names and dates of birth and about 780,000 that also contained delivery and shipping details. The company said no credit card information was leaked and temporarily suspended its online shopping site while responding to the breach. Shipments of already placed orders were scheduled to resume gradually from 23 November, but no date was given for full restoration of online services; News On Japan cited FNN as the original source.
