# Cybersecurity firm hired by Hamilton says director arrested by FBI didn't handle city's ransomware response

[Source](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/hamilton/ed-dubrovsky-cypfer-hamilton-ransom-fbi-arrest-9.7383552)

(U) October 19, 2026 — CYPFER told CBC that former managing director Ed Dubrovsky did not work on Hamilton's response to its 2024 ransomware attack, which the firm handled through its delivery team. The FBI arrested Dubrovsky in Pennsylvania in connection with an investigation involving the ShinyHunters hacking group; CBC reported that the allegations had not been proven in court. Hamilton said it could not comment on the case and stated that it continued strengthening its cybersecurity.
