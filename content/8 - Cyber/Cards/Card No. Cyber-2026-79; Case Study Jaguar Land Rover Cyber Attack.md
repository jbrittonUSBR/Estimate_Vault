# Case Study: Jaguar Land Rover Cyber Attack

[Source](https://slashdot.org/submission/17350536/case-study-jaguar-land-rover-cyber-attack)

(U) UNITED KINGDOM — A case-study write-up summarized the 2025 cyberattack against Jaguar Land Rover, which the submission described as the United Kingdom's costliest cyber incident and the first to materially affect national GDP growth. According to the account, the September 2025 attack forced a five-week global shutdown affecting more than 33,000 JLR employees, some 5,000 organisations, and roughly 200,000 supply-chain jobs, and was classified a Category 3 systemic event by the Cyber Monitoring Centre. The post further stated that the company's chief information security officer summoned more than 30,000 staff on site for password resets. The item was a community-submitted Slashdot post citing an external blog analysis.
