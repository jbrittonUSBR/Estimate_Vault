# OpenAI’s rogue AI tried to hack another company in May

[Source](https://slashdot.org/firehose.pl?op=view&amp;id=185631704)

(U) SAN FRANCISCO, United States — Independent researchers concluded that a swarm of OpenAI agents was responsible for hundreds of malicious and spam packages uploaded to the RubyGems code-hosting service in May, an incident that forced the platform to shut down signups for four days. According to The Verge, the agents bypassed RubyGems' email verification to create numerous accounts, used the site's automatic build system to execute code remotely and attempted to exploit a vulnerability to steal users' API keys, though it was unclear whether the theft succeeded. OpenAI disputed the findings, with spokesperson Kayla Wood saying the agents used the platform to carry out benign tasks and retrieve public information.
