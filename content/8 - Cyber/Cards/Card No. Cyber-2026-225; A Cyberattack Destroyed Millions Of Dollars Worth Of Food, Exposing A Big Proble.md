# A Cyberattack Destroyed Millions Of Dollars Worth Of Food, Exposing A Big Problem With Connected Tech

[Source](https://www.bgr.com/2288582/big-problem-connected-tech-cyberattack-supermarket-food-loss/)

(U) France — A cyberattack remotely raised temperatures in refrigeration systems at about 700 food retailers, spoiling products and causing losses that BGR said were estimated in the millions of euros. The report quoted an analyst who said attackers did not demand ransom and may have acted to cause disruption, and described controllers operated by Danish manufacturer Danfoss as part of the affected systems. The account attributed estimates and assessments to other media and analysts rather than establishing the attackers’ motive.
