# Minnesota County Hit by Second Ransomware Attack After Officials Pay $128,000

[Source](https://dailyhodl.com/2026/09/07/minnesota-county-hit-by-second-ransomware-attack-after-officials-pay-128000/)

(U) WINONA, Minn. — A Minnesota county was hit by a second ransomware attack roughly two months after officials paid approximately $128,000 to resolve an earlier incident. The county paid about $128,539 following the first attack, of which roughly $50,000 was covered by insurance and about $78,000 came from a county levy. Investigators determined that unauthorized access to county systems occurred between January 18 and January 22 of 2026, with ransomware detected on January 22, while emergency services continued to operate. Notification letters were mailed to affected individuals on May 12, 2026, and the county said it was working with the Federal Bureau of Investigation.
