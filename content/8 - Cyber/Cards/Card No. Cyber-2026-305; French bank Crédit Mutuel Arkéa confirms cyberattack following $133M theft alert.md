# French bank Crédit Mutuel Arkéa confirms cyberattack following $133M theft alert

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/cyberattack-french-bank-fraudulent-transactions/)

(U) Brittany, France — Crypto Briefing reported that Credit Mutuel Arkea traced fraudulent transfers from its own account to a platform operated by a service provider, while saying customer accounts and its Fortuneo subsidiary were not involved. The bank did not confirm the reported 118-million-euro loss, said some funds had been frozen, and reported that recovery efforts and investigations continued.
