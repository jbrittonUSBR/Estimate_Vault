# Dozens of oil and shipping tankers hacked in recent weeks, per report

[Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/24/dozens-of-oil-and-shipping-tankers-hacked-in-recent-weeks-per-report/)

(U) WASHINGTON — U.S. officials suspected Iranian hackers of as many as 50 cyberattacks on oil and gas shipping vessels in recent weeks, including breaches of navigation systems on tankers headed toward the U.S. coast in August that prompted FBI and Coast Guard boardings, according to TechCrunch citing the Wall Street Journal. The attackers may have exploited a Fortinet router flaw dubbed FortiBleed, and officials were investigating whether Iran sought to disrupt shipping routes or cause an explosion at a port; the FBI declined to comment. TechCrunch noted ships retain backup controls if automated systems are compromised.
