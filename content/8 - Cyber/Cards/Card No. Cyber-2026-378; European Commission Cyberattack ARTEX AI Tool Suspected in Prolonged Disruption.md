# European Commission Cyberattack: ARTEX AI Tool Suspected in Prolonged Disruption

[Source](https://securityaffairs.com/203483/hacking/european-commission-cyberattack-artex-ai-tool-suspected-in-prolonged-disruption.html)

(U) Brussels — A cyberattack disrupted European Commission internal systems for more than ten days, affecting payroll, leave, medical-reimbursement and translation services, Security Affairs reported. Commission officials said most systems had been restored, but the full extent of damage remained unclear and investigators had not confirmed data theft. CERT-EU identified ARTEX as a possible tool used in the attack; the outlet cautioned that the tool and attack method were not confirmed.
