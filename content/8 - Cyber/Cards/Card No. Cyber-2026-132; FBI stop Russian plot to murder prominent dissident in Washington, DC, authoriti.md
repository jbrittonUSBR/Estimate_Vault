# FBI stop Russian plot to murder prominent dissident in Washington, DC, authorities say

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/09/16/us-news/fbi-stopped-russian-plot-to-murder-a-prominent-dissident-in-dc-authorities-say/)

(U) WASHINGTON — The FBI stopped a Russian intelligence-backed plot to murder a prominent dissident in Washington, D.C., leading to the indictment of five operatives linked to a "Colonel Yuri" network. Prosecutors said a Venezuelan recruit was offered $40,000 to carry out the killing but declined, while a similar $25,000 offer was made for a plot in Lithuania. The Kremlin declined to comment, and Russia has no extradition treaty with the United States.
