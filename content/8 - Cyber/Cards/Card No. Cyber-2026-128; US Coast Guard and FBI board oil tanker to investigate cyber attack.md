# US Coast Guard and FBI board oil tanker to investigate cyber attack

[Source](https://www.bitdefender.com/en-us/blog/hotforsecurity/us-coast-guard-fbi-board-oil-tanker-investigate-cyber-attack)

(U) GALVESTON, United States — An oil tanker bound for Texas was boarded mid-voyage by the US Coast Guard and FBI after its network may have been compromised, according to a Bitdefender Hot for Security blog post. The Liberian-flagged supertanker VL Prosperity, carrying roughly 2.3 million barrels of crude from Egypt's Sidi Kerir terminal to Galveston, left Egypt on August 1 and was passing through the Strait of Gibraltar a week later when hackers allegedly penetrated its systems, interfering with fuel systems and engine speed and cutting communications for 30 hours. Two weeks later a specialized FBI and Coast Guard team boarded the vessel for four days, investigating and helping the crew eradicate the threat from its IT and OT systems; the Coast Guard said the ship was boarded after indications its network "may have been compromised by a foreign actor," with no disruption to operations, injuries or environmental impact reported. The post noted speculation that Iran-backed hackers may have been responsible but said no evidence had been presented.
