# Air traffic failure was avoidable, says Transport Secretary

[Source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5y42v5n05do)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — The technical failure of the UK's air traffic control system that caused widespread flight chaos was avoidable, Transport Secretary Heidi Alexander told the House of Commons, according to BBC News. Alexander said she ordered an independent investigation by the aviation watchdog into the system operated by Nats, which went down for four hours on Tuesday, and that a cyberattack could be ruled out. More than 2,000 flights were cancelled as disruption continued for a second day, and Nats apologised for its third major technical fault in three years.
