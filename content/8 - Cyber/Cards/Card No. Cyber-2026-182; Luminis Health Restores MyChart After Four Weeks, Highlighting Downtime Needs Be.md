# Luminis Health Restores MyChart After Four Weeks, Highlighting Downtime Needs Beyond the EHR

[Source](https://healthsystemcio.com/2026/10/05/luminis-health-cyberattack-downtime-plans/)

(U) ANNAPOLIS, Maryland — Luminis Health restored its MyChart patient portal after a four-week outage following a cyberattack, underscoring the broader operational challenges healthcare systems face during extended IT downtime. The incident highlighted the need for robust downtime procedures extending beyond electronic health records to cover clinical and administrative functions.
