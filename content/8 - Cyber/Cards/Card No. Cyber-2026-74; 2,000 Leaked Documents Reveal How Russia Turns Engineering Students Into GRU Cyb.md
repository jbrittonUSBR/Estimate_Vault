# 2,000 Leaked Documents Reveal How Russia Turns Engineering Students Into GRU Cyber Operators

[Source](https://securityaffairs.com/198332/intelligence/2000-leaked-documents-reveal-how-russia-turns-engineering-students-into-gru-cyber-operators.html)

(U) — More than 2,000 internal documents from Bauman Moscow State Technical University were reviewed by an international consortium of journalists, including The Insider, The Guardian, Le Monde, Der Spiegel, Delfi, VSquare, and FRONTSTORY.PL, alongside the security firm DomainTools. The records described a hidden "Department No. 4" that trained roughly 250 career and reserve students over six academic years in espionage, offensive cyber operations, and influence campaigns. Former GRU officer Major General Viktor Netyksho and graduate Aleksei Kondrashov were linked in the material to GRU units 26165 (associated with APT28), 74455 (Sandworm), and 29155, illustrating how Russian military intelligence recruits technical talent from civilian academia.
