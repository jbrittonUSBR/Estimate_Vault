# Boston Scientific Expects to Miss Its 2026 Targets After a Cyberattack and Restores Cardiac Monitoring Signups

[Source](https://www.medicaldaily.com/boston-scientific-cyberattack-2026-guidance-cardiac-monitoring-restored-478412)

(U) MARLBOROUGH, United States — Boston Scientific told investors it is unlikely to meet the sales growth and adjusted earnings targets set in July for both the third quarter and the full year because of a cyberattack that hit its systems in late August. In an update on the incident, the company reported no impact on the function of implanted devices, on in-person device checks, or on remote monitoring for patients already enrolled before the outage, though new cardiac monitoring enrollments could not be processed during the disruption. Boston Scientific said its distribution network was substantially restored and that a revised outlook was scheduled for its third-quarter earnings call on October 28; it added that it does not expect the incident to materially affect its long-term financial condition.
