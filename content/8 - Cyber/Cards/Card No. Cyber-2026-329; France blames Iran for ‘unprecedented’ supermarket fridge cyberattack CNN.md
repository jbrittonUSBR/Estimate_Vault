# France blames Iran for ‘unprecedented’ supermarket fridge cyberattack | CNN

[Source](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/09/europe/france-accuses-iran-of-fridge-cyberattack-intl)

(U) Paris, France — CNN reported that France attributed October's supermarket refrigeration cyberattacks to the IRGC-linked Shahid Kaveh unit, saying about 700 retail locations were targeted and food losses and fires resulted. Iran rejected the accusation, and French authorities said investigations and possible sanctions were proceeding.
