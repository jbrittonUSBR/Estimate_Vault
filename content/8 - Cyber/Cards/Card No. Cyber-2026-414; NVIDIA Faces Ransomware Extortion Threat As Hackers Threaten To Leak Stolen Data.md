# NVIDIA Faces Ransomware Extortion Threat As Hackers Threaten To Leak Stolen Data On November 28, Company Investigates Possible Partner Breach

[Source](https://wccftech.com/nvidia-faces-ransomware-extortion-threat-as-hackers-threaten-to-leak-stolen-data-on-november-28-company-investigates-possible-partner-breach/)

(U) Santa Clara, California, United States — Wccftech, a technology news site citing BleepingComputer, reported that the ransomware group 'The Gentlemen' added NVIDIA to its extortion site on November 18 with a countdown to November 28, but provided no sample files or other evidence of a breach. NVIDIA told BleepingComputer it had found no indication that its own systems or networks were affected and was investigating a possible incident involving an external partner. Microsoft Threat Intelligence, which tracks the group as Storm-2697, described it as a double-extortion ransomware operation; Wccftech noted that the group's claimed victim count has not been independently verified.
