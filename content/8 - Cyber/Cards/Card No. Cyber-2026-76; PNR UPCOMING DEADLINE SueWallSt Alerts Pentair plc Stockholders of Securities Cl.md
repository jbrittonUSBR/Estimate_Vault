# PNR UPCOMING DEADLINE: SueWallSt Alerts Pentair plc Stockholders of Securities Class Action

[Source](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/pnr-upcoming-deadline-suewallst-alerts-pentair-plc-stockholders-of-securities-class-action-302868818.html)

(U) — In a press release distributed via PRNewswire, the law firm Levi & Korsinsky LLP, operating the SueWallSt alert service, announced a securities class action against Pentair plc (NYSE: PNR) on behalf of investors who purchased shares between April 28 and July 14, 2026. The release stated that PNR shares closed at $82.86 on April 28, 2026, and fell to $64.33 on July 15, 2026, and alleged the company failed to disclose destocking in its Pool distribution channel that represented roughly 37% of fiscal year 2025 net sales, with an estimated $170 million impact on sales and $105 million on income. The lead plaintiff deadline was set at October 2, 2026. The claims are allegations contained in a press release and have not been adjudicated.
