# Cyberattack on Osaka University Shuts Down 500 Servers

[Source](https://newsonjapan.com/article/150954.php)

(U) OSAKA, Japan — Osaka Metropolitan University suspended classes across all faculties after a suspected ransomware attack shut down about 500 servers and disabled key campus systems, News On Japan reported on October 6, 2026. The disruption began on October 2 when a failure in the university's core campus network caused roughly 500 servers and related systems to stop operating simultaneously. The attack also encrypted much of the university's backup data, leaving internal systems for classes and assignment submissions unavailable, and disrupted university email. Officials said they were investigating whether personal information relating to at least 130,000 students, graduates, faculty and staff was leaked. Classes at all faculties were canceled through October 8, with in-person teaching set to resume on October 9.
