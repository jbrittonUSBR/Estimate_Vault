# A Supply Chain Attack Put Malicious Anthropic Claude Extensions on Thousands of Devices

[Source](https://www.wired.com/story/trojan-malware-targets-thousands-of-anthropic-claude-developer-systems/)

(U) San Francisco, California, United States — WIRED reported that a tampered Claude Code extension appeared in Cursor's marketplace for about 11 hours on October 29 and could have reached as many as 5,000 developer machines. The extension attempted to install a persistent SSH backdoor, but researchers said coding errors likely prevented remote access on many systems.
