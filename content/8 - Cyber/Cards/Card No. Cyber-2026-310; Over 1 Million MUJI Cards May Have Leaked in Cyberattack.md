# Over 1 Million MUJI Cards May Have Leaked in Cyberattack

[Source](https://newsonjapan.com/article/151251.php)

(U) Tokyo, Japan — News On Japan, citing TBS, reported that Ryohin Keikaku said unauthorized access may have exposed data associated with about 1.09 million MUJI Card members. The potentially affected records included card numbers but not card security codes or cardholder names, and the company was investigating the incident.
