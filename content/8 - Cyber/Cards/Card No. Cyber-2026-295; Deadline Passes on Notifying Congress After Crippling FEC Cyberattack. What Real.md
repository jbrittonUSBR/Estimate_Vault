[Source](https://joehoft.com/deadline-passes-notifying-congress-after-crippling-fec-cyberattack/)

(U) Washington, D.C., United States — Joe Hoft's article said the FEC's electronic-filing system was taken offline for nearly 41 hours after an attempted intrusion during early voting.
