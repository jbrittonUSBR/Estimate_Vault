# Panic buying frenzy sweeps US as outages and cyber attacks spark fears of impending apocalyptic event

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16302611/outages-tech-security-expeert-cyber-attack.html)

(U) United States — A series of outages affecting Microsoft, the FAA, banks, United Airlines and Apple prompted some consumers to stock up on supplies, the Daily Mail reported. Mizuho Securities analysts linked increased demand for emergency-preparedness goods at Costco to public anxiety, and Costco’s chief financial officer said shoppers had bought more rice, canned goods, water, generators and batteries. Officials attributed the reported outages to separate technical problems, and the article noted that none had been attributed to a cyberattack.
