# Lord Of The Rings RPG Game Studio Hit By ‘Serious’ Cyberattack As The Internet Continues Breaking

[Source](https://kotaku.com/lord-of-the-rings-rpg-game-studio-hit-by-serious-cyberattack-as-the-internet-continues-breaking-2000750137)

(U) Prague, Czech Republic — Warhorse Studios, the developer of Kingdom Come: Deliverance 2 and a forthcoming Lord of the Rings role-playing game, said attackers accessed company systems and data, Kotaku reported. The studio was investigating the breach with cybersecurity experts and authorities, but had not said what data was accessed or whether it was stolen. Kotaku’s headline also connected the incident to broader internet disruptions.
