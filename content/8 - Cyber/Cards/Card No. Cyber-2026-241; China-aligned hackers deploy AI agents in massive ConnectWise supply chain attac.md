# China-aligned hackers deploy AI agents in massive ConnectWise supply chain attack

[Source](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/cyber-security/microsoft-says-china-aligned-hackers-used-ai-agents-in-connectwise-supply-chain-attack-agents-attempted-sign-ins-at-more-than-2-250-microsoft-365-tenants-with-some-of-the-groups-attacks-reaching-privileged-access-in-under-three-hours)

(U) Online — Tom’s Hardware reported Microsoft’s assessment that China-aligned hackers used AI agents during a ConnectWise supply-chain campaign and attempted sign-ins at more than 2,250 Microsoft 365 tenants. The account said some activity reached privileged access quickly; attribution, scale, and operational details were based on Microsoft’s threat-intelligence findings.
