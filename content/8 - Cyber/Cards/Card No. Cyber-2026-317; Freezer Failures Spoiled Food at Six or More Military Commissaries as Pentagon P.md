# Freezer Failures Spoiled Food at Six or More Military Commissaries as Pentagon Probes a Possible Cyberattack

[Source](https://www.medicaldaily.com/pentagon-investigates-military-commissary-freezer-failures-480732)

(U) United States — Reuters reported that Pentagon investigators treated freezer failures at at least six military commissaries in August as suspected cyberattacks after food spoiled and some cooling units appeared to be set to higher temperatures. Officials had not publicly confirmed hacking or identified a cause or perpetrator, and investigators were coordinating with French authorities.
