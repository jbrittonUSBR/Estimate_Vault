# Vendor Breach Exposes Chase and Mastercard Marketing Data; n0n Ransomware Claims Qubole Attack

[Source](https://labs.cosmicbytez.ca/news/2026-11-20-vendor-data-breach-exposes-chase-visa-mastercard-customer-info)

(U) New York, United States — CosmicBytez Labs, a small security blog citing BleepingComputer, reported that JPMorgan Chase said attackers accessed marketing data held by third-party providers, possibly including customer names and email addresses, while its services were not disrupted. Mastercard said a small subset of cardholders in one benefit program had limited email and non-payment information exposed, and Visa said its systems were not involved. The blog reported that the n0n ransomware group claimed on November 11 to have attacked Qubole, a cloud analytics company owned by Idera, and leaked purported Chase, Visa and Mastercard data on November 16, including claims of 53.9 GB of Qubole files and nearly a petabyte from customer networks. CosmicBytez noted those figures came from the attackers and had not been verified.
