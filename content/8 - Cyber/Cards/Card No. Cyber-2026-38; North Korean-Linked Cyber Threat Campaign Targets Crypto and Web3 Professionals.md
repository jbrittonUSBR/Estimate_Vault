(U) North Korean threat actors (primarily subgroups of the Lazarus Group, including BlueNoroff) continue running sophisticated social-engineering campaigns against cryptocurrency and Web3 professionals. Common tactics include:

- Fake job offers, recruiter outreach on LinkedIn/Telegram, and fabricated Zoom or Teams interview invitations (often using typo-squatted domains).
- Deep reconnaissance on targets to make approaches credible.
- Delivery of malware designed to steal private keys, wallet credentials, browser data, and access to exchange admin panels.

(U) Targets have included decentralized finance founders, exchange operators, blockchain developers, and senior executives across more than 20 countries (with a heavy concentration in the U.S., Singapore, and the U.K.). The goal is direct theft of cryptocurrency and access to high-value wallets or infrastructure. These operations form part of a broader, long-running DPRK effort that has generated billions in stolen crypto to fund the regime’s weapons programs.
