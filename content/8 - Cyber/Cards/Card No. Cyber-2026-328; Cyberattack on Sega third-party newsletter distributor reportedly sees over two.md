# Cyberattack on Sega third-party newsletter distributor reportedly sees over two million email addresses leaked, but Sega accounts unaffected

[Source](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/cyberattack-on-sega-third-party-newsletter-distributor-reportedly-sees-over-two-million-email-addresses-leaked-but-sega-accounts-unaffected)

(U) Tokyo, Japan — Rock Paper Shotgun reported that a breach at Sega's third-party newsletter provider, Arara, exposed about 2.2 million email addresses associated with Sega newsletters. Sega said the exposure did not include Sega Account addresses or account passwords and that it had no confirmed evidence of misuse while it investigated additional delivery-related records.
