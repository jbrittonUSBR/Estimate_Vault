# Cybersecurity CEO Indicted for Allegedly Scamming Millions From Ransomware Victims

[Source](https://www.propublica.org/article/zohar-pinhasi-monstercloud-ransomware-indictment)

(U) United States — ProPublica reported that federal prosecutors indicted cybersecurity-company CEO Zohar Pinhasi, alleging he defrauded ransomware victims and obtained millions of dollars. The allegations concerned payments and recovery services presented to victims; the indictment contained accusations, not findings of guilt.
