# DreamHost admits major cyberattack saw hackers steal customer data and access inboxes

[Source](https://www.techradar.com/pro/security/dreamhost-admits-major-cyberattack-saw-hackers-steal-customer-data-and-access-inboxes)

(U) United States — TechRadar reported that DreamHost found attackers had accessed its File Manager server by exploiting a software vulnerability, exposing FTP/SFTP credentials and session information for some webmail users. DreamHost reset affected passwords and sessions, said its investigation remained open, and reported no evidence at that time that billing data or control-panel credentials were accessed.
