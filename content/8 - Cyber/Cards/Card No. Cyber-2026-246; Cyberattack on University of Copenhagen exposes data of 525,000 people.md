# Cyberattack on University of Copenhagen exposes data of 525,000 people

[Source](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/27/university-of-copenhagen-cyberattack-data-breach/)

(U) Copenhagen, Denmark — The University of Copenhagen said data belonging to about 525,000 people were exposed in a breach of a test environment, Help Net Security reported. The information included names, national identity numbers, phone numbers and personal email addresses. The university said a later wave of suspicious activity was linked to the original breach, not a separate attack, and maintained that core operational systems were not affected.
