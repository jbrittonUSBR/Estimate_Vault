(U//FOUO) On 22 April 2026, the Statewide Information and Analysis Center (SIAC) received 71 indicators of compromise (IOCs) that were reported as attacking the water sector in Utah. 1 The water sector observed the IOCs targeting their firewall on 20 April 2026, utilizing ports 20, 21, 22, 23, 500, and 8443. The SIAC assesses these IOCs are likely working to target the water sector to obtain remote access to systems. This assessment is made with medium confidence due to the vulnerable ports used that are often associated with remote access protocols. 
(U//FOUO) The SIAC identified multiple of the 71 reported IOCs active against Utah assets. Three of these IOCs were analyzed in depth. This information can also be found in IIR 4 007 S0388 26. The SIAC assesses with almost certainty the three IOCs performed port scanning and network enumeration against Utah assets. This assessment is made with high confidence due to the specific, uncommon ports used, the large number of Utah target internet protocol (IP) addresses, and consistent targeting of Utah assets organized into specific subnets.
(U//FOUO) The use of evasion techniques can allow malicious actors to maintain persistent access to Utah cyber assets, enabling them to discover and exploit additional target devices. The systematic scanning of sequential IP blocks allows actors to map entire networks and gain deep insight into Utah’s infrastructure. Continued scanning and network enumeration pose a significant threat to Utah, enabling malicious actors to gather the critical intelligence required to execute complex, obfuscated cyberattacks.
