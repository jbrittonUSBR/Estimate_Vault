(U) Cybersecurity researchers have identified a social engineering campaign linked to North Korean threat actors that targets cryptocurrency and Web3 professionals. The operation uses fraudulent recruitment tactics—posing as legitimate job opportunities or recruiters—to compromise individuals in the cryptocurrency and blockchain sectors. Researchers describe the activity as part of a broader, ongoing effort by North Korean-linked groups to target people in the crypto industry, typically for financial gain (stealing cryptocurrency or accessing wallets and credentials). This aligns with a pattern of North Korean cyber operations (often associated with groups like Lazarus) that rely heavily on tailored social engineering, fake job offers, and related lures against crypto, DeFi, and Web3 developers, executives, and professionals. Specific technical details of malware, exact victim numbers, or the research firm behind the latest findings are not fully available in the publicly indexed snippets of the HSToday article.
