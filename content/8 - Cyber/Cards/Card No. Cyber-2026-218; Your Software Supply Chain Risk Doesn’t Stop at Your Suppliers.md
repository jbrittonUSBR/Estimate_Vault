# Your Software Supply Chain Risk Doesn’t Stop at Your Suppliers

[Source](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/your-software-supply-chain-risk-doesnt-stop-at-your-suppliers/)

(U) ONLINE — Forrester analyst Janet Worthington argued that software supply-chain risk extended beyond an organization’s direct vendors to the vendors and dependencies used by those suppliers. The analysis recommended mapping these indirect relationships and incorporating them into risk assessment and oversight. It was Forrester’s advisory analysis rather than an independent incident report.
