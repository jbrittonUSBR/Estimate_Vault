# China-backed hackers suspected of carrying out massive cyberattack on European Commission

[Source](https://en.protothema.gr/2026/11/23/china-backed-hackers-suspected-of-carrying-out-massive-cyberattack-on-european-commission/)

(U) Brussels, Belgium — The European Commission said its EU Academy training platform was affected by a cyberattack detected on 22 October, leaking users' names and email addresses, and disabled public access to the platform as a precaution. Several internal applications were taken offline, some of which remained unavailable, while the Commission said it had not confirmed data theft involving staff. Citing expert assessments and sources familiar with the matter, Protothema reported that the intruders were suspected of links to Chinese security services because they used an AI-assisted tool called ARTEX, an attribution the Chinese government has denied.
