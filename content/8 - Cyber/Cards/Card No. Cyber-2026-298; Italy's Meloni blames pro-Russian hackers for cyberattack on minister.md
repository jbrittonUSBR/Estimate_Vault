# Italy's Meloni blames pro-Russian hackers for cyberattack on minister

[Source](https://www.dw.com/en/italys-meloni-blames-pro-russian-hackers-for-cyberattack-on-minister/a-79885200)

(U) Rome, Italy — Deutsche Welle reported that Foreign Minister Antonio Tajani said personal information about him had been stolen and posted online, and that he believed the incident related to Italy's support for Ukraine. Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni blamed pro-Russian hackers, while the report said officials had not described the data or publicly detailed the ongoing investigation.
