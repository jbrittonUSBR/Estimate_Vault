(U) The Linux Foundation, together with major technology and financial companies including AWS, Microsoft, Google, IBM, OpenAI, Anthropic, NVIDIA, Red Hat, and others, has launched Akrites, a new industry-wide initiative to coordinate the discovery, validation, remediation, and responsible disclosure of vulnerabilities in critical open source software amid the rise of AI-enabled cyber threats. Akrites establishes a shared Security Incident Response Team and standardized Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure processes to address the growing problem where AI tools can now identify vulnerabilities in minutes rather than weeks, overwhelming individual maintainers and leading to fragmented, inefficient responses. The initiative commits engineering resources, expertise, and funding to harden widely used open source components that underpin critical infrastructure such as banking, healthcare, energy, telecommunications, and AI systems, while ensuring fixes flow back to upstream projects on maintainers’ terms and providing support as a “maintainer of last resort” when needed. Founding members emphasize that collective, coordinated action—rather than siloed efforts—is essential to keep pace with AI-accelerated threats, with the effort building on related programs like OpenAI’s Patch the Planet and aiming to strengthen the security of the open source ecosystem that the modern digital economy depends on.
