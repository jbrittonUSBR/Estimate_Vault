# Giant Food Stores website, app still down as parent company urged to file criminal complaint over cyberattack

[Source](https://lancasteronline.com/news/local/giant-food-stores-website-app-still-down-as-parent-company-urged-to-file-criminal-complaint/article_428ec231-fb2d-46a4-a3a4-eb198a9afbca.html)

(U) October 16, 2026 — Giant Food Stores’ website and app remained unavailable after a cybersecurity incident at parent company Ahold Delhaize. The company said it had taken systems offline as a precaution and reported no evidence that customer, associate, or partner data had been taken; it did not provide a restoration timeline. LancasterOnline reported that the Netherlands’ justice minister urged Ahold Delhaize to file a criminal complaint, while the company had not characterized the incident as a hack.
