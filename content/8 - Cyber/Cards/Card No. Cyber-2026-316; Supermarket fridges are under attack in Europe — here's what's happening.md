# Supermarket fridges are under attack in Europe — here's what's happening

[Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/03/supermarket-fridges-hack-cyberattack-europe.html)

(U) Europe — CNBC reported that cyberattacks on refrigeration systems affected supermarkets in Belgium, Luxembourg, Sweden, Finland, Ireland, and Estonia, forcing some stores to discard food after fridge and freezer temperatures rose. Ahold Delhaize said dozens of its Delhaize stores in Belgium and Luxembourg were affected, while Colruyt, Carrefour, Intermarché, IKEA, and ICA also reported incidents. Authorities and retailers were investigating; no group had claimed responsibility, and experts cited internet-exposed refrigeration controls and unpatched equipment as vulnerabilities.
