# Cyberattack keeps Alabama nursing license system offline

[Source](https://www.wbrc.com/2026/09/17/cyberattack-keeps-alabama-nursing-license-system-offline/)

(U) BIRMINGHAM, United States — A cyberattack kept the Alabama Board of Nursing's entire online licensing system offline as more than 80,000 nurses statewide worked to renew licenses and new graduates sought first-time licenses. Board officials said staff were securing data, wiping compromised devices and processing all applications on paper by hand, and that there was no timeline for restoring online services; Honor Ingels, the board's chief policy and communications officer, said keeping the system offline was necessary to protect licensees' data. The board said employers could verify a nurse through the national Nursys database, which was current through August 31, or a daily list on the board's website, and Rebecca Huie of the Alabama State Nurses Association said the outage was delaying hiring at hospitals, clinics and other health care facilities.
