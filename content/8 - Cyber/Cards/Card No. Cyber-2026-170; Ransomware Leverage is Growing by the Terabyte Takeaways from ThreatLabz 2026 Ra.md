# Ransomware Leverage is Growing by the Terabyte: Takeaways from ThreatLabz 2026 Ransomware Report

[Source](https://www.zscaler.com/blogs/security-research/ransomware-leverage-growing-terabyte-takeaways-threatlabz-2026-ransomware)

(U) SAN JOSE, Calif. — Zscaler ThreatLabz reported on September 30, 2026, that the ten most active ransomware groups exfiltrated 896.2 terabytes of data between April 2025 and March 2026, a 275.8% increase from 123.8 terabytes a year earlier. The vendor's 2026 ransomware report recorded 7,366 victims on leak sites, a 3% decline, with 62% of victims at manager level or above and 75% concentrated in finance, sales, operations, human resources and marketing. Known ransom payments fell 15.8% to $327.8 million even as the average payment rose 5.3% to $431,995, and 52 new groups appeared, accounting for 60% of the top 15. The report also described attackers using Microsoft Teams vishing combined with Quick Assist to gain remote access.
