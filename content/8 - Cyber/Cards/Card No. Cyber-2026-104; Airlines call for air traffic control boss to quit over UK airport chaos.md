# Airlines call for air traffic control boss to quit over UK airport chaos

[Source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-10/uk-airport-chaos-continues-with-cyber-attack-ruled-out/107135996)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — The UK government gave National Air Traffic Services (NATS) one week to investigate a flight data processing system failure that prompted the cancellation of at least 2,000 flights, according to aviation analytics company Cirium. NATS CEO Martin Rolfe said the company had ruled out cyber activity as the cause, and Transport Secretary Heidi Alexander summoned him to explain the outage and told parliament she did not believe the issue was unavoidable. Ryanair CEO Michael O'Leary called for Rolfe to step down, saying the outage was a repeat of a 2023 failure; Ryanair reported losses of at least 3 million pounds and British Airways said it had cancelled nearly 200 flights.
