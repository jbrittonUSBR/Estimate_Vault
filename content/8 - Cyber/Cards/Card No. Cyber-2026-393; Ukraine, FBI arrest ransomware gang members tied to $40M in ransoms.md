# Ukraine, FBI arrest ransomware gang members tied to $40M in ransoms

[Source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ukraine-fbi-arrest-ransomware-gang-members-tied-to-40m-in-ransoms/)

(U) Ukraine — Ukrainian authorities and the FBI arrested four suspected members of an international ransomware organization, Ukrainian police said. Investigators linked the group to at least 28 companies that paid more than $40.5 million in ransom, and alleged that the gang used AI-generated voice calls impersonating IT support to obtain credentials or persuade employees to install remote-access software. The suspects faced charges under Ukrainian law and remained in custody; the investigation continued to identify other members.
