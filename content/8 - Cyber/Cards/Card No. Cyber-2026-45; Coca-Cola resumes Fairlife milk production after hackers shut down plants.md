(U) The Coca-Cola Company temporarily suspended U.S. production operations at its Fairlife dairy subsidiary following a targeted ransomware attack that compromised several of its technology and manufacturing systems. While unauthorized third-party hackers managed to access internal networks and steal certain data, Coca-Cola assured the public that product safety and quality remained uncompromised throughout the ordeal. Existing product inventory helped buffer retail availability, preventing widespread shortages for consumers, and operations in Canada continued without disruption. After working alongside external cybersecurity experts and notifying law enforcement, Coca-Cola successfully resumed the majority of its U.S. manufacturing capacity and did not expect the temporary disruption to cause a material impact on its overall financial performance.
