# Next Plc’s Supplier Hit by Cyberattack in Latest UK Retail Breach

[Source](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/11/23/893480.htm)

(U) London, United Kingdom — Next Plc said on Monday that an unnamed online supplier suffered a breach that allowed unauthorized access to certain data relating to the retailer. The company said the affected information was benign and could not be used to facilitate fraud, that no customer personal data was compromised, and that its own systems had not been breached, but it did not identify the supplier or specify what data was accessed. Next said it was working with the supplier, law enforcement and relevant regulators while investigating the nature and scope of the incident.
