# Hackers breach Microsoft 365 tenants via ConnectWise supply-chain attack

[Source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-breach-microsoft-365-tenants-via-connectwise-supply-chain-attack/)

(U) October 17, 2026 — ConnectWise disabled customer access to its Cloud Backup service after attackers used application identities to access and compromise Microsoft 365 tenants, according to BleepingComputer. Microsoft confirmed malicious activity involving the service and said it had identified unauthorized authentication attempts from an IP address on October 15. Huntress reported at least 162 affected tenants in its environment.
