# A Chinese Hacker Used AI To Attack 100+ Companies In One Of Largest AI Hacks Yet

[Source](https://www.forbes.com/sites/thomasbrewster/2026/09/22/huge-cyberattack-uses-anthropic-and-deepseek-ai-to-target-100-companies/)

(U) NEW YORK — A Chinese-speaking cybercriminal used AI agents built on DeepSeek, Moonshot's Kimi and an older version of Anthropic's Claude to attack as many as 100 organizations over five days in September, obtaining data on more than 600,000 credit cards, according to research firm Gambit Security. The campaign cost about $8,000, and the hacker gained access to at least 30 websites between Sept. 10 and 15, the researchers said. Anthropic said it identified and banned the account; the hacker has not been identified and the attacks appeared to be ongoing.
