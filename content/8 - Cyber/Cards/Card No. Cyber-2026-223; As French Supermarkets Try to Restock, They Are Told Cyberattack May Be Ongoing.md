# As French Supermarkets Try to Restock, They Are Told Cyberattack May Be Ongoing

[Source](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/10/16/889220.htm)

(U) France — France’s cybersecurity authority warned that a cyberattack affecting supermarket refrigeration systems might still be underway and advised stores to reconnect equipment only through protected networks, update firmware, and change passwords. The report said attackers remotely altered temperatures in hundreds of stores, causing fresh and frozen goods to be discarded, and quoted Intermarché as reporting losses of tens of millions of euros in one day. Authorities in France and the Netherlands were investigating related incidents.
