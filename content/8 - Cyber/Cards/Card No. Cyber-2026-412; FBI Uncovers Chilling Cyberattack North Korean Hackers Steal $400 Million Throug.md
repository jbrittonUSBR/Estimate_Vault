# FBI Uncovers Chilling Cyberattack: North Korean Hackers Steal $400 Million Through Fake Video Meetings

[Source](https://www.naturalnews.com/2026-11-20-north-korean-hackers-steal-400m-through-fake-video.html)

(U) Washington, D.C., United States — NaturalNews.com, a low-credibility outlet whose site labels its content as commentary, reported that the FBI said on November 12 that North Korean state-backed hackers stole more than $400 million in cryptocurrency over 18 months through a scheme it called 'FaceStealer.' The FBI attributed the activity to Sapphire Sleet (UNC1069) and said targets were lured into video calls on imposter versions of Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Google Meet after their contacts' accounts were compromised; the bureau said it seized more than 900 victim webcam videos. The article said the Justice Department reported disrupting the scheme and that the FBI warned of tailored social-engineering techniques against cryptocurrency and Web3 firms. NaturalNews noted that no independent confirmation beyond the FBI's statements was available at publication.
