# Osaki Electric ransomware attack disrupts smart meter production, raising supplier continuity concerns across Asia-Pacific

[Source](https://industrialcyber.co/threats-attacks/osaki-electric-ransomware-attack-disrupts-smart-meter-production-raising-supplier-continuity-concerns-across-asia-pacific/)

(U) TOKYO — A ransomware attack on Osaki Electric halted production-related operations at three Japanese facilities after server data encryption was detected on November 18, disrupting smart-meter manufacturing and shipments, according to Industrial Cyber's report on an OT-ISAC advisory. The OT-ISAC said supplier continuity, not a confirmed attack on the power grid, was the immediate concern; public trackers attributed the incident to the Qilin group but that attribution, data-theft claims and any compromise of operational technology or deployed meters remained unverified. Osaki owns Singapore-based metering provider EDMI, which has more than 5 million meters on its Storm platform in Oceania, and the advisory urged utilities to verify dependencies and inventory rather than disconnect field devices indiscriminately.
