# GMO Refuses to Pay $50 Million Cyberattack Ransom

[Source](https://newsonjapan.com/article/151147.php)

(U) Japan — GMO Internet Group president Masatoshi Kumagai said attackers who gained unauthorized access to servers used for foreign-exchange trading and other services demanded $50 million, and that the company refused to pay, News On Japan reported, citing his blog. The report also said the company had offered a reward for information and pledged compensation to affected customers; these details were attributed to the company.
