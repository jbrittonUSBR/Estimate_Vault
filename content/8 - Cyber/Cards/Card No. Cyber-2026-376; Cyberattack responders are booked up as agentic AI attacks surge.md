# Cyberattack responders are booked up as agentic AI attacks surge

[Source](https://www.digitaljournal.com/article/cyberattack-responders-are-booked-up-as-agentic-ai-attacks-surge/)

(U) Canada — Incident-response executive Greg Davison told Digital Journal that response firms were at capacity and turning away clients without existing retainers. He attributed the surge in requests to AI-enabled attacks that used agents to scan public systems and exploit common weaknesses such as leaked passwords and unpatched web applications. The article also cited FBI reporting that attackers had used ARTEX against organizations in several sectors; the FBI said some intrusions reached administrator access in under three hours.
