# EU Commission confirms personal data leak after cyberattack, warns of further disruption

[Source](https://www.politico.eu/article/european-commission-personal-data-leak-cyberattack-disruption/)

(U) Brussels, Belgium — European Commission spokesperson Thomas Regnier told reporters on Monday that the EU Academy platform was affected by a cyber campaign and that users' names and email addresses were leaked, with one EU official saying the full extent of the breach remained unclear. Regnier said public access to the platform was disabled as a precaution, the European Data Protection Supervisor was informed, and the Commission posted a general notice rather than emailing affected users individually. Commissioners Henna Virkkunen and Piotr Serafin warned staff in an internal note to expect further temporary system interruptions during security upgrades, after officials were unable to enter salary and leave information. Regnier declined to say the attack was contained or to attribute responsibility, though the Commission confirmed that an AI-assisted tool was involved.
