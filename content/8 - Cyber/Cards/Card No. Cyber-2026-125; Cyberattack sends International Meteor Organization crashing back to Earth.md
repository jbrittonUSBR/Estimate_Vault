# Cyberattack sends International Meteor Organization crashing back to Earth

[Source](https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/09/14/cyberattack-sends-international-meteor-organization-crashing-back-to-earth/5296282)

(U) BRUSSELS, Belgium — The International Meteor Organization, a Belgium-based nonprofit that coordinates meteor observations and brings together amateur and professional astronomers worldwide, said a cyberattack dealt a "critical blow" to its aging infrastructure and took much of its website offline. The group said it expected several weeks of partial downtime while transitioning to new infrastructure and services, and that it had prioritized restoring fireball reporting, which was back online. IMO did not disclose when the attack occurred, how attackers gained access, whether data was accessed or encrypted, or who was responsible, and The Register said it had not received a response to questions.
