# Cyberattack on Austrian Foreign Ministry Exposes Diplomatic Staff Data

[Source](https://securityaffairs.com/203680/hacking/cyberattack-on-austrian-foreign-ministry-exposes-diplomatic-staff-data.html)

(U) Vienna, Austria — The Austrian Foreign Ministry confirmed that a website run by an external provider was accessed without authorization between November 12 and 13 during a global Microsoft outage, and that experts found intruders had viewed names and professional contact details of ministry and diplomatic mission staff. The ministry said the breach was detected on November 13 and stopped immediately, did not affect citizens' personal data, and was reported to the Federal Office for Cyber Security and the Data Protection Authority. Security Affairs reported that officials did not say how many records were accessed, name a suspect or establish a link to the Microsoft outage.
