(U) Forescout’s 2026 H1 Threat Review found that published vulnerabilities rose **51% year-over-year** to 37,137 in the first half of 2026 (averaging ~205 new CVEs per day). More than half were rated high or critical, and 54 were exploited as zero-days. CISA added 146 CVEs to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog (up 11%), with 46% of those additions involving pre-2026 flaws. Ransomware claims increased **25%** to 4,544 incidents (about 25 per day), with 103 active groups. Key trends included:

- Growing use of AI by both researchers (accelerating vulnerability discovery) and attackers.
- Sophisticated software supply-chain compromises.
- Continued targeting of specialized devices across IT, OT, IoT, and IoMT environments (PLCs, HMIs, tank gauges, medical devices, routers, firewalls, etc.).

(U) The report emphasizes that older, unpatched systems and overlooked connected devices remain major risks.
