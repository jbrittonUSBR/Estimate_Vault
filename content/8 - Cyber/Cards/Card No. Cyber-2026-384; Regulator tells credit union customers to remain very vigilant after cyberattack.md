# Regulator tells credit union customers to remain very vigilant after cyberattack

[Source](https://www.irishtimes.com/business/2026/11/19/credit-union-customers-told-by-regulator-to-remain-very-vigilant-after-cyber-attack/)

(U) Ireland — Central Bank of Ireland deputy governor Mary-Elizabeth McMunn said the regulator was working with credit unions affected by a cyberattack and urged customers to be wary of suspicious calls, texts and emails. The attack on banking-software provider Wellington IT potentially exposed saved payee details at about 40 to 50 credit unions, including names and bank-account identifiers. A government minister said there was no evidence that members’ funds had been accessed or that fraudulent transactions had occurred.
