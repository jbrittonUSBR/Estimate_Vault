# Pro-Russia hacktivists launching attacks that could damage OT

[Source](https://www.cybersecuritydive.com/news/russian-hacktivists-critical-infrastructure-remote-access-advisory/807493/)

(U) WASHINGTON — A 26-agency advisory led by the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency warned that pro-Russian hacktivist groups are exploiting poorly secured remote access to operational technology and industrial control systems in ways that could cause physical damage, Cybersecurity Dive reported. The advisory named the groups CARR, Sector16, NoName057(16) and Z-Pentest, and it said such actors have already caused physical damage in some cases by manipulating human-machine interfaces at exposed endpoints. The article noted that the Justice Department had indicted Russian national Victoria Eduardovna Dubranova in connection with related activity. It also summarized recommended mitigations, including removing internet exposure and enforcing multifactor authentication.
