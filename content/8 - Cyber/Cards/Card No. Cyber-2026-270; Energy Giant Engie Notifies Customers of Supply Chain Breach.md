# Energy Giant Engie Notifies Customers of Supply Chain Breach

[Source](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/energy-giant-engie-supply-chain/)

(U) France — Infosecurity Magazine reported that Engie notified customers that personal data may have been exposed after unusual access to a third-party marketing tool used by partners. Engie said its own systems were not compromised and that banking data was not involved. No group had claimed the incident in the report.
