[Source](https://www.techradar.com/pro/security/state-bank-of-india-reports-cyberattack-thousands-of-customer-records-affected-heres-what-we-know)

(U) India — TechRadar reported that State Bank of India told the stock exchange it was investigating a loss of customer data after agencies alerted it to records on the dark web. A filing cited 3,897 customer records among a larger dump; the bank had not confirmed the source or authenticity.
