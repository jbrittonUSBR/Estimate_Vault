# Cyberattack compromised personal customer information, Bell MTS says

[Source](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/manitoba/cyberattack-compromised-customer-information-bell-mts-9.7432132)

(U) Winnipeg, Canada — Bell MTS said in a statement to CBC that a cyberattack earlier in the week caused customer email account lockouts and password resets, and that a third-party provider supporting its email service determined the intruder may have accessed some email accounts on Tuesday. The company said an "agentic threat actor" also accessed some Bell internal systems and obtained personal information about Bell MTS customers, but that no credit card or banking data was taken. Bell MTS said the incident was remediated, online password reset was re-enabled, and it had engaged the RCMP cybercrime unit and shared indicators of compromise with the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security. Several customers told CBC they were frustrated by the service disruptions and customer-service delays.
