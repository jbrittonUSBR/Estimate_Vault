(U) A cyberattack and data breach hitting global shipping and logistics giant **Ceva Logistics** triggered cascading supply chain disruptions and exposed customer data across multiple industries throughout Europe.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Scope of the Incident:** The breach targeted at least eight European contract logistics warehouses used for regional fulfillment. While Ceva's core global air, ocean, ground, and rail transit networks remained operational, the intrusion heavily impacted regional warehousing and order-processing systems.
- **Wide-Ranging Downstream Victims:** Because third-party logistics providers handle fulfillment for numerous brands, the breach rippled outward to affect major European retailers, banks, and gaming companies. Impacted client organizations included online retail giants **Bol** and **De Bijenkorf**, luxury eyewear brand **Ace & Tate**, football club **AFC Ajax**, banking giant **ING**, and gaming corporation **Valve**.
- **Exposed Data:** Hackers accessed systems containing customers' personal delivery information—including names, home addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, and specific order details. However, companies confirmed that financial information, payment card numbers, passwords, and account credentials were not compromised.
- **Operational Fallout:** The disruption caused severe delivery delays, temporary product removals, and, in some cases, order cancellations for customers relying on the affected warehouses. Valve specifically alerted European customers who recently purchased Steam hardware (as Ceva retains shipping data for up to 90 days).
- **Response:** Ceva activated internal security protocols, brought in external cybersecurity experts, and notified affected clients and regulatory authorities (such as the Dutch Data Protection Authority), though the company declined to disclose specific ransom demands or the initial entry vector.
