# European Commission confirms personal data stolen in AI-assisted cyberattack

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/european-commission-eu-academy-data-breach/)

(U) BRUSSELS, BELGIUM — The European Commission confirmed on November 23 that hackers stole names and email addresses of EU Academy users in a cyberattack that investigators said used AI tools, according to Crypto Briefing. The intrusion was detected October 22, EU Academy was taken offline November 11, and the Commission disclosed the attack November 19; the number of affected users was not yet known and the Commission did not attribute the attack to any actor. Officials notified the European Data Protection Supervisor and chose a website notice over direct emails, with the platform expected to remain offline until January 14, 2027, while security upgrades were made.
