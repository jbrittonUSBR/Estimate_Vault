# Cyberattack on Arizona’s court system leads to over a million people’s personal information breached

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/10/06/us-news/cyberattack-on-arizonas-court-system-leads-to-over-a-million-peoples-personal-information-breached/)

(U) Phoenix, United States — A cyberattack on Arizona’s court system compromised personal information of more than 1.3 million people with unpaid court fees, fines, or restitution, as well as records related to orders of protection and foster care cases; the Arizona Supreme Court said the intrusion began when an employee clicked a malicious email link and that no court data was altered or deleted.
