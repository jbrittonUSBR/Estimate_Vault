# Luminis Cybersecurity Incident Highlights ‘Concerning’ Rise in Attacks on Healthcare

[Source](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/east/2026/09/09/884453.htm)

(U) ANNAPOLIS, United States — Luminis Health announced that a "cyber incident by an unauthorized criminal actor" disrupted certain medical appointments and services across its facilities, including Anne Arundel Medical Center in Annapolis, though its hospitals remained open during the investigation. Benjamin Yelin, of the University of Maryland, Baltimore's Center for Cyber, Health and Hazard Strategies, cited FBI data showing the healthcare industry was the largest target for reported ransomware attacks on critical infrastructure in 2025, with 460 of 2,118 attacks hitting the healthcare and public health sphere. Yelin said hospitals face enhanced risk because they are interconnected and hold sensitive personal health data, and warned that any downtime is potentially life-threatening.
