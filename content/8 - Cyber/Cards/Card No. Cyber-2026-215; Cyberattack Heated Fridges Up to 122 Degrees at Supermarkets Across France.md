# Cyberattack Heated Fridges Up to 122 Degrees at Supermarkets Across France

[Source](https://www.breitbart.com/tech/2026/10/14/cyberattack-heated-fridges-up-to-122-degrees-at-supermarkets-across-france/)

(U) FRANCE — Breitbart reported that a cyberattack disrupted refrigeration systems at supermarkets in France, causing some display cases to heat to a reported 122°F (50°C). The report described food-safety and operational consequences while framing the incident as a compromise of connected equipment; the attribution and technical details should be treated as reported claims.
