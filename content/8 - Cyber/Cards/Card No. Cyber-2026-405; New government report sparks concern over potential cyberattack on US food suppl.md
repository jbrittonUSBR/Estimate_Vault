# New government report sparks concern over potential cyberattack on US food supply

[Source](https://abcnews.com/GMA/Food/new-government-report-sparks-concern-potential-cyberattack-us/story?id=138316014)

(U) Washington, D.C. — ABC News reported that the U.S. Department of Agriculture was hit by a cyberattack affecting its Farm Production and Conservation systems, including GIS mapping, payments and contracting, with systems shut down until further notice. Under Secretary Richard Fordyce told reporters the incident was a "cyberthreat" rather than a software update and said most systems were expected back by the end of the day. The article also cited a Government Accountability Office report warning that USDA and FDA have limited resources for cybersecurity and have not conducted food-sector emergency response scenarios since 2024; the Iowa Farmers Union called for answers about disrupted payments.
