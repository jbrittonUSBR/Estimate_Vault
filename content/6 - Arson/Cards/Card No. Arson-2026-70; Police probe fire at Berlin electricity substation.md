# Police probe fire at Berlin electricity substation

[Source](https://www.thelocal.de/20260907/police-probe-fire-at-berlin-electricity-substation)

(U) BERLIN, Germany — A fire caused damage at an electricity substation in Berlin on Monday, September 7, 2026, and authorities investigated whether it was caused by sabotage or a technical fault, German police said. Emergency services were alerted to the blaze in the Moabit area in the early hours, and police said "a construction site and part of the substation were burning and were damaged by the fire," adding there was no disruption to supply. The incident came as authorities hunted a suspected militant climate activist accused of a string of attacks on substations in North Rhine-Westphalia and Brandenburg the previous week that sought to cut supplies from coal-fired power plants. Germany had been hit by a number of attacks on its power grid in recent months, with far-left militants claiming responsibility in some cases, and authorities also remained on alert for Russia-linked sabotage.
