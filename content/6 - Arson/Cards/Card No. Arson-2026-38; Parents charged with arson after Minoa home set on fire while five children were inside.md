(U) **Parents charged with arson after Minoa home set on fire while five children were inside** (CNY Central, Gary Robinson Jr. and Matthew Benninger, July 21, 2026) Dale and Aubree Oneal were charged by the Manlius Police Department after investigators determined they intentionally set a fire inside their own home at 100 Crisfield Circle in Minoa, New York, while five children (all under age 17) were inside.

**Charges**:
- Felony arson
- Felony conspiracy
- Misdemeanor endangering the welfare of a child
- Misdemeanor reckless endangerment

(U) Emergency crews responded on the evening of July 18. Police said both parents “took actions” that led to the fire being intentionally set. Dale Oneal extinguished the fire, and everyone got out of the home. Officers were later called back for a “subsequent dispute”; both parents appeared intoxicated, according to police. The house sustained only minor damage, and no injuries were reported. The children are staying with a family member. The Oneals were being held at the Onondaga County Justice Center as of Tuesday and were expected to return to Manlius Town Court on July 24.
