# TikTok couple charged with arson after house fire causes $10K in damage

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/us/tiktok-couple-charged-arson-house-fire-causes-10k-damage)

(U) CHARLESTON, United States — Dakota Harper, 21, and Felicity Eatmon, 22, a couple with about 28,000 TikTok followers, were arrested on September 29, 2026 and each charged with first-degree arson and conspiracy after allegedly setting fire to an abandoned home on Charleston's West Side the previous night. Surveillance footage reportedly showed Eatmon handing Harper a piece of paper that was lit and placed inside the house; the blaze caused more than $10,000 in damage to the adjacent Canaan United Methodist Church, and both were held on $10,000 bond.
