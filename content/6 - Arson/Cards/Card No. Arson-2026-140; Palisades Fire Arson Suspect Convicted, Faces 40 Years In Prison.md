# Palisades Fire Arson Suspect Convicted, Faces 40 Years In Prison

[Source](https://deadline.com/2026/11/palisades-fire-trial-verdict-1236967822/)

(U) Los Angeles, United States — A Los Angeles jury found Jonathan Rinderknecht, 30, guilty of destruction of property by means of fire and arson affecting property used in interstate commerce for igniting the Lachman Fire just after midnight on January 1, 2025, near Pacific Palisades. Prosecutors said the fire smoldered for a week before Santa Ana winds reignited it on January 7 as the Palisades Fire, which killed 12 people and destroyed about 6,500 structures; defense counsel Steven Haney argued the case was circumstantial and had no eyewitnesses. A prior jury had deadlocked 10-2 in June, and U.S. District Judge Anne Hwang set sentencing for March 17, 2027, with a statutory maximum of 40 years in federal prison.
