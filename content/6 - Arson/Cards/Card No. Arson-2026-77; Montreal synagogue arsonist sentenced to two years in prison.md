# Montreal synagogue arsonist sentenced to two years in prison

[Source](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/433044)

(U) MONTREAL, Canada — Quebec Court Judge Karine Giguère sentenced 20-year-old Mohamed Ilyess Akodad to two years in prison, followed by three years of probation, for firebombing Congregation Beth Tikvah in Dollard-des-Ormeaux after being offered money for a series of arsons. Akodad, who pleaded guilty in January, also smashed windows at a nearby Jewish community centre; taking into account time already served, he had 127 days remaining and was ordered to complete 100 hours of community service. B'nai Brith Canada welcomed the sentence.
