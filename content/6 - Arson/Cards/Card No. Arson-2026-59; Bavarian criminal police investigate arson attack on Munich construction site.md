# Bavarian criminal police investigate arson attack on Munich construction site

[Source](https://www.thelocal.de/20260903/bavarian-criminal-police-investigate-arson-attack-on-munich-construction-site)

(U) MUNICH, Germany — The Bavarian State Criminal Police Office (LKA) investigated an arson attack on a construction site in Munich's Berg am Laim district, suspecting the intended target was a defense or arms manufacturer based nearby. Several incendiary devices were thrown from a car around midnight; at least one started a fire that police extinguished with no major damage, and a second device was defused by special forces. A witness alerted police, who arrested a 28-year-old woman and a 38-year-old man at a nearby gas station; both were identified by the LKA as Bulgarian citizens. A BR24 report noted the site was located in front of the Rohde & Schwarz technology group, though an LKA spokesman did not name a specific company.
