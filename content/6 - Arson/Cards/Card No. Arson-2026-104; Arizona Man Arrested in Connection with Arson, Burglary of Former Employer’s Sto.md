# Arizona Man Arrested in Connection with Arson, Burglary of Former Employer’s Store After He Was Fired

[Source](https://people.com/arizona-man-arrested-in-connection-with-arson-burglary-former-employer-store-after-he-was-fired-12135826)

(U) NEW RIVER, Ariz. — Noah Aaron Ross, 23, was arrested on Sept. 21 and charged with arson, third-degree burglary, unlawful entry and criminal damage over a June 30 fire at Three Bars Feed and Tack, where he had worked. The blaze burned hay and a storage container and caused about $60,000 in losses, according to the store's owner. Investigators said they linked Ross to the scene through the store's Wi-Fi, whose password had not been changed after his termination, and he allegedly admitted setting the hay alight.
