# Petrol bomber jailed over attack on family home

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — A man has been jailed for attacking a family home with petrol bombs in an arson attack. The court heard the incident targeted a residential property, endangering the occupants inside. The perpetrator was sentenced to a significant prison term for the deliberate firebombing, which was treated as a serious violent offence.
