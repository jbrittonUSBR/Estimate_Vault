# Waterford community held vigil after fatal house fire

[Source](https://www.thejournal.ie/waterford-arson-attack-vigil-7193637-Oct2026/)

(U) WATERFORD, Ireland — TheJournal.ie reported that residents gathered for a vigil after four members of the English family died in a suspected arson attack at their home. Family members and a friend described the victims and called for anyone with information to come forward; two surviving children remained in critical condition in hospital. The article also recounted a family friend's claim that the family had previously feared for its safety, without presenting that claim as an official finding.
