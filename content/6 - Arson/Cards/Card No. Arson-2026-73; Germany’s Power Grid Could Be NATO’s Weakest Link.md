# Germany’s Power Grid Could Be NATO’s Weakest Link

[Source](https://oilprice.com/Energy/Energy-General/Germanys-Power-Grid-Could-Be-NATOs-Weakest-Link.html)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — An OilPrice analysis argued that Germany's fragmented civilian power grid could be NATO's weakest link as the alliance prepares to move large numbers of troops eastward. The article noted that the grid is operated by 851 distribution operators and has suffered sabotage incidents at substations including Jänschwalde, Bergheim and Dormagen. It warned that this infrastructure vulnerability could hinder the rapid movement of up to 800,000 troops in a crisis.
