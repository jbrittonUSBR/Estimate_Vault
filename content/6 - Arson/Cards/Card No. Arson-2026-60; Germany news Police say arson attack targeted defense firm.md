# Germany news: Police say arson attack targeted defense firm

[Source](https://www.dw.com/en/germany-news-police-say-arson-attack-targeted-defense-firm/live-78818089)

(U) MUNICH, Germany — Bavarian Interior Minister Joachim Herrmann said "arson attacks" early Thursday targeted "defense and security companies in Munich" and likely had a "political background," after incendiary devices were thrown at a construction site in the Berg am Laim borough. Two Bulgarian citizens were arrested after police, alerted by a witness shortly after midnight, extinguished one small fire that caused no damage and defused a second device with a bomb disposal unit. The Bavarian Central Office for Combating Extremism and Terrorism (ZET) took over the investigation, which focused on whether one of the nearby defense contractors was targeted; the Bild newspaper reported the site was near the headquarters of Rohde & Schwarz. Herrmann linked the incident to a broader pattern of attacks in Germany, including an attempted drone attack at Leipzig airport and suspected sabotage of power infrastructure.
