# Louisiana Man Pleads Guilty to Burning Church

[Source](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/southcentral/2026/09/10/884669.htm)

(U) CALDWELL PARISH, United States — Anthony Dillon Jr., 19, of Columbia, Louisiana, pleaded guilty in federal court to arson in connection with the burning of St. John Chapel on March 27, 2026. According to court documents, Dillon and an accomplice stole televisions from inside the church and then ignited a fire in the administrative area using an accelerant in a wooden filing cabinet; he faces a mandatory minimum of five years and up to 20 years in prison. US Attorney Zachary A. Keller for the Western District of Louisiana announced the plea.
