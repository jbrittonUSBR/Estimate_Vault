# Houston Businessman Sentenced in Arson-For-Hire Scheme

[Source](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/southcentral/2026/11/02/891216.htm)

(U) Houston, Texas, USA - Insurance Journal reported that Lyndell Leroy Price was sentenced to 105 months in federal prison after admitting he directed others to set fire to Bar 5015, a business owned by his former partner. The 2020 fire caused significant damage and endangered nearby homes, businesses and electrical infrastructure; four co-conspirators had also pleaded guilty and were awaiting sentencing.
