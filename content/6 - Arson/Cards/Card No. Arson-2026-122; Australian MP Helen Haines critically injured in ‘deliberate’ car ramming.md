# Australian MP Helen Haines critically injured in ‘deliberate’ car ramming

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/19/australian-mp-helen-haines-critically-injured-in-deliberate-car-ramming)

(U) Wangaratta, Australia — Independent MP Helen Haines was critically injured after a Toyota Land Cruiser struck her outside her electoral office, and Prime Minister Anthony Albanese described the incident as deliberate. Bystanders detained the 43-year-old driver and handed him to police, who said they found a knife in his vehicle.
