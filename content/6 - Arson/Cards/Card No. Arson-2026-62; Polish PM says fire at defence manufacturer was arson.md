# Polish PM says fire at defence manufacturer was arson

(U) WARSAW, Poland — Prime Minister Donald Tusk said a fire at the WB Group defence manufacturing plant in Skarżysko-Kamienna was deliberate arson, with Polish police and the Internal Security Agency investigating. A separate fire at JFG Composites in Lublin, which produces aviation components, is not yet confirmed as arson but cannot be ruled out. Tusk linked the incidents to Russia's continued escalation across the region, coming amid heightened concerns over sabotage and recent accusations of Russian involvement in other incidents across Europe.
