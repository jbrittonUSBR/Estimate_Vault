(U) Three men—Kais Kassoua, 45, Logan Drake, 34, and Chayce Krause, 31—were charged with arson after a fire early on March 16 destroyed a Calgary automotive facility housing Kulu Motorcar, a luxury and exotic vehicle dealership, and Tunerworks performance shop, causing an estimated $7 million in damage. The blaze consumed the building and approximately 25 high-end vehicles, including rare Porsches such as a 996 GT3 RS and a 992 GT3, though no injuries were reported. Police determined the fire was intentionally set, leading to the charges; Drake and Krause appeared in court on June 24 and were remanded in custody, while Kassoua is scheduled for July 2. No motive was disclosed, and authorities believe no other suspects were involved in the incident that devastated a collection of irreplaceable vehicles.
