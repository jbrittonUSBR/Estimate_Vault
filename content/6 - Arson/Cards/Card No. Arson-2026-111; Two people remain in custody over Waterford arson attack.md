# Two people remain in custody over Waterford arson attack

[Source](https://www.rte.ie/news/2026/1016/1595588-waterford-fire-investigation/)

(U) Waterford — Garda Commissioner Justin Kelly said investigators expected further arrests over the fatal arson attack that killed Kim and Philip English and their two sons. He said seven searches had been carried out and a man and a woman remained in custody, while investigators had made significant progress. Kelly described the case as a difficult investigation and urged people with information to contact Gardaí.
