# FBI Investigating Suspicious Substances Mailed to Congress (2)

[Source](https://news.bgov.com/bloomberg-government-news/fbi-investigating-suspicious-materials-mailed-to-capitol-hill)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — The FBI was investigating suspicious substances mailed to multiple congressional offices on Capitol Hill, prompting precautionary responses from security personnel. Authorities had not publicly identified the materials or any suspect, and the incident remained under active investigation.
