# Sister of 13yo critically injured in alleged arson pleads with other teens

[Source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-09/sister-melbourne-teenager-burned-alleged-botched-arson/107247014)

(U) MELBOURNE, Australia — A 13-year-old boy was critically injured when a firebombing of two cars in Taylors Hill, Melbourne, backfired, leaving him with burns to almost his entire body. Police alleged the group was tasked by middlemen via WhatsApp to carry out the arson for a promised payment, and in the aftermath a co-accused admitted involvement, apologized, and offered the boy's family cash. Two 16-year-olds were charged, while the boy remained in critical condition following a lengthy skin graft surgery.
