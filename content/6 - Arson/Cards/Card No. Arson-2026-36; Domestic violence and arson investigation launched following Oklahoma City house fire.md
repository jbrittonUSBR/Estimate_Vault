(U) Oklahoma City police and arson investigators launched a domestic violence and arson probe after a house fire on Northeast 13th Street where firefighters found a woman bleeding from her head and removed her in serious or critical condition; she was later reported in surgery and critical. The woman’s 35-year-old boyfriend was arrested on two counts of domestic assault and held on $250,000 bond after authorities determined the fire was set following an alleged assault. The victim’s mother described waking to her daughter screaming and sounds of someone being hit, then finding her bleeding in the hallway with smoke coming from the bedroom and the boyfriend missing. Oklahoma City Fire Department shift commander Sean Cobb confirmed the woman was located at the front of the house and treated on scene in serious critical condition, while the investigation into the arson and assault continues.
