# Lithuania foils arson attempt on company storing Starlink equipment

[Source](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/10/18/8058591/)

(U) Lithuania — Authorities thwarted an overnight attempt to set fire to a company storing Starlink communications equipment, Prime Minister Mindaugas Sinkevičius said. He said the company had been warned of possible attacks and security had been increased; the attempted fire did not succeed, and he called a National Security Commission meeting to discuss the incident and a separate drone incursion.
