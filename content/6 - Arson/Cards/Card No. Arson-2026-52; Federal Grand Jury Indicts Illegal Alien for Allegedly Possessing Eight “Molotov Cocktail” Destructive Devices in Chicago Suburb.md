(U) A federal grand jury returned an indictment against a 31-year-old man residing in Oak Forest, Illinois, following the discovery of multiple explosive devices in a Chicago suburb.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Charges:** Hamed Alsaidi was indicted on eight counts of possessing an unregistered destructive device. If convicted, each count carries a potential penalty of up to 10 years in federal prison.
- **The Incident:** According to court documents, Alsaidi knowingly possessed eight glass liquor and beer bottles packed with an ignitable liquid and wicking components—commonly referred to as "Molotov cocktails"—on August 1, 2026, in Mokena, Illinois.
- **Custody Status:** Federal prosecutors noted that Alsaidi is an illegal alien in the United States and was placed into the custody of U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE).
- **Investigation:** The case was investigated jointly by the U.S. Attorney’s Office for the Northern District of Illinois and the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives (ATF) Chicago Field Division, with assistance from local police and the state fire marshal.
