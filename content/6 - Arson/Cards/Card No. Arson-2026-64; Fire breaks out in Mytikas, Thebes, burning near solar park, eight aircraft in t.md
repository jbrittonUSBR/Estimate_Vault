# Fire breaks out in Mytikas, Thebes, burning near solar park, eight aircraft in the battle

[Source](https://en.protothema.gr/2026/09/02/fire-breaks-out-in-mytikas-thebes-burning-near-solar-park-eight-aircraft-in-the-battle/)

(U) THEBES, Greece — A wildfire in low vegetation broke out at midday Wednesday in the Mytikas area of the Municipality of Thebes, burning close to a solar park. The Fire Service reported that 42 firefighters were operating at the scene, supported by a ground patrol unit from the 4th Special Forest Fire Force (EMODE), volunteers, 16 fire engines, and eight firefighting aircraft conducting water drops; water tankers and heavy machinery from local authorities were also mobilized. The Fire Service Operations Centre was informed at about 1:50 pm, drone footage with thermal and optical cameras fed continuous updates to ESKEDIK, and the day had been classified as a category 3 high fire risk day in the area. The Arson Crimes Investigation Unit of Fthiotida was mobilized to examine the cause of the fire.
