# German police arrest suspect in sabotage attacks on power grid

[Source](https://www.thelocal.de/20260908/german-police-arrest-suspect-in-sabotage-attacks-on-power-grid)

(U) COLOGNE, Germany — Police apprehended a 48-year-old suspect, partially named in German media as Daniel V., near the Weisweiler power plant in North Rhine-Westphalia after a spate of sabotage attacks on the power grid in Brandenburg and NRW. Police said the man was carrying additional explosive devices when arrested and offered no resistance, and that a tent containing further explosive charges was found nearby. The attacks followed a similar pattern, using firework-like rockets to shoot wires across high-voltage cables to cause short circuits; they caused no major outages but forced some power-generating units temporarily off the grid. Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt said authorities had to assume the case involved climate extremism.
