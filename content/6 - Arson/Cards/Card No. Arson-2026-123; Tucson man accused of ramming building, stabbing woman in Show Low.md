# Tucson man accused of ramming building, stabbing woman in Show Low

[Source](https://www.azfamily.com/2026/10/19/tucson-man-accused-ramming-building-stabbing-woman-show-low/)

(U) Show Low, Arizona — Police said a Tucson man rammed a vehicle into a convenience store and stabbed a woman multiple times on October 17. Officers later located and arrested the suspect after a standoff at a nearby commercial property; he was booked on charges including attempted homicide, aggravated assault, armed robbery, and criminal damage.
