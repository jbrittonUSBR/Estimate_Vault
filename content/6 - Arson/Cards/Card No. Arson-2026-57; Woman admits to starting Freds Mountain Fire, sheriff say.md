(U) A 47-year-old woman was arrested after allegedly confessing to emergency personnel that she intentionally started the Fred Mountain Fire near the California-Nevada border.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Incident & Confession:** Christine Mitchell allegedly left a residence near Freds Mountain Road and intentionally set multiple fires. When firefighters arrived to tackle the blazes, Mitchell flagged them down and explicitly told them she had started the fires.
- **The Arrest & Charges:** Fire personnel transferred Mitchell to the custody of the Washoe County Sheriff’s Office. She was booked on charges of **third-degree arson** and **possession of a controlled substance**.
- **Broader Impact:** The Fred Mountain Fire merged with the nearby human-caused Bug Fire, combining to burn tens of thousands of acres north of Reno, Nevada. The resulting blazes triggered widespread evacuations, school closures, and a state of emergency declaration.
