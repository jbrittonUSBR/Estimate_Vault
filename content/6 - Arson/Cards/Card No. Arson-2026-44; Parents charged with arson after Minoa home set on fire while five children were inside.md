(U) Dale and Aubree Oneal were charged with felony arson and conspiracy, plus misdemeanor endangering the welfare of a child and reckless endangerment, after allegedly intentionally setting a fire inside their home at 100 Crisfield Circle in Minoa, New York, on the evening of July 18. Five children under age 17 were inside at the time. Dale Oneal extinguished the fire, and everyone got out safely with no injuries and only minor damage to the house. Police later responded to a dispute at the scene and noted both adults appeared intoxicated. The couple is being held at the Onondaga County Justice Center; the children are staying with a family member. They are due back in Manlius Town Court on July 24.
