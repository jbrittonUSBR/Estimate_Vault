# Man convicted of arson over deadly 2025 Palisades fire in Los Angeles

[Source](https://www.dw.com/en/man-convicted-of-arson-over-deadly-2025-palisades-fire-in-los-angeles/a-80007933)

(U) Los Angeles, United States — A US District Court jury in Los Angeles convicted a 30-year-old dual US-French man of federal arson charges for igniting a hilltop brush fire on January 1, 2025, in the Santa Monica Mountains National Recreation Area that smoldered underground and reignited days later amid high winds. Prosecutors cited security-camera footage and cellphone location data showing he was the only person near the origin, while the defense argued fireworks could have caused the blaze; an earlier trial had ended in a hung jury and a mistrial in June. The resulting Palisades Fire killed 12 people, destroyed about 6,800 homes and other structures, and caused an estimated $150 billion in property losses. Sentencing was set for March 17, with First Assistant US Attorney Bill Essayli saying the man could face up to 40 years in prison.
