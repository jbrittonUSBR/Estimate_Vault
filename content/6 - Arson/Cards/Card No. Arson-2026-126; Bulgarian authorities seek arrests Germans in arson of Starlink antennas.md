# Bulgaria seeks arrests of two Germans over Starlink antenna arson

[Source](https://thehill.com/homenews/ap/ap-international/ap-bulgarian-authorities-seek-arrests-germans-in-arson-of-starlink-antennas/)

(U) Sofia, Bulgaria — Bulgarian authorities sought European arrest warrants for two German citizens accused of setting fire to Starlink antennas near Sofia on October 9. Prosecutors said three antennas were destroyed, two partially damaged, and three others affected; the two men were charged in absentia, and the allegations had not been adjudicated.
