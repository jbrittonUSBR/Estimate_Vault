# Gardaí feared further violence after fatal Waterford house fire; family cited alleged drug debt

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16210189/Gardai-violence-couple-children-killed-arson-attack.html)

(U) WATERFORD, IRELAND — The Daily Mail reported that Gardaí feared further violence after a fatal house fire killed a couple and two of their children, with two other children seriously injured. The article cited an alleged €20,000 drug debt as a possible motive and reported that two arrests had been made; the motive remained an allegation.
