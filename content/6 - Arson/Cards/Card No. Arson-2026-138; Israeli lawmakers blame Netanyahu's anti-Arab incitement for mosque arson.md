# Israeli lawmakers blame Netanyahu's anti-Arab incitement for mosque arson

[Source](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/2026-11-19/ty-article/.premium/israeli-lawmakers-blame-netanyahus-anti-arab-incitement-for-mosque-arson/000001a1-f35a-d175-abf5-ff7e06e40000)

(U) Northern Israel — A mosque was set ablaze in a suspected arson attack, prompting police to search for suspects and the Shin Bet to join the investigation, Haaretz reported. Arab and Jewish opposition lawmakers accused Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and his government of fueling anti-Arab incitement; several linked the attack to car-ramming incidents in an Arab town the previous evening.
