(U) DES MOINES, Iowa — Des Moines police say the third suspect, identified as a 17-year-old female, was taken into custody and referred to juvenile courts. Arson detectives in Des Moines are seeking the public's help to identify individuals involved in a fire early Friday morning. Police and firefighters responded to a report of a fire in front of the Krause Gateway Center on Grand Avenue just after 1 a.m. When they arrived, they found a couch fully engulfed in flames that had been discarded in the sculpture park early Thursday night. Evidence indicates three people were involved in setting the fire. Authorities have identified 21-year-old Caleb Beuter and a 17-year-old female as two of the suspects. Both are charged with felony arson and felony conspiracy.
