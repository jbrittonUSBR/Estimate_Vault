# Man pleads guilty to arson at mosque on Talbot Street

[Source](https://www.rte.ie/news/regional/2026/1119/1599910-khosroabadi-courts-dublin/)

(U) Dublin, Ireland — Saeid Khosroabadi, 42, an Iranian national from Seville Place, pleaded guilty at Dublin Circuit Criminal Court to arson aggravated by hatred for the June 29 fire at the Faizan E Madinah Mosque on Talbot Street. Judge Martin Nolan granted an extension of legal aid for a psychological report, adjourned the case to January 15, 2027, for sentencing, and remanded Khosroabadi in custody.
