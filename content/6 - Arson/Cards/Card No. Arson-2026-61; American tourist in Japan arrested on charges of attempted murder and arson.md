# American tourist in Japan arrested on charges of attempted murder and arson

[Source](https://soranews24.com/2026/09/03/american-tourist-in-japan-arrested-on-charges-of-attempted-murder-and-arson/)

(U) OKINAWA, Japan — A 32-year-old American tourist was rearrested on charges of arson and attempted murder over a fire that broke out in the pre-dawn hours of August 15 at the Hamby Resort in the Chatan neighborhood of Okinawa City. The hotel, located a few blocks from U.S. Marine Corps Camp Foster, had 19 guests staying in its trailer-style bungalows; no lives were lost, though a 55-year-old man from Osaka lost consciousness from smoke inhalation before being rescued by police and a U.S. Marine volunteer and remained hospitalized. Six days after the blaze, Posedion Roberson was arrested for trespassing and sleeping in a building complex in Urasoe, and investigators later identified him as having booked a room at the hotel on the night of the fire before disappearing; he was formally rearrested on September 1 and remained in police custody.
