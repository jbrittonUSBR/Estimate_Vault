# Sonoma County deputies arrest ‘prolific’ copper thief

[Source](https://www.kron4.com/news/bay-area/sonoma-county-deputies-arrest-prolific-copper-thief/)

(U) SONOMA COUNTY, California — KRON4 reported that Sonoma County deputies arrested Robert Hamilton, 58, after a days-long investigation into copper thefts. Deputies said they found hundreds of pounds of copper components in his truck, along with tools and other evidence associated with a burglary.
