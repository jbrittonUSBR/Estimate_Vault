(U) Missouri fire officials determined that a house fire during a police standoff in the 700 block of Brookside Avenue in Independence on June 16, 2026, was arson, deliberately started on a bed in a first-floor bedroom using available combustibles and an open flame with no accelerant. The blaze occurred hours after suspected shooter Oscar Sanchez-Munoz allegedly fired at vehicles along Interstate 670, Interstate 70, Paseo Boulevard, and other roads, injuring four people and killing Jeremy Keenan in a crash. Sanchez-Munoz’s body was later found in the flooded basement on June 24 after his family reported a strong odor, ending a weeklong manhunt; a cadaver dog had alerted to possible remains the night of the fire, but initial searches missed it amid flooding and debris. The Missouri Division of Fire Safety assisted the Independence Fire Department in confirming the cause as arson, clarifying that their focus was on the fire’s origin rather than locating a body, with a final report expected in coming weeks.
