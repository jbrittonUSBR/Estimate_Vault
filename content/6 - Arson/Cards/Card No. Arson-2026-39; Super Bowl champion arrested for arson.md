(U) **Super Bowl champion arrested for arson** (al.com / PennLive, Matt Connolly, July 20, 2026) Former NFL running back Javian Hawkins, who won a Super Bowl with the Los Angeles Rams, was arrested in Florida on arson-related charges. According to reports from FOX 35 in Orlando, Hawkins allegedly unlawfully entered a secured property at Tropic Care of Florida (a landscaping company that had recently fired him), took gasoline from a work vehicle, and set three company pickup trucks on fire early on the morning of July 15. The flames spread to the outside of the building before Brevard County Fire Rescue extinguished them. He was arrested on July 16.

(U) Hawkins was identified through surveillance footage, witness interviews, and physical evidence. Charges include armed burglary, arson of a structure or business, three counts of arson, and felony criminal mischief. Hawkins starred at Louisville (rushing for 1,525 yards and nine touchdowns as a sophomore with All-ACC honors, plus 822 yards and seven touchdowns in 2020) before going undrafted in 2021. He signed with the Atlanta Falcons as an undrafted free agent and also spent time with the Titans and Rams that year—his only NFL season.
