(U) Andrew Arrabaca, 43 (a U.S. Army veteran who worked on Patriot missile systems), faces a federal arson charge for the July 20 incident outside 26 Federal Plaza in Manhattan (home to an immigration court, ICE, and FBI offices). He did not enter a plea and was ordered detained. Prosecutors say he planned the attack for months, arriving with a utility wagon bearing an “ICE Off Our Streets” sign that contained fireworks, pellet/BB guns, and other items. He allegedly poured flammable liquid, ignited a fire, and fired 5–7 pellets from an airsoft rifle toward the building. Three people were injured. Upon arrest he was found with axes, knives, a hammer, and a machete.The FBI described him as an anti-government extremist who made anti-ICE statements and said he was willing to hurt or kill people entering the building. A preliminary hearing is set for August 4; the charge carries a mandatory minimum of seven years.
