(U) A Saturday afternoon vegetation fire near a Medford shopping center damaged a local restaurant and multiple vehicles before being fully extinguished, resulting in the quick arrest of an arson suspect.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Incident:** On a Saturday afternoon, emergency crews responded to a vegetation fire that broke out between a Chuck E. Cheese and the New China Restaurant near the WinCo Shopping Center on East Barnett Road.
- **Damage:** The blaze destroyed three vehicles, a trailer, patches of vegetation, and caused partial structural damage to the New China Restaurant. Swift intervention by firefighters prevented the flames from reaching a nearby hotel and other commercial buildings. No injuries were reported.
- **The Suspect:** Police identified and arrested 41-year-old **Byron Hays Rogers** in connection with the fire. Surveillance video from a nearby business captured the suspect acting aggressively and bending over near the vegetation just before smoke began.
- **Arrest & Charges:** Rogers was located by officers on South Riverside while reportedly attempting to start another fire. He was booked into the Jackson County Jail without bail and charged with **second-degree arson, first-degree criminal mischief, and reckless burning**.
