# Man arrested on suspicion of arson for allegedly starting Spokane fire

[Source](https://abcnews.com/US/man-arrested-suspicion-arson-allegedly-starting-spokane-fire/story?id=135343244)

(U) SPOKANE, United States — The Spokane County Sheriff's Office announced the arrest of Aaron Farinacci, 37, on suspicion of first-degree arson for allegedly starting the Old Trails Fire, one of three blazes burning in the Spokane area. Officials said Farinacci was arrested on August 3, 2026, and remained in custody on a $1 million bond after prosecutors argued he poses a danger to the community. Sheriff John Nowels said a witness reported seeing a man "looking very nervous" near the fire's origin before smoke appeared, and that Farinacci was later found about a mile and a half from the fire carrying waterproof matches and a butane lighter; he had previously been convicted of manslaughter in Arizona in 2010.
