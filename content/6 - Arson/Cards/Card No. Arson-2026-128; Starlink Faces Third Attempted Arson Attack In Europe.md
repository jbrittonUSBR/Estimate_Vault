# Starlink Faces Third Attempted Arson Attack In Europe

[Source](https://www.pcmag.com/news/starlink-faces-third-attempted-arson-attack-in-europe)

(U) October 19, 2026 — PCMag reported a third attempted arson incident targeting Starlink-related infrastructure in Europe, this time at a Lithuanian site storing Starlink equipment. Lithuanian officials said the attempt was thwarted and suggested it was connected to earlier incidents in Poland and Bulgaria; the article described Russian involvement as an allegation, not a confirmed attribution. The reported incident caused no disruption to Starlink operations.
