# NH man wanted on felony arson charge found near scene of fire after cross-border search

[Source](https://www.wmur.com/article/derrick-couture-arrested-nashua-arson/73839853)

(U) NASHUA, N.H. — Derrick Couture, 39, was arrested on a felony arson warrant after police found him walking near the scene of the Barnesdale Road house fire he was accused of setting, the Nashua Police Department said. Couture ran from officers before being taken into custody and was also charged with criminal trespass, criminal threatening and resisting arrest. He was held on preventive detention pending arraignment in Nashua.
