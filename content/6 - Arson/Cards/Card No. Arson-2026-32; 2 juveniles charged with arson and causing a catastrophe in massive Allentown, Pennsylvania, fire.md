(U) Two juveniles were charged with arson, causing a catastrophe, recklessly endangering another person, and conspiracy after a massive fire at an old furniture factory in the 300 block of North Front Street in Allentown, Pennsylvania, on Wednesday night. The blaze destroyed several properties, forced nearby residents to evacuate, and left hundreds in the area without power. The Lehigh County District Attorney’s Office announced the charges on Friday, with the juveniles in custody in the juvenile system; their names and ages were not released. The cause of the fire remains under investigation as authorities continue to examine the extent of the damage and disruption caused by the incident.
