# Fireworks fired toward London synagogue as Jews mark Rosh Hashanah

[Source](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/433177)

(U) LONDON — Five youths set off two rocket-style fireworks toward the Lauderdale Road Synagogue in Maida Vale, northwest London, at about 3:23 p.m. on Sunday, September 13, as Jews marked Rosh Hashanah, Israel National News reported. The building was empty at the time, and no injuries or damage were reported. The incident was reported to police and to the Community Security Trust, and the S&P Sephardi Community issued a statement on the attack, which the report described as serious. The article said the episode followed other antisemitic incidents in the British capital.
