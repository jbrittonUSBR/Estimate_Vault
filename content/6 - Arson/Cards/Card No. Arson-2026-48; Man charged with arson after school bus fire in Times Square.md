(U) Jonathan Strahs, 30, of Brooklyn, faces a federal arson charge for allegedly setting an empty school bus on fire in Times Square around 2:45 a.m. on June 14.
The bus, which had been used to shuttle fans to a World Cup match at MetLife Stadium, was parked on West 42nd Street as crowds celebrated the New York Knicks’ NBA championship victory. Surveillance video reportedly shows Strahs entering and exiting the bus shortly before it became fully engulfed; the fire also spread to another vehicle. He was arrested at his home early Monday following a joint investigation by the FDNY, NYPD, and ATF. FDNY officials noted the fire endangered bystanders and first responders during the celebration.
