(U) An early-morning fire at the **Alan Webb Chevrolet** dealership in Vancouver, Washington, heavily damaged or destroyed a dozen vehicles, prompting an active investigation into suspected arson.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Incident:** Vancouver Fire Department crews responded to the dealership parking lot shortly after 4:00 a.m. on a Monday after multiple 911 calls reported vehicles on fire near a fence line and trees.
- **The Damage:** A total of 12 vehicles—including large SUVs, trucks, and pre-owned cars awaiting resale—sustained severe fire damage. Some cars were burned beyond recognition, though no injuries were reported.
- **Arson Investigation:** Vancouver Fire Marshal Heidi Scarpelli noted that it is highly unusual for multiple cars to catch fire simultaneously, and officials are investigating the incident as a **suspected arson**.
- **No Arrests:** As of the initial reports, no arrests have been made, and investigators are reviewing surveillance footage while appealing to the public for tips.
