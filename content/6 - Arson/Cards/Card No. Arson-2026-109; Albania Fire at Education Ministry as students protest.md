# Albania: Fire at Education Ministry as students protest

[Source](https://www.dw.com/en/albania-fire-at-education-ministry-as-students-protest/a-79661193)

(U) TIRANA, Albania — A fire broke out at Albania's Education Ministry during ongoing student protests over education reforms, causing damage to parts of the building. Authorities were investigating the cause of the blaze as demonstrations continued in the capital.
