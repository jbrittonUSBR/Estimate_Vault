# Man arrested over fire that disrupted rail service

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — A man was arrested on suspicion of arson after a grass fire near London Paddington station damaged overhead cables, causing major disruption to Heathrow Express, Elizabeth Line, and Great Western Railway services. Nearly 400 passengers were evacuated from a train that lost power. The suspect has been released on bail pending further enquiries.
