# Russia calls Leipzig drone attack accusations 'start of real war'

[Source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-06/russia-responds-to-leipzig-accusations/107122062)

(U) MOSCOW, Russia — Russian Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov said accusations that Moscow was implicated in a drone incident at Germany's Leipzig Airport amounted to "by and large, the start of a real war," state news agency TASS reported on Sunday, September 6, 2026. Lavrov said statements by German Chancellor Friedrich Merz about making Germany the leading military power in Europe were being put into practice, adding "he is, in effect, declaring war on us." The German government had said on September 1 that it concluded Russia was responsible for the alleged drone attack at Leipzig Airport the previous month and ordered measures including the closure of a consulate and a Russian cultural centre in Berlin. Russia denied involvement and accused Berlin of planting evidence to frame it, while German Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt outlined plans for an anti-sabotage "cyberdome."
