# Wanted man accused of Winnipeg arsons and extortions may be in Ontario, police say

[Source](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/manitoba/winnipeg-police-warrant-arson-extortion-charges-9.7339119)

(U) WINNIPEG, Canada — Winnipeg police said Farhan Nabil, 29, remained wanted on a Canada-wide warrant on two counts of arson and two counts of extortion tied to a string of incidents in July 2025, and that investigators believed he may now be in Ontario. Police alleged Nabil was part of a group that extorted local businesses "using arson as a weapon," with fires set between July 16 and 23, 2025 at a warehouse on Spruce Street and two convenience stores on Selkirk Avenue, while three other convenience stores paid for protection. Six other people have already been arrested in connection with the incidents, including a 41-year-old Ontario man taken into custody in Toronto in May.
