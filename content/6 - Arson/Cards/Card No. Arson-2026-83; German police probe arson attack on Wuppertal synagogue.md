# German police probe arson attack on Wuppertal synagogue

[Source](https://www.dw.com/en/wuppertal-synagogue-attack/a-79331270)

(U) WUPPERTAL, Germany — Prosecutors in the western German city of Wuppertal took over the investigation into an attempted arson attack on the local synagogue, saying on Friday, September 18, 2026, that a 37-year-old Afghan national arrested near the scene made comments indicating an antisemitic motive. The attack took place midafternoon on Thursday, when the suspect allegedly threw three containers of flammable liquid at a stairwell and doorway serving as an entrance to the synagogue and its adjoining eatery, Cafe Negev, and set it alight; a staff member quickly extinguished the fire, so no one was hurt and structural damage was minimal. Police on security duty arrested the suspect nearby, and a search of his apartment uncovered knives, a machete, and documents. He had a prior criminal record for threatening a female neighbor and was being investigated for attempted aggravated arson. The Central Council of Jews in Germany condemned the attack ahead of Yom Kippur.
