(U) A Cornish man is being charged in connection with a fire at a residence that occurred May 25, according to officials. Cornish firefighters responded at 10:47 p.m. to a fire at 32 Old Pike Road, according to Shannon Moss of the Maine Department of Public Safety. “Upon arrival, fire crews determined that the two-story residence had been occupied by a single individual at the time of the fire,” Moss stated at the time. “It was determined this occupant lived in the front half of the structure. The occupant was able to safely escape without injury.” The back of the residence, which was unfinished, had “significant fire damage,” according to Moss, State Fire Marshal’s Office investigators found the fire suspicious. On May 30, Pather Joyce, 18, of Cornish was arrested and charged with arson and with failing to control or report a dangerous fire, according to a statement from Moss issued May 30. “The investigation revealed that Joyce acted alone,” Moss said. Joyce was transported to York County Jail. Bail was set at $50,000, according to Moss. The investigation continues.
