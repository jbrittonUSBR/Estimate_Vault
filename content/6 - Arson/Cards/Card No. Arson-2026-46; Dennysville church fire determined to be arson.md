(U) A fire at the Dennysville Edmunds Congregational Church in Downeast Maine has been ruled arson by the Maine State Fire Marshal’s Office. Firefighters responded around 8 a.m. on Monday (July 20). The fire started near the back of the church and was extinguished quickly, limiting damage. No injuries were reported.
