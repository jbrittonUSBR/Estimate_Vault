(U//FOUO) We assess HVE and DVE threat actors will very likely continue to use arson and improvised incendiary devices (IIDs) as a tactic through 2026. DVEs from across the ideological spectrum, including white supremacists, nihilistic violent extremists (NVEs), environmental extremists, and HVEs inspired by FTOs, have historically engaged in arson and IID attacks in the US. Propaganda and tactical guidance from DVEs and FTOs regularly advocate for the use of arson and IIDs as a viable tactic due to its relative ease of implementation, low cost, easily acquired materials, difficult attribution, and amount of potential destruction against a variety of accessible targets. Attacks utilizing fire as a weapon often do not require extensive planning, access to traditional weapons, or technical expertise, but have the potential to inflict significant property and economic damage and result in injuries and causalities. Considering the regular use of fire as a weapon by threat actors, the consistent encouragement from DVE and FTO groups to use fire as a weapon, and the relative ease of access to incendiary materials and arson targets, it follows that these tactics will continue to be used in attack planning.
