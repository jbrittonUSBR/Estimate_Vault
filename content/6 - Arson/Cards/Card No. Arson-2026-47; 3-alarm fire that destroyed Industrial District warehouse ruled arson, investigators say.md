(U) A three-alarm fire that destroyed a commercial warehouse in Seattle’s Industrial District (4500 block of 7th Avenue South) on July 15 has been ruled arson by Seattle Fire Department investigators. The fire was reported around 12:55 a.m. and involved two tractor trailers and the warehouse, producing a large column of black smoke. Plastic and metal barrels were present. It was quickly upgraded to three alarms, with 115 personnel responding. The roof collapsed, and the fire was brought under control by about 2:07 a.m. and fully extinguished by 3:22 a.m. Investigators determined the fire was intentionally set and shared their findings with Seattle police. No further details on the cause or suspects were provided in the update.
