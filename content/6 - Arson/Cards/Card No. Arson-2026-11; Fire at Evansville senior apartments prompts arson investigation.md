(U) EVANSVILLE, Ind. (WFIE) - Evansville Police Department officials say they are investigating a fire that took place at a senior living facility on Sunday afternoon. Crews were called to Leisure Living Apartments just after 2 p.m. for reports of a dryer fire in the laundry room. According to a media report, investigators determined the fire didn’t start in the dryer, but in the corner of the room. An arson investigation is underway.
