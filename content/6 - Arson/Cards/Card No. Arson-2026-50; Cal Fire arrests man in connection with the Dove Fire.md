(U) CAL FIRE law enforcement officers arrested 30-year-old Jacob William Hoskins of Sonora, California, in connection with the Dove Fire that broke out in Tuolumne County. The vegetation fire burned over 220 acres near downtown Sonora, prompting road closures and evacuation orders for hundreds of residents. Authorities were initially tipped off by community members who reported seeing a man wearing a distinctive straw hat running from the area where the fire started, with photos and videos quickly circulating on social media. Following a collaborative investigation involving CAL FIRE, the Tuolumne County Sheriff's Office, and the Sonora Police Department, officers located and arrested Hoskins. He was subsequently charged by the district attorney's office with arson and recklessly causing a fire.
