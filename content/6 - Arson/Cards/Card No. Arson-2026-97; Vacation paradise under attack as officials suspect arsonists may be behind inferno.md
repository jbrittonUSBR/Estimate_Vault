# Vacation paradise under attack as officials suspect arsonists may be behind inferno

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/travel/vacation-paradise-attack-officials-suspect-arsonists-behind-inferno)

(U) POSITANO, Italy — A wildfire that broke out in scrubland near Amalfi on the evening of September 14 forced precautionary evacuations of restaurants and tourist facilities along Italy's Amalfi Coast and Sorrento Peninsula, with crews working through the night to keep flames away from homes. Italy's national fire service, Vigili del Fuoco, said seven ground teams and three aircraft responded to fires stretching between Positano, Amalfi, Agerola and Vico Equense, and no injuries or damage to homes were reported. Local leaders warned of severe environmental damage and raised suspicions of arson, with Positano Mayor Gabriella Guida confirming the fire burned the Tordigliano woods and telling the Italian newspaper Il Mattino that simultaneous fires across four towns looked like a plan rather than a coincidence.
