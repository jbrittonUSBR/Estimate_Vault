(U) CORNISH, Maine — According to the fire marshal's office, an 18-year-old was arrested Saturday in connection to a fire in Cornish earlier in the week. Pather Joyce of Cornish was charged with one count of arson and one count of failing to control or report a dangerous fire. Crews responded to 32 Old Pike Road in Cornish on the night of Monday, May 25. Officials released a video Friday asking for help to identify three people. The fire marshal's office now says those three people have been identified, but they are not suspects in this ongoing investigation.
