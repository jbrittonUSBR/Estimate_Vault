# National Guard member expected to recover from Southeast DC shooting

[Source](https://www.militarytimes.com/news/your-military/2026/10/19/national-guard-member-expected-to-recover-from-southeast-dc-shooting)

(U) October 19, 2026 — Military Times reported that a National Guardsman and a suspect suffered non-life-threatening injuries in a shooting in Southeast Washington, D.C. Police said Guard members had exited a van to intervene in a fight when one participant drew a firearm and shot toward them; Guard members returned fire, striking the suspect. Police said the guardsman was wounded in the leg and the suspect in the abdomen, both were hospitalized, and the incident remained under investigation.
