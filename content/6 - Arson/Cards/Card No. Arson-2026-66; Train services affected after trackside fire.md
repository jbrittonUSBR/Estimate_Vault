# Train services affected after trackside fire

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — A trackside fire near Scrubs Lane caused significant disruption to rail services into London, damaging an electrical substation at Kensal Green and affecting Great Western Railway, Elizabeth Line, and Heathrow Express services. Passengers were evacuated from affected trains. British Transport Police arrested a man on suspicion of arson; he was later released on bail pending further enquiries.
