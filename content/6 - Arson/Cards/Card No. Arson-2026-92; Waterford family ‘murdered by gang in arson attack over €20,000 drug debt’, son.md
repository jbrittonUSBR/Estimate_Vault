# Waterford family killed in arson attack over alleged €20,000 drug debt, son says

(U) WATERFORD, Ireland — The Irish Times reported that Philip and Kim English and two of their children died in a suspected petrol-bomb attack on their home, while two other children remained critically ill. Their adult son, John James English, alleged that the family was targeted over a €20,000 drug debt owed by someone else. The newspaper presented the debt and motive as the son's account; investigators had not established them publicly in the report.
