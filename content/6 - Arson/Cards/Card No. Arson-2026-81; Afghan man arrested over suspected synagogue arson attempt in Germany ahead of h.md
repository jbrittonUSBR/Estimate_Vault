# Afghan man arrested over suspected synagogue arson attempt in Germany ahead of holiest Jewish day

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/europe/afghan-man-arrested-over-suspected-synagogue-arson-attempt-in-germany-ahead-of-holiest-jewish-day/articleshow/134366910.cms)

(U) Wuppertal, Germany — A 37-year-old Afghan man was arrested on suspicion of attempting to set fire to the Bergische Synagogue, allegedly hurling flammable liquid at the building and making antisemitic remarks days before Yom Kippur; synagogue members extinguished the flames and no injuries were reported.
