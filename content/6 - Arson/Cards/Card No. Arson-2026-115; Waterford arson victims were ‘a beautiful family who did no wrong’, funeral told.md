(U) October 19, 2026 — The Irish Times reported that hundreds attended the funeral of Philip and Kim English and their sons, who died in a Waterford arson attack. A priest described them as “a beautiful family who did no wrong,” and the article reported tributes from relatives and community members; those descriptions were attributed remarks at the service.
