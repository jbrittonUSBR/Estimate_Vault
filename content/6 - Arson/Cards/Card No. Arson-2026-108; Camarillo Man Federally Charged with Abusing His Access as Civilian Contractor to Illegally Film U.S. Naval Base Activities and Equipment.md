# Camarillo Man Federally Charged with Abusing His Access as Civilian Contractor to Illegally Film U.S. Naval Base Activities and Equipment

[Source](https://www.justice.gov/usao-cdca/pr/camarillo-man-federally-charged-abusing-his-access-civilian-contractor-illegally-film)

(U) CAMARILLO, United States — A civilian contractor was federally charged after allegedly using authorized access to a U.S. Naval Base to illegally film sensitive activities and equipment in violation of federal security restrictions. The case, announced by the U.S. Attorney's Office, underscored ongoing concerns about insider access to military installations.
