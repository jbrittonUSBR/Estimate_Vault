# London Jewish community remains on edge after antisemitic arson wave

[Source](https://www.jpost.com/diaspora/antisemitism/article-908781)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — The London Assembly Fire Committee met on September 15, 2026, to examine the London Fire Brigade's response to a wave of antisemitic arson attacks across the city between March and May, including the burning of four Hatzolah ambulances overnight on March 23. Mark Gardner, chief executive of the Community Security Trust, said the "randomness" of the attacks initially confused police and that the only thing connecting the targets was that they were Jewish or, as the group blamed for the attacks put it, "Zionist." Gardner praised the brigade's "terrific" response but warned that awareness relaxes once attacks pause and that the incidents exposed a broader security crisis for British Jews.
