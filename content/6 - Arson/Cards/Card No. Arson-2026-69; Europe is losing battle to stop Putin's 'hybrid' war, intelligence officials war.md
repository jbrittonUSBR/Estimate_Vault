# Europe is losing battle to stop Putin's 'hybrid' war, intelligence officials warn as Germany opens sabotage probe after another substation fire

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16111147/Europe-losing-battle-stop-Putins-hybrid-war-intelligence-officials-warn-Germany-opens-sabotage-probe-substation-fire.html)

(U) BERLIN, Germany — Intelligence officials warned that Europe was losing the battle to stop Vladimir Putin's growing "hybrid" war, as Germany launched a sabotage investigation following yet another substation fire, the Daily Mail reported on September 7, 2026. Defense officials quoted by the Financial Times said the continent must rush to develop new methods to counter the Kremlin's campaign against NATO, with one source saying "we aren't doing enough to deter sub-Article 5 provocations." The article cited a fire that damaged an electricity substation in Berlin's Moabit area early Monday, which authorities investigated as possible sabotage or a technical fault, alongside a manhunt for a suspected militant climate activist accused of a string of substation attacks in late August and early September. It also noted that Germany had formally blamed Russia for an explosive-laden drone incident at Leipzig Airport the previous month.
