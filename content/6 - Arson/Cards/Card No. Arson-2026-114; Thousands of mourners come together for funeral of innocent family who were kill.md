(U) October 19, 2026 — The Daily Mail reported that mourners gathered in Waterford for the funeral of Philip and Kim English and their sons, who died in a house fire described by authorities as an arson attack. The article quoted mourners’ and clergy’s expressions of grief and described the family as innocent; these were attributed tributes, while the criminal circumstances remained subject to investigation.
