# Two arrested in investigation into fatal Waterford house fire

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16212079/Man-woman-arrested-arson-attack-killed-Irish-couple-two-children.html)

(U) WATERFORD, Ireland — The Daily Mail reported that Irish police arrested a man in his 20s and a woman in her 30s on suspicion of facilitating a criminal organisation while investigating a fatal house fire. The outlet said a petrol bomb attack killed Kim and Philip English and two of their children, while two other children remained critically ill in hospital. Gardaí said the investigation was ongoing; claims about a drug debt and organized-crime involvement were attributed by the newspaper to family members and local reports, not established as fact.
