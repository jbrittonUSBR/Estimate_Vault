# Algerians battle deadly wildfires as Tebboune seeks death penalty for arson

[Source](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/algerians-battle-deadly-wildfires-tebboune-seeks-death-penalty-136165747)

(U) ALGIERS, Algeria — Algerian President Abdelmadjid Tebboune called for the reinstatement of the death penalty for arsonists after a wave of deadly wildfires swept northeastern Algeria, killing at least 12 people and injuring dozens, according to official figures. The blazes followed more than 100 fires during an intense heatwave in northern Africa, and officials said investigations suggested possible links to "foreign parties." Independent journalists and outlets disputed the official toll, with one tally of family death notices and local authority data putting the figure near 200. Capital punishment remains in Algeria's penal code but has not been carried out since 1993.
