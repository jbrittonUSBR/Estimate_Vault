# Funeral held for four family members killed in Waterford arson attack

[Source](https://www.thejournal.ie/funeral-waterford-english-family-arson-attack-7197725-Oct2026/)

(U) Waterford, Ireland — A joint funeral Mass was scheduled for Kim and Philip English and their sons Callum, 12, and Jayden, 9, who died in a suspected arson attack on their home. The family was to be buried at Kilbarry Cemetery; two other children injured in the fire remained critically ill in hospital, according to the report.
