# France reports almost 2,400 arrests after riots, looting and arson during cost-of-living protests

[Source](https://www.dw.com/en/france-reports-almost-2400-arrests-after-riots-looting-and-arson-during-cost-of-living-protests/a-79692322)

(U) October 18, 2026 — Deutsche Welle, citing French authorities and news agencies, reported that almost 2,400 people were arrested nationwide after cost-of-living demonstrations escalated into riots, looting, and arson. Rioters set fire to public buildings and looted shops, and Limoges’ historic town hall was largely destroyed. The article described the unrest as occurring during nationwide protests and attributed the arrest total to official reporting.
