(U) Ryanair condemned an arson attack on seven rented properties in the Fosterstown area of Swords, Dublin, where wheelie bins were set alight and placed against the front doors of homes occupied by its Irish and EU staff in the early hours of June 23, 2026. Fire services quickly extinguished the blazes, limiting damage to burns on doors and walls with no injuries reported, though residents described the incident as frightening—one couple woke to loud window punching and screaming before calling police and sheltering inside until the area was secured. Gardaí are investigating the criminal damage by fire, with Ryanair assisting and describing the perpetrators as “morons” for the senseless attack on hardworking employees’ homes. The airline offered to relocate affected staff but noted some, including one resident who plans to stay with added security cameras, were unnerved but unharmed.
