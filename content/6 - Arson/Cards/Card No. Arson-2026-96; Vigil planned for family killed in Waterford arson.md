# Vigil planned for family killed in Waterford arson

[Source](https://www.rte.ie/news/2026/1014/1595397-waterford-vigil/)

(U) WATERFORD, Ireland — A balloon release ceremony and a church vigil were held in Waterford to commemorate four people who died after an incendiary device set fire to their home in the Birchwood estate, RTÉ reported on October 14, 2026. Hundreds attended the vigil for Kim English, Philip English, and their sons, 12-year-old Callum and nine-year-old Jayden, at the Sacred Heart Church, The Folly. Prayers were said for two other children, aged 14 and six, who remained in a critical condition at CHI Crumlin. Parish priest Fr Gerry Chestnutt told the congregation of around 500 that the family had been taken "in a most horrific and violent way" and urged those responsible to come forward. Family members sang "You'll Never Walk Alone" through tears as hundreds of balloons were released. Books of condolence were opened at the church.
