(U) An early morning vehicle fire parked outside a residential home in Hampstead, Quebec, was deemed a suspected arson attack by local authorities.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Incident:** The fire broke out shortly after 2 a.m. on Belsize Road, near Applewood Crescent. Montreal fire crews quickly extinguished the flames, but the vehicle was completely destroyed.
- **Evidence of Arson:** Montreal police reported that traces of an accelerant were discovered nearby. Additionally, a witness reported seeing a person fleeing the premises shortly after the fire began.
- **Investigation Status:** The investigation remains ongoing by local authorities, and no arrests have been made at this time.
