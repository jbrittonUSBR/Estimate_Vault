# Russia shuts German consulate as drone dispute escalates

[Source](https://www.politico.eu/article/russia-germany-consulate-st-petersburg-drone-dispute/)

(U) BRUSSELS, Belgium — Russia ordered the closure of Germany's consulate in St. Petersburg by September 18 in retaliation amid an escalating drone dispute, POLITICO Europe reported. The move followed a drone incident near Leipzig-Halle and a tit-for-tat exchange that included the closure of the Russian consulate in Bonn. The reciprocal measures marked a further deterioration in Russian-German relations.
