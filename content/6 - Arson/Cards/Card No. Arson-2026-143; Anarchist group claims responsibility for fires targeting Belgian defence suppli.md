# Anarchist group claims responsibility for fires targeting Belgian defence suppliers

[Source](https://www.brusselstimes.com/2422349/anarchist-group-claims-responsibility-for-fires-targeting-belgian-defence-suppliers)

(U) LIÈGE, BELGIUM — A group calling itself the "International Fire Faction Against War" claimed responsibility for three fires targeting defence-industry companies in Liège province, per The Brussels Times. The group said it set fire to a vehicle at Mockel on September 30, five vehicles including three military ones at ReulParts on November 7-8, and a facility at defence technology firm BATS at Sart Tilman on August 31, where prosecutors found three separate points of ignition. Belgian civilian and military intelligence services were examining possible links and the Liège prosecutor opened investigations, while companies disputed some details and it remained unclear whether the fires were deliberately set or the group's claims authentic.
