# Accused in Kochi synagogue arson case sent in NIA custody

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/kochi/accused-in-kochi-synagogue-arson-case-sent-in-nia-custody/articleshow/136112663.cms)

(U) KOCHI, INDIA — An NIA special court in Ernakulam remanded Ebin B. Paul, the prime accused in a synagogue arson case, to five days of NIA custody, according to The Times of India. The NIA alleged Paul set fire to the gate of a shop in front of the old Kadavumbhagam Jewish Synagogue on Telegram instructions, filmed the act, sent the video to handlers and received cryptocurrency payments afterward. Investigators said two seized phones revealed a conspiracy with handlers using aliases including "Vikram Singh" and "SR" to attack Jewish places of worship; Paul's counsel said he was a psychiatric patient who had stopped taking medication in prison.
