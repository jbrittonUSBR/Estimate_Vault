(U) UTICA, N.Y. -- Three people were evacuated from a burning home after a fire broke out on Watson Place. According to the Utica Police Department, officers and Utica Fire units responded at about 3 a.m. on May 31 to a structure fire at a residence on Watson Place. Police officers used fire extinguishers to put out visible flames and helped evacuate three tenants from different apartments before Utica Fire arrived to continue fighting the fire. Investigators reviewed surveillance footage that showed a suspect intentionally setting the fire. Officers recognized the person in the video and identified him as someone in a relationship with a second floor tenant. Later that day, police found Jose Perez, 63, of Utica, on Stark Street and brought him to the Utica Police Department. Perez was charged with arson for setting fire to an occupied home.
