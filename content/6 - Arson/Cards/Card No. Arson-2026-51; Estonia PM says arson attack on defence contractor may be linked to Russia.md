(U) Estonian authorities are investigating potential Russian involvement in a suspected arson attack targeting a building used by **Milrem Robotics**, a prominent defense contractor that manufactures autonomous unmanned ground vehicles supplied to Ukraine.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Incident & Investigation:** Prime Minister Kristen Michal stated that investigators are actively examining whether the arson attack was an act of sabotage orchestrated by Russia. Authorities have identified suspects in the case and requested their arrest. No injuries were reported from the fire.
- **Impact on Operations:** Milrem Robotics Chief Executive Kuldar Väärsi confirmed that the incident **did not disrupt production, supply chains, or delivery schedules**, assuring that military vehicle deliveries to Ukraine will continue as planned for current and upcoming years.
- **Broader Context:** The incident highlights growing security concerns among European nations regarding hybrid warfare. Officials note an uptick in suspicious activities, sabotage, and provocations across Europe tied to backing Ukraine, though Moscow has routinely denied involvement in such cross-border operations.
