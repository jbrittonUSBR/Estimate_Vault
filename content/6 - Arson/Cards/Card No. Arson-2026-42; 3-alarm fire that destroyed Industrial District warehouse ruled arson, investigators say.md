(U) **3-alarm fire that destroyed Industrial District warehouse ruled arson** (KOMO News, July 21, 2026) A three-alarm fire that destroyed a commercial warehouse in Seattle’s Industrial District on July 15 has been ruled arson by Seattle Fire Department investigators. At 12:55 a.m. on July 15, 911 calls reported a fire in a commercial warehouse in the 4500 block of 7th Avenue South. First-arriving engines found the fire involving two tractor trailers and the warehouse, with a large column of black smoke. Large plastic and metal barrels were present inside one trailer and around the building (contents unknown). An AlertSeattle notification advised nearby residents to close windows and doors.

(U) The fire was upgraded to two alarms at 1:06 a.m. and three alarms six minutes later. A total of 115 personnel responded (15 engines, seven ladder trucks, Rescue 1, and support units). The warehouse roof collapsed at 1:15 a.m., making entry unsafe. The fire was brought under control by 2:07 a.m. and declared extinguished at 3:22 a.m. In a July 21 update, investigators determined the fire was intentionally set and shared their findings with Seattle police. No further details on the basis for the arson ruling, suspects, or motive were provided.
