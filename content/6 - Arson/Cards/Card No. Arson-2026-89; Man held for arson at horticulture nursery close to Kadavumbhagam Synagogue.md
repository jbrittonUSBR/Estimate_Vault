# Man held for arson at horticulture nursery close to Kadavumbhagam Synagogue

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/kochi/man-held-for-arson-at-horticulture-nursery-close-to-kadavumbhagam-synagogue/articleshow/134925056.cms)

(U) KOCHI, India — Police arrested a man in connection with an arson attack at a horticulture nursery near the Kadavumbhagam Synagogue in Kerala. The incident raised local security concerns given the proximity to a place of worship, though the motive remained under investigation.
