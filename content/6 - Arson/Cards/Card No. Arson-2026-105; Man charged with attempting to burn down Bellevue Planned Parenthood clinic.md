# Man charged with attempting to burn down Bellevue Planned Parenthood clinic

[Source](https://www.seattletimes.com/seattle-news/law-justice/man-charged-with-arson-at-bellevue-planned-parenthood-clinic/)

(U) BELLEVUE, Wash. — King County prosecutors charged Winston Churchill Corbett, 36, with second-degree arson, alleging he shattered a window at a Planned Parenthood clinic and threw lit pieces of paper inside in the early hours of Sept. 18. The Bellevue Fire Department quickly extinguished the fire, which charred the glass and windowsill but caused minor damage, and the clinic reopened the next day. Court documents said Corbett told police God instructed him to start the fire and that he walked three hours from Seattle to avoid hurting anyone.
