# Tributes paid to 'precious little boy' following death in arson attack

[Source](https://www.thejournal.ie/elliot-english-waterford-death-7218716-Nov2026/)

(U) Waterford, Ireland - The Journal reported that six-year-old Elliot English died three weeks after an arson attack at his family home killed his parents and two brothers; a 14-year-old girl rescued from the fire remained in hospital. Two people had appeared in court charged in connection with the attack, and four others arrested had been released without charge.
