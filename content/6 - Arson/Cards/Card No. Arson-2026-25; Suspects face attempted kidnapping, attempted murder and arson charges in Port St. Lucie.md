(U) PORT ST. LUCIE, Fla. (CBS12) — A man and a woman were arrested on Sunday in connection with two separate, violent domestic disputes that left one woman hospitalized with broken bones and another narrowly escaping an armed kidnapping attempt, police said. The [Port St. Lucie Police Department](https://cbs12.com/topic/Port%20St.%20Lucie%20Police%20Department) noted that the agency's Criminal Investigation Division has assumed control of both active cases, and Chief Leo Niemczyk will provide more information at a news conference at 3 p.m. The first incident occurred just before 11 a.m. on May 31, when officers said they responded to an attempted kidnapping at the First United Methodist Church at 290 SW Prima Vista Blvd. According to the police report, **Jose Tsu Zamora**, 64, of North Miami, ambushed a former long-term girlfriend as she arrived for Sunday services. The victim had previously secured a domestic violence injunction against Zamora in March, and he was already wanted on an active warrant for violating that court order. When the woman exited her vehicle, Zamora blocked her path, grabbed her, held a handgun against her body, and began dragging her toward his vehicle, a dark-colored two-door hatchback, a reporting officer wrote. The abduction failed when the victim threw herself to the ground to prevent being moved, according to police. Two male bystanders, who told police they were armed, intervened and confronted Zamora. Chief Leo Niemczyk with PSLPD credited the two bystanders for saving the woman's life. Had it not been for these courageous citizens who stepped in and intervened, this kidnapping would have been successful. We do believe that the suspect's intent was to harm her,” said Niemczyk. Niemczyk also revealed Zamora told the victim, "If I can't have you, no one can". Zamora already had a court order to stay away from the victim after investigators say the relationship turned violent earlier this year. Zamora released the woman and fled the scene in the hatchback. The victim declined medical treatment at the scene but reported physical pain from the struggle, according to the affidavit. Police tracked down Zamora later that evening and booked him into the St. Lucie County Jail. He faces charges of kidnapping/false imprisonment and violation of an injunction, according to the news release. Authorities revealed Zamora has a long criminal history in St. Lucie County and around the state, with previous attempted kidnapping and witness retaliation arrests in Miami-Dade County.

Now the police are looking at the evidence. Niemczyk says a pellet gun turned over by the family could potentially be the weapon used in the attempted kidnapping.

“His family did turn over a gun to us. It's a pellet gun. We're trying to verify whether or not that is the actual weapon. It might make sense because he did have an injunction. He couldn't have bought a firearm, and he's a convicted felon,” Niemczyk said.

## Arson and Assault

Hours later, at approximately 9 p.m., officers said they responded to a residential emergency after a homeowner reported that her daughter was setting the house on fire and attacking her.

Arriving officers observed heavy smoke billowing from the residence and encountered **Michelle Sylvia Jastram Picard**, 26, standing outside, authorities said.
