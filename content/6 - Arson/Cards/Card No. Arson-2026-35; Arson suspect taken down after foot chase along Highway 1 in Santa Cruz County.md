(U) Santa Cruz police arrested an arson suspect after a foot chase across Highway 1 in the DeLaveaga area on June 26, 2026, following a reported fire around 1:45 p.m. The unidentified male suspect fled when officers attempted to contact him, leading to a pursuit through brush alongside the highway captured on video. Three officers chased the man; one caught up and pushed him to the ground, after which all three detained him. Police stated the investigation is ongoing and provided no further details on the suspect’s identity, motive, or the extent of the fire at the time of the report.
