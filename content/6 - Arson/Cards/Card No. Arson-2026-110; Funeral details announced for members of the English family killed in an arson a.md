# Funeral details announced for members of the English family killed in an arson attack

[Source](https://www.thejournal.ie/english-family-funeral-waterford-7195098-Oct2026/)

(U) Waterford — The English family announced funeral arrangements for Philip and Kim English and their sons Jayden and Callum, who were killed in an arson attack on their Waterford home. A funeral Mass was scheduled at Sacred Heart Church, followed by burial at Kilbarry Cemetery; two other children injured in the attack remained in critical condition. Gardaí had arrested a man and a woman in connection with the investigation.
