(U) The suspect accused of sparking the catastrophic Old Trails Fire in Spokane County, Washington—which destroyed hundreds of homes and burned thousands of acres—was hit with four additional arson charges after confessing to a string of other fires.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **Additional Charges:** Aaron Farinacci, 37, faced four new counts of first-degree arson following admissions made during a recorded interview with detectives after his initial arrest.
- **Expanded Confession:** Prosecutors stated that Farinacci confessed to setting roughly 25 fires in the area since the previous summer (including past blazes like the Aubrey White, TJ Meenach, Fort George, and Indian Bluff fires). Detectives corroborated evidence for at least four of those additional fires.
- **Pre-Planning & Motive:** Court documents revealed that Farinacci admitted to planning the Old Trails Fire weeks in advance. He reportedly used a weather app to pick a day with high heat, low humidity, and high winds, and used a homemade time-delay device (incorporating items like a cigarette and matches) to ignite the blaze.
- **Court Action & Bond:** Following the new charges, a judge doubled his bond to $2 million, and his defense requested a mental competency evaluation before proceeding with a plea. Meanwhile, firefighters on the ground continued working to gain containment on the region's active blazes.
