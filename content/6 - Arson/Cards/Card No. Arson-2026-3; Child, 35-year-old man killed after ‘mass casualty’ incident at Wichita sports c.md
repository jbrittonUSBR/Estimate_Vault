# Child, 35-year-old man killed after ‘mass casualty’ incident at Wichita sports complex have been identified

[Source](https://www.kctv5.com/2026/10/17/child-adult-dead-after-mass-casualty-incident-wichita-sports-complex/)

(U) Wichita, Kansas — A vehicle drove through the Stryker Sports Complex during a youth sports event, killing a six-year-old child and a 35-year-old man, police said. Authorities identified both victims as residents of McPherson and said they were not related; the incident prompted evacuation of the facility and suspension of nearby games.
