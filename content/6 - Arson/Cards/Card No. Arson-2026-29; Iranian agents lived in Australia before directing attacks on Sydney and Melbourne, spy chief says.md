(U) Australia’s spy chief revealed that Iranian Revolutionary Guard agents with ties to the country directed arson attacks on Jewish targets in Sydney and Melbourne in 2024, including the firebombing of a kosher restaurant and a synagogue. An Australian citizen operating from Iran orchestrated the Sydney attack on Lewis’ Continental Kitchen, while a former Australian resident based in Iraq, recruited through militia connections, directed the Melbourne attack on the Adass Israel Synagogue. ASIO Director-General Mike Burgess stated that Iran continues to view Australia as a legitimate target for covert violence, noting the incidents followed Israel’s war against Hamas and contributed to a broader wave of antisemitic crimes. Australia expelled Iran’s ambassador after attributing the attacks to Tehran, which denies involvement, while several individuals have been charged and an investigation described as one of the most complex in recent years continues.
