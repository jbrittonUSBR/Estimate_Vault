# Wider Europe Briefing: Can The EU Put Russia's GRU On Its Terror List?

[Source](https://www.rferl.org/a/wider-europe-briefing-can-the-eu-put-russia-s-gru-on-its-terror-list/33885122.html)

(U) European Union — RFE/RL’s Rikard Jozwiak said the EU was at an early stage of examining whether Russia’s military intelligence agency, the GRU, could be added to the bloc’s terrorist list. He linked the discussion to a series of hybrid attacks in EU states, many described as directly or indirectly connected to the GRU, and also reviewed further EU sanctions on Moscow.
