(U) **Brooklyn man charged with arson after school bus set on fire in Times Square** (FOX 5 New York, July 21, 2026) Jonathan Strahs, 30, of Brooklyn, faces a federal arson charge for allegedly setting an empty school bus on fire in Times Square. According to authorities, the incident occurred around 2:45 a.m. on June 14, hours after the New York Knicks won the NBA championship (their first title since 1973). The bus, which had been used to shuttle fans to a World Cup match at MetLife Stadium, was parked on West 42nd Street between Eighth and Ninth Avenues amid street celebrations. Surveillance video allegedly captured Strahs entering and exiting the bus shortly before it went up in flames. The fire fully engulfed the bus and spread to another vehicle before the FDNY extinguished it.

(U) Strahs was arrested early Monday morning at his home after a joint investigation by the FDNY, NYPD, and Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives (ATF). FDNY Commissioner Lillian Bonsignore stated that the fire put bystanders and first responders at risk and praised the multi-agency investigation. He was charged with one count of federal arson.
