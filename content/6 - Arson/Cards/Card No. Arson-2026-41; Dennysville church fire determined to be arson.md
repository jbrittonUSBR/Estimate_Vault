(U) **Dennysville church fire determined to be arson** (WGME, July 21, 2026) A fire at the Dennysville Edmunds Congregational Church in Downeast Maine has been ruled an arson by the Maine State Fire Marshal’s Office. Fire crews responded around 8 a.m. Monday. The fire began near the back of the church. Firefighters extinguished it quickly, limiting damage to the building. No injuries were reported. The article notes that investigators are looking into the cause, but provides no further details on suspects, motive, or the extent of the damage beyond it being limited.
