(U) Former NFL running back Javian Hawkins (who won a Super Bowl with the Los Angeles Rams) was arrested in Florida for allegedly setting fire to property belonging to his former employer, Tropic Care of Florida (a landscaping company).
Police say that on the morning of July 15, Hawkins unlawfully entered a secured property, took gasoline from a work vehicle, and set three company pickup trucks on fire. The flames spread to the outside of the building before being extinguished. He had recently been fired by the company. He was identified via surveillance footage, witnesses, and physical evidence, and was charged with armed burglary, arson of a structure/business, three counts of arson, and felony criminal mischief. He was arrested on July 16.
