(U) KOCHI, India — The Times of India reported that police formed a special investigation team to examine suspected extremist connections and possible other criminal activity involving Eldho Paul, who had been arrested over an alleged arson attack. Police said the fire was set around 2:30 a.m. Monday at boards outside the historic Kadavumbhagam Synagogue near Ernakulam Market. The alleged extremist links remained under investigation and were not established findings.
