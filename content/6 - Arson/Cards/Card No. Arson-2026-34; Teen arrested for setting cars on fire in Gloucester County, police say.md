(U) A 19-year-old was arrested after two cars were set on fire in Washington Township, [Gloucester County](https://www.nbcphiladelphia.com/tag/gloucester-county), on Saturday night, police said. The investigation began at around 9:42 p.m. on Saturday, June 27, after officers for the Washington Township Police Department responded to a report of a car that was intentionally set on fire on Tara Drive, police said. The victim had seen the incident on his surveillance camera, and has shared the video with NBC10. In it, a man can be seen smashing the rear window of the car with a hatchet and then lighting a fire inside the car. As officers investigated the first incident, a second car fire was reported in the area of Harrogate Court and Tenby Drive, police said. After police searched the area, they arrested Aiden Logrande, 19, of Washington Township, who they said matched the description of the suspect. Logrande faces arson and criminal mischief charges for the two car fires, and police believe he may also be linked to other broken windshield incidents in the area.
