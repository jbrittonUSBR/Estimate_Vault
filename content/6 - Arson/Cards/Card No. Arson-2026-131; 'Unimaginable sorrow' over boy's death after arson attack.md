# 'Unimaginable sorrow' over boy's death after arson attack

[Source](https://www.rte.ie/news/ireland/2026/1107/1598351-wateford-arson-attack/)

(U) Waterford, Ireland — RTÉ reported that six-year-old Elliot English died in hospital from injuries suffered in an arson attack at his family home the previous month. The fire had killed his parents and two brothers; Elliot and a 14-year-old girl were rescued, and the girl remained in hospital. Two people had appeared in court charged in connection with the attack, while four others had been arrested and released without charge.
