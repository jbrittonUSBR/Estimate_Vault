# Moscow likely behind drone-factory arson, says Poland’s Tusk

[Source](https://www.politico.eu/article/polands-donald-tusk-blames-moscow-over-a-drone-factory-fire/)

(U) WARSAW, Poland — Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk said at a cabinet meeting Wednesday that a fire at a Polish drone factory supplying weapons to Ukraine was "deliberate arson" and that Russia was likely behind the attack. The fire broke out Monday morning at WB Electronics, one of Poland's largest private defense companies, which produces reconnaissance drones and loitering munitions including the FlyEye and Warmate used by Ukraine; it was soon extinguished with no casualties. Polish prosecutors classified the incident as a "terrorist crime" committed by "foreign intelligence," and Tusk described it as "a continuation of Russia's escalatory actions throughout the region." The incident came amid an intensification of suspected Russian attacks on facilities linked to military production supporting Ukraine, including a separate fire at a JFG Composites facility in Lublin.
