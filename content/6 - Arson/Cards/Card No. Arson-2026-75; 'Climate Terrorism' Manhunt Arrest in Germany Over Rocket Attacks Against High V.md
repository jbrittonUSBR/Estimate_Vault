# 'Climate Terrorism': Manhunt Arrest in Germany Over Rocket Attacks Against High Voltage Power Lines

[Source](https://www.breitbart.com/europe/2026/09/09/hold-hold-germany-energy-sabotage/)

(U) BERLIN, Germany — Police arrested a suspect in a spate of attacks on high-voltage power infrastructure across three German states, in which homemade rockets were reportedly used to sling lightweight wire over transmission lines and cause short circuits. More than 20 rockets were fired at the Turnow-Preilack substation near the Jänschwalde plant in Brandenburg, causing two short circuits, and explosive devices were found during a raid on the suspect's home; handwritten letters sent to German newspapers citing the fight against fossil fuels claimed responsibility for the sabotage. German Federal Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt characterized the attacks as "climate terrorism," while adding that a possible Russian false-flag operation could not be ruled out.
