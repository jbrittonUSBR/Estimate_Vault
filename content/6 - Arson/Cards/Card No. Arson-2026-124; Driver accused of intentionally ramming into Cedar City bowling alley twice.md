# Driver accused of intentionally ramming into Cedar City bowling alley twice

[Source](https://kutv.com/news/local/driver-accused-of-intentionally-ramming-into-cedar-city-bowling-alley-twice)

(U) Cedar City, Utah — Police said a woman drove into the exterior wall of Cedar Bowling Center, reversed, and struck the building a second time late Friday, October 16. Investigators described the crashes as intentional; the driver was arrested and booked while police investigated reckless driving and criminal mischief, with damage estimated at up to $130,000.
