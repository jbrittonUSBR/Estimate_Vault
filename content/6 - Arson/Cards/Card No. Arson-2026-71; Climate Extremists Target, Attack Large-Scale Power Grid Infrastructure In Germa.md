# Climate Extremists Target, Attack Large-Scale Power Grid Infrastructure In Germany!

[Source](https://wattsupwiththat.com/2026/09/06/climate-extremists-target-attack-large-scale-power-grid-infrastructure-in-germany/)

(U) BERLIN, Germany — A blog post published September 6, 2026 reported that German authorities suspected climate extremists were behind a wave of coordinated attacks on major power grid infrastructure. According to the report, Federal Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt characterized the attacks as acts of "climate extremism," citing confession letters sent by a 48-year-old suspect who claimed responsibility to protest fossil fuel use. It described attacks on a substation at Turnow-Preilack near Jänschwalde in Brandenburg on September 1, a substation in Bergheim, North Rhine-Westphalia that same day, and an attempted attack at a Dormagen substation on September 3. The post also referenced a January 2026 arson attack on electricity infrastructure in southwest Berlin that cut power and heating to roughly 45,000 to 50,000 households, for which a group calling itself Vulkangruppe claimed responsibility. This item was an opinion-oriented blog post citing state claims and commentary, and its characterizations were not independently verified.
