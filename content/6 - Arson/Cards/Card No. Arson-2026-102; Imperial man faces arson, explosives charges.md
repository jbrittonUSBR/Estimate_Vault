# Imperial man faces arson, explosives charges

[Source](https://www.knopnews2.com/video/2026/09/23/imperial-man-faces-arson-explosives-charges/)

(U) IMPERIAL, Neb. — Logan White, 34, was arraigned in Chase County Court on charges of first-degree arson, possession of a destructive device and unlawful possession of explosives after investigators said he was injured by a homemade explosive inside his home. Police began investigating after a teenager reported that White had been severely hurt by an explosive device; a search of the house found a large hole in the living room floor, burn marks, explosive materials and a cylinder device, and White was arrested Sept. 14. He was held in the Red Willow County Jail on a $100,000 bond.
