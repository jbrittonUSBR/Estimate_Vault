# Spokane has an arson problem

[Source](https://www.spokesman.com/stories/2026/sep/20/spokane-has-an-arson-problem/)

(U) SPOKANE, United States — A Spokesman-Review report published on September 20, 2026, detailed a persistent arson problem in Spokane, Washington, reporting that since the start of 2025, 384 brush fires had been reported within city limits and the fire department had determined 87 fires were intentionally set. Officials said the problem appeared worse than in many other Northwest cities, citing 464 total outdoor fires in Spokane versus 117 in Yakima, 151 in Boise, 138 in Bend, and 197 in Billings. The article linked many fires to the homeless population and to drug use and mental health problems, and noted several arson arrests following the August 1 Spokane Complex fires, which destroyed more than 800 homes and caused over $1 billion in damage. It also reported that one suspect, Aaron F. Farinacci, faces state and federal arson charges and that prosecutors want state arson statutes strengthened.
