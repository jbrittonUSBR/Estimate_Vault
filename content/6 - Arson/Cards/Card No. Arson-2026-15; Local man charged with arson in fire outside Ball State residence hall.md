(U) MUNCIE, IN — A local man has been accused of setting a fire outside a Ball State University residence hall. Steven Skyler Wolfe, 24 — listed in court documents both at a Yorktown address and as being homeless — was charged on Friday, May 29, with arson, a Level 4 felony carrying up to 12 years in prison. According to Ball State police, Wolfe on May 2 was outside DeHority Complex, on the BSU campus, when he used a candle to ignite a mattress pad he had removed from a trash container.
