# 1 student dead, another hurt in shooting on Atlanta Technical College campus

[Source](https://www.wsbtv.com/news/local/1-student-hurt-another-dead-shooting-atlanta-technical-college/Z3VVXRLWH5HKBFIRULGH3UHAT4/)

(U) October 19, 2026 — WSB-TV reported that Atlanta police responded to a shooting at Atlanta Technical College after a firearm discharged in a classroom, injuring one student. Police said the student carrying the gun then left the classroom and was later found dead in a bathroom from an apparent self-inflicted gunshot wound; the injured student was taken to a hospital. The report attributed the account to police, and the circumstances remained under investigation.
