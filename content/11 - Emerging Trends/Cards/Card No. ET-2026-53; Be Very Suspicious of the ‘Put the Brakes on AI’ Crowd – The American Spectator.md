# Be Very Suspicious of the ‘Put the Brakes on AI’ Crowd

[Source](https://spectator.org/be-very-suspicious-of-the-put-the-brakes-on-ai-crowd/)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — In an American Spectator opinion column, Scott McKay argued that doomsday warnings from major artificial intelligence companies come packaged with a convenient regulatory solution that would entrench incumbents and amount to regulatory capture. McKay characterised the push to "put the brakes on AI" as self-interested positioning by Big Tech rather than a neutral safety appeal. The column was opinion commentary rather than reported news.
