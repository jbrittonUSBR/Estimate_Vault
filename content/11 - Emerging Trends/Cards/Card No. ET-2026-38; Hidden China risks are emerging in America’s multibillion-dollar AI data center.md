# Hidden China risks are emerging in America’s multibillion-dollar AI data center boom

[Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/03/us-ai-data-centers-china-supply-chain.html)

(U) NEW YORK — Chinese firms supply significant portions of key equipment used in US data centers, including transformers, switchgear, batteries, and optical transceivers, raising supply-chain and national-security concerns as Washington and Beijing compete for AI supremacy. President Donald Trump signed an executive order declaring a national emergency over the "extraordinary foreign threat" involving bulk-power system equipment produced abroad, authorizing the Energy Department to prohibit or impose conditions on certain transactions involving grid and data-center components. Analysts said US and Western suppliers may struggle to quickly replace Chinese manufacturing capacity, potentially raising costs or worsening shortages for the AI buildout.
