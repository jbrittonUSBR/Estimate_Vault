# Chinese hackers are running AI on stolen networks to avoid detection, Google says

[Source](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/security/chinese-hackers-are-running-ai-stolen-networks-avoid-detection-google-rcna596155)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — Google said hackers working for Chinese intelligence were increasingly targeting American AI research and using AI in their operations, according to its quarterly Threat Intelligence Group report released Tuesday, September 8, 2026. The report said several hacker groups, including intelligence services and cybercrime gangs, had moved from basic AI prompting to using AI agents that automate wide swaths of an intrusion, in some cases allowing an entire campaign to be conducted in under six hours. One Chinese group tracked since 2023 had focused on academic, medical and military research organizations in North America and specifically targeted proprietary AI research, and had compromised unrelated victims' cloud networks to install open-source AI models; the Chinese Embassy in Washington broadly denied the claims, while Google did not name any victims.
