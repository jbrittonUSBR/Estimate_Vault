(CUI) As of 21 May 2026, “Swat Bunnies” (SWTB) A, a decentralized online Nihilistic Violent Extremist group, reportedly plans to “swat” unspecified military installations on 22-23 May 2026. SWTB, along with associated COM affiliate responsible for recent swatting events against businesses and schools in Florida, Idaho, South Carolina, South Dakota, Texas, Virginia, and Washington.
(U) Swatting is a criminal harassment tactic where a person makes a false report to emergency services (such as a 911 operator) in order to trigger a high-intensity police response—often involving a SWAT (Special Weapons and Tactics) team—to a target's location. The objective is typically to harass, intimidate, or endanger the victim.
(CUI) NCIS assesses a roughly even chance that SWTB would target military installations due to the group’s past efforts to target schools and businesses. NCIS makes this assessment with HIGH confidence based on recent reporting of targeted attacks known to be associated with the group.8,9 DoW school systems have recently been targeted with swatting attacks, but it was unconfirmed if tied to SWTB or other COM-affiliated groups.10 Current repositories do not reveal significant information regarding SWTB and lack additional reporting indicating timing and location of their planned activities. NCIS continues to partner with federal and local law enforcement to monitor this threat stream.
