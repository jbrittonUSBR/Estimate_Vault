(U) A ThreatDown report warns that artificial intelligence is fundamentally reshaping cybercrime by lowering the barrier to sophisticated attacks and enabling more autonomous operations. Key findings include:

- Criminals are using both jailbroken frontier models and thousands of openly available guardrail-free models (over 6,600 on Hugging Face with millions of downloads) to scale attacks.
- AI is being integrated into malware that can generate malicious logic dynamically or run locally on compromised systems, reducing detection.
- Malicious AI “agent skills” on public marketplaces can steal credentials, install malware, or persist by altering an agent’s memory.
- “Shadow AI” (unauthorized employee use of generative AI tools) is expanding attack surfaces; one in five organizations reported a related breach in 2025.
- AI could dramatically accelerate zero-day discovery; researchers cite early criminal use of AI for exploit development and predict similar capabilities may appear on criminal markets within 6–12 months.

(U) The report urges organizations to accelerate vulnerability management and patching, maintain continuous monitoring, and actively discover and govern shadow AI tools before these capabilities become mainstream.
