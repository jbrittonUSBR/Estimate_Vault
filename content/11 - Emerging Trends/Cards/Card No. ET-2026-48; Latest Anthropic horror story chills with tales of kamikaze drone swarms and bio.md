# Latest Anthropic horror story chills with tales of kamikaze drone swarms and bioweapons research

[Source](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/09/10/latest-anthropic-horror-story-chills-with-tales-of-kamikaze-drone-swarms-and-bioweapons-research/5295702)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — Anthropic's September 10 threat intelligence report described seven "harm areas" in which criminals and state-linked actors misused Claude Haiku, Sonnet, and Opus between December 2025 and August 2026, including a Russian state-nexus espionage group tracked as GTG-20006, consistent with the SVR, that used AI-driven workflows to automate much of its operations against more than 20 embassies, think tanks, defense-industrial firms, and government agencies across Ukraine, Europe, the Middle East, Asia, and North Africa. A ShinyHunters affiliate used AI agents to conduct a session-store dump of more than 2,100 Azure AD token sets spanning over 40 corporate tenants in about 34 hours after breaching a software-as-a-service provider, and a likely Russia-based freelance team attempted to build a full-stack autonomous first-person-view kamikaze drone swarm, with Claude writing and testing the drones' core software. The report also documented five cases of users in unsupported regions seeking biological weapons-related research, plus six conventional weapons cases — three in China, two in Russia, and one in Yemen — and Anthropic said it banned the associated accounts and shared threat information with partners.
