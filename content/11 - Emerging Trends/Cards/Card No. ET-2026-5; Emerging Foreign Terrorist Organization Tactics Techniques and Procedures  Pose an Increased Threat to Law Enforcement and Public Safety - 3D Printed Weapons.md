Pressure bearing parts, which contain the explosion of a shot, are less commonly printed due to 3D printing materials typically softening under heat and cracking after repeated trauma. These pressure bearing parts are usually the barrel, chamber, and smaller firing components. The most commonly printed 3D firearm components are those that house components and are non-pressure bearing, such as the lower receivers, grips and magazine shells. Accessories and attachments which make a shooter more efficient at hitting targets are also very commonly printed. For example, handguards are a popular printed attachment and functions to protect a shooter's hand from the heat of a barrel after rapid, consecutive shots while also giving additional stability. Another common attachment used for handguns are auto sears (switches) which can convert a semi-automatic handgun to a fully automatic machine gun.
