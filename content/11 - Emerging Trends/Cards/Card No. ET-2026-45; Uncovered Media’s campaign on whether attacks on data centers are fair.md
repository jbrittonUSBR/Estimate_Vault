# Uncovered: Media’s campaign on whether attacks on data centers are fair

[Source](https://www.wnd.com/2026/09/uncovered-medias-campaign-whether-attacks-data-centers-are/)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — In an opinion piece republished by WorldNetDaily on September 5, 2026, RealClearWire columnist Gary Abernathy argued that data centers have become a central target of political attack ads in the 2026 US midterm elections and that outlets across the ideological spectrum had pushed back on what he called unfair criticism. Abernathy cited a New York Times magazine article, a Washington Post editorial and a National Review column that, he said, acknowledged data centers' benefits, including driving grid improvements, generating local tax revenue and creating skilled-trade jobs. He pointed to Virginia's Loudoun County, where taxes on data-center equipment were expected to bring in about $1.3 billion the next year, roughly 40 percent of county revenue. He argued that a crucial solution to US energy challenges should not fall prey to misleading attack ads, framing AI leadership as a strategic competition with China.
