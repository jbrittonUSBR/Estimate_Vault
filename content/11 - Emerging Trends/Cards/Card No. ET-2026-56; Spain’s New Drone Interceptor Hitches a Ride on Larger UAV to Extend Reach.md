# Spain’s New Drone Interceptor Hitches a Ride on Larger UAV to Extend Reach

[Source](https://thedefensepost.com/2026/09/22/spain-drone-interceptor-uav/)

(U) SPAIN — Spanish defense firm Indra unveiled "DRIZZLE," an autonomous drone interceptor designed to hitch a ride aboard a larger mothership UAV to extend its operational reach. The compact, lightweight system can exceed 400 km/h and is intended for counter-drone missions protecting critical infrastructure, military convoys, and personnel, with launch from a mothership allowing coverage of larger areas.
