# Security firm linked AI tool Artex to cyberattacks on Japanese companies

[Source](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/east-asia/china-ai-tool-artex-japan-cyberattack-6474611)

(U) Tokyo, Japan — GMO Cybersecurity by Ierae said it found evidence that Artex, a Chinese-developed AI security-testing tool, was used in recent cyberattacks targeting Japanese companies. The firm said it confirmed the tool's use through forensic logs; the report did not establish that the tool's developer or the Chinese state directed the attacks.
