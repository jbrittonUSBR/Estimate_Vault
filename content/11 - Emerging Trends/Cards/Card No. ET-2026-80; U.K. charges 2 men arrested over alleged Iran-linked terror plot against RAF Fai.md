# U.K. charges 2 men arrested over alleged Iran-linked terror plot against RAF Fairford air base used by U.S.

[Source](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/uk-raf-fairford-terror-plot-iran-2-men-charged-security-offenses/)

(U) LONDON, U.K. — Two men were charged under the U.K. National Security Act over alleged Iran-linked activity targeting RAF Fairford, the base used by U.S. forces to launch strikes on Iran; police said six others remained on bail, and Iranian officials denied involvement.
