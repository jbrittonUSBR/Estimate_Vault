# Anthropic Reveals How It Stopped ‘Kamikaze Drone’ Swarms, Biological Weapons And More

[Source](https://dailycaller.com/2026/09/10/anthropic-report-kamikaze-drone-swarms-biological-weapons)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — Anthropic published its latest threat intelligence report on September 10 describing how it disrupted misuse of its Claude models, including accounts linked to Chinese state security organs that used the AI to support "stability maintenance," the Chinese Communist Party's term for suppressing dissent, and a system attributed to a municipal cyber police unit that identified 10 Chinese citizens as surveillance targets, among them Hong Kong pro-democracy figures, organizers of Tiananmen Square commemorations, and Uyghur advocates. The report described GTG-20006, an actor whose actions and targeting are consistent with Russian state-nexus espionage, as targeting government ministries, defense and intelligence groups, embassies, think tanks, and defense industrial companies, and said a likely Russia-based freelance actor sought to build a "kamikaze drone swarm" using Claude Code, with possible ties to the Russian Academy of Sciences rather than a Russian state entity. Anthropic also accused several Chinese labs, including Alibaba, Moonshot AI, DeepSeek, Z.ai, Xiami, and MiniMax, of distillation, and the Chinese Embassy in Washington rejected the allegations as attempts to "smear China," according to the Daily Caller News Foundation report.
