# A Utah rancher learned of a 3-million-square-foot data center across from his home when a gas company sought a pipeline easement; now he is appealing its permit

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/us/a-utah-rancher-learned-of-a-3-million-square-foot-data-center-across-from-his-home-when-a-gas-company-sought-a-pipeline-easement-now-he-is-appealing-its-permit/articleshow/135566025.cms)

(U) Mona, Utah, USA — The Times of India reported that rancher Taylor Flake learned of a proposed data-center campus across from his home when a gas company sought a pipeline easement through his property. Flake and the Mona Cares group appealed Juab County’s permit, arguing that neighbors received inadequate notice and requesting a new review; residents also raised concerns about traffic, noise, light, and pollution.
