# PaperCut Attacker Uses Hundreds of AI Agents to Compromise 440+ Instances

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/papercut-attacker-uses-hundreds-of-ai.html)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — A suspected Russian-speaking cyber actor used artificial intelligence to devise exploits targeting two recently disclosed security flaws in PaperCut NG/MF, compromising hundreds of instances, according to reports from Blackpoint Cyber and GreyNoise cited by The Hacker News on September 10, 2026. The activity was attributed to the IP address 45.142.193[.]132 and exploited CVE-2026-81578 and CVE-2026-82078, a chain of authentication bypass and remote code execution, mainly targeting the education sector in the US, UK, France, Spain, Canada, Belgium, Portugal, Australia, Germany and Switzerland. GreyNoise said the adversary, after building exploits in a self-hosted lab, unleashed hundreds of AI agents powered by OpenAI Codex, a DeepSeek model and public offensive-security tools to compromise no fewer than 440 instances of PaperCut MF/NG hosted by 395 identified victim organizations in 48 countries. The firm reported the attacker went from an empty workspace to first achieving remote code execution against a real victim in under four hours and compromised at least 11 organizations in 26 seconds; the actor's end goals remained unclear.
