(U) House Republican Leadership Chairwoman Elise Stefanik led a House Intelligence Committee hearing examining new and emerging threat streams since the September 11 attacks. The hearing is part of a year-long bipartisan review of the 9/11 Commission Report recommendations ahead of the 25th anniversary of the attacks. In her opening statement, Stefanik highlighted the “failure of imagination” that contributed to the 9/11 attacks and stressed the need to avoid a similar shortfall today. She listed current challenges that are broader and more complex than those of 2001, including:

- Great power competition
- Artificial intelligence
- Foreign malign influence campaigns
- Drones
- Hostile use of emerging biotechnologies
- Vulnerabilities in critical infrastructure

(U) She called for the intelligence community and law enforcement to think creatively, act proactively, and take preemptive steps against these threats.
