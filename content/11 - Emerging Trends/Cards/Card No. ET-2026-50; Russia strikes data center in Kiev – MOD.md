# Russia strikes data center in Kiev – MOD

[Source](https://www.rt.com/russia/645430-russian-strikes-kiev-datacenter/)

(U) MOSCOW, Russia — Russia's Defense Ministry said its forces struck a commercial data center in Kiev and infrastructure in the port of Odessa during an overnight barrage on September 10-11, 2026. The ministry identified the 4,500-square-meter Be Mobile facility in the Ukrainian capital as one of a string of military-linked industrial, logistics, and port targets hit with precision weapons and attack drones. It also reported strikes on a drone-component storage site near Gostomel, two logistics hubs in the Khmelnitsky and Dnepropetrovsk regions, and a repair shop, storage facility, cargo ships, and fuel depots in Odessa region. RT is a Russian state outlet and the claims could not be independently verified.
