# Whole-of-state defense: How SLED agencies can secure critical infrastructure in the AI era

[Source](https://www.tenable.com/blog/whole-of-state-security-sled-critical-infrastructure)

(U) United States — Tenable argued that AI adoption by state, local and education agencies and AI-assisted attacker reconnaissance were expanding the cyberattack surface for critical infrastructure. Citing NASCIO research, the company said nearly 90% of state CIOs rated attacks on critical systems a high concern and advocated a whole-of-state model with shared visibility, services and governance.
