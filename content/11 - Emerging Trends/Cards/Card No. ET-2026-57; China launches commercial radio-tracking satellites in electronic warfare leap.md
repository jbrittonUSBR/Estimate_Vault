# China launches commercial radio-tracking satellites in electronic warfare leap

[Source](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/science/article/3368432/china-launches-its-first-commercial-radio-tracking-satellites-electronic-warfare-leap)

(U) JIUQUAN, China — China launched its first commercial trio of radio-tracking satellites, Diting-01, aboard a Lijian-1 Y18 rocket from Jiuquan Satellite Launch Centre. Named after a mythical all-hearing beast, the satellites are designed to passively detect and track radio emissions from enemy warships, hidden radars, and electronic jammers from orbit, marking a commercial leap in space-based electronic warfare capability.
