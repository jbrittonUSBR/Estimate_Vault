# The Ukraine Laboratory: How a Full-Spectrum War Is Rewriting Modern Warfighting

[Source](https://smallwarsjournal.com/2026/09/23/ukraine-laboratory-of-modern-war/)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — An analysis published by Small Wars Journal argues that the Russia-Ukraine war has become the defining laboratory for 21st-century warfare, compressing the full spectrum of modern combat into one theater. The essay identifies six vectors of transformation: the transparent battlefield enabled by ubiquitous surveillance, the drone revolution across every scale, the missile/air-defense contest, the electromagnetic and cyber struggle, AI-assisted decision cycles, and industrial attrition. It concludes that while the fundamental logic of war is unchanged, its character has shifted—favoring forces that can disperse, adapt quickly, and integrate uncrewed and electronic warfare capabilities at every echelon.
