# China Is Lapping the US in Race To Develop Weather-Changing Technology That Would Be a Powerful Weapon, American Researchers Warn

[Source](https://freebeacon.com/china/china-is-lapping-the-us-in-race-to-develop-weather-changing-technology-that-would-be-a-powerful-weapon-american-researchers-warn/)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — American researchers warned that China is outpacing the United States in developing weather-modification technology, including solar radiation management, that could be weaponised to inflict ecological harm, according to a Washington Free Beacon report. The article, published by the conservative outlet, said the Chinese military is investing heavily in technologies capable of controlling the weather. It characterised such capabilities as a potential strategic threat to the United States.
