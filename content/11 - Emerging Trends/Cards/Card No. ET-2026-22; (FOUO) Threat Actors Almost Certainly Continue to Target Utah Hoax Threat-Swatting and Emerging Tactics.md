(U//FOUO) The SIAC assesses threat actors, including Nihilistic Violent Extremists (NYE), will almost
certainly continue to target Utah institutions with hoax threats and swatting to gain notoriety for causing fear and chaos in the community. Additionally, it's likely their tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) will continue to evolve to obfuscate their identity, challenging law enforcement's traditional investigative methods. However, law enforcement is adapting by uti lizing technical investigative resources throughout the state.

**(U) Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures (TTPs)

(U) Voice over Internet Protocol (VoIP) Phone Numbers

(U//FOUO) Telephoned swatting attempts may be recognized because they' re routed through non-emergency, administrative phone lines via Voice over Internet Protocol (VoIP) numbers, because the Vo[P numbers cannot place calls to a local emergency dispatch district for a 9-1-1 call. Additionally, it's likely the only call received by dispatchers reporting an urgent threat, where in a typical active shooter or mass violence incident, dispatchers receive multiple calls from victims and bystanders. 

(U//FOUO) Asking the threat actor for specific details may fluster the caller because they're unfamiliar with the school or business they're threatening, and they may make mistakes, making hoax threats more easily identified.

(U) Blocked Phone Numbers

(U//FOUO) Threat actors are often technically skilled in veiling their phone number to hide their true identity and phone numbers may be reused by the same actor allowing investigators to tie multiple incidents to the same threat actor or NVE group. Additionally, phone numbers can be spoofed to target specific victims, making it appear that their victim is conducting the swatting cal l.
(U//FOUO) While calls may come through as blocked or anonymous, investigators may obtain phone number data from the telecom provider.
- (U//FOUO) On 13 May 2026, two Utah middle schools received phone calls from an individual claiming they were going to the school with an AR-I 5. Both calls came in on blocked numbers, but one of the schools was able to obtain the telephone number.
