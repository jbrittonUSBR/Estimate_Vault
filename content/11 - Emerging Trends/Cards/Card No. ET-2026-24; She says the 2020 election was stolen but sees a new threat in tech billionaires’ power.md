(U) Amy Kremer, a former Tea Party leader who helped organize a rally near the White House urging Congress to overturn the 2020 election results in the hours before the January 6, 2021, Capitol attack, continues to maintain that the election was stolen. Five years later, she has shifted her activism toward opposing the growing power of artificial intelligence companies and tech billionaires, which she views as a significant new threat comparable to the populist grievances that have long motivated her political involvement. Kremer is now engaged in right-wing populist efforts to regulate AI, framing the issue as a battle against concentrated corporate influence and elite control. The profile portrays her transition from election-related activism to tech regulation as part of an ongoing resistance against powerful institutions, with AI positioned as the latest front in that struggle.
