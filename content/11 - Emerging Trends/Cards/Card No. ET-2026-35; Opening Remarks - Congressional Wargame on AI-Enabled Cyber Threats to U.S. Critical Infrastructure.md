(U) The Center for Strategic and International Studies (CSIS) hosted a private congressional wargame for members of the House Committee on Homeland Security and the House Select Committee on China. The exercise examined:

- AI-enabled cyber threats
- Critical infrastructure resilience
- Homeland security implications if China obtains advanced (“Mythos-level”) artificial intelligence capabilities

(U) Opening statements were webcast; the wargame itself was closed-door. It was organized by CSIS’s Futures Lab, Intelligence, National Security, and Technology Program, and Defense and Security department. Featured speakers included Benjamin Jensen, Emily Harding, and former House Homeland Security Committee Chairman Rep. Michael McCaul (R-TX).
