(U//FOUO) An anonymous image board user created a thread titled "Why is /g/ Against This?" with an image of a data center (Collector Comment: /g/ refers to the technology topic on the anonymous image board platform). Users discussed causing "tens of millions of dollars worth of damage" and questioned, "How hard would it be for somebody to do the same thing to a $10B+ data center?" Additional users suggested physical attack methods, including the use of explosive devices, such as "a suicidal strike team with demolitions", "buy a data center then blow it up yourself as a DIY project", and "blow some water return lines in the data halls so the gear gets soaked".

(U//FOUO) Users also proposed several cyber attack methods, including "get a job at" an identified U.S.-based technology company, then "add secret code that fries the power across a cluster" (NFI). Another user commented, "Data centers won't burn but what you would need to do is destroy the hardware through thermal stress". The user advised, "hijack their EPMS systems to disable water cooling which will kill their air conditioning in the data halls", and use Compute Unified Device Architecture (CUDA) to "tell the GPU cards to continue operating above their safe operating thresholds" (Collector Comment: EPMS refers to Electrical Power Management Systems). The user explained that "the thermal mass of all the gpus will hold steady which should wear them out faster" and "knock it offline for a few weeks to months".
