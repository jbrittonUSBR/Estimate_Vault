(U) A mid-2026 analysis highlights persistent volatility across key business trends, with geopolitical risks elevated due to ongoing conflicts, supply chain disruptions, cyber threats, and regulatory shifts, while inflation remains unstable and labor markets show signs of cooling with lower quit and hire rates. AI adoption continues at a rapid pace amid an “arms race” among companies and nations, though organizations are grappling with unexpected costs, energy and water constraints for data centers, data governance challenges, and evolving liability frameworks. The technology is reshaping 50-55% of U.S. jobs according to BCG estimates, creating new roles while placing a premium on human judgment, critical thinking, and AI oversight skills, even as public backlash—particularly from younger generations—grows over its economic and environmental impacts. Companies are refining hybrid work models and investing in scenario planning to navigate these interconnected risks, balancing short-term pressures with long-term strategic bets on AI and talent development. The report underscores that while many predicted trends are unfolding as expected, the pace of change and emerging complexities require leaders to remain agile, proactive in risk mitigation, and focused on both technological integration and human capital.
