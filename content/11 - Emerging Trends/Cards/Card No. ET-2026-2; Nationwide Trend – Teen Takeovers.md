(U//FOUO) National law enforcement agencies have reported an increase in "street takeovers" and "teen takeovers," which are characterized by uncontrolled driving stunts, large crowds gathered in retail areas, and related offenses, resulting in police warnings and arrests. These incidents have triggered heightened security and law enforcement attention throughout the country.
(U//FOUO) Out of State Events: Several takeover events have occurred throughout the nation, impacting California, New York, Florida, Maryland, District of Columbia, Connecticut, and more. While some participants seek safe social spaces, many of these events have escalated into public disorder, including fighting, property damage, and robbery. 
(U//FOUO) Authorities across the U.S. are implementing strict measures to curb these incidents such as initiating curfews, warning parents that they may be held legally responsible for their children's conduct during these gatherings, issuing warnings, and increasing police patrols at malls, parks, and city centers.
