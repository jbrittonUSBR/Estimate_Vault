# 'Remember, there is a lot of stuff in our critical infrastructure that’s unpatchable': Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella warns more outages of key networks could be coming

[Source](https://www.techradar.com/pro/security/remember-there-is-a-lot-of-stuff-in-our-critical-infrastructure-thats-unpatchable-microsoft-ceo-satya-nadella-warns-more-outages-of-key-networks-could-be-coming)

(U) New York, United States — Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella warned at the New York Times' DealBook Summit that internet-connected chillers, HVAC equipment and other industrial firmware in critical infrastructure cannot receive conventional security updates, leaving essential services exposed as AI tools accelerate discovery of software flaws, TechRadar reported. He said patches must move "instantly" from release to production because vulnerabilities were already being exploited in the wild, creating instability when rushed deployments were insufficiently tested. Nadella cited recent disruptions involving Microsoft Outlook, the National Security Agency, the Federal Aviation Administration and Apple, noting that officials had ruled out malicious activity in some cases and that Microsoft was applying operational and architectural lessons from its own incident response.
