# Fable Launches Briefing Agent to Turn Emerging Threats Into Targeted Employee Interventions in Minutes

[Source](https://www.01net.it/fable-launches-briefing-agent-to-turn-emerging-threats-into-targeted-employee-interventions-in-minutes/)

(U) SAN FRANCISCO, United States — Fable Security announced the general availability of Briefing Agent, an AI agent that converts emerging threats and new policies into targeted employee video briefings in less than 20 minutes, according to a Business Wire release republished by the Italian technology site 01net. The company said the tool researches current threat intelligence, drafts a scene-by-scene script tailored to specific roles or behavioral cohorts flagged by its Human Risk Engine, and delivers two- to three-minute briefings through email, Slack or Teams. Fable stated that all generated content requires explicit customer approval before distribution and that the capability was validated in an early access program involving more than 20 customers in insurance, financial services, enterprise technology and other industries. The item is a corporate press release rather than independent reporting.
