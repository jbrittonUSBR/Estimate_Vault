(U) **Opening Remarks: Congressional Wargame on AI-Enabled Cyber Threats to U.S. Critical Infrastructure** (CSIS Events page, July 21, 2026) The Center for Strategic and International Studies (CSIS) hosted a congressional wargame for members of the House Committee on Homeland Security and the House Select Committee on China. The exercise examined AI-enabled cyber threats, critical infrastructure resilience, and the homeland security implications of China obtaining “Mythos-level” artificial intelligence capabilities.

**Event details**:
- Date/time: July 21, 2026, 2:00–2:30 p.m. EDT (opening remarks).
- The opening statements were webcast; the wargame itself was private and closed-door.
- Credentialed press could attend the opening remarks in person by RSVP.
- Hosted by CSIS’s Futures Lab, Intelligence, National Security, and Technology Program, and Defense and Security programs.
- Featured speakers/hosts included Benjamin Jensen (Director, Futures Lab), Emily Harding (Vice President, Defense and Security; Director, Intelligence, National Security, and Technology Program), and Rep. Michael McCaul (R-TX), former Chairman of the House Homeland Security Committee.
