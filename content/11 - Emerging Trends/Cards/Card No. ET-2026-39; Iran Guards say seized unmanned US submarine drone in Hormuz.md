# Iran Guards say seized unmanned US submarine drone in Hormuz

[Source](https://www.arabnews.com/middle-east/iran-guards-say-seized-unmanned-us-submarine-drone-in-hormuz-3000956)

(U) TEHRAN, Iran — Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps said it seized an American unmanned underwater vehicle at the entrance to the Strait of Hormuz in what it described as a "complex intelligence and operational action at dawn today." In a statement carried by Iranian state television, the IRGC claimed the vehicle was among the most advanced unmanned subsurface craft and had been delivered to the U.S. fleet in 2025, adding that images would be released in the coming hours. Iranian media said the craft's capabilities included subsurface reconnaissance and surveillance, seabed mapping, mine detection, and inspection of submarine cables and pipelines. The claim, which could not be independently verified, came amid Iran's blockade of the Strait — normally carrying about a fifth of world oil and liquefied natural gas exports — since the war began in late February, and a reciprocal U.S. counter-blockade of Iranian ports.
