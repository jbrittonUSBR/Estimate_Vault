(U//FGI BRA//LES) Recently encountered adult Somali nationals posing as unaccompanied alien children (UACs) along the U.S. Southwest Border (SWB) are likely using this tactic to attempt to exploit U.S. immigration policies. We have moderate confidence in this assessment based on law enforcement reporting indicating that many encountered Somali nationals presented themselves to CBP as minors after entering the Western Hemisphere as adults with African travel documents, which they later allegedly lost or discarded. The tactic of entering the WHEM with legitimate passports for initial travel, then discarding them and adopting fraudulent identities, is an established practice; however, posing as a minor is a less common tactic used by East Africans* (see Appendix A). The attempt to obfuscate their age or true nationality is likely due to U.S. immigration detention policies for UACs. Once DHS processes UACs, they are transferred to the Health and Human Services (HHS) Office of Refugee Resettlement (ORR) and are required to be placed into the least restrictive care, which can include being released to the custody of vetted suitable sponsors. (U//LES/FGI:FTD) Somali nationals and other East Africans posing as Somalis will likely continue to conceal their true identities after entering the WHEM, hindering efforts to determine their actual travel routes to the United States. Encounter interviews with Somali nationals revealed that many entered the WHEM through Brazil using fraudulent documents; disembarked in WHEM countries like Mexico, Peru, and Bolivia; and then traveled by land to the U.S. SWB.
