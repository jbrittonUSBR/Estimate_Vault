# Security Experts: AI Data Centers Contain Hidden Risks from China

[Source](https://www.breitbart.com/tech/2026/09/07/security-experts-ai-data-centers-contain-hidden-risks-from-china/)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — The Trump administration moved to tighten scrutiny of Chinese-made equipment powering US data centers, citing national security risks as demand for electricity to run AI systems surges, Breitbart News reported on September 7, 2026, citing CNBC. President Donald Trump signed an executive order declaring a national emergency over foreign-made equipment used in the bulk-power system, calling it an "extraordinary foreign threat" and giving the Energy Department authority to prohibit or condition certain transactions involving grid and data-center equipment. Analysts cited in the report said Chinese firms supply significant shares of critical components, with Johns Hopkins University associate professor Yury Dvorkin stating China's share of certain transformer and switchgear categories runs near 30 percent and over 40 percent of US battery imports, and Chinese firms together accounting for roughly two-thirds of global optical transceiver unit supply. Some companies, including Hitachi Energy and Siemens Energy, have announced billion-dollar US manufacturing investments in response.
