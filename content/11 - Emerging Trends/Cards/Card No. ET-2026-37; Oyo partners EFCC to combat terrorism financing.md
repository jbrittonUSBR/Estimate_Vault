# Oyo partners EFCC to combat terrorism financing

(U) IBADAN, Nigeria — The Oyo State government, through Acting Governor Bayo Lawal, has pledged to strengthen collaboration with the Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC) to combat terrorism financing and illicit financial flows. At a capacity-building workshop in Ibadan, officials warned that criminal networks are increasingly exploiting crowdfunding platforms, humanitarian interventions, non-governmental organizations, and informal financial systems to raise and move funds. The state urged relevant ministries, departments, and agencies to establish dedicated desks for intelligence sharing, monitor suspicious financial activity, and improve oversight of legitimate humanitarian assistance to prevent its abuse.
