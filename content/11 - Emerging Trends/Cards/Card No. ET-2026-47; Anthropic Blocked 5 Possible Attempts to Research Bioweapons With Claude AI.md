# Anthropic Blocked 5 Possible Attempts to Research Bioweapons With Claude AI

[Source](https://me.pcmag.com/en/ai/38028/anthropic-blocked-5-possible-attempts-to-research-bioweapons-with-claude-ai)

(U) SAN FRANCISCO, United States — Anthropic's September threat intelligence report, a 154-page document covering misuse of its Claude models between December 2025 and August 2026, detailed seven categories of abuse including five instances in which actors may have used the models to support biological weapons development in unnamed countries. The cases included a user planning avian influenza mammalian-adaptation experiments and another working to redesign toxins while attempting to obscure their identity, with one scientist also seeking help drafting a grant application for chikungunya virus research at a military institute that the report's authors said gave them "cause of concern." Anthropic said it banned the users, implemented further safeguards, and reported relevant criminal activity to authorities, and noted that it withheld countries of origin and institution names because it could not confirm intent, given that such biological research can be dual-use; the report also documented six scenarios involving weapons software, including firearms, missiles, armed drones, and their targeting and control systems.
