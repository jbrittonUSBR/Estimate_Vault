# Electronic Warfare Market to Reach USD 51.97 Billion by 2035 Amid Rising Defence Modernisation

[Source](https://timestech.in/electronic-warfare-market-to-reach-usd-51-97-billion-by-2035-amid-rising-defence-modernisation/)

(U) NEW DELHI, India — According to Cervicorn Consulting, as reported by TimesTech, the global electronic warfare market was valued at USD 26.45 billion in 2025 and is projected to reach approximately USD 51.97 billion by 2035, growing at a 7% CAGR from 2026 to 2035. The growth is attributed to defence modernisation, rising investment in military electronics, AI-enabled and cognitive EW architectures, and the increasing importance of electromagnetic spectrum superiority. Counter-UAS operations were identified as one of the fastest-growing application areas, with North America accounting for about 38% of global EW revenue in 2025 and Asia Pacific expected to grow fastest.
