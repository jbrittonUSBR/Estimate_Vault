(U) A high-security investigation was launched in Germany after an explosive-laden drone was discovered near a Ukrainian cargo plane at **Leipzig/Halle Airport**, marking what officials described as a dangerous escalation in foreign-backed hybrid warfare.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Target and Threat:** The security breach occurred at Leipzig/Halle Airport—a crucial European freight hub heavily utilized by NATO and international logistics providers to move defense equipment and supplies supporting Ukraine. The drone was found parked near a Ukrainian **Antonov An-124 Ruslan** heavy cargo aircraft.
- **The Explosive Payload:** Investigators confirmed that the quadcopter drone was equipped with a professional-grade charge (including plastic explosives like Semtex and PETN) and a detonator. Video footage and forensic analyses later indicated the drone actually struck the aircraft's wing near fuel tanks, but failed to detonate due to a faulty detonator.
- **How It Was Discovered:** The device went unnoticed for hours until a worker accidentally spotted it on the ground near the runway, prompting an emergency response, temporary runway closures, and the deployment of a bomb disposal robot to safely neutralize the device.
- **Government Response and Suspicions:** German Chancellor Friedrich Merz convened the national security council to address the incident. Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt characterized the event as a "hybrid attack" representing a "new level of danger." While German officials proceeded carefully with attribution, U.S. and European intelligence assessments linked the operation to Russian state actors.
