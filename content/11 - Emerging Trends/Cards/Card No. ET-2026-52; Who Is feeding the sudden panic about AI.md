# Who Is feeding the sudden panic about AI?

[Source](https://issuesinsights.com/2026/09/15/whos-orchestrating-the-anti-ai-campaign/)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — An Issues & Insights editorial argued that the sudden, cross-platform panic over artificial intelligence was not organic and was likely being orchestrated by organized interests. The board pointed to the rapid elevation of a former Anthropic researcher's warnings, prominent New York Times coverage, and calls by major AI laboratories to slow development as evidence of a coordinated communications campaign. The piece was an opinion column reflecting the outlet's editorial perspective.
