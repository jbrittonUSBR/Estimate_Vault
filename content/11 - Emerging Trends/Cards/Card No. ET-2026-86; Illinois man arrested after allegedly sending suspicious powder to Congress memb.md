# Illinois man arrested after allegedly sending suspicious powder to Congress members using his return address

[Source](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/oct/27/man-sent-powder-congress-arrest)

(U) QUINCY, ILLINOIS, U.S. — An Illinois man was arrested on federal charges after allegedly mailing envelopes containing white powder and “ha ha” notes to four members of Congress, using his own name and return address, officials said.
