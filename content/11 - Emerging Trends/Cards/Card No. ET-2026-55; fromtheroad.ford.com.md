# Team UAV Turns Ford Ranger into Mobile Drone Base

[Source](https://www.fromtheroad.ford.com/eur/en/articles/2026/team-uav-ford-ranger-mobile-drone-base.html)

(U) COLOGNE — Ford of Europe profiled "Team UAV," a specialist team that has converted a Ford Ranger pickup into a mobile drone base, demonstrating the growing integration of small unmanned aircraft systems with commercial vehicle platforms. The modification allows the vehicle to serve as a launch, control, and recovery platform for drones in field operations, illustrating the emerging trend of vehicle-mounted UAS deployment for inspection, surveying, and tactical use.
