# Killer driver who mowed down 'uniquely talented' headteacher in two-ton SUV and dragged him for over a mile convicted of his murder

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16245779/Driver-mowed-headteacher-dragged-mile-convicted-murder.html)

(U) Altrincham, Greater Manchester, United Kingdom — The Daily Mail reported that a jury convicted Simon Richardson of murdering headteacher Michael Mullins after striking him as he cycled home and driving on with him trapped beneath an SUV for more than a mile. Richardson was also convicted of dangerous driving; sentencing coverage later reported a life term with a 25-year minimum and a lifetime driving ban.
