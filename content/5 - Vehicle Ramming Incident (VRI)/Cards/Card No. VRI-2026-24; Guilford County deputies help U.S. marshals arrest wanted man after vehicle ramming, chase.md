(U) **Guilford County deputies help U.S. marshals arrest wanted man after vehicle ramming, chase** (ABC45, Daisy Gonzalez-Quezada, July 21, 2026) Guilford County, North Carolina, deputies assisted the U.S. Marshals Fugitive Task Force in arresting Nicholas Kane Conner after he allegedly rammed law enforcement vehicles while trying to evade capture. On the morning of July 21, shortly before 8 a.m., members of the Guilford County Sheriffâ€™s Office Street Crimes Unit, Narcotics Task Force, and Warrant Squad, along with Greensboro Police, helped locate and safely take Conner into custody on Landmark Center Boulevard following an extensive manhunt. Earlier that day, Conner had fled from U.S. Marshals after intentionally ramming several law enforcement vehicles.

**Charges against Conner include:**
- Larceny of a firearm
- Possession of a firearm by a felon
- Felony larceny
- Assault with a deadly weapon on a law enforcement officer (four counts)
- Felony flee to elude arrest
- Assault on a law enforcement officer inflicting serious injury (two counts)
- Assault by pointing a gun at a law enforcement officer (two counts)

He was also served with outstanding warrants from multiple jurisdictions, including:
- Guilford County: Failure to report/register as a sex offender (multiple counts)
- Randolph County: Driving while license revoked, sex-offender reporting violations, and related charges
- Asheboro: Felony flee to elude, driving while license revoked, aggressive driving, failure to heed lights/sirens, and speeding

(U) Conner is being held at the Greensboro Detention Center without bond (plus a $426,000 secured bond on prior charges). Authorities also arrested Israel Aaron Foxx on charges of harboring a fugitive and felony aiding and abetting; he was released on a $10,000 bond.
