# Murder trial heard headteacher was dragged beneath car for more than a mile

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16210925/Trail-blood-mile-long-dying-headteacher-murder-trial.html)

(U) ALTRINCHAM, England — The Daily Mail reported that a murder trial heard that cyclist and headteacher Michael Mullins was struck by a Volvo while riding home and dragged beneath it for more than a mile. Prosecutors alleged that driver Simon Richardson knew Mullins was trapped, continued driving at speed and later ran over him again before leaving the scene. A motorcyclist testified that he followed a blood trail to Mullins, who had suffered fatal injuries. These were allegations and evidence reported during the trial, not a verdict.
