# Man accused of driving 118 MPH on I-4 in Orlando with loaded gun, ramming detective’s car

[Source](https://www.clickorlando.com/video/news/2026/09/21/man-accused-of-driving-118-mph-on-i-4-in-orlando-with-loaded-gun-ramming-detectives-car/)

(U) ORLANDO, Fla. — Jose Rosario, 21, of Sanford was arrested around 2:30 a.m. Sunday near Interstate 4 and State Road 408 after police said he drove 118 mph with a loaded handgun within reach, refused to stop and rammed a detective's patrol car. An Orlando Police arrest affidavit led to three felony charges, according to WKMG. No injuries were reported.
