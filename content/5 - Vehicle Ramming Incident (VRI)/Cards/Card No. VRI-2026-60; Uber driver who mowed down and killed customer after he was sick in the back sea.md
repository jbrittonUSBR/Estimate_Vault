# Uber driver found not guilty of murder after fatal collision with customer

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16223885/Uber-driver-mowed-killed-customer-sick-seat-car-acquitted-murder.html)

(U) October 20, 2026 — A Basildon Crown Court jury found Uber driver Hayadulla Turkmani not guilty of murder and manslaughter in the death of customer Michael Ashton, but guilty of causing death by dangerous driving, the Daily Mail reported. Ashton died after being struck by Turkmani’s vehicle outside his home in Essex in September 2025. Prosecutors argued that Turkmani deliberately drove into Ashton after a dispute; Turkmani testified that he reversed in fear and did not see him.
