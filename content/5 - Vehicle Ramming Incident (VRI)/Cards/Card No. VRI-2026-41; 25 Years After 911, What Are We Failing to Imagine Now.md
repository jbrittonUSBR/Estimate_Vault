# 25 Years After 9/11, What Are We Failing to Imagine Now?

[Source](http://foreignpolicy.com/2026/09/07/9-11-failure-of-imagination-new-terrorist-technologies/)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — In a Foreign Policy essay, Colin P. Clarke and Clara Broekaert argued that 25 years after the September 11 attacks, the "failure of imagination" that preceded them still persists. The authors contended that novel technologies, including drones, artificial intelligence, 3D printing and virtual currencies, could be combined by terrorists in ways officials have not anticipated. They called for policymakers to better anticipate emerging attack methods.
