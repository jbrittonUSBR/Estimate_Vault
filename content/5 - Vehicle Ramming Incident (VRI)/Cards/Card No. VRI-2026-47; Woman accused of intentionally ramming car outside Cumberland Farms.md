# Woman accused of intentionally ramming car outside Cumberland Farms

[Source](https://cbs6albany.com/news/local/woman-accused-of-intentionally-ramming-car-outside-cumberland-farms-two-car-property-christina-millington-fort-edward-washington-county-sheriffs-office-cumberland-farms-ems-washington-county-centralized-arraignment-argyle-broadway-reckless-cbs6-wrgb)

(U) FORT EDWARD, N.Y. — Washington County sheriff's deputies said Christina Millington, 45, of Argyle intentionally rammed another vehicle at the Cumberland Farms on Broadway on Monday, Sept. 21, 2026. Authorities said Millington followed a person she knew to the store, then accelerated across the lot and struck the vehicle as it pulled onto Broadway; no one was injured. She was charged with second-degree criminal mischief and three counts of second-degree reckless endangerment and remanded to the Washington County Jail.
