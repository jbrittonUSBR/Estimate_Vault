(U) A single-vehicle accident occurred in Englewood, Ohio, when a pickup truck veered off the side of the Englewood Dam, resulting in injuries and drawing a local emergency response.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Incident:** A pickup truck left the roadway and crashed down the side embankment of the Englewood Dam.
- **Casualties:** At least one person sustained injuries in the accident and required medical attention.
- **Response & Investigation:** Local police officers and medical personnel quickly responded to the scene to secure the area, manage traffic impacts, and assist the injured occupant. The exact cause of the crash remains under investigation.
