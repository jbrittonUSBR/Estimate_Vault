# Moment M&S worker is mowed down while trying to stop suspected thief on an e-bike

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16262779/Moment-worker-trying-stop-suspected-thief-bike.html)

(U) Giltbrook, Nottinghamshire, United Kingdom — The Daily Mail reported that a suspected shoplifter rode an e-bike into an M&S worker while trying to leave with about £200 of children’s clothing. The worker suffered minor injuries; police arrested a 36-year-old man on suspicion of robbery after the incident.
