# Cameron man faces three felonies in alleged motel vehicle ramming

[Source](https://www.kttn.com/cameron-man-faces-three-felonies-in-alleged-motel-vehicle-ramming/)

(U) CAMERON, Mo. — Robert Leroy Justus III, who lists a Cameron address, faces three felony charges in DeKalb County after authorities alleged a Jeep Cherokee repeatedly struck an occupied vehicle at a local motel and was later repainted. A complaint filed Sept. 21 charges him with second-degree assault, first-degree property damage and tampering with physical evidence over the March 19 incident; the victim told police the Jeep followed her from the motel and rammed her until she was blocked behind a semitrailer. Justus has not been convicted, and the charges remain allegations.
