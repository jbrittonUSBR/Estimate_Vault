# Son of Israeli ambassador to US critically injured in West Bank ramming attack

[Source](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/23/world/son-of-israeli-ambassador-to-the-us-injured)

(U) JERUSALEM — Neria Leiter, an Israeli reserve soldier and the son of Ambassador to the United States Yechiel Leiter, was critically injured Wednesday when a Palestinian man rammed a car into a checkpoint near the Beit Horon settlement in the occupied West Bank, according to Israeli military and law enforcement officials. Israeli forces opened fire at the attacker as he tried to flee and killed him, and Leiter was taken to a Jerusalem hospital unconscious, CNN reported. The attack came a day before Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu was scheduled to address the UN General Assembly.
