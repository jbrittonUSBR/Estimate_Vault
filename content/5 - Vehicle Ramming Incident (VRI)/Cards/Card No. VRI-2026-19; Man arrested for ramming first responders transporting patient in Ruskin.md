(U) A 35-year-old man, Johnny Arman, was arrested after intentionally ramming an ambulance carrying a female patient and two Hillsborough County paramedics in Ruskin, Florida, on June 25, 2026, causing it to crash into utility boxes and a pole with over $100,000 in damage. The incident began when Arman became hostile at a home medical call, repeatedly trying to force his way into the ambulance and interfere with paramedics before following the departing vehicle, blocking its path on Upper Creek Drive, and accelerating toward it. Arman faces multiple felony charges, including two counts of aggravated battery on a firefighter with a deadly weapon, aggravated battery with a deadly weapon, and burglary of an authorized emergency vehicle. Hillsborough County Sheriff Chad Chronister condemned the attack, stating it deliberately targeted first responders and a patient in need of care, putting lives in jeopardy and emphasizing that such violence against emergency personnel will not be tolerated.
