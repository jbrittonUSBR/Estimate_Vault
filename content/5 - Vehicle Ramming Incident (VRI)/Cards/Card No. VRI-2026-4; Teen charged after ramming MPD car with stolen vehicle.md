(U) MEMPHIS, Tenn. (WMC) - A 19-year-old is behind bars Sunday after Memphis Police said he rammed a police cruiser with a stolen car. Jarquise Oneal has been charged with two counts of assault on law enforcement, possession of a prohibited weapon (machine gun), theft of property $25,000-$60,000 (motor vehicle), evading arrest in a vehicle and evading arrest on foot. Two Memphis Police officers on a routine patrol early Sunday morning noticed a stolen vehicle, a 2026 Dodge Durango, near Felts Station and Thomas Street. Records state that when the officers approached the vehicle, the driver switched into reverse and struck the marked Memphis Police patrol car with the officers still inside. After that, MPD said that Oneal fled the scene while being pursued by officers. They pursued the Durango until it stopped on St. Paul Avenue and Cleaborne Street. Then, five people bailed out of the vehicle, including the driver Oneal, according to police. Officers pursued Oneal on foot and eventually took him into custody. Records show that police located a modified Glock 19 9mm with an extended magazine with a machine gun conversion kit which allowed it to fire fully automatic. Jarquise Oneal is expected in court on June 1. His bail information has not yet been posted.
