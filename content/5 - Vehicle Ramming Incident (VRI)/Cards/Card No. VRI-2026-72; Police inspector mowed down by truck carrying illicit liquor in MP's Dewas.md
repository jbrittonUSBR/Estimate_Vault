# Police inspector mowed down by truck carrying illicit liquor in MP's Dewas

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/bhopal/police-inspector-mowed-down-by-truck-carrying-illicit-liquor-in-mps-dewas/articleshow/135445400.cms)

(U) Dewas district, Madhya Pradesh, India — The Times of India reported that police inspector Dilip Puri died after a truck allegedly carrying illicit liquor ran over him during an anti-smuggling operation on the Indore–Betul highway. Police said the driver fled after the incident and that officers found about 1,800 litres of liquor in the truck; an investigation was opened.
