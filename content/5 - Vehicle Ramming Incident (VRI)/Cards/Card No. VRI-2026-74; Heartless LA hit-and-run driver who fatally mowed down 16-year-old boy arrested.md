# Heartless LA hit-and-run driver who fatally mowed down 16-year-old boy arrested after a month on the lam

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/10/29/us-news/emotionless-los-angeles-murder-suspect-linked-to-fatal-teen-crash/)

(U) Hyde Park, Los Angeles — The New York Post reported that LAPD arrested Raygine Glentasia Evans on suspicion of murder in connection with a September 22 hit-and-run in which a car allegedly struck two siblings, killing 16-year-old Hernan Martinez and critically injuring his 14-year-old sister. Police said the car had run a red light at about 80 mph, and detectives were investigating alleged links between Evans and other robberies.
