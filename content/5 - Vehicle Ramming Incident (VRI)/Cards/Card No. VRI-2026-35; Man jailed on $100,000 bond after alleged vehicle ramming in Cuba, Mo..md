(U) A 56-year-old man was arrested and jailed on a $100,000 bond following a violent vehicle-ramming incident in Cuba, Missouri, that sent another car crashing into a nearby house.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Incident:** Kenneth Hurst allegedly drove his Nissan and forcefully rammed it into another vehicle, causing that car to careen into a residential house and resulting in significant property damage.
- **The Arrest:** Hurst fled the scene on foot, abandoning his heavily damaged vehicle. Local law enforcement quickly tracked him down and took him into custody. According to authorities, he showed signs of impairment, including slurring his words and appearing to be under the influence of drugs.
- **Charges & Bond:** Hurst was booked into the Crawford County Jail and is facing multiple serious felony charges, including **second-degree assault, armed criminal action, and first-degree property damage**. His bond was set at $100,000.
