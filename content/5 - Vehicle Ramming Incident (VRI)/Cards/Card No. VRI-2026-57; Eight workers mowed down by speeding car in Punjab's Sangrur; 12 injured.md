# Eight workers mowed down by speeding car in Punjab's Sangrur; 12 injured

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/india/eight-workers-mowed-down-by-speeding-car-in-punjabs-sangrur-12-injured/articleshow/135031156.cms)

(U) October 18, 2026 — PTI reported that a speeding car struck MGNREGA laborers resting and drinking water beside a road in Sangrur district, Punjab. Its initial account cited the local police station chief as reporting nine deaths and a senior medical officer as saying eight people had been brought to hospital; the card headline and other contemporaneous reports cited eight deaths and 12 injured. An injured worker told reporters that the group had been sitting by the roadside when the car hit them.
