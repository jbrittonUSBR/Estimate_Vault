# EPD: Man arrested after ramming woman’s vehicle during chase

[Source](https://www.14news.com/video/2026/09/16/epd-man-arrested-after-ramming-womans-vehicle-during-chase/)

(U) EVANSVILLE — Evansville Police Department officials said a man faced multiple charges after he chased a woman for several miles and repeatedly rammed her vehicle, 14 News reported on September 16, 2026. According to an affidavit, dispatchers received a be-on-the-lookout report from the woman, who said Richard Connor was pursuing her and trying to run her off the road; she was directed to continue toward EPD headquarters, where officers stopped him near Main Street and found a gun in his vehicle. Police determined Connor was intoxicated and had stolen both the vehicle and the firearm, and he was booked into the Vanderburgh County Jail on charges that included stalking, auto theft, firearm theft, intimidation, criminal recklessness and operating a motor vehicle while intoxicated.
