# Beauty queen returns to scene where she was mowed down by drivers aged 4 and 6

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/11/02/us-news/beauty-queen-kiara-bowling-returns-to-crash-site-after-being-struck-by-child-drivers-in-oakland/)

(U) Oakland, California, USA — The New York Post reported that beauty pageant winner Kiara Bowling revisited the intersection where two boys, ages four and six, allegedly drove their parents’ car into her and a dog in August. Bowling sustained severe brain injuries, spent months in critical care, and was relearning basic functions; she said she forgave the children.
