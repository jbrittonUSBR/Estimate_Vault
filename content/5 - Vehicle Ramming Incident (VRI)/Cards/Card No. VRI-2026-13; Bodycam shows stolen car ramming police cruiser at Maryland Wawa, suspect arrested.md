(U) Bodycam footage captured a stolen vehicle ramming an Anne Arundel County police cruiser multiple times at a Wawa in Hanover, Maryland, on June 19, 2026, before officers tackled the driver, Djaen Marquise Jacobs, 28, of Glen Burnie, as he attempted to flee on foot and recovered a gun from him. The driver reversed into the cruiser, drove forward into a concrete post, and reversed into the cruiser again after an officer pulled up behind the vehicle with lights flashing. Jacobs, identified as the suspect in the stolen car, was taken into custody following the confrontation at the Milestone Parkway location. Police did not specify charges at the time of the report, but the incident was documented on body-worn camera and shared by the department.
