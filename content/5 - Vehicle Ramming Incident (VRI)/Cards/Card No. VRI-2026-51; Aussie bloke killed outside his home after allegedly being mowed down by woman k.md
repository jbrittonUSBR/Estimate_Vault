# Aussie bloke killed outside his home after allegedly being mowed down by woman known to him

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16165487/Bradley-Bullock-dead-brisbane-fernvale.html)

(U) BRISBANE — Bradly Bullock, 50, was killed outside his home on Stoneywood Street in Fernvale, about 60 kilometers west of Brisbane, at about 5:55 p.m. on Sunday when he was struck by a vehicle, the Daily Mail reported. His wife, Lacey Jay Bullock, 45, was charged with domestic-violence murder and disqualified driving. The report said the couple was known to police and did not allege a terrorism link.
