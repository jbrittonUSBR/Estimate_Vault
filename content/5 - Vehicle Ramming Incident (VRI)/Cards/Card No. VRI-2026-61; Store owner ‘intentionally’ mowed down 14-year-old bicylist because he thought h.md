# Florida store owner charged after fatal e-bike collision with 14-year-old, police say

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/10/20/us-news/store-owner-intentionally-mowed-down-14-year-old-bicylist-because-he-thought-he-was-a-burglar-cops/)

(U) October 20, 2026 — The New York Post reported that a Florida convenience-store owner, Kavan Patel, was charged with second-degree murder after 14-year-old Hunter Russey died following a vehicle collision in West Melbourne. Police said Patel told investigators he intentionally struck the teenager after suspecting him of attempting to break into the store; the accusation was reported from police statements and charging documents. Police said Russey was found injured in a roadside ditch hours later and was taken off life support.
