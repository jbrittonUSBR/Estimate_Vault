# Man accused of deliberately hitting 'blameless' 12yos with ute granted bail

[Source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-08-12/man-bailed-over-allegedly-mowing-down-innocent-youths-with-ute/107028344)

Magistrate Patrick Southey heard the boys were "completely blameless" and were not involved in earlier damage to Brien's partner's business. One boy was thrown about 20 meters and suffered concussion and a sore arm, while the other sustained a collapsed lung, a broken arm, leg, ankle and sternum, and multiple rib fractures. Brien faces seven charges, including four counts of reckless conduct endangering life, dangerous driving causing serious injury and intentionally causing injury; his bail conditions prohibit driving and impose a 9 p.m. curfew, with a committal mention set for December 2.
