### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Incident:** On April 18, a group of suspects arrived at Chinna Jewelry along Stockton Boulevard in six recently stolen vehicles. One of the vehicles was deliberately driven into the storefront to smash it open.
- **The Fatal Strike:** The vehicle crashed into the front door area and struck 71- or 72-year-old employee Harjinder Singh, leaving him critically injured. He succumbed to his injuries 10 days later.
- **The Outcome:** The suspects fled empty-handed after encountering an armed private security guard.
- **The Arrests & Charges:** Following a multi-jurisdiction investigation aided by automated license plate readers, detectives arrested two suspects:
    - **Alfredo Colmenero-Martinez** (24, of Oakland) was arrested in Tracy and charged with **murder** for allegedly driving the vehicle that struck the victim.
    - **Jarren Frazier** (35, of Stockton) was arrested in Concord and charged with **attempted robbery and conspiracy** for allegedly planning and coordinating the plot.
- Both men were booked into the Sacramento County Main Jail and are being held without bail.
