(U) An Odessa man, Josenarciso Rodriguez Santiago, 44, was arrested after surveillance video captured him ramming the SUV of a 20-year-old family member twice with his Ford F-250 on June 19, 2026, on Roger Avenue, once while she was inside the vehicle. The woman had gone to the home to confront Santiago, believing he had stolen her cell phone from her apartment, and during the resulting argument he got into his truck and struck the back of her Lincoln MKX SUV. Odessa police confirmed the incident through the video evidence and arrested Santiago on suspicion of aggravated assault with a deadly weapon; he was booked into the Ector County jail with bail set at $35,000.
