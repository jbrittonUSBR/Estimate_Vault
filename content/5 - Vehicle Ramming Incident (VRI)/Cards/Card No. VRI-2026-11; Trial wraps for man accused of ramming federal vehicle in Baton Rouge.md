(U) A federal bench trial concluded for Evodio Merida-Tavera, who is accused of ramming a federal vehicle in Baton Rouge in December 2025 while two agents and another person were inside. The trial before U.S. District Judge John deGravelles wrapped up on June 23, 2026, with a ruling expected in about three weeks. According to state arrest records, Merida-Tavera allegedly struck the unmarked vehicle after noticing attention from its occupants and later told investigators he acted because he was in the country illegally and feared arrest. State prosecutors declined to pursue charges, allowing the federal case to proceed on assault allegations. The defense argued the collision was accidental, claiming Merida-Tavera was backing up quickly when the vehicle pulled in behind him without warning and was difficult to see in his rearview mirror due to its angle. Body-camera footage was presented or expected during the proceedings.
