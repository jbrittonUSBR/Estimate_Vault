# Dramatic video shows SWAT vehicle ramming tractor during tense Oregon standoff

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/us/dramatic-video-shows-swat-vehicle-ramming-tractor-tense-oregon-standoff)

(U) PORTLAND, Oregon — Video captured the moment a Clackamas County SWAT vehicle rammed a compact tractor during a standoff with an Oregon man accused of firing more than 200 rounds near his rural home south of Portland. Erik Gordon Shunn, 53, allegedly targeted tree workers before barricading himself and confronting responding deputies, according to the Clackamas County Sheriff's Office. Authorities pinned the tractor against the house to apprehend him, and Shunn was later charged with attempted murder and multiple other offenses and held without bail.
