# Accused teen killer's own dashcam could prove murder of two girls mowed down, lawyer says: 'not an accident'

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/us/accused-teen-killer-dashcam-prove-murder-two-girls-mowed-down-lawyer-says-not-accident)

(U) Cranford, New Jersey — Fox News reported that an attorney for one victim's family said dashcam footage could help establish intent in the deaths of Maria Niotis and Isabella Salas, both 17, who were struck while riding an e-bike in September 2025. The attorney said investigators' recording appeared to show the driver turning around to follow them; a grand jury later indicted Vincent Battiloro on two first-degree murder counts. The intentional-driving claim is an allegation, and Battiloro is presumed innocent unless proven guilty.
