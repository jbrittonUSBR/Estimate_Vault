# Car-ramming terror attack seriously wounds IDF soldier

[Source](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/article-909470)

(U) JERUSALEM — Neria Leiter, an IDF reservist and the son of Israeli Ambassador to the United States Yechiel Leiter, was seriously wounded in a car-ramming attack at a checkpoint on Route 443 on Wednesday. He underwent multi-hour brain surgery at Shaare Zedek Medical Center, which said his condition remained very serious and his life was in danger; the attacker was killed. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu visited the family before departing for the UN General Assembly, and Yechiel Leiter returned to Israel.
