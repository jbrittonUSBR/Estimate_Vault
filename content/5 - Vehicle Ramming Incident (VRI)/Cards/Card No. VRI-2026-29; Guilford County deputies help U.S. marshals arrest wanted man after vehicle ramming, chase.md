(U) Nicholas Kane Conner was arrested in Guilford County, North Carolina, after ramming several law enforcement vehicles while trying to evade U.S. Marshals. He fled, leading to an extensive manhunt before being safely taken into custody on Landmark Center Boulevard without further incident. Conner faces multiple charges, including larceny of a firearm, possession of a firearm by a felon, felony larceny, several counts of assault with a deadly weapon on a law enforcement officer, felony flee to elude, and assault by pointing a gun. He also had outstanding warrants from Guilford, Randolph, and Asheboro jurisdictions related to sex offender registration failures and other offenses. He is being held without bond (plus a $426,000 secured bond on prior charges). Israel Aaron Foxx was also arrested and charged with harboring a fugitive and felony aiding and abetting; he was released on a $10,000 bond.
