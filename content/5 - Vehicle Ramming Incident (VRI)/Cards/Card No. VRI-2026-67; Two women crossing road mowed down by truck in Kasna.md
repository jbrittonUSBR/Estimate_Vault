# Two women crossing road mowed down by truck in Kasna

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/noida/two-women-crossing-road-mowed-down-by-truck-in-kasna/articleshow/135279388.cms)

(U) Kasna, Greater Noida — The Times of India reported that a truck struck and killed two women, Manisha and Vimlesh, as they crossed a road near an industrial area. Police said they detained the driver, who fled after the crash, and registered a case for rash driving and death by negligence.
