# Three pedestrians mowed down as car smashes into California grocery store

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/09/29/us-news/car-crashes-into-los-angeles-grocery-store-hitting-3-people/)

(U) EL MONTE, Calif. — A car crashed into the entrance of a Food 4 Less grocery store on Garvey Avenue in El Monte at about 7:30 a.m. on September 29, 2026, striking three pedestrians, the New York Post reported. One victim was critically injured and two others suffered minor to moderate injuries. The driver remained at the scene and cooperated with investigators, and authorities had not disclosed whether impairment or intent played a role. The crash temporarily closed the store and drew a large emergency response.
