(U) A 34-year-old North Charleston woman, Tanisha Vernell Deas-Gardner, was arrested after allegedly intentionally ramming the rear of another vehicle in a parking lot while an unrestrained child was in the front passenger seat of her own car. According to police, Deas-Gardner followed the victim from a merging lane on Dorchester Road into the Dorchester Park Business Center parking lot on June 23, 2026, struck the victim's car, shouted profanities during a brief confrontation, and then fled the scene. A witness corroborated the victim's account of the road-rage incident, which occurred after the victim had picked up her children and was heading to dinner. Officers later stopped Deas-Gardner's vehicle and discovered the unrestrained child, leading to her arrest on three counts of second-degree assault and battery and one count of unlawful conduct toward a child; she was booked into the Al Cannon Detention Center.
