# Accused NJ teen stalker indicted a year later for murdering two girls, 17, mowed down on e-bikes

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/10/15/us-news/accused-nj-teen-stalker-indicted-a-year-later-for-murdering-two-girls-17-mowed-down-on-e-bikes/)

(U) Cranford, New Jersey — The New York Post reported that Vincent Battiloro, then 18, was indicted on two first-degree murder charges in the September 2025 deaths of Maria Niotis and Isabella Salas, both 17, who were riding e-bikes when struck by a vehicle. The report said prosecutors alleged Battiloro had stalked Niotis and that the vehicle was traveling about 70 mph in a 25-mph zone. Those claims are allegations in a criminal case, not established findings of guilt.
