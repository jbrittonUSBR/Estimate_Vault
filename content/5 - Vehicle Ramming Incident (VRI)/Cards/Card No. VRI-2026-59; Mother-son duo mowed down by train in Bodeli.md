# Mother-son duo mowed down by train in Bodeli

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/vadodara/mother-son-duo-mowed-down-by-train-in-bodeli/articleshow/135075882.cms)

(U) October 21, 2026 — The Times of India reported that a 65-year-old woman and her 32-year-old son died after being struck by a passenger train while crossing a narrow railway bridge over the Narmada canal in Bodeli, Gujarat. Railway police said the bridge lacked space for pedestrians to step aside when a train approached; an eyewitness said the pair had seen the train and tried to cross.
