# Ice cream killer's vile weasel words to his victim's mum after he mowed down Ethan, 14 - and then helped hack the teen to death

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16150007/ethan-hoac-killer-begs-mother-forgiveness.html)

(U) MELBOURNE — A 20-year-old man convicted of murdering Ethan Hoac had a letter of apology read in the Supreme Court of Victoria on Tuesday, more than three years after the 14-year-old was struck by a car and then stabbed in St Albans. The court heard Ethan was walking along Furlong Road with two friends late on June 26, 2023, when a Volkswagen Tiguan drove at the group; the killer had pleaded guilty to murder in May. Ethan's mother, Annie Huynh, raised no objection to the letter being read, and the offender told her he was "deeply sorry" for the pain his actions caused.
