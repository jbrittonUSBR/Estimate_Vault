(U) President Trump said the U.S. would target Pickaxe Mountain (Kūh-e Kolang), an Iranian nuclear facility under construction and buried about 100 meters underground near Natanz, “pretty soon, and very heavily.” The Wall Street Journal reported that Israeli intelligence assessed Iran moved centrifuges there last fall. Trump did not confirm the report but said the U.S. tracks the highly enriched uranium itself (Iran is believed to hold nearly 1,000 pounds near weapons-grade). He also warned the U.S. would strike any site where Iran tries to reconstitute its nuclear program. Analysts note the deeply buried site has so far been spared in U.S. and Israeli strikes, suggesting it may not yet be viewed as an imminent threat. Destroying it would be difficult even with massive ordnance penetrators; collapsing tunnel entrances could still hinder construction and provide early-warning signs of rebuilding efforts. IAEA inspectors have been barred from Iran, leaving major uncertainties about remaining centrifuges and enrichment capabilities.
