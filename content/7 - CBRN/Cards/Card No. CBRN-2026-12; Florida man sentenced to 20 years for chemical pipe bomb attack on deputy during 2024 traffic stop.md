(U) **Florida man sentenced to 20 years for chemical pipe bomb attack on deputy during 2024 traffic stop** (News4JAX, Francine Frazier, July 20, 2026) Charles Legault has been sentenced to 20 years in prison for a chemical pipe bomb attack on a Putnam County deputy during a traffic stop in April 2024. He pleaded no contest in May 2026 to charges including making/possessing/throwing/discharging a destructive device resulting in bodily harm or property damage; carrying a concealed firearm during a felony; armed trafficking in methamphetamine; possession of meth with intent to sell; and possession of drug paraphernalia.

**Incident details (April 5, 2024)**:
- A Putnam County deputy pulled Legault over on Mosely Avenue. Body camera footage shows the deputy smelling marijuana, obtaining consent to search the truck, and finding drugs and paraphernalia.
- Legault was searched and ordered to stand at the front of the truck. The deputy continued searching, found a handgun and more drugs, then the chemical pipe bomb exploded in the deputy’s face about 8 minutes and 45 seconds into the stop.
- The deputy struggled to breathe but called for backup and handcuffed Legault. His uniform was covered in white chemical powder; he was hospitalized for respiratory injuries.
- Investigators determined the PVC pipe bomb was filled with chlorine and brake fluid. The bomb squad cleared the scene. Detectives later found Legault had intentionally assembled the device and was also armed with a suppressor, loaded magazines, and ammunition. A large amount of methamphetamine was recovered from the vehicle.
- The next day, a search of Legault’s home found more illegal drugs but no additional bombs.

(U) State Attorney R.J. Larizza described Legault as a “very dangerous individual” whose removal from the streets makes communities safer.
