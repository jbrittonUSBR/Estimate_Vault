# Iran Denies ‘Dirty Bomb’ Plot, Continues Attacking Neighbors with Drones

[Source](https://www.breitbart.com/middle-east/2026/11/18/iran-denies-dirty-bomb-plot-continues-attacking-neighbors-with-drones/)

(U) Tehran, Iran — Parliamentary speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf called U.S. reporting about a possible Iranian dirty-bomb plot a “false flag,” Breitbart reported. The article said CNN’s cited intelligence sources characterized the reported planning as an option rather than evidence of an imminent attack, while Iran’s UN mission rejected the allegations and Iranian forces continued reported drone attacks in the region.
