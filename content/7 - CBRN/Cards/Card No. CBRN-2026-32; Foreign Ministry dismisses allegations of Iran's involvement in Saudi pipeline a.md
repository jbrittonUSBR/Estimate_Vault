# Foreign Ministry dismisses allegations of Iran's involvement in Saudi pipeline attack

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/saudi/2026/saudi-260914-irna01.htm)

(U) TEHRAN, Iran — Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei dismissed US allegations of Iranian involvement in a drone attack launched from Iraqi soil that struck Saudi Arabia's East-West oil pipeline, saying there was "absolutely no truth" to the claim. Speaking at his weekly press conference, Baqaei said Washington was producing false reports to sow divisions among West Asian states and complicate the situation, according to the state-run Islamic Republic News Agency. He said regional countries themselves must resolve the consequences of such claims.
