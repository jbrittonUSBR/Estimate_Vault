# International Atomic Energy Agency confirms new construction at Iranian nuclear site

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/iaea-confirms-iran-nuclear-construction/)

(U) NATANZ, Iran — The International Atomic Energy Agency confirmed construction activity at Pickaxe Mountain, a deeply buried tunnel complex near Iran's main uranium enrichment plant at Natanz, based on satellite imagery, according to Crypto Briefing. IAEA Director General Rafael Grossi announced the findings on September 10, the same day the agency's Board of Governors voted to refer Iran to the UN Security Council for non-compliance with nuclear safeguards, the first such referral in two decades. The report noted that Iran has refused inspectors access to the site and that verification has been severely limited since military strikes in June 2025.
