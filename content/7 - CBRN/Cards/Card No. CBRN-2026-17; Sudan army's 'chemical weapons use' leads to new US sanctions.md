(U) The United States has imposed new sanctions on Sudan’s military-aligned government over the alleged use of chemical weapons by the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) against the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) during the ongoing civil war.
The measures, effective Monday, restrict Sudan’s access to U.S. financing and exports and ban state-owned Sudanese airlines from using American airspace. The State Department had previously determined that the Sudanese government used chemical weapons in 2024 in violation of the Chemical Weapons Convention.
Residents reported chlorine use in Bahri (part of greater Khartoum) in an area already evacuated of civilians. Both sides face serious accusations: the SAF of indiscriminate shelling and chemical weapons, and the RSF of ethnically motivated killings and sexual violence in Darfur. The war has left about 20 million people facing hunger and roughly 14 million displaced.
