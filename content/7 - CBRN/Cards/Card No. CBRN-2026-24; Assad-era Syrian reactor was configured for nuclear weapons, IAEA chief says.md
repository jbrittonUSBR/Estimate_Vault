# Assad-era Syrian reactor was configured for nuclear weapons, IAEA chief says

[Source](https://abcnews.com/Technology/wireStory/assad-era-syrian-reactor-configured-nuclear-weapons-iaea-136255438)

(U) VIENNA, Austria — International Atomic Energy Agency Director General Rafael Grossi said on September 7, 2026 that a Syrian reactor built during the Assad era was configured for the production of nuclear weapons. Grossi said the facility was "very efficient in the production of fissile material that could be directly used for the fabrication of nuclear weapons." The remarks followed inspections that confirmed Syria's undeclared nuclear program, including a North Korea-built reactor at Deir el-Zour and the recovery of approximately 73 tonnes of natural uranium metal. The Associated Press reported that the material was found in the form of fuel rods with cladding.
