# Ukraine reports new power outages as occupied Zaporizhzhia nuclear plant switches to diesel generators

[Source](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/11/25/8064551/)

(U) ZAPORIZHZHIA, UKRAINE — Russian overnight attacks on energy facilities caused new power outages in Dnipropetrovsk, Donetsk, Zaporizhzhia, Odesa and Kharkiv regions, and the Russian-occupied Zaporizhzhia Nuclear Power Plant lost external power and switched to diesel generators for the 17th time in 2026, according to Ukrainska Pravda citing Ukraine's Energy Ministry. The ministry said repairs were underway but repeat strikes and air-raid warnings were delaying restoration, with Zaporizhzhia and Dnipropetrovsk the worst affected. Rolling blackouts and industrial power restrictions were in effect across most regions on November 25, and about 9,000 consumers in Odesa and Bolhrad districts were left without power.
