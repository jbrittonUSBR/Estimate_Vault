# Iran hails US withdrawal from Iraq as ‘historic victory’

[Source](https://www.rt.com/news/646554-iran-us-irag-irgc/)

(U) Tehran, Iran — RT, a Russian state outlet, reported that Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps welcomed the US withdrawal from Iraq, after the Pentagon announced the last American troops had departed following a 23-year presence, and said the Americans should leave all other countries in the region. The IRGC said the US presence "was itself the source of insecurity, terrorism and instability in West Asia," and cited Pentagon figures of 4,419 US troops lost in Iraq. Iraqi Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi called the withdrawal the beginning of a "new phase" defined by Iraq's sovereignty, while US President Donald Trump described the involvement as "a very expensive excursion into Hell."
