# Iran’s IRGC downs US drone over Hormuz as efforts to end war remain stalled

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/21/irans-irgc-downs-us-drone-over-hormuz-as-efforts-to-end-war-remain-stalled)

(U) Tehran, Iran — The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps said through state broadcaster IRIB that a new air defence system shot down a US MQ-9 drone over the Strait of Hormuz on Saturday, calling it the 58th such downing since the war began nearly nine months ago, and later claimed an MQ-1 over Qeshm Island; the US military did not immediately comment. Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi told state media that seven Iranian conditions, including ending the war and lifting sanctions and the naval blockade on southern ports, must be met before the strait could reopen, and he ruled out sending Iran's 60 percent enriched uranium stockpile abroad. President Donald Trump said at a White House news conference that Tehran wanted a deal but not one acceptable to him, and the US Treasury announced sanctions on four Iranians it said acted as "oil trustees" to evade sanctions.
