# The Pentagon tests lasers and microwaves against drone swarms

[Source](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/the-pentagon-tests-lasers-and-microwaves-against-drone-swarms/)

(U) WHITE SANDS MISSILE RANGE, N.M. — The U.S. Army demonstrated a prototype weapon based on Epirus's Leonidas high-power microwave system at White Sands Missile Range in June, downing a swarm of 12 drones, Scientific American reported. The demonstration formed part of a broader Pentagon push into directed-energy defenses, including lasers and microwaves, following drone incursions over U.S. bases; according to the article, Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth moved to cancel the LOCUST laser program. Task Force 401, established in August 2025, selected five bases for accelerated counter-drone work, and Leonidas had previously defeated a 49-drone swarm in August 2025, the report said. The article also cited incidents involving Customs and Border Protection near Fort Bliss and at Fort Hancock and concluded that no single system can defeat swarms, favoring layered defenses.
