# Israel's defence minister says Iran developing chemical weapons: Reports

[Source](https://www.straitstimes.com/world/middle-east/israels-defence-minister-says-iran-developing-chemical-weapons-reports)

(U) JERUSALEM, ISRAEL — Israeli Defence Minister Israel Katz said on November 24 that Iran was developing chemical weapons but had not yet reached the capability to strike Israel, and vowed Israel would not allow it, per The Straits Times. Katz, speaking at a media briefing and in a Likud party statement, raised the possibility of a pre-emptive strike if Iran appeared ready to attack; Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi dismissed related allegations of a "dirty bomb" plot as "Iraq War-style propaganda." Caretaker Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's rival Gadi Eisenkot, tasked with forming a government, said on November 23 he saw no immediate threat requiring action before a government was formed.
