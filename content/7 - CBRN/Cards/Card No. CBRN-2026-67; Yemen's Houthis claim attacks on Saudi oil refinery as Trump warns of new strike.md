# Yemen's Houthis claim attacks on Saudi oil refinery as Trump warns of new strikes next year

[Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/trump-iran-war-hormuz-saudi-.html)

(U) Rabigh, Saudi Arabia — The Iran-aligned Houthis said on Sunday they had directly hit the Rabigh oil refinery on Saudi Arabia's Red Sea coast, while the Saudi-led coalition said it had intercepted a Houthi drone headed toward the kingdom's western region; CNBC noted the extent of the damage was unclear and Saudi authorities did not immediately comment. Bahrain said an Iranian attack on a civilian industrial facility injured two people on Saturday, and the UK Maritime Trade Operations reported a tanker incident in the Gulf of Oman after two tankers were struck by unknown projectiles the previous day. President Donald Trump told Fox News he was inclined to order strikes on Tehran next year if no agreement was reached, while Iranian Deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi said after two hours of talks with Qatari mediators that another chance for diplomacy was being examined.
