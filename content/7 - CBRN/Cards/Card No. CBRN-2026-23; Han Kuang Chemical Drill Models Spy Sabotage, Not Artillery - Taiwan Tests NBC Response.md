(U) During Taiwan's annual live-fire **Han Kuang military exercises**, the military conducted a specialized nuclear, biological, and chemical (NBC) decontamination drill in Hualien County.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Scenario:** Rather than modeling a traditional artillery strike, the drill simulated an asymmetric threat where enemy (Chinese) spies successfully infiltrated and released toxic gases around multiple military positions. The objective was to delay eastern-based Taiwanese troops from reinforcing units defending the northern part of the main island.
- **Decontamination Response:** Troops from the Army's Chemical Group set up temporary field decontamination stations within 30 minutes of receiving orders. Wearing full NBC protective gear and respirators, soldiers thoroughly cleaned armored vehicles and tanks using hot water, decontamination powder, and specialized cleansing pits.
- **Rapid Turnaround:** Each armored vehicle completed the rigorous chemical scrubbing and safety check process using detection instruments in roughly 8 to 10 minutes.
- **Broader Context:** The Han Kuang exercises are Taiwan's largest annual war games, designed to test total combat readiness, reserve mobilization, and defense responses against potential invasion scenarios amid persistent cross-Strait tensions.
