# Flagship Pioneering Awarded First-of-Its-Kind Department of War Contract for Next-Generation Medical Countermeasures Against Biological Threats

[Source](https://www.morningstar.com/news/pr-newswire/20260923ne54165/flagship-pioneering-awarded-first-of-its-kind-contract-by-department-of-war-to-advance-next-generation-medical-countermeasure-technologies-against-evolving-biological-threats)

(U) CAMBRIDGE, Mass. — Flagship Pioneering has been awarded a first-of-its-kind contract by the Department of War under the ONTARGET (Operationalizing Novel Technologies for Advanced Rapid Generation of Effective Medical Countermeasures) program, valued at up to $47 million over five years. Administered by the Joint Science and Technology Office within DoW's Chemical and Biological Defense Program, the agreement establishes a new model under which DoW evaluates a broad range of platform technologies across Flagship's ecosystem of more than 40 companies rather than contracting for a specific product. Initial projects involve Flagship-founded Apriori Bio, applying its AI-enabled Octavia platform to design variant-resilient, broadly protective countermeasures, and Prologue Medicines, using its DELVE platform to mine viral proteins for immunomodulatory functions. The program targets broad-spectrum countermeasures, precision delivery, toxin-neutralizing platforms, novel immune enhancement, and AI/ML integration to accelerate development timelines against adaptive and engineered biological threats.
