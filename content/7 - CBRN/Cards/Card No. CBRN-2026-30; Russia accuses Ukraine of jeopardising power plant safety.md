# Russia accuses Ukraine of jeopardising power plant safety

[Source](https://www.rte.ie/news/2026/0913/1591343-ukraine-russia/)

(U) ZAPORIZHZHIA, Ukraine — The head of Russia's state nuclear energy corporation, Alexei Likhachev, accused Ukrainian forces of launching a series of combined strikes on diesel fuel trucks near the Russian-held Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant, killing two Russian servicemen and injuring others. Likhachev said the Friday strikes hit close to the perimeter of the station's site and deliberately jeopardized safety at the plant, Europe's largest with six reactors. Each side has regularly accused the other of endangering safety at the facility, which Russian forces seized in the early weeks of the war that began in February 2022. The UN's International Atomic Energy Agency maintains a permanent observer team at the site and has repeatedly mediated disputes, including arranging a local ceasefire last month to restore external power links.
