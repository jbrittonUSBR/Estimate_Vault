# Iran’s secret underground nuclear site is busier than ever: Satellite images reveal major construction surge at Pickaxe Mountain amid US war

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/defence/irans-secret-underground-nuclear-site-is-busier-than-ever-satellite-images-reveal-major-construction-surge-at-pickaxe-mountain-amid-us-war/articleshow/133976419.cms)

(U) NATANZ, Iran — A Center for Strategic and International Studies (CSIS) analysis of six years of satellite imagery found that construction activity at Iran's fortified Pickaxe Mountain site reached its highest level since work began, shifting from large-scale excavation toward internal construction and reinforcement of tunnel entrances. The deeply buried complex lies roughly two kilometers south of the Natanz uranium enrichment facility, and the CSIS analysis did not confirm or disprove reports that centrifuges or components had been moved inside. The International Atomic Energy Agency has sought access to the site without success, and CSIS analysts assessed that even the U.S. GBU-57 Massive Ordnance Penetrator may struggle against a facility buried so deep beneath granite.
