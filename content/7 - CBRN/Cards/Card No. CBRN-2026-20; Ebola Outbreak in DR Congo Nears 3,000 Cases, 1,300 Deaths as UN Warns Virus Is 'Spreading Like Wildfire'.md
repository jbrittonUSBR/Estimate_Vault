(U) The Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC) has escalated rapidly, approaching 3,000 recorded cases and a death toll exceeding 1,300. United Nations agencies and health officials have warned that the virus is spreading "like wildfire" across several provinces, making it one of the fastest-growing epidemics on record. Containment efforts have been severely complicated by regional armed conflict, community mistrust, fragile healthcare infrastructure, and the specific strain driving the outbreak, which lacks the readily available vaccines and treatments used in prior outbreaks.
