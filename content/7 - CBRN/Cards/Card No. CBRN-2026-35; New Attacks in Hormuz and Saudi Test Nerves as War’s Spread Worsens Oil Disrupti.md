# New Attacks in Hormuz and Saudi Test Nerves as War’s Spread Worsens Oil Disruption

[Source](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/09/14/884921.htm)

(U) RIYADH, Saudi Arabia — New strikes on Saudi Arabia and on ships in the Gulf tested regional nerves after an attack shut the kingdom's East-West pipeline and Houthi advances threatened to worsen wartime disruption to global energy supplies, Reuters reported. The British maritime security agency UKMTO said a vessel was struck by a projectile as it moved through the Strait of Hormuz, causing a fire and forcing the crew to evacuate, while Saudi state media showed damage to homes and a mosque in Jazan province from what it described as a cross-border Houthi attack. Traders told Reuters that Riyadh had enough oil stored at Yanbu to sustain exports for only five to seven days if the 1,200-kilometer pipeline remained shut, and US retail diesel hit a record above $6.20 a gallon.
