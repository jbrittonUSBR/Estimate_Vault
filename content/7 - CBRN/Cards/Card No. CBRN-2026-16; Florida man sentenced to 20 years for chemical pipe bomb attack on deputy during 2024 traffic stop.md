(U) Charles Legault was sentenced to 20 years in prison after pleading no contest to multiple charges stemming from an April 5, 2024, traffic stop in Putnam County, Florida. During the stop, a deputy searching Legault’s truck found drugs and a handgun. A PVC pipe bomb filled with chlorine and brake fluid then exploded in the deputy’s face, causing respiratory injuries that required hospital treatment. Body-camera video captured the incident. Legault was also found with a suppressor, loaded magazines, ammunition, and a large amount of methamphetamine. Charges included making/possessing/discharging a destructive device that caused bodily harm, carrying a concealed firearm during a felony, armed trafficking in meth, and related drug offenses. A subsequent search of his home turned up additional illegal drugs but no other bombs.
