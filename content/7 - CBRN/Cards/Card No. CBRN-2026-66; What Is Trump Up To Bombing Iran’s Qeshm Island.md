# What Is Trump Up To Bombing Iran’s Qeshm Island?

[Source](https://oilprice.com/Geopolitics/Middle-East/What-Is-Trump-Up-To-Bombing-Irans-Qeshm-Island.html)

(U) Qeshm Island, Iran — In a November 23 commentary, OilPrice.com analyst Simon Watkins argued that recent US airstrikes on Qeshm Island may be intended to open the Strait of Hormuz so President Donald Trump could wind down the Iran war after Republicans lost both chambers of Congress in the 2026 midterms. Watkins, citing unnamed US and EU energy-security sources, noted that Qeshm hosts much of the IRGC's anti-ship missiles, drones and fast-attack boats, and quoted one source assessing that clearing the strait would let Trump declare victory while leaving a carrier group to deter re-closure. The piece, which is analysis rather than independently verified reporting, also recalled Trump's March threat to "obliterate" Iranian infrastructure if the strait did not fully reopen.
