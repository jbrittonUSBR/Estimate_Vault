(U) A suspicious substance discovered in the mailroom of the Los Angeles Police Department (LAPD) headquarters triggered a targeted hazardous materials response and temporary facility restrictions.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **The Discovery:** The suspicious material was found inside the mailroom at the main LAPD headquarters in downtown Los Angeles (located near First and Main streets).
- **Emergency Response:** Following the discovery, authorities cordoned off the area and deployed a specialized hazardous materials (HazMat) response team to evaluate and test the substance safely.
- **Safety Protocols:** Part of the facility or processing areas were restricted or temporarily closed while first responders conducted their investigation and protocol checks to ensure the safety of personnel and the public.
