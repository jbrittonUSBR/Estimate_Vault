# Houthis Claim 30 Killed in Yemen Market Strike as Government Disputes Responsibility

[Source](https://www.naturalnews.com/2026-11-09-houthis-claim-30-killed-in-yemen-market-strike.html)

(U) Taiz province, Yemen — Natural News reports that the Houthis said Saudi airstrikes killed 30 people and wounded eight at a market in Al-Waziya, while Taiz authorities and Yemen’s internationally recognized government denied an air raid and blamed Houthi missile fire. The outlet states that neither the casualty toll nor responsibility had been independently verified at publication, leaving the account of the November 6 strike contested.
