# Times of India reported Houthi missile attack on Yemen presidential complex

(U) ADEN, Yemen — The Times of India reported that Houthi forces fired several missiles at a presidential palace complex in Aden, with witnesses reporting loud booms and the Associated Press reporting multiple explosions. The paper said two government officials told Reuters that Yemen's prime minister and ministers at the complex were unharmed, while some reports claimed two security guards were killed. Casualty details and other information remained attributed to sources cited by the outlet and were not independently confirmed.
