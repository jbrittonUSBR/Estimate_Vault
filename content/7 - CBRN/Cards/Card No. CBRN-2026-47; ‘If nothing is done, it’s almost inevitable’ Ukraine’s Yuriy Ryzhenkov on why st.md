# ‘If nothing is done, it’s almost inevitable’: Ukraine’s Yuriy Ryzhenkov on why steelmaking is on the brink of collapse as Russian bombing intensifies

[Source](https://fortune.com/2026/10/12/ukraine-yuriy-ryzhenkov-metinvest-steelmaking-brink-collapse-russian-bombing-intensifies/)

(U) KYIV, Ukraine — In an interview with Fortune, Metinvest CEO Yuriy Ryzhenkov warned that Ukraine's steelmaking industry is on the brink of collapse as intensified Russian bombing, port blockades, and steep EU tariff cuts squeeze operations. He noted repeated attacks on facilities, heavy personnel losses, and falling production, and said "if nothing is done, it's almost inevitable" that the sector could end without continued market access and support.
