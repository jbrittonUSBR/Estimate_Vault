(U) Türkiye has fielded the PUHU-KBRN unmanned aerial system developed by Zyrone for remote chemical, biological, radiological, and nuclear decontamination missions, reducing the need for personnel to enter hazardous areas. The system, in service with the Turkish armed forces since 2025 and first publicly demonstrated during Exercise ‘EFES-2026’ in Izmir in late May 2026, can perform liquid and powder-based decontamination of horizontal surfaces up to 200–250 m² from a stand-off distance of 10 km, vertical surfaces such as vehicles and buildings from 2–3 m, and personnel from up to 15 km. It carries a 25-litre decontamination liquid payload, has a maximum takeoff weight under 75 kg and payload capacity of 30 kg, and can also be configured for cargo transport. The UAS enables safer and more efficient response operations in contaminated environments by supporting cleaning of personnel, vehicles, and infrastructure without direct human exposure.
