# This Color-Changing Spray Could Reveal Invisible Biological Threats - iHLS

[Source](https://i-hls.com/archives/138753)

(U) Columbus, USA — iHLS reported that the Battelle Memorial Institute, a private nonprofit applied science and technology organization based in Ohio, developed a spray-based system designed to detect biological threats in military environments by changing colour when it encounters a hazardous agent. The technology relies on a proprietary formulation that binds to targeted threats and produces a visible colour change visible to the naked eye, and can be adapted to detect specific agents rather than merely indicating general contamination. The liquid was developed in response to requirements from the US Defense Threat Reduction Agency and was first showcased at a defence event at Camp Dawson, West Virginia, earlier in the year.
