# Houthis seize 2 strategic Red Sea islands, and other Mideast developments

[Source](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/houthis-seize-2-strategic-red-sea-islands-mideast-136416872)

(U) CAIRO, Egypt — Yemen's Houthi rebels seized the strategic islands of Greater and Lesser Hanish in the southern Red Sea, strengthening the Iran-backed group's ability to control a key maritime shipping route, according to Houthi officials cited by The Associated Press. The AP reported that the takeover, following the seizure of the Red Sea port of Mokha and a strategic island the previous week, came as Saudi-backed government forces tried to recover territory around the Bab el-Mandeb Strait and as nearly 94,000 Yemenis fled their homes this month, according to the International Organization for Migration. Two regional officials said repairs to the Saudi East-West pipeline struck on September 10 could take three to five weeks.
