# Iran War: Europe Oil and US Diesel Crunch Worsens; Tucker Exposes Massive Trump/Israel Bombing Campaign Right After Midterms; Experts Question Survival of Regimes in Saudi Arabia, Jordan

[Source](https://www.nakedcapitalism.com/2026/09/iran-war-europe-oil-and-us-diesel-crunch-worsens-tucker-exposes-massive-trump-israel-bombing-campaign-right-after-midterms-experts-question-survival-of-regimes-in-saudi-arabia-jordsn.html)

(U) NEW YORK, United States — In a naked capitalism analysis post, Yves Smith wrote that the energy crunch from the Iran war was arriving on both the oil and diesel fronts, citing reports that Saudi Aramco had cancelled all of its September European crude allocations and had no cargoes slated for Europe until November after Ansar Allah interrupted Saudi petroleum exports. Smith cited figures showing US diesel prices at a record $6.26 a gallon, up more than 80 percent in nine months, and Strait of Hormuz transits falling to four vessels against a 10-day average of 18, while noting that Senate Majority Leader John Thune was open to considering a diesel export ban. She also reproduced in full a post by Tucker Carlson, attributed to an informed federal official, alleging the administration planned to send Israel 40,000 2,000-pound bombs in the coming months. The item is an opinionated aggregation and commentary post rather than original reporting.
