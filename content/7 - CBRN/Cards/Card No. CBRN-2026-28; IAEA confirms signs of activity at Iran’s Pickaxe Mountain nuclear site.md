# IAEA confirms signs of activity at Iran’s Pickaxe Mountain nuclear site

[Source](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/433019)

(U) JERUSALEM, Israel — The International Atomic Energy Agency detected construction activity at Iran's heavily fortified Pickaxe Mountain facility, IAEA Director General Rafael Grossi said, according to Israel National News. Speaking to Bloomberg TV, Grossi said remote imagery showed indications of movement around the site though inspectors have not entered the tunnel complex. The comments followed the IAEA Board of Governors' referral of Iran's nuclear file to the UN Security Council, and President Donald Trump warned Iran against conducting nuclear activity at the site, saying the United States "will have to hit them very hard."
