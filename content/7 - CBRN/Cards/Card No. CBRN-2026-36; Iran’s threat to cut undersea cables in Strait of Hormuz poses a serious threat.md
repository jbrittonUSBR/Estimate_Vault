# Iran’s threat to cut undersea cables in Strait of Hormuz poses a serious threat to Gulf states

[Source](https://theconversation.com/irans-threat-to-cut-undersea-cables-in-strait-of-hormuz-poses-a-serious-threat-to-gulf-states-291618)

(U) CAMBRIDGE, United Kingdom — In a commentary published by The Conversation, John Aitken of RAND Europe argued that Iran's reported threat to sever undersea cables in the Strait of Hormuz poses a serious risk to Gulf states. Aitken cited an August 16 Wall Street Journal report that Iran was weighing attacks on the region's submarine cables, which he said carry more than 95 percent of international data traffic. He noted that the Gulf is shallow, averaging about 50 meters and never exceeding roughly 100 meters, and he described Iranian mine-clearing remotely operated vehicles as a potential means of cutting the cables. The piece, which is analysis rather than a report of a new incident, also weighed deterrence options available to Gulf states and their partners.
