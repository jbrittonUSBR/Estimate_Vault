(U) Ukraine’s military intelligence agency (HUR) has warned that Russian forces in occupied parts of the Kherson region are improperly disposing of anthrax-infected livestock by burying carcasses in poorly secured animal burial grounds without proper sanitary measures, creating conditions for a potential biological incident or false-flag operation. Dozens of sites, including high-risk locations near Askania-Nova, Skadovsk, and Zaliznyi Port, are situated close to roads and populated areas—some less than one kilometer from settlements—lack fencing or biosecurity, and in some cases are in areas with high groundwater levels, allowing long-viable anthrax spores to contaminate soil, water, agriculture, and healthy animals. HUR described the situation as a form of “biological terrorism” and cautioned that Russia could deliberately damage the sites while launching an information campaign to accuse Ukraine of using biological weapons. The agency reported the findings on June 23, 2026, with Russia offering no immediate response to the allegations.
