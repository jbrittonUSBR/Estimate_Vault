# UK bans all imports from Israeli settlements in West Bank, cites 'settler terrorists;' France, Canada to follow

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/world-news/uk-bans-all-imports-from-israeli-settlements-in-west-bank-cites-settler-terrorists-france-canada-to-follow/articleshow/133925941.cms)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — The United Kingdom announced on September 8, 2026 that it would ban trade with Israeli settlements in the occupied West Bank, with Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband telling the House of Commons that imports of all goods from the settlements would cease. The ban also covered a range of services linked to the settlements, including financing, construction, infrastructure, real estate, and advertising. Miliband said the government was acting to preserve the possibility of a two-state solution, and officials stated that France and Canada would follow with bans of their own.
