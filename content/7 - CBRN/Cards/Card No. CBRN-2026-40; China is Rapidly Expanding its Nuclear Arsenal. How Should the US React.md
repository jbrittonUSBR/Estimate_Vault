# China is Rapidly Expanding its Nuclear Arsenal. How Should the US React?

[Source](https://inkstickmedia.com/china-nuclear/)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — An analysis piece by Hawaii-based journalist Jon Letman, published by Inkstick Media on September 21, 2026, examines China's rapid nuclear expansion ahead of an expected White House visit by Xi Jinping on September 24 and asks how the United States should respond. The article reports that China's arsenal has grown from an estimated 300 warheads around 2020 to roughly 620, citing the disclosure of three new ICBM silo fields, while Russia and the US still hold more than 4,400 and 3,700 warheads respectively. It contrasts a 2025 Hudson Institute report predicting China could become a nuclear peer by the early-to-mid 2030s with analysts from the Arms Control Association and the Federation of American Scientists who say parity is uncertain and perhaps unlikely. The piece argues that arms control and diplomacy are under severe strain, with no formal high-level US-China negotiations underway, and quotes experts urging caution against a reactive US buildup.
