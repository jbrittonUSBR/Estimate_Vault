# Kiev strikes emergency fuel convoy to Europe’s biggest nuclear plant – Rosatom

[Source](https://www.rt.com/russia/645619-zaporozhye-emergency-fuel-convoy/)

(U) MOSCOW, Russia — RT, Russia's state-controlled broadcaster, reported that Rosatom CEO Aleksey Likhachev accused Ukraine of striking tankers carrying emergency diesel fuel to the Zaporozhye Nuclear Power Plant, killing two Russian servicemen and wounding multiple others. Likhachev said the strikes occurred near the plant's perimeter and called on the IAEA to condemn them; the account is a Russian state-media claim and could not be independently verified. RT cited IAEA warnings that the plant's diesel reserves had been critically depleted during a recent blackout.
