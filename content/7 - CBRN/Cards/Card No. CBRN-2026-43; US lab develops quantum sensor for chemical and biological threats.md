# US lab develops quantum sensor for chemical and biological threats

[Source](https://interestingengineering.com/innovation/quantum-sensor-chemical-biological-threat-detection)

(U) LAUREL, Md. — Scientists at Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory said they are developing a quantum sensing platform that could shrink nuclear magnetic resonance spectroscopy from lab-scale equipment to a device roughly the size of a laptop for field detection of chemical and biological threats. The approach uses nitrogen-vacancy centers in diamond and proposed "nano-containers" to improve sensitivity, with simulations suggesting roughly an order-of-magnitude gain. Researchers cautioned that the work has not yet demonstrated field-ready chemical or biological identification.
