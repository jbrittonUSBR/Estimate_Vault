# AI’s greatest threat is biological, not robotic

[Source](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/commentary/2026/09/23/world/ais-greatest-threat-biological/)

(U) TOKYO — In a commentary published by The Japan Times and syndicated from Bloomberg, Gautam Mukunda argued that AI's most serious security risk is biological rather than robotic, pointing to an Institute for Security and Technology survey in which 70% of 111 national security practitioners said AI increases or soon will increase bioweapon risk. He contended that AI is eroding hands-on biological expertise and lowering the skill and experience needed to manipulate pathogens. The piece is analysis and opinion, not a report of a new incident.
