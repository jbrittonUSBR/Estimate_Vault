# Your smart home devices are vulnerable to cyberattack — protect yourself with these tips

[Source](https://www.theblaze.com/tech/iot-cyberattacks)

(U) No specific location — Zach Laidlaw warns that internet-connected household devices can create pathways into a home network and describes possible privacy and safety consequences, including unauthorized access to cameras, locks, and appliances. He recommends keeping device software updated, placing IoT devices on a separate guest network where available, disabling remote router management, and disconnecting devices that are not in use.
