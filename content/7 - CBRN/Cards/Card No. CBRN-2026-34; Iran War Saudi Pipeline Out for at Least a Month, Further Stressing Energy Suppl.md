# Iran War: Saudi Pipeline Out for at Least a Month, Further Stressing Energy Supplies; Trump + Israel Readying Post-Midterms Big Attack? Iran Weighing NPT Exit

[Source](https://www.nakedcapitalism.com/2026/09/iran-war-saudi-pipeline-out-for-at-least-a-month-further-stressing-energy-supplies-trump-israel-readying-post-midterms-big-attack-iran-weighing-npt-exit.html)

(U) NEW YORK, United States — In a naked capitalism analysis post, Yves Smith wrote that Ansar Allah had seized roughly 2,000 square kilometers of Yemeni territory, including the port city of Mokha and Perim Island, and had damaged Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline badly enough to force its closure, with repairs taking as long as six weeks. Smith cited satellite imagery showing destruction at a pipeline pumping station and argued the group could disable the line again once it was restored, while also flagging reporting that President Donald Trump and Israel might be preparing a post-midterms strike and that Iran was weighing withdrawal from the Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty.
