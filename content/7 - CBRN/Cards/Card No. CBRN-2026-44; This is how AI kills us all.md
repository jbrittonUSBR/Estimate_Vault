# This is how AI kills us all

[Source](https://globeopinion.substack.com/p/this-is-how-ai-kills-us-all)

(U) Boston, USA — In a paid opinion essay for Globe Opinion, author Annie Jacobsen argued that the most concrete danger posed by artificial intelligence is not that it will invent a novel deadly virus but that it could enable existing lethal pathogens to be used as weapons. Writing after AI researcher Jacob Coxon quit Anthropic and publicly warned that the technology could "kill us all," Jacobsen drew on her research into biological weapons, including her book "Biological War: A Scenario," to examine how AI might facilitate a mass-casualty biological attack. The piece is an analytical commentary rather than a report of a specific incident.
