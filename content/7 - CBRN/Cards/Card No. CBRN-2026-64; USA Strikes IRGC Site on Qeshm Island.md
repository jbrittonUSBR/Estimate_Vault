# USA Strikes IRGC Site on Qeshm Island

[Source](https://www.rigzone.com/news/wire/usa_strikes_irgc_site_on_qeshm_island-18-nov-2026-185116-article/)

(U) Qeshm Island, Iran — U.S. warplanes struck an Iranian missile launch site at an Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps facility late Tuesday, a U.S. military official told Bloomberg, as reported by Rigzone. The official said the strike responded to earlier Iranian targeting of U.S. personnel and assets; Iranian media reported no casualties or damage to critical or public-service infrastructure.
