# From mountain fighters to Red Sea power: How the Houthis rose to control Bab el-Mandeb

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/world-news/from-mountain-fighters-to-red-sea-power-how-the-houthis-rose-to-control-bab-el-mandeb/articleshow/134307243.cms)

(U) LONDON — A Reuters explainer traced the Houthis' rise from a Zaidi revivalist movement to a Red Sea power controlling much of Yemen, including the Bab el-Mandeb chokepoint. Since seizing Sanaa in 2014, the group has grown to an estimated 350,000 fighters per a UN panel, acquired missiles with a 1,800 km reach, and received support from Iran and Hezbollah. Their attacks on shipping and ability to threaten regional oil flows have drawn repeated U.S. and allied strikes, including operations following February 28 attacks on Iran-linked targets.
