(U) Russian journalist Grigory Nekhoroshev, 69, who lived in exile in Riga, Latvia, for 11 years after exposing Vladimir Putin’s secret relationship with Olympic gymnast Alina Kabaeva in 2008, died on June 20, 2026, after eating poisonous mushrooms from his yard. As editor-in-chief of Moskovsky Korrespondent, Nekhoroshev reported that Putin planned to divorce his wife Lyudmila to marry Kabaeva, leading to the newspaper’s closure, threats from Russian secret services, and his flight abroad as a political refugee; he had long feared assassination linked to the Kremlin. Friends described him as healthy and full of ideas, with an autopsy planned to determine the exact circumstances, though the death was reported as accidental mushroom poisoning. The 2008 story, initially denied by Putin and Kabaeva, is now widely accepted, including confirmation that the couple have two children together.
