# CI Sector Brief 2026-10-29

DATAMINR ALERT REPORT

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Bottom line up front, Dataminr alerts indicate a cluster of urgent US critical infrastructure and public safety disruptions on 29 October, led by a countywide school lockdown in Meriwether County, Georgia, a resolved lockdown scare at West Florida High School in Pensacola, Florida, and multiple notable power outages in Massachusetts, Florida, Texas, Ohio, Wisconsin, Arkansas, Washington, and Georgia, with immediate significance for school security posture, utility continuity, and localized emergency response.

## DETAIL

In Meriwether County, Georgia, reporting shows a developing school security incident that escalated from a large armed police response at Greenville High School to broader countywide lockdown measures.¹²³ Local reporting first described a large armed police response at Greenville High School, followed by reports that the school was on lockdown and that police had blocked entrances to the campus.¹²⁴ The Meriwether County School System then said all schools in the county were on lockdown and secured by law enforcement, while the sheriff's office said a threat involving county schools had prompted the lockdown.⁵⁶ Subsequent local reporting said an escaped prisoner reportedly prompted lockdowns at Greenville High School and other county schools, and later updates described heavy police activity and helicopters overhead after the incident, as well as a separate reported lockdown at Manchester High School.⁷⁸⁹

At West Florida High School in Pensacola, Florida, early eyewitness reporting described a lockdown tied to an unauthorized person on campus, an armed suspect, and a shooter reportedly prompting the lockdown, with one report saying students were told to run to the closest classroom as fast as possible.¹⁰¹¹¹²¹³¹⁴ Later reporting said the principal reportedly told students that a student armed with a gun had prompted the lockdown, and a separate post described an active shooter situation.¹⁵¹⁶ Official follow on reporting from the Escambia County Sheriff's Office said the lockdown was caused by a bomb threat originating in New York and posing no local danger, and later said there was no indication of anything at all after deputies responded to a caller report of an active shooter.¹⁷¹⁸

Utility disruption reporting indicates several geographically dispersed but locally significant outages.¹⁹²⁰²¹²²²³²⁴²⁵²⁶ National Grid reported more than 5,000 customers without power across 23 counties and two states, with more than 4,500 affected in Plymouth, Massachusetts, while separate corporate reporting cited 3,371 affected customers in Hanover, Pembroke, and Hanson.¹⁹²⁰²¹ SWEPCO reported more than 2,000 customers affected across 18 counties and three states, with main impact in Texas and more than 1,000 affected in Gregg County.²²²³ Jacksonville Electric Authority reported outages affecting more than 3,000 customers in Jacksonville and Duval County, Florida, while Georgia Power reported more than 2,000 affected customers in Fulton County, Georgia.²⁴²⁵²⁶ Additional outage reporting showed more than 3,500 Cleveland Public Power customers affected in Cleveland and Cuyahoga County, more than 3,000 East Central Energy customers affected across five counties and two states with main impact in Wisconsin, more than 1,500 affected in Douglas, Wisconsin, more than 1,500 Carroll Electric customers affected in Benton, Arkansas, more than 1,500 affected across five counties and two states with main impact in Arkansas, more than 2,000 FirstEnergy customers affected across 45 counties and five states, and more than 1,000 Puget Sound Energy customers affected in Duvall, Washington.²⁷²⁸²⁹³⁰³¹³²³³³⁴

Hazardous materials and gas related incidents were also reported across multiple jurisdictions.³⁵³⁶³⁷³⁸³⁹⁴⁰⁴¹⁴² In Gainesville, Florida, the University of Florida first warned of a gas leak at the Biomedical Sciences Building and advised people to avoid the area or shelter in place if nearby, then said the fire department was responding, before local news reported no gas leak was found and normal operations resumed.³⁵³⁶³⁷ In Miami, emergency responders said a hazmat team investigated a gas leak at a strip mall on West Flagler Street and evacuated all employees, while scanner traffic separately reported a gas leak at the same block in Coral Gables.³⁸³⁹ Scanner reporting also cited a struck gas main in Baraboo, Wisconsin, a gas line struck in West Chester Township, Ohio, a gas odor on Harris Street in Rochester, New York, and hazmat incidents in Staten Island and on West 4th Street in New York City.⁴⁰⁴¹⁴²⁴³⁴⁴

Other public safety reporting included an uncontained hazmat incident on South McLean Boulevard in Wichita, Kansas, followed later by a reported commercial fire at the same location.⁴⁵⁴⁶

## IMPACT

The most immediate operational shift is in Meriwether County, where reporting indicates a countywide school security posture supported by law enforcement rather than an isolated single campus event.⁵⁶ Multiple reports indicate the incident at Greenville High School had spillover effects across other schools in the county, which would support decisions to maintain close coordination with school administrators, law enforcement, and transportation officials until the triggering threat is clarified.¹²⁵⁶⁷⁸⁹

In Pensacola, the reporting shows how an initially severe school threat picture can narrow quickly once official information emerges.¹⁰¹¹¹²¹³¹⁴¹⁵¹⁶¹⁷¹⁸ The official explanation of a bomb threat posing no local danger, combined with the sheriff's later statement that deputies found no indication of an active incident, suggests the immediate life safety risk at West Florida High School likely decreased materially after the first wave of eyewitness reports.¹⁷¹⁸ This supports decisions to distinguish between early unverified school threat reporting and later official clarification before expanding protective actions beyond the campus.

The outage reporting points to a broad but fragmented utility disruption picture rather than a single common cause.¹⁹²⁰²¹²²²³²⁴²⁵²⁶²⁷²⁸²⁹³⁰³¹³²³³³⁴ For operators, this supports prioritizing local consequence management, especially where outages overlap with schools, traffic control, or emergency response demand, rather than treating the outages as one coordinated event. It is possible that some outage totals reflect overlapping utility map snapshots rather than wholly separate incidents, so exact aggregate customer impact remains uncertain.

The gas leak and hazmat reporting indicates continued demand on local fire and hazardous materials resources across several states, but the Gainesville case also shows that some incidents may resolve as false alarms or non confirmed leaks after initial protective messaging.³⁵³⁶³⁷³⁸³⁹⁴⁰⁴¹⁴²⁴³⁴⁴⁴⁵⁴⁶ This supports decisions to monitor for secondary effects such as evacuations, shelter in place orders, road closures, and business interruptions rather than assuming all reported leaks will escalate.

Outlook should focus on three indicators. First, any official statement identifying the specific threat driver in Meriwether County, including whether the reported escaped prisoner link is confirmed or refuted, would determine whether lockdown measures are likely to persist or unwind. Second, restoration updates from National Grid, JEA, SWEPCO, Georgia Power, Cleveland Public Power, East Central Energy, Carroll Electric, FirstEnergy, and PSE would show whether outages are stabilizing or expanding. Third, any escalation from scanner or responder reporting into confirmed evacuations, injuries, or extended closures would confirm that the current hazard pattern is moving from localized disruption toward broader operational impact.
