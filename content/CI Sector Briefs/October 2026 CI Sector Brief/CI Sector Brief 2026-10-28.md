# CI Sector Brief 2026-10-28

DATAMINR ALERT REPORT

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Bottom line up front: Dataminr reporting indicates a broad critical infrastructure picture dominated by dispersed power outages across the United States and Canada, alongside localized gas line, fuel spill, lockdown, and protest activity, with all reporting at URGENT priority and no FLASH alerts present. The most immediate significance is the simultaneous strain on utility service continuity in multiple regions, especially New England, Georgia, and several county level outage clusters, while separate public safety incidents at schools, roadways, and commercial sites could create localized operational disruptions.

DETAIL

Utility disruptions are the dominant theme, with outage reporting spanning New England, the Southeast, Midwest, Mountain West, Pacific Coast, and Canada across multiple providers and service territories. In New England, Eversource reported more than 10,000 affected customers regionwide, including 9,943 customers in Merrimack, Amherst, Nashua, and Hollis, New Hampshire, while Bar Harbor, Maine remained affected with 1,113 customers out and one update attributing that outage to distribution maintenance¹˒²˒⁶˒⁸⁷. Additional northeastern utility impacts included 1,789 customers out in South Burlington, Vermont and more than 1,000 Orange and Rockland customers affected in Highland Falls, New York³˒²⁰. In Georgia, outage reporting included more than 3,000 Georgia Power customers across 54 counties, more than 1,500 in Fulton County, and more than 1,000 Flint Energies customers in Centerville and across three counties with main impact in Houston County, while a separate Citizen post claimed more than 549,000 customers affected in Berner, a figure not corroborated elsewhere in the alert set⁵⁴˒⁶⁵˒⁷⁰˒⁷¹˒⁸⁰. Other notable U.S. outages included more than 3,500 Cleveland Public Power customers in Cleveland and Cuyahoga County, more than 2,500 FirstEnergy customers in Morgantown, more than 1,500 customers in Scottsville and Allen County, Kentucky, more than 1,500 Northern Lights customers in Bonner County, Idaho, more than 3,500 Idaho Power customers in Ada County, and more than 2,500 SDG&E customers in Borrego Springs, California¹⁷˒¹⁹˒²⁸˒³⁸˒³⁹˒⁴⁰˒⁴⁸˒⁷⁶. Canadian outages were also significant, with BC Hydro reporting more than 5,000 customers affected in North Saanich and Sidney and across three regions with main impact in Vancouver Island, while Nova Scotia reporting showed more than 2,000 customers affected in Sydney and across two regions with main impact in Cape Breton, later refined to 2,122 customers in Port Morien, Donkin, and other Cape Breton areas³²˒³³˒³⁴˒³⁵˒⁵⁸.

Several alerts pointed to localized infrastructure hazards involving gas lines, gas odors, fuel spills, and hazmat conditions across multiple states and Mexico⁷˒⁸˒⁹˒¹²˒¹³˒²¹˒²²˒²³˒²⁷˒⁴¹˒⁴⁴˒⁴⁹˒⁵⁰˒⁵⁹˒⁶⁰˒⁶¹˒⁷⁴˒⁷⁸. Earlier in the day, Salem, New Hampshire authorities reported a shelter in place order had been lifted after a suspect reportedly fled following an armed robbery⁶⁸.

IMPACT

The reporting indicates a multi node utility disruption environment rather than a single cascading grid event, with the clearest concentration of outage activity in New England, Georgia, parts of the Midwest, and scattered county level service territories across the United States and Canada¹˒²˒³˒⁶˒¹⁷˒¹⁹˒²⁰˒²⁸˒³²˒³³˒³⁴˒³⁵˒⁵⁴˒⁵⁸˒⁶⁵˒⁷⁰˒⁷¹˒⁸⁰˒⁸⁷. Multiple reports indicate that most impacts remain localized by provider footprint, but the volume and spread of outages could still complicate restoration prioritization, customer communications, and mutual aid allocation where several utilities are managing concurrent incidents. The New England cluster is especially notable because regional and local Eversource reporting align on a broad service impact with specific concentrations in New Hampshire and Maine¹˒²˒⁶˒⁸⁷.

The gas line, gas odor, spill, and hazmat alerts suggest a steady background of localized response demands that can disrupt roads, commercial sites, schools, and nearby utilities even when customer outage counts are not yet reported⁷˒⁸˒⁹˒¹²˒¹³˒²¹˒²²˒²³˒²⁴˒²⁷˒⁴¹˒⁴²˒⁴⁴˒⁴⁹˒⁵⁹˒⁶⁰˒⁶¹˒⁷⁸˒⁸⁸. These incidents support decisions around route monitoring, field crew safety posture, and coordination with local fire and law enforcement for perimeter control and access restrictions. The school related lockdown reporting in Arkansas and Ohio, along with the lockdown in Columbia, points to a separate but relevant operational concern for facilities and transportation stakeholders because even precautionary actions can interrupt normal movement and service delivery⁴˒¹⁴˒⁵¹˒⁵²˒⁵⁷.

Planned demonstrations are unlikely to create systemwide infrastructure effects on their own, but they do provide actionable notice for site security, traffic management, and continuity planning near government buildings, hospitals, schools, and major intersections¹⁰˒¹⁵˒²⁹˒⁴⁶˒⁴⁷˒⁵⁵˒⁶²˒⁶⁴˒⁶⁶˒⁶⁷˒⁶⁹˒⁷²˒⁷³˒⁷⁵˒⁷⁷˒⁷⁹˒⁸¹˒⁸⁴. The Mexico reporting also shows how prolonged service issues can translate into protest and traffic disruption, as seen in Las Juntitas after a month long drinking water outage⁸². That pattern is relevant for operators because unresolved utility interruptions can evolve into access and reputational issues even after the immediate technical problem stabilizes.

One outage figure stands out as potentially anomalous, the Citizen post claiming more than 549,000 customers affected in Berner, Georgia, because it is far larger than the corroborated Georgia utility figures elsewhere in the reporting and lacks matching support in the alert set⁵⁴˒⁶⁵˒⁸⁰. It is possible that this figure is inaccurate, mislocated, or reflects a broader context not captured here, so it should not drive decisions without confirmation.

Outlook will depend on whether regional outage totals continue to rise, whether localized gas and spill incidents trigger evacuations or road closures, and whether school or public venue lockdowns expand beyond precautionary measures. Key indicators to watch are updated utility customer counts in New England and Georgia, official clarification of the Stuttgart and Columbus school related threats, and any evidence that planned protests shift from scheduled gatherings to roadway blockades or facility access disruptions.
