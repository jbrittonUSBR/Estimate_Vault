# CI Sector Brief 2026-11-24

Created 24 November 2026.
## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Dataminr reporting indicates a broad, multi region critical infrastructure picture dominated by dispersed power outages, localized hazardous materials and gas incidents, and a smaller set of planned protest activity across the United States, Canada, and Mexico, with the most immediate significance centered on utility disruptions affecting thousands of customers and several active public safety responses.¹

## IMPACT

The main operational shift is the breadth of concurrent utility disruption rather than a single dominant failure point, with outages spread across multiple states and provinces and several utilities reporting customer impacts above 2,000.¹˒²˒⁵˒⁶˒⁸˒¹³˒¹⁵˒¹⁷˒¹⁹˒²¹˒²³˒²⁵˒²⁷˒²⁹˒³¹˒³³˒³⁵˒³⁷ This supports near term decisions around utility coordination, customer communications, field crew prioritization, and contingency planning for facilities dependent on stable commercial power.

The hazard reporting suggests a parallel burden on local emergency response capacity, especially where gas leaks, struck lines, fuel spills, and hazmat incidents are appearing at the same time as weather or infrastructure related outages.⁴⁰˒⁴¹˒⁴³˒⁴⁴˒⁴⁵˒⁵²˒⁵³˒⁵⁴˒⁵⁵˒⁵⁶˒⁵⁷˒⁵⁸˒⁵⁹˒⁶⁰˒⁶² Multiple reports indicate that some outages may be linked to physical infrastructure damage such as downed lines, transformer failure, or pole strikes, which can slow restoration and expand safety perimeters.⁵⁸˒⁶¹˒⁶²˒⁶³˒⁶⁴

The communications disruption picture is less severe than the power picture, but the reported service issues affecting consumer platforms, internet access in Oaxaca, and taxpayer services in Puebla show that secondary digital disruption is also present.⁶⁷˒⁶⁸˒⁶⁹˒⁷⁰ This supports monitoring for knock on effects to customer service, municipal operations, and public information channels where outages overlap.

Planned demonstrations do not yet indicate a single concentrated unrest pattern, but they do create a distributed traffic and access management requirement across several cities in the coming days.⁷¹˒⁷²˒⁷³˒⁷⁴˒⁷⁵˒⁷⁶˒⁷⁷˒⁷⁸˒⁷⁹˒⁸⁰˒⁸¹˒⁸²˒⁸³˒⁸⁴˒⁸⁵˒⁸⁶ Where protests coincide with already active infrastructure or emergency incidents, localized congestion and response delays are possible.

Outlook remains fluid because many of the utility alerts are increase notices, which suggests some incidents were still expanding at the time of reporting. Confirmation of a worsening trajectory would include additional increase alerts from the same utilities, more reports of line or transformer damage, and new service disruption reports affecting telecom or municipal functions. Indicators that would refute escalation would include restoration updates similar to Barrie, declining customer counts, and the absence of follow on hazard reports around current outage clusters.²˒⁴
