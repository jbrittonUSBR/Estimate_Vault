## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Dataminr reporting indicates a broad critical infrastructure picture dominated by dispersed utility outages, localized hazardous materials and fire incidents, and a resolved but still significant hospital security event in the United States, with additional protest planning and infrastructure disruptions in Mexico and Canada at URGENT and ALERT priority levels. The most operationally significant developments are the sequence of reports from Baptist Health Floyd in New Albany, Indiana, multiple sizable power outages across California, Florida, Georgia, Wyoming, and other states, and industrial and commercial fire related incidents in La Porte, Texas and Glace Bay, Nova Scotia.

## IMPACT

The most immediate life safety concern in this reporting remains the hospital incident in New Albany because open source reporting moved from a possible active shooter to an all clear while still carrying a later report of a person reportedly shot in the head¹˒²˒³˒⁴. That combination supports continued monitoring for official casualty confirmation, access restrictions, and any renewed security posture at or around the hospital.

The outage picture suggests a distributed strain on regional utility operations rather than a single nationwide grid event. Multiple reports indicate California and Florida were the most active states for customer impacts, while Georgia, Wyoming, North Carolina, Missouri, Arizona, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, New York, and Massachusetts also saw notable service interruptions⁵˒⁶˒⁷˒⁸˒⁹˒¹⁰˒¹¹˒¹²˒¹³˒¹⁴˒¹⁵˒¹⁶˒¹⁷˒¹⁸˒¹⁹˒²⁰˒²¹˒²²˒²³˒³⁰˒³¹˒³²˒³³˒³⁴˒³⁵˒³⁶˒³⁷˒³⁸˒³⁹˒⁴⁰˒⁴¹˒⁴²˒⁴³˒⁴⁴˒⁴⁵˒⁴⁶˒⁴⁷˒⁴⁸˒⁴⁹˒⁵⁰˒⁵¹˒⁵²˒⁵³˒⁵⁴˒⁵⁵˒⁵⁶˒⁵⁷˒⁵⁸˒⁵⁹˒⁶⁰˒⁶¹˒⁶²˒⁶³˒⁶⁴˒⁶⁵˒⁶⁶˒⁶⁷˒⁶⁸˒⁶⁹˒⁷⁰˒⁷¹˒⁷²˒⁷³˒⁷⁴˒⁷⁵˒⁷⁶. For operators, this supports prioritizing continuity checks for facilities dependent on commercial power, especially where outages coincide with other hazards such as flooding, downed lines, or fire activity¹⁷˒²⁴˒²⁷˒⁸⁴˒⁸⁵.

The Clean Harbors and Glace Bay incidents raise separate concerns about secondary hazards and prolonged site disruption. In Texas, the combination of a hospitalized worker, extinguished fire, suspected hydrocarbons, and later reporting on earlier fires points to possible extended investigative and remediation activity at the facility²⁵˒²⁶. In Nova Scotia, the progression from fire and smoke to a destroyed structure and explosion indicates likely sustained local access and utility impacts around Commercial Street²⁷˒²⁸˒²⁹.

The hazardous materials and roadway incidents are individually localized, but together they indicate a steady background of municipal response activity that could complicate traffic flow, emergency access, and nearby business operations in affected corridors⁷⁷˒⁷⁸˒⁷⁹˒⁸⁰˒⁸¹˒⁸²˒⁸³˒⁸⁴˒⁸⁵˒⁸⁶. The digital outage reporting may matter for business continuity, but the current alert set does not provide enough corroboration to determine whether the service issues are linked or whether they reflect separate platform specific problems²⁴˒⁹⁰˒⁹¹˒⁹²˒⁹³˒⁹⁴.

Planned protest activity in Mexico and Wisconsin supports advance route and facility awareness rather than immediate crisis response, although prior protest linked disruption in Coronango shows some gatherings can affect operations or worksites⁹⁵˒⁹⁶˒⁹⁸˒⁹⁹˒¹⁰⁰˒¹⁰¹. Outlook will depend on whether utility providers report restoration progress or further customer increases, whether officials clarify casualties and motive in New Albany, and whether investigators release additional findings from the La Porte facility. Key indicators to watch are new evacuation or shelter directives, outage escalation into critical service areas, and confirmation that planned demonstrations are drawing turnout large enough to affect transport or facility access.
