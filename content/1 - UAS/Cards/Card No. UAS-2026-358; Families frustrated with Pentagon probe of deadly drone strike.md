# Families frustrated with Pentagon probe of deadly drone strike

[Source](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/pentagon-troops-killed-iran-port-shuaiba-kuwait-families/)

(U) Port Shuaiba, Kuwait — CBS News reported that several Gold Star families briefed by the US Army on its investigation into the 1 March Iranian drone strike on the Port Shuaiba command post said the findings failed to answer their concerns; six Army Reserve soldiers were killed in that attack. Army Maj. Stephen Ramsbottom alleged in a complaint submitted to Congress on 13 November that commanders ignored repeated warnings and left the site without dedicated drone protection. The Pentagon said the internal review concluded in June but has not released it publicly, and families criticised the delay.
