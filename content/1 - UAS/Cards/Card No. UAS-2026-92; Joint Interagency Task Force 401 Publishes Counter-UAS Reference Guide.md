# Joint Interagency Task Force 401 Publishes Counter-UAS Reference Guide

[Source](https://media.defense.gov/2026/Sep/08/2003992822/-1/-1/1/JOINT-INTERAGENCY-TASK-FORCE-401-2026-COUNTER-SMALL-UNMANNED-AIRCRAFT-SYSTEMS-QUICK-REFERENCE-GUIDE.PDF)

(U) WASHINGTON, D.C., United States — Joint Interagency Task Force 401 published its 2026 Counter-Small Unmanned Aircraft Systems Quick Reference Guide, establishing a shared baseline of technical and tactical knowledge across the joint force and interagency partners. The pocket-sized guide translates strategic doctrine into repeatable tactics, techniques and procedures for detecting, commanding and intercepting Group 1-3 drones in deployed and homeland settings, and includes a lessons-learned section drawn from real-world engagements. Army Brig. Gen. Matthew Ross, the task force director, and Air Force Col. Jonathan M. Beha both described it as a practical tool for operators, planners and policymakers facing a rapidly evolving threat from state and nonstate drone proliferation.
