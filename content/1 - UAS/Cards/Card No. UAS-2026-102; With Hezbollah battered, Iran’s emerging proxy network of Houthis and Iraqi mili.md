# With Hezbollah battered, Iran’s emerging proxy network of Houthis and Iraqi militias target Saudi Arabia and other U.S. allies

[Source](https://fortune.com/2026/09/09/iran-hezbollah-houthis-saudi-arabia/)

(U) RIYADH, Saudi Arabia — The Associated Press reported on September 9, 2026 that regional officials said Houthi operatives helped Iraqi militias plan and execute a two-day drone swarm attack on Saudi oil facilities, revealing a new level of coordination across Iran's network. The Houthi involvement in the earlier attack on Saudi oil facilities was confirmed by two Saudi officials citing intelligence and a senior Iraqi security official, who said Houthi emissaries worked in an operations room run by Iraqi militias. Saudi Arabia and the United States responded with joint airstrikes that killed at least 20 Iraqi fighters, six Iranian advisers, and at least one Houthi official, as Iran shifted to using the Iraqi militias and Houthis to threaten U.S. allies in the Gulf.
