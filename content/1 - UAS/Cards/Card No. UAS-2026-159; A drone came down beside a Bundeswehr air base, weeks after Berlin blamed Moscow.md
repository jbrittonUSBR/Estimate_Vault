# A drone came down beside a Bundeswehr air base, weeks after Berlin blamed Moscow for the Leipzig airport attack

[Source](https://euromaidanpress.com/2026/09/16/a-drone-came-down-beside-a-bundeswehr-air-base-weeks-after-berlin-blamed-moscow-for-the-leipzig-airport-attack/#google_vignette)

(U) WUNSTORF, Germany — A drone of unknown origin crashed about one kilometer from the Bundeswehr's Wunstorf air base in Lower Saxony, with debris found east of Lake Steinhude, Bild reported on September 15, 2026. The state criminal police office said the drone came down in an open, uninhabited area and that there were no indications of danger to the public, while a large-scale search was launched for additional parts. Investigators said the owner and origin of the aircraft were not yet known and that further analysis of recovered components would be needed before conclusions could be drawn. The incident followed the August discovery of a Semtex-laden drone beside a Ukraine-bound cargo aircraft at Leipzig/Halle airport, which Berlin has attributed to Moscow, and Euromaidan Press noted that German military counterintelligence had also attributed part of the drone activity over Bundeswehr sites to Chinese intelligence.
