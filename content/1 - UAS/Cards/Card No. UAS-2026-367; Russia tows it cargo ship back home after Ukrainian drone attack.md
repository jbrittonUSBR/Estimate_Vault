# Russia tows it cargo ship back home after Ukrainian drone attack

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/11/24/russia-tows-it-cargo-ship-back-home-after-ukrainian-drone-attack)

(U) GULF OF FINLAND — Russia towed the cargo ship Sparta II back to home waters after a Ukrainian drone attack in the Gulf of Finland sparked a fire and forced the crew's evacuation, per Al Jazeera. Water cannons were used off Saint Petersburg to prevent the blaze from reigniting. No injuries were reported.
