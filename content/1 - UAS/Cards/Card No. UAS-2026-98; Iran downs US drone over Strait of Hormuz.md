# Iran downs US drone over Strait of Hormuz

[Source](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/americas/iran-downs-us-mq-1-drone-over-strait-of-hormuz-report/4051017)

(U) ISTANBUL, Türkiye — Iranian air defenses shot down a US MQ-1 drone over the Strait of Hormuz, the semi-official Fars news agency reported, saying the aircraft was detected and targeted by army air-defense units stationed in southeastern Iran. Fars said the drone was tracked by systems operating under Iran's integrated air defense network before being destroyed using domestically developed technology. In a statement carried by state-run broadcaster IRIB, the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps also said its naval forces seized a US unmanned submarine at the entrance to the Strait of Hormuz early Tuesday. There was no immediate comment from the US on the reports, which were relayed by Turkey's state-run Anadolu Agency and reflect Iranian claims.
