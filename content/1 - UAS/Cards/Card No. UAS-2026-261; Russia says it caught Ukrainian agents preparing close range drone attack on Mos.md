# Russia says it caught Ukrainian agents preparing close range drone attack on Moscow’s air defenses

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/10/26/world-news/russia-says-it-caught-ukrainian-agents-preparing-close-range-drone-attack-on-moscows-air-defenses/)

(U) Moscow region, Russia — The New York Post, citing Reuters, reported the FSB’s claim that it arrested two men accused of preparing remotely controlled explosive drones to target a component of Moscow’s air-defence system and an electricity facility. The FSB alleged the suspects acted on SBU instructions; neither the Ukrainian service nor Ukraine had commented, and the claim remained unverified.
