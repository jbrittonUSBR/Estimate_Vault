# Russia Strikes Moldova Border as Ukraine Hits Black Sea Port

[Source](https://oilprice.com/Geopolitics/Europe/Russia-Strikes-Moldova-Border-as-Ukraine-Hits-Black-Sea-Port.html)

(U) KYIV, Ukraine — A Russian drone attack targeted the Starokazache–Tudora border crossing between Ukraine and Moldova in Ukraine's Odesa region on September 9, 2026, killing two civilians and injuring three, according to Ukrainian authorities and Moldovan President Maia Sandu. The strike damaged checkpoint infrastructure, vehicles and buildings, sparked a major fire, and forced the evacuation of 16 people, and the crossing was closed. The attack followed a Russian drone's entry into Moldovan airspace the previous day, which briefly halted flights at Chișinău airport and delayed Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy's departure for Norway. Separately, Russian drones struck Kyiv, killing one person and wounding 17, while Ukraine said it struck a naval base, oil-loading infrastructure and Kalibr-carrying warships at the Black Sea port of Novorossiysk.
