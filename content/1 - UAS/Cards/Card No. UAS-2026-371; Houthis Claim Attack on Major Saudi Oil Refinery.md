# Houthis Claim Attack on Major Saudi Oil Refinery

[Source](https://www.breitbart.com/national-security/2026/11/24/houthis-claim-attack-on-major-saudi-oil-refinery/)

(U) RABIGH, SAUDI ARABIA — Houthis claimed a "direct hit" on the Petro Rabigh refinery on the Red Sea coast on Sunday, with unverified video showing fires at the facility, per Breitbart News. The Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted a Houthi ballistic missile aimed at Saudi Arabia's western region but did not confirm refinery damage, and the Houthis threatened shipping, airlines and civilians in retaliation for Saudi operations. The refinery is one of Saudi Arabia's largest petrochemical complexes, jointly developed by Saudi Aramco and Sumitomo Chemical of Japan.
