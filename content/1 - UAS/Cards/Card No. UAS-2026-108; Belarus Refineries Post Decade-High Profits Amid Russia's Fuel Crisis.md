# Belarus Refineries Post Decade-High Profits Amid Russia's Fuel Crisis

[Source](https://oilprice.com/Latest-Energy-News/World-News/Belarus-Refineries-Post-Decade-High-Profits-Amid-Russias-Fuel-Crisis.html)

(U) MINSK, Belarus — Belarusian oil refineries recorded their highest profitability in a decade in 2026, according to the independent Pozirk news agency, as Russia's refining crisis pushed record volumes of gasoline and diesel across the border. Belneftekhim head Ilya Ikan credited "favorable conditions in foreign markets, primarily in the eastern direction," while Reuters trade data showed Belarusian gasoline shipments to Russia rose 25-fold over the first seven months of 2026 to nearly 665,000 tons and diesel deliveries climbed roughly sevenfold to about 418,000 tons. Ukrainian drone strikes had cut Russian refining output to near 4 million barrels a day in August from a pre-crisis baseline of 5.3 million to 5.5 million, a 25 to 30 percent shortfall. Pozirk published no specific profit figures, and Ikan's "decade-high" characterization remained Belneftekhim's own assessment rather than audited data.
