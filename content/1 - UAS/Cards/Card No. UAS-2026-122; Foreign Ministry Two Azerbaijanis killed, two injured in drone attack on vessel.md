# Foreign Ministry: Two Azerbaijanis killed, two injured in drone attack on vessel in Black Sea

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/ukraine/2026/09/ukraine-260910-azertac01.htm)

(U) BAKU, Azerbaijan — Azerbaijan's Foreign Ministry said that on September 9, 2026 a foreign cargo vessel named "TEDY," whose crew included Azerbaijani citizens, came under drone attacks in the Black Sea, according to the state news agency AZERTAC. Azerbaijani citizens Aslan Aliyev and Asim Gasimov were killed, while Imran Novruzov and Rauf Aliyev were injured and hospitalized in Chornomorsk, Ukraine. The ministry said it was coordinating with Azerbaijani state bodies, diplomatic missions and the competent authorities of the relevant countries, and was working to repatriate the wounded and provide consular support and funeral arrangements. It extended condolences to the victims' families and again urged Azerbaijani citizens to refrain from travelling to or working in areas where military operations are ongoing.
