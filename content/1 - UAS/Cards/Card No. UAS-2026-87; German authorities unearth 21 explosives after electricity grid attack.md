# German authorities unearth 21 explosives after electricity grid attack

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/09/07/world-news/german-police-find-21-explosives-after-electricity-attack/)

(U) BERLIN, Germany — On September 7, 2026, German prosecutors in the eastern state of Saxony said authorities had recovered a total of 21 explosive devices as part of an investigation into attacks on the country's power grid, according to Reuters. Nine additional devices were found over the preceding weekend near high-voltage transmission lines south of the Graustein substation in the Goerlitz district. An unidentified suspect remained at large after sending letters claiming responsibility for the attacks. The discoveries came amid a series of power-grid sabotage incidents in Germany that followed an August drone incident at Leipzig/Halle Airport, which Berlin blamed on Russia.
