# Poland pledges to strengthen security after drone strikes along border with Ukraine

[Source](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/poland-pledges-strengthen-security-after-drone-strikes-border-136418920)

(U) WARSAW, Poland — Poland pledged increased security along its border with Ukraine after a wave of Russian drone strikes near the frontier, including two that landed within kilometers of Polish territory, the Associated Press reported. Prime Minister Donald Tusk warned that Moscow appeared poised to intensify its campaign against Ukraine's borders with European countries and said the escalation could affect Polish territory. Defense Minister Wladyslaw Kosiniak-Kamysz said the Polish army recovered what was believed to be a Russian military drone from the Baltic Sea near the coastal village of Jaroslawiec, and Polish officials said intelligence reports, including a CIA briefing, suggested Russia was likely to attack border points and intensify sabotage inside European countries.
