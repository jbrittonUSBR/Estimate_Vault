# Taiwan nuclear drill simulates drone attack, drawing on Ukraine war lessons

[Source](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20260909PD247/taiwan-nuclear-war-infrastructure-security.html)

(U) TAIPEI, Taiwan — Taiwan was planning to deploy drone detection and countermeasure systems at nuclear power plants and other critical infrastructure to strengthen its ability to respond to security threats, Digitimes reported on September 10, 2026. The effort drew on lessons from the war in Ukraine, where drone strikes have targeted energy and nuclear facilities. The report described the initiative as part of Taiwan's broader push to harden critical infrastructure against hostile drone activity. (Article content was partially behind a paywall.)
