# Iran War: Ansar Allah Strikes Yanbu Port as Rising Energy Prices Help Push 10 Year Treasury Yields Over 5%

[Source](https://www.nakedcapitalism.com/2026/09/iran-war-ansar-allah-strikes-yanbu-port-as-rising-energy-prices-help-push-10-year-treasury-yields-over-5.html)

(U) SANAA, Yemen — Ansar Allah forces conducted ballistic missile and drone attacks on Saudi Arabian targets including Yanbu, Jeddah, Taif, Abha, and Al-Ula, according to a naked capitalism roundup that compiled social media and open-source reporting. The post also cited satellite imagery indicating damage to pump stations on Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline and reported that rising energy prices pushed 10-year U.S. Treasury yields above 5 percent. The roundup aggregated claims that had not been independently verified by the outlet.
