# Russia's internet giant Yandex halts operations at major data center after drone attack

[Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/08/russia-yandex-ukraine-drone-strike-data-center.html)

(U) Moscow region, Russia — Russian internet giant Yandex halted operations at a major data center in the Moscow region after a reported drone attack, with the company saying services were affected while authorities assessed damage; the incident occurred amid ongoing cross-border drone activity in the war in Ukraine.
