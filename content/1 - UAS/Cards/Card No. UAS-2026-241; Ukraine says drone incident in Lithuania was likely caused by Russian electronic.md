# Ukraine says drone incident in Lithuania was likely caused by Russian electronic warfare

[Source](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/10/18/8058627/)

(U) Lithuania — Ukrainska Pravda reported that Ukraine’s military intelligence agency assessed that Russian electronic warfare likely caused a drone involved in an incident in Lithuania to deviate from its intended route. The explanation was an official Ukrainian claim; the report did not present independent confirmation of the cause.
