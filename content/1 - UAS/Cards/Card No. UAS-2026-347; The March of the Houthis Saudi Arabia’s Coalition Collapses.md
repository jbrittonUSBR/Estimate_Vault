# The March of the Houthis: Saudi Arabia’s Coalition Collapses

[Source](https://www.juancole.com/2026/11/houthis-coalition-collapses.html)

(U) Yemen — Houthi forces advanced along Yemen’s southern coast toward Aden after government-aligned Southern Armed Forces withdrew from the Bab el-Mandeb area, according to the article. The Yemeni government’s position weakened as Saudi support and coalition coordination came under strain, while Houthi missile and drone attacks on Saudi targets continued. The author cited reports of substantial civilian displacement amid the prolonged conflict.
