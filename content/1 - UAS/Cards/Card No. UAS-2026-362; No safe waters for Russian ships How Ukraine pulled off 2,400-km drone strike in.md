# No safe waters for Russian ships? How Ukraine pulled off 2,400-km drone strike in Libya

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/europe/no-safe-waters-for-russian-ships-how-ukraine-pulled-off-2400-km-drone-strike-in-libya/articleshow/136098386.cms)

(U) Benghazi, Libya — The Times of India reported, citing The War Zone and satellite imagery, that the Russian corvette Merkury caught fire and capsized at Benghazi port on November 22, and that President Volodymyr Zelenskyy confirmed on November 23 that seven Ukrainian-made naval drones were used. The SBU claimed a Ka-27 helicopter aboard the ship was destroyed; two Libyan military officials told the Associated Press the drones were launched from a base in western Libya, with four intercepted and two hitting the corvette, while Tripoli's defence ministry denied that Libyan facilities were used for attacks abroad. The article also noted a strike a day earlier on the Russian defence logistics ship Sparta II in the Gulf of Finland.
