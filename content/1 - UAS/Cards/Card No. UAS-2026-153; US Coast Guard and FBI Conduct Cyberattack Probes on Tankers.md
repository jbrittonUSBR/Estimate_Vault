# US Coast Guard and FBI Conduct Cyberattack Probes on Tankers

[Source](https://gcaptain.com/us-coast-guard-and-fbi-conduct-cyberattack-probes-on-tankers/)

(U) WASHINGTON — The U.S. Coast Guard and the FBI boarded an unnamed foreign-flagged vessel on August 21 as part of an investigation into a network compromise, Bloomberg reported via gCaptain. The tanker VL Prosperity was idling off the Texas coast, and the Mehr news agency reported a 30-hour communications cut after a cyberattack near the Strait of Gibraltar. The vessel was later cleared for normal operations under its manager, HMM Ocean Service, and a second LNG carrier was inspected on August 24 following reporting by the Wall Street Journal and the FBI. Analyst Mark Douglas said the growing digital attack surface on ships was expanding the risk.
