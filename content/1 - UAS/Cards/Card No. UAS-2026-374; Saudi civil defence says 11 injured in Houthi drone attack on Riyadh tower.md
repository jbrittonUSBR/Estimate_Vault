# Saudi civil defence says 11 injured in Houthi drone attack on Riyadh tower

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/24/saudi-led-coalition-condemns-terrorist-attack-on-tower-in-riyadh)

(U) RIYADH, SAUDI ARABIA — Saudi civil defence said at least 11 people were wounded, including two Saudis and nine foreign nationals, in a Houthi drone attack on a tower in Riyadh's King Abdullah Financial District on Tuesday, per Al Jazeera and AFP. The Saudi-led coalition condemned the "terrorist" attack and vowed a firm response, while GCC, Qatar, UAE, Kuwait, Pakistan and the US denounced the strike; US Secretary of State Marco Rubio condemned Iran-backed Houthi attacks in a call with Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan Al Saud. The strike marked the second attack on Riyadh's financial district in less than a week, with most international flights to Riyadh cancelled.
