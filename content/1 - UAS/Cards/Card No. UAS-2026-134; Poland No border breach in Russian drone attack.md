# Poland: No border breach in Russian drone attack

(U) WARSAW, Poland — Polish authorities stated there was no border breach during a Russian drone attack targeting western Ukraine, contradicting earlier reports of drones entering Polish airspace. The clarification followed heightened alert and air-defense activity along Poland's eastern border. The incident underscored the recurring risk of spillover into NATO territory during Russian strikes near the frontier.
