# Russia retrieves burning cargo ship from Gulf of Finland after drone strike

[Source](https://yle.fi/a/74-20258435)

(U) Gulf of Finland — Russia began towing the sanctioned, Russian-flagged cargo ship Sparta II out of the eastern Gulf of Finland on Sunday after a Saturday drone strike ignited a fire about 50 km west of Suursaari island and the vessel drifted into Finnish territorial waters. Yle reported the ship, owned by defence ministry-run Oboronlogistika, was sailing from St Petersburg to Kaliningrad; its crew was evacuated to another Russian vessel, and the Finnish Border Guard said no oil spill had been observed. Finland's defence ministry said a Russian helicopter was suspected of entering Finnish airspace, Prime Minister Petteri Orpo said the war was spilling into Finland's immediate vicinity, and Kyiv had not claimed the attack.
