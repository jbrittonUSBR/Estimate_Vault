# Romanian intelligence agency says it identified Russian-linked cyber attack

[Source](https://www.irishtimes.com/world/europe/2026/11/11/romanian-intelligence-agency-says-it-identified-russian-linked-cyber-attack/)

(U) Bucharest, Romania, November 11 — The Irish Times reported that Romania’s SRI attributed a December 2025 cyberattack on an unnamed government organisation to the Russian-linked Turla group. SRI said attackers exploited vulnerabilities in Microsoft systems, but the intrusion was stopped, no sensitive data was found to have been exfiltrated, and the affected email service was repaired.
