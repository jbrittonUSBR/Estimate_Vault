# Girl put into care after allegedly contacting ISIS, spreading jihadist propaganda - TopNews

(U) ITALY — State police executed a warrant placing an Albanian girl living in Sicily's Ragusa province into a care home over allegations she contacted ISIS and spread jihadist propaganda online, including videos of executions and manuals on explosives; she is under investigation for self-training and incitement to terrorism.
