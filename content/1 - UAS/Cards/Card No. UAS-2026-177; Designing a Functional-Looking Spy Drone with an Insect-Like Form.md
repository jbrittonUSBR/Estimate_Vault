# Designing a Functional-Looking Spy Drone with an Insect-Like Form

[Source](https://80.lv/articles/designing-a-functional-looking-spy-drone-with-an-insect-like-form)

(U) ONLINE — 3D hard-surface artist Sandro Bonfanti detailed the workflow behind "B.E.E.," a concept for a roughly 10 cm bee- and dragonfly-inspired spy drone built in Blender and Plasticity and presented as a plausible commercial surveillance product. The design incorporated a 4K camera, microphone, night-vision system, carbon-fiber propellers and LED lighting to suggest a covert listening and observation capability. The project was a concept-design exercise rather than a fielded system, with product-style renders staged in forest settings to convey military reconnaissance use.
