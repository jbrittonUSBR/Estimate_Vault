# Iran Targets US Warships In Shock Clashes Ongoing In Strait Of Hormuz

[Source](https://activistpost.com/iran-targets-us-warships-in-shock-clashes-ongoing-in-strait-of-hormuz-saudi-gas-pipeline-eliminated/)

(U) TEHRAN — A commentary republished by Activist Post on October 1, 2026, and credited to MintPress News' program "The Target" with Robert Inlakesh, asserted that Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps was targeting U.S. warships and tankers that violated a blockade of the Strait of Hormuz. The account said Houthi forces in Yemen had captured the country's west coast and were closing on Marib, Taiz and Aden, and that attacks on at least three pumping stations had severed Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline, cutting about 6 million barrels per day of exports. It cited a Citigroup projection of oil at roughly $150 a barrel and reported protests in Syria after a 40% diesel price increase. The item is aggregator commentary and its battlefield claims could not be independently confirmed.
