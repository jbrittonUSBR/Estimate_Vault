# Ukrainian drone plot against Moscow air defenses foiled – FSB (VIDEO)

[Source](https://rt-english.com/russia/647735-fsb-drone-attack-thwarted/)

(U) Moscow region, Russia — RT reported the FSB’s claim that it foiled a Ukrainian-directed plot to place explosive-laden drones near an air-defence site and an energy facility. The FSB said two suspects were arrested and alleged that Ukraine’s SBU supplied the equipment; these claims were not independently verified in the report.
