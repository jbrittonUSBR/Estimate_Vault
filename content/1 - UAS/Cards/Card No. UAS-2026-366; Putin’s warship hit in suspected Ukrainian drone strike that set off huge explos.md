# Putin’s warship hit in suspected Ukrainian drone strike that set off huge explosion

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/11/22/world-news/putins-warship-hit-in-suspected-ukrainian-drone-strike-that-set-off-huge-explosion/)

(U) Benghazi, Libya — Citing Libyan military officials and CBS News, the New York Post reported that a drone strike at Benghazi Naval Base severely damaged the Russian corvette Merkury and started a fire; satellite imagery cited from the BBC later showed the ship partially submerged. The report said Ukraine had not claimed responsibility, but an unnamed military source told Axios the SBU carried out the attack, and Naval News said the vessel had escorted cargo ships carrying military supplies. No casualty information was given, and the strike came hours after a Russian defence ministry-linked cargo ship caught fire in the Gulf of Finland.
