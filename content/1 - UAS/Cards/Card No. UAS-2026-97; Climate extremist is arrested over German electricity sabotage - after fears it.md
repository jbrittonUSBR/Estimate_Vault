# Climate extremist is arrested over German electricity sabotage - after fears it was Russian hybrid war attack

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16114421/Climate-extremist-arrested-German-electricity-sabotage-Russian-hybrid-attack.html)

(U) WEISWEILER, Germany — German police arrested a 48-year-old man identified as Daniel V. on suspicion of carrying out a series of sabotage attacks on Germany's electricity grid, ending a manhunt that began after authorities received letters claiming responsibility for attacks on electricity substations in three German states. The suspect, detained near Weisweiler in western Germany, was carrying explosive devices at the time, and officers found a nearby tent containing further explosive charges. The local prosecutor's office said it was investigating a September 1 attack near the Jaenschwalde coal-fired power plant and that the probe in Brandenburg state was being conducted on charges of causing an explosion and disrupting public utilities, with additional charges being considered. The attacks had raised fears of a Russian hybrid-war campaign, and Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt said the case was likely "climate extremism" but that it was unclear whether the man acted alone or on behalf of a third party.
