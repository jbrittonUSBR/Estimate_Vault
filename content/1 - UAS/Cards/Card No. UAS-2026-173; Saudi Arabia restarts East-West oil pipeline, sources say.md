# Saudi Arabia restarts East-West oil pipeline, sources say

[Source](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/saudi-arabia-restarts-east-west-100845429.html)

(U) SINGAPORE/NEW DELHI — Saudi Arabia restarted operations on its East-West Pipeline following drone attacks that forced a shutdown on September 11, 2026, with sources reporting pumping at reduced rates before a gradual return toward capacity. The restart allowed resumption of crude loadings at the Red Sea port of Yanbu and helped ease oil prices as alternative export flows reduced reliance pressure on the Strait of Hormuz. Full recovery to pre-attack throughput was expected to take several weeks as repairs and bypass work continued.
