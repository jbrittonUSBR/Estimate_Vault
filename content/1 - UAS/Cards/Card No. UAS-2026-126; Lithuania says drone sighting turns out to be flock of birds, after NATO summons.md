# Lithuania says drone sighting turns out to be flock of birds, after NATO summons jets

[Source](https://www.cbc.ca/news/world/lithuania-airport-closed-possible-drone-9.7342142)

(U) VILNIUS, Lithuania — Lithuania closed Vilnius airport and NATO scrambled at least one fighter jet on September 13 after a possible drone sighting in national airspace, but the object turned out to be a flock of birds, the country's National Crisis Management Centre said. The jet, dispatched from Šiauliai in northern Lithuania where the Italian air force is based for the NATO Baltic Air Defence mission, made a visual identification, and the air alert was lifted with the airport reopened within 38 minutes. The incident followed repeated airspace violations in the region that have prompted NATO members to reinforce air policing along the eastern flank.
