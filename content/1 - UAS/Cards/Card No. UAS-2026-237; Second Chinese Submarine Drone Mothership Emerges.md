# Second Chinese Submarine Drone Mothership Emerges

[Source](https://www.twz.com/sea/second-chinese-submarine-drone-mothership-emerges)

(U) Guangzhou, China — TWZ reported that a second Chinese vessel configured to deploy uncrewed underwater vehicles emerged at Guangzhou Shipyard International. The publication described a flight deck, hangar, stern garage door and well deck, and compared the vessel with a larger mothership previously reported in Shanghai. TWZ assessed the ships as potentially experimental and argued that a larger fleet could have tactical and strategic significance.
