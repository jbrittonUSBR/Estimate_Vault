# Austrian Airlines flight from Tel Aviv makes emergency landing after bomb threat

[Source](https://www.dw.com/en/austrian-airlines-flight-from-tel-aviv-makes-emergency-landing-after-bomb-threat/a-79695070)

(U) Vienna, Austria — Deutsche Welle reported that an Austrian Airlines flight from Tel Aviv made an emergency landing after a bomb threat. Authorities searched the aircraft after landing and found no explosive device, according to the report; the threat nevertheless prompted an emergency response.
