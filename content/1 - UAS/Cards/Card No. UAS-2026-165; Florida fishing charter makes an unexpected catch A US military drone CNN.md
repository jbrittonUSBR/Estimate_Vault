# Florida fishing charter makes an unexpected catch: A US military drone

[Source](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/21/us/florida-fishing-charter-drone-found-hnk-ml)

(U) Gulf of Mexico off Florida — A fishing charter off the Florida Panhandle recovered a floating unarmed U.S. military drone, identified as a clone of Iran’s Shahed-136 (the Air Force’s LUCAS variant), near Eglin Air Force Base; the Air Force confirmed a test article went down and later recovered it.
