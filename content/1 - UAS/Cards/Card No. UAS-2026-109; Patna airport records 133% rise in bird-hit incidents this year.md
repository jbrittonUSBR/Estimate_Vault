# Patna airport records 133% rise in bird-hit incidents this year

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/patna/patna-airport-records-133-rise-in-bird-hit-incidents-this-year/articleshow/133980512.cms)

(U) PATNA, India — Bird-hit incidents at Jayprakash Narayan International Airport more than doubled in 2026, with 21 cases reported between January and August against nine during the same period last year, a 133.3 percent increase. Airport data showed the problem was most acute during the monsoon, with six incidents in July and five in August accounting for more than half of the year's cases. Airport director Chandra Pratap Dwivedi said the airport had adequate bird-control systems inside its premises but attributed the rise to bird-attracting hazards outside its boundary, including open meat and fish shops, animal waste, garbage, waterlogging and overgrown grass, and called on the state government to act.
