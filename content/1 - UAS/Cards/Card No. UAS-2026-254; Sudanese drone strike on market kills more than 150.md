# Sudanese drone strike on market kills more than 150

[Source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-21/deadly-sudanese-drone-attack-amiet-market-abyei/107291466)

(U) Abyei, Sudan–South Sudan border — ABC News Australia reported that South Sudan accused Sudan of carrying out drone strikes on Amiet Market, killing more than 150 people. Abyei’s caretaker information minister put the toll at 153 and said it could rise; the UN condemned the strike, while the attribution and casualty details were reported claims rather than independently established findings.
