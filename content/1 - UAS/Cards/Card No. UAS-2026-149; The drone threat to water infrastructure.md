# The drone threat to water infrastructure

[Source](https://www.wwdmag.com/utility-management/article/55383686/the-drone-threat-to-water-infrastructure)

(U) HOUSTON, United States — Gabriel Collins, a fellow at Rice University's Baker Institute, argued that the growing availability of long-range precision-strike drones has created new security challenges for water utilities, particularly those on U.S. coastlines. The commentary, published in Wastewater Digest, assessed how commercially accessible unmanned systems could be used against large reservoirs, treatment plants, and conveyance infrastructure. Collins framed the risk as an emerging gap in utility threat planning rather than a record of specific incidents.
