# Drone strike kills at least 21 and injures 40 in Sudan’s Nyala

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/27/drone-strike-kills-at-least-21-and-injures-40-in-sudans-nyala)

(U) Nyala, Sudan � A drone strike hit a market in Nyala on Monday evening, killing at least 21 people and injuring more than 40, according to a medic and witnesses cited by AFP. A resident said the market was frequented by armed men, including Rapid Support Forces fighters; the Sudanese military regularly struck the city during its war with the RSF.
