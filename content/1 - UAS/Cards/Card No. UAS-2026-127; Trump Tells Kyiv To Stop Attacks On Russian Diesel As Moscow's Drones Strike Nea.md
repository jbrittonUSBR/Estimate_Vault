# Trump Tells Kyiv To Stop Attacks On Russian Diesel As Moscow's Drones Strike Near Polish Border

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/ukraine/2026/09/ukraine-260913-rferl03.htm)

(U) KYIV, Ukraine — U.S. President Donald Trump called on Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy to stop targeting Russian diesel infrastructure, telling reporters during a visit to Ireland on September 13, "You have to stop knocking out diesel fuel in Russia," and adding that there were "plenty of other targets." The remarks came as two Russian drone strikes hit near the Polish border in Ukraine's Volyn region: one struck a petrol station near the Yahodyn border crossing and the other hit the locomotive of a Kyiv-to-Warsaw passenger train two kilometers from the frontier, with no casualties reported in either case. Ukraine has portrayed its long-range strikes on Russian refineries and fuel depots as "long-range sanctions" aimed at curbing Moscow's energy revenue, and the attacks have caused nationwide fuel shortages in Russia since the start of the summer.
