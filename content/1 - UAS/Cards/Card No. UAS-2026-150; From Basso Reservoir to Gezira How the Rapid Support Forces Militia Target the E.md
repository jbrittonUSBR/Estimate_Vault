# From Basso Reservoir to Gezira: How the Rapid Support Forces Militia Target the Essentials of Life in Sudan

[Source](https://www.alestiklal.net/en/article/from-basso-reservoir-to-gezira-how-the-rapid-support-forces-militia-target-the-essentials-of-life-in-sudan)

(U) TINA, Sudan — A drone belonging to the Rapid Support Forces struck the Basso water reservoir west of the city of Tina near the Chadian border on May 26, 2026, according to an Al-Estiklal report. The outlet described the attack as the latest in a widening pattern of strikes on water, electricity, health, and agricultural infrastructure in Sudan that had worsened humanitarian conditions well beyond the battle zones. It characterised the campaign as a strategy of using essential services as tools of war and pressure on areas outside RSF control.
