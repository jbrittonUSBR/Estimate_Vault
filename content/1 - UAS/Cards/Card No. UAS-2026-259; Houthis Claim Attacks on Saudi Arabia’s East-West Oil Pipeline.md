# Houthis Claim Attacks on Saudi Arabia’s East-West Oil Pipeline

[Source](https://oilprice.com/Latest-Energy-News/World-News/Houthis-Claim-Attacks-on-Saudi-Arabias-East-West-Oil-Pipeline.html)

(U) Saudi Arabia — OilPrice.com reported that the Houthis claimed attacks on pumping stations along the East-West pipeline and Riyadh airport, but Saudi Arabia had not confirmed damage to the pipeline. The article noted the route carries crude to the Red Sea while Hormuz is disrupted, and recalled that earlier drone strikes had damaged pumping stations and halted Yanbu loadings for two weeks.
