# Russian drone strike collapses apartment building in Kyiv region, 14 feared trapped

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/europe/russian-drone-strike-collapses-apartment-building-in-kyiv-region-14-feared-trapped/articleshow/136072334.cms)

(U) Kyiv region, Ukraine — President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said a Russian drone struck a five-storey apartment building in the Kyiv region on Sunday, collapsing part of the structure with up to 14 people feared trapped. Emergency crews evacuated nine residents, contained the fire and reported two injuries, including a teenager. The article also cited TASS saying five people were injured by overnight Ukrainian drone attacks in Russia's Krasnodar region.
