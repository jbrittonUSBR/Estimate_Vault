# Russia launches drone attack on Zaporizhzhia after Ukraine claims warship sinking

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/short-videos/russia-launches-drone-attack-on-zaporizhzhia-after-ukraine-claims-warship-sinking/videoshow/136119824.cms)

(U) ZAPORIZHZHIA, UKRAINE — Russia launched drone attacks on Ukraine's Zaporizhzhia region after Kyiv claimed to have sunk a Russian warship, per The Economic Times. The report attributed the strikes to Moscow as retaliation amid heightened tensions between the two sides. No casualty or damage figures were provided in the short video report.
