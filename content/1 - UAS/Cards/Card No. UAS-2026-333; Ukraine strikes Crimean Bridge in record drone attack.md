# Ukraine strikes Crimean Bridge in record drone attack

[Source](https://www.irishtimes.com/world/europe/2026/11/16/ukraine-strikes-crimean-bridge-in-record-drone-attack/)

(U) Kerch, Crimea — On November 16, Ukraine launched its largest drone attack of the war, striking the Crimean Bridge during a barrage across 15 Russian regions; Russia’s Defence Ministry said it intercepted 2,049 drones in 24 hours. Russian officials reported minor bridge damage and restricted road traffic, while rail traffic resumed; Ukraine had not immediately commented.
