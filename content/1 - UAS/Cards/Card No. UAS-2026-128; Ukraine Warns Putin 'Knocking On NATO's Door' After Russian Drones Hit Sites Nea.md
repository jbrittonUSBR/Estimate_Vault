# Ukraine Warns Putin 'Knocking On NATO's Door' After Russian Drones Hit Sites Near Polish Border

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/ukraine/2026/09/ukraine-260913-rferl02.htm)

(U) KYIV, Ukraine — Two Russian drone strikes hit near the Polish border early on September 13, prompting Ukrainian Foreign Minister Andriy Sybiha to declare that Moscow was "knocking directly on the doors of the EU and NATO" and to urge European leaders to impose a full trade embargo, entry bans, and sweeping sanctions on Russia's military-industrial complex. One strike hit a petrol station near the Yahodyn border crossing in the Volyn region, temporarily closing and later reopening the crossing, while the second hit the locomotive of a Kyiv-to-Warsaw passenger train two kilometers from the frontier; no casualties were reported because a monitoring group warned the train crew in advance. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Russia had fired 2,100 drones, 1,700 glide bombs, and 73 missiles at Ukraine over the preceding week, and the strikes followed Germany's formal attribution of an explosive-laden drone incident at Leipzig Airport to Moscow.
