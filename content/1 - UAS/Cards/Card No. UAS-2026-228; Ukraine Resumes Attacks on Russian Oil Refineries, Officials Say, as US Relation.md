# Ukraine Resumes Attacks on Russian Oil Refineries, Officials Say, as US Relations Teeter

[Source](https://military.com/ukraine-resumes-attacks-on-russian-oil-refineries-officials-say-as-us-relations-teeter)

(U) RYAZAN REGION, RUSSIA — Military.com reported, citing officials, that Ukrainian drones struck a refinery in Russia’s Ryazan region early Thursday, starting a fire. The report said the strike followed President Volodymyr Zelenskyy’s announcement that Ukraine would resume attacks on Russian oil refineries after a brief pause. Zelenskyy said Russian attacks on Ukrainian regions had continued and criticized what he described as pressure on Ukraine and recent sanctions relief for Russia.
