# Fiber-optic drones and cartel members at the front: Ukraine’s imprint on Mexico’s narco groups

[Source](https://english.elpais.com/international/2026-10-18/fiber-optic-drones-and-cartel-members-at-the-front-ukraines-imprint-on-mexicos-narco-groups.html)

(U) Mexico — EL PAÍS reported that Mexican cartel members had traveled to Ukraine and allegedly learned to operate fiber-optic drones before returning home. The article described this as a possible transfer of battlefield tactics and technology into cartel activity, based on sources and reporting cited by the outlet; it did not independently establish the extent of the alleged training.
