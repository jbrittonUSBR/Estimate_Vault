# Explosion on Russian ship at Libya port likely caused by drone attack, officials say

[Source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cr894gjz0j0yo)

(U) Benghazi, Libya — Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said seven naval drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury at the Benghazi naval base on Sunday and destroyed a Ka-27 helicopter, while Libyan officials reported an explosion and fire as the ship refueled. Zelensky said a second vessel, the cargo ship MV ANGARA, was also hit. Both Libya's rival governments condemned the attack and the eastern administration said it was investigating jointly with Russia, while Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov declined to comment on the Libya strikes.
