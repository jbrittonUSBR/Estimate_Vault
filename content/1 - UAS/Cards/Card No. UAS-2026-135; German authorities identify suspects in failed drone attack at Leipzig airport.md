# German authorities identify suspects in failed drone attack at Leipzig airport

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/german-suspects-drone-attack-leipzig-airport/)

(U) LEIPZIG, Germany — German investigators identified two suspects in the August 4–5 drone attack on a Ukrainian Antonov An-124 cargo aircraft at Leipzig/Halle Airport, a Russian national traveling on a Latvian passport described as a logistics coordinator with ties to the GRU and a Belarusian national carrying a Russian passport identified through DNA recovered from the explosives. The drone struck the aircraft but its detonator malfunctioned, leaving roughly 1.8 kilograms of Semtex and PETN unexploded, and a third drone site recovered on August 14 yielded about 50 grams of military-grade RDX, according to findings announced September 1 by Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt and Foreign Minister Johann Wadephul. DNA from the explosives also matched traces from a 2024 arson attack at the same airport, and Germany responded by announcing closure of the Russian consulate in Bonn effective September 18 and calling for additional sanctions, per the Crypto Briefing account.
