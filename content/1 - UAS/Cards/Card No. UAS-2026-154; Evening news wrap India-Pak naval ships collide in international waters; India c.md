# Evening news wrap: India-Pak naval ships collide in international waters; India condemns drone attack near Mecca & more

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/india/evening-news-wrap-india-pak-naval-ships-collide-in-international-waters-india-condemns-drone-attack-near-mecca-more/articleshow/134287261.cms)

(U) NEW DELHI — India summoned Pakistan's charge d'affaires after a Pakistani naval ship collided with an Indian Navy vessel in international waters, The Times of India reported in its evening news wrap. New Delhi said the Indian ship suffered no major damage and cited Article 10 of the 1991 bilateral agreement on preventing incidents at sea. In the same roundup, India condemned an alleged Houthi drone attack near Mecca after Saudi air defenses intercepted a drone south of the city, and the Houthis denied targeting Mecca. The wrap also included items on an organization designation and on Rahul Gandhi and the UPI payments system.
