# In Ukraine, the "Chahar" barrier was created, which cuts FPV drones’ fiber-optic cables in one second

[Source](https://unn.ua/en/news/in-ukraine-the-chahar-barrier-was-created-which-cuts-fpv-drones-fiber-optic-cables-in-one-second)

(U) UKRAINE — Ukraine’s Logika Design Bureau has developed the automated "Chaharnyk" barrier system designed to counter fiber-optic FPV drones. The system uses acoustic detection to identify approaching drones and activates a mechanical mechanism with hooks that severs their fiber-optic control cable within about one second, with an estimated success rate of at least 95%. A single unit can protect up to 100 meters of frontage and multiple units can be linked to form longer defensive lines to protect positions, command posts, and critical infrastructure.
