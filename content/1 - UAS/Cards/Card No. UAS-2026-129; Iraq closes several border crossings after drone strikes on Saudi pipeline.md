# Iraq closes several border crossings after drone strikes on Saudi pipeline

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/iraq/2026/iraq-260913-azertac01.htm)

(U) BAGHDAD, Iraq — Iraq's Security Media Cell announced on September 12 the closure of several border crossings as "administrative and security" measures and an investigation into unspecified "breaches and violations," one day after Baghdad confirmed that drone attacks on Saudi Arabia had been launched from southern Maysan province. Iraqi Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi ordered an investigative council into the conduct of the Maysan Operations Command and dismissed its commander, with his office stating the government "will not tolerate any activity that endangers Iraq or its relations with friendly countries." The cell did not name the affected crossings or say how long they would remain closed, but confirmed that the Zurbatiyah and Al-Mundhiriyah crossings with Iran remained open to passenger traffic, according to the report carried by Azerbaijan's state news agency AZERTAC and citing Anadolu Agency.
