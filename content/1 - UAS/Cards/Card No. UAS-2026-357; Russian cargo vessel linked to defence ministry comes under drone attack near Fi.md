# Russian cargo vessel linked to defence ministry comes under drone attack near Finland

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/europe/russian-cargo-vessel-linked-to-defence-ministry-comes-under-drone-attack-near-finland/articleshow/136060025.cms)

(U) Gulf of Finland, Finland — The cargo ship Sparta II was hit by two drones in international waters west of Russia's Gogland Island on Saturday while bound for Kaliningrad, caught fire and drifted into Finnish territorial waters. Leningrad region governor Alexander Drozdenko said the attack occurred about three nautical miles from Russia's maritime border, and Finnish Border Guard units prepared rescue and pollution-response operations. The vessel is operated by Oboronlogistics, linked to Russia's defence ministry and under US sanctions since 2022; Finnish authorities said Ukraine was likely responsible, though Kyiv did not claim the strike.
