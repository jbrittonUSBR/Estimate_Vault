# Pentagon Creates 'AutoWarCom' to Expand AI and Drone Capabilities

[Source](https://www.reuters.com/world/pentagon-creates-autowarcom-expand-ai-drone-capabilities-2026-09-30/)

(U) WASHINGTON — Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth announced the creation of the Autonomous Warfare Command, or "AutoWarCom," a new four-star-led military command dedicated to building and providing autonomous and robotic capabilities across the U.S. military, during a "State of the Force" address at Marine Corps Base Quantico, Virginia. The move — the first new combatant-style command since U.S. Space Command in 2019 — reflects the Pentagon's push to shift from costly conventional weapons platforms to cheaper, AI-driven autonomous systems that have proved decisive in recent conflicts, with Hegseth citing drone warfare in the Russia-Ukraine war, where drones are estimated to account for about 70% of Russian casualties. As an interim step, the department established Project Agincourt, led by Defense Innovation Unit Director Owen West and Navy SEAL Senior Chief Max Strasiser, to prototype a "warfighting acquisition" model linking operators with industry in rapid adaptation cycles. DoW plans to seek legislation for the command with a target establishment date of October 1, 2027, under a four-star commander.
