# Child rescued in Ukraine after Russian drone attack kills 10

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/11/23/child-rescued-in-ukraine-after-russian-drone-attack-kills-10)

(U) Dymer, Ukraine — A Russian drone struck a five-storey apartment building in Dymer, north of Kyiv, at about 05:00 on November 22, collapsing part of the block, according to Ukrainian officials. Rescuers pulled seven-year-old David from the rubble after about 14 hours trapped, and Al Jazeera's newsfeed reported that at least ten other people were killed in the strike. Ukrainian officials said a 79-year-old woman was also rescued alive from the debris.
