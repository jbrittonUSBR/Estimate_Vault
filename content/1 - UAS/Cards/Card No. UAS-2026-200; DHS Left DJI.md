# DHS Left DJI's Scores Out of Its Blue UAS Report. Here They Are

[Source](https://dronexl.co/2026/10/01/dhs-addendum-dji-matrice-scores-blue-uas-hudson-yards/)

(U) New York, USA — DroneXL reported that it obtained a copy of a 12-page DHS National Urban Security Technology Laboratory addendum to its Blue UAS assessment, numbered SAVER-T-R-40 and dated July 2026, showing that evaluators at Hudson Yards scored the DJI Matrice 4T at 4.6 out of 5 and the DJI Matrice 30T at 4.3, above the Skydio X10D at 3.7 and the Parrot ANAFI USA GOV at 2.4. The public report DHS published in September described the comparison in a single sentence and did not disclose the scores or their direction. The addendum also recorded one Matrice 30T losing satellite and vision positioning at night in an urban canyon and drifting into a structure, and listed cybersecurity concerns tied to the manufacturer's country of origin as a disadvantage for both DJI aircraft.
