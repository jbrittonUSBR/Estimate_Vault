# Russian drone attack on Ukrainian train station kills at least 13, officials say

[Source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c9p8gdlz1p2ko)

(U) Dnipro, Ukraine — BBC News reported that Ukrainian officials said a Russian jet-powered drone struck a crowded platform at Dnipro’s main railway station, killing at least 14 people and wounding others, including a child. Russia had not commented in the report and has denied deliberately targeting civilians.
