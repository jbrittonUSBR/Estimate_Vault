# Houthis and Saudis strike each other in latest escalation

[Source](https://www.dw.com/en/houthis-and-saudis-strike-each-other-in-latest-escalation/a-79649973)

(U) RIYADH, Saudi Arabia — On October 13, 2026, DW reported that Iran-backed Houthi rebels and Saudi forces exchanged airstrikes across the Yemen–Saudi border, with Houthis claiming attacks on Kamaran island and Qanawis while Saudi Arabia struck targets in Yemen’s Jawf and Saada regions. The escalation followed days of intense Houthi drone and missile attacks on Riyadh, including one strike that killed 13 people, as fighting reignited alongside regional tensions from the US–Israeli campaign against Iran.
