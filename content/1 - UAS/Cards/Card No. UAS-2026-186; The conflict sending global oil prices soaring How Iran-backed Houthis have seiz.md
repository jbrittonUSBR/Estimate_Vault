# The conflict sending global oil prices soaring: How Iran-backed Houthis have seized islands in Red Sea shipping route in escalating war with Saudi Arabia

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16153679/The-conflict-sending-global-oil-prices-soaring-Iran-backed-Houthis-seized-islands-Red-Sea-shipping-route-escalating-war-Saudi-Arabia.html)

(U) BAB EL-MANDEB, Yemen — Iran-backed Houthi forces seized several Red Sea islands and coastal positions, prompting a sharp escalation in their conflict with Saudi Arabia along a critical shipping route. Following a Sept. 11 drone strike that shut Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline, oil prices surged above $100 per barrel before Riyadh restarted the pipeline on Sept. 22. The G7 later condemned the Houthi strikes as attacks on global energy security.
