# Russian Forces Keep Hammering Railway Hubs in Western Ukraine with Military Cargo from Europe

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/ukraine/2026/09/ukraine-260914-sputnik02.htm)

(U) MOSCOW, Russia — Russia's Ministry of Defense reported that strikes had continued on railway infrastructure facilities in western Ukraine used for transporting military cargo from European countries, according to the state-run Sputnik news agency, republished by GlobalSecurity.org. The ministry said Russian drone strikes in the port of Odesa also hit a maritime vessel carrying supplies for the Ukrainian military. The report consisted of official Russian military statements and did not include independent confirmation or Ukrainian comment.
