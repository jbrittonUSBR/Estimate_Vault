# Ukraine Restarts Attacks on Refineries Deep Inside Russia

[Source](https://www.rigzone.com/news/wire/ukraine_restarts_attacks_on_refineries_deep_inside_russia-07-sep-2026-184552-article/)

(U) KYIV, Ukraine — Ukraine said it struck oil refineries deep inside Russia overnight on September 6–7, 2026, resuming long-range attacks after a pause during a visit by U.S. envoys to Moscow, according to Bloomberg. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy confirmed the strikes, which targeted a Lukoil refinery in the Perm region some 1,500 kilometers east of Moscow and facilities in the Republic of Tatarstan, including Tatneft's Taneco complex and the TAIF-NK refinery. The Perm plant has a reported capacity of about 260,000 barrels per day, while Taneco and TAIF-NK have capacities of roughly 300,000 and 145,000 barrels per day respectively. The governor of Perm said 27 drones were downed, and Russia responded by imposing temporary bans on gasoline and diesel exports.
