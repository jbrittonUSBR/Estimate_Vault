# Russia closes German consulate in St Petersburg after drone spat

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/world-news/russia-closes-german-consulate-in-st-petersburg-after-drone-spat/articleshow/133886009.cms)

(U) MOSCOW, Russia — On September 7, 2026, Russia said it was closing the German Consulate-General in St. Petersburg and shutting down German cultural institutions, in retaliation for Berlin's closure of Russia's consulate in Bonn and a Russian cultural centre in Berlin, according to Reuters. The German consulate-general was to cease operations by September 18, and staff at the Goethe-Institut cultural centres were to leave by September 13. The tit-for-tat expulsions followed Germany's accusation that Russia was behind an attempted drone attack at Leipzig/Halle Airport, an allegation Moscow dismissed as a fabrication. The moves marked a further escalation in diplomatic tensions between the two countries.
