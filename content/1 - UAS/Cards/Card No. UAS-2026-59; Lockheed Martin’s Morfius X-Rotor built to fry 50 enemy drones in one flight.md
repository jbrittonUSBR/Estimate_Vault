(U) Lockheed Martin unveiled the **Morfius X-Rotor** at the Farnborough Airshow — a ground-launched, recoverable high-power microwave (HPM) counter-drone system designed to neutralize more than 50 enemy drones in a single flight.

(U) Key points:

- It is a reusable “one-to-many” airborne HPM system that does not require specialized sensors or fire-control radars and can work with existing command-and-control setups.
- The company positions it as a low cost-per-kill solution against drone swarms, in contrast to expensive traditional interceptors.
- It has undergone flight testing in Arizona, California, and Oklahoma, with more tests planned.
- Lockheed is accelerating prototype production to get the system to frontline units faster.

(U) The announcement comes amid growing emphasis on affordable defenses against mass low-cost drones, including the jet-powered Shaheds seen in Ukraine.
