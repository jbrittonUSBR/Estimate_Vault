# Eleven sailors missing after drone attack sinks cargo ship off Bulgarian coast

[Source](https://www.irishtimes.com/world/europe/2026/10/13/eleven-sailors-missing-after-drone-attack-sinks-cargo-ship-off-bulgarian-coast/)

(U) KRAPETS, Bulgaria — On October 13, 2026, The Irish Times reported that drones struck two cargo ships off Bulgaria’s Black Sea coast, sinking one vessel about 30 km east of Krapets and leaving 10 crew members missing while six were rescued. A second ship, the Malta-flagged Chelsea 3, was hit by three drones near Bulgarian territorial waters and its 15 crew members were rescued. Bulgarian Prime Minister Rumen Radev said the pattern suggested benefit to Russia, though no attribution had been conclusively established as investigations continued.
