# Ukraine: Drone attacks on Chornobyl are 'gambling with the safety of millions of people'

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/ukraine/2026/09/ukraine-260910-unnews01.htm)

(U) KYIV, Ukraine — UN Development Programme Administrator Alexander De Croo told reporters via video link from Kyiv on September 10, 2026 that a drone strike on the protective structure at the Chornobyl Nuclear Power Plant could have endangered the safety of millions of people. De Croo, who visited the site and went inside the shelter over Reactor Four the previous day, saw firsthand the recent strike damage and said "a nuclear installation can never be a target" with "absolutely no excuse." He said the drone's origin must be formally established through an investigation, while military personnel guarding the site told him Russian drones fly over the facility daily. During the visit he also took part in opening a new solar power plant, supported by Lithuania and Japan, that will supply electricity to Reactor Four's cooling systems; UNDP has mobilised $1.3 billion over four years, giving 6.6 million people access to electricity.
