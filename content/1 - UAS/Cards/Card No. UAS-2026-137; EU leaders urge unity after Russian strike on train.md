# EU leaders urge unity after Russian strike on train

[Source](https://www.dw.com/en/eu-leaders-urge-unity-after-russian-strike-on-train/a-79259807)

(U) HELSINKI, Finland — Several top EU politicians called a Russian drone strike on a passenger train near Ukraine's border with Poland a bid by Moscow to intimidate Kyiv's allies that should be met with greater resolve, DW reported. In the incident, a drone hit the train's locomotive at a railway station in Ukraine's Volyn region, about two kilometers from the Polish border, shortly after a diplomatic train carrying former British Prime Minister Boris Johnson and other European diplomats had been at the station. EU foreign policy chief Kaja Kallas said the attack was meant to scare Western countries from supporting Ukraine and that allies should show they "are not scared," while German Foreign Minister Boris Pistorius cautioned against being forced into escalation.
