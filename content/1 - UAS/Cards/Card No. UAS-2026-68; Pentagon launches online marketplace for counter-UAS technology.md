(U) The Department of Defense launched an online, e-commerce-style marketplace designed to streamline the procurement of counter-unmanned aerial system (C-UAS) technology. Developed by **Joint Interagency Task Force 401 (JIATF 401)**, the platform aims to help military commands and government agencies quickly find and acquire vetted anti-drone equipment.
### **Key Details**

- **The Platform's Function:** While it mimics a consumer e-commerce website in design, it is a secure procurement portal. Buyers can search, filter based on tactical needs, review validated performance data, and submit acquisition requests.
- **Development & Cost:** JIATF 401 awarded software firm Kaizen Laboratories a **$15 million contract** in May 2026 using an Other Transaction Agreement (OTA) to build and deploy the platform rapidly.
- **Access & Vetting:** Access is strictly restricted.
    - _Buyers:_ Limited to federal procurement officers, military commands, and pre-cleared allied or partner-nation defense ministries.
    - _Vendors:_ Companies must undergo rigorous vetting (including business verification, export control reviews, and product evaluations) before publishing listings. Major defense tech companies like Anduril, AeroVironment, DroneShield, and SMARTSHOOTER have already utilized the space.
- **Strategic Motivation:** Leadership emphasizes that the rapid proliferation of small drones poses an urgent, defining threat to critical infrastructure and defense assets. The marketplace bypasses traditionally slow, paper-based bureaucratic acquisition processes to get effective countermeasures to the field much faster.
