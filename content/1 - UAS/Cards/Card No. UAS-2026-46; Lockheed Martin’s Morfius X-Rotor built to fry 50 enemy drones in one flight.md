(U) **Lockheed Martin’s Morfius X-Rotor built to fry 50 enemy drones in one flight** (Defense News, July 20, 2026) At the 2026 Farnborough Airshow, Lockheed Martin introduced the Morfius X-Rotor, a ground-launched, recoverable “one-to-many” airborne high-power microwave (HPM) counter-UAS system. The company says it can neutralize more than 50 enemy drones in a single flight while keeping cost per kill low.

Key features highlighted:
- It is designed to be recovered and reused.
- It can integrate with any command-and-control system and does not require unique sensors or fire-control radars.
- The system uses a lightweight, field-reusable HPM architecture.

Randy Crites, vice president and general manager of Lockheed Martin Missiles and Fire Control Advanced Programs, described it as setting “a new benchmark for counter-drone capability — delivering a high kill rate while keeping the cost per kill low.”

Lockheed Martin is accelerating prototype production of both the air vehicle and its HPM payload to field it more quickly. Recent flight tests occurred in Arizona, California, and Oklahoma, demonstrating interception and lethal effects; further testing is planned.

The announcement comes amid growing concern over the high cost of traditional interceptors against massed, low-cost drone and missile threats (including lessons from defending against Iranian missiles and Shahed drones). On the same day, Lockheed also unveiled a lower-cost Patriot interceptor as another option to reduce reliance on expensive munitions.
