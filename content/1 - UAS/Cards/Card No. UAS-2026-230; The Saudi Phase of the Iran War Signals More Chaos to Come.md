# The Saudi Phase of the Iran War Signals More Chaos to Come

[Source](https://www.thenation.com/article/world/iran-war-saudi-arabia-houthis-trump/)

(U) SAUDI ARABIA — In an analysis for The Nation, David Faris argued that the widening conflict involving Saudi Arabia and Yemen’s Houthis risked producing further regional instability. He described Houthi attacks on Saudi targets, including the Aramco headquarters, as adding pressure to the conflict and Saudi Arabia’s economic ambitions. Faris questioned claims that the broader war had been decisively won by the United States.
