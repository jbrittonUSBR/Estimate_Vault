# Two Patriot radars hit? Iran launches missile-drone attack on US bases in Bahrain

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/middle-east/two-patriot-radars-hit-iran-launches-missile-drone-attack-on-us-bases-in-bahrain/articleshow/136066291.cms)

(U) Manama, Bahrain — Bahrain's interior ministry said an Iranian attack on an unspecified civilian industrial facility caused material damage but no casualties, and civil defence teams brought the resulting fire under control. Iran's Revolutionary Guard Corps claimed in a statement carried by state broadcaster IRIB that it destroyed two US Patriot radars at the al-Riffa and Awali bases in retaliation for MQ-1 and MQ-9 drones it said violated Iranian airspace; the claim was not independently verified. State news agency IRNA separately reported that a US interceptor launched from the Fifth Fleet base crashed on Sitra Island in a large fire, an account the article said was unconfirmed.
