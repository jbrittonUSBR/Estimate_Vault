# Drone strike kills dozens near Sudan displacement camp – rights group

[Source](https://rt-english.com/africa/647083-drone-strike-kills-dozens-sudan/)

(U) EL FASHER, Sudan — A drone strike near a displacement camp in North Darfur has killed dozens of civilians, according to a local rights group cited by RT. The attack comes amid intensifying fighting between Sudan's rival military forces, with both sides accused of conducting air and drone strikes on populated areas; attribution remains contested as details continue to emerge.
