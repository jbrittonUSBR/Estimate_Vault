# Ukraine strikes oil facility in St. Petersburg as part of ‘massive’ drone attack

[Source](https://www.politico.eu/article/ukraine-strikes-oil-facility-st-petersburg-part-massive-drone-attack/)

(U) ST. PETERSBURG, RUSSIA — POLITICO reported that a large Ukrainian drone attack targeted St. Petersburg, with damage and fire reported at an oil facility. The report described the strike as part of a broader wave and relayed statements from officials about the attack; it did not establish that the oil site was the sole or primary target. This account emphasized the reported oil-facility impact and did not report a confirmed fatality in its headline.
