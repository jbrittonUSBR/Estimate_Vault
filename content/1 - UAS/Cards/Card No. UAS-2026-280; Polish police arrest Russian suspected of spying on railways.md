# Polish police arrest Russian suspected of spying on railways

[Source](https://www.yahoo.com/news/world/articles/polish-police-arrest-russian-suspected-121836754.html)

(U) POLAND — Polish police arrested a 55-year-old Russian citizen suspected of spying on railway infrastructure for a foreign intelligence service, after officers found him photographing trains and seized hundreds of thousands of image files from his devices.
