# Russia pounds the Kyiv region, killing 4 and targeting the power grid

[Source](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/russia-pounds-kyiv-region-killing-4-targeting-power-136881645)

(U) KYIV, Ukraine — On September 30, 2026, an Associated Press dispatch carried by ABC News reported that Russia launched a missile and drone barrage against the Kyiv region, targeting the power grid and killing at least four people, with subsequent wire accounts putting the toll at seven, including a child. The strikes concentrated damage on Ukraine's energy infrastructure amid the continuing Russian campaign against the country's power network.
