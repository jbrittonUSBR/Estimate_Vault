# Zelenskyy’s plane ‘almost hit’ by drone on way to Oslo, Norwegian PM says

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/9/zelenskyys-plane-almost-hit-by-drone-on-way-to-oslo-norwegian-pm-says)

(U) OSLO, Norway — Norwegian Prime Minister Jonas Gahr Store said a plane carrying Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy from Moldova to Oslo was almost hit by a drone. A Moldovan government spokesperson confirmed the incident occurred on Tuesday, September 8, 2026, before take-off and involved a Russian drone, with the departure delayed after Moldova closed its airspace over a drone alert. Zelenskyy had arrived in the Norwegian capital for the funeral of King Harald V and met Store and defense industry executives to discuss a European anti-ballistic system codenamed FREYJA.
