# Russia Says Oil Output Drop Is Temporary as Refineries Restart

[Source](https://oilprice.com/Latest-Energy-News/World-News/Russia-Says-Oil-Output-Drop-Is-Temporary-as-Refineries-Restart.html)

(U) MOSCOW, Russia — Russian Deputy Prime Minister Alexander Novak said the country's recent decline in oil output was temporary, attributed to refinery maintenance and restarts, and that production would recover as facilities came back online. The statement came as Ukrainian drone strikes on refineries and export terminals continued to reshape crude and diesel forecasts, with Rystad Energy citing the attacks as a factor in revised Russian output projections. The remarks were made around the Eastern Economic Forum in Vladivostok amid an ongoing domestic fuel crisis and a diesel export ban.
