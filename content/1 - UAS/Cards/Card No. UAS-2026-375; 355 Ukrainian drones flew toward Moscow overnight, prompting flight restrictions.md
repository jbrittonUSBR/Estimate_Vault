# 355 Ukrainian drones flew toward Moscow overnight, prompting flight restrictions at two airports

[Source](https://meduza.io/en/news/2026/11/24/355-ukrainian-drones-flew-toward-moscow-overnight-prompting-flight-restrictions-at-two-airports)

(U) MOSCOW, RUSSIA — Moscow Mayor Sergei Sobyanin said 355 Ukrainian drones flew toward the capital overnight on November 24, with air defense forces shooting down most at distant approaches and destroying 63 more near Moscow, per Meduza. Vnukovo and Sheremetyevo airports operated only with special clearance for several hours, and Russia's Defense Ministry said 699 Ukrainian drones were shot down overnight over Russian territory and occupied Crimea. No casualties or damage were reported.
