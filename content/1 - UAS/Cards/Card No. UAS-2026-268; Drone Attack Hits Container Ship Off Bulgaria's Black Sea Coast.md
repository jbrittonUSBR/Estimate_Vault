# Drone Attack Hits Container Ship Off Bulgaria's Black Sea Coast

[Source](https://www.breitbart.com/europe/2026/10/28/drone-attack-hits-container-ship-off-bulgarias-black-sea-coast/)

(U) Off Cape Kaliakra, Bulgaria — Breitbart, citing the Associated Press, reported that Defense Minister Dimitar Stoyanov said three maritime drones struck the Liberian-flagged Contship Jet on 28 October. The 18 crew were evacuated. Russia had not claimed the attack; Bulgarian officials said they could not prove attribution.
