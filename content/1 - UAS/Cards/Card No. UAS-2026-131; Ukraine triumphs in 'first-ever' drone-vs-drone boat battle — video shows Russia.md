# Ukraine triumphs in 'first-ever' drone-vs-drone boat battle — video shows Russian MBeK destroyed by Sargan 3000's 12.7mm automatic turret

[Source](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/drones/ukraines-sargan-3000-triumphs-in-first-ever-drone-vs-drone-boat-battle-video-shows-russian-mbek-destroyed-by-its-foes-12-7mm-automatic-turret)

(U) BLACK SEA — Ukraine's Navy claimed on September 13 that its Sargan 3000 uncrewed surface vessel destroyed a Russian MBeK naval drone in the Black Sea, in what it described as "the first-ever battle of unmanned naval boats." Video released on the navy's official Telegram channel showed the Sargan 3000 engaging the Russian vessel with its 12.7mm automatic turret, with the MBeK sinking nose-first after the exchange. The claim, which has not been independently verified, marks an escalation in the use of armed sea drones, which had previously been employed mainly for strike and reconnaissance missions rather than vessel-to-vessel combat.
