# Iranian drone damages residential building in Kuwait City

(U) KUWAIT CITY, Kuwait — An Iranian drone attack damaged a residential building in Kuwait, authorities said, with no casualties reported. The strike was part of a wider Iranian campaign targeting U.S. military assets across Kuwait, Bahrain, Jordan and the UAE in retaliation for U.S. strikes on Iran.
