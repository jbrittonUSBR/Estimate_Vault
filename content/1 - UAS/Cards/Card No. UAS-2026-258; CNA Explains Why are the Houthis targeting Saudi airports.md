# CNA Explains: Why are the Houthis targeting Saudi airports?

[Source](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/world/cna-explains-houthis-targeting-saudi-arabia-airports-6492256)

(U) Saudi Arabia — CNA explained that Houthi attacks on airports were part of a broader campaign against Saudi civilian and economic infrastructure, disrupting aviation and events and threatening investor confidence. The report said the Houthis call Riyadh’s airport a military target, but noted that their assertion does not establish its status under international humanitarian law; it also reviewed the group’s missile and drone capabilities and travel advisories.
