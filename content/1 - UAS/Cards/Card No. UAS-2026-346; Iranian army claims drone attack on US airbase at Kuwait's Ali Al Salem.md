# Iranian army claims drone attack on US airbase at Kuwait's Ali Al Salem

[Source](https://www.business-standard.com/world-news/iranian-army-claims-drone-attack-on-us-airbase-at-kuwait-s-ali-al-salem-126111801563_1.html)

(U) Ali Al Salem Air Base, Kuwait — Iran’s army claimed it targeted air-defense systems and drone and missile launch platforms at the U.S. base with drones, according to a statement carried by Iranian state media. Kuwait reported that its air defenses were intercepting drone attacks but initially reported no damage; U.S. Central Command had not immediately commented. Kuwait’s Foreign Ministry condemned the attacks.
