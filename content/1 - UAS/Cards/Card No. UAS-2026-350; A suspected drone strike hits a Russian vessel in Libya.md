# A suspected drone strike hits a Russian vessel in Libya

[Source](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/suspected-drone-strike-hits-russian-vessel-libya-138359178)

(U) Benghazi, Libya — Two Libyan military officials said suspected Ukrainian sea drones struck the Russian corvette Merkury at the Benghazi naval base on Sunday, severely damaging it and igniting a fire that was later extinguished. The officials, speaking anonymously, said four drones were intercepted and two hit the vessel, launched from a base in western Libya; the eastern government said it opened a joint inquiry with Russia. Local media reported a separate drone hit a Maltese-flagged commercial vessel off the port, whose crew was rescued by the Libyan navy.
