# Why Germany Isn’t Punishing Russia Harder

[Source](http://foreignpolicy.com/2026/09/09/germany-russia-leipzig-drone-attack-response-hybrid-warfare-eu/)

(U) BERLIN, Germany — Foreign Policy reported that Germany's response to the attempted Russian drone attack at Leipzig/Halle Airport fell short of what critics and some European partners sought. Germany had concluded that Russia was responsible for the August 4, 2026, incident, in which an explosive-laden drone was found near a Ukrainian cargo aircraft at the freight hub and NATO logistics base, and announced countermeasures including closing Russia's consulate in Bonn and terminating the agreement on the Russian House in Berlin. The analysis frames Germany's calibrated measures and longstanding caution as reflective of a reluctance to escalate despite pressure from EU allies for harsher action; the article is an opinion and analysis piece rather than a report of new events.
