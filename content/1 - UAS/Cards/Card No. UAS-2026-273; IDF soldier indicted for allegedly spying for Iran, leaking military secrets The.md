# IDF soldier indicted for allegedly spying for Iran, leaking military secrets | The Jerusalem Post

[Source](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/defense-news/article-913273)

(U) ISRAEL — The IDF Military Prosecution indicted a soldier for allegedly providing information to Iran and maintaining contact with a foreign agent, per a joint statement by the Shin Bet, Israel Police and IDF; investigators alleged he disclosed sensitive details about his unit's activities, IDF operational readiness and military base coordinates over roughly a year in exchange for cryptocurrency payments.
