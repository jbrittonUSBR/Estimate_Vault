(U) Overnight drone strikes on July 22 caused fires at multiple power substations in Russian-occupied Crimea. 

- In Yalta: Fires reported at the 110/10 kV Massandra substation and the 110 kV Darsan substation; electricity went out around 03:30.
- In Alushta: Fire hotspots near the 110/10 kV Luchyste substation (near wastewater facilities) and damage reported at the 110 kV Alushta substation.

(U) The information comes from the Crimean Wind Telegram monitoring channel, citing satellite imagery. This follows Ukraine’s Unmanned Systems Forces “Crimean Switch Off” operation, which the report states struck 104 power substations and electrical facilities in occupied territories (including Crimea) between July 1 and July 20.
