(U//FOUO/REL TO USA, FVEY) SUMMARY: In early March 2026, a social media account posted anonymous messages alleging that a Russian in Mexico for “work” tried to sell military-grade drones to Mexican cartels. The anonymous source who is allegedly affiliated with CJNG was contacted via Telegram and offered Geron-2 (Shahed-136) drones by the Russian seller. The email address provided by the alleged Russian seller was inspected using OSINT tools. 

(U//FOUO/REL TO USA, FVEY) TEXT: 1. On 3 March 2026, a social media account that reports on cartel conflicts shared messages sent to him by an anonymous source reportedly affiliated with the New Generation Jalisco Cartel (CJNG) from an alleged Russian contact. The Russian claimed to be in Mexico for “work” and contacted the anonymous source via Telegram, attempting to sell them military-grade Geron-2 (Shahed-136) drones. The alleged Russian seller claimed to be using a translation app and shared a Russian-language video depicting multiple drones used in the Russia-Ukraine conflict .  The anonymous source told the social media account that the Russian seller is attempting to find people who can connect them to potential buyers.
