# Trump scales back public appearances after CIA and Mossad intercept plans for Iranian drone attack... as the line of succession is shuffled away to secure locations

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16292989/Trump-CIA-Mossad-Iran-drone-plot.html)

(U) United States, November 14 — The Daily Mail reported, citing Israeli newspaper Israel Hayom, that Mossad warned the CIA of an alleged Iranian plot to assassinate President Donald Trump using explosive drones; the claims were not independently substantiated in the article. It also said Trump had not held a public event for several days and that several cabinet officials had been moved from Washington housing amid reported threats.
