# Russia hits Poland-Ukraine border area amid wave of attacks targeting gas stations, Kyiv says

[Source](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/13/europe/russia-poland-ukraine-border-area-attack-intl)

(U) KYIV, Ukraine — A Russian drone struck a gas station near Ukraine's border with Poland early on September 13, Ukrainian authorities said, one of a series of attacks on fuel and energy sites on both sides of the war as Moscow's overnight strikes also hit the western regions of Ivano-Frankivsk, Ternopil, and Lviv and the southern Odesa region. Ukrainian Foreign Minister Andriy Sybiha said the border strikes amounted to Putin's terror "knocking directly on the doors of the EU and NATO," and President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Russia had launched 2,100 drones, 1,700 glide bombs, and 73 missiles at Ukraine in the previous week. The strikes came as U.S. President Donald Trump urged Kyiv to stop attacking Russian diesel infrastructure, telling reporters in Ireland, "There are plenty of other targets. Don't hit diesel fuel."
