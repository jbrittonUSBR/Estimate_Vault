# NATO fighter jet shoots down drone over Lithuania

[Source](https://www.cbc.ca/news/world/nato-response-lithuania-drone-9.7344227)

(U) VILNIUS, Lithuania — An Italian NATO fighter jet shot down a drone over Lithuania shortly after midnight on Tuesday, September 15, 2026, according to the Lithuanian National Crisis Management Centre. The shoot-down occurred as Russia and Ukraine continued strikes on energy infrastructure despite U.S. President Donald Trump's appeal for a pause in such attacks, CBC News reported. Debris identified by the centre as belonging to the drone was found at the site.
