# Russian drone strike kills 5 and injures at least 7 on passenger bus in Ukraine

[Source](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/russian-drone-strike-kills-5-injures-7-passenger-136488519)

(U) KYIV — A Russian drone struck a passenger minibus in the Nikopol district of Ukraine's Dnipropetrovsk region early Wednesday, killing five people at the scene and wounding at least seven, the Associated Press reported, citing regional head Oleksandr Hanzha. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy called the attack an atrocity with "no military rationale." In the same reporting, a passenger train was struck in the Mykolaiv region and about 170 people were evacuated, and a locomotive was hit in Kovel. More than 500 locomotives had been damaged or destroyed since February 2022, according to the report.
