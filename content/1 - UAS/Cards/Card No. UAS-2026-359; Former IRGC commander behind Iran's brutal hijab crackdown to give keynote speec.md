# Former IRGC commander behind Iran's brutal hijab crackdown to give keynote speech at London 'Islamophobia' conference

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16299701/Former-IRGC-commander-Irans-brutal-hijab-crackdown-keynote-speech-London-Islamophobia-conference.html)

(U) London, United Kingdom — The Daily Mail reported that Saied Reza Ameli, a former IRGC commander who served as secretary of Iran's Supreme Council of Cultural Revolution from 2019 to 2023, was announced as a remote keynote speaker at the Islamic Human Rights Commission's December 13 conference on AI and Islamophobia. The article said Canada sanctioned Ameli in 2023 over human rights abuses, while the IHRC said Canadian sanctions had no force in the UK and described him as an academic who opposes enforced hijab. Britain's Charity Commission said it had opened a statutory inquiry into the IHRC Trust over its relationship with the non-charitable IHRC.
