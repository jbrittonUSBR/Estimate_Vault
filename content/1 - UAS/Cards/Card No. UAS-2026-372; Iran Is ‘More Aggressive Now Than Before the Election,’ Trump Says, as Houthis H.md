# Iran Is ‘More Aggressive Now Than Before the Election,’ Trump Says, as Houthis Hit Riyadh’s Tallest Skyscraper

[Source](https://www.thegatewaypundit.com/2026/11/iran-is-more-aggressive-now-than-before-election/)

(U) RIYADH, SAUDI ARABIA — Yemen's Iran-backed Houthis struck Riyadh's tallest skyscraper, the Public Investment Fund tower, on November 19, causing material damage with no injuries reported, per The Gateway Pundit. President Trump told Fox News on November 22 he was inclined to order military strikes against Iran after the new year if no agreement was reached, calling Iranian negotiators "professional tap dancers." The Houthis subsequently threatened to attack Saudi Red Sea ports, prompting coalition strikes on Hodeidah and Sanaa, and claimed a strike on Saudi Arabia's Rabigh oil refinery, per the outlet.
