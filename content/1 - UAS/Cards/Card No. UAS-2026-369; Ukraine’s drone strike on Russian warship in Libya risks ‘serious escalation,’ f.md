# Ukraine’s drone strike on Russian warship in Libya risks ‘serious escalation,’ former official warns

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/world/ukraines-drone-strike-russian-warship-libya-risks-serious-escalation-former-official-warns)

(U) BENGHAZI, LIBYA — Former White House official Morgan Murphy told Fox News Digital that Ukraine's sea-drone strike sinking a Russian warship in Benghazi port on Sunday risks a "serious escalation," calling it a "giant tantrum" by President Zelenskyy. Murphy argued the strike could strain Zelenskyy's ties with President Trump, who recently met Putin and Xi without the Ukrainian leader, and complicate Washington's diplomatic push to end the war. Zelenskyy praised the operation as "exemplary," while questions remained about where the drones originated.
