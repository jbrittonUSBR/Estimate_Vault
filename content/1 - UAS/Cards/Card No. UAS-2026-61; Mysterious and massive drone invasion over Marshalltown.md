(U) The writer describes seeing what he estimates as more than 200 white drones hovering high over Riverside Cemetery in Marshalltown, Iowa, on ==July 26, 2025==. He initially mistook them for a flock of snow geese, then photographed them as they slowly moved south toward the courthouse area. One drone appeared much higher and stationary, which he speculated might be a “mother” drone. He contacted a city councilor, who said neither city officials nor the police chief knew anything about the drones or any permit. Another person on Facebook reported seeing over 100 drones the same day. Nearly a year later, the councilor still said no one had answers. The writer expresses surprise that such a large operation could occur without local knowledge or permits and questions its purpose (possible tech company scouting, data collection, etc.).
