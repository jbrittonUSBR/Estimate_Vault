# Statement by the Deputy Prime Minister/Foreign Minister on the Houthi Drone Attack Targeting the Holy City of Makkah Al-Mukarramah

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/library/news/2026/09/mil-260916-pakistan-mfa01.htm)

(U) ISLAMABAD — Pakistan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs condemned "in the strongest possible terms" a Houthi drone attack that targeted Makkah Al-Mukarramah, according to a statement reproduced by GlobalSecurity.org. The statement, issued as Press Release No. 244/2026 on September 16, said the attack violated Saudi sovereignty and offended the sentiments of Muslims worldwide. It expressed solidarity with Saudi Arabia's leadership over the Haramain Sharifain and the safety of pilgrims. The text was a government press release rather than an independent news report.
