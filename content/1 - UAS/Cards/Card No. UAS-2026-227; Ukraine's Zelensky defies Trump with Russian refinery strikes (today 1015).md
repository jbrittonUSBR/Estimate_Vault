# Ukraine's Zelensky defies Trump with Russian refinery strikes

[Source](https://freerepublic.com/focus/f-news/4399260/posts)

(U) UKRAINE — Free Republic republished a France 24 report that President Volodymyr Zelensky said Ukraine would no longer comply with President Donald Trump’s request to avoid strikes on Russian oil refineries. The report linked the shift to worsening Kyiv-Washington relations following a US-Russia diesel arrangement and continued Russian attacks on Ukrainian energy infrastructure. France 24’s correspondent provided analysis of the political context.
