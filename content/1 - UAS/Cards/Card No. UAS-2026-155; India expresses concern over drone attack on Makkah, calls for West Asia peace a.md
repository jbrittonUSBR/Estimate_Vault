# India expresses concern over drone attack on Makkah, calls for West Asia peace and stability

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/india/india-expresses-concern-over-drone-attack-on-makkah-calls-for-west-asia-peace-and-stability/articleshow/134287647.cms)

(U) NEW DELHI — India's Ministry of External Affairs said it was "deeply concerned" by a drone attack near Makkah, with spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal condemning the strike, The Economic Times reported. Saudi air defenses intercepted and destroyed a Houthi drone on Tuesday evening, and the Houthis denied targeting the holy city. India condemned attacks on Saudi sovereignty and civilian infrastructure, stressed the importance of freedom of navigation through the Bab el-Mandeb strait, and said it was monitoring security along the western coast. The report also noted talks between External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar and Saudi Foreign Minister Faisal bin Farhan on the sidelines of the BRICS summit.
