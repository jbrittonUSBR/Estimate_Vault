# US imposes 100% drone tariffs to curb China reliance

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/defence/us-imposes-100-drone-tariffs-to-curb-china-reliance/articleshow/133725012.cms)

(U) WASHINGTON — New US tariffs of up to 100 percent on drones and certain components took effect Thursday, following an August executive order by President Donald Trump citing "the national security threat posed by imports of drones and their components." Drones with a takeoff weight exceeding 25 kilograms, thermal-imaging drones, and docking stations face a 100-percent tariff, while smaller drones face a 25-percent duty, with duties on some less-sensitive components delayed until February 9 of next year. China's commerce ministry opposed the measures, saying they would "disrupt the global drone supply chain and further undermine a fair and competitive market environment"; Chinese company DJI has captured more than two-thirds of the global drone market and has been on a US list of firms linked to China's military since 2022.
