# Ukraine’s Fiber-Optic Drones Can Now Hear Russian Troops Talking From 30 Meters Away

[Source](https://united24media.com/defense-tech/ukraines-fiber-optic-drones-can-now-hear-russian-troops-talking-from-30-meters-away-23595)

(U) Ukraine — UNITED24 Media reported that Ukrainian developers had fitted fiber-optic drones with audio sensors said to detect Russian troops speaking from about 30 meters away. The outlet said the system could support reconnaissance without radio links, attributing the capability claims to Ukrainian sources rather than independently verifying them.
