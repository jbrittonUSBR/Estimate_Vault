(U//FOUO/REL TO USA, FVEY) On 10 July 2026, Mexican cartel-focused social media accounts posted an image of a downed fiber-optic-enabled drone (Figure 1) in Concordia, Sinaloa, allegedly circulated by a member of Los Mayos. One account claimed that the drone belonged to Los Chapitos, the IED-armed drone "failed," and a member of Los Mayos recovered it. One account noted that the drone in the image appears intact but the fiber-optic spool appeared damaged or possibly tangled. Another account recalled that Los Chapitos's first documented use of a fiber-optic enabled drone occurred in October 2025. On 10 July 2026, a Mexican cartel-focused social media account shared "recently surfaced" footage of a separate instance of a fiber-optic-enabled, first-person view (FPV) drone at an unspecified location in Mexico. The account claimed the drone appears to be intact but is entangled in a tree. The screen captures appear to show optical fiber hanging from the tree.
