# Lithuania shuts down airport amid drone threat

(U) VILNIUS, Lithuania — Lithuanian authorities temporarily shut down an airport in response to a drone threat, suspending flights while the airspace was secured. The closure is the latest in a string of airspace disruptions across the Baltic region attributed to incursions by drones, which NATO members have linked to Russian activity. The incident highlights the fragility of civilian aviation to low-cost unmanned intrusions.
