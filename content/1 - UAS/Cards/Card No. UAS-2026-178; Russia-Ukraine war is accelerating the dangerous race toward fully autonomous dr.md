# Russia-Ukraine war is accelerating the dangerous race toward fully autonomous drones

[Source](https://theconversation.com/russia-ukraine-war-is-accelerating-the-dangerous-race-toward-fully-autonomous-drones-290877)

(U) UKRAINE/RUSSIA — The Russia-Ukraine war is accelerating the race toward fully autonomous drones, with both sides fielding systems that can recognize targets and guide attacks with minimal human intervention. Ukraine has deployed AI-assisted modules and reported use of drones that can operate with limited connectivity, while Russia has fielded systems like the V2U with onboard AI for autonomous target selection and engagement. The trend raises serious concerns about human control, compliance with proportionality in targeting, and escalation risks as swarming autonomous systems move closer to large-scale deployment.
