# Aselsan shows integrated EW capabilities on TB2 UAV

[Source](https://www.flightglobal.com/defence/2026/09/aselsan-shows-integrated-ew-capabilities-on-tb2-uav/)

(U) TURKEY — Turkish firm Aselsan demonstrated integrated electronic warfare and ISR capabilities on a Bayraktar TB2 UAV in live trials. The coordinated system combined EW and intelligence/surveillance payloads to support air-defense suppression missions, showing an end-to-end operational capability by linking detection/targeting support with electronic effects from the TB2 platform.
