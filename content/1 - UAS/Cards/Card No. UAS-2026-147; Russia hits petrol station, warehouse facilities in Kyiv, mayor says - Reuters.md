# Russia hits petrol station, warehouse facilities in Kyiv, mayor says

[Source](https://slashdot.org/firehose.pl?op=view&amp;id=185656438)

(U) KYIV, Ukraine — Russian forces struck petrol stations and warehouse facilities in the Ukrainian capital, Kyiv's mayor said, according to a Reuters report syndicated through Slashdot. The strikes came as Russia and Ukraine continued tit-for-tat attacks on energy infrastructure despite a U.S. appeal for a pause, and Ukraine struck a Russian oil refinery the same day. The syndicated summary did not provide detailed casualty figures.
