# IRGC Commander Reported Killed In Clashes In Iran's Southeastern City Of Saravan

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/iran/2026/09/iran-260923-rferl02.htm)

(U) SARAVAN, Iran — Iran's Tasnim news agency reported that Brig. Gen. Hossein Zarifi, commander of the Shahid Sajjad base in Saravan, was killed in clashes in Sistan-Baluchestan province. The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps said five "terrorists" were killed and that additional suspects were arrested in the operation. RFE/RL, republished by GlobalSecurity.org, described the IRGC account as unconfirmed and noted that the province has long been the site of separatist and smuggling-related violence.
