# DHS S&T Tests C-UAS Capabilities for State and Local Law Enforcement

[Source](https://www.dhs.gov/science-and-technology/news/2026/10/01/feature-article-testing-c-uas-state-and-local-law-enforcement)

(U) WASHINGTON — The Department of Homeland Security's Science and Technology Directorate published a feature article describing testing of counter-unmanned aircraft systems (C-UAS) capabilities intended for state and local law enforcement. The effort evaluates detection, tracking, and mitigation technologies suitable for the domestic environment and assesses how such systems can support law enforcement agencies responding to unauthorized or hostile drone activity. The testing supports the broader federal push to equip non-federal agencies with drone-response capabilities.
