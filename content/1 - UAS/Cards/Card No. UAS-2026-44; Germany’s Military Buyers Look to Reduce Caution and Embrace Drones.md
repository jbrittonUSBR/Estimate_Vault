(U) As threats from Russia increase and traditional U.S. support appears less reliable, Germany is moving beyond its long post-World War II caution on military matters. The German military (Bundeswehr) is reinventing how it researches, funds, and deploys 21st-century weapons systems. The piece focuses on a shift toward greater innovation, including autonomous weapons, drones, and artificial intelligence.

One related public snippet notes that mass-produced, relatively inexpensive AI-powered systems from German startups (such as Helsing SE) illustrate a broader change in how military spending and procurement are approached.
