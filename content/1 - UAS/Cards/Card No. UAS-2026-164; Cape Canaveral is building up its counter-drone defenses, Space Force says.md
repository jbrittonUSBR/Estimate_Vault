# Cape Canaveral is building up its counter-drone defenses, Space Force says

[Source](https://www.defenseone.com/defense-systems/2026/09/cape-canaveral-building-its-counter-drone-defenses-space-force-says/416096/?oref=d1-topic-lander-top-story)

(U) Cape Canaveral, Fla. — The U.S. Space Force said it is adding counter-drone defenses at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station with support from the Joint Interagency Task Force 401, as launch activity ramps up and officials warn of growing vulnerability to small drones.
