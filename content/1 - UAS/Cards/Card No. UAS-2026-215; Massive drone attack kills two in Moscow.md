# Massive drone attack kills two in Moscow

[Source](https://www.rte.ie/news/2026/1006/1594202-massive-drone-attack-moscow/)

(U) MOSCOW, Russia — Hundreds of drones struck the Moscow region overnight, killing two men on the outskirts of the capital and injuring nine others, Russian authorities said, RTÉ reported on October 6, 2026. Moscow Mayor Sergei Sobyanin said on Telegram that 650 drones were launched toward the region and that most were destroyed by air defenses at long range. Regional governor Andrei Vorobyov said 185 drones were "destroyed or neutralised" over 16 municipalities, and that the attack killed two men, aged 71 and 20, while damaging 47 houses and four apartments. State news agency TASS described it as the second-largest drone attack on the capital region in two years. Operations at Moscow's four airports were disrupted, with air traffic restrictions imposed repeatedly overnight and into the morning.
