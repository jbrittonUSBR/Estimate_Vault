(U) Defense contractor Anduril Industries unveiled its first finished FQ-44 "Fury" autonomous aircraft—a semi-autonomous "loyal wingman" drone built for the U.S. Air Force—at its new "Arsenal-1" facility in Pickaway County, Ohio. The milestone comes just 18 months after the project was initially announced, marking a rapid turnaround supported by state incentives as the plant scales up to eventually produce thousands of advanced defense manufacturing jobs.
