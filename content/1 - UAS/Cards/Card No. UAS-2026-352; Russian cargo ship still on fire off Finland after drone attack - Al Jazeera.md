# Russian cargo ship still on fire off Finland after drone attack

[Source](https://slashdot.org/firehose.pl?op=view&id=186830269)

(U) Gulf of Finland, Finland — A fire burned aboard the Russian cargo ship Sparta II into Sunday after Finnish coastguard vessels spent the night spraying water on the vessel, which had drifted into Finnish territorial waters following a drone strike in international waters. Finland's military said the Saturday attack was likely carried out by Ukraine, President Alexander Stubb said the ship had since left Finnish waters after posing a 'serious danger,' and Prime Minister Petteri Orpo said the war was spilling into the region's immediate vicinity. All 16 crew members were evacuated to a nearby Russian ship; the vessel had been bound for Kaliningrad when it was damaged west of Gogland Island.
