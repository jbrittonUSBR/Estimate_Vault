# Businesses evacuated as Oxnard man arrested for possessing suspected IEDs

[Source](https://ktla.com/news/ventura-county/businesses-evacuated-as-oxnard-man-arrested-for-possessing-suspected-ieds/)

(U) Oxnard, California — KTLA reported that police arrested a man after suspected improvised explosive devices were found, prompting nearby business evacuations. Authorities examined the items and cleared the area; their identification as IEDs and the allegations against the arrested man remained subject to investigation.
