(U) Romania summoned the Russian ambassador after its forces shot down a third drone that entered its airspace. The incident is the latest in a series of drone intrusions linked to the war in Ukraine. Romanian authorities reported that the drone was neutralized without causing casualties or significant damage on the ground. Officials described the repeated violations as unacceptable and said they would continue to defend national airspace. The summons of the Russian envoy is intended to formally protest the incursions and demand explanations.
