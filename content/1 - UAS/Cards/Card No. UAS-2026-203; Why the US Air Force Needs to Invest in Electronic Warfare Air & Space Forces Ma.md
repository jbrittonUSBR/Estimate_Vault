# Why the US Air Force Needs to Invest in Electronic Warfare

[Source](https://www.airandspaceforces.com/why-the-us-air-force-needs-to-invest-in-electronic-warfare/)

(U) ARLINGTON, Va. — In a commentary published by Air & Space Forces Magazine, retired Lt. Gen. Lance Landrum argued that the U.S. Air Force should buy electronic warfare pods for its F-16 fleet, warning that roughly half of the active-duty, Air National Guard and Reserve F-16s will lack electromagnetic warfare protection with no clear plan. Landrum, a former F-16 weapons instructor who helped stand up the interagency Electromagnetic Spectrum Operations Cross Functional Team, cited Operation Epic Fury and Ukraine's experience to argue that radar warning receivers and electronic countermeasures are essential for surviving modern air defenses. He urged the service to leverage more than $1 billion that international partners have already invested in the L3Harris Viper Shield system, which the commentary promoted, and said pod-mounted systems would offer flexibility across deploying squadrons.
