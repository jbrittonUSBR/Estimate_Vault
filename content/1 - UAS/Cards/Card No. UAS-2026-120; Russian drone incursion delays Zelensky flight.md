# Russian drone incursion delays Zelensky flight

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — A Russian drone incursion into Polish airspace forced the delay of President Volodymyr Zelensky's flight as he departed for the United Kingdom. Polish authorities scrambled air defenses in response to the drones, highlighting growing concerns over Russian airspace violations and the security risks they pose to high-profile diplomatic travel.
