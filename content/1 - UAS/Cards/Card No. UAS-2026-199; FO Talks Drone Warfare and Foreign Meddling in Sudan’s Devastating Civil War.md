# FO Talks: Drone Warfare and Foreign Meddling in Sudan’s Devastating Civil War

[Source](https://www.fairobserver.com/region/middle_east_north_africa/fo-talks-drone-warfare-and-foreign-meddling-in-sudans-devastating-civil-war/)

(U) BELMONT, Calif. — In an October 2, 2026 episode of Fair Observer's FO Talks, assistant editor Casey Herrmann interviewed Fernando Carvajal, executive director of the American Center for South Yemen Studies, about Sudan's civil war between Gen. Abdel Fattah al-Burhan's Sudanese Armed Forces and Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo's Rapid Support Forces. Carvajal traced the conflict to the October 2021 military coup and the April 2023 collapse of the Burhan-Dagalo partnership, and argued that foreign rivalries over the Red Sea corridor — involving Chinese, Russian, Iranian, Turkish, Egyptian and Gulf interests — have deepened the humanitarian catastrophe. He said drone operations increasingly precede ground offensives, cited a Darfur strike that reportedly killed about 35 people and an attack on a UN humanitarian convoy, and described a diplomatic impasse over Islamist participation that has blocked humanitarian de-escalation efforts.
