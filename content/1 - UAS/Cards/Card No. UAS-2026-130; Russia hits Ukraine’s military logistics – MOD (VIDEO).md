# Russia hits Ukraine’s military logistics – MOD (VIDEO)

(U) MOSCOW, Russia — Russia's Ministry of Defense said its forces struck Ukrainian military logistics targets, releasing video footage of the strikes. According to the MoD, the operations employed drones and other standoff munitions to hit supply and transport nodes supporting Ukrainian frontline operations, part of an ongoing campaign against Ukraine's military sustainment infrastructure.
