# Russia markets drone-like missiles in Egypt during Su-57 jet's Africa air show debut

[Source](https://uk.news.yahoo.com/russia-markets-drone-missiles-egypt-161415144.html)

(U) EL ALAMEIN, Egypt — Russian state-owned arms exporter Rosoboronexport displayed drone-like missiles for its Sukhoi Su-57 fifth-generation stealth fighter in Egypt on September 9, 2026, as the warplane made its African air show debut at the second International Air Show and Aerospace Expo at El Alamein International Airport. On display in Russia's section were S-71M and S-71K missiles, which combine features of cruise missiles and low-cost attack drones, according to defence industry outlets; a Rosoboronexport representative said it was the first public showing, though the weapons had previously been used in combat. Egypt hosted the event from September 8 to 10, with more than 100 aircraft expected, and China also displayed its J-16 fighter jet weeks after joint exercises with Egypt.
