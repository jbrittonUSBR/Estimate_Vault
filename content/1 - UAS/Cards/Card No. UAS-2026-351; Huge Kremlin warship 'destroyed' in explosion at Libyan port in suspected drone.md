# Huge Kremlin warship 'destroyed' in explosion at Libyan port in suspected drone attack - after Russian cargo ship attacked in Baltic Sea

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16313277/Huge-Kremlin-warship-destroyed-explosion-Libyan-port-suspected-drone-attack-Russian-cargo-ship-attacked-Baltic-Sea.html)

(U) Benghazi, Libya — Libyan military officials told the Daily Mail that several sea drones launched from western Libya hit the Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi on Sunday, with four intercepted and two striking the ship, as video showed a large explosion and fire. Naval analyst H I Sutton, writing for Naval News, assessed that the vessel was almost certainly destroyed, but Libyan authorities suggested the blast may have originated in an ammunition depot and the cause remained unconfirmed. Eastern Libya's government said it opened a joint investigation with Russia and reported injuries but no deaths, while Ukraine had not claimed responsibility.
