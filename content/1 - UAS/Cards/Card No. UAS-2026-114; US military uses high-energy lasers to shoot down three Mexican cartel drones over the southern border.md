# US military uses high-energy lasers to shoot down three Mexican cartel drones over the southern border — narcos suspected of using UAVs for surveillance and reconnaissance to support illegal activities

[Source](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/drones/us-military-uses-high-energy-lasers-to-shoot-down-three-mexican-cartel-drones-over-the-southern-border-narcos-suspected-of-using-uavs-for-surveillance-and-reconnaissance-to-support-illegal-activities)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — The U.S. military shot down three drones suspected of operating for Mexican cartels along the southern border, destroying the uncrewed aerial vehicles with an "Army Multipurpose High Energy Laser System" operated by U.S. Northern Command between the evening of Tuesday, August 25, 2026, and the following morning. Maj. Gen. Curtis Taylor, commander of the border's Joint Task Force, said cartel networks were increasingly employing unmanned aircraft systems to facilitate illicit human smuggling and to spy on U.S. personnel and law enforcement, adding that the laser capability signaled the U.S. "will not tolerate hostile surveillance." It was unclear whether the drones were downed over American or Mexican airspace, and the military declined to release further details citing operational security.
