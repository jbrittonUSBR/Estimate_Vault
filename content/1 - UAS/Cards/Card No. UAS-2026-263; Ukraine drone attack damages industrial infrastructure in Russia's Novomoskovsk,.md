# Ukraine drone attack damages industrial infrastructure in Russia's Novomoskovsk, governor says

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/defence/ukraine-drone-attack-damages-industrial-infrastructure-in-russias-novomoskovsk-governor-says/articleshow/135252672.cms)

(U) Novomoskovsk, Russia — Reuters reported that falling debris from Ukrainian drones damaged industrial infrastructure, apartment buildings and private homes in Novomoskovsk, a chemical production hub about 200 km south of Moscow. Regional Governor Dmitry Milyayev said health and safety officials were dispatched to assess the situation, but no further details were provided.
