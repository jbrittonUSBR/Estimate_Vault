# NORTHCOM Expands Counter-UAS Operations Against Cartel Drones at the Southern Border

[Source](https://smallwarsjournal.com/2026/10/19/northcom-expands-counter-uas-operations-against-cartel-drones-at-the-southern-border/)

(U) U.S.-Mexico border — Small Wars Journal reported that U.S. Northern Command expanded Joint Task Force–Southern Border counter-UAS capabilities, including training 22 soldiers to operate a counter-small-UAS kit at Yuma Proving Ground. It cited NORTHCOM figures of more than 300 unmanned aircraft defeated during 2026 and 11 aircraft associated with cartel activity defeated by an Army high-energy laser. The article characterized the developments as a layered counter-UAS approach; the operational figures were attributed to the military.
