# Petraeus Has Close Call With Russian Drone Strike

[Source](https://www.newser.com/story/396373/petraeus-has-close-call-with-russian-drone-strike.html)

(U) YAHODYN, Ukraine — Former CIA director and retired four-star general David Petraeus was aboard a train near Ukraine's border with Poland when Russian drones flew overhead and prompted an evacuation; moments later an explosive drone struck the locomotive of a neighbouring train at Yahodyn station. No casualties were reported because that train had also been evacuated. Petraeus, who later reboarded and crossed safely into Poland, called the strike "the act of a truly barbaric leader trying to terrorize Ukrainian civilians," and Ukraine's railway system said a train carrying European diplomats, including former UK prime minister Boris Johnson, may have been the intended target.
