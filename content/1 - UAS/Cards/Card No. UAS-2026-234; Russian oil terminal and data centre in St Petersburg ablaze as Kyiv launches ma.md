# Russian Oil Terminal and Data Centre in St Petersburg Ablaze as Kyiv Launches Massive Drone Attack

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16218265/Russian-oil-St-Petersburg-Kyiv-drone-Trump-sanctions.html)

(U) ST. PETERSBURG, RUSSIA — The Daily Mail reported fires at an oil terminal and a data centre in St. Petersburg during a large Ukrainian drone attack, citing local reports and official statements. Its article connected the attack to contemporaneous U.S. policy decisions, but that political framing did not establish a causal link or Ukrainian intent. The report’s focus on damage at the two sites differed from Al Jazeera’s account, which reported one death, and POLITICO’s emphasis on the oil facility; the outlets’ impact claims should remain separately attributed.
