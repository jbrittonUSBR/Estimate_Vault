# Estonia blames Russia for arson at defence factory that produces vehicles Ukraine has used

[Source](https://www.cbc.ca/news/world/estonia-russia-allegation-9.7362016)

(U) TALLINN — Estonian Foreign Minister Margus Tsahkna said on September 29, 2026, that Russia's security services ordered the August 15 arson attack on Milrem Robotics in Tallinn, which makes ground drones used by Ukraine, and called it an act of sabotage. Three suspects were detained in Latvia and handed to Estonia, and Russia's ambassador was summoned; the Kremlin dismissed the findings as "baseless." NATO and EU leaders, including Mark Rutte, Emmanuel Macron and German Foreign Minister Johann Wadephul, expressed solidarity with Estonia and warned that Moscow was seeking to deter support for Ukraine.
