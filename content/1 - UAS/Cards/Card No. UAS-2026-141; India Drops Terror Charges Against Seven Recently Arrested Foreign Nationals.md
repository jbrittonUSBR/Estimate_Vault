# India Drops Terror Charges Against Seven Recently Arrested Foreign Nationals

[Source](https://thediplomat.com/2026/09/india-drops-terror-charges-against-seven-recently-arrested-foreign-nationals/)

(U) NEW DELHI, India — India's National Investigation Agency charged seven foreign nationals, including US citizen Matthew Aaron Van Dyke and six Ukrainians, under the Immigration and Foreigners Act rather than the anti-terrorism Unlawful Activities Prevention Act, six months after their March arrest under the tougher law. The seven were accused of entering Mizoram without the required protected-area permit and crossing into Myanmar, where the agency alleged they demonstrated drones and jamming technology to ethnic armed groups. The shift triggered opposition Congress accusations that the government had bowed to US pressure to release Van Dyke, while officials said the immigration charges had already been established and the UAPA investigation was continuing.
