# Drone Stocks Slide as Risk-Off Sentiment Hits High-Beta Names: AeroVironment Sinks 4%, Red Cat Drops 3%, Ondas Eases

[Source](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/09/09/drone-stocks-slide-as-risk-off-sentiment-hits-high-beta-names-aerovironment-sinks-4-red-cat-drops-3-ondas-eases/)

(U) NEW YORK, United States — Shares of drone and defense technology companies fell on September 9, 2026, as risk-off sentiment hit high-beta names despite no company-specific news. AeroVironment (NASDAQ: AVAV) dropped 4 percent to $142.40, extending a 41 percent year-to-date decline even with a record $2.7 billion backlog, while Red Cat (NASDAQ: RCAT) fell 3 percent to $8.41 and Ondas (NASDAQ: ONDS) eased 2 percent to $7.50. The Defiance Drone and Modern Warfare ETF (NYSEARCA: JEDI) slid 2 percent against a 0.4 percent decline in the SPDR S&P 500 ETF Trust (SPY), with the outlet framing the move as a rotation out of speculative beta rather than broad-market weakness.
