# Trump Says Iran Strikes to Be Short, Asserts Hormuz Control

[Source](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/09/03/883847.htm)

(U) WASHINGTON — President Donald Trump said renewed US attacks on Iran would likely be "not too long," reiterating his claim that the US controls the Strait of Hormuz after fresh fighting spiked energy prices. The US military carried out a second round of strikes in three days, targeting radar systems and mine-laying capabilities along Iran's southern coast, while Iran retaliated with drone and missile volleys on US bases across the Middle East. Tehran accused the US of hitting a wedding ceremony in the southern city of Sirik, with the Iranian Red Crescent reporting at least four dead and about 67 injured, while US Central Command denied targeting civilians; Brent crude slipped 0.6% to $95.10 a barrel after Trump's comments.
