# More than 300 calls to police after hoax bomb threats at schools

[Source](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/ckwy4549y0eqo)

(U) Scotland and Northern Ireland — The BBC reported that Police Scotland received more than 300 calls after hoax bomb-threat emails went to schools, and that similar messages were sent to about 300 schools in Northern Ireland. Police cybercrime officers investigated the source, while authorities said they did not consider the threats credible. The teaching union described the scale as unusual, and councils contacted parents with reassurance; disruption to learning was limited.
