# Trends in UAS at AFA 2026

[Source](https://dsm.forecastinternational.com/2026/09/23/trends-in-uas-at-afa-2026/)

(U) NATIONAL HARBOR, Maryland — At the 2026 Air & Space Forces Association Air, Space & Cyber Conference, industry unveiled several autonomous UAS developments. Textron Aviation and Merlin introduced the SkyCourier UX, an autonomous variant of the Cessna SkyCourier for contested logistics with up to 6,000 lb payload and ~900 nm range. Swarm Aero revealed a full-scale mockup of its Group 5 Gamera UAS designed for the DIU Mass Modular Aircraft program with ~2,500 nm mission radius, modular payloads, and an estimated ~$10 million unit cost. Observers noted broader trends toward rapid acquisition, distributed capabilities at tactical levels, and pairing low-cost airframes with software-defined autonomy and swarming concepts.
