# Ukraine targets Russian space infrastructure to disrupt military operations

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/ukraine-targets-russian-space-infrastructure-to-disrupt-military-operations/)

(U) KYIV, Ukraine — Ukrainian long-range drones and missiles targeted Russian space-related infrastructure, striking facilities that support Moscow's military satellite, navigation, and communications capabilities, in what analysts described as a strategic shift toward disrupting deep-seated military assets. The Russian Aerospace Forces, led by commander Aleksandr Chayko since May, operate satellites and ground-space facilities used for intelligence gathering, navigation, and coordination of ground troops and drone and missile attacks, most of which are located in western Russia within reach of Ukrainian weapons. Researchers said the attacks could produce problems across Russia's space programme, including its civilian and international components.
