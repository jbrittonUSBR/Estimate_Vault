# Saudi Arabia intercepts missiles and drones as fighting with Houthis rages

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/25/saudi-arabia-intercepts-missiles-and-drones-as-fighting-with-houthis-rages)

(U) Riyadh and southern Saudi Arabia — Al Jazeera reported that the Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted missiles and drones launched toward Riyadh and southern areas. Saudi Civil Defence said debris from two intercepted missiles injured two people and damaged buildings and vehicles in Khamis Mushait and Ahad Rafidah; Houthi claims and Saudi statements were attributed to their respective sources.
