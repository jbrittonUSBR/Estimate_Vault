# Texas Neighborhood Fights Back Over Amazon Prime Air Drones

[Source](https://www.today.com/video/texas-neighborhood-fights-back-over-amazon-prime-air-drones-270356037616)

(U) TEXAS, United States — Residents in a Texas neighborhood have pushed back against Amazon Prime Air drone deliveries, citing concerns over noise, privacy, safety and quality of life as the company tests autonomous package delivery in the area. Local opposition has highlighted growing community resistance to low-altitude commercial drone operations in residential neighborhoods as FAA-regulated delivery programs expand.
