# US military orders 4,000 one-way attack UAVs with drone swarm control

[Source](https://interestingengineering.com/military/4000-attack-drones-swarm-control-us-military)

(U) AUBURN HILLS, Michigan — The U.S. Department of War ordered 4,000 Kiwi one-way attack drones from Swarm Defense Technologies under the Drone Dominance Program, with a minimum contract value of $25 million. The quadcopter strike drones are paired with Swarm Defense’s Strike Deck ground control software, which allows a single operator to coordinate multiple aircraft simultaneously with automated navigation and terminal guidance while leaving target selection and engagement authorization to human operators. Production will take place at the company’s 40,000-square-foot facility in Michigan.
