# Russian cargo ship burns in Baltic Sea after drone strike

[Source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-22/russian-cargo-ship-burns-in-baltic-sea-after-drone-strike/107413782)

(U) Helsinki, Finland — The Russian cargo ship Sparta II caught fire in the Gulf of Finland after a drone strike in international waters west of Gogland Island on Saturday, then drifted into Finnish waters where its 16 crew transferred to a nearby Russian ship. Finnish armed forces spokesman Aki-Mauri Huhtinen said Ukraine was likely behind the strike, and Prime Minister Petteri Orpo said the war was 'spilling over into our neighbouring areas' as Finland imposed temporary air and maritime restrictions. The US-sanctioned vessel is linked to defence logistics company Oboronlogistika, and the Finnish Border Guard said military cargo could not be ruled out.
