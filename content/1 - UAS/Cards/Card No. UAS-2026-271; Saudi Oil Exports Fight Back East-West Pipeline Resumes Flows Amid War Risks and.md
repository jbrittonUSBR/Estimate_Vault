# Saudi Oil Exports Fight Back: East-West Pipeline Resumes Flows Amid War Risks and Shipping Disruptions

[Source](https://www.naturalnews.com/2026-10-29-saudi-east-west-pipeline-resumes-amid-war-risks.html)

(U) Saudi Arabia — Natural News, citing Energy Minister Prince Abdulaziz bin Salman and other reporting, said the East-West pipeline had moved 5.8 million barrels of crude by 6 October after a September drone attack shut the line and halted loadings at Yanbu. Saudi officials disputed a later Houthi claim that pipeline flows had been interrupted.
