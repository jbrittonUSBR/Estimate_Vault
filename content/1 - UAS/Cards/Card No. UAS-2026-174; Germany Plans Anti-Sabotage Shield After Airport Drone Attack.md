# Germany plans anti-sabotage shield after airport drone attack

[Source](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/germany-plans-anti-sabotage-shield-after-airport-drone-attack-bild-reports-2026-09-06/)

(U) BERLIN, Germany — Following a failed drone attack at a German airport in late August 2026, the German government announced plans for a broad anti-sabotage package. The measures include expanding police mobile drone-defense capabilities, granting critical infrastructure operators authority to actively counter drones, establishing a national "cyberdome" for digital intrusion detection, and increasing AI-supported surveillance. Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt said Russian hybrid attacks had become part of daily reality and that "these attacks will increase, but we can defend and protect ourselves."
