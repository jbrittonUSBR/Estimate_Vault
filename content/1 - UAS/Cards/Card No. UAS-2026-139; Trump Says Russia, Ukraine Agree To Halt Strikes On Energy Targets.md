# Trump Says Russia, Ukraine Agree To Halt Strikes On Energy Targets

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/ukraine/2026/09/ukraine-260914-rferl01.htm)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — U.S. President Donald Trump said Russia and Ukraine had agreed not to target each other's energy facilities, a day after he criticized Kyiv for hitting Russian oil refineries, RFE/RL reported via GlobalSecurity.org. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy responded that Kyiv was ready to stop strikes on Russia if Ukraine's allies ensured Moscow would cease hitting Ukrainian energy facilities, infrastructure and food supply routes, saying a de-escalatory step on critical infrastructure could be the first step toward peace. The Kremlin welcomed Trump's call for Ukraine to halt strikes on Russian energy infrastructure, but spokesman Dmitry Peskov did not say whether Moscow would reciprocate and said Russian forces would continue operating across Ukraine.
