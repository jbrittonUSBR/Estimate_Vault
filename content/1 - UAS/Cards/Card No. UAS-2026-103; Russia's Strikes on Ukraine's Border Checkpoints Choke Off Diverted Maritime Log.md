# Russia's Strikes on Ukraine's Border Checkpoints Choke Off Diverted Maritime Logistics Pipeline

[Source](https://sputnikglobe.com/20260909/russias-strikes-on-ukraines-border-checkpoints-choke-off-diverted-maritime-logistics-pipeline-1124704396.html)

(U) MOSCOW, Russia — Sputnik reported on September 9, 2026 that a string of Russian drone strikes on the Starokozachye border crossing between Ukraine and Moldova would "seriously hinder — or at least temporarily delay" the shipment of arms into Ukraine and exports going out, according to Institute of CIS Countries senior fellow Alexander Dudchak. Combined with a recent strike on the Orlovka-Isaccea crossing with Romania, Dudchak said the targeting of Ukraine's southern border checkpoints was designed to counter Kyiv's attempts to circumvent Russia's de facto blockade of its sea and river ports. He stated that shipments had frequently been rerouted to Romanian ports and moved overland through Romania and Moldova, and that Russia's military leadership was tracking arms supply routes and taking preemptive measures.
