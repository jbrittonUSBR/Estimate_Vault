(U) New details from the investigation into the August 2026 explosive-laden drone found near a Ukrainian cargo plane at Germany's Leipzig-Halle Airport reveal that the explosive materials were cleverly concealed inside a **can of corn**.
### **Key Details**

- **The Smuggling Method:** According to reports from European investigative outlets (such as RMF24), the containers were smuggled into Europe as part of a wider logistics pipeline operated by Russian military intelligence. A recruited courier retrieved the containers—disguised as ordinary canned corn—from a Lithuanian cemetery before transporting them and mailing parcels containing detonators and flammable substances.
- **Broader Sabotage Campaign:** Investigators have linked these containers to a series of coordinated hybrid warfare attacks and mysterious package fires across Europe, including incidents at a DHL warehouse in Birmingham, a transport hub near Warsaw, and a prior 2024 firebombing at the Leipzig airport logistics center.
- **The Connection to the Drone:** A similar can packed with powerful explosives instead of food was attached to the quadcopter drone found resting near the Ukrainian An-124 cargo aircraft. The device ultimately failed to detonate on-site because its detonator was jarred loose.
- **Ongoing Investigation:** While security authorities continue to piece together the international courier network run via encrypted messaging apps, the discovery further reinforces Western intelligence assessments pointing to state-sponsored Russian sabotage targeting critical European aviation and NATO supply hubs.
