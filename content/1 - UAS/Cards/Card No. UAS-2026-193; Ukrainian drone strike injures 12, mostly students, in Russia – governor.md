# Ukrainian drone strike injures 12, mostly students, in Russia – governor

[Source](https://www.rt.com/russia/646479-drone-ukraine-civilians-bryansk/)

(U) Bryansk, Russia — RT, a Russian state outlet, reported that acting Governor Egor Kovalchuk said 12 people, most of them students, were injured in a Ukrainian drone attack on the city of Trubchevsk in Russia's Bryansk Region. Kovalchuk said at least seven of the wounded were students and one victim was in serious condition, adding that children were among the injured. The report said that two days earlier Ukrainian drone strikes had damaged a railway bridge and tracks at three locations in the region, causing several trains to derail, and that Moscow accused Kiev of deliberately targeting civilians with long-range drones.
