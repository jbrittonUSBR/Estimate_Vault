# Pentagon Clears Way for Anti-Drone Tech in Hegseth Memo

[Source](https://thehill.com/policy/defense/6125703-pentagon-clears-way-for-anti-drone-tech-hegseth-memo/)

(U) WASHINGTON — Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth signed a memo clearing the way for the Pentagon to accelerate procurement and fielding of counter-unmanned aircraft systems (C-UAS) technology. The directive streamlines acquisition pathways and delegates expanded authority to service components and combatant commands to buy and deploy anti-drone systems, reflecting concern over the rapid proliferation of hostile and unauthorized drone activity over U.S. and overseas military installations. The memo is intended to compress the fielding timeline for detection, electronic-warfare, and kinetic defeat capabilities that had been slowed by the traditional requirements and acquisition process.
