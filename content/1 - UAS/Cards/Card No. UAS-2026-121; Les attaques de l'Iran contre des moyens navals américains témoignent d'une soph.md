# Iran's attacks on US naval assets show increased missile sophistication

[Source](https://english.hani.co.kr/arti/english_edition/e_international/1277211.html)

(U) SEOUL, South Korea — In a September 10, 2026 analysis, the Hankyoreh reported that Iran had escalated attacks on US naval assets in response to a US blockade of the Strait of Hormuz, launching ballistic missiles at US warships, with the US retaliating by striking Iranian oil tankers near Kharg Island. The report said Iran's recent strikes, though not damaging any US warships, involved increasingly sophisticated weapons, raising alarms that Tehran may be receiving assistance from China or Russia; US officials cited in The Wall Street Journal also voiced concern over how Iran tracks US vessels. Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps was said to have warned crew members of tankers in Kuwaiti and Bahraini ports to abandon ship, and claimed to have captured a US underwater drone that the US acknowledged had become defective. Separately, the report said Yemen's Iran-backed Houthis attacked four Saudi cities, wounding over 70 people and setting fire to oil facilities in what Reuters called the biggest strike on Saudi Arabia since the start of the Iran war, as Brent crude rose toward US$100 a barrel.
