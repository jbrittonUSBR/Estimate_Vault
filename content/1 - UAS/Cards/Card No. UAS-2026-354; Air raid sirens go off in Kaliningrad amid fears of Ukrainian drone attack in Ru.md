# Air raid sirens go off in Kaliningrad amid fears of Ukrainian drone attack in Russia's European enclave - a month after Putin threatened to use nuclear weapons to defend it

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16312901/Air-raid-sirens-Kaliningrad-fears-Ukrainian-drone-attack-Russia-Kaliningrad.html)

(U) Kaliningrad, Russia — Kaliningrad governor Alexei Besprozvannykh said air raid sirens sounded after a 'suspicious object' was detected coming from the direction of Lithuania and restrictions were imposed on airport flights in the Russian exclave. The Daily Mail found no evidence of a Ukrainian attack and reported that images circulated online as purported Kaliningrad strikes actually came from the war in Iran. Lithuania's foreign minister categorically denied authorising its airspace for an attack on Russia, days after President Vladimir Putin warned that a direct attack on Kaliningrad would put all available Russian weapons on the agenda.
