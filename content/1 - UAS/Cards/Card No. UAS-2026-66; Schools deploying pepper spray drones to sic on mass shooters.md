(U) **New Security Measure:** Several schools across Colorado, Florida, and Georgia are implementing "Campus Guardian Angel" drones developed by Mithril Defense to counter active shooters.

(U) **How They Work:** Housed in secure indoor boxes, the drones are remotely piloted from Texas within seconds of an alert. They travel up to 50–60 mph and are designed to overwhelm an assailant using sirens, strobe lights, and pepper gel/balls, or to act as a kinetic barrier by ramming the attacker as a last resort. They can also stream live footage and help guide law enforcement.

(U) **Controversy:** While proponents view the technology as a vital, proactive layer of defense inspired by battlefield tactics, critics and child safety experts raise concerns over student anxiety, potential technical malfunctions, misidentified targets, and whether funds would be better spent addressing the root causes of violence.
