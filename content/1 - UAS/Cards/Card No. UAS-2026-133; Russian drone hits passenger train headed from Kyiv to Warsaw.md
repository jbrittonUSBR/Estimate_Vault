# Russian drone hits passenger train headed from Kyiv to Warsaw

[Source](https://www.politico.eu/article/russia-attacks-gas-station-passenger-train-near-ukraine-poland-border/)

(U) BRUSSELS, Belgium — Russian drones attacked a border crossing on the Polish-Ukrainian frontier on the morning of September 13, striking the locomotive of a passenger train bound for Warsaw two kilometers inside Ukraine's Volyn Oblast. Ukraine's state rail operator said the crew had been warned of the threat in advance and no one was injured, while separate footage showed a large fire at a gas station near the Yahodyn–Dorohusk crossing, with no reported casualties. Ukrainian Foreign Minister Andriy Sybiha said Russian forces were "knocking directly on the doors of the EU and NATO" and urged allies to "throw the full weight" of sanctions at Moscow.
