# Ukraine says it hit 2 Russian oil refineries as Zelenskyy hails weapons produced in the country

[Source](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/ukraine-hit-2-russian-oil-refineries-zelenskyy-hails-138250394)

(U) Krasnodar region, Russia — Ukraine’s General Staff said strikes hit storage tanks at the Ilsky refinery and a processing unit at the Afipsky refinery. It said both facilities supply Russian forces and can process about 95 million barrels of oil annually; Russian officials confirmed attacks on two industrial sites but did not identify them. The Associated Press also reported that President Volodymyr Zelenskyy praised Ukraine’s domestically produced weapons after a separate strike damaged the Kerch Bridge.
