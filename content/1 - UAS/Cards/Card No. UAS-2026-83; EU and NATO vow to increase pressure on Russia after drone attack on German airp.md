# EU and NATO vow to increase pressure on Russia after drone attack on German airport

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/eu-nato-pressure-russia-germany-drone-attack/)

(U) BRUSSELS, Belgium — The EU and NATO vowed to increase pressure on Russia after Germany accused Moscow of carrying out an explosive drone attack at Leipzig/Halle Airport, where a drone carrying explosives was discovered near a Ukrainian cargo aircraft on August 4. European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen called the incident a serious escalation and pledged to keep Europe united, while NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte said Germany had presented clear evidence of Russian involvement. Germany announced it would close the Russian Consulate General in Bonn and terminate the agreement covering the Russian House of Science and Culture in Berlin; Moscow rejected the allegations as "entirely fabricated" and warned of consequences.
