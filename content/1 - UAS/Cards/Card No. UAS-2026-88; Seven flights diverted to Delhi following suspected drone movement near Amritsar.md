# Seven flights diverted to Delhi following suspected drone movement near Amritsar airport

[Source](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/economy/logistics/seven-flights-diverted-to-delhi-following-suspected-drone-movement-near-amritsar-airport/article71437681.ece)

(U) AMRITSAR, India — On the night of September 6, 2026, seven flights were diverted from Sri Guru Ram Dass Ji International Airport in Amritsar to Delhi after the reported sighting of multiple suspected drones near the airport, according to the Press Trust of India. Two of the diverted flights were international services, and carriers affected included Malaysia Airlines, IndiGo, Air India, and Air India Express. Airport operations were suspended for roughly one and a half hours following the sightings, which occurred around 10:00 p.m. local time. Punjab Police were alerted and were investigating the incident.
