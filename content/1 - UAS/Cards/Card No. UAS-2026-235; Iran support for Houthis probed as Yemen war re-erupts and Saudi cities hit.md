# Iran support for Houthis probed as Yemen war re-erupts and Saudi cities hit

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/19/iran-support-for-houthis-probed-as-yemen-war-re-erupts-and-saudi-cities-hit)

(U) Yemen and Saudi Arabia — Al Jazeera reported that media accounts and officials alleged Iran had provided the Houthis with training, intelligence, and tactical support for drone and missile attacks. The article said the allegations were difficult to verify and discussed them amid renewed Houthi attacks on Saudi cities and infrastructure. Claims of Iranian assistance and attack details were attributed to cited sources rather than independently confirmed.
