# Police arrest suspect in Germany power grid attacks after dayslong search

[Source](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/police-arrest-suspect-germany-power-grid-attacks-after-136269467)

(U) Berlin, Germany — Police arrested a 48-year-old man in connection with a series of attacks on power distribution systems across Germany that prompted a massive, dayslong search. The suspect was detained near a power plant in Weisweiler, in the western state of North Rhine-Westphalia, carrying explosives and offering no resistance, Cologne police said; a nearby tent was found containing additional explosive charges. He was alleged to have used homemade rockets to shoot thin wires across high-voltage power lines to cause short circuits spanning North Rhine-Westphalia, Brandenburg and Saxony, and letters found at the scenes justified the attacks as part of the fight against fossil fuels. Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt said the acts were believed to have been committed by a lone activist connected to climate extremism.
