# Ministry of Defence and Brave1 conduct large-scale testing of drones with AI guidance

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/ukraine/2026/09/ukraine-260908-ukraine-mod03.htm)

(U) KYIV, Ukraine — The Ministry of Defence of Ukraine and the Brave1 defense innovation cluster conducted large-scale testing of unmanned aerial vehicles equipped with automatic guidance modules for moving ground targets. Seven companies developing autonomous drone solutions took part; in the test scenario each drone had to fly 2 kilometers, detect and acquire a moving lightly armored vehicle from at least 500 meters, and hit it in fully autonomous mode. Six of the seven manufacturers passed the test and had their solutions recommended for procurement, and the Ministry stated that successful AI-guided strikes have increased tenfold since the start of the year. The announcement was issued by the Ukrainian Ministry of Defence, which noted that a human still makes the decision to carry out a strike.
