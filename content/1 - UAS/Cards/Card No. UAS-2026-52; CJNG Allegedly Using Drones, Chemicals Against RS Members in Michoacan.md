(U//FOUO/REL TO USA, FVEY) TEXT: 1. On 15 July 2026, a Mexican cartel-focused social media account claimed sicarios from the Dos Aguas faction-an armed group affiliated with the New Generation Jalisco Cartel (CJNG)-clashed with "R5" cartel members in Michoacan, Mexico, and highlighted their use of drones to launch explosives during the attacks. The social media account posted a video that included text claiming CJNG forces carried out a chemical attack against "R5" members and emphasized that this specific allegation remained unverified and warranted caution.1
