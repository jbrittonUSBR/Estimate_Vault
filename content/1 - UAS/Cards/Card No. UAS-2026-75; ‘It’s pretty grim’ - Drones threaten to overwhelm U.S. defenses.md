(U) Former defense and intelligence officials have raised serious alarms, warning that the United States is currently "not ready" to effectively stop a drone attack on its home soil.
### **Key Takeaways**

- **Vulnerabilities at Home:** Security focus has heightened following escalating threats and a thwarted plot involving drones targeting a White House event over the summer, as well as heightened security measures around high-profile locations like President Trump's Mar-a-Lago and Bedminster estates.
- **Slow Modernization:** While the Pentagon established air defense frameworks around Washington, D.C., following the September 11 attacks, critics and former officials emphasize that modernization efforts have moved too slowly to keep pace with rapid advancements in unmanned aerial systems.
- **Calls for Urgency:** Retired General Glenn Van Herk, who previously oversaw North American aerial defense, stressed the urgent need to accelerate the deployment of defensive infrastructure to adequately protect critical domestic infrastructure.
