# ‘Energy ceasefire’ a mirage as Ukraine races to repair and protect critical infrastructure

[Source](https://www.irishtimes.com/world/europe/2026/10/15/energy-ceasefire-a-mirage-as-ukraine-races-to-repair-and-protect-critical-infrastructure/)

(U) UKRAINE — The Irish Times reported that Ukraine was repairing and reinforcing energy infrastructure after continued Russian attacks, as an announced energy ceasefire appeared to unravel. The article described the strain on utilities and the preparations for winter, including work to protect facilities and restore services. It also said Kyiv intended to resume strikes on Russian oil refineries after Russia continued attacks on Ukrainian energy targets.
