# Russian drone strike hits Ukrainian train station near Poland border

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/russian-drone-strike-hits-ukrainian-train-station-near-poland-border/)

(U) LUTSK, Ukraine — Russian Shahed drones struck railway facilities, including a passenger train, at the station in Lutsk, about two kilometres from Ukraine's border with Poland, according to an account citing reporting by The New York Times. Passengers and staff were evacuated and no casualties were reported. The strike was described as part of an escalating pattern of Russian attacks on Ukrainian transport infrastructure intended to disrupt logistics and civilian mobility, and its proximity to NATO member Poland was characterised as a warning to Kyiv's allies.
