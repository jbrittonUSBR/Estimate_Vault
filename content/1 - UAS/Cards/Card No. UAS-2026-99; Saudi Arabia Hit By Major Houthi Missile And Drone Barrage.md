# Saudi Arabia Hit By Major Houthi Missile And Drone Barrage

[Source](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/politics/saudi-arabia-attacks-73-hurt-as-houthis-strike-4-cities-and-oil-facilities-burn)

(U) RIYADH, Saudi Arabia — Yemen's Tehran-backed Houthis attacked four cities in southern Saudi Arabia, wounding 73 people and setting oil installations ablaze in what appeared to be a major expansion of the six-month-old Middle East war. The Houthis said they used drones and missiles to strike a Saudi airbase in the southern city of Khamis Mushait as well as Saudi state oil company assets in Abha, Najran and Jazan; NASA satellite imagery showed thick black smoke over the Jazan refinery. Saudi authorities said women and children were among the 73 hurt, and reports of such large casualty numbers suggested the attacks were among the biggest against Saudi Arabia since the US and Israel launched their war against Iran in February. The strikes came as fighting in the Gulf resumed, with Iran and the US exchanging fire and global oil prices climbing back toward $100 a barrel.
