# Iran captures US submarine drone in Strait of Hormuz

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/iran/2026/09/iran-260908-irna05.htm)

(U) TEHRAN, Iran — The Islamic Revolution Guards Corps (IRGC) Navy announced the seizure of an unmanned American submarine near the entrance to the Strait of Hormuz, claiming its naval forces captured "one of the most modern, smart and unmanned submarines of the US terrorist army" in a complex intelligence and operational operation at dawn. The IRGC identified the craft as a Dive-LD manufactured by Anduril Industries, describing it as about 5.8 meters long and nearly three tons and designed for independent long-duration deep-water missions. The report was carried by Iran's state-run Islamic Republic News Agency (IRNA) and reflects Iranian state-media claims that had not been independently verified at the time of publication.
