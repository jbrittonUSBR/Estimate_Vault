# Two German citizens arrested over suspected Starlink equipment arson attempt in Vilnius

[Source](https://www.lrt.lt/en/news-in-english/19/3097187/two-german-citizens-arrested-over-suspected-starlink-equipment-arson-attempt-in-vilnius)

(U) LITHUANIA — Lithuanian authorities identified two men suspected of attempting to set fire to Starlink equipment in Vilnius earlier this month; both men, who live in Germany, were arrested there at Bulgaria's request, and investigators linked them to similar arson attacks targeting Starlink equipment in Poland and Bulgaria.
