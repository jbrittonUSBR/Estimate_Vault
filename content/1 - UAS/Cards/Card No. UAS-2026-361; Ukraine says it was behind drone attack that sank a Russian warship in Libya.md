# Ukraine says it was behind drone attack that sank a Russian warship in Libya

[Source](https://www.yahoo.com/news/world/articles/zelenskiy-says-ukraine-sank-russian-075344713.html)

(U) Benghazi, Libya — President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said on Monday that maritime drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury at the Benghazi naval base on Sunday and also struck the cargo ship MV Angara, which he said Moscow used to transport weapons; Reuters said it verified video showing fire and smoke from both vessels. Ukraine's SBU said the drones were launched from a mother ship and that a Ka-27 helicopter was destroyed, while Russia's Defence Ministry made no immediate comment. Osama Hamad, prime minister of Libya's eastern-based government, said he asked the UN to investigate suspicions the drones were launched from western Libya, and the Tripoli-based government condemned the operation.
