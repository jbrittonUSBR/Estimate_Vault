# Suspected Russian spy behind drone attack in Leipzig

[Source](https://www.politico.eu/article/suspect-russia-spy-drone-attack-in-leipzig-halle-airport-germany/)

(U) BERLIN, Germany — German security authorities identified with high probability a Belarusian citizen and a Russian national linked to Russia's GRU military intelligence as the main suspects in the August explosive-laden drone attack on Leipzig-Halle airport, according to confidential investigative files reviewed by Welt and reported by POLITICO. The Russian suspect allegedly handled planning and logistics, entering Germany via Turkey through Berlin-Brandenburg Airport and later departing for Serbia, while the Belarusian man entered the Schengen area through Latvia on July 28 as a suspected "disposable agent" hired to carry out sabotage. Investigators said about 1.8 kilograms of Semtex was to be spread across three drones targeting a Ukrainian Antonov cargo aircraft holding roughly 25,000 liters of jet fuel, and German authorities were coordinating with counterparts in Lithuania, Poland, Latvia, Finland and the U.S. FBI. Foreign Minister Johann Wadephul and Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt publicly held Russia responsible on September 1, and Moscow denied involvement.
