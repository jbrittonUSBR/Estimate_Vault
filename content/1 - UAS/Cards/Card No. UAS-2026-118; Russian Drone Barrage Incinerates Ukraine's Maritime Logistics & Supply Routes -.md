# Russian Drone Barrage Incinerates Ukraine's Maritime Logistics & Supply Routes - MoD

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/ukraine/2026/09/ukraine-260910-sputnik04.htm)

(U) MOSCOW, Russia — Russia's Defense Ministry said in a statement that its Armed Forces struck a Ukrainian drone depot in the city of Mykolaiv, hitting "a warehouse for unmanned aerial vehicles intended to supply the armed forces of Ukraine," according to a Sputnik report republished by GlobalSecurity.org on September 10, 2026. The ministry also claimed Russian forces struck two dry-cargo ships bound for the port of Odesa that it said were transporting supplies for Ukraine's armed forces. It further stated that Russian forces hit infrastructure facilities used for storing military cargo in the port of Chornomorsk. (State-media claim; not independently verified.)
