# 16 Ecuadorian fishermen sue US government, allege drone attack destroyed their boat near the Galápagos

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/16-ecuadorian-fishermen-sue-us-government-allege-drone-attack-destroyed-boat-galapagos)

(U) Pacific Ocean near the Galápagos — Sixteen Ecuadorian fishermen sued the U.S. government, alleging a U.S. drone strike destroyed their fishing boat on March 17 while they were returning from a two-week trip. The lawsuit said all 16 survived, two suffered serious burns, and the crew was detained and later held at a migrant center in El Salvador. The Defense Department and Coast Guard denied knowledge of or involvement in the attack.
