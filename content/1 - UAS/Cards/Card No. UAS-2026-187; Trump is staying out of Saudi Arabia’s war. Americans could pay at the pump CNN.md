# Trump is staying out of Saudi Arabia’s war. Americans could pay at the pump

[Source](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/24/middleeast/trump-saudi-houthis-war-oil-gas-prices-intl)

(U) RIYADH, Saudi Arabia — In a CNN analysis from Riyadh, correspondent Matthew Chance reported that Yemen’s Iran-backed Houthis struck the Saudi capital for the first time in years, with thick black smoke seen rising from fuel tanks near King Khalid International Airport, and that the group has seized effective control of the Bab al-Mandeb Strait and declared a blockade of Saudi shipping. A source familiar told CNN that Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman personally requested U.S. military intervention and was rebuffed, while President Donald Trump said his red line remains strikes on the Houthis, with whom he says Washington is talking. Analysts and a former Gulf official quoted by CNN warned that attacks on the Saudi East-West pipeline, together with the partial closure of the Strait of Hormuz since the February 28 U.S.-Israeli war on Iran, threaten global oil supply and U.S. gasoline prices ahead of the November midterm elections.
