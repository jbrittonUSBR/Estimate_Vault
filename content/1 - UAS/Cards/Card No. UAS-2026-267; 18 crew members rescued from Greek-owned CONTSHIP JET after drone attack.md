# 18 crew members rescued from Greek-owned CONTSHIP JET after drone attack

[Source](https://www.naftemporiki.gr/english/2181537/18-crew-members-rescued-from-greek-owned-contship-jet-after-drone-attack/)

(U) Black Sea, off Bulgaria — Naftemporiki reported that the shipowner confirmed all 18 crew of the Greek-owned CONTSHIP JET were rescued after three unmanned surface vessels struck the ship on 28 October about 16 nautical miles southeast of Cape Kaliakra. The article said the attacker had not been identified.
