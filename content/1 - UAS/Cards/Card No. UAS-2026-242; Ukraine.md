# Ukraine's Fourth Law: Jamming Fails Once a Drone Picks Its Own Target

[Source](https://dronexl.co/2026/10/19/fourth-law-jamming-fails-autonomous-drone-picks-target/)

(U) Ukraine — DroneXL reported that Ukrainian autonomy developer The Fourth Law argued that electronic warfare becomes less effective against drones able to navigate and attack autonomously. A company representative said Ukraine was encountering new Russian autonomous systems regularly and argued that physical interceptors could remain effective when there was no operator link to jam. The article also cited Ukrainian Defense Ministry and Brave1 figures on drone orders and AI-guided strikes; these capability and scale claims were attributed to Ukrainian sources.
