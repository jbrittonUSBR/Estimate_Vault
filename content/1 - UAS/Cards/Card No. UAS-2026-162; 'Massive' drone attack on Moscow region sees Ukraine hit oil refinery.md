# 'Massive' drone attack on Moscow region sees Ukraine hit oil refinery

[Source](https://freerepublic.com/focus/f-news/4396226/posts)

(U) Moscow, Russia — A “massive” Ukrainian drone attack on the Moscow Region killed at least two people and injured six, according to the regional governor. Moscow Mayor Sergey Sobyanin said hundreds of drones were shot down en route to the capital, while the city’s main oil refinery was damaged and several homes were set ablaze; Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy confirmed Kyiv had struck a key Russian oil facility and a logistics site.
