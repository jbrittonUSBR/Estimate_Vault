# New attacks in Hormuz and Saudi test nerves as war's spread worsens oil disruption

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/world-news/us-iran-war-new-attacks-on-strait-of-hormuz-shipping-fans-fears-of-threats-to-oil-supplies/articleshow/134192179.cms)

(U) DUBAI, United Arab Emirates — The British maritime security agency UKMTO reported on September 13 that a vessel was struck by a projectile while transiting the Strait of Hormuz, causing a fire and forcing the crew to evacuate, while Iran said one person was killed and four crew wounded aboard an Iranian commercial vessel struck off its coast. The incidents followed drone strikes two days earlier that forced Saudi Arabia to shut down its 1,200-kilometer East-West oil pipeline, the main route bypassing Hormuz, with traders estimating that crude stored at the Red Sea port of Yanbu could sustain exports for only five to seven days and that as much as 4 percent of global oil supply could be jeopardized. U.S. diesel prices rose to a record above $6.20 a gallon and oil had already surged above $100 a barrel, as Houthi forces advanced in Yemen and Iranian officials prepared to meet Gulf states in Oman over future shipping arrangements through the strait.
