# Russia pounds Kyiv with drone strikes after Zelenskiy-Trump talks

[Source](https://www.detroitnews.com/story/news/world/2026/09/23/russia-pounds-kyiv-drone-strikes-after-zelenskiy-trump-talks/91901359007/)

(U) KYIV, Ukraine — Russian forces launched a large-scale drone attack on Kyiv on September 23, 2026, striking railway infrastructure, warehouses, petrol stations and other civilian sites hours after Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskiy met U.S. President Donald Trump at the UN General Assembly. The assault killed at least two people and injured 22, involved harder-to-intercept jet-powered drones, and targeted key transport and energy-adjacent facilities. The Kremlin indicated no immediate plans for high-level peace talks, as Zelenskiy reiterated readiness for an energy-ceasefire only if Russia halted attacks on Ukrainian infrastructure.
