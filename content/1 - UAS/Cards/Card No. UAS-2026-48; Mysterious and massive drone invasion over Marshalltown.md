(U) **Mysterious and massive drone invasion over Marshalltown** (Times Republican, Your View / opinion letter, July 22, 2026) This is a first-person opinion piece recounting an incident the author says occurred on July 26, 2025, over Marshalltown, Iowa. The writer was in their backyard when they noticed what initially looked like a large flock of snow geese high over Riverside Cemetery. On closer look, the objects were hovering (unlike geese) and appeared to be more than 200 white drones reflecting sunlight. One drone was positioned much higher and remained stationary, which the author speculated might have been a “mother” drone controlling the others.

(U) The swarm slowly moved south toward the courthouse area before the author lost sight of it. Photos were taken. The author contacted city councilor Gary Thompson, who said neither city officials nor the police chief knew anything about the drones or any related permit. Nearly a year later, Thompson reportedly still had no information. Another person on Facebook claimed to have seen more than 100 drones the same day. The author expresses surprise that such a large number of drones could operate over a populated area without local officials’ knowledge, questions their origin and purpose (including speculation about a tech company scouting for a data center), and notes that the event happened under the previous mayor’s administration.
