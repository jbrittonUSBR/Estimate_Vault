# Ukraine Targets Russia's Key Black Sea Oil Port

[Source](https://oilprice.com/Latest-Energy-News/World-News/Ukraine-Targets-Russias-Key-Black-Sea-Oil-Port.html)

(U) NOVOROSSIYSK, Russia — Ukrainian drones struck Russia's Novorossiysk oil port and naval base, killing four people and sparking a fire at a fuel terminal, OilPrice.com reported on September 9, 2026. The attack targeted one of Russia's key Black Sea oil export hubs, which handles crude shipments including flows from the Caspian Pipeline Consortium. The strike was part of Ukraine's ongoing campaign against Russian energy infrastructure and export terminals.
