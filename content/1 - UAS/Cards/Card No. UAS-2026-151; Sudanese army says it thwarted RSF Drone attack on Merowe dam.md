# Sudanese army says it thwarted RSF Drone attack on Merowe dam

[Source](https://www.geeska.com/en/sudanese-army-says-it-thwarted-rsf-drone-attack-merowe-dam)

(U) MEROWE, Sudan — Sudan's armed forces announced that their air defences intercepted multiple explosive drones targeting the Merowe Dam, a nearby air base, and the 19th Infantry Division headquarters in Northern State, Geeska reported, citing a military statement. The army said all the drones launched by the Rapid Support Forces were downed before reaching their targets, with no casualties or material damage recorded. The Merowe Dam supplies the bulk of Sudan's hydroelectric power, and the military warned that repeated RSF drone strikes on energy facilities had caused power outages that authorities struggled to repair.
