# Saudi Arabia’s oil price slides amid pipeline plans, Hormuz diplomacy

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/saudi-oil-price-pipeline-hormuz-diplomacy/)

(U) RIYADH, Saudi Arabia — Drone attacks forced a precautionary shutdown of Saudi Aramco’s East-West (Petroline) pipeline in mid-September 2026, prompting a surge in Gulf port loadings and ship-to-ship transfers through the Strait of Hormuz. As Saudi Arabia demonstrated its ability to reroute exports and diplomatic talks around Hormuz progressed, Brent crude fell roughly 2.6% to around $103.05, retreating from triple-digit risk premiums. Saudi authorities subsequently discussed expanding the Petroline by up to 2 million barrels per day to reduce reliance on the vulnerable Hormuz corridor.
