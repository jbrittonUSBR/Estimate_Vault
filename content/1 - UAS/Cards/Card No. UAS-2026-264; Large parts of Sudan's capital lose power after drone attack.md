# Large parts of Sudan's capital lose power after drone attack

[Source](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/large-parts-sudans-capital-lose-power-after-drone-137614732)

(U) Khartoum, Sudan — A drone strike hit the Al-Markhiyat transformer station in Omdurman, leaving large parts of Sudan's capital without electricity for over 12 hours. The Sudanese Electricity Distribution Company reported serious damage to the network; a company official blamed the Rapid Support Forces and said repairs would require extended power rationing, affecting water supplies, hospitals and bakeries.
