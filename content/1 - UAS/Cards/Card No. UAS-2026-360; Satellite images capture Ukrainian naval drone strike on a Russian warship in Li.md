# Satellite images capture Ukrainian naval drone strike on a Russian warship in Libya, over 1,000 miles away from the war

[Source](https://www.businessinsider.com/satellite-images-before-and-after-ukrainian-drone-attack-russian-warship-2026-11)

(U) Benghazi, Libya — Satellite imagery captured by Vantor and reviewed by Business Insider showed the Russian corvette Merkury capsized with only its bow visible after a Sunday attack. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said seven Ukrainian naval drones struck the Merkury and the cargo ship MV Angara, while Ukraine's Security Service said the drones were launched from a mother ship and described both vessels as legitimate military targets. Later imagery showed fire trucks and vehicles on the pier beside the damaged Angara.
