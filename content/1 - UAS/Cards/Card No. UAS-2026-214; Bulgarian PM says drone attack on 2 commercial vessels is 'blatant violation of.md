# Bulgarian PM says drone attack on 2 commercial vessels is 'blatant violation of international law'

[Source](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/bulgarian-pm-drone-attack-2-commercial-vessels-blatant-137025648)

(U) Black Sea, Bulgaria — On October 6, 2026, drones struck two commercial vessels in Bulgaria's exclusive economic zone, sinking the Togo-flagged *Alfa Watan* and damaging the Ukrainian-owned *Chelsea 3*; Bulgarian Prime Minister Rumen Radev called the attack a "blatant violation of international law," and officials evacuated crew members with no immediate confirmed fatalities.
