(U) The United States and Ukraine have signed a bilateral statement of intent establishing a framework for deeper drone cooperation. It allows Ukrainian combat drones (land, air, and maritime) to be exported to the U.S. for military testing and evaluation—the first agreement of its kind between the two countries. This is not a production contract but is viewed as a key first step that could lead to joint manufacturing of Ukrainian-designed combat drones in the United States. The deal comes as the Pentagon seeks access to Ukraine’s low-cost, combat-proven unmanned systems developed during more than four years of war with Russia. Related development: Ukrainian firm UFORCE and U.S. shipbuilder ReconCraft signed a memorandum to produce MAGURA maritime attack drones in the United States, with production potentially scaling to hundreds or thousands of units.
