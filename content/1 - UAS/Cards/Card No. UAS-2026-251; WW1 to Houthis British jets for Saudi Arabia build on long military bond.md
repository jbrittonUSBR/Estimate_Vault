# From World War I to the Houthis: British jets for Saudi Arabia build on long military ties

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/21/ww1-to-houthis-british-jets-for-saudi-arabia-build-on-long-military-bond)

(U) October 21, 2026 — The UK said it would send Typhoon fighter jets, an A400M transport aircraft, and advisers to Saudi Arabia, with the aircraft assigned a defensive role and not tasked with attacking the Houthis, according to the British Ministry of Defence. Al Jazeera traced the deployment to longstanding UK-Saudi military ties and reported that Houthi missiles and drones had recently targeted Saudi oil facilities and Riyadh's main airport.
