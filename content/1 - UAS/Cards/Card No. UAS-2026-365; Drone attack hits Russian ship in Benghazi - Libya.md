# Drone attack hits Russian ship in Benghazi - Libya

[Source](https://www.rte.ie/news/2026/1123/1600240-libya-drone/)

(U) Benghazi, Libya — President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said seven Ukrainian-made sea drones struck the Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi Naval Base, with Libyan officials saying four drones were intercepted, two hit the ship and at least 10 people were wounded. Ukraine's SBU said the drones were launched from a mother ship and that a Ka-27 helicopter sank with the corvette, while eastern Libya's government asked the UN to investigate suspicions that western Libya was used to prepare the attack. Russian officials made no immediate comment.
