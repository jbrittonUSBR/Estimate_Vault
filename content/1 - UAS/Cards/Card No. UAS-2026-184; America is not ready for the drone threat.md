# America is not ready for the drone threat

[Source](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/dispatches/america-is-not-ready-for-the-drone-threat/)

(U) WASHINGTON, D.C. — The United States remains inadequately prepared for the growing domestic drone threat, as cheap, accessible, and hard-to-detect unmanned systems can be used for surveillance or kinetic attacks on critical infrastructure. While counter-UAS technology is advancing, no system can guarantee protection nationwide; instead, the U.S. should prioritize hardening critical sites, improving anticipation and intelligence, clarifying authorities and response roles, and building resilience so infrastructure can withstand attacks if defenses fail.
