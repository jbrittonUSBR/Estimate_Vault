# Addis Ababa explosions: Blasts heard in Ethiopia's capital after drone flights banned

[Source](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c3vgygpqky9zo)

(U) ADDIS ABABA — Explosions were heard in Ethiopia's capital overnight on October 1, 2026, near the Torhayloch military headquarters and in the Gotera area at about 00:30 local time, hours after the National Intelligence and Security Service imposed an indefinite ban on drone flights over the city, the BBC reported. The blasts came a week after fighting erupted between the army and a new alliance that includes the Tigray People's Liberation Front and Fano militias. An opposition source told Reuters the explosions were drone strikes, but officials did not confirm the cause or report casualties. Ethiopia simultaneously moved to close its embassy in Eritrea and expel ten diplomats, and 52 civilians were reported killed in fighting in Alamata.
