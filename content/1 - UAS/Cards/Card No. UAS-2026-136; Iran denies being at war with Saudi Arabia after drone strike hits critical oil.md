# Iran denies being at war with Saudi Arabia after drone strike hits critical oil pipeline

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/iran-denies-war-saudi-arabia-drone-strike/)

(U) TEHRAN, Iran — President Masoud Pezeshkian stated publicly on September 12 that Iran is "not at war with Saudi Arabia," after a swarm of drones struck Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline near Riyadh and Medina around September 10–11, injuring several people and prompting a precautionary shutdown of the roughly 1,200-kilometer line that carries an estimated 4 to 5 percent of global oil supply. The drones were reportedly launched from Iraq's Maysan province, no group formally claimed responsibility, and U.S. President Donald Trump said Iran was "probably" behind the attack, a characterization Tehran rejected. Pezeshkian coupled the denial with a call for regional cooperation and urged Turkey and Pakistan to help build a comprehensive Middle East security framework, while Baghdad opened an investigation and coordinated with Iranian officials, according to Crypto Briefing.
