# Mass Ukrainian drone attack on Moscow kills two, Russia says

[Source](https://slashdot.org/firehose.pl?op=view&amp;id=185715582)

(U) Moscow, Russia — Al Jazeera reported that a mass Ukrainian drone attack on the Moscow region killed at least two people and damaged a major oil refinery, with Russian authorities saying more than 1,600 drones were intercepted nationwide as the strikes coincided with the final day of Russia’s elections.
