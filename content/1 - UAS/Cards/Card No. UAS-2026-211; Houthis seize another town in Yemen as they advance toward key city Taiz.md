(U) TAIZ, Yemen — Iran-backed Houthi rebels seized another town in Yemen as they advanced toward the key city of Taiz, tightening their grip on strategic territory in the country's west. The push threatened supply lines to government-held areas as fighting escalated along critical routes near the Red Sea.
