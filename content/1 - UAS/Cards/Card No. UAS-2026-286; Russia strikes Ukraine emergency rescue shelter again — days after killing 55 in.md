# Russia strikes Ukraine emergency rescue shelter again — days after killing 55 in brutal bombing

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/11/08/world-news/russia-strikes-ukraine-emergency-rescue-shelter-again/)

(U) Komyshuvakha, Zaporizhzhia region, Ukraine — The Associated Press reports that a Russian drone struck emergency workers at a former school and nearby shelter on November 8, several days after a separate bombing at the site killed at least 55 people, according to Ukrainian authorities. Ukraine’s State Emergency Service said no one was injured in the later strike, but specialized rescue equipment was damaged.
