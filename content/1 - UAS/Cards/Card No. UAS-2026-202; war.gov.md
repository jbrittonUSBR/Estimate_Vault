# Department of War Accelerates Counter-UAS Employment to Defend American Airspace Sovereignty

[Source](https://www.war.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/4617709/department-of-war-accelerates-counter-uas-employment-to-defend-american-airspac/)

(U) WASHINGTON — The Department of War issued a memorandum titled "Accelerating Employment of Counter-Unmanned Aircraft Systems to Protect American Airspace Sovereignty," directing the department to eliminate administrative and procedural barriers that delay counter-drone capabilities for forces, installations, and assets. The policy, signed by Secretary of War Pete Hegseth, requires approval authorities to treat delay as operational risk, use prior approvals, test data, and established risk mitigations to accelerate comparable decisions, and grant interim, conditional, or time-bound approvals where testing supports low or manageable risk. Any denial of a requested c-UAS employment must be documented with a rationale and submitted to Joint Interagency Task Force-401 (JIATF-401), and any approval request unactioned within 30 days must be escalated to the Secretary of War's office. The memorandum targets four delay categories: authority to operate/connect (a single Department-wide process reducing approval timelines for validated systems from months to days or weeks), spectrum certification and frequency assignment (supporting DIU's Project Spectrum Strike), weapons safety review (no duplicative reviews absent material change, with JIATF-401 as central synchronizer), and legal reviews (expedited within 10 days of receipt of necessary information). It states it does not change existing operational c-UAS policy under 10 U.S.C. § 130i.
