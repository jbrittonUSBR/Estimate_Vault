# Drone attack on Colombia police base injures civilians, including infant

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/23/drone-attack-on-colombia-police-base-injures-civilians-including-infant)

(U) CUCUTA, COLOMBIA — A drone attack on a police base in Cucuta injured at least three civilians, including an 18-month-old infant, and damaged about 60 homes, per Al Jazeera and AP. The attack came amid President Abelardo de la Espriella's offensive against rebel groups and drug gangs, which have responded with drone strikes, gas-canister rockets and roadside bombs. Another police station in the Cesar region was also attacked with drones the same day, injuring an officer, and a separate attack on a military base in Cesar killed one soldier and wounded 12 on Saturday.
