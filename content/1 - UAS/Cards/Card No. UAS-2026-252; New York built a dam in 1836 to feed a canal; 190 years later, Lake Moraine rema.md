# Lake Moraine remains lowered during rehabilitation of 1836 New York dam

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/us/new-york-built-a-dam-in-1836-to-feed-a-canal-190-years-later-lake-moraine-remains-lowered-for-a-major-rehabilitation-project/articleshow/135087814.cms)

(U) October 21, 2026 — The Times of India reported that New York's Madison Reservoir Dam, built in 1836 to supply the Chenango Canal, was undergoing rehabilitation and that Lake Moraine remained at a reduced level during the work. The state Canal Corporation said the reservoir was held about 10 feet below the spillway crest, with low levels expected through the end of 2027 and construction scheduled into 2028; it said the dam was not in imminent danger of failure.
