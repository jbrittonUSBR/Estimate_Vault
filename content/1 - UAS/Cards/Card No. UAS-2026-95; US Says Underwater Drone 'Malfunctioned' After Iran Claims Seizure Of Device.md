# US Says Underwater Drone 'Malfunctioned' After Iran Claims Seizure Of Device

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/library/news/2026/09/mil-260908-rferl01.htm)

(U) WASHINGTON, D.C., United States — U.S. Central Command (CENTCOM) said one of its underwater drones had "malfunctioned" while surveying regional waters, after Iran claimed it had seized the device in the Strait of Hormuz. CENTCOM spokesman Captain Tim Hawkins said the drone malfunctioned more than a day earlier and that the "defective drone was an older model that neither collected sensitive data nor carried any classified sonar or radar equipment." The Iranian claim was made by the Islamic Revolutionary Guards Corps, which said its naval forces captured the US submarine drone near the entrance to the Strait of Hormuz around dawn on September 8; Iranian media said the craft's capabilities included subsurface reconnaissance, seabed mapping, mine detection and submarine-cable inspection.
