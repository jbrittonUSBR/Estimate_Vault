# Bagdogra airport closed for 1 hr after ‘drone sighting’

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/kolkata/bagdogra-airport-closed-for-1-hr-after-drone-sighting/articleshow/136110765.cms)

(U) BAGDOGRA, INDIA — A suspected drone sighting near Bagdogra airport forced authorities to suspend flight operations for over an hour on Monday, cancelling three flights, diverting one and delaying seven others, per The Times of India. The runway remained closed from 2:55 pm to 4:00 pm while airport authorities and security agencies investigated and cleared the airspace; affected carriers included Akasa Air, IndiGo, Air India Express and Drukair. No casualties or damage were reported.
