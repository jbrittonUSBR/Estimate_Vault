# US withdrew bombers from RAF Fairford 'over fears of Iran plot to launch drone attack similar to Ukraine's 'Operation Spiderweb' strike on Russian aircraft'

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16186241/US-withdrew-bombers-RAF-Fairford-fears-Iran-plot-Operation-Spiderweb.html)

(U) Gloucestershire, United Kingdom — The United States redeployed B-1 bombers from RAF Fairford on October 5, 2026, after intelligence indicated Iran’s IRGC had considered a drone attack on the base similar to Ukraine’s Operation Spiderweb; six people were arrested in connection with a related suspected plot, though no explosives were found and those detained were later released on bail.
