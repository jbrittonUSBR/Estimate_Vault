# Drone strike in disputed Sudan-South Sudan area kills dozens, minister says

[Source](https://www.reuters.com/5f374b32b07b/world/africa/drone-strike-disputed-sudan-south-sudan-area-kills-dozens-minister-says-2026-10-20/)

(U) Abyei, Sudan–South Sudan border — Reuters reported that Abyei’s caretaker information minister said a drone strike hit a market on October 19, killing about 73 people and injuring 19, and accused Sudan’s armed forces of carrying it out. Reuters said it could not independently verify responsibility; Sudanese officials separately said the Rapid Support Forces were active in parts of Abyei.
