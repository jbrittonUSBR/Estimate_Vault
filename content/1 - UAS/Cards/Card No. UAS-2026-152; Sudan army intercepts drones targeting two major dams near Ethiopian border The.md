# Sudan army intercepts drones targeting two major dams near Ethiopian border

[Source](https://eastleighvoice.co.ke/sudan/400772/sudan-army-intercepts-drones-targeting-two-major-dams-near-ethiopian-border)

(U) KHARTOUM, Sudan — Sudan's army said it intercepted drones targeting two major dams near the country's border with Ethiopia, The Eastleigh Voice reported. The reported attack formed part of a widening pattern of drone strikes on Sudanese strategic infrastructure during the war between the army and the Rapid Support Forces. Independent confirmation of the incident, including the specific facilities involved and any damage, was not available.
