# Mali drone strike kills 13 civilians, activists say; army says it targeted militants

[Source](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/mali-drone-strike-kills-13-civilians-activists-army-137642600)

(U) Hassi Laghdaf, Mali — The Associated Press reported that local activists and a separatist group said a Malian military drone strike killed at least 13 civilians, including women and children, near the Mauritania border. The army said it had struck militants gathered under cover.
