# Soldiers and Gold Star families press Army leadership after deadly Iranian drone strike

[Source](https://www.yahoo.com/news/us/articles/exclusive-soldiers-gold-star-families-103134796.html)

(U) PORT SHUAIBA, Kuwait — On March 1, an Iranian drone struck the Port Shuaiba logistics facility in Kuwait, killing six U.S. soldiers and wounding dozens more in the deadliest attack on U.S. troops since Operation Epic Fury began. Survivors and Gold Star families alleged that commanders ignored warnings about the facility’s vulnerability to drones and delayed adequate medical treatment, prompting calls for accountability from Army leadership.
