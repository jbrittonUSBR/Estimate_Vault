# Skill Make Builds a Homemade EMP Device That Fries Real Gadgets

[Source](https://www.techeblog.com/skill-make-homemade-emp-device-12000-turns/)

(U) Unspecified location — TechEBlog reported on an October 2, 2026 video in which the YouTuber Skill Make built a homemade electromagnetic pulse device, winding 0.1 mm wire in roughly 800-turn sections to a 12,000-turn secondary around a 3D-printed ferrite core. The build paired a flyback driver with an eight-stage Marx bank of 15 kV nanofarad capacitors and a flat pancake coil, and the pulse disabled a digital caliper, a counterfeit Apple Watch and an old monitor at close range. The report noted that the device worked only within a few inches and was a bench experiment rather than the citywide "EMP" depicted in films.
