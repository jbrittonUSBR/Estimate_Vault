# Russian drone attack near Polish border raises NATO security concerns

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/russian-drone-attack-near-polish-border-raises-nato-security-concerns/)

(U) KYIV, Ukraine — A Russian drone attack targeted a passenger train in western Ukraine near the Polish border, according to a Crypto Briefing account citing the Kyiv Post, raising concerns about risks to NATO citizens. Poland confirmed that its airspace was not violated, but the proximity of the strike to NATO territory prompted security concerns. The outlet also reported a decline in prediction-market odds that Ukraine would recapture Crimea by the end of 2026.
