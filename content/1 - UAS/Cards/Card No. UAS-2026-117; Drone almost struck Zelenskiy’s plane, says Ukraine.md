# Drone almost struck Zelenskiy’s plane, says Ukraine

[Source](https://www.irishtimes.com/world/europe/2026/09/10/drone-almost-struck-zelenskiys-plane-says-ukraine/)

(U) CHIȘINĂU, Moldova — A jet-powered attack drone nearly struck Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy's aeroplane on September 8, 2026, according to a Ukrainian official flying with him and Norwegian Prime Minister Jonas Gahr Støre. Støre told public broadcaster NRK that the plane "was almost hit by a drone as it was about to take off from Moldova," and a senior Ukrainian official aboard said they believed it was a Russian Shahed-type drone powered by a jet engine. Moldovan authorities had temporarily closed the country's airspace after detecting a Russian drone that crossed from Ukraine's direction and later exploded near Mihăilenii Vechi, about 160 km from Chișinău airport. The Ukrainian official said there was no damage or casualties, and Ukrainian authorities opened an investigation; the Kremlin did not immediately respond to a request for comment.
