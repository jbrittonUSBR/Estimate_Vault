# Civilian cargo ship sinks and crew is missing after Black Sea drone attack

[Source](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c8ly0v5r602eo)

(U) SOFIA, Bulgaria — A civilian cargo ship sank and its crew went missing after being attacked by drones in the Black Sea, Bulgarian officials said, the BBC reported on October 6, 2026. A second vessel carrying grain was also hit within Bulgaria's exclusive economic zone, and its 18 crew were rescued, including two injured in a fire. Prime Minister Rumen Radev said air and sea drones struck at about 03:00 local time some 70 miles off the coastal town of Byala. The sunken ship, the Togo-flagged, Turkish-owned general cargo vessel Alfa Watan, was reportedly bound for Sulina, Romania; a search operation was launched. Radev did not accuse any party but condemned the strikes as a "flagrant violation of maritime law," while EU Commission President Ursula von der Leyen called attacks on civilian shipping "unacceptable."
