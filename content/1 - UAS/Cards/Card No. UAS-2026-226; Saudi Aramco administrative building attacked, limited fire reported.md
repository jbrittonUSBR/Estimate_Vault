# Saudi Aramco administrative building attacked, limited fire reported

[Source](https://www.cbc.ca/news/world/saudi-arabia-aramco-houthis-missile-9.7379854)

(U) Dhahran, Saudi Arabia — On October 14, 2026, a Houthi attack targeted an administrative building at a Saudi Aramco facility in Dhahran, causing a limited fire that was quickly brought under control. Saudi authorities reported no major casualties, and the incident was attributed to the ongoing escalation of Houthi cross-border attacks against Saudi energy infrastructure.
