# Libyan naval officers die following Ukrainian drone attack, and other Mideast news

[Source](https://apnews.com/article/mideast-israel-gaza-ukraine-libya-israel-november-25-2026-6995de4ee91278459742a359eeb7439e)

(U) BENGHAZI, LIBYA — Two Libyan naval officers died Tuesday from wounds sustained in a Ukrainian sea-drone attack on a Russian warship docked at the Benghazi Naval Base on Sunday, Libyan National Army authorities said, vowing unspecified retaliation. Ukraine's President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said his forces deployed seven sea drones in the operation against the Russian corvette, which Libyan authorities said was refueling and resupplying at the base. The report, attributed to AP, also noted continued Israeli airstrikes in Gaza overnight that killed a man identified by health officials as a senior internal security official along with his wife and children.
