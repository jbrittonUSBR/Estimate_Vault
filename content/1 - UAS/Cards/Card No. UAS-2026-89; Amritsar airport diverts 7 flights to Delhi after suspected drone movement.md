# Amritsar airport diverts 7 flights to Delhi after suspected drone movement

[Source](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/aviation/amritsar-airport-diverts-7-flights-to-delhi-after-suspected-drone-movement-126090700353_1.html)

(U) AMRITSAR, India — Seven flights were diverted from Amritsar's Sri Guru Ram Dass Ji International Airport to Delhi on the night of September 6, 2026, following reports of suspected drone movement near the airport, according to the Press Trust of India. The diversion included two international flights, and operations at the airport were halted for approximately one and a half hours before being restored. Carriers affected included Malaysia Airlines, IndiGo, Air India, and Air India Express. Punjab Police were alerted to the reported drone sightings, which occurred near the airport around 10:00 p.m. local time.
