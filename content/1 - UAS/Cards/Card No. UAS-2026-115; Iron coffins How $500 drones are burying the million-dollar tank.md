# Iron coffins: How $500 drones are burying the million-dollar tank

[Source](https://www.naturalnews.com/2026-09-09-iron-coffins-how-500-drones-are-burying-the-million-dollar-tank.html)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — An opinion article argued that the main battle tank is being rendered obsolete on modern battlefields by cheap first-person-view (FPV) drones, citing the Russo-Ukrainian War and Israel's operations in southern Lebanon as proving grounds. The piece, published by the outlet NaturalNews.com, claimed FPV drones costing a few hundred to a few thousand dollars are routinely destroying tanks worth $4.5 million or more, and that countermeasures such as "cope cages" and "turtle tanks" have proven only partially effective. It further asserted, attributing the effort to the Pentagon and the Trump administration, that the U.S. is racing to adapt through Executive Order 14307 and a Department of War memorandum; these are the outlet's characterizations and analysis rather than independently verified developments.
