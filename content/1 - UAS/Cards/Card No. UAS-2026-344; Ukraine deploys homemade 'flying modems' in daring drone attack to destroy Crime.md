# Ukraine deploys homemade 'flying modems' in daring drone attack to destroy Crimean Bridge, ditching Elon Musk's Starlink

[Source](https://slashdot.org/firehose.pl?op=view&id=186791740)

(U) Crimea, Ukraine/Russia — Ukrainian forces reportedly used homemade airborne relay devices, described as “flying modems,” to maintain a live video feed during a drone attack on the Crimean Bridge. The network reportedly helped preserve the feed while Russia attempted to disrupt the signal, without relying on Starlink.
