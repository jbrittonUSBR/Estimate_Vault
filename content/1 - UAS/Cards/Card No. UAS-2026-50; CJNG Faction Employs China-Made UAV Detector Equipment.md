(U//FOUO/REL TO USA, FVEY) SUMMARY: In mid-July 2026, social media accounts documented an alleged member of the New Generation Jalisco Cartel (CJNG) Gente del Jardinero faction operating drone detection equipment in southwest Mexico. The accounts identified the equipment as a China-made Skyfend Tracer P portable UAV detector and a Skyfend Skyshield Edge anti-UAV control system tablet running the Skyshield Nexus software. 

(U//FOUO/REL TO USA, FVEY) Tracer is a purpose-built device designed to monitor and manage aerial drone activity. It can accurately extract detailed information such as drone brand, model, serial number (SN), location, and operator position by decoding drone broadcast signals. Additionally, it employs advanced spectrum detection technology to detect, locate, and intercept signals from various types of drones. Its flexible customization capabilities enable users to quickly understand the electromagnetic landscape, respond to emerging threats in a timely manner, and safeguard critical sites and personnel.
