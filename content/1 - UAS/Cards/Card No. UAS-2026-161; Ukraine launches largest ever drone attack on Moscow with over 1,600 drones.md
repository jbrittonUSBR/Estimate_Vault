# Ukraine launches largest ever drone attack on Moscow with over 1,600 drones

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/ukraine-largest-drone-attack-moscow/)

(U) Moscow, Russia — Ukraine launched a large-scale drone attack on Moscow and the Moscow Region on the final day of Russian elections, with Russian authorities reporting over 1,600 drones intercepted nationwide and 450 in the vicinity of the capital. The strikes killed two people, damaged the Gazprom Neft Moscow Oil Refinery, and other infrastructure; Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy confirmed hits on a key oil and logistics facility.
