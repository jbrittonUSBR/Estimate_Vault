# Saudi Arabia Restarts East-West Pipeline, Bypassing Strait of Hormuz

[Source](https://www.naturalnews.com/2026-09-30-saudi-arabia-restarts-east-west-pipeline-bypassing-hormuz.html)

(U) DHAHRAN, Saudi Arabia — On September 30, 2026, Natural News reported that Saudi Aramco had restarted the East-West Pipeline (Abqaiq to Yanbu, approximately 745 miles, 7 million barrels per day) after it was shut down following drone attacks on pumping stations between September 10 and 11, 2026. According to the report, the drones were traced to Maysan, Iraq, and the restart came as oil flows through the Strait of Hormuz remained at roughly two-thirds of prewar levels.
