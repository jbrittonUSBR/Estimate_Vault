# White House Flags Cybersecurity Risks in Foreign-Made Industrial Robots

[Source](https://www.israeldefense.co.il/en/node/72210#google_vignette)

(U) ISRAEL — A new Trump administration proclamation warned that imported robotics and industrial machinery could expose sensitive data and critical infrastructure to cyber threats, directing the Commerce Secretary and U.S. Trade Representative to negotiate agreements with foreign trading partners over imports of robotics, industrial machinery and components, with tariffs or quotas possible if negotiations fail.
