(U) **Drone attack sparks fires at power substations in Yalta and Alushta, occupied Crimea – social media** (Ukrinform, July 22, 2026) Fires broke out at power substations in Yalta and Alushta in Russian-occupied Crimea following a drone attack during the night of July 22, according to social media reports cited by the monitoring group of the Telegram channel Crimean Wind (which referenced satellite imagery).

In Yalta:
- Signs of a fire were detected at the 110/10 kV Massandra substation.
- A fire also broke out at the 110 kV Darsan substation after overnight explosions.
- Electricity went out in Yalta at approximately 03:30.

In Alushta:
- Fire hotspots were reported near the 110/10 kV Luchyste substation (next to the city’s wastewater treatment facilities).
- The 110 kV Alushta substation on Zhovtneva Street was also reported damaged.

(U) The report notes that between July 1 and July 20, Ukraine’s Unmanned Systems Forces, as part of Operation “Crimean Switch Off,” struck 104 power substations and electrical facilities in Russian-occupied territories, including Crimea.
