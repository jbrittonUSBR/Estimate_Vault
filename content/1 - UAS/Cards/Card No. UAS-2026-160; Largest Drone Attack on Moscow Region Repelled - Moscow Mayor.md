# Largest Drone Attack on Moscow Region Repelled - Moscow Mayor

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/ukraine/2026/09/ukraine-260920-sputnik07.htm)

(U) Moscow, Russia — According to Russian officials, Moscow Mayor Sergey Sobyanin claimed that Russian air defenses had repelled what he described as the largest drone attack on Moscow and the Moscow Region, intercepting more than 1,600 UAVs across defensive lines, including 450 approaching the capital. He also stated that several drones reached the Moscow Oil Refinery and one struck a residential building, with no fatalities reported.
