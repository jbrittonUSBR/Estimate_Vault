# Ukrainian drones strike energy facility in Russia’s Arctic for first time

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/ukrainian-drones-strike-russia-arctic-energy/)

(U) NOVY URENGOY, Russia — Crypto Briefing reported on September 9, 2026 that Ukrainian drones struck an energy facility in Russia's Arctic for the first time, hitting the Yamalo-Nenets Autonomous Okrug region more than 3,000 kilometers from Ukraine's border. Ukraine's Special Operations Forces said the strike, carried out with Fire Point FP-1 drones, targeted the Novy Urengoy Gas Condensate Treatment Plant and the nearby Purovsky Gas Condensate Processing Plant. Regional Governor Dmitry Artyukhov said a drone attack had been repelled and that falling debris caused a fire, with no deaths or injuries reported.
