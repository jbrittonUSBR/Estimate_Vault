# Drone That Crashed Near German Wunstorf Air Base May Have Been a Combat UAV

[Source](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/drone-crashed-near-wunstorf-base-combat-uav/)

(U) WUNSTORF, Germany — Wreckage of a drone found near Wunstorf Air Base (home to German A400M transport aircraft) in mid-September 2026 contained a suspicious substance suspected to be explosive, prompting investigation by Germany’s Federal Prosecutor and Federal Criminal Police Office. Authorities are examining whether the device was a combat-capable UAV intended for an attack, noting parallels to an August 2026 sabotage attempt at Leipzig/Halle Airport where explosive-laden drones were found near a Ukrainian An-124, linked by investigators to Russia’s GRU.
