# Drone Round Counter-UAS Ammunition Integrated Into Marine Corps Indoor Simulated Marksmanship Trainer

[Source](https://soldiersystems.net/2026/09/08/drone-round-counter-uas-ammunition-integrated-into-marine-corps-indoor-simulated-marksmanship-trainer/)

(U) UNITED STATES — Drone Round announced that its 5.56x45mm L Variant counter-UAS cartridge had been integrated into the Indoor Simulated Marksmanship Trainer (ISMT), the U.S. Marine Corps virtual small arms training system produced by InVeris. The Marine Corps has purchased 400,000 L Variant cartridges to date and stated its intent to procure an additional one million rounds in fiscal year 2027, with deliveries to ammunition supply points underway. The integration lets Marines practice detecting, tracking and engaging aerial drone threats on the same simulators installed across the Corps, and InVeris will field the C-UAS training capability across more than 200 ISMT systems as part of the ISMT Tech Refresh. The L Variant separates into five projectiles after firing, is effective to 100 meters, and requires no modification to standard 5.56mm NATO weapons.
