# Drone attack in disputed Sudan-South Sudan border region kills at least 153

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/21/drone-attack-in-disputed-sudan-south-sudan-border-region-kills-at-least-153)

(U) Abyei, Sudan–South Sudan border — Al Jazeera reported that a drone attack on Amiet Market killed at least 153 people, citing Abyei’s caretaker information minister. South Sudanese officials blamed Sudan’s military, while Sudan’s military denied targeting civilians and said those killed were Rapid Support Forces members and fuel smugglers; Doctors Without Borders said it treated more than 20 wounded people.
