# Russia wanted to cow Ukraine allies with railway drone attack, says top EU diplomat

[Source](https://www.politico.eu/article/russia-wanted-frighten-western-countries-with-drone-attack-warsaw-bound-train-kaja-kallas/)

(U) ROVANIEMI, Finland — EU foreign policy chief Kaja Kallas said the drone strike on a Warsaw-bound passenger train near Ukraine's border with Poland was intended to frighten Western countries into ceasing their support for Kyiv, telling reporters on 14 September after landing for an Arctic summit. The drone hit the locomotive of a passenger train about two kilometres from the frontier in the Volyn region; nobody was injured, and the attack came shortly after a train carrying former UK prime minister Boris Johnson, former CIA director David Petraeus and UK National Security Adviser Jonathan Powell had passed along the same line. Ukraine's state rail operator said it was "quite likely" the diplomatic train was the target, while Russia's defence ministry said it had struck railway infrastructure used to transport military cargo from Europe.
