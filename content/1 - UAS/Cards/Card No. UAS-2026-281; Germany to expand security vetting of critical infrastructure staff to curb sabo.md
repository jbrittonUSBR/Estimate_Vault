# Germany to expand security vetting of critical infrastructure staff to curb sabotage risk

[Source](https://www.cleanenergywire.org/news/germany-expand-security-vetting-critical-infrastructure-staff-curb-sabotage-risk)

(U) GERMANY — Germany's cabinet approved plans to extend security vetting of staff at critical infrastructure sites to curb sabotage risk, requiring additional screening for operators at facilities such as water works, rail services, grids and power stations.
