(TLP: Amber) On June 14, an on onsite employee spotted a drone hovering then leaving the airspace of a plant in West Virginia. The employee stated that the drone was hovering about 75-100ft and had two lights, one solid and one blinking. The drone hovered and then departed northeast from the location. This incident lasted about 15-20 seconds and occurred at night, making it difficult to see any additional details or unique markings on the drone.

Incident Type: Suspicious Activity / Unmanned Aircraft System (UAS)  
Asset Affected: Generation / Coal  
Incident State/Province: West Virginia  
NERC Region: SERC
