# U.S. Strikes Iranian Tankers, Iran Attacks Jordan, Oil Surges Past $100

[Source](https://www.breitbart.com/national-security/2026/09/09/u-s-strikes-iranian-tankers-iran-attacks-jordan-oil-surges-past-100/)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — Breitbart News reported on September 9, 2026 that the United States destroyed five Iranian oil tankers after Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps launched ballistic missiles at a U.S. Navy warship twice in two days, and that Iran retaliated by attacking civilian vessels and launching missiles at U.S. forces in Jordan. U.S. Central Command said the tankers were part of a multibillion-dollar shadow network funding the IRGC and its regional proxies, and benchmark oil prices rose above $100 per barrel for the first time since July amid the escalation. The IRGC claimed it attacked at least ten ships near the Strait of Hormuz, while Iraqi officials said a Panama-flagged tanker was hit by a drone in Iraqi territorial waters and caught fire.
