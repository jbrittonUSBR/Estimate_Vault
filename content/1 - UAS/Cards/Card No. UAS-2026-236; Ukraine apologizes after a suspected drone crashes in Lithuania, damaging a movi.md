# Ukraine apologizes after a suspected drone crashes in Lithuania, damaging a moving car

[Source](https://www.dw.com/en/ukraine-apologizes-after-a-suspected-drone-crashes-in-lithuania-damaging-a-moving-car/a-79692905)

(U) Northern Lithuania — Deutsche Welle reported that Ukraine apologized after a suspected drone crashed, damaging a moving car and prompting shelter warnings and a NATO fighter-jet response. Lithuanian authorities said there were no injuries and that the drone's origin and whether it carried explosives remained under investigation. Ukrainian officials suggested Russian electronic jamming may have diverted a drone aimed at Russia; Lithuania's defense minister offered a different possibility, that it entered from Poland.
