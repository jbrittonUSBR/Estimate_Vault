# What to know as trials over the assassination of Haiti's president resume in Miami

[Source](https://abcnews.com/US/wireStory/trials-assassination-haitis-president-resume-miami-136813318)

(U) MIAMI — Federal trials connected to the July 7, 2021, assassination of Haitian President Jovenel Moïse resumed in Miami five years after the killing, with dozens of defendants still awaiting trial, the Associated Press reported via PBS NewsHour. U.S. prosecutors have charged more than 40 people and obtained a dozen convictions, including five life sentences; Florida pastor Christian Emmanuel Sanon, accused of aspiring to replace Moïse, went on trial on eight charges and pleaded not guilty. Moïse was shot 12 times at his home near Port-au-Prince by about two dozen mostly Colombian mercenaries, and prosecutions were proceeding in the United States because Haiti's courts had been stalled by judicial resignations and gang control.
