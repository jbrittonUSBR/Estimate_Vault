# US and Canadian Court Records Breached Following Thomson Reuters Incident

[Source](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/us-canada-court-breach-thomson/)

(U) TORONTO, Canada — Thomson Reuters disclosed a cybersecurity incident affecting its C-Track court management software that resulted in a breach of sensitive case data across Canada and the US. The company detected activity affecting information held in C-Track on June 30, and an investigation found an unauthorized party obtained files associated with three Ontario courts — the Court of Appeal for Ontario, the Ontario Superior Court of Justice, and the Ontario Court of Justice — with a subset of court records potentially including names and personal information, and some confidential, redacted, or sealed information possibly affected. The incident also impacted appellate courts in 11 US states and the US Virgin Islands, where records could contain names, Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, medical information, dates of birth, and health insurance information; Thomson Reuters said there was no evidence financial transaction systems were impacted and that the incident was not caused by the courts' networks or systems.
