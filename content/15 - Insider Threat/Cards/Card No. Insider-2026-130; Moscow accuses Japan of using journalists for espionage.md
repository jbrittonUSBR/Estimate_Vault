# Moscow accuses Japan of using journalists for espionage

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/world-news/moscow-accuses-japan-of-using-journalists-for-espionage/articleshow/135092531.cms)

(U) Moscow, Russia — AFP reported that Moscow accused Japan of using journalists as cover for espionage, following the arrest of a Japanese journalist in Russia. The claim was attributed to Russian officials; the report did not independently establish that the journalist had conducted espionage.
