# Le Danemark réclame des restrictions de déplacement des Russes dans toute l'UE à la suite d'un assassinat déjoué (Anglais)

[Source](https://kyivindependent.com/denmark-calls-for-eu-wide-travel-restrictions-on-russians-following-foiled-assassination-plot/)

(U) Copenhagen, Denmark — Denmark circulated a diplomatic note to EU governments proposing suspension of tourist visas for Russian nationals, an end to recognition of Russian non-biometric passports across the Schengen Area, and tighter entry rules for current and former Russian combatants after a foiled plot to kill Ukrainian-American rapper Kyivstoner that Danish intelligence attributed to Russia, Reuters reported. Copenhagen had expelled Russia's defense attaché a day earlier, while the Russian Embassy said no evidence of Russian involvement had been provided. Danish officials planned to present the measures to EU ambassadors in Brussels on November 23 as Commission President Ursula von der Leyen voiced solidarity with countries targeted by Russian hybrid operations.
