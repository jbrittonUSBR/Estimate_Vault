# EXCLUSIVE: Candace Owens Dealt Major Court Blow as Judge Refuses to Toss Lawsuit Over Charlie Kirk Assassination Plot Claims

[Source](https://radaronline.com/p/candace-owens-judge-refuses-defamation-lawsuit-charlie-kirk-assassination-plot-claims/)

(U) TENNESSEE — RadarOnline reported that Chief U.S. District Judge William L. Campbell Jr. denied Candace Owens' motion to dismiss a defamation lawsuit brought by Brian Harpole, who alleged she falsely linked him to a plot to assassinate Charlie Kirk. The two-page order, filed November 20 in the U.S. District Court for the Middle District of Tennessee, found the complaint contained sufficient factual allegations to proceed and rejected Owens' anti-SLAPP argument under Tennessee's Public Participation Act. RadarOnline noted the ruling did not establish liability or resolve the truth of the underlying claims, and the case remained pending.
