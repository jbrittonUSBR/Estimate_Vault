(U) **Florida Republicans back Rubio's announcement on Cuban espionage** (Florida’s Voice, Kennedy Owens, July 20, 2026) Florida Republican lawmakers are supporting U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio’s announcement highlighting decades of Cuban espionage and political subversion in the United States. Rubio stated on X: “For more than six decades, the Cuban regime has been the leading sponsor of radical leftism and Third Worldism in the United States. The State Department is exposing the full history of Cuban espionage and subversion in our country. The American people deserve to know.”

Key reactions from Florida Republicans:
- **Sen. Ashley Moody** linked the Cuban government’s record to the rise of “Democratic” Socialists in the U.S. She urged people to speak with Florida’s Cuban community, whose relatives still live under the totalitarian regime, and quoted Fidel Castro’s rhetoric about a revolution “of the working people” while noting the reality of imprisoned political opponents.
- **Rep. María Elvira Salazar** praised the State Department report, saying Havana has for decades “exported intelligence operations, political subversion, and the machinery used to build dictatorships across our hemisphere.” She described Cuba as “the command center of 21st-century communism” and “the mothership of evil.”

(U) The article frames the State Department effort as documenting Cuba’s long-term influence operations and presents the Florida lawmakers’ comments as aligning Rubio’s focus on Cuban activities with concerns about socialist movements in the United States.
