# Belgian-Chinese man to face trial over suspected semiconductor espionage

[Source](https://apnews.com/article/belgium-china-spying-semiconductors-belgan-854428cfb9cbcc914910624293923f5a)

(U) BRUSSELS, Belgium — A Belgian-Chinese man will face trial over suspected semiconductor espionage, Belgian prosecutors said. The 52-year-old was detained in connection with the alleged transfer of proprietary chip technology from the Belgian semiconductor firm BelGaN. Prosecutors did not disclose further details of the case.
