# Pakistani spy posing as DRDO's 'Soniya' one of those flagged in 14 CAPF, defence WhatsApp groups; agencies probe how many more are inside

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/defence/pakistani-spy-posing-as-drdos-soniya-one-of-those-flagged-in-14-capf-defence-whatsapp-groups-agencies-probe-how-many-more-are-inside/articleshow/136108331.cms)

(U) INDIA — The Economic Times reported, citing security sources and officials, that Pakistani intelligence operatives allegedly infiltrated 13 to 14 private WhatsApp groups used by Indian defence personnel and Central Armed Police Forces, with one operative impersonating a DRDO scientist named "Soniya". Officials said the suspects used spoofed or compromised Indian mobile numbers to monitor conversations silently and ordered audits of communication platforms to identify and remove unauthorised members. A senior officer said a group involving defence-civilian officials may have seen sensitive information shared in exchange for money, and cyber-hygiene guidance was reiterated against posting classified material on social media.
