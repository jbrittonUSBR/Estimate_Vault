# Denmark says Russia behind plot to kill U.S.-Ukrainian citizen on its soil

[Source](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/denmark-accuses-russia-assassination-plot-against-us-ukrainian-citizen/)

(U) Copenhagen, Denmark — Danish police arrested three men with links to the criminal underworld in a car north of Copenhagen in late September, foiling an alleged attempt to kill Ukrainian-American rapper Kyivstoner before a concert, PET said. A search found firearms and knives, and two people were formally investigated for attempted murder and for assisting a foreign intelligence service, with police suspecting the gang Loyal to Familia of involvement. Denmark said it would expel Russia's defense attaché; the Russian embassy in Copenhagen said Denmark had presented no evidence.
