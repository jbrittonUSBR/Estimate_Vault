# Alec Baldwin says JFK's assassination 'doesn't smell right to me' as he marks 63rd anniversary with wild conspiracy theory video

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/tvshowbiz/article-16315539/Alec-Baldwin-JFK-conspiracy-theory-video.html)

(U) UNITED STATES — The Daily Mail reported that Alec Baldwin marked the 63rd anniversary of John F. Kennedy's assassination with a nearly 10-minute video in which he said the official account "doesn't smell right" and that he believed Kennedy was killed by his own government. Baldwin also claimed MSNBC cancelled his 2013 talk show after a two-hour JFK episode, while the Daily Mail cited Today.com as saying the show was suspended following an anti-gay slur and falling ratings. The outlet noted his video followed the dismissal of his malicious-prosecution lawsuit over the 2021 Rust shooting, which a judge threw out on prosecutorial-immunity grounds.
