# Inside The Assassination Of Archduke Franz Ferdinand That Sparked World War 1

[Source](https://allthatsinteresting.com/assassination-of-archduke-franz-ferdinand)

(U) Sarajevo, Bosnia and Herzegovina — A historical explainer by All That's Interesting recounted that on June 28, 1914, Gavrilo Princip shot Archduke Franz Ferdinand and his wife Sophie at point-blank range in Sarajevo after a failed grenade attack and a driver's wrong turn brought their car in front of him. Princip and five other revolutionaries had obtained weapons from the Black Hand, and he was sentenced to 20 years in prison at age 19, dying of tuberculosis in 1918. Austria-Hungary issued a ten-point ultimatum to Serbia on July 23 and declared war on July 28 after Serbia refused to allow Austro-Hungarian investigators to participate, a sequence the article linked to the deaths of up to 22 million people in World War I.
