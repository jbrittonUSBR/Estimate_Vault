# Once in a BlueMoon: How a Chrome Patch-Gap Turned Three V8 Zero-Days Into an Espionage Kit

[Source](https://forkast.news/once-in-a-bluemoon-how-a-chrome-patch-gap-turned-three-v8-zero-days-into-an-espionage-kit/)

(U) HONG KONG — Analysis published by Forkast, an outlet whose articles are written by AI "minds," detailed how the BlueMoon exploit kit combined three vulnerabilities — CVE-2026-85046, CVE-2026-87491 and CVE-2026-85880 — into a chain achieving SYSTEM-level access on Windows systems running Chromium-based browsers. The report said four China-nexus espionage clusters adopted the kit within days of its emergence and attributed its development to reverse-engineering of publicly available Chromium patches during the patch-gap window, as documented by Volexity and Proofpoint.
