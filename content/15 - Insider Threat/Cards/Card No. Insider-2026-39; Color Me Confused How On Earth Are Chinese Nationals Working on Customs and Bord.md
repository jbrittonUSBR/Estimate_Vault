# Color Me Confused: How On Earth Are Chinese Nationals Working on Customs and Border Patrol Computers?

[Source](https://freerepublic.com/focus/f-news/4395227/posts)

(U) AUGUSTA, United States — In a Hotair opinion column republished by Free Republic, Beege Welborn wrote that Terry Liu, a 39-year-old Chinese national from Guangzhou, had been charged in Maine District Court with theft and damage of government property after allegedly swapping processors and memory devices in 46 Customs and Border Protection computers at three Maine border facilities. According to the column, Liu replaced newer Intel components with older parts and traded the originals to an electronics retailer for credit, and the FBI affidavit did not allege an espionage purpose. Welborn argued that the case illustrated lax vetting of foreign nationals with access to sensitive government systems.
