# EXCLUSIVE: Russian Intelligence Network Accused of U.S. Murder-for-Hire Plot

[Source](https://radaronline.com/p/russian-intelligence-network-us-murder-for-hire-plot/)

(U) WASHINGTON, D.C., United States — On October 13, 2026, RadarOnline reported that federal authorities indicted five men suspected of working for Russian intelligence services in an alleged murder-for-hire plot targeting a Putin critic in Washington, D.C. The suspects, including a father-and-son pair linked to Russia’s FSB, are accused of recruiting U.S. citizens for surveillance and an attempted assassination, with national security experts warning of a broader Kremlin network operating inside the United States.
