# Nvidia reinstates former CEO Jensen Huang despite China espionage scandal

[Source](https://www.dw.com/en/nvidia-reinstates-former-ceo-jensen-huang-despite-china-espionage-scandal/a-80021926)

(U) New York, United States — Nvidia's board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive "effective immediately," days after he resigned following admissions of extramarital affairs, with the board saying the move served the company's best interests, DW reported. President Donald Trump welcomed the decision as the "correct and proper decision" and said the US government had no evidence of unlawful conduct or national security risk, while reporting had linked Huang romantically to a Singapore-based businesswoman described as a Chinese intelligence asset, allegations that remained unproven. Nvidia shares, which had fallen from nearly $220 per share in late October to about $125 the previous week, rallied into the $160 to $170 range after the announcement on Wall Street.
