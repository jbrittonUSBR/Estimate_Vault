# In 2019, Three former Jet Airways pilots and a ground staffer unable to access PF dues due to EPFO portal glitches pay private intermediary to get claims processed, face bribery case after he turns out to be EPFO staffer, they now get relief as Bombay HC quashes FIR and chargesheet, says prosecution failed to establish criminal intent

(U) Mumbai, India — The Times of India reported that the Bombay High Court quashed the FIR, charge sheet, and related bribery proceedings against four former Jet Airways employees who had paid an intermediary to process provident-fund claims. The court said prosecutors had not shown that the employees knew the intermediary was an EPFO official or intended to bribe him; proceedings against other accused remained in place.
