# Morning recap: Police warn terrorists spying on schools, Police recover another corpse as Accord chieftain buried in Osun, other top stories

[Source](https://punchng.com/morning-recap-police-warn-terrorists-spying-on-schools-police-recover-another-corpse-as-accord-chieftain-buried-in-osun-other-top-stories/)

(U) LAGOS — In a September 15, 2026 morning news roundup, Punch reported that Nigerian police had warned that terrorists and their informants were conducting surveillance on schools, National Youth Service Corps camps and places of worship nationwide, and that an armed group was moving from Katsina through Kaduna toward Plateau State; the Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps said it had doubled its intelligence network around learning institutions. The roundup also cited SBM Intelligence figures showing 7,825 people abducted between July 2025 and June 2026 and ₦7.78 billion paid in ransoms, and reported that police recovered a second corpse at Mayfair Motor Park in Ile-Ife, Osun State, after the shooting that killed Accord Party chieftain Olalekan Oyeyemi, who was buried in Osogbo. Other items included the extradition of five South Africa-based Nigerians accused of leading a Black Axe zone and running romance scams that defrauded American women of more than $6 million, charges carrying a combined maximum of up to 100 years, and the arrest of a 32-year-old mother, Ogechi Ofoma, in Anambra State. The items were attributed to police, security agencies and analysts and were summarized as a roundup rather than a single reported investigation.
