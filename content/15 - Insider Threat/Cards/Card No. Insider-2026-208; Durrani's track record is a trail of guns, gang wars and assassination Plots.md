# Durrani's track record is a trail of guns, gang wars and assassination Plots

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/delhi/durranis-track-record-is-a-trail-of-guns-gang-wars-and-assassination-plots/articleshow/136036050.cms)

(U) New Delhi, India — A Times of India profile described Naseem Durrani, a 37-year-old alleged D-Company operative, as having helped arrange financing and firearms for a plot to kill Delhi Bajrang Dal activist Robin Sharma at the behest of Chhota Shakeel. Police said Durrani later scouted Film City in Noida ahead of a planned 2017 attack on Pakistani-Canadian writer Tarek Fatah, and that Sharma's brother Deepak was mistaken for Robin and killed in Dwarka in 2005. The article reported 22 cases against Durrani between 2010 and 2025, three more outstanding cases and a 50,000-rupee reward from Uttar Pradesh Police.
