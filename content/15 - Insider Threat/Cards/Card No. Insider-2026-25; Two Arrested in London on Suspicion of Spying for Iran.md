# Two Arrested in London on Suspicion of Spying for Iran

[Source](https://order-order.com/2026/09/10/two-arrested-in-london-on-suspicion-of-spying-for-iran/)

(U) LONDON — Counter Terrorism Policing London arrested two people as part of an investigation into suspected National Security Act offences linked to Iran, the Metropolitan Police said. A 42-year-old woman was arrested at an address in north London and a 60-year-old man at an address in north-west London on September 9, 2026, both on suspicion of assisting a foreign intelligence service under Section 3 of the 2023 Act, following earlier searches on June 24. The pair were released on bail with strict conditions until late October.
