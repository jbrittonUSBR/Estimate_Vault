# Florida pastor convicted in Haitian president's assassination plot funded partly by US COVID relief money

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/us/florida-pastor-convicted-haitian-presidents-assassination-plot-funded-partly-us-covid-relief-money)

(U) Miami, USA — A federal jury in Miami convicted Christian Emmanuel Sanon, a Florida pastor and dual Haitian-American citizen, on all eight charges after a nearly eight-week trial over the 2021 killing of Haitian President Jovenel Moïse. Prosecutors said the plot was planned in South Florida, partly funded with pandemic relief loans, and involved about 18 trained Colombian mercenaries, while Sanon testified he did not know of a plan to kill Moïse. He faces up to life in prison at sentencing on February 10, and his lawyer said the defense would appeal; the article noted that more than 40 people were charged in the case.
