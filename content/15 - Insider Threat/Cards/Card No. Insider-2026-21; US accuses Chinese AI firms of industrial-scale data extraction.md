# US accuses Chinese AI firms of industrial-scale data extraction

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/us-accuses-chinese-ai-firms-of-industrial-scale-data-extraction/)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — US cyber and law enforcement officials accused Chinese AI companies of industrial-scale 'distillation' to extract capabilities from American models, a practice they characterized as data extraction. China denied the allegations, and officials discussed the possibility of sanctions in response. The report was framed around prediction-market coverage and provides limited independently verified detail.
