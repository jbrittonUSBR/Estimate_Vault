# The Changing Face of Chinese Espionage Strategies

[Source](https://english.khabarhub.com/2026/09/571301/)

(U) KATHMANDU, Nepal — An analysis published by Khabarhub on October 9, 2026, examined how Chinese intelligence operations increasingly rely on personal relationships, employment platforms, academic networks and diaspora communities rather than conventional espionage. It cited the October 4 arrest of 34-year-old Wanying "Heather" Zhang at Los Angeles International Airport on allegations she surveilled the family of the son of Taiwan's President Lai Ching-te in Seattle and transmitted photographs, videos and license-plate data to Chinese officials. The piece also referenced the case of former US Navy sailor Jinchao Wei, sentenced in January 2026 to more than 16 years after being approached online and paid over $12,000 for information on the USS Essex, and a June 2026 Five Eyes warning that Chinese military intelligence used LinkedIn, Indeed and Upwork to identify individuals with sensitive access. It noted that MI5 accused a Chinese state-linked body of funding research involving more than 100 UK academics, allegations Beijing rejected.
