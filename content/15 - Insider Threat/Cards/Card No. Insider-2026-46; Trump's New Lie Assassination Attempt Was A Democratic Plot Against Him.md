# Trump's New Lie: Assassination Attempt Was A Democratic Plot Against Him

[Source](https://crooksandliars.com/2026/09/trump-lie-assassination-dem-plot)

(U) WASHINGTON — In a September 16, 2026 commentary for Crooks and Liars, John Amato characterized President Donald Trump's claim that the 2024 Butler, Pennsylvania, assassination attempt was a "Democrat Plot" as a lie, writing that Trump offered no evidence and was reacting to falling poll numbers and dissatisfaction with his Iran policy. Amato cited FBI Director Kash Patel's testimony that the bureau found no evidence shooter Thomas Matthew Crooks had communicated with anyone other than his parents, and criticized Attorney General Todd Blanche for declining to provide evidence of a plot. The piece is explicitly opinionated and partisan in tone and reflects the author's views.
