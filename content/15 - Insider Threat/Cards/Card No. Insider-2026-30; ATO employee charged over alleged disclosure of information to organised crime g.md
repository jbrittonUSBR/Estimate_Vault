# ATO employee charged over alleged disclosure of information to organised crime groups

[Source](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-11/gold-coast-ato-employee-charged-over-alleged-corrupt-conduct/107143728)

(U) GOLD COAST, Australia — Federal police charged a 32-year-old Australian Taxation Office employee with two counts of unlawfully disclosing information as a Commonwealth officer to organised crime syndicates. The charges followed a joint investigation by the AFP, the National Anti-Corruption Commission and the ATO that included a raid on a Surfers Paradise home, where devices were also alleged to contain child abuse material. The man, suspended without pay, was due to appear in Southport Magistrates Court later in September.
