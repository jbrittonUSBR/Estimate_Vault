# Army Major's wife, ChatGPT, Claude & China links: How this Indian woman landed in the middle of Jammu espionage probe

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/new-updates/army-majors-wife-chatgpt-claude-china-links-how-this-indian-woman-landed-in-the-middle-of-jammu-espionage-probe/articleshow/135034478.cms)

(U) JAMMU, India — The Times of India reported that investigators were examining allegations that the wife of an Indian Army major had links to Chinese operatives and had used AI chatbots, including ChatGPT and Claude, in connection with an espionage probe. The report described an ongoing investigation and attributed the claims to officials; it did not establish that the allegations had been proven.
