# Security official warns of more suspicious letters to lawmakers

[Source](https://rollcall.com/2026/10/19/security-official-warns-more-suspicious-letters-lawmakers/)

(U) October 19, 2026 — House security officials warned that suspicious letters sent to lawmakers were likely to continue, Roll Call reported, after multiple congressional offices received envelopes containing a white powder. Preliminary tests on samples reported in the wider series of mailings found no hazardous substance, though investigations and testing continued. The official’s warning reflected concern about additional mailings; no sender or motive was identified in the report.
