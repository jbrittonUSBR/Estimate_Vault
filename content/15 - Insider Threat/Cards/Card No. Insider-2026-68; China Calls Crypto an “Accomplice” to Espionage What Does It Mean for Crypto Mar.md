# China Calls Crypto an “Accomplice” to Espionage: What Does It Mean for Crypto Market?

[Source](https://bitcoinfoundation.org/news/regulation/china-calls-crypto-an-accomplice-to-espionage-what-does-it-mean-for-crypto-market/)

(U) BEIJING, China — On September 29, 2026, the Bitcoin Foundation reported that Chinese authorities had labeled cryptocurrency an "accomplice" to espionage, raising concerns about increased regulatory scrutiny of digital assets. The commentary examined potential implications for the crypto market as Beijing framed the technology as a tool exploited in intelligence activities, though specific policy measures were not detailed in the report.
