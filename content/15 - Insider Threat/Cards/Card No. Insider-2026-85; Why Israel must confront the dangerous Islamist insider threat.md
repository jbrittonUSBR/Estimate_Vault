# Why Israel must confront the dangerous Islamist insider threat

[Source](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/434039)

(U) JERUSALEM, Israel — In an opinion column for Israel National News published October 6, 2026, policy analyst and Middle East Forum fellow Amine Ayoub argued that the attempted hijacking of a Flydubai flight from Dubai to Tel Aviv exposed an aviation security blind spot that conventional airport screening cannot address. He wrote that Israel excels at detecting external threats but faces a harder challenge from threats entering "from within" through trusted professional access. Recounting the incident in which an Omani first officer launched a knife attack on the flight deck, he contended that violent Islamist ideology can penetrate vetted institutions after an individual has already gained legitimate access. Ayoub recommended focusing on concrete behavioral indicators rather than nationality or religion, establishing confidential internal reporting channels, monitoring behavioral changes among personnel with sensitive access, and improving cross-border intelligence cooperation.
