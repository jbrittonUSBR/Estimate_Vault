# Belgian-Chinese semiconductor researcher arrested over alleged GaN trade-secret theft — prosecutors suspect BelGaN insiders transferred chip IP to China

[Source](https://slashdot.org/firehose.pl?op=view&amp;id=185520248)

(U) BRUSSELS, Belgium — Belgian authorities arrested a former BelGaN researcher on suspicion of industrial espionage, alleging that proprietary gallium nitride semiconductor technology was transferred to a Chinese rival as the Belgian chipmaker headed toward bankruptcy. Prosecutors suspect BelGaN insiders transferred chip intellectual property to China, according to the Slashdot report. No further details were provided.
