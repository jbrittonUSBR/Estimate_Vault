# Secret Service stop attempted assassination of Trump as Venezuelan national arrested outside White House with car full of guns

[Source](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16273163/Secret-Service-Venezuelan-man-kill-Donald-Trump.html)

(U) Washington, D.C., United States — The Daily Mail reported that Secret Service officers arrested Venezuelan national Ronny Yordrix Guanipa Chrinos after finding him in a stolen vehicle with three firearms near the White House. DHS said he had told officers he heard voices urging him to kill President Trump; prosecutors later filed federal threats, firearms, and stolen-vehicle charges.
