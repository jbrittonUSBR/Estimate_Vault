# MICHAEL RUIZ: Tyler Robinson judge to give long-awaited trial date ruling in assassination of Charlie Kirk

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/us/michael-ruiz-tyler-robinson-judge-give-long-awaited-trial-date-ruling-assassination-charlie-kirk)

(U) Provo, Utah, United States — Fox News reported that a Utah judge set jury selection in the trial of Tyler Robinson, accused of killing Charlie Kirk, to begin on June 21, 2028. The date followed competing proposals from prosecutors and defense attorneys, and Robinson remained accused rather than convicted.
