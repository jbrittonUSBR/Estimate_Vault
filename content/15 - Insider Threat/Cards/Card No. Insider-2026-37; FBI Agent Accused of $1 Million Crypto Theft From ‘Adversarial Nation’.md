# FBI Agent Accused of $1 Million Crypto Theft From ‘Adversarial Nation’

[Source](https://activistpost.com/fbi-agent-accused-of-1-million-crypto-theft-from-adversarial-nation/)

(U) ASHBURN, United States — Court documents unsealed in a Virginia district court alleged that Patrick Steven Yaroch, a former FBI supervisory special agent in the bureau's Headquarters Counterintelligence and Espionage Division, transferred $925,426.07 in cryptocurrency from wallets tied to unnamed "adversarial" nations into his personal wallets over numerous transactions. According to the affidavit and a detention motion, Yaroch said he was frustrated that the FBI could not or would not act against the accounts and "took matters into his own hands," later confessing to a Justice Department employee; he was fired from the FBI on 31 July and faces two felonies carrying maximum penalties of 10 years each. The account, republished by Activist Post from Bitcoin Magazine, said the country involved was not named in the documents and that Yaroch, of Ashburn, Virginia, had considered retiring to Portugal.
