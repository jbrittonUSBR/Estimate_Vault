# Chinese-Belgian national charged with espionage involving microchips

[Source](https://intelnews.org/2026/09/13/01-3455/)

(U) OUDENAARDE, Belgium — A 52-year-old dual Belgian-Chinese national and former senior employee at the bankrupt microchip manufacturer BelGaN remained in custody in Belgium on suspicion of espionage involving gallium nitride semiconductors. Identified only as H.L., the suspect was arrested on May 10 at Zaventem airport in Brussels as he prepared to board a flight to Beijing, and faces four charges: espionage, participation in a criminal organization, misuse of company assets, and unlawful disclosure of trade secrets. Investigators suspect he simultaneously served as a director of Gankool, a Chinese-funded company in Fujian province accused of replicating BelGaN's technology. A second suspect, former BelGaN CEO Alan Zhou Zhen, reportedly remains at large. Belgium's State Security Service has described the pattern as "mirror companies," in which Chinese investors acquire underfunded European tech firms and extract their technology.
