# NIA chargesheets 13 accused in espionage case involving sensitive installations

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/lucknow/nia-chargesheets-13-accused-in-espionage-case-involving-sensitive-installations/articleshow/133797997.cms)

(U) LUCKNOW, India — India's National Investigation Agency filed a chargesheet against 13 accused in a Lucknow special court in an espionage case involving illegal access to sensitive installations and the transmission of geo-tagged photographs and videos to entities in an enemy country. Twenty-one people were arrested in all, and a directive was issued against five juveniles. The accused were charged under provisions of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, the Official Secrets Act and the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act.
