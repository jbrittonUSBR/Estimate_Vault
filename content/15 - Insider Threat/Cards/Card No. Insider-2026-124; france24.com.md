# Two Men Re-Arrested for Suspected Spying for Iran in UK Airbase Scare

[Source](https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20261021-two-men-re-arrested-for-suspected-spying-for-iran-in-airbase-scare-uk-police)

(U) LONDON — UK police re-arrested two men on suspicion of spying for Iran in connection with a security scare at a British airbase, according to a France 24/AFP report. The re-arrest followed earlier detention of the suspects as counterterrorism officers investigated alleged hostile-state intelligence activity linked to Tehran targeting a military airbase. The case is part of a broader pattern of heightened UK counter-intelligence concern over Iran-linked espionage, arrests, and plotting against individuals and sites in the country.
