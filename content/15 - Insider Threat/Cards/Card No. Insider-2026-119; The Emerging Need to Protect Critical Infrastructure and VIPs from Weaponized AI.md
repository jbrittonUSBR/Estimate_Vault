# The Emerging Need to Protect Critical Infrastructure and VIPs from Weaponized AI Drone Threats

[Source](https://thesoufancenter.org/intelbrief-2026-october-19/)

(U) October 19, 2026 — In an analysis, the Soufan Center argued that consumer AI could make it easier to adapt commercial drones with facial- and object-recognition capabilities for targeted attacks on infrastructure and prominent individuals. The authors assessed that these developments increase the need for stronger counter-drone capabilities and coordination between counter-unmanned-aircraft and AI security efforts. The piece presents the center’s threat analysis and recommendations, not reporting of a specific attack.
