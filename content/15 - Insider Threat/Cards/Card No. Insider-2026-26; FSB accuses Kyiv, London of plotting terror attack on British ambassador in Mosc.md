# FSB accuses Kyiv, London of plotting terror attack on British ambassador in Moscow

[Source](https://www.naturalnews.com/2026-09-10-fsb-accuses-kyiv-london-plotting-terror-attack.html)

(U) MOSCOW — Russia's Federal Security Service announced on September 10, 2026, that it had disrupted an alleged Ukrainian intelligence operation targeting British Ambassador Nigel Casey in Moscow, according to a report by NaturalNews. The FSB alleged that a Russian national employed as a security guard at the British Embassy collected personal data on diplomatic personnel at the direction of Ukrainian blogger Dmytro Karpenko, working with Ukraine's SBU, to stage a false-flag attack and blame Russia. The British Embassy did not comment on the allegations, which the outlet noted were unverified and unconfirmed by independent sources.
