# Trump accuses Democrats of mishandling his assassination attempt probe

[Source](https://www.rt.com/news/645878-trump-democrats-assassination-attempt-probe/)

(U) WASHINGTON — President Donald Trump accused Democrats of mishandling the probe into his assassination attempts, alleging in a Truth Social post that information was "missing, altered, corrupted, or gone." The Russian state media outlet RT reported Trump's claims as FBI Director Kash Patel said there were no known accomplices and Deputy Attorney General Todd Blanche cited new information in the investigation, drawing renewed partisan attacks over federal agencies' handling of the Butler incident.
