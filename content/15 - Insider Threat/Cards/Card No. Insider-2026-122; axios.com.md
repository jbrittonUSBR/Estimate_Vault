# New Research: Top AI Models Can Blueprint Cyberattacks on Power and Water Systems

[Source](https://www.axios.com/2026/10/21/ai-hacking-cybersecurity-water-power-grid)

(U) WASHINGTON — New research from cybersecurity firm Frenos found that freely available AI models are increasingly capable of planning cyberattacks on critical services such as power grids and water systems. Researchers tested 10 AI models' ability to plan attacks against industrial systems, including software monitoring water-treatment processes; three of the 10 models successfully constructed attack plans in roughly 90% of attempts, though Frenos did not disclose which models were tested. Frenos co-founder and CTO Harry Thomas said that once attackers gain access to an industrial network, AI can help them quietly map systems and identify weaknesses without the time constraints facing human hackers. Former American Electric Power security executive Steve Swick warned it is "a clock that is moving faster than we've ever seen." The report notes critical infrastructure operators face slow-moving regulatory and funding hurdles — including the rescission of an EPA water-utility cybersecurity rule after legal challenges — while recent attacks, including an Iran-linked intrusion at a U.K. power plant, have so far caused limited disruption.
