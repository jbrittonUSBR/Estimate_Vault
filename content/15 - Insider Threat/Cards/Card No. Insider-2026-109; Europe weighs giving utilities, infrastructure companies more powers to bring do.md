# Europe weighs giving utilities, infrastructure companies more powers to bring down drones

[Source](https://www.defensenews.com/global/europe/2026/10/21/europe-weighs-giving-utilities-infrastructure-companies-more-powers-to-bring-down-drones/)

(U) October 21, 2026 — European governments were considering whether to grant utilities and other critical-infrastructure operators greater legal authority to detect, disrupt, or bring down drones near sensitive facilities, Reuters reported in Defense News. Companies and officials cited concern about drone activity around energy and other infrastructure, while existing restrictions and safety risks limited private operators’ ability to respond. The measures were under consideration, not described as adopted policy.
