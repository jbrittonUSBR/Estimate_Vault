# Maharashtra TET papers smuggled in shoe insoles for ₹8K, says chargesheet

[Source](https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/maharashtra-tet-papers-smuggled-in-shoe-insoles-for-8k-says-chargesheet-126091900113_1.html)

(U) Thane, India — According to a Thane police chargesheet, employees at an Agra printing press smuggled Maharashtra TET question papers out hidden in shoe insoles for ₹8,000 each as part of an interstate exam-leak racket involving 18 accused.
