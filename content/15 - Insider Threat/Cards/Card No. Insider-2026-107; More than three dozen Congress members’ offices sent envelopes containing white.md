# More than three dozen Congress members’ offices sent envelopes containing white powder

[Source](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/oct/19/white-powder-envelopes-congress)

(U) October 19, 2026 — More than three dozen congressional offices received envelopes containing a white powder, according to the Guardian, amid a broader wave of suspicious mailings to elected officials. At least 10 people were exposed to powder sent to some House offices, but no injuries were reported; preliminary testing found no hazardous substances in the letters examined. Federal authorities investigated the mailings, and the sender and motive were not publicly identified.
