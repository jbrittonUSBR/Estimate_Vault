# Russian threats ‘less and less hybrid,’ Von der Leyen says after assassination plot in Denmark - POLITICO.eu

[Source](https://slashdot.org/firehose.pl?op=view&id=186821184)

(U) Copenhagen, Denmark — A Slashdot Firehose item relayed POLITICO's report that European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen said Russian hybrid threats 'are becoming less and less hybrid' after Danish police stopped an alleged assassination attempt. Danish intelligence said a Russian service had hired two members of the Copenhagen gang Loyal to Familia to kill rapper Kyivstoner before a September 25 concert, and the government ordered Russia's defense attaché to leave within seven days. The suspects denied the allegations, and the Russian embassy in Denmark said the accusations were unsupported by evidence.
