# Lavrov: Assassination of Ayatollah Khamenei 'unacceptable' manifestation of coercive diktat

[Source](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/iran/2026/09/iran-260926-presstv05.htm)

(U) MOSCOW, Russia — According to a Globalsecurity.org aggregator citing PressTV, Russian Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov said that any assassination of Iran's Ayatollah Ali Khamenei would be an "unacceptable" manifestation of a coercive diktat, in comments reflecting Russian opposition to threats against Iran's leadership.
