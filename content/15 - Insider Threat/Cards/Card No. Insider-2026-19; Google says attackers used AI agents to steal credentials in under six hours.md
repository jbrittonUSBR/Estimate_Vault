# Google says attackers used AI agents to steal credentials in under six hours

[Source](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/08/google-says-attackers-used-ai-agents-to-steal-credentials-in-under-six-hours/)

(U) MOUNTAIN VIEW, California — Google reported that attackers used a multi-agent AI framework to harvest thousands of credentials in under six hours, according to SiliconANGLE. The company's Threat Intelligence Group described how the agentic system automated each stage of the intrusion at machine speed. The disclosure underscored how AI is compressing the time required to carry out large-scale credential theft.
