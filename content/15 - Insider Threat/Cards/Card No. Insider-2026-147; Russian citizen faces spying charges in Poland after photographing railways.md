# Russian citizen faces spying charges in Poland after photographing railways

[Source](https://www.politico.eu/article/russian-citizen-detained-in-poland-over-espionage/)

(U) Swidnica, Poland — POLITICO reported that Polish police arrested a 55-year-old Russian citizen while he was photographing trains at an unguarded railway crossing. Prosecutors charged him with espionage after police said they found photographs of strategic infrastructure in Poland and other locations; the case remained under investigation.
