# 'The Assassin(s)' producers face probe over depiction of Park Chung-hee plotting wife's death

[Source](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/entertainment/films/20260929/cover-story-the-assassins-producers-face-probe-over-depiction-of-park-chung-hee-plotting-wifes-death)

(U) SEOUL — On September 29, 2026, The Korea Times reported that producers of the film *The Assassin(s)* were facing a defamation probe after a former People Power Party city councilor filed a police complaint on Tuesday, September 29, 2026, over the film's depiction of then-President Park Chung-hee plotting the assassination of his wife, Yuk Young-soo. The complaint centered on the film's portrayal of events surrounding the August 15, 1974 shooting of the first lady by Mun Se-gwang, coming as the historical drama drew over 1.4 million viewers during Chuseok and a total of 1.63 million per the Korean Film Council.
