# Chinese National Accused of Illegally Modifying Homeland Security Computers at Maine-Canada Border Station

[Source](https://www.themainewire.com/2026/09/chinese-national-accused-of-illegally-modifying-homeland-security-computers-at-maine-canada-border-station/)

(U) CALAIS, United States — An FBI affidavit alleged that Terry "Jiajia" Liu, a 39-year-old Chinese national employed as a Customs and Border Protection supervisor, illegally modified Department of Homeland Security computers at Maine border facilities, stripping components and replacing them with inferior parts he then traded for credit. Investigators said hidden-camera footage captured Liu, charged with theft and damage of government property, swapping hardware during overnight shifts across 46 computers at three ports of entry. The affidavit did not allege that the altered hardware served an espionage purpose, and Liu maintained that one explanation for his actions was frustration with slow CBP repairs.
