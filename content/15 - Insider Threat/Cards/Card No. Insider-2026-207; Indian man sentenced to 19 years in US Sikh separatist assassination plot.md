# Indian man sentenced to 19 years in US Sikh separatist assassination plot

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/20/indian-man-sentenced-to-19-years-in-us-sikh-separatist-assassination-plot)

(U) New York, USA — A federal judge in Manhattan sentenced Nikhil Gupta, 55, to 235 months in prison on November 20 after he pleaded guilty in February to three conspiracy charges in a murder-for-hire plot against Sikh separatist lawyer Gurpatwant Singh Pannun. Court documents said Gupta and Indian government official Vikash Yadav arranged $15,000 in cash for a supposed hitman who was actually an undercover officer, and Gupta apologized in court. Prosecutors said Yadav remained at large, while India's government denied involvement and Pannun called Gupta 'just a foot soldier' acting on behalf of Prime Minister Narendra Modi.
