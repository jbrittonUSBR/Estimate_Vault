# The Pentagon is reportedly ramping up economic warfare and espionage. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s affair with a suspected Chinese spy shows why

[Source](https://fortune.com/2026/11/08/pentagon-economic-warfare-espionage-nvidia-jensen-huang-chinese-spy-romantic-affair/)

(U) Washington, D.C., United States — Fortune reported that the Pentagon was planning a deep-cover intelligence program using officers posing as business executives as part of a broader economic-warfare effort. Fortune placed the report alongside Nvidia's board investigation into whether CEO Jensen Huang had been targeted by foreign intelligence, while Nvidia said it had no reason to believe company secrets or national security were compromised.
