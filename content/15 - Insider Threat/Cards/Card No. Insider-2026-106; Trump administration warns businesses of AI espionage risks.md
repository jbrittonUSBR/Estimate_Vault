# Trump administration warns businesses of AI espionage risks

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/trump-administration-ai-espionage-warning/)

(U) October 19, 2026 — Crypto Briefing, an aggregator, summarized a warning by Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton that foreign actors could use artificial intelligence to pursue espionage against U.S. companies and government entities. Clayton urged organizations to recognize the risk and strengthen security around sensitive technology and information. The account presents the administration’s threat assessment, rather than independently verified incidents attributed to particular actors.
