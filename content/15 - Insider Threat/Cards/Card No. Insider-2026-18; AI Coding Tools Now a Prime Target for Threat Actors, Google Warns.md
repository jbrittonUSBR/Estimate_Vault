# AI Coding Tools Now a Prime Target for Threat Actors, Google Warns

[Source](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/ai-coding-tools-threat-actors/)

(U) MOUNTAIN VIEW, California — Google's Threat Intelligence Group warned that AI coding tools have become a prime target for threat actors seeking to compromise software supply chains. Infosecurity Magazine reported that a China-nexus group tracked as UNC6780, also known as Dustmaker, conducted supply-chain compromises targeting developer ecosystems including PyPI, npm and Docker Hub. The warning highlighted the growing risk to developers and the software they build.
