# Former German spy chief arrested on suspicion of espionage, attempted treason

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/world/former-german-spy-chief-arrested-suspicion-espionage-attempted-treason)

(U) Nordwalde, Germany — German authorities arrested August Hanning, the 80-year-old former head of the Federal Intelligence Service (BND), on suspicion of espionage and attempted treason after prosecutors alleged he received roughly 2,000 classified documents from a former aide in exchange for payment and used them in private consulting, including for a foreign intelligence service.
