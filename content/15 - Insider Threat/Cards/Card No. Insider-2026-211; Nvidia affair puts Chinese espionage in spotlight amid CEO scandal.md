# Nvidia affair puts Chinese espionage in spotlight amid CEO scandal

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/nvidia-affair-puts-chinese-espionage-in-spotlight-amid-ceo-scandal/)

(U) United States — Crypto Briefing's market-focused analysis said prediction markets priced an 88.5 percent chance that Jensen Huang would testify before Congress by January 1, 2027, as the Nvidia affair drew scrutiny to alleged Chinese espionage. The analysis said an internal Nvidia board review found no breach of company secrets or US national security, while FBI, Justice Department and Commerce Department reviews continued and China rejected claims that Alice Huang had intelligence links. The outlet cautioned that its interpretation relied on public information and market prices rather than original reporting.
