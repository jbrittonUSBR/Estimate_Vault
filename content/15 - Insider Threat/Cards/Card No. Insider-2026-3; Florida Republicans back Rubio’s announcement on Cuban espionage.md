(U) Florida Republican lawmakers voiced support for Secretary of State Marco Rubio’s release of a State Department report alleging decades of Cuban espionage and political subversion in the United States. Rubio described the Cuban regime as “the leading sponsor of radical leftism and Third Worldism in the United States” and said the report exposes the full history of its activities. Sen. Ashley Moody linked the findings to the rise of democratic socialist politicians in the U.S., urging people to hear from Florida’s Cuban community about the realities of the totalitarian regime. Rep. María Elvira Salazar praised the report for documenting how Havana has exported intelligence operations, political subversion, and tools for building dictatorships across the hemisphere, calling Cuba “the command center of 21st-century communism.”
