# Spy and sell: The murky history of economic espionage

[Source](https://www.rte.ie/news/business/2026/1122/1600078-spy-nvidia-china/)

(U) Dublin, Ireland — In an analysis piece, RTÉ business journalist Adam Maguire framed the allegation that former Nvidia chief Jensen Huang had a relationship with a suspected Chinese spy as the latest in a long series of economic-espionage claims involving China. He cited the 2026 US conviction of Linwei Ding for stealing more than 2,000 pages of Google AI data, noting that the economic-espionage count was later overturned while trade-secret charges stood, alongside cases involving GE turbine designs and alleged Chinese use of joint ventures and networking equipment. Maguire also noted Chinese and corporate denials and described honeypot techniques as a longstanding espionage method.
