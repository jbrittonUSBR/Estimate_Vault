# DeepSeek, Alibaba among major Chinese AI firms accused of stealing trade secrets of American counterparts

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/us/deepseek-alibaba-among-major-chinese-ai-firms-accused-of-stealing-trade-secrets-of-american-counterparts/articleshow/133963450.cms)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — US authorities accused major Chinese AI firms, including DeepSeek and Alibaba, of stealing trade secrets from American counterparts through a technique known as 'distillation,' in a joint advisory from the FBI, NSA and CISA. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent warned of potential sanctions and Entity List designations. Separately, Anthropic reported that three Chinese labs ran an industrial-scale campaign involving more than 16 million exchanges and about 24,000 fraudulent accounts.
