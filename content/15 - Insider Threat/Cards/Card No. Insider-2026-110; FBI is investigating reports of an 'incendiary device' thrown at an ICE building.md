# FBI is investigating reports of an 'incendiary device' thrown at an ICE building

[Source](https://abcnews.com/US/wireStory/fbi-investigating-reports-incendiary-device-thrown-ice-building-137435718)

(U) October 21, 2026 — The FBI investigated reports that an incendiary device was thrown at a U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement building in downtown Atlanta, the Associated Press reported. The incident caused minor damage, according to the FBI, and no injuries were reported. The investigation was ongoing, with no arrest or suspect publicly announced in the report.
