# Alito Hints That The Dobbs Knew A Member of the Majority Would Be Subject To Assassination

[Source](https://reason.com/volokh/2026/10/04/alito-hints-that-the-dobbs-knew-a-member-of-the-majority-would-be-subject-to-assassination/)

(U) WASHINGTON, D.C. — Writing in a Reason opinion piece, Josh Blackman reported that Justice Samuel Alito had hinted the majority in Dobbs v. Jackson Women's Health Organization knew a member of the majority would be subject to assassination. The libertarian publication noted Alito's comments as raising concerns about security threats to Supreme Court justices, though the claim remained a matter of interpretation.
