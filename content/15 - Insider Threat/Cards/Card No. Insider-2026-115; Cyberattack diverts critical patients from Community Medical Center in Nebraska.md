# Cyberattack diverts critical patients from Community Medical Center in Nebraska

[Source](https://dysruptionhub.com/community-medical-center-nebraska-cyberattack/)

(U) October 20, 2026 — DysruptionHub, an industry cyber-incident tracking blog, reported that a cyberattack disrupted Community Medical Center in Falls City, Nebraska, over the October 17–18 weekend. According to the blog, the hospital shifted to paper-based workflows, disrupted appointments, and diverted critical patients to other facilities. The report did not identify a perpetrator or specify the attack method, and the operational details have not been independently confirmed here.
