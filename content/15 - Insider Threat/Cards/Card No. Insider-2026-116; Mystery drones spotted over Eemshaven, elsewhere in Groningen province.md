# Mystery drones spotted over Eemshaven, elsewhere in Groningen province

[Source](https://nltimes.nl/2026/10/20/mystery-drones-spotted-eemshaven-elsewhere-groningen-province)

(U) October 20, 2026 — Residents and workers reported unidentified drone sightings near the port of Eemshaven and elsewhere in Groningen province, NL Times reported. A local broadcaster said sightings near the port began in early October, while police were investigating reports in the area and at other locations. Authorities had not confirmed who operated the aircraft or their purpose.
