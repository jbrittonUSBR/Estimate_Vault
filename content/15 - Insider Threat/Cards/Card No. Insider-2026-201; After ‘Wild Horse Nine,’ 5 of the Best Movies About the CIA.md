# After ‘Wild Horse Nine,’ 5 of the Best Movies About the CIA

[Source](https://foreignpolicy.com/2026/11/20/cia-movies-wild-horse-nine-review-espionage-us-intelligence/)

(U) United States — In a film-review listicle, Foreign Policy critic Jordan Hoffman ranked Argo, Charlie Wilson's War, Hopscotch, The In-Laws and Burn After Reading from least to most 'zany,' arguing that Hollywood has usually treated the CIA comically rather than seriously. He described Martin McDonagh's new dark comedy Wild Horse Nine, set during the 1973 Chilean coup, as using the agency as a backdrop for mortality and remorse. Hoffman noted that Argo won the best-picture Oscar and that the other films portrayed agency figures as buffoons or klutzes.
