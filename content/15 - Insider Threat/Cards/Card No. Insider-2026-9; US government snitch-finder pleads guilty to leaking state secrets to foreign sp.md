# US government snitch-finder pleads guilty to leaking state secrets to foreign spies

[Source](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/08/28/us-government-snitch-finder-pleads-guilty-to-leaking-state-secrets-to-foreign-spies/5293248)

(U) WASHINGTON — Nathan Vilas Laatsch, a former Defense Intelligence Agency IT specialist, pleaded guilty to trying to pass secret and top-secret information to foreign spies following a successful FBI sting, The Register reported. Laatsch was arrested in May 2025 after an undercover FBI agent caught him transmitting intelligence packages in a public park that he believed would be collected by a foreign government's spy; he had been employed at the DIA as a civilian since 2019 and held top-secret clearance. In March 2025 he offered to transmit classified information to an overseas administration, and he was assigned to the DIA's Insider Threat Division in spring 2025, a unit dedicated to identifying government workers likely to leak classified information. The foreign country has never been revealed, but court documents describe it as "a friendly foreign government."
