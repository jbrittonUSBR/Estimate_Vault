# Critics warn of espionage risks, loss of trust within NATO as Slovakia restores military-diplomatic representation with Russia

[Source](https://www.dw.com/en/critics-warn-of-espionage-risks-loss-of-trust-within-nato-as-slovakia-restores-military-diplomatic-representation-with-russia/a-79649641)

(U) BRATISLAVA, Slovakia — On October 13, 2026, DW reported that Slovakia is restoring military-diplomatic representation with Russia for the first time since its 2022 invasion of Ukraine, sending military officers to Moscow and preparing to receive a Russian military attache in Bratislava. Critics warn the move risks espionage, facilitates Russian intelligence operations, and could erode trust within NATO, while the government defends it as necessary for information gathering ahead of next year’s election.
