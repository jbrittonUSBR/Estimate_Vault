# Ex-OpenAI engineer accused of espionage had Saudi backing, prosecutors say

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/ex-openai-engineer-espionage-saudi-backing/)

(U) October 19, 2026 — Crypto Briefing, an aggregator, reported that federal prosecutors alleged former OpenAI engineer Abdulrahman Alfozan received support from Saudi-linked figures while under investigation for allegedly installing a concealed audio-recording device at OpenAI. The Justice Department said Alfozan was arrested and charged in connection with the device; the alleged Saudi backing and espionage-related conduct remained prosecutors’ claims, not findings of guilt.
