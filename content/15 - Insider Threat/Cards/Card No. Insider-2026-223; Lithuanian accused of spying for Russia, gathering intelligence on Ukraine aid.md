# Lithuanian accused of spying for Russia, gathering intelligence on Ukraine aid

[Source](https://www.lrt.lt/en/news-in-english/19/3125143/lithuanian-accused-of-spying-for-russia-gathering-intelligence-on-ukraine-aid)

(U) KAUNAS, LITHUANIA — LRT, citing BNS and the Kaunas Regional Prosecutor's Office, reported that a Lithuanian man was charged with assisting Russia by gathering intelligence on strategically important infrastructure and aid to Ukraine while carrying out assignments for Russian intelligence services from August 2021. Prosecutors said he had received money from Russian citizens and was identified after he allegedly photographed aid for Ukrainian soldiers at a private company in March 2025 and sprayed an employee with tear gas when confronted. He was also charged with illegally possessing firearms, ammunition and explosives including hand grenades, and LRT said the case was referred to Kaunas Regional Court with a maximum sentence of eight years if convicted.
