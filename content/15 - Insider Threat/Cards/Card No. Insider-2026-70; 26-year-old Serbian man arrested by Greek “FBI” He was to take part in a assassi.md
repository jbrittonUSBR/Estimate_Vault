# 26-year-old Serbian man arrested by Greek “FBI”: He was to take part in a assassination in Greece

[Source](https://en.protothema.gr/2026/09/30/26-year-old-serbian-man-arrested-by-greek-fbi-he-was-to-take-part-in-a-assassination-in-greece/)

(U) THESSALONIKI, Greece — Heavily armed officers from Greece's Organised Crime Sub-Directorate arrested a 26-year-old Serbian man at a food and drink establishment in Thessaloniki. Police sources said the man, wanted under an Interpol Red Notice in connection with a robbery, had entered Greece on Sept. 26 and that his presence was linked to a premeditated attack. Three other people were taken in with him, and Serbian authorities were reportedly examining his involvement in other serious cases.
