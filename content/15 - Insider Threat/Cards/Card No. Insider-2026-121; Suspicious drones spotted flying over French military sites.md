# Suspicious drones spotted flying over French military sites

[Source](https://www.politico.eu/article/suspicious-drone-fly-over-france-military-site/)

(U) October 21, 2026 — POLITICO reported that the French Navy said suspicious drones flew over military facilities in southeastern France earlier in October. The report described the incursions as occurring during a military exercise; the operators and purpose of the drones were not identified in the available account.
