# China sentences official to death for espionage

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/china/chinese-official-sentenced-to-death-for-leaking-100-intelligence-reports-including-35-state-secrets/articleshow/136102986.cms)

(U) Beijing, China — A Chinese government official identified only by the surname Ma was sentenced to death and had his property confiscated after passing more than 100 intelligence reports, including 35 classified as state secrets, to a foreign agency, according to a social media post by the country's state secrets regulator cited by state media. The post said Ma was recruited while serving abroad by an officer who befriended him under the pretext of teaching Chinese and later blackmailed him into cooperating, and that he received over one million yuan (about $150,000) in espionage payments. The case highlighted a campaign by China's state secrecy regulator and Ministry of State Security to publicly warn officials against spying for foreign powers.
