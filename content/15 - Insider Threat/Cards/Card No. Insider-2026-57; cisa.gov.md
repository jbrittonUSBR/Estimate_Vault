# CISA Releases Updated Insider Threat Guide with New Insights to Mitigate Physical and Cyber Threats

[Source](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/news/cisa-releases-updated-insider-threat-guide-new-insights-mitigate-physical-and-cyber-threats)

(U) WASHINGTON — The Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) released an updated insider threat guide providing new insights to help organizations mitigate physical and cyber insider threats. The updated guidance addresses the evolving insider-threat landscape, offering practical measures for detecting, deterring, and responding to insider risks that span both physical security and cyber domains, aimed at critical infrastructure owners and operators and other organizations.
