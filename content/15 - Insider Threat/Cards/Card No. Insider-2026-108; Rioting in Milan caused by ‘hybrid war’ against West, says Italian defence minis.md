# Rioting in Milan caused by ‘hybrid war’ against West, says Italian defence minister

[Source](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/europe/italy-milan-riots-protests-hybrid-war-b3069752.html)

(U) October 20, 2026 — Italian Defence Minister Guido Crosetto attributed violence during protests in Milan to a wider “hybrid war” against the West, the Independent reported. His remarks followed clashes and rioting during demonstrations, and represented the minister’s assessment rather than a publicly established finding about who organized or influenced the unrest. The government announced additional security measures in response.
