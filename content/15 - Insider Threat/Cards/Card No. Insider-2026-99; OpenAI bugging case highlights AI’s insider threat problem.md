# OpenAI bugging case highlights AI’s insider threat problem

[Source](https://www.nextgov.com/artificial-intelligence/2026/10/openai-bugging-case-highlights-ais-insider-threat-problem/416596/)

(U) UNITED STATES — Nextgov reported that federal prosecutors alleged an individual had attempted to secretly record sensitive areas at OpenAI without authorization. The complaint said security personnel found a small recording device and another similar device in a bag containing the suspect’s identification. Researchers cited in the article said the case illustrated risks from employee access to sensitive information and the need for stronger internal controls.
