# Ex-Army jawan, Bihar man held over Pak espionage

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/guwahati/ex-army-jawan-bihar-man-held-over-pak-espionage/articleshow/134679324.cms)

(U) GUWAHATI, India — Indian authorities arrested a former Army soldier and another man from Bihar on charges of espionage for Pakistan. The suspects were alleged to have shared sensitive military information with Pakistani intelligence operatives, raising concerns about insider threats to India's defense infrastructure.
