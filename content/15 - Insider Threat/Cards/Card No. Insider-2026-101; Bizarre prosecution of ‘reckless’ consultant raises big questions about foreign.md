# Bizarre prosecution of ‘reckless’ consultant raises big questions about foreign interference laws

[Source](http://www.crikey.com.au/2026/10/16/alexander-csergo-china-foreign-interference-laws-espionage/)

(U) Sydney, Australia — In an opinion article, Crikey's Bernard Keane argued that the prosecution of consultant Alexander Csergo illustrated what he viewed as vagueness and selective application in Australia's foreign-interference law. Keane questioned the scope of the legal provisions and their use in the case, presenting a critical interpretation rather than a neutral account. Csergo's alleged conduct and the prosecution's case remain distinct from the columnist's opinion about the law.
