# Ben Macintyre on a spy who was the opposite of James Bond but helped win the cold war

[Source](https://www.irishtimes.com/culture/books/2026/09/08/ben-macintyre-on-cold-war-espionage-we-could-have-been-living-in-a-different-world/)

(U) DUBLIN, Ireland — Historian Ben Macintyre discussed his book "Redwood," which recounts the story of Soviet KGB officer Vladimir Kuzichkin, a double agent who spied for Britain's MI6. The interview examined how Kuzichkin, described as the antithesis of a James Bond figure, operated in Tehran after the 1979 revolution and helped shape Cold War intelligence. Macintyre argued the outcome of the Cold War might have differed had such spies not turned.
