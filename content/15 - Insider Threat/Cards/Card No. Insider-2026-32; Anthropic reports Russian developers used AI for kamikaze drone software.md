# Anthropic reports Russian developers used AI for kamikaze drone software

[Source](https://cryptobriefing.com/anthropic-russian-ai-kamikaze-drone-software/)

(U) SAN FRANCISCO, United States — Anthropic published a 154-page threat intelligence report stating that Russian developers used its Claude Code assistant to build software for autonomous kamikaze drones intended for use against Ukraine. The project, tracked as GTG-27005 and internally called DronDoc or Serafim, incorporated a computer-vision targeting classifier trained on Ukrainian combat footage and was designed to let drones select targets and detonate autonomously; the company assessed the developers as freelancers and noted no confirmed operational deployment. Crypto Briefing, a crypto-news outlet, summarized the report.
