# Iran Warns US of Severe Retaliation, Threatens Critical Infrastructure Amid Regional Tensions

[Source](https://www.ndtvprofit.com/world/iran-warns-us-of-severe-retaliation-threatens-critical-infrastructure-amid-regional-tensions-12209419)

(U) TEHRAN — Iran has warned the United States that renewed military action against it would trigger severe retaliation extending beyond military targets to regional critical infrastructure, including energy supplies and undersea communications cables transiting the Strait of Hormuz. Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) spokesman Brigadier-General Hossein Mohebi said Tehran had acquired more advanced weaponry since previous clashes and that any response would be significantly more severe than before. Supreme National Security Council Secretary Mohsen Rezaei warned a resumed war with the US and Israel would place roughly $2 trillion in regional technology infrastructure — including undersea cables, data centers, and AI facilities — "within range," and hardline outlets called for using Persian Gulf fiber-optic cables to pressure Washington. The IRGC also told regional governments, funds, and firms to deny finance, equity, land, and energy to any AI or data company supporting military operations, and Iran's parliament speaker announced plans for a proposed "Persian Gulf Data Authority." The warnings raised concern over the vulnerability of the Middle East's interconnected energy and digital networks.
