# Taiwan’s Six-Year Hunt for China’s Undercover Chip Labs

[Source](https://restofworld.org/2026/taiwan-china-chip-investigations/)

(U) HSINCHU, Taiwan — Taiwan's Ministry of Justice Investigation Bureau told Rest of World that authorities had completed 166 investigations over six years into technology companies accused of concealing Chinese ownership while recruiting chip talent and pursuing sensitive technology, alongside 67 China-related trade-secret probes in the tech sector. The previously unpublished data showed the bureau launched an operation involving all of its 2,000 investigators in 2020, with the Hsinchu District Prosecutors Office alone charging about 60 cases involving nearly 190 individuals, and on 5 August the bureau announced raids on another 18 technology companies. Cases cited included Blue Ocean Smart System, raided in August 2021 over alleged disguised Chinese ownership, and OnePlus founder Pete Lau, for whom Taiwan issued an arrest warrant over an alleged unauthorised software research operation that recruited more than 70 engineers.
