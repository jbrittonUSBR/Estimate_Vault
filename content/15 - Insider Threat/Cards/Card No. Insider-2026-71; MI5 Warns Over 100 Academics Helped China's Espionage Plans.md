# MI5 Warns Over 100 Academics Helped China's Espionage Plans

[Source](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/mi5-alerts-academics-chinese/)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — MI5 issued a rare espionage alert warning that more than 100 UK-linked academics contributed to projects funded by China's Ministry of State Security through the China General Technology Research Institute. The agency said CGTRI's primary purpose is to fund research that directly improves MSS technical capability, including work on artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, covert communications systems and steganography. MI5 advised universities to review current or planned collaboration with CGTRI, trace funding sources, and consider the legal consequences under the National Security Act 2023.
