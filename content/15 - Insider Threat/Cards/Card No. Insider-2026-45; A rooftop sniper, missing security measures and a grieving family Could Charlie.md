# A rooftop sniper, missing security measures and a grieving family: Could Charlie Kirk’s assassination have been prevented? Family raises new questions

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/global-trends/a-rooftop-sniper-missing-security-measures-and-a-grieving-family-could-charlie-kirks-assassination-have-been-prevented-family-raises-new-questions/articleshow/134288439.cms)

(U) OREM, Utah — Attorneys for Charlie Kirk's parents and widow filed a wrongful death notice dated September 9, 2026, accusing Utah Valley University, former president Astrid Tuminez, the campus police department, Police Chief Jeffrey Long and the state of Utah of reckless security decisions before Kirk was fatally shot on September 10, 2025. The notice, a required precursor to a possible lawsuit under Utah law, alleges inadequate risk assessment, no rooftop surveillance, no drone monitoring, no bag checks or metal detectors and no ambulance immediately available; it does not establish liability. Kirk, 31, was speaking to about 3,000 people in a campus courtyard when a gunman fired from a rooftop more than 400 feet away, and the criminal case against accused shooter Tyler Robinson was ordered toward trial on September 1, 2026.
