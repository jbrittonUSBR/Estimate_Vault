# Venezuelan illegal immigrant arrested in Utah after soliciting assassination of Trump, DHS says

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/us/venezuelan-illegal-immigrant-arrested-utah-soliciting-assassination-trump-dhs)

(U) PROVO, UTAH — Fox News reported, citing the Department of Homeland Security, that Venezuelan national Hector Alejandro Mujica-Hernandez was arrested in Provo after an FBI tip linked him to stickers distributed at an anti-ICE meeting that urged President Donald Trump's assassination. DHS said he had entered the U.S. legally in 2016 but overstayed his visa, and Secretary Markwayne Mullin said the agency would not tolerate death threats. The investigation centred on material reading "Shoot Trump, be a hero, become unforgettable" at a People of Provo Against ICE gathering.
