# Pak handlers ‘Chacha’, ‘Shah’ may be named in rly blast chargesheet

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/jaipur/pak-handlers-chacha-shah-may-be-named-in-rly-blast-chargesheet/articleshow/136037220.cms)

(U) Jaipur, India — Rajasthan Police said they intended to name two Pakistan-based ISI operatives known as 'Chacha' and 'Shah' in the chargesheet for the November 3 railway-track explosion in Sriganganagar. Investigators alleged the pair groomed 21-year-old Mahakdeep Kaur to plant an IED and linked one of them to an earlier drone-delivered RDX consignment. A senior police officer told The Times of India that Kaur, arrested with aide Davinder Singh on November 14, was not cooperating and that her online chats showed she had been swayed by her handler.
