# Denmark to expel Russian diplomat over alleged plot to assassinate Ukrainian rapper

[Source](https://www.thejournal.ie/denmark-expel-russian-diplomat-assassination-plot-ukraine-rapper-7232897-Nov2026/)

(U) Copenhagen, Denmark — Danish public broadcaster DR reported that two Danish men were arrested before rapper Kyivstoner's September 25 concert in Charlottenlund and remanded in custody on suspicion of attempted murder; both denied wrongdoing. PET said there were indications that leading members of the banned gang Loyal to Familia helped plan the attack, which Danish authorities attributed to Russian intelligence. Foreign Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen ordered Russia's defense attaché to leave within seven days and said the government planned to stop issuing non-essential Russian visas and increase penalties for crimes committed for a foreign state.
