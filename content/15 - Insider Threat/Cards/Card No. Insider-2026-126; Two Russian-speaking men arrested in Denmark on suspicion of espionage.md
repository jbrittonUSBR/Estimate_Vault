# Two Russian-speaking men arrested in Denmark on suspicion of espionage

[Source](https://en.protothema.gr/2026/10/20/two-russian-speaking-men-arrested-in-denmark-on-suspicion-of-espionage/)

(U) October 20, 2026 — Danish police said they arrested two men on October 19 on suspicion of espionage and that the case was being investigated with intelligence services. The men appeared at Aalborg Court and were accused under Section 108(1) of Denmark’s Criminal Code. Protothema, citing Danish broadcaster DR, reported that the roughly 20-year-old men had Russian interpreters and that drone-detection and jamming company MyDefence was reportedly the target; police did not identify a target.
