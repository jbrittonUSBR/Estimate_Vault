# Germany's former intelligence chief faces espionage allegations

[Source](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/espionage-allegations-germanys-former-intelligence-chief-137062477)

(U) BERLIN, Germany — German authorities have launched espionage proceedings against Hans-Georg Maaßen, the former head of the domestic intelligence agency (BfV), over allegations that he passed sensitive information to unauthorized recipients. The case has raised concerns over potential insider threats within Germany's security apparatus, though specific details of the alleged disclosure remain under investigation.
