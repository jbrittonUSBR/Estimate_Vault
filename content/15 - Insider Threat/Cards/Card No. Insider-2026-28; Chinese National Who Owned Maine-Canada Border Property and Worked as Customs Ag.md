# Chinese National Who Owned Maine-Canada Border Property and Worked as Customs Agent Arrested at Bangor International Airport: "Damage of Government Property"

[Source](https://www.themainewire.com/2026/09/chinese-national-who-owned-maine-canada-border-property-and-worked-as-customs-agent-arrested-at-bangor-international-airport-damage-of-government-property/)

(U) BANGOR, United States — A Chinese national, Terry Liu, also known as Jia Jia Liu, of Calais, Maine, was arrested and charged with theft of government property and destruction of government property, according to Penobscot County Jail records and court records cited by The Maine Wire. Liu, who held a job as a customs officer at the Ferry Point Bridge, was apprehended at Bangor International Airport while trying to catch a flight back to China, a Washington County source said, and his Poole Street property on the Canadian border was raided. The Penobscot County Sheriff's Department declined to comment.
