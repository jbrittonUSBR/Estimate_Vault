# AI 'espionage' is a reality that companies need to be 'wary of,' DNI Jay Clayton says

[Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/16/ai-espionage-openai-jay-clayton.html)

(U) Washington, D.C. — Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton told CNBC that commercial and state-sponsored espionage were ongoing risks and that companies and the government should be wary of them. He said artificial intelligence could amplify existing cybersecurity risks to interconnected U.S. infrastructure. Clayton made the remarks while discussing a separate federal case involving an alleged recording device at an OpenAI executive conference room; the defendant had not been charged with espionage.
