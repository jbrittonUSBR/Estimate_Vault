# Army officers' wife booked for 'espionage'

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/india/wife-of-army-officer-in-jammu-and-kashmir-arrested-for-espionage-ties-with-chinese-operatives/articleshow/135033839.cms)

(U) JAMMU AND KASHMIR, India — Police booked the wife of an Army officer on espionage-related allegations, The Times of India reported. The report said investigators suspected links with Chinese operatives; the case remained an allegation and did not establish guilt.
