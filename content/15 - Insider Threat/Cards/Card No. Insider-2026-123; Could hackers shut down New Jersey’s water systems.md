# Could hackers shut down New Jersey’s water systems?

[Source](https://njbiz.com/could-hackers-shut-down-new-jerseys-water-systems/)

(U) October 20, 2026 — A contributor article published by NJBIZ described cyber incidents affecting municipal water systems in Minnesota and Michigan, including an outage at Braham’s treatment plant and communications disruptions elsewhere. It said the FBI was investigating incidents in seven states and that officials had not publicly attributed them to Iran; the article presented New Jersey’s exposure as a preparedness concern rather than reporting an attack there.
