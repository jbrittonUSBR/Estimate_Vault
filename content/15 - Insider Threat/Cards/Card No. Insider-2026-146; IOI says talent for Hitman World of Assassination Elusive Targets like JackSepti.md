# IOI says talent for Hitman World of Assassination Elusive Targets like JackSepticEye are found first, before a location is decided that 'thematically suits the talent' and with a story that 'synergizes with the talent in tone and genre'

[Source](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/ioi-says-talent-for-hitman-world-of-assassination-elusive-targets-like-jacksepticeye-are-found-first-before-a-location-is-decided-that-thematically-suits-the-talent-and-with-a-story-that-synergizes-with-the-talent-in-tone-and-genre)

(U) Copenhagen, Denmark — TechRadar reported that IO Interactive selected celebrity guests for Hitman: World of Assassination's Elusive Target missions before choosing a setting and story suited to each guest. The developers said the JackSepticEye mission was designed around the creator's persona and that character likenesses were crafted by artists rather than generated from 3D scans.
