# Members of Russian Intelligence Services Network Charged with Conspiring to Finance Terrorism and Commit Murder for Hire in the United States

[Source](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/members-russian-intelligence-services-network-charged-conspiring-finance-terrorism-and)

(U) WASHINGTON — The Justice Department announced that five members of a Russian intelligence services network — Yuri Khrameev, 63; Kirill Khrameev, 27; Oemis Romagoza Durruthy, 35; Yaidel Delgado Suarez, 35; and Angel Eduardo Castro, 22 — were charged with conspiring to finance terrorism and, in three cases, conspiring to commit murder for hire. According to the September 15, 2026 press release, the network had been active since at least 2024, paid recruits $1,000 to $1,500 for surveillance, offered $40,000 to "eliminate" a target in the United States and discussed $200 and then $25,000 for a killing in Lithuania. The release said Durruthy coordinated attacks in Prague in June 2024 and in Lithuania in September 2024. Count One carries up to 20 years and Count Two up to 10; the FBI's New York Counterintelligence Division and the U.S. Attorney's Office for the Southern District of New York handled the case, and the charges remain allegations.
