# Polish Police Arrest Man on Suspicion of Plotting Assassination of PM Tusk

[Source](https://www.breitbart.com/europe/2026/10/13/polish-police-arrest-man-on-suspicion-of-plotting-assassination-of-pm-tusk/)

(U) WARSAW, Poland — On October 13, 2026, Breitbart News reported that Polish police arrested a man in his 60s on suspicion of plotting to assassinate Prime Minister Donald Tusk and Deputy Prime Minister/Defense Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz. The suspect was traced through online posts expressing extreme views and was allegedly seeking volunteers to carry out an attack with firearms; authorities said the case was treated as deadly serious while the motive remained under investigation.
