# From Nvidia to Micron: Major China-linked espionage cases under scrutiny

[Source](https://www.business-standard.com/world-news/from-nvidia-to-micron-major-china-linked-espionage-cases-under-scrutiny-126110900779_1.html)

(U) United States/China — Business Standard reported that Nvidia's investigation into alleged Chinese intelligence targeting of CEO Jensen Huang revived scrutiny of Megaspeed executive Alice Huang and possible diversion of restricted chips to China, allegations denied by China and Megaspeed. The article also revisited the U.S. case alleging former Micron employees took confidential DRAM files before moving to a Chinese-linked company.
