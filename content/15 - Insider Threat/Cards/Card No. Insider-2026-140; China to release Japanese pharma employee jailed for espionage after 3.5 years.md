# China to release Japanese pharma employee jailed for espionage after 3.5 years

[Source](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/china/china-to-release-japanese-pharma-employee-jailed-for-espionage-after-3-5-years/articleshow/135651027.cms)

(U) Beijing, China — Kyodo reported that an Astellas Pharma employee in his 60s, detained in March 2023 and sentenced in July 2025 to three and a half years for espionage, was expected to be released in December. Japanese media said he would likely be deported after serving the full term, while the details of the case had not been made public.
