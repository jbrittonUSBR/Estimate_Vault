# Denmark Arrests Two for Spying

[Source](https://english.alarabiya.net/News/world/2026/10/20/denmark-arrests-two-for-spying)

(U) COPENHAGEN — Danish authorities arrested two people on suspicion of espionage, according to Al Arabiya's report of the case. The arrests add to a series of European counter-intelligence operations in October 2026 targeting suspected hostile-state spying, including cases in the UK and elsewhere, amid heightened concern over foreign intelligence activity against European targets.
