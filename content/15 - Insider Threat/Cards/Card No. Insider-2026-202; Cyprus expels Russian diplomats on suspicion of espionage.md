# Cyprus expels Russian diplomats on suspicion of espionage

[Source](https://www.politico.eu/article/cyprus-expels-russian-diplomats-on-suspicion-of-espionage/)

(U) Nicosia, Cyprus — Two Russian diplomats left Cyprus last week after Cypriot intelligence uncovered a network seeking classified information on Cyprus and EU security as well as technology barred from transfer to Russia under EU sanctions, two senior government officials told POLITICO. The officials, who spoke anonymously, said the men were suspected of working for Russian intelligence under diplomatic cover and had contacted Cypriot citizens with access to confidential information. POLITICO said it received no response from the Russian embassy and noted that European states have expelled more than 700 Russian officials since February 2022, according to Ukraine's foreign intelligence service.
