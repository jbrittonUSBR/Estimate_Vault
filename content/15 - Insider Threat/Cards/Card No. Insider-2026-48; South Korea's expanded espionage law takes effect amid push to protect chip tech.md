# South Korea's expanded espionage law takes effect amid push to protect chip technology

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/technology/south-koreas-expanded-espionage-law-takes-effect-amid-push-to-protect-chip-technology/articleshow/134195142.cms)

(U) SEOUL, South Korea — South Korea's expanded espionage law took effect on September 13, 2026, extending espionage offences beyond acts benefiting North Korea to cover all foreign countries as Seoul seeks to protect semiconductors and other strategic technologies. The National Assembly passed the revised Criminal Act on February 26 and the amendment was promulgated on March 12 after a six-month grace period; it creates a new offence of espionage for a foreign country or equivalent organization carrying a minimum three-year prison sentence. The National Intelligence Service welcomed the change, which followed the indictment of five former Samsung Electronics employees accused of transferring DRAM chip technology to Chinese memory maker CXMT, while China's foreign ministry spokesperson Mao Ning said countries should provide a fair, just and non-discriminatory business environment.
