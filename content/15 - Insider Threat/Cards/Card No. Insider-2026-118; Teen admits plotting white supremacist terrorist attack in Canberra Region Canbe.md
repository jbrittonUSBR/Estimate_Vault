# Teen admits plotting white supremacist terrorist attack in Canberra

[Source](https://region.com.au/teen-admits-plotting-white-supremacist-terrorist-attack-in-canberra/1009052/)

(U) October 21, 2026 — An 18-year-old man admitted in the ACT Supreme Court to terrorism-related charges involving a planned white-supremacist attack in Canberra, Region Canberra reported. Prosecutors alleged that he had planned to attack members of the public and possessed extremist material; the guilty pleas established the admitted offenses, while further details remained part of the court proceedings. He was due to be sentenced at a later date.
