# Inside Man: How Chinese spies used lies, love and betrayal to target the Federal Reserve

[Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/30/john-rogers-fed-china-espionage-case.html)

(U) WASHINGTON — John Harold Rogers, 65, a former senior adviser at the Federal Reserve, was sentenced on July 15, 2026, to 38 months in prison after his February 3 conviction for making false statements, CNBC reported in a September 30 profile of the case. Prosecutors said Rogers carried on a years-long relationship with a Chinese contact he knew as "Hummin Lee," believed to be Jin Chuan, and he was acquitted of a charge of conspiring to commit economic espionage. The case exposed gaps in the Fed's insider-risk controls, with the central bank's inspector general documenting weaknesses in how sensitive information was handled. Rogers had been arrested in January 2025.
