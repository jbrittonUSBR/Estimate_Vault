# Intelligence agency employee pleads guilty to trying to share classified info with foreign government

[Source](https://www.nextgov.com/people/2026/08/intelligence-agency-employee-arrested-trying-share-classified-info-foreign-government/405703/)

(U) WASHINGTON — Nathan Vilas Laatsch, a former civilian IT specialist for the Defense Intelligence Agency, pleaded guilty to trying to share classified information with a foreign government, according to the Justice Department. Laatsch had expressed dismay at the actions of the current administration in an email to an account associated with a friendly foreign nation and supplied a dead drop of a USB device, which the FBI intercepted through a dead-drop operation. He had held top-secret clearance and had been employed at the DIA as a civilian since 2019.
