# Food for onslaught: Supreme Court to decide if feeding extremists is supporting terror

[Source](https://www.wnd.com/2026/10/food-onslaught-supreme-court-decide-if-feeding-insurgents/)

(U) WASHINGTON, United States — The U.S. Supreme Court agreed to consider whether providing food, water, or other basic aid to designated extremist groups can constitute material support for terrorism, a case that could significantly expand the scope of federal anti-terrorism statutes. (U) WND (partisan) reporting indicated the dispute centered on legal interpretations of support provisions, though the precise lower-court rulings and scope remained the subject of debate. The outcome could have broad implications for humanitarian aid in conflict zones and domestic terrorism prosecutions.
