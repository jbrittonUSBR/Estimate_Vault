# The Canadian suspected of spying on NATO was denied bail. Here’s what comes next

[Source](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/canadian-nato-intern-spy-suspect-belgium-case-analysis-9.7310969)

(U) BRUSSELS, Belgium — Biwei Zhang, a 33-year-old Canadian computer scientist accused of spying on NATO's military headquarters in Belgium where she worked as an intern, lost an appeal for release from pretrial detention when a Belgian judge denied it on Monday. The federal prosecutor's office said the Chamber of Indictments decided to extend her pretrial detention by one month, and she must appear before a judge at regular intervals; a Belgian legal expert told CBC News it is "rather unlikely" she will be released before trial, which is not expected to begin until next year at the earliest. Zhang faces up to a decade in prison if convicted of espionage.
