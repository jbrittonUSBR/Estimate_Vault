# Lawmaker reportedly loses leg as car ramming stuns Australia

[Source](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/asia/australia-lawmaker-car-ramming-attack-helen-haines-leg-driver-charged-rcna604526)

(U) October 20, 2026 — Australian federal lawmaker Helen Haines was critically injured when a car struck her outside her electorate office in Wangaratta, and she later underwent amputation, NBC News reported. Police arrested and charged a man over the incident; authorities described it as a targeted attack but said they had no intelligence indicating a broader threat. The case remained under investigation.
