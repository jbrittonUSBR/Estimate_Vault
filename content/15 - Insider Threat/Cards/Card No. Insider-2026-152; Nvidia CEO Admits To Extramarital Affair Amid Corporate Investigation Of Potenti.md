# Nvidia CEO Admits To Extramarital Affair Amid Corporate Investigation Of Potential Chinese Espionage Targeting

[Source](https://dailycaller.com/2026/11/08/nvidia-ceo-admits-to-extramarital-affair-amid-corporate-investigation-of-potential-chinese-espionage-targeting)

(U) Santa Clara, California, United States — The Daily Caller reported that Jensen Huang told Nvidia employees he had been unfaithful to his wife and apologized to his family and colleagues. The report connected the admission to Nvidia's board investigation following allegations that a Chinese businesswoman tied to Megaspeed had targeted Huang, allegations the company said had not shown that secrets were compromised.
