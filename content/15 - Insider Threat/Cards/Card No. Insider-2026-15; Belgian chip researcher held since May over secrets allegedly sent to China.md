# Belgian chip researcher held since May over secrets allegedly sent to China

[Source](https://thenextweb.com/news/belgan-belgium-chip-espionage-arrest-china-mirror-company)

(U) BRUSSELS, Belgium — Belgian prosecutors have held a 52-year-old Belgian-Chinese researcher at chipmaker BelGaN since May 10 on suspicion of passing proprietary gallium nitride semiconductor secrets to a Chinese "mirror" company. Authorities are seeking a second suspect in the case, which centers on alleged technology transfer to China. The company and the investigation remained under scrutiny as prosecutors prepared the case.
