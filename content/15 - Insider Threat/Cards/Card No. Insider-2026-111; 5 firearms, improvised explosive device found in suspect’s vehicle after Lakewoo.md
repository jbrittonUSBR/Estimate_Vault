# 5 firearms, improvised explosive device found in suspect’s vehicle after Lakewood shelter-in-place call

[Source](https://www.denver7.com/news/crime/5-firearms-improvised-explosive-device-found-in-suspects-vehicle-after-lakewood-shelter-in-place-call)

(U) October 20, 2026 — Lakewood police said officers found five firearms and a suspected improvised explosive device in a vehicle after taking a man into custody, prompting a shelter-in-place alert for nearby residents and businesses. The device was assessed and secured by authorities, and the suspect faced multiple charges, Denver7 reported. The available account did not establish a motive for the incident.
