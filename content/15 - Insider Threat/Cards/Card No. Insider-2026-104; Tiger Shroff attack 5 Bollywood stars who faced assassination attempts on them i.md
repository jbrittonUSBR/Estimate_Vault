# Tiger Shroff attack: 5 Bollywood stars who faced assassination attempts on them in the past

[Source](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/magazines/panache/tiger-shroff-attack-5-bollywood-stars-who-faced-assassination-attempts-on-them-in-the-past/articleshow/135030476.cms)

(U) October 18, 2026 — The Times of India reported that gunmen fired at actor Tiger Shroff’s car in Mumbai’s Juhu neighborhood; Shroff escaped injury, while his driver and another person were reportedly hurt. Police said the attackers were linked to a gang and characterized the shooting as an effort to extort money and instill fear. The article also recounted earlier attacks and threats involving other Bollywood figures, including Salman Khan and Rohit Shetty.
