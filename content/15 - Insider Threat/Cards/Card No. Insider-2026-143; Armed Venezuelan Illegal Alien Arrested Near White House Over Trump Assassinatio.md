# Armed Venezuelan Illegal Alien Arrested Near White House Over Trump Assassination Threat

[Source](https://www.thegatewaypundit.com/2026/11/armed-venezuelan-illegal-alien-arrested-near-white-house/)

(U) Washington, D.C., United States — The Department of Homeland Security said Secret Service officers arrested Venezuelan national Ronny Guanipa-Chrinos near the White House after finding three firearms and ammunition in a stolen vehicle. DHS said he told officers that voices had directed him to come to Washington and kill President Trump, and federal prosecutors later charged him with threats and firearms and stolen-vehicle offenses.
