# Germany target of 'intensive' espionage, Merz says after suspected Russian spy arrest

[Source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmx28y5e04ggo)

(U) Germany — BBC News reported that Chancellor Friedrich Merz said Germany faced intensive espionage after prosecutors arrested a man suspected of spying for Russia. The arrest and suspected Russian direction were described as allegations under investigation, while the characterization of the broader espionage threat was attributed to Merz.
