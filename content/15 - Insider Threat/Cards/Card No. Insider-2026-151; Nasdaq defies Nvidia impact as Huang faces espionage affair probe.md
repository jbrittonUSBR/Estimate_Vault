# Nasdaq defies Nvidia impact as Huang faces espionage affair probe

[Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/09/nasdaq-defies-nvidia-impact-huang-faces-espionage-affair-probe.html)

(U) Santa Clara, California, United States — CNBC reported that Nvidia shares fell as investors weighed CEO Jensen Huang's admission of an affair and the board's investigation into reports he had been targeted by foreign intelligence. The company said the independent review would examine the allegations, while the board said it had no reason to believe national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised.
