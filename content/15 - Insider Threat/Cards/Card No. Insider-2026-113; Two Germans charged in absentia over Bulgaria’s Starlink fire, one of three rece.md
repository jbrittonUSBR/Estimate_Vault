# Two Germans charged in absentia over Bulgaria’s Starlink fire, one of three recent attacks on satellite links Ukraine relies on against Russia

[Source](https://euromaidanpress.com/2026/10/20/two-germans-charged-in-absentia-over-bulgarias-starlink-fire-one-of-three-recent-attacks-on-satellite-links-ukraine-relies-on-against-russia/#google_vignette)

(U) October 20, 2026 — Bulgarian authorities charged two 19-year-old German nationals in absentia over a fire that damaged Starlink antennas at a ground facility near Sofia, according to Euromaidan Press. The outlet reported prosecutors said the suspects entered from Serbia, allegedly set fire to antennas at the Plana teleport, and left Bulgaria that night; a court approved European arrest warrants after finding reasonable grounds to suspect them. Bulgarian authorities had not announced who might have directed the attack, and the outlet’s discussion of other Starlink-site arsons did not establish a link between the cases.
