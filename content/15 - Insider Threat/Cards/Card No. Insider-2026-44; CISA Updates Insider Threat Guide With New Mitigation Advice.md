# CISA Updates Insider Threat Guide With New Mitigation Advice

[Source](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/cisa-updates-insider-threat-guide/)

(U) WASHINGTON — The Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency published a revision of its Insider Threat Mitigation Guide on September 9, 2026, adding case studies, statistics and guidance on hybrid and remote work, artificial intelligence, access control, visitor screening and adverse employee separations. First issued in 2020, the guide was streamlined and expanded to address what CISA described as a dynamic operational landscape and the growing impact of insider threats on critical infrastructure. Scott Breor, CISA's acting executive assistant director for infrastructure security, said insider threats continue to evolve as technology advances and urged organizations to build programs that protect assets, prevent violence and safeguard sensitive data.
