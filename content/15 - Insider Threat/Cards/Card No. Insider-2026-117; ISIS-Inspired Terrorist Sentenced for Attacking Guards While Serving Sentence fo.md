# ISIS-Inspired Terrorist Sentenced for Attacking Guards While Serving Sentence for Separate NYC Terror Plot

[Source](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/isis-inspired-terrorist-sentenced-attacking-guards-while-serving-sentence-separate-nyc)

(U) October 19, 2026 — The U.S. Department of Justice announced that Abdulrahman El Bahnasawy was sentenced to 50 years in prison for offenses related to a 2020 assault on correctional officers at the federal prison where he was serving a sentence for a separate New York terror plot. The department said he attacked guards with a makeshift weapon and caused serious injuries. These details and the characterization of his motivation are presented in the DOJ press release.
