# Historian charged with espionage by Israel after traveling to Iran multiple times: report

[Source](https://www.jns.org/news/israel-news/report-russian-israeli-historian-charged-with-espionage)

(U) JERUSALEM, Israel — Israeli authorities charged Russian-Israeli historian Artyom Kirpichenok with espionage after he reportedly traveled to Iran several times, according to Russian media accounts cited by the Jewish News Syndicate. Kirpichenok was detained by Israel's Shin Bet security service in connection with the allegations. The report did not detail the evidence underlying the charges.
