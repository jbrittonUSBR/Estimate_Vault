# FBI's spy hunting squads hit with deep cuts even as foreign espionage threats soar

[Source](https://www.npr.org/2026/10/06/g-s1-145511/fbi-trump-counterintelligence-units-cuts)

(U) Washington, D.C., United States — On October 6, 2026, NPR reported that the FBI had made deep cuts to its counterintelligence squads tasked with hunting spies, even as officials warned of soaring foreign espionage threats; current and former officials expressed concern that reduced staffing would hamper efforts to detect insider threats and foreign intelligence operations.
