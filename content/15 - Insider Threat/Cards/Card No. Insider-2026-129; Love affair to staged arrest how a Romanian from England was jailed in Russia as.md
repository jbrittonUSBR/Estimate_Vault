# Love affair to staged arrest: how a Romanian from England was jailed in Russia as a spy

[Source](https://nypost.com/2026/10/22/world-news/romanian-man-david-adrian-kercso-sentenced-to-15-years-in-russian-prison-for-spying-but-fsb-arrest-video-was-staged-report/)

(U) Russia — Reuters, republished by the New York Post, reported that Romanian-British citizen David Adrian Kercso was sentenced to 15 years in Russia for espionage. The account described his relationship with a Russian woman and alleged that an FSB arrest video was staged; these details were attributed to the reporting and Russian case materials, and the espionage charge was prosecuted by Russian authorities.
