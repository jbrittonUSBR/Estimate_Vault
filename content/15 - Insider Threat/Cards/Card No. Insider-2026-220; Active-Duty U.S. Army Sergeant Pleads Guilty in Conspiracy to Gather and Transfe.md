# Active-Duty U.S. Army Sergeant Pleads Guilty in Conspiracy to Gather and Transfer National Defense Information

[Source](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/active-duty-us-army-sergeant-pleads-guilty-conspiracy-gather-and-transfer-national-defense)

(U) WASHINGTON — The Department of Justice announced that Jian Zhao, a 37-year-old active-duty Army sergeant at Joint Base Lewis-McChord, pleaded guilty to conspiring to gather and transfer national defense information to people connected to China. Court documents cited by DOJ said Zhao provided excerpts from High Mobility Artillery Rocket System manuals, offered military computers and drives for sale and photographed restricted presentations, receiving thousands of dollars from overseas buyers. U.S. Attorney Scott Bradford and FBI Assistant Director Roman Rozhavsky described the case as a betrayal of trust and oath, and DOJ said Zhao faced up to 10 years in prison and was due to be sentenced on March 1, 2027.
