# Novel Blue Moon kit targeting Chrome and Windows reflects new reality of AI-driven exploits

[Source](https://www.theregister.com/research/2026/09/09/novel-blue-moon-kit-targeting-chrome-and-windows-reflects-new-reality-of-ai-driven-exploits/5295399)

(U) LONDON, United Kingdom — Proofpoint researchers documented a new exploit kit, named BlueMoon, that chains two Chromium-based browser flaws and a Windows privilege-escalation bug to breach networks, with at least four espionage groups — most with suspected China links — adopting it within days. The kit exploited the so-called "patch-gap" window, in which fixes are visible in upstream open-source code before reaching stable releases, and researchers said AI-assisted development was increasingly lowering the barrier to such capabilities.
