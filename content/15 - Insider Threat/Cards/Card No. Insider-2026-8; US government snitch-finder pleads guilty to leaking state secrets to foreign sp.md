# US government snitch-finder pleads guilty to leaking state secrets to foreign spies

[Source](https://slashdot.org/firehose.pl?op=view&amp;id=185261102)

(U) WASHINGTON — Nathan Vilas Laatsch, a former Defense Intelligence Agency IT specialist, pleaded guilty to trying to pass secret and top-secret information to foreign spies following an FBI sting, according to a Slashdot Firehose item summarizing a report by The Register. The item noted that Laatsch began contacting a foreign government within days of being assigned to the DIA's Insider Threat Division, the unit dedicated to identifying government workers likely to leak classified information.
