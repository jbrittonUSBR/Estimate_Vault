# “If we do not respond, Putin’s pressure will intensify”: Relentless cyberattacks, assassination plots and missiles in the sky are shaking Europe

[Source](https://en.protothema.gr/2026/11/24/if-we-do-not-respond-putins-pressure-will-intensify-relentless-cyberattacks-assassination-plots-and-missiles-in-the-sky-are-shaking-europe/)

(U) BRUSSELS, BELGIUM — NATO and EU ambassadors held meetings after Russian missiles violated Polish airspace and Denmark briefed allies on an alleged Russian-intelligence assassination plot against a Ukrainian-American rapper, Proto Thema reported, citing DW sources. NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte called the Poland incident "absolutely unacceptable" and warned that Europe faced relentless cyberattacks, sabotage and other hybrid attacks requiring mobilisation by governments, businesses and citizens. European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen linked the escalation to a proposed 22nd sanctions package covering former Russian combatants, energy, shipping and financial transactions.
