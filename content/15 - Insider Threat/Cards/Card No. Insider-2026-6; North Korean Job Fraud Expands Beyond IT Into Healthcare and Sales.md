# North Korean Job Fraud Expands Beyond IT Into Healthcare and Sales

[Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/north-korean-job-fraud-expands-beyond.html)

(U) UNDISCLOSED — Threat actors tied to North Korea were observed seeking job opportunities beyond the information technology sector, with recent investigations identifying suspected workers employed in sales and marketing and in the medical profession, according to The Hacker News. The activity is part of the wider "IT worker scheme," tracked under names including Famous Chollima, Jasper Sleet, and Wagemole, in which Pyongyang uses skilled workers with stolen or forged identity documents and VPNs to fraudulently land remote jobs at companies worldwide and earn income for its weapons programs. In one February 2026 case, three employees of an Australian healthcare company were flagged as North Korean workers impersonating Chinese individuals, and a second case at a financial services firm uncovered the use of a PiKVM switch associated with laptop farms.
