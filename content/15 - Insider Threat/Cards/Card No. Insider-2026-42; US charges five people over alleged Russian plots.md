# US charges five people over alleged Russian plots

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/16/us-charges-five-people-over-alleged-russian-assassination-plots)

(U) WASHINGTON — U.S. authorities charged five people — Yuri Khrameev, 63; Kirill Khrameev, 27; Oemis Romagoza Durruthy, 35; Yaidel Delgado Suarez, 35; and Angel Eduardo Castro, 22 — with conspiring to finance terrorism, and three of them also with conspiracy to commit murder for hire, Al Jazeera reported, citing Reuters. Prosecutors said the group, linked to a Russian intelligence services network, plotted to kill a Russian dissident living near Washington, D.C., and recruited individuals to conduct surveillance, offering $40,000 to make one target "disappear" and $25,000 for a killing in Lithuania. All five remained at large, and Russia's embassy in the United States had not commented on the case. Attorney General Todd Blanche announced the charges at a press conference; the allegations have not been proven in court.
