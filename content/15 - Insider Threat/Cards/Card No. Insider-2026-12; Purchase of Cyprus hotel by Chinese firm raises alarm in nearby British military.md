# Purchase of Cyprus hotel by Chinese firm raises alarm in nearby British military base

[Source](https://intelnews.org/2026/09/07/01-3453/)

(U) NICOSIA, Cyprus — A Chinese-owned luxury hotel in Cyprus sparked concerns among British intelligence and military officials that it could be used to spy on Royal Air Force Akrotiri, according to a report published September 7, 2026. The 15-acre, 16-story City of Dreams resort, owned by Melco Resorts and Entertainment, stands roughly 4.5 miles north of the base and affords a panoramic view of the facility, which hosts British aircraft as well as U.S. Air Force F-35 and U-2 aircraft. Officials noted the site's potential to monitor aircraft movement and personnel, and one source said "there was no world in which the Chinese state would not seek to exploit the hotel's location." Melco and City of Dreams firmly denied the allegations as "entirely unfounded," and no official evidence of espionage or wrongdoing has been presented.
