# Saudi national charged in OpenAI bugging to be released on $1 million bond

[Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/openai-bugging-aflozan-release-saudi.html)

(U) SAN FRANCISCO, CALIFORNIA — CNBC reported that U.S. Magistrate Judge Alex Tse ruled Abdulrahman Alfozan, a 35-year-old Saudi national accused of bugging an OpenAI executive conference room, could be released on a $1 million bond and an ankle monitor. His attorney David Rizk said prosecutors had found no new evidence supporting trade-secret theft or unregistered foreign-agent theories, while the San Francisco U.S. Attorney's Office opposed release, calling him a flight risk with considerable resources and ties to Saudi Arabia. Prosecutors said a hidden recording device found in a television's USB port captured dozens of meetings, and CNBC said jury selection was set to begin January 20.
