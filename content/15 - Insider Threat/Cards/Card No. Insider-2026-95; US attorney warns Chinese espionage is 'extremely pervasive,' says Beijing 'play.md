# US attorney warns Chinese espionage is 'extremely pervasive,' says Beijing 'playing the long game'

[Source](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/us-attorney-warns-chinese-espionage-extremely-pervasive-beijing-playing-long-game)

(U) LOS ANGELES, United States — On October 12, 2026, Fox News reported that First Assistant U.S. Attorney Bill Essayli warned Chinese government-backed espionage is “extremely pervasive” in the United States, citing recent cases including the arrest of Wanying Zhang for allegedly surveilling a relative of Taiwan’s president on Beijing’s behalf. He said China is “playing the long game” and that his office is committed to bringing charges to disrupt spying operations.
