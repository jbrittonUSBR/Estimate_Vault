# Hungary expels 10 Russian diplomats amid espionage concerns

[Source](https://www.politico.eu/article/hungary-expels-10-russian-diplomats-amid-espionage-concerns/)

(U) BUDAPEST, Hungary — Hungary ordered the expulsion of 10 Russian diplomats on grounds of 'security and sovereignty,' according to Foreign Minister Anita Orbán, who said they had engaged in activities unacceptable under the Vienna Convention. Prime Minister Péter Magyar cited 'unacceptable activities' without providing specifics. Russia's Foreign Ministry spokeswoman, Maria Zakharova, vowed a 'harsh and painful' response.
