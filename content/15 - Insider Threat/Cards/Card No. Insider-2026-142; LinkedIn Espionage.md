# LinkedIn Espionage

[Source](https://www.lawyersgunsmoneyblog.com/2026/11/linkedin-espionage)

(U) United States — Luke Jacobus wrote in The Diplomat that Five Eyes agencies had warned Chinese intelligence officers used fake recruiter and consulting profiles on LinkedIn and other platforms to approach U.S. and NATO personnel, academics, and others with access to sensitive information. Jacobus said the approaches were tailored to targets' resumes with AI-assisted messages and could progress from paid reports on routine topics to requests for more sensitive material.
