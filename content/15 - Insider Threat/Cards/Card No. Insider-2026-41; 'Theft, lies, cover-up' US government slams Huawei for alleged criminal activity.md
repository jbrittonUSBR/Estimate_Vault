# 'Theft, lies, cover-up': US government slams Huawei for alleged criminal activity as blockbuster trial begins

[Source](https://www.techradar.com/pro/theft-lies-cover-up-us-government-slams-huawei-for-alleged-criminal-activity-as-blockbuster-trial-begins)

(U) NEW YORK — The U.S. Justice Department's long-running criminal case against Huawei opened in federal court, with prosecutor Taylor Stout describing a 20-year campaign to victimize American companies including Cisco, T-Mobile, Motorola, CNEX Labs and Akhan Semiconductor, TechRadar reported. Defense attorney Brian Heberling characterized the government's case as "competition, not conspiracy," and Huawei denied wrongdoing. The trial, which took eight years to reach a courtroom and was expected to last about three months, will also make use of admissions by Huawei executive Meng Wanzhou. The account reflected opening arguments by both sides; the charges remain allegations.
