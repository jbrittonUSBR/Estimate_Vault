(U) Singapore — Livemint described Megaspeed as a cloud-computing provider that rented access to Nvidia GPUs from data centers in Southeast Asia. It reported that U.S. authorities had scrutinized whether chips were diverted to China, while Megaspeed denied wrongdoing and said reviews had found no illegal transfers.
