# Hackers build AI frameworks for widescale credential theft

[Source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-build-ai-frameworks-for-widescale-credential-theft/)

(U) MOUNTAIN VIEW, California — Google's Threat Intelligence Group reported that hackers have moved beyond simple prompt-based use of large language models to build multi-agent AI frameworks that automate credential-theft attacks. BleepingComputer reported that the tooling chains multiple AI agents together to carry out reconnaissance, exploitation and credential harvesting with minimal human involvement. The finding reflected an evolution in how attackers are applying generative AI to offensive operations.
