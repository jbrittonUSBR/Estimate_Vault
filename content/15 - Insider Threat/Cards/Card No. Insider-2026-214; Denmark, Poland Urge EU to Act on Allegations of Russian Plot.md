# Denmark, Poland Urge EU to Act on Allegations of Russian Plot

[Source](https://www.newser.com/story/400444/denmark-poland-urge-eu-to-act-on-allegations-of-russian-plot.html)

(U) Copenhagen, Denmark — Danish authorities said they thwarted a plot, planned for September 25 at a concert north of Copenhagen, to kill Ukrainian-American rapper Albert Vasiliev, known as Kyivstoner, and charged two men with attempted murder and assisting a foreign intelligence service, according to the Danish Security and Intelligence Service as reported by the Associated Press. Poland disclosed that the same man had allegedly been targeted by Russian intelligence in Poland the previous summer, and Denmark said it would seek EU-wide restrictions on Russian citizens' entry and travel, a move Poland said it backed. The Russian Embassy called the accusations unsubstantiated, and Denmark expelled Russia's defense attaché.
