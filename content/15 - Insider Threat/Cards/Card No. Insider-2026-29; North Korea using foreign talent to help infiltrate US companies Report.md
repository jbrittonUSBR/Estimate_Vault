# North Korea using foreign talent to help infiltrate US companies: Report

[Source](https://cointelegraph.com/news/north-korea-using-foreign-talent-to-help-infiltrate-us-companies-report)

(U) SEOUL, South Korea — Cointelegraph, citing an NBC News report, reported that North Korea has turned to third-country IT workers, including from Iran and Lebanon, to pass job interviews at US companies, after which the positions are usually taken over by North Korean operatives. Foreign workers were reportedly scouted on LinkedIn, with some offered $500 a month in cryptocurrency to work part-time as "interview associates." A July US government alert warned that North Korean IT workers pose an insider threat involving data exfiltration, cryptocurrency theft and theft of sensitive information.
