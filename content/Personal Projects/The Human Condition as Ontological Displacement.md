## From Anomie to Abolition

The Abolition of Man is the cultural and ontological endpoint of sustained anomie. When ontological anomie becomes normative—when prelest is dignified as authentic freedom rather than recognized as teleological estrangement—human nature itself begins to disintegrate. Existentialism attempts to formalize this telos-refusal into philosophy, while technology amplifies it structurally, creating conditions where communion becomes systematically unavailable and the biological hypostasis reigns unchallenged.Ontological-Communion_-The-Call-to-Metanoia-in-an-Age-of-Man-s-Abolition-through-Technology.pdf+1

This is not mere social dysfunction but the clinical presentation of an ontological disorder: beings attempting to exist as though they were not teleologically structured, generating restless, fragmented selves in restless, fragmented worlds. The alternative is metanoia—the repeated, humble willingness to ask in each moment not what it should have been, but what it is, and what it asks.
